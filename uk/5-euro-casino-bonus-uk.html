What Is a 5 Euro Casino Bonus?

A 5 pound casino bonus is a welcome offer where you deposit £5 and receive additional bonus funds or free spins in return. The bonus might be a 100% match (deposit £5, get £5 extra) or a set number of free spins (deposit £5, get 20 free spins). The key is that £5 alone triggers the offer.
