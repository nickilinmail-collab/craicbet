How to Withdraw Casino Winnings in the UK

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Withdrawing your casino winnings should be straightforward. In practice, the process varies by casino and payment method. This guide covers everything UK players need to know to get paid efficiently.

Step 1: Complete Identity Verification (KYC)

All licensed casinos require identity verification before processing withdrawals. This is a legal requirement under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations. You'll need to provide: government-issued photo ID (passport or driving licence), proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, usually within 3 months), and sometimes proof of payment method (card photo or bank statement).

Complete KYC as soon as you open your account — before you need to withdraw. Casinos that have already verified you process withdrawals much faster.

Step 2: Ensure No Bonus is Active

If you have an active bonus with pending wagering requirements, you usually can't withdraw until it's met — or you must forfeit the bonus first. Check your bonus status in the account section before requesting a withdrawal.

🔒 Licensing: No UK casino currently holds a UKGC gaming licence. UKGC issued only remote betting licences from 1 July 2026; casino licensing is expected from 2027–2028. UK players can legally use MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar-licensed casinos.

Step 3: Request Your Withdrawal

Go to the cashier/banking section. Select 'Withdraw'. Choose your preferred method (usually the same as your deposit). Enter the amount. Confirm. The casino's system will process this — typically within 0–48 hours during their pending period.

Withdrawal Methods and Times

  • E-wallet (Skrill, Neteller) — 0–24 hours after casino processing
  • Revolut — 1–4 hours to 2 business days
  • Debit card (Visa/Mastercard) — 1–3 business days
  • Bank transfer — 2–5 business days
  • Crypto — Minutes to 2 hours

Withdrawal Limits

Casinos typically impose daily, weekly, and monthly withdrawal limits. Standard limits: £2,000–£10,000/week. VIP players often get higher limits. If your win is large, check withdrawal limits and plan the withdrawal schedule accordingly — there's no way to override hard limits quickly.

If Your Withdrawal Is Delayed

Contact live chat first — most issues resolve quickly. If KYC is pending, submit documents immediately. If the delay exceeds the stated timeframe without explanation, contact the casino in writing (email creates a paper trail). If unresolved after a reasonable period, file a complaint with the casino's licensing authority (MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar).
