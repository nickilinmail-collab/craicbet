PokerStars the UK 2026: Betting Licence Status & What UK players Need to Know

But UK players searching for PokerStars betting or PokerStars Sports often hit a confusing picture: Flutter holds UKGC licences in the UK, but not under the PokerStars brand. As of November 2026, PokerStars does not appear separately on the UKGC’s public register. Here is what that means for UK players.

What Is PokerStars?

PokerStars is the world’s largest online poker room by player traffic, operating globally and known for its depth of cash game and tournament offerings. It is owned by Flutter Entertainment, the UK-headquartered betting and gaming group listed on the New York Stock Exchange and the London Stock Exchange. Flutter is one of the largest gambling companies in the world by revenue.

PokerStars holds licences from the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) and the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) for its poker products — both reputable regulators. In addition, PokerStars Sports, the brand’s sportsbook arm, is available in certain markets alongside the core poker offering. That said, holding an MGA or UKGC licence does not automatically authorise an operator to target UK customers under the UK’s new regulatory framework. Since 1 July 2026, that requires a separate UKGC remote betting licence.

The connection to Flutter creates a natural point of confusion for UK players: Flutter is the UK-headquartered and holds UKGC licences here, so it is reasonable to wonder whether that coverage extends to the PokerStars brand. The short answer is that UKGC licences are issued per brand, not per parent company — and PokerStars does not appear on the UKGC register as a separately licensed brand.

Does PokerStars Have a UKGC Licence in the UK?

the UK’s Gambling Regulation Act 2024 established the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) as the country’s dedicated gambling regulator. The UKGC began issuing remote betting licences from 1 July 2026. Any operator wishing to legally target UK customers with betting products must hold one of these licences.

As of this writing in November 2026, PokerStars does not appear on the UKGC’s public register of licensed remote betting operators as a separately named brand. Flutter Entertainment holds UKGC remote betting licences in the UK under other brand names within its portfolio, but those licences do not cover the PokerStars brand. PokerStars is not listed separately on the UKGC register.

However, it does mean that PokerStars is not currently authorised to operate under the UK’s specific regulatory framework as a separately named brand.

PokerStars Poker vs PokerStars Sports in the UK

It is worth distinguishing between the two main products under the PokerStars brand, as the picture for UK players differs between them.

PokerStars Poker may be accessible to UK users via its MGA licence, depending on whether PokerStars has implemented geographic restrictions for the UK market. Many MGA-licensed poker operators have continued to accept players from jurisdictions that have new but not yet fully enforced licensing regimes. However, as with all non-UKGC operators, UK players using PokerStars Poker would not receive UKGC-mandated protections.

PokerStars Sports, the sportsbook arm, exists in a number of markets. However, without a UKGC remote betting licence specifically covering the PokerStars brand in the UK, the status of PokerStars Sports for UK players is unclear. Under the UK’s Gambling Regulation Act 2024, sports betting is explicitly covered by the UKGC licensing requirement. An operator offering sports betting to UK customers without a UKGC licence is not operating under UK regulatory authorisation for that product.

UK players considering either product should be clear on the distinction: accessible via another jurisdiction’s licence is not the same as being UKGC-licensed for the UK.

Flutter Entertainment’s UK Presence

Flutter Entertainment is headquartered in Dublin and is one of the UK’s most prominent listed companies. Its UK roots run deep — it was formed from the merger of Paddy Power and Betfair in 2016, and has since grown into a global group through further acquisitions including PokerStars’ parent company The Stars Group in 2020.

Flutter holds multiple UKGC remote betting licences in the UK under various brands within its portfolio. These UKGC licences are brand-specific: each licensed brand has its own licence number and regulatory obligations. The PokerStars brand does not appear to hold its own separate UKGC licence at this time, even though the parent company’s other brands do.

This is an important structural point: a parent company holding UKGC licences for some brands does not automatically extend that regulatory coverage to all brands under its umbrella. UKGC licensing is brand-level, not group-level. UK players should not assume that Flutter’s overall UK presence means every Flutter brand is UKGC-licensed in the UK.
