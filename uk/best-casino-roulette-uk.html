Live roulette uses a real wheel and a human dealer, streamed from a studio. Evolution Gaming's Lightning Roulette adds random number multipliers (50x–500x) on top of standard European payouts. RNG Roulette vs Live Roulette

RNG (Random Number Generator) roulette uses a computer to determine outcomes. It's faster and cheaper to run. Live roulette uses physical equipment, so outcomes are determined by genuine physics — the ball landing on a pocket. Both are audited for fairness by independent testing laboratories. If you prefer the social feel of a real wheel, live is the better choice.

Bankroll Tips for Roulette

Roulette is a negative-expectation game. No betting system (Martingale, D'Alembert, Fibonacci) changes the house edge. Set a session budget you're comfortable losing, stick to even-money bets (Red/Black, Odd/Even) to minimise variance, and use the deposit and loss limits available in your casino account.
