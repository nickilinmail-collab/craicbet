Casino Deposit Limits the UK — Your Complete Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Deposit limits are one of the most effective responsible gambling tools available at online casinos. Setting them before you need them — not after — is the recommended approach. This guide explains how they work at UK-facing casinos.

Types of Deposit Limits

  • Daily deposit limit — Maximum amount you can deposit in a 24-hour period
  • Weekly deposit limit — Maximum over 7 days
  • Monthly deposit limit — Maximum over 30 days
  • Session limit — Maximum per single session (some casinos also offer this)

How to Set a Deposit Limit

Log into your casino account. Go to Account Settings → Responsible Gambling → Deposit Limits. Enter your preferred daily, weekly, or monthly limit. The limit takes effect immediately (or within 24 hours, depending on the casino). Reductions take effect immediately; increases are subject to a cooling-off period (typically 24–72 hours under UKGC rules).

🔒 Licensing: No UK casino currently holds a UKGC gaming licence. UKGC issued only remote betting licences from 1 July 2026; casino licensing is expected from 2027–2028. UK players can legally use MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar-licensed casinos.

Cooling-Off Periods

Increasing a deposit limit is not instant. Regulators require a cooling-off period before an increased limit takes effect — by design, to prevent impulsive decisions. Under UKGC rules, most limit increases have at least a 24-hour delay; some have 7 days. This is intentional friction that protects players from themselves during moments of pressure.

UKGC and Deposit Limits

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) will require UK-licensed casinos to offer enhanced player protection tools, including mandatory deposit limits, once casino gaming licences are issued (expected 2027–2028). Currently, UK players rely on the responsible gambling tools of their casino's existing regulator (UKGC, MGA, or Gibraltar).

Using Limits Proactively

Set limits that reflect your entertainment budget — before you start playing. A simple rule: your monthly casino deposit limit should be money you're comfortable losing entirely. If you'd be uncomfortable losing it, it's too high. Setting limits before you need them avoids the difficulty of making responsible decisions in the moment.
