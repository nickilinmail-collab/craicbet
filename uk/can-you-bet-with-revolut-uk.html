Can You Bet With Revolut in the UK? Everything You Need to Know

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Yes — Revolut works at most UKGC-licensed UK betting sites. Because Revolut is classified as an e-money/prepaid debit card rather than a credit instrument, it falls outside the UKGC credit card ban and is fully accepted for deposits and withdrawals at the majority of UK bookmakers in 2026.

Why Revolut Is Allowed for Betting

The UKGC credit card ban specifically prohibits credit card deposits at licensed gambling sites. Revolut is an e-money account — you top it up with funds you already have, and spending is limited to that balance. It is functionally a prepaid debit card and is not classified as credit under UK financial regulations. UKGC-licensed operators accept it on this basis.

How to Deposit With Revolut

At most UKGC-licensed bookmakers, Revolut deposits work via the standard Visa or Mastercard debit card route — just enter your Revolut card number, expiry, and CVV from the Revolut app. Some operators also support instant bank transfer via Open Banking, which works with Revolut accounts directly.

Deposits are instant. The minimum deposit is typically £10. Daily deposit limits on the betting site apply, as do your own Revolut card spending limits (adjustable in the Revolut app).

Revolut Withdrawals From Betting Sites

Withdrawals to Revolut work in the same way as debit card withdrawals. The operator sends the funds to your Revolut Visa or Mastercard card number, and they appear in your Revolut account. Processing typically takes 1–2 business days, which is slightly faster than traditional bank debit cards because Revolut’s infrastructure processes incoming transfers in near-real time.

Are There Any Limits With Revolut?

Revolut itself may have card spending limits depending on your plan tier (Standard, Plus, Premium, Metal). These limits apply to all card transactions, not just gambling. If you have a free Standard plan, check your spending limits in the Revolut app before making large deposits. Premium plans have higher or unlimited limits.

How to Enable and Disable the Revolut Gambling Block

  1. Open the Revolut app and go to Hub → Payments (or the grid/controls icon).
  2. Tap Gambling (or find it under payment controls).
  3. Toggle the gambling block on or off. When on, all gambling-category transactions are declined.
  4. To disable: toggle off, confirm with biometrics or PIN. There may be a short cooling-off period before it deactivates.
