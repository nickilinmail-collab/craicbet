Can You Get Free Bets Without Deposit in the UK?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

No-deposit free bets do exist at UK betting sites, but they are rare in 2026. The majority of welcome offers require a qualifying deposit. When no-deposit offers do appear, they are typically small amounts (£5–£10) with specific conditions attached. Here is what UK punters should realistically expect.

The Reality of No-Deposit Bonuses in 2026

Under the UKGC framework, operators must comply with inducements regulations that restrict how and where bonuses can be advertised. This has not banned no-deposit bonuses, but the market has moved toward deposit-based offers because they attract customers with actual gambling intent, not purely bonus-hunters.

Genuine no-deposit free bets in the UK market in 2026 are typically offered by: smaller or newer operators trying to build a customer base, existing operators running limited-time new account promotions, or operators offering a small account credit on registration.

Typical No-Deposit Free Bet Conditions

When no-deposit offers exist, expect:

  • Small amounts: £5–£10, not the £30–£50 headline figures of deposit-based offers
  • High minimum odds: Usually 2/1 (3.0) or higher to use the free bet
  • Withdrawal wagering: Winnings from the free bet often need to be wagered once before withdrawal
  • Short expiry: Often 7 days
  • Verification required: Full KYC (name, address, age) before the free bet is credited, even without a deposit

How to Find Genuine No-Deposit Offers

Check the promotions page of newly licensed UKGC operators (track new licences on our UKGC register tracker). Subscribe to push notifications from operators you are interested in. Some operators email verified customers with no-deposit free bets after a period of inactivity as a re-engagement offer.

Minimum-Deposit Offers as an Alternative

Many operators offer enhanced welcome bonuses for new customers who deposit small amounts (£5–£10). These are often more valuable than no-deposit offers and have more relaxed conditions. If you are hesitant to deposit a large amount, a minimum-deposit offer is often the better practical option.

Can you get free bets without depositing in the UK?

Yes, but they are rare — typically £5–£10, with high minimum odds and short expiry. Most UK bookmaker welcome offers require a qualifying deposit. Check newly licensed UKGC operators for no-deposit promotions.

Are no-deposit bonuses legal under UKGC rules?

Yes — no-deposit bonuses are legal. UKGC inducements rules require clear terms disclosure but do not ban no-deposit offers. The offers are simply less common commercially.

Why are no-deposit free bets so rare in the UK?

Commercially, deposit-based offers attract higher-value customers. No-deposit offers tend to attract bonus hunters who do not deposit and generate no long-term revenue. Most operators find them unprofitable.

What is the smallest deposit to get a free bet in the UK?

Some operators offer welcome bonuses for deposits as low as £5–£10. These minimum-deposit offers often provide better value than no-deposit free bets in terms of the amount you receive.

Do I need to verify my identity for a no-deposit free bet?

Yes — UKGC-licensed operators must verify age and identity before allowing any gambling activity, including free bet use. You will need to complete KYC even without depositing.
