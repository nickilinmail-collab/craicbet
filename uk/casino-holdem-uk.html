How Casino Hold'em Works

Casino Hold'em was developed by Stephen Au-Yeung in the early 2000s as a way to bring Texas Hold'em mechanics into a classic casino table game format. It has since become a staple of live dealer lobbies worldwide, particularly through Evolution Gaming's live studio production.

A hand unfolds as follows:

  1. Place the Ante bet (and optionally the AA Bonus side bet).
  2. You receive two hole cards; the dealer receives two hole cards. Three community cards (the Flop) are dealt face up in the middle of the table.
  3. Decision point: you can Fold (lose your Ante) or Call (place a Call bet worth twice the Ante).
  4. The dealer deals two more community cards (Turn and River).
  5. Both you and the dealer make the best five-card hand from the two hole cards and five community cards. The best hand wins.
  6. Payout: if the dealer qualifies (a pair of 4s or better), and you beat the dealer, the Ante pays according to the Ante Bonus pay table and the Call bet pays 1:1. If the dealer doesn't qualify, the Ante pays 1:1 and the Call bet is returned (push).

Casino Hold'em Strategy

Casino Hold'em strategy is remarkably simple compared to its Texas Hold'em poker counterpart. With optimal play, the correct action on the flop is to call in approximately 82% of situations — fold only the weakest possible holdings.

As a general guide:

  • Always call if you have any pair (even a low pair on the board).
  • Always call with four to a flush or four to a straight.
  • Always call with overcards (two cards higher than the board) when the board is unpaired.
  • Consider folding only when you have no pair, no draw, and your hole cards are both below the board cards with no hope of improvement.

The house edge under optimal strategy is approximately 2.16%. Folding too often is by far the most expensive mistake a player can make.

The AA Bonus Side Bet

The AA Bonus is a popular side bet that pays if your two hole cards combined with the three flop cards contain a pair of Aces or better. It is entirely independent of the main game outcome. The pay table (at a typical Evolution Gaming live Casino Hold'em table) includes:

  • Royal Flush: 100:1
  • Straight Flush: 50:1
  • Four of a Kind: 40:1
  • Full House: 12:1
  • Flush: 8:1
  • Straight: 7:1
  • Three of a Kind: 4:1
  • Two Pair: 3:1
  • Pair of Aces: 7:2

The house edge on the AA Bonus typically falls between 6–9%. Place it only if you enjoy the extra excitement; it is not part of an optimal strategy for minimising losses over time.
