Bank Transfer Casinos the UK

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Bank transfers remain one of the most widely available payment methods at UK online casinos — every major casino supports them. They're not the fastest option, but they carry no card details to expose, support high transaction limits, and work with every UK bank account including AIB, Bank of the UK, Permanent TSB, and Ulster Bank.

How Bank Transfers Work at Online Casinos

When you choose bank transfer in a casino cashier, you'll receive the casino's IBAN and BIC details. You then initiate the payment from your own online banking app or branch. SEPA Credit Transfers — the standard for pound payments across the UK and the eurozone — typically settle within one business day, though casino processing adds extra time on each end.

For withdrawals, the process reverses: you request a payout in the casino cashier, provide your IBAN, and the casino initiates the transfer to your account. The casino's own processing time (1–3 days) plus the interbank transfer (1–2 days) means most bank transfer withdrawals take 2–5 business days in total.

Deposit and Withdrawal Limits

Bank transfers support the highest transaction limits of any payment method at UK casinos. Typical minimums are £10–20 for deposits and £20–50 for withdrawals. Upper limits can reach £50,000 per transaction or more for verified accounts. This makes bank transfers the preferred option for high-value withdrawals that would exceed card or e-wallet limits.

FAQ — Bank Transfer Casinos the UK

Can I deposit at an UK online casino by bank transfer?▾
Yes, most UK-facing online casinos accept bank transfers for deposits. SEPA transfers from AIB, Bank of the UK, Permanent TSB and other UK banks are widely supported. Some casinos also support Faster Payments for UK-linked accounts.
How long does a bank transfer withdrawal take at an UK casino?▾
Bank transfer withdrawals typically take 2–5 business days once the casino has processed the payment. The casino's own processing time (usually 1–3 days) plus interbank transfer time (1–2 days) make up the total. Some casinos using Trustly or open-banking rails can deliver funds in under 24 hours.
Are bank transfer withdrawals free at online casinos?▾
Most casinos do not charge a fee for bank transfer withdrawals, though some apply a small fee (typically £1–3) for SEPA transfers. Always check the cashier's fee schedule before requesting a withdrawal.
What are the minimum and maximum limits for bank transfers at casinos?▾
Minimums for bank transfer deposits are usually £10–20. Withdrawal minimums are often £20–50. Maximum single withdrawals can range from £5,000 to £50,000 per transaction. VIP players often receive higher or unlimited limits.
Is bank transfer safer than card payments at online casinos?▾
Bank transfers are considered very secure because your card details are never shared with the casino. The main drawback is speed — transfers take days rather than seconds. For the safest and fastest experience, many UK players use open-banking services built on the same infrastructure as bank transfers but with near-instant settlement.
