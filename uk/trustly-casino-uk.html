Trustly has become one of the most popular payment methods at UK-facing online casinos. Unlike a debit card, it connects directly to your bank account via Open Banking — so there's no card number to share and no separate e-wallet to fund. This guide explains exactly how it works, which banks are supported, and what to watch for with bonuses.

What Is Trustly?

Trustly is a Swedish fintech company licensed as a payment institution across the EU. It uses Open Banking (PSD2) to let you authorise payments directly from your bank account. When you select Trustly at a casino cashier, you're redirected to your own bank's secure login — the casino sees only that the payment was authorised, never your login details.

Trustly operates in the UK under EU financial regulation and is supervised by the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finansinspektionen).

How to Deposit at a Casino Using Trustly

  1. Go to the casino cashier and select Trustly (sometimes listed as "Bank Transfer" or "Open Banking")
  2. Enter your deposit amount
  3. Select your UK bank from the list (AIB, Bank of the UK, PTSB, Ulster Bank)
  4. Log in to your bank using your normal online banking credentials
  5. Approve the payment — funds appear in your casino account instantly

Trustly and Casino Bonuses — The Catch

This is where many UK players get caught out. A significant number of online casinos exclude bank transfer deposits from welcome bonuses. Trustly is classified as a bank transfer at some casinos, meaning your first deposit may not trigger the 100% match offer.

Before depositing with Trustly, check the bonus terms for a line like: "Deposits via bank transfer, Trustly, or Skrill do not qualify for the welcome bonus." If you want the bonus, use your Visa or Mastercard debit card for the first deposit, then switch to Trustly for subsequent deposits.

Is Trustly Safe for UK Casino Players?

Yes, for two reasons. First, Trustly is regulated under EU financial law — it does not hold your bank credentials, only passes your authorisation. Second, you never give the casino your card number, so there is no card data to be compromised in a data breach. Your bank's own security (including 2FA) protects every transaction.

From a gambling regulation perspective, Trustly transactions leave a clear paper trail in your bank statement, which makes it easier to track spending and set budgets.

Trustly Withdrawal Times at UK Casinos

Trustly withdrawals go directly to your bank account. Typical timelines:

  • Casino processing time: 0–24 hours (faster at casinos with instant withdrawal)
  • Trustly to bank: 1–3 business days
  • Total: usually 1–4 days

Some casinos offer Pay N Play via Trustly — a feature that handles registration and withdrawal in one step. This can make same-day cash-out possible, but it is not widely available in the UK market yet.
