Pay by Phone Casinos the UK

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

Pay by phone lets UK players deposit at online casinos without entering bank details or card numbers — the deposit is billed to your mobile account instead. It's a quick, private option for small deposits, though it comes with daily limits and does not support withdrawals.

How Pay by Phone Deposits Work

The process is straightforward. In the casino cashier, select "Pay by Phone" or "Mobile Billing" and enter your UK mobile number. You'll receive an SMS with a confirmation code. Enter it, and the deposit amount is charged to your phone bill (postpaid contract) or deducted from your prepaid top-up balance.

Services like Boku and Zimpler power most pay by phone casino deposits. They act as an intermediary between the casino and your mobile network, handling the billing instruction. The deposit lands in your casino account instantly once confirmed.

Deposit Limits — What to Expect

Pay by phone carries the lowest deposit limits of any casino payment method. Daily limits are typically £10–30, set by your mobile network operator rather than the casino. These limits exist because the charge appears on a phone bill rather than a dedicated payment account, so networks cap exposure to protect customers.

📲 Typical Pay by Phone Limits at UK Casinos

  • Minimum deposit: £5–10
  • Maximum single deposit: £10–30
  • Daily limit: £30 (most networks)
  • Withdrawals: Not available

Privacy Benefits

The main appeal of pay by phone is privacy. No bank account number, card number, or sort code is shared with the casino — only your mobile number. For players who prefer to keep gambling transactions off their bank statement, pay by phone deposits appear as a third-party billing item on the phone bill rather than a direct casino transaction.

This also makes it accessible to players who don't have or don't want to use a bank account or debit card for gambling. Prepaid SIM users on networks like Eir, Three, or Vodafone can deposit from their top-up balance with no bank account needed.

The Withdrawal Problem — and How to Solve It

Pay by phone is deposit-only. You cannot withdraw winnings back to your phone bill. Before depositing via mobile billing, register a withdrawal method — a debit card or bank transfer IBAN — in the casino cashier. This ensures you can access any winnings without delay.

FAQ — Pay by Phone Casinos the UK

How does pay by phone work at an online casino?▾
Pay by phone lets you fund your casino account by charging the deposit to your mobile phone bill (postpaid) or deducting it from your prepaid credit. You enter your mobile number at the casino cashier, receive a confirmation text, and the amount is added to your next bill or deducted from your top-up balance.
Can I withdraw using pay by phone at an UK casino?▾
No. Pay by phone services do not support withdrawals. Winnings must be cashed out via a different method — typically a bank transfer, e-wallet, or debit card. You'll need to register a withdrawal method before requesting a payout.
What is the daily deposit limit for pay by phone at casinos?▾
Daily deposit limits for pay by phone are typically £10–30. Mobile network operators set these limits to control billing exposure. Some premium accounts may have slightly higher limits, but pay by phone is not suitable for large deposits.
Does pay by phone work with UK mobile networks?▾
Yes. Eir, Three, Vodafone and other UK mobile operators support pay by phone casino deposits via services like Boku. Availability varies by casino and by the specific network. Check the casino cashier to confirm your network is supported.
Is pay by phone good for responsible gambling?▾
Pay by phone has built-in spending controls — daily limits are enforced by the mobile network, not just the casino. This makes it a naturally low-risk deposit method. However, because withdrawals go to a different account, players should still register a separate payout method before depositing.
