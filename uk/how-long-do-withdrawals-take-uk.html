How Long Do Betting Withdrawals Take in the UK?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Withdrawal speeds at UKGC-licensed bookmakers vary by payment method. This guide covers every route available to UK punters, what UKGC rules require, and exactly what to do if your withdrawal is delayed.

Withdrawal Times by Payment Method

Under UKGC licence conditions, operators must process withdrawal requests promptly. In practice, times depend on the payment rail:

  • E-wallets (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller): Same day in most cases, often within 1–2 hours once approved.
  • Revolut: Treated as a debit card; typically 1–2 business days.
  • Debit card (Visa/Mastercard): 1–5 business days depending on your bank's processing time.
  • Bank transfer: 1–3 business days once the operator dispatches the payment.

These are operator-to-bank timelines. Your bank may add an extra day for incoming transfers, particularly at weekends.

What UKGC Rules Say About Withdrawal Delays

UKGC-licensed operators (licence format: UKGC-XXXX-RB-26-XXXX) must pay out winnings as a condition of their licence — this is a legal obligation, not a courtesy. Operators cannot withhold a withdrawal without a valid reason: active KYC review, unmet bonus wagering requirements, or a fraud investigation. If an operator refuses a withdrawal without explanation, that is a potential licence breach you can escalate to the UKGC.

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, the UKGC can investigate complaints about withdrawal refusals. First go through the operator's internal complaints process, then escalate to the UKGC at grai.ie if unresolved within 8 weeks (or sooner if the operator fails to respond).

Why Your Withdrawal Might Be Delayed

The most common causes of a delayed withdrawal are: identity verification (KYC) outstanding, bonus wagering not yet completed, using a different payment method than you deposited with (the "same method rule"), or a large amount triggering an enhanced security check. Check your account notifications first — most operators flag the specific reason.

If your KYC is the hold-up, uploading a clear photo of your passport or driving licence and a utility bill dated within 3 months usually resolves it within 24–48 hours.

The Same-Method Rule Explained

Most UKGC-licensed bookmakers apply a same-method withdrawal policy: if you deposited by debit card, you must withdraw to the same card first. This is an anti-money-laundering requirement, not a bookmaker invention. Once your deposit amount has been returned to the original card, any excess can usually go to a bank account.

How long do betting withdrawals take in the UK?

E-wallets are typically same-day; Revolut and debit cards take 1–3 business days; bank transfers 1–3 business days once dispatched. Total time varies by operator and your bank.

Can a UKGC-licensed bookmaker refuse to pay out?

No. Paying out winnings is a UKGC licence condition. An operator can delay while verifying your identity or checking bonus compliance, but an outright refusal without cause is a licence breach you can report to the UKGC.

What do I do if my withdrawal is stuck?

Check your account for KYC or bonus requirements. If none apply, contact support. If unresolved in 8 weeks, escalate to the UKGC at grai.ie. Our guide on withdrawal declines has the step-by-step process.

Do UK betting sites charge withdrawal fees?

Most UKGC-licensed sites offer at least one free withdrawal method. Some charge for bank transfers or expedited processing. Check the cashier section before choosing your withdrawal method.

Does the same-method rule apply to all UK bookmakers?

Most apply it for anti-money-laundering compliance. Your deposit amount must typically return to the original funding source before excess funds can go elsewhere.
