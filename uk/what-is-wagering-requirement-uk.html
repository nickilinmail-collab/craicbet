What Is a Wagering Requirement? Explained for UK Punters

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A wagering requirement (also called a rollover or playthrough requirement) is the number of times you must bet through a bonus before you can withdraw the resulting funds as cash. It is the most important number to check on any bonus offer. Here is a plain-English explanation with real calculations.

How Wagering Requirements Work

When you receive a bonus with a wagering requirement attached, the bonus funds (or the bonus winnings, depending on the offer type) must be bet a specified number of times before they become “real” withdrawable cash. The requirement is expressed as a multiplier: 5x, 20x, 40x, etc.

Formula: Total wagering required = Bonus amount × Wagering multiplier

  • £50 bonus × 5x = £250 total bets required
  • £50 bonus × 20x = £1,000 total bets required
  • £50 bonus × 40x = £2,000 total bets required

The Real Cost of Higher Wagering Requirements

Every time you place a bet, the operator has an edge (the margin). On football, this is typically 4–6%. On slots, it can be 3–10% or more. The higher the wagering requirement and the higher the margin, the more of your bonus you are expected to lose clearing it.

Qualified Contributions: Not All Bets Count Equally

Many bonuses specify that bets on certain markets contribute less than 100% to wagering. For example, football accumulator bets might count 100% but each-way horse racing bets might count 50%. Casino slot play might count 100% but live casino bets count 10%. Check the contributions table in the bonus T&Cs.

UKGC Requirements for Transparency

Under UKGC inducements regulations, wagering requirements must be displayed clearly alongside any bonus headline figure. “Hidden” wagering requirements buried in terms pages are a potential compliance breach you can report to the UKGC at grai.ie.

What is a wagering requirement on a betting bonus?

The number of times you must bet through the bonus amount before winnings are withdrawable. A 5x wagering on a £50 bonus means placing £250 in total bets that qualify before the bonus converts to cash.

Is 5x wagering good for a sports betting bonus in the UK?

5x is considered low and very achievable. The expected cost of clearing it at typical sports margins is £12–£15 on a £50 bonus. Bonuses above 20x for sports are poor value.

What is the worst wagering requirement to look out for?

Casino bonuses with 35–40x wagering played primarily on slots. At typical slot margins, the expected losses to clear the wagering exceed the bonus value — these bonuses have negative expected value.

Do all bets count towards wagering requirements?

Not always. Many bonuses have contribution rates: sports bets typically 100%, live casino bets often 10–20%, some markets excluded. Check the contributions table in the bonus T&Cs.

Are wagering requirements legal in the UK?

Yes — bonus wagering requirements are legal. UKGC rules require them to be clearly disclosed. The UKGC does not cap wagering requirements but enforces transparency obligations.
