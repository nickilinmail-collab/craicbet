Cluster Pays Explained: Push Gaming's Core Mechanic

Traditional slots pay on fixed paylines (left-to-right symbol matches). Push Gaming's cluster mechanic works differently:

  • Groups of 5+ matching symbols anywhere on the grid trigger a win — position relative to other symbols matters, not reel position.
  • Cascading wins: After a win, symbols disappear and new ones drop in. This can chain multiple wins from a single initial cluster.
  • Super wilds (Jammin' Jars): Wild symbols fly around the grid and multiply wins as they land. The chaotic movement creates large variance — many sessions will produce nothing, with occasional massive chains.
  • Bankroll management: High-variance cluster pays require a session bankroll of 100–200× your stake to withstand the variance between wins.
