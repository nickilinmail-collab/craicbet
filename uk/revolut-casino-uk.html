Revolut Casino the UK: Does It Work? [2026 Guide]

✏️ By Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

💡 Quick Answer

Revolut works at most UK-facing casinos — but you must enable gambling payments in the app first: Security → Payment controls → Gambling → toggle ON. Some casinos block Revolut because it is classified as an e-money institution rather than a bank. If declined, use your AIB or Bank of the UK debit card instead.

Revolut has over 3.5 million users in the UK, making it the country's most widely used fintech app. Naturally, many UK casino players try to use it for deposits and withdrawals. Here is exactly what works, what does not, and how to avoid your card being declined.

Does Revolut Work at Online Casinos in the UK?

In most cases, yes. Revolut issues Visa and Mastercard debit cards, which are accepted at the vast majority of online casinos serving UK players. However, two things can cause a failed transaction:

  1. Gambling blocked in-app — Revolut has an internal gambling toggle that may be off by default on some accounts
  2. Casino rejects e-money institutions — Some operators' payment terms require a traditional bank debit card for KYC compliance

How to Enable Gambling on Revolut (Step by Step)

  1. Open the Revolut app on your phone
  2. Tap Security (padlock icon) in the top right
  3. Select Payment controls
  4. Find Gambling and toggle it ON
  5. Confirm your PIN or biometrics if prompted

This takes under 60 seconds and works immediately. If a deposit still fails after enabling gambling, the casino itself is blocking Revolut — not your app settings.

Why Some UK Casinos Block Revolut

Revolut is regulated as an e-money institution by the Central Bank of the UK (not as a traditional bank). Some casino operators' anti-money-laundering (AML) policies require a traditional bank account for KYC purposes — particularly operators that need to verify the source of funds easily. When a casino declines Revolut, it is usually this reason, not your individual account.

Operators most likely to decline Revolut are those requiring same-name bank account withdrawals or those processing under stricter jurisdictions. If this happens, your AIB, Bank of the UK, or permanent tsb debit card will almost always work instead.

Revolut's Gambling Limit Feature — Responsible Gambling Tool

One genuine advantage of using Revolut for casino play: the in-app gambling spend limit. Under Payment controls, you can set a monthly cap on gambling transactions. Once you hit the limit, further gambling payments are automatically declined until the next calendar month.

This is separate from — and in addition to — the deposit limits that UKGC-supervised operators are required to offer UK players. Using both gives you two independent layers of financial control.

Revolut Withdrawals from UK Casinos

Most casinos process withdrawals to the same card used for the deposit. Revolut Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted for withdrawals at the majority of operators. Typical processing times:

  • Casino processing: 0–48 hours (faster at licenced operators)
  • Visa debit to Revolut: 1–3 business days
  • Mastercard debit to Revolut: 1–5 business days

Revolut shows pending transactions in real time, so you will see the incoming transfer before it fully clears.
