GamStop Guide 2026: UK Self-Exclusion Explained

Last updated: 6 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read
🛡️ If you need help now: National Gambling Helpline — 0808 8020 133 (free, 24 hours, 7 days) • begambleaware.org

GamStop is the UK's national online self-exclusion scheme, operated independently and free to use. When you register, you are blocked from all UKGC-licensed gambling websites and apps for the period you choose. All UK-licensed operators are legally required to participate. This guide explains how it works, how to register and what happens after you do.

What GamStop Does

GamStop creates a central exclusion that applies automatically across every UKGC-licensed gambling operator in the UK. You register once at gamstop.co.uk and your exclusion is applied across all participating sites. You do not need to contact each operator individually.

GamStop covers online gambling only — sports betting sites, online casinos, poker rooms, bingo sites and apps. It does not cover physical betting shops, land-based casinos or lottery tickets. For land-based exclusion, contact the operator directly or visit your local council gambling support service.

How to Register with GamStop

  1. Go to gamstop.co.uk
  2. Enter your name, date of birth, email address, phone number and postal address
  3. Choose your exclusion period: 6 months, 1 year or 5 years
  4. Confirm your registration — the exclusion takes effect within 24 hours

Registration is free. GamStop is a not-for-profit organisation funded by the gambling industry. Your data is shared only with UKGC-licensed operators for the purpose of enforcing the exclusion.

Exclusion Periods

Period Can it be shortened? What happens after?
6 months No You must actively opt back in
1 year No You must actively opt back in
5 years No You must actively opt back in

Once your exclusion period ends, it does not automatically reverse. You need to contact GamStop to lift the exclusion, and even then there is a 7-day cooling off period before it takes effect. This is intentional — it gives you time to reconsider.

What GamStop Does Not Cover

  • Physical betting shops (William Hill, Ladbrokes, Coral, Paddy Power high street)
  • Land-based casinos
  • Lottery tickets (National Lottery, scratchcards)
  • Gambling sites without a UKGC licence (offshore operators) — this is an important gap; unlicensed sites are not required to participate

GamStop vs GRAI Self-Exclusion (Ireland)

Ireland does not yet have a GamStop equivalent. The GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) is working on a national self-exclusion register, but it was not live as of November 2026. Irish punters wanting self-exclusion need to contact each GRAI-licensed operator individually. See our responsible gambling guide for Ireland-specific resources.

Other UK Support Services

  • National Gambling Helpline: 0808 8020 133 (free, 24/7, run by GamCare)
  • GamCare: gamcare.org.uk — counselling, online chat, forum
  • Gamblers Anonymous UK: gamblersanonymous.org.uk — peer support groups
  • BeGambleAware: begambleaware.org — advice and treatment finder
