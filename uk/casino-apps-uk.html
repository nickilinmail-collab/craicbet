Frequently Asked Questions — Casino Apps UK

Do casino apps in the UK need a UKGC licence?

Yes. Any casino app that accepts real-money bets from UK players must hold a valid remote operating licence from the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC). Operating without one while targeting UK players is illegal. Check any licence at gamblingcommission.gov.uk.

Can I use Apple Pay on casino apps in the UK?

Yes, but not at every UKGC-licensed app. Apple Pay is supported for deposits at a growing number of UK mobile casinos — look for it in the cashier section. Withdrawals to Apple Pay are less common; most casinos pay out to your linked debit card instead.

How do I self-exclude from casino apps in the UK?

Register with GamStop at gamstop.co.uk — this blocks all UKGC-licensed casino apps simultaneously for 6 months, 1 year, or 5 years. The exclusion cannot be shortened once set. See our GamStop Guide for full details.

Is there a stake limit on UK casino apps?

Yes. All UKGC-licensed casino apps must cap online slot stakes at £5 per spin. This applies to every standard slot game. Live casino table games and sports betting are not affected by this limit.

Are casino apps free to download in the UK?

Yes, always. UKGC-licensed casino apps are always free to download from the App Store or Google Play. You are never charged to install an app — the only money involved is what you choose to deposit and wager.
