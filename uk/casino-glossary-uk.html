Casino Glossary A–Z

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 31 November 2026

Every term you will meet in our reviews and in casino T&Cs, explained in plain English. 44 entries, A–Z.

BCDEFGHJKLMPRSTVW

B

Bankroll
The total money a player sets aside for gambling. Good bankroll management means betting a small, fixed share of it per session.
Bet Level
In slots, a multiplier of the base coin value; raising it raises the total stake per spin.
Bonus Buy
A feature letting you pay (typically 100x stake) to trigger the bonus round instantly. Banned in some regulated markets.

C

Cashback
A bonus returning a percentage of losses over a period. Check whether it carries wagering requirements.
Cluster Pays
A win mechanic where matching symbols pay by forming adjacent groups instead of landing on paylines.
Comp Points
Loyalty points earned from real-money play, convertible into bonus funds or prizes.

D

Demo Mode
A free-play version of a game using virtual credits. Identical maths to the real game.
Deposit Match
A bonus matching a percentage of your deposit, e.g. 100% up to £200. Always read the wagering terms.

E

Exclusion Register
the UK's national database letting a person block themselves from every UKGC-licensed operator in one request.
Expanding Symbol
A symbol that stretches to cover its whole reel, used in book-style slots' free spins.

F

Free Spins
Bonus rounds played without staking your own money. Winnings often carry wagering requirements.

G

Gamble Feature
An optional double-or-nothing offered after a win. The expected value is at best neutral.
GambleAware / GambleAware
UK support organisations for gambling harm. Freephone 0808 8020 133.
UKGC
The UK Gambling Commission, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Issues licences and maintains the public register at grai.ie.

H

House Edge
The casino's built-in mathematical advantage: 100% minus RTP.

J

Jackpot (Fixed)
A top prize of a set amount, unlike a progressive which grows with play.
Jackpot (Progressive)
A prize pool fed by a share of every bet across a network, growing until won.

K

KYC
Know Your Customer: identity verification (ID, address) required before withdrawals at licensed operators.

L

Live Casino
Games with real human dealers streamed in real time: blackjack, roulette, game shows.

M

Max Win
The cap on what a slot can pay, expressed as a multiple of stake, e.g. 5,000x.
Megaways
A licensed engine where reel heights change every spin, giving up to six-figure numbers of ways to win.
MGA
Malta Gaming Authority: the offshore regulator licensing many casinos serving UK players.
Min Deposit
The smallest amount a casino accepts; bonuses usually require a higher qualifying deposit.
Multiplier
A factor applied to a win. In tumble slots multipliers often accumulate during free spins.

P

Payline
A fixed pattern across the reels on which matching symbols pay.
Payout Percentage
See RTP.
Pending Time
The window between requesting a withdrawal and the casino approving it: the main speed difference between casinos.
Provably Fair
A crypto-casino mechanism letting players cryptographically verify each result.

R

Reality Check
A responsible-gambling tool showing how long you have been playing at set intervals.
Remote Betting Licence
The UKGC licence category live since July 2026 covering online bookmakers. Does not cover casino games.
Remote Gaming Licence
The future UKGC category that will cover online casinos. None issued yet.
Respin
A feature re-spinning some reels while others hold, often with locked symbols.
RNG
Random Number Generator: certified software ensuring each outcome is independent and random.
RTP
Return to Player: the theoretical percentage of stakes a game returns over millions of spins. Casinos may run reduced-RTP versions — check the in-game info panel.

S

Scatter
A symbol that pays or triggers features regardless of position, usually the key to free spins.
Self-Exclusion
Blocking yourself from gambling: per-site, via the national register, or with device software like Gamban.
Sticky Wild
A wild symbol that stays in place for subsequent spins.

T

Tumble / Cascade
Winning symbols vanish and new ones fall in, allowing chains of wins from one spin.

V

Variance
See Volatility.
Volatility
How wins are distributed: low = frequent small wins; high = rarer but larger. Choose to match your bankroll.

W

Wagering Requirement
How many times a bonus must be bet before withdrawal, e.g. 35x. The single most important bonus term.
Welcome Bonus
The offer for new players: deposit match, free spins, or both. Compare by wagering terms, not headline size.
Wild
A symbol substituting for others to complete wins; variants include expanding, sticky and multiplier wilds.
Withdrawal Limit
The maximum you can cash out per day/week/month. Critical if you hit a large win.
