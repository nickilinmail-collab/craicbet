Casino Reload Bonuses in the UK

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

A reload bonus is a deposit-match offer for existing casino players. Unlike a welcome bonus — which you can only claim once — a reload repeats: weekly, fortnightly, or monthly, depending on the casino. The structure is simpler than the marketing suggests, and the honest maths is worth understanding before you opt in.

This guide explains what reload bonuses are, how they compare to welcome offers, and how to calculate whether a specific reload is worth claiming.

What Is a Reload Bonus?

A reload bonus tops up a subsequent deposit with extra funds. The most common structure is a percentage match up to a fixed ceiling — for example, 50% up to £100. Deposit £200 and the casino credits your account with an additional £100 in bonus funds, giving you £300 to play with (£200 of your own money plus £100 bonus).

The match percentage on reloads is typically lower than on welcome bonuses. Where a welcome offer might be 100% (doubling your deposit), a reload is more usually 25%–50%. The trade-off is that the reload comes back every week or month, so a loyal player can capture significant value over time even from a modest percentage.

Some casinos tie the reload to a specific day — Friday is common — or to a promotion window (e.g. the first deposit of each calendar month). Others are on-demand via the promotions page whenever you need a boost.

Wagering Requirements on Reload Bonuses

Reload bonuses carry wagering requirements just like welcome bonuses. The requirement is typically expressed as a multiple of the bonus amount only (not deposit + bonus), which is more player-friendly than some welcome offers.

🧮 Worked Example: 50% Reload, 30x Wagering

Deposit amount: £200
Reload bonus (50%): £100
Wagering required (30x bonus): £3,000
Expected loss at 96% RTP slots: £3,000 × 4% = £120
Net expected value of bonus: £100 − £120 = −£20

At 30x wagering and 96% RTP, the reload has a slightly negative expected value on average. At 20x wagering the same bonus turns positive (+£20). The lower the wagering requirement, the better the deal.

One additional variable: does wagering apply to the bonus only, or to deposit + bonus? The latter doubles the effective requirement. Always check the small print. “30x the bonus” on a £100 bonus = £3,000 total bets. “30x (deposit + bonus)” on a £200 deposit + £100 bonus = £9,000 total bets — a very different proposition.

Weekly vs Monthly Reloads

Weekly reloads (most common on a Friday or weekend) tend to have smaller ceilings — £50–£100 — but arrive frequently. If you play regularly, the cumulative bonus across 52 weeks can easily exceed the value of a single welcome offer.

Monthly reloads often have larger caps (£200–£500) and are sometimes tied to a minimum deposit or activity threshold (e.g. you must have wagered £500 during the month to qualify). Read the conditions carefully.

On-demand reloads require you to opt in via the promotions page each time. The casino won’t remind you — it’s worth bookmarking the promo page and checking before every deposit.

How to Find Reload Bonuses

Reload bonuses are rarely as prominently advertised as welcome offers. Look in: the casino’s promotions or offers page (usually under “Existing Players”); your registered email (opt into marketing for reload alerts); the casino’s mobile app notifications; and in some cases via a dedicated VIP or loyalty programme page.

Always confirm the bonus is active before you deposit, not after. Most casinos will not credit a reload retrospectively if you forgot to enter the code or opt in first.

FAQ — Reload Bonuses the UK

What is a casino reload bonus?▾
A reload bonus is a deposit-match offer for existing players, typically 25%–50% up to a set amount (e.g. 50% up to £100). Unlike a welcome bonus, reload bonuses repeat — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly — rewarding players who continue depositing.
Are reload bonuses worth claiming?▾
It depends on the wagering requirement. A 50% reload at 30x wagering can be worthwhile; at 50x or above the expected value turns negative on most slot RTPs. Always run the maths: multiply wagering by the game’s house edge and compare that cost to the bonus amount.
How does a reload bonus differ from a welcome bonus?▾
A welcome bonus is a one-time offer for new accounts, usually larger (100%+ match). A reload bonus is for existing players on subsequent deposits, typically a smaller match (25%–50%) but recurring — so the cumulative value over months can rival a welcome offer.
Do reload bonuses require a bonus code in the UK?▾
Many do, yes. Some casinos credit the reload automatically; others need you to enter a code at the deposit screen or opt in through the promotions page. Always check before depositing, as a missed code means no bonus.
Can I withdraw a reload bonus straight away?▾
No. Reload bonuses carry wagering requirements, typically 25x–40x the bonus amount. You must wager the total before any winnings derived from the bonus become withdrawable. Your own deposited funds usually remain withdrawable at any time, though the bonus would be forfeited.
