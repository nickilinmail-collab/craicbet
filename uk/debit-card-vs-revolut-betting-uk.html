Debit Card vs Revolut for Betting the UK 2026: Speed, Fees and Acceptance

Last updated: 5 November 2026

With credit cards banned under UKGC regulations, debit cards and Revolut have become the two dominant deposit methods for UK punters. This guide compares both on speed, withdrawal processing, operator acceptance and any practical caveats.

Withdrawal Speed: Where Revolut Wins Clearly

Revolut withdrawals at most UKGC-licensed operators process same-day or overnight, compared to 1–2 business days for debit card withdrawals (which go through the card processor). If you want your winnings back quickly, Revolut is the faster route. This is the primary practical advantage of Revolut over debit card for active punters.

Revolut’s Gambling Blocks

Revolut offers a built-in gambling block that prevents the card being used for gambling transactions. This is entirely voluntary and controlled by the user in the Revolut app under “Spending controls.” It is a useful responsible gambling tool: if you want to restrict your own access to betting sites, the Revolut gambling block is effective and instant. UKGC-licensed operators also offer deposit limits and self-exclusion independently.

Potential Revolut Issues

A minority of UKGC-licensed operators have at times restricted Revolut deposits. This typically reflects a commercial payment processing decision by the operator, not a Revolut policy. If a Revolut deposit is declined at an operator, a standard debit card will work as a fallback. Always check accepted payment methods before depositing significant sums.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Revolut on UK betting sites?

Yes. Revolut is accepted at most UKGC-licensed operators for deposits and withdrawals. It is not classified as a credit card, so the UKGC credit card ban does not apply.

Is Revolut faster for withdrawals than a debit card?

Yes. Revolut withdrawals at most UKGC-licensed operators process same-day. Debit card withdrawals typically take 1–2 business days.

Can I block gambling on Revolut?

Yes. Revolut has a built-in gambling block under ‘Spending controls’ in the app. When enabled, Revolut transactions to gambling merchants are declined. It is voluntary and reversible by the user.

Are credit cards banned for betting in the UK?

Yes. UKGC regulations ban credit card deposits at all licensed gambling sites from July 2026. Debit cards, Revolut, PayPal and bank transfer are the permitted alternatives.

Does every bookmaker accept Revolut?

Most major UKGC-licensed operators accept Revolut. A small number have restrictions. Check the operator’s payment page before depositing.
