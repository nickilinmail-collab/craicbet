Last updated: 5 November 2026

Pay N Play casinos allow you to deposit and play without creating a traditional account. Identity verification is handled instantly via your bank login. Available in the UK, though with fewer operators than the Nordic markets where it originated.

How Pay N Play Works

Instead of a registration form, you deposit via your bank (using Open Banking or Trustly). The bank login verifies your identity — the casino receives your name, address, and account details directly. No separate KYC documentation is required, and you can start playing in under a minute.

Pay N Play in the UK

Pay N Play (via Trustly or similar) is well established in Sweden and Finland. in the UK, Open Banking integration at casinos is growing but not yet universal. The number of UK-facing casinos offering genuine no-registration play has increased since 2024, though it remains a niche offering compared to traditional registration casinos.

Instant Withdrawals

The key advantage of Pay N Play beyond easy sign-up: withdrawals go back to the same bank account instantly. Because the casino already knows your bank details from the deposit, payouts don't require separate verification. This is the fastest withdrawal method available at participating casinos.

Which UK Banks Support Pay N Play?

AIB, Bank of the UK, and PTSB generally support Open Banking connections. Revolut and N26 support varies by operator. Check the specific casino's supported bank list during the deposit process.

Limitations of Pay N Play

  • Fewer casinos offer it compared to traditional accounts
  • Smaller game libraries in some cases
  • Bonuses may be less generous (some operators don't offer sign-up bonuses to no-account players)
  • Customer support history is harder without an account
