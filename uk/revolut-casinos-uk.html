Revolut Gambling Block: How to Turn It On or Off

Revolut lets you control gambling transactions directly in the app. If your casino deposits are being declined, check this first:

  3. Toggle it on (to allow) or off (to block)

If the gambling toggle is already on and your deposit is still declined, the issue is likely on the casino's side (MCC code not recognised) or a temporary 3D Secure issue. Try re-initiating the deposit or contact Revolut in-app chat for a transaction decline reason code.
