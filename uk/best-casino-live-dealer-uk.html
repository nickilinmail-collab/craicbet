Live dealer games bridge the gap between online and land-based gambling. A good live casino offers multiple roulette and blackjack variants, baccarat, poker games, and game shows — all streamed from purpose-built studios in HD quality.

What to Look for in a Live Casino

The key factors: number of live tables (100+ is good, 150+ is excellent), software providers (Evolution is the gold standard), table limits suitable for your budget, mobile streaming quality, and whether UK bank hours match peak dealer availability.

Table Limits for UK players

Most live casinos offer a wide range: roulette from £0.10 to £10,000 per spin, blackjack from £1 to £5,000 per hand. VIP tables push limits higher. If you're a high-stakes player, verify before depositing that the limits you need are available during your playing hours.

Mobile Live Casino

Live streams are data-heavy. On a strong 4G or WiFi connection, most modern live casino platforms stream smoothly at 720p or 1080p on iOS and Android. The leading providers (Evolution, Pragmatic Play Live) have optimised their mobile players significantly since 2024.

Bonuses and Live Casino: The Catch

Many welcome bonuses explicitly exclude live casino games from wagering contributions. A bonus that contributes 0% from live tables is effectively useless for live casino players. Always check the bonus T&Cs before claiming.
