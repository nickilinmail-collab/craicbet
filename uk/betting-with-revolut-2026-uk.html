Betting with Revolut in the UK 2026: Full Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Revolut is the UK’s most widely used digital banking app and is accepted at virtually all UKGC-licensed betting sites for both deposits and withdrawals. This guide covers everything you need to know about using Revolut for betting in the UK under the 2026 UKGC framework.

Why Revolut Works Well for Betting

Revolut is classified as an e-money or prepaid card product rather than a credit card, which means it is fully compliant with the UKGC’s ban on credit card gambling deposits. Revolut deposits at UKGC-licensed sites are instant, which is one of the main reasons UK punters prefer it. Withdrawals back to Revolut typically take one to three business days at most operators.

Setting Up Revolut for Betting

  1. Download the Revolut app and complete identity verification (required for full account access)
  2. Add funds to your Revolut account via bank transfer or debit card top-up
  3. At your chosen UKGC-licensed bookmaker, select Revolut or Visa/Mastercard as your payment method
  4. Enter your Revolut card details and complete the deposit
  5. Revolut will send an in-app notification for each transaction

Revolut Gambling Controls

Revolut offers a built-in gambling block feature in its app that prevents any transactions to gambling merchants. This is a useful tool for anyone who wants to manage their gambling spend. The block can be switched on and off in the Revolut settings. Using Revolut’s gambling block is entirely separate from the deposit limit tools at UKGC-licensed bookmakers — you may choose to use both.

Withdrawal Speed with Revolut

Withdrawals from UKGC-licensed bookmakers back to a Revolut account are processed in one to three business days at most operators. Some operators offer same-day e-wallet withdrawals if Revolut is processed as a digital wallet rather than a card. Check your chosen operator’s payment terms for exact withdrawal timelines.

Revolut Gambling Block: How to Enable and Disable It

Revolut's gambling block is one of the most useful responsible gambling tools available to UK bettors — it prevents any transactions to gambling merchants without you having to contact your bank. To toggle it:

  • Open Revolut app → tap your profile icon or the bottom menu
  • Go to Security → scroll to Gambling
  • Toggle Block gambling transactions on or off
  • A short delay (sometimes 24 hours) may apply before gambling deposits work after disabling the block

Revolut Gambling Block How to Turn Off the UK

To re-enable betting deposits via Revolut, open the app, go to your card, tap Spending controls, and toggle the Gambling category back on. If you set a cool-off period when activating the block, Revolut requires you to wait the full period before removal — this is intentional friction. Standard toggle-off (no cool-off) takes effect immediately.

Can I Use Revolut for Online Betting the UK 2026

Yes. Revolut functions as a standard Mastercard Debit at UKGC-licensed betting sites. Deposits are instant. Withdrawals take 2–24 hours typically. Monthly gambling category limits within Revolut itself may apply — check Settings → Spending limits → Gambling if deposits start declining unexpectedly.

How to Enable Gambling on Revolut the UK

To enable gambling on Revolut in the UK: open your Revolut app, tap the card icon, select Settings (cog icon), tap Payment categories, find Gambling & Lotteries and toggle it on. The change is immediate -- no waiting period, unlike high-street UK banks.

This setting controls Revolut card payments only. Revolut bank transfers (GBP SEPA) to e-wallets like Skrill are not affected by the category block. So even if gambling is blocked on your Revolut card, you can transfer funds to Skrill and use Skrill to deposit at betting sites.
