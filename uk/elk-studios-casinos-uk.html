ELK Betting Strategies: What They Are

  • Optimised: Default flat betting. No change to stake based on session history.
  • Levelled: Automatically increases stake by 1 level after a losing streak. Martingale-lite approach — increases variance.
  • Booster: Gradually increases stake as winnings accumulate. Session bankroll management built in.
  • Jumper: Large stake increase after a win. High-risk, high-reward pattern.

Important note: Built-in betting strategies do not change the game's RTP or your long-term expected return — the game is not affected by what you bet on previous spins. These are stake-management tools, not winning systems. The EV (expected value) per pound bet is identical regardless of which strategy you use.
