Revolut Blocked My Betting Deposit in the UK

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | GambleAware — begambleaware.org · National Gambling Helpline: 0808 8020 133 (free, 24/7)

Your Revolut declined a betting deposit and you're not sure why. This is one of the most common questions UK punters search for — here is exactly what's happening and how to fix it.

The most likely cause: Revolut's gambling block

Revolut has a built-in gambling block that you can toggle on or off. When it's on, all transactions to gambling merchants — sports betting sites, online casinos — are declined before the money even leaves your account.

The block may be on because:

  • You turned it on yourself previously and forgot
  • Revolut prompted you after a spending alert and you accidentally enabled it
  • Someone else with access to your account toggled it
  • Revolut auto-enabled it in response to unusual activity (rare)
Fix it now: Revolut app → Settings → Security → Gambling block → toggle Off. Change takes effect immediately.

What if the toggle is greyed out?

When you enable the gambling block, Revolut lets you set a cooling-off period (24 hours to 6 months). During that period, the toggle cannot be switched off — not even by Revolut support. This is a deliberate responsible gambling feature.

Contact Revolut in-app to find out when your cooling-off period expires. You will need to wait until it does.

Other reasons Revolut may decline a betting deposit

Even with the gambling block off, deposits can fail for other reasons:

  • Insufficient funds — check your Revolut balance including any reserved amounts
  • The betting site doesn't accept Revolut — some processors don't support Revolut's card BINs
  • 3D Secure failure — approve the push notification that appears in the Revolut app when a deposit is in progress
  • Card expired or not activated — check your virtual card details in the Revolut app
  • AML hold — if Revolut has flagged your account for a source-of-funds check, outgoing payments may be paused

Your money is safe if Revolut declined the payment

A declined Revolut transaction means the money never left your account. If you see a temporary hold on the funds, it will reverse automatically within minutes to 5 business days depending on the merchant's payment processor.

Alternative payment methods that work reliably

If you can't resolve the Revolut issue quickly, the following alternatives work reliably at UKGC-licensed UK betting sites:

  • Standard Visa/Mastercard debit card from AIB, Bank of the UK, or Permanent TSB
  • Apple Pay or Google Pay linked to a working card — see our Apple Pay betting guide
  • Bank transfer — slower (1–2 days) but always works

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Revolut blocking my betting deposits?
Revolut has a gambling block under Settings → Security. If it is on, all gambling transactions are declined. Toggle it off and the block lifts immediately (unless a cooling-off period is active).
Can I get a refund if Revolut declined a deposit?
No refund is needed — a declined transaction means the money never left your account. Any temporary hold on the funds reverses automatically, usually within minutes.
Does the Bank of the UK gambling block affect my Revolut?
No. The Bank of the UK block applies only to BOI cards. Revolut is a separate institution with its own independent gambling block setting.
Can I withdraw winnings to Revolut even with the gambling block on?
Yes. Revolut's gambling block only blocks outgoing payments to gambling sites. Incoming winnings paid to your Revolut account are not affected.
Does Revolut work for casino deposits in the UK?
Yes, with the same gambling block caveat. Casino and betting deposits both use the same merchant category code, so the block affects both. Toggle it off in Settings → Security to enable both.
