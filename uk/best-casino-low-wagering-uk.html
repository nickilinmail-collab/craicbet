Wagering requirements are the main reason casino bonuses rarely convert to real withdrawable money. Low wagering casinos make bonuses more attainable — but truly low wagering is rare. Here's how to tell the difference.

What is a Wagering Requirement?

A wagering requirement is a multiplier applied to your bonus (sometimes bonus + deposit) that you must bet through before withdrawing bonus-related winnings. A 30x wagering requirement on a £50 bonus means £1,500 in bets before withdrawal.

Cashback Bonuses: Often the Lowest Effective Wagering

Some casinos offer cashback bonuses (% of losses returned) with no wagering requirement or very low wagering (1x–5x). These are among the most player-friendly bonus formats. £100 cashback at 1x wagering means you just need to bet £100 — not £3,000+.

Game Contribution to Wagering

Not all games contribute equally to wagering. Slots typically contribute 100%. Live casino games often contribute 10% or 0%. This means if you prefer live games, most welcome bonuses are effectively unplayable — the wagering would take thousands of spins.

No-Wagering Casinos

A small number of casinos offer truly wagering-free bonuses — often in the form of cashback on losses, with the cashback paid as real cash. These are genuinely the most player-friendly, though they usually offer smaller headline amounts.
