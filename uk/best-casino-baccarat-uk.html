Baccarat Side Bets

Many baccarat tables offer side bets like Banker Pair, Player Pair, and Perfect Pair. These can pay 11:1 or 25:1 but carry house edges typically between 10% and 25%. They're entertainment bets — not recommended for value-conscious players.

Baccarat Myths Debunked

Baccarat outcomes are independent events. Scorecards tracking winning streaks have no predictive value. Each shoe is shuffled; each hand has the same mathematical probabilities regardless of previous results. Systems that claim to exploit patterns in baccarat results are mathematically unfounded.

How to Play Baccarat: Quick Reference

  • Bet on Banker, Player or Tie before each hand is dealt
  • Cards 2–9 are face value; 10, J, Q, K = 0; Ace = 1
  • Hand total uses only the rightmost digit (e.g. 7+6=13, counts as 3)
  • Natural 8 or 9 wins immediately — no more cards dealt
  • Third-card rules are automatic — no player decisions needed
  • Live baccarat is the most popular version at UK-facing online casinos (Evolution Gaming tables)
