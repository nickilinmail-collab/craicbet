Free spins are the most common casino bonus in the UK. Available as part of welcome packages, reload offers, and ongoing promotions — but their real value depends heavily on the terms. Here's the complete guide.

How Free Spins Work

Free spins let you play specified slot games without using your own money. Each spin has a fixed value (usually £0.10–£0.20). Winnings from free spins are added to your bonus balance and are subject to wagering requirements before withdrawal.

Free Spins Value Calculator

To calculate the value of a free spins offer: multiply the number of spins by the per-spin value. 100 spins × £0.10 = £10 gross value. At 30x wagering on winnings, you'd need to wager £300 from winnings (which likely average well under £10 at typical RTP).

Types of Free Spins Offers

  • No deposit free spins — Triggered by registration, no payment required
  • Deposit free spins — Awarded alongside a deposit bonus
  • Free spins on specific games — Restricted to one or a few titles
  • Wager-free free spins — Winnings paid as cash, no wagering (rare, premium)

Game Restrictions on Free Spins

Free spins are almost always restricted to specific games — often the same slot, usually a popular title from Pragmatic Play, NetEnt, or Play'n GO. The game's RTP affects how much you can expect to win from the spins. Book of Dead (96.21%) is a common free spins game; some casinos use lower-RTP titles.

Wager-Free Free Spins

Some casinos now offer "wager-free" free spins where winnings are credited as real cash, no wagering required. These are significantly more valuable than standard free spins — a £0.10/spin × 20 wager-free spins offer (£2 gross) may be more valuable than 100 spins with 40x wagering.

How to Calculate the Real Value of Free Spins

A "100 free spins" offer at £0.10 per spin generates £10 in bonus funds if the slot's RTP is 96%, the expected return before wagering is £9.60. With a 40× wagering requirement, you need to wager £400 through slots to withdraw. At 96% RTP each spin through, your expected loss during wagering is roughly £16 — meaning the offer has a negative expected value for most players.

Wager-free spins — even at just 20 spins — often deliver better real-money value because winnings convert to withdrawable cash immediately. Always check the spin value, wagering multiplier, and game restrictions (some free spins are locked to low-RTP slots) before claiming.
