How to Verify a UKGC Licence

Before depositing at any UK online casino, verify the operator holds a valid UKGC licence:

  1. Go to gamblingcommission.gov.uk/check-if-gambling-site-is-licensed
  2. Enter the casino's name or website domain
  3. Confirm the licence is active (not suspended or revoked)
  4. Check the licence type covers casino gaming — not just betting

All UKGC-licensed casinos must display their licence number in the footer of their website. If the footer shows no licence number, the site is not operating legally for UK players.

UK Casino Regulations in 2026 — What Changed

Slot Stake Limit: £5 per Spin

All UKGC-licensed online casinos cap slot stakes at £5 per spin. This applies to all online slots regardless of the operator. Land-based slot machines are not subject to the same cap.

Wagering Requirements: Maximum 10×

From January 2026, UKGC regulations require that no casino bonus offered to UK players carries wagering requirements above 10×. A £50 bonus at 10× wagering means you must wager £500 before withdrawing. Any advertised bonus with higher wagering than 10× is non-compliant.

Mandatory Deposit Limit Prompts

From June 2026, all UKGC-licensed casinos must prompt new players to set a deposit limit during the registration process. The limit is optional — you can set it to any amount — but the prompt cannot be skipped.

Affordability Checks

UK Casino FAQs

Are online casinos legal in the UK?

Yes. Online casinos are fully legal under the Gambling Act 2005. All operators serving UK players must hold a UKGC licence. Check any operator at gamblingcommission.gov.uk before depositing.

Do I pay tax on casino winnings in the UK?

No. Gambling winnings are not subject to income tax for UK recreational players. Operators pay gaming duty; you keep 100% of winnings.

What is the slot stake limit in the UK?

£5 per spin on all UKGC-licensed online slots. This was introduced to reduce problem gambling linked to high-stake online slots.

What are the max wagering requirements in the UK?

How do I self-exclude from online casinos in the UK?

Can I use a credit card at UK online casinos?

No. Credit card deposits for gambling are banned in the UK since April 2020. Use debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, or bank transfer instead.
