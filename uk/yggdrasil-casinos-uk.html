Yggdrasil Gigablox Mechanic: Explained

Gigablox is a Yggdrasil mechanic where large symbol blocks (2×2, 3×3 or even 4×4) land on the reels, covering multiple positions simultaneously. What it means in practice:

  • All symbols in a Gigablox are identical — a 2×2 Gigablox fills 4 reel positions with the same symbol, dramatically increasing win frequency when it lands.
  • Variance changes: Gigablox games are typically medium-high variance — the mechanic creates streaks (multiple Gigablox spins can be very profitable, then long dry spells).
  • Splitz: Another Yggdrasil mechanic — symbols split into smaller copies during free spins, increasing ways to win dramatically. Used in games like Stacked.

GigaBlox is a Yggdrasil mechanic that generates oversized symbol blocks (2x2, 3x3, up to 6x6) on reels, paying as multiple individual symbols. It significantly affects winning potential on a given spin.
