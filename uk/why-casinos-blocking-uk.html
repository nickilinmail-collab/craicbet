Why Are Gambling Sites Blocking the UK?

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 31 November 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

What Happened

Starting in September 2026, several high-profile gambling platforms abruptly blocked access for users in the UK. Media reporting linked the wave to direct engagement by the UKGC, which confirmed that some operators geo-blocked after its interventions and said it was supervising refunds of customer balances. Reports also put over a hundred operators under investigation for serving the UK without a licence.

Why Operators Are Pulling Out

Since 1 July 2026, taking bets from people in the UK requires a UKGC licence. The regulator can pursue sanctions and High Court action against operators acting without one. For betting-led platforms, the maths is simple: get licensed, geo-block, or face enforcement. Several chose to block first and consider a licence application after.

Does This Affect Online Casinos?

Here is the nuance most coverage misses: the current licence requirement covers betting. Online casino games are not yet inside the licensing regime, so a casino-only operator is not “unlicensed” in the same sense — there is no UK casino licence to hold. That said, platforms offering both betting and casino have tended to block entirely rather than run a partial service, and more exits are likely as the regime expands to gaming.

What To Do if Your Site Blocked the UK

  1. Withdraw is usually still possible. Operators that blocked under UKGC engagement have been refunding balances under the regulator’s supervision. Contact support through the official channels and request your balance.
  2. Document everything — screenshots of your balance, account details, correspondence.
  3. Escalate if ignored: complain to the operator’s own licensing regulator (Malta, Curaçao etc.), and the UKGC has shown it monitors refund behaviour of exiting operators.
  4. Never chase a blocked site through a VPN. It usually breaches the operator’s terms, which can void winnings — you would be converting a refund case into a confiscation case.

The Bigger Picture

The exits are the first visible proof that UK enforcement has teeth. As the licensing programme reaches casino games, expect the available operator pool to reshuffle again — we log every development on the UKGC register tracker.
