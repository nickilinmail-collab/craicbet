Casino Deposit Limits in the UK

Deposit limits are one of the most practical responsible gambling tools available to UK casino players. This guide explains how they work, what UKGC-licensed operators are required to offer, how cooling-off periods protect you, and how casino limits differ from those on betting accounts.

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

💡 Key Points

  • Casino deposit limits cap how much you can add to your account per day, week, or month
  • UKGC-licensed operators must offer deposit limits under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024
  • Reductions take effect immediately; increases require a cooling-off period
  • Casino limits and betting limits are separate tools — manage both if you use both products
  • Setting a limit is free, quick, and does not affect your ability to withdraw

What Are Casino Deposit Limits?

A casino deposit limit is a cap you place on how much real money you can add to your online casino account within a specified time window. The three standard periods are:

  • Daily limit — the maximum you can deposit in any 24-hour period
  • Weekly limit — the maximum over any rolling 7-day window
  • Monthly limit — the maximum over any rolling 30-day window

Once you reach your limit for a period, the casino will decline any further deposit attempts until the period resets. You can set one type of limit or all three simultaneously. The most effective approach is to set all three to reflect your genuine entertainment budget.

UKGC Requirements for Licensed Operators

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 requires all UKGC-licensed operators to make deposit-limit tools available to every customer. This is not optional — operators must:

  • Provide the facility to set deposit limits at or before the point of opening an account
  • Apply any reduction immediately upon request, with no delay
  • Apply any increase only after a mandatory cooling-off period
  • Remind customers of their current limits periodically

For online casinos not yet holding a UKGC licence (most operate on MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar licences while UKGC casino licensing rolls out), those offshore regulators impose comparable requirements. Any reputable casino will offer deposit limits regardless of its licensing jurisdiction.

How Cooling-Off Periods Work

The cooling-off rule is a deliberate friction mechanism designed to protect players from impulsive decisions. If you try to increase a limit — say, raising your daily limit from £50 to £200 — the casino is required to wait before applying the change. This delay varies by operator but is commonly:

  • 24 hours for modest increases
  • 48–72 hours for larger increases
  • 7 days for significant or unlimited increases (some operators)

During the cooling-off period your original lower limit remains in force. You can cancel the requested increase at any time, and your original limit stays. If you decide during the wait that you do not actually want to increase the limit, simply cancel the request in your account settings.

How to Set a Casino Deposit Limit

Setting a limit takes under two minutes at most casinos:

  1. Log in to your casino account and navigate to Responsible Gambling or Account Settings
  2. Select Deposit Limits
  3. Enter your chosen amount for the daily, weekly, and/or monthly period
  4. Confirm — the lower limit takes effect immediately

If you cannot find the deposit limit tool, contact customer support. UKGC-licensed operators are required to offer it, and international operators licensed by the MGA or UKGC are equally obliged to do so.

Casino Deposit Limits vs Betting Deposit Limits

Casino and betting products are typically managed as separate accounts — even on combined platforms. This means:

  • A deposit limit set on your casino account applies only to casino game deposits
  • Your sportsbook account has its own separate deposit limit
  • If your budget covers both products, you need to set limits on both accounts

See our companion guide on betting deposit limits in the UK for sportsbook-specific details. If you are unsure how your operator structures combined accounts, the customer support team can clarify.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a casino deposit limit?▾
A casino deposit limit is a cap you set on how much money you can transfer into your casino account over a given period — daily, weekly, or monthly. Once you reach your limit, the casino will not process further deposits until the period resets. It is a voluntary but powerful responsible gambling tool.
Are online casinos in the UK required to offer deposit limits?▾
Yes. All operators holding a UKGC licence are required under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 to provide deposit-limit tools. For online casinos operating on international licences (MGA, UKGC, Gibraltar), responsible gambling tools including deposit limits are required by those licensing bodies as well.
Can a casino increase my deposit limit immediately if I ask?▾
No. Responsible operators apply a cooling-off period before any increase takes effect — often 24 hours for small increases and up to 7 days for larger ones. This delay is deliberate: it gives you time to reconsider. Reductions take effect immediately.
What is the difference between a casino deposit limit and a betting deposit limit?▾
Casino deposit limits are set on your casino account and apply to casino games (slots, live casino, table games). Betting deposit limits apply to your sportsbook account and cover sports wagering. If you use a combined site, limits on each product are typically managed separately — check your account settings.
