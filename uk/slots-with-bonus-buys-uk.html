Bonus Buy Slots the UK 2026 — Feature Buy Mechanic Explained Honestly

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

The bonus buy mechanic lets you skip to a slot's bonus round immediately by paying a fixed multiple of your bet. Available at MGA-licensed casinos for UK players (banned in the UK since 2019), it is one of the most searched-for features in online slots. This guide explains how it works, what it really costs, and what UK players need to know in 2026.

How the Bonus Buy Mechanic Works

Most video slots have a bonus feature triggered by landing scatter symbols (typically 3 or more across the reels). The probability of naturally landing these scatters is built into the game's RNG. A bonus buy bypasses this:

  • You pay a fixed multiple of your current bet (typically 50x–200x) to immediately trigger the bonus round.
  • The bonus round plays identically to a naturally triggered one — same free spins, same multiplier mechanics, same RNG.
  • The cost reflects the mathematical value of skipping the base game to reach the bonus.

The Real Cost — Maths Explained

Suppose a game's bonus round is triggered on average every 300 spins at 1x bet per spin. The expected cost to reach the bonus naturally is 300 spins × £1 = £300. The bonus buy price is set to reflect this expected cost — often priced at a slight premium (e.g. 100x bet = £100 for a game where the bonus RTP portion equates to approximately that cost). The bonus buy does not improve your expected return; it removes the variance of waiting and pays a known upfront cost.

Key point: You are paying for certainty and immediacy, not improved odds. The RTP of a purchased bonus equals the bonus portion RTP of the game.

Bonus Buy vs UK Players

The UKGC banned bonus buys in October 2019 on the grounds that they can accelerate gambling and may appeal to vulnerable players. the UK has no equivalent ban as of November 2026 — MGA-licensed casinos available to UK players can offer bonus buys. This is a regulatory difference, not a reflection on safety; at a licensed, MGA-regulated casino, the game's RNG and RTP are independently certified regardless.
