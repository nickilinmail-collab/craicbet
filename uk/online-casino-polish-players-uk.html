UK Law Applies, Not Polish Law

Poland runs one of the most restrictive online gambling regimes in the EU. Online casino games are a state monopoly (Total Casino), and playing at unlicensed foreign sites from Polish territory is prohibited for players. Poland also maintains a register of blocked domains.

None of that applies to you once you are resident in the UK. UK law is based on where you are physically located and resident, not on your citizenship or country of birth. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 — the foundation of the UK's new regulatory framework — places the licensing obligation on operators, not players. As an UK resident, you are legally free to play at any casino licensed by a reputable regulator such as the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) or the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) that accepts UK customers.

Poland's domain-blocking register and player prohibitions are tools of Polish territorial law. They cover gambling activity taking place from Polish territory. If your connection and residence are in Dublin, Cork or Galway, you are outside the jurisdiction of Polish gambling regulation.

💡 Key Legal Takeaway

As an UK resident, your gambling rights are defined by the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and overseen by UKGC (the UK Gambling Commission). Polish gambling restrictions do not follow you across the border.

UKGC Licensing: What Polish Players Should Know

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) is the UK's new gambling regulator, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Casino licences are a separate category and are expected to follow in 2027–2028.
