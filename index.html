Yes. Online gambling is legal in Ireland. The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) was established in 2024 and began issuing licences in 2026. Irish players can legally play at casinos licensed by international regulators like the UKGC, MGA, and Gibraltar.

No. Gambling winnings are not taxable in Ireland. Irish players keep 100% of their winnings from online casinos, sports betting, and poker. The tax burden falls on the operators, not the players.
