Betmaster Ireland 2026: GRAI Licence Status & What Irish Players Need to Know

What Is Betmaster?

Betmaster is an online sportsbook and casino brand operated by BM Solutions Limited. The sportsbook covers football, tennis, basketball and other sports, alongside a casino product.

Betmaster’s GRAI Licence Status in Ireland

Betmaster is GRAI-licensed for betting. The public register of the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland lists BM Solutions Limited with remote betting licence GRAI-0735-RB-26-0001, issued on 1 July 2026 and valid until 30 June 2029. We checked the register entry on 11 November 2026.

The register lists the licensed company, not the brand. That is why a search for “Betmaster” can come up empty while a search for “BM Solutions Limited” finds the licence.

⚠️ Verify This Yourself

Licence statuses change. Before depositing with any operator, check the official GRAI licence register and search for the company name. Our guide at how to check a bookmaker’s GRAI licence walks through the check.

What the Licence Means for Irish Players

A GRAI-licensed bookmaker is covered by Ireland’s regulatory framework. For a customer in Ireland that means:

  • Deposit limits: GRAI licensees must apply the deposit-limit rules set under Irish regulations.
  • National self-exclusion: licensees must integrate with the National Gambling Exclusion register.
  • Complaints: a dispute that cannot be resolved with the operator can be escalated to the GRAI.
  • Credit card ban: licensees may not accept credit cards for gambling.

More detail is in our guide to GRAI player protection.

What the Licence Does Not Cover

The licence held by BM Solutions Limited is a remote betting licence. The GRAI has not yet issued any gaming licences, so no online casino product in Ireland, Betmaster’s included, is GRAI-licensed today. Casino licences are expected in 2027–2028.

A licence also says nothing about prices. Odds, margins, limits and withdrawal speed differ between licensed bookmakers, so it is still worth comparing before you open an account.
