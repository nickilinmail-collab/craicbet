Cheltenham Festival Betting Ireland 2027: Ante-Post, Each-Way & GRAI Guide

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

The Cheltenham Festival is the biggest four days in Irish betting. More money is wagered by Irish punters during Cheltenham week than on any other racing event in the calendar. This guide covers how the Festival works, how ante-post betting differs from race-day betting, and what GRAI licensing means for Irish punters placing bets online in 2026 and 2027.

When Is the Cheltenham Festival?

The 2027 Cheltenham Festival runs from Tuesday 9 March to Friday 12 March 2027 at Cheltenham Racecourse, Prestbury Park, Gloucestershire. It features 28 championship races across four days, anchored by the Champion Hurdle (Tuesday), the Queen Mother Champion Chase (Wednesday), the Stayers' Hurdle (Thursday) and the Gold Cup (Friday).

Irish stables — Willie Mullins, Gordon Elliott, Henry de Bromhead and Gavin Cromwell — dominate Cheltenham annually. Irish-trained horses have won more than 40% of all Festival races over the past decade.

Ante-Post vs Race-Day Betting: The Honest Difference

Most of the value at Cheltenham is captured months in advance, but ante-post betting carries risks that race-day betting does not. Understanding the difference is the first step to using your betting budget intelligently.

Non-runner no bet (NRNB) ante-post markets have become more common in recent years — these refund your stake if your selection doesn't run. Always check whether the market you're betting into is NRNB or standard ante-post.

The Four Days: What Each Day Means for Betting

  • Champion Day (Tue): Champion Hurdle — the two-mile hurdling championship. Irish trainers have dominated this race. Short prices are common for the favourite.
  • Ladies Day (Wed): Queen Mother Champion Chase — two-mile chasing championship. Mullins and Elliott have traded this prize in recent years.
  • Stayers' Day (Thu): Stayers' Hurdle + Ryanair Chase. The Ryanair is often a stronger each-way market than the Gold Cup for value.
  • Gold Cup Day (Fri): The Cheltenham Gold Cup — three miles two furlongs, the Blue Riband of jump racing. Dominated by Willie Mullins-trained horses in recent seasons. The betting market opens within 24 hours of the Gold Cup finish.

Each-Way Betting at Cheltenham: The Numbers

Each-way bets at Cheltenham pay 4 places at 1/4 the win odds for championship races (fields of 8+). For handicaps, place terms are often 4 or 5 places at 1/4 or 1/5 odds depending on field size. Use our each-way calculator to find the exact return before placing.

Example: A horse at 12/1 (13.0 decimal) each-way at standard 4-places 1/4 odds:

  • Win part: €10 × 13.0 = €130 return (if wins)
  • Place part: €10 × (12/4 + 1) = €10 × 4.0 = €40 return (if places 1st–4th)
  • Total stake: €20. A horse that places but doesn't win returns €40 on a €20 stake — a €20 profit.

For Cheltenham 2027, any bookmaker without a GRAI licence is an unlicensed operator — meaning no Irish legal protection for you if a dispute arises, no access to GRAI's complaint process, and no connection to Ireland's National Gambling Exclusion Register.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Cheltenham Festival 2027?

The 2027 Cheltenham Festival runs from Tuesday 9 March to Friday 12 March 2027 at Cheltenham Racecourse, Prestbury Park, Gloucestershire. The four days are Champion Day, Ladies Day, Stayers' Day and Gold Cup Day.

What is ante-post betting and is it worth it at Cheltenham?

Ante-post betting means placing a bet before final declarations, usually at longer odds than on race day. The risk: if your horse doesn't run, you lose your stake (unless the market is Non-Runner No Bet / NRNB). Ante-post value is strongest for confirmed Festival targets — a horse trading at 10/1 in December for the Gold Cup may be 7/1 by March.

Do I need to use a GRAI-licensed bookmaker for Cheltenham betting?

Since 1 July 2026, bookmakers must hold a GRAI remote betting licence to operate legally in Ireland. As a consumer you are not breaking the law by using an offshore operator, but a GRAI-licensed bookmaker gives you access to Ireland's dispute resolution process, the National Gambling Exclusion Register, and full protection under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. What are the each-way place terms at Cheltenham?

Standard each-way terms for Cheltenham championship races (8+ runners) are 4 places at 1/4 the win odds. Some bookmakers offer enhanced terms on handicaps (5 places or 1/5 odds). Which Irish trainers dominate Cheltenham?

Willie Mullins has become the most successful trainer in Cheltenham Festival history in recent years. Gordon Elliott, Henry de Bromhead and Gavin Cromwell also send strong teams.
