County Football Betting Ireland 2026: Sam Maguire & Leinster Championship

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 7 min read

County football — specifically the All-Ireland Senior Football Championship for the Sam Maguire Cup — is one of the biggest betting events in the Irish sports calendar. From provincial championships through the round-robin group phase to the All-Ireland final at Croke Park, Gaelic football generates consistent betting interest from January through August. This guide covers the Sam Maguire outright market, provincial championship betting, key match markets, and where to place your bets with a GRAI-licensed bookmaker.

The Sam Maguire Outright: How It Works

The Sam Maguire outright winner market covers the full All-Ireland Senior Football Championship. GRAI-licensed bookmakers open the market in January and update prices throughout the season based on National Football League form, provincial championship results, and injury news. The championship runs from early spring through to the All-Ireland final in late July or August at Croke Park.

The market is historically led by Kerry and Dublin, who have dominated Irish football for much of the last two decades. Other regular contenders include Galway, Mayo, Tyrone, and Derry, with the specific order varying year to year. The 2026 season will have a market shaped by the previous year's form and the winter National Football League results.

For betting purposes, the Sam Maguire outright offers most value in the early months (January–February) when the draw is set and information is limited, or immediately after provincial championship results dramatically shift the picture — an unexpected early exit by a favourite can create value on the remaining contenders.

Leinster Football Championship

The Leinster Senior Football Championship features counties from Leinster including Dublin, Meath, Kildare, Westmeath, Wicklow, and others. It runs as a knockout competition with the provincial final at Croke Park. Dublin's historic dominance of Leinster football has meant they are almost always the outright Leinster favourite — but the format means upset results do occur.

For betting on Leinster championship matches, the handicap market is particularly useful for Dublin games against weaker Leinster counties, where the match winner market offers little value. The outright Leinster football championship winner market is usually available from early spring.

Munster Football Championship

The Munster Senior Football Championship features Kerry, Cork, Limerick, Clare, Tipperary, and Waterford. It is the most competitive provincial football championship after Ulster. Kerry are the dominant force and usually enter as short outright favourites, though Cork have produced notable upsets in recent years.

Munster football matches often carry tighter handicap lines than Leinster fixtures because the county standard gap between Kerry and their opponents is narrower than Dublin's gap against weaker Leinster sides.

Key Match Betting Markets

Match winner (two-way, no draw in championship), handicap (points spread, goals = 3 points each), total points over/under, and half-time/full-time are the main match markets. First scorer and anytime scorer markets are available for major championship games. In-play betting is available for all televised matches.
