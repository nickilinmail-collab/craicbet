Cashless Betting in Ireland 2026: Contactless & Mobile Payment Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The shift to cashless betting in Ireland has accelerated significantly since the GRAI framework came into force in July 2026. Online betting by definition requires digital payment, and even on-course bookmakers at racecourses are increasingly moving to contactless card terminals.

Digital Payment Options for GRAI-Licensed Betting

GRAI-licensed online bookmakers accept a range of cashless payment methods:

  • Revolut: Ireland’s most popular digital banking app; instant deposits
  • Visa debit / Mastercard debit: Standard debit card payments; widely accepted
  • Apple Pay: Available at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers where Visa/Mastercard supports it
  • Google Pay: Similar availability to Apple Pay
  • Bank transfer: Slower (1-3 business days) but widely accepted for larger deposits
  • PayPal: Available at selected GRAI-licensed operators

Credit cards are banned under GRAI rules. This includes Visa credit, Mastercard credit and American Express. The ban applies to all gambling transactions, not just deposits at betting sites.

Contactless On-Course Betting

At Irish racecourses, on-course bookmakers are increasingly equipped with contactless card terminals. Cash remains accepted, but most larger on-course operators accept debit card payments. The GRAI retail licensing process (applications opening December 2026) is expected to include cashless requirements for new licence holders.

Setting Cashless Spending Limits

Using digital payments for betting makes it easier to track and control your spending. Most banking apps including Revolut, AIB, Bank of Ireland and Permanent TSB allow you to set gambling spending limits or block gambling transactions entirely. Use these in combination with the deposit limit tools at your GRAI-licensed bookmaker.

Future of Cashless Betting in Irish Shops

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 introduced mandatory cashless payment options in licensed betting shops. Key implications:

  • All retail GRAI-licensed bookmakers must accept cashless payments (debit card at minimum)
  • Cash limits: discussion of maximum cash bet per day — not yet finalised as of November 2026
  • Digital spending limit tools must be made available in retail shops
  • Online betting remains predominantly cashless by nature

Are credit cards banned for betting in Ireland?

Yes. The GRAI prohibits credit card deposits for all gambling transactions under its jurisdiction. This includes Visa credit, Mastercard credit and all charge cards. Debit cards, Revolut and bank transfer are allowed.

Can I use Apple Pay for betting in Ireland?

Apple Pay is accepted at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers where Visa or Mastercard debit underpins the Apple Pay transaction. Check your chosen operator's payment methods page for current availability.

Is it safe to use contactless payments at racecourses?

Yes. Standard contactless payment security applies at racecourse terminals as at any merchant. Keep your card details secure and check your bank statement after any on-course transactions.

How do I block gambling transactions on my Irish bank account?

Most major Irish banks (AIB, Bank of Ireland, Permanent TSB) offer gambling blocks in their mobile banking apps. Revolut also has a built-in gambling block. These are separate from the deposit limit tools at GRAI-licensed bookmakers.
