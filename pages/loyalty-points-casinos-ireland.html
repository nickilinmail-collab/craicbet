Casino loyalty points (also called comp points) are the building blocks of most online casino reward programmes. You earn them automatically on every real-money bet, then redeem them for bonus funds or, occasionally, cash. The concept sounds simple; the actual cash value per point is usually very low, and understanding the conversion maths is essential before deciding how much weight to give loyalty points in your choice of casino.

How Loyalty Points Work: Earn, Accumulate, Redeem

The earn-and-redeem loop works like this: for every € amount you wager at the casino, the system credits your account with a set number of points. Points accumulate in your account until you choose to redeem them, usually through a loyalty or rewards section of the casino. Redemption converts points into bonus funds (or occasionally withdrawable cash) at a fixed rate.

A common earn rate is 1 point per €10 wagered. A common redemption rate is 100 points = €1 in bonus funds. That means you must wager €1,000 to earn €1 in bonus value — a 0.1% gross return before wagering requirements on the redeemed bonus are applied.

Not all games earn points at the same rate. Slots typically earn at the full rate. Live dealer games may earn at 50% or less. Table games such as blackjack — which have lower house edges — often earn at 10%–25% of the standard rate. This design protects the casino’s economics: the lower the house edge on a game, the less generous the loyalty earn rate.

Point-to-cash conversion is structurally low. The loyalty scheme adds a small positive layer on top of normal play — it is not a path to material profit.

Some casinos offer better rates, particularly at higher VIP tiers. A Gold tier player might earn at 2× or 3× the base rate — meaning 0.2%–0.3% gross return. Tier Systems and How to Move Up

Most loyalty programmes use points as the dual currency of rewards and tier progression. Earn enough points in a qualifying period and you advance to the next tier, unlocking better earn rates, redemption rates, and perks. Some casinos separate “status points” (which advance your tier) from “reward points” (which you redeem) — always check which type you are accumulating.

Tier qualification periods are typically monthly or quarterly. If you don’t maintain the wagering volume, you drop back a tier at the next review. This design ensures the casino continues to earn from you — there is no permanent tier status at most online casinos.

How to Maximise Loyalty Point Value

Play slots for point accumulation. Slots earn at the full rate while also contributing 100% to bonus wagering. If your aim is loyalty points, slots are the most efficient vehicle.

Redeem at a minimum threshold. Some casinos require a minimum number of points before you can redeem (e.g. 500 points = €5 minimum). Don’t leave points to expire — redeem in batches as soon as you hit the minimum.

Compare conversion rates before choosing a casino. A casino offering 1 point per €5 wagered at the same 100-point rate doubles the gross return to 0.2%. Over a year of regular play, the difference compounds meaningfully.

Watch expiry dates. Points typically expire after 90 days to 12 months of inactivity. Set a calendar reminder if you play infrequently, and redeem before points expire.

Loyalty Points vs Direct Cashback

Cashback offers return a fixed percentage of net losses directly, often as bonus funds or real cash with no wagering. A 5% weekly cashback is structurally more transparent than a loyalty points scheme: you know exactly what you will receive for your activity. Points are a less direct system — the earn-and-redeem mechanism obscures the real value behind a layer of numerics. FAQ — Casino Loyalty Points Ireland

How do casino loyalty points work?▾
You earn loyalty (or comp) points automatically for every real-money bet placed at the casino, usually at a fixed rate such as 1 point per €10 wagered. Once enough points accumulate, you can redeem them for bonus funds or, less commonly, real cash. The conversion rate determines the actual cash value.
What is the typical cash value of loyalty points?▾
Conversion rates vary, but a common structure is 100 points redeemable for €1, earned at a rate of 1 point per €10 wagered. That equals €1 of bonus value per €1,000 wagered — a 0.1% return before wagering requirements on the redeemed bonus. The real return is lower still once wagering is factored in.
Can I convert loyalty points to cash in Ireland?▾
Most casinos convert loyalty points to bonus funds, not withdrawable cash. Bonus funds then carry their own wagering requirements before they become real money. A smaller number of casinos offer direct cash conversion with no wagering, which is more valuable.
Do loyalty points expire?▾
Many do. Typical expiry periods are 90 days to 12 months from the date points were earned, or from the last login. Some casinos reset points at the start of each calendar year. Always check the terms — expired points represent real value that cannot be recovered.
Are loyalty points counted towards VIP tier status?▾
Usually, yes. The points that determine your tier level are often the same points you can redeem for bonuses, though some casinos use separate ‘status points’ for tier progression and ‘comp points’ for redemption. Check which metric governs your tier before assuming all points serve double duty.
