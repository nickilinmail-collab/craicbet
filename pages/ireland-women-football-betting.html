Ireland Women’s Football in 2026

The Republic of Ireland women compete under FIFA and UEFA regulations, participating in European Championship qualifying and FIFA World Cup qualifying cycles. The team is ranked in the top 30 in the world by FIFA, a significant rise in recent years. Home games at Tallaght Stadium and the Aviva Stadium attract growing attendances.

Vera Pauw’s era raised the team’s profile significantly. The current management setup continues to develop a squad with significant club experience in the Women’s Super League in England and other professional leagues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do Ireland women play home games?

Ireland women's home games are played at Tallaght Stadium (capacity approximately 8,000) and the Aviva Stadium for higher-profile fixtures.

Can I bet in-play on Ireland women's international matches?

In-play markets for Ireland women's internationals are available at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers for major competitive fixtures. Availability is lower than for the men's international team.

Are there outright markets for Ireland women's qualifying campaigns?

Outright qualifying group winner and qualification markets are available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers when Ireland are participating in an active qualifying campaign.

Is the women's game well-covered by bookmakers?

Coverage has improved significantly in line with the team's rising profile. All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry match betting for Ireland women's competitive internationals.
