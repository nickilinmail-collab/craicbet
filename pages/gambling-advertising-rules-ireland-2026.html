Gambling Advertising Rules in Ireland: The 2026 Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

The Five Pillars

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 attacks gambling advertising from five angles. 1. The broadcast watershed — no TV or radio gambling ads between 5:30am and 9pm (dedicated page). 2. Child protection — a ban on advertising designed to appeal to children, and on gambling sponsorship of events or clubs aimed at them. 3. Social media opt-in — gambling ads on social platforms may only target users who actively opted in. 4. Inducement limits — restrictions on targeted VIP offers and pressure tactics. 5. Volume controls — the GRAI can regulate the frequency and placement of ads across media.

The Social Media Opt-In, In Practice

This is the quiet revolution. Instead of platforms deciding what you see, Irish users must affirmatively choose to receive gambling marketing. If you never opt in, licensed operators cannot put promoted bets in your feed. Expect to notice this as retargeting ads thinning out as licensing phases cover more of the market.

Inducements and the End of the Chase

The Act takes aim at the industry habit of chasing losing players with personalised offers — the "we miss you, here is 50 free spins" email at 11pm. Licensed operators face limits on targeted inducements; combined with the credit card ban and the exclusion register, the toolkit targets the mechanics of harm, not just its imagery.

Who Enforces All This

The GRAI — with penalties up to €20m or 10% of turnover. Advertising rules bind licensed operators, so coverage expands with each licensing phase. Offshore casinos outside the system are exactly that: outside — another reason the register matters more than any ad.

What This Means for What You See

Through 2026-2027, expect: daytime sports broadcasts without betting ads, gambling marketing disappearing from your social feeds unless you opted in, and a cleaner line between licensed operators (quiet, compliant) and offshore ones (loud, everywhere). That contrast itself becomes a useful trust signal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can gambling companies advertise on social media in Ireland?

Only to users who have opted in to receive gambling advertising — that is the model under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024.

Are gambling ads banned during the day in Ireland?

On TV and radio, yes — the Act provides for a watershed between 5:30am and 9pm.

Do these rules apply to offshore casinos?

They bind licensed operators. Offshore operators outside the Irish system are not directly bound — one more reason to prefer operators on the register.
