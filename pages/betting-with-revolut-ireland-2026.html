Betting with Revolut in Ireland 2026: Full Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Revolut is Ireland’s most widely used digital banking app and is accepted at virtually all GRAI-licensed betting sites for both deposits and withdrawals. This guide covers everything you need to know about using Revolut for betting in Ireland under the 2026 GRAI framework.

Why Revolut Works Well for Betting

Revolut is classified as an e-money or prepaid card product rather than a credit card, which means it is fully compliant with the GRAI’s ban on credit card gambling deposits. Revolut deposits at GRAI-licensed sites are instant, which is one of the main reasons Irish punters prefer it. Withdrawals back to Revolut typically take one to three business days at most operators.

Setting Up Revolut for Betting

  1. Download the Revolut app and complete identity verification (required for full account access)
  2. Add funds to your Revolut account via bank transfer or debit card top-up
  3. At your chosen GRAI-licensed bookmaker, select Revolut or Visa/Mastercard as your payment method
  4. Enter your Revolut card details and complete the deposit
  5. Revolut will send an in-app notification for each transaction

Revolut Gambling Controls

Revolut offers a built-in gambling block feature in its app that prevents any transactions to gambling merchants. This is a useful tool for anyone who wants to manage their gambling spend. The block can be switched on and off in the Revolut settings. Using Revolut’s gambling block is entirely separate from the deposit limit tools at GRAI-licensed bookmakers — you may choose to use both.

Withdrawal Speed with Revolut

Withdrawals from GRAI-licensed bookmakers back to a Revolut account are processed in one to three business days at most operators. Some operators offer same-day e-wallet withdrawals if Revolut is processed as a digital wallet rather than a card. Check your chosen operator’s payment terms for exact withdrawal timelines.

Revolut Gambling Block: How to Enable and Disable It

Revolut's gambling block is one of the most useful responsible gambling tools available to Irish bettors — it prevents any transactions to gambling merchants without you having to contact your bank. To toggle it:

  • Open Revolut app → tap your profile icon or the bottom menu
  • Go to Security → scroll to Gambling
  • Toggle Block gambling transactions on or off
  • A short delay (sometimes 24 hours) may apply before gambling deposits work after disabling the block

If you use the gambling block as a self-control measure, Revolut also lets you set a monthly gambling spend notification — useful even without the full block. 18+. For further support: Gambling Awareness Trust freephone 1800 753 753.

Revolut Gambling Block How to Turn Off Ireland

To re-enable betting deposits via Revolut, open the app, go to your card, tap Spending controls, and toggle the Gambling category back on. If you set a cool-off period when activating the block, Revolut requires you to wait the full period before removal — this is intentional friction. Standard toggle-off (no cool-off) takes effect immediately.

Can I Use Revolut for Online Betting Ireland 2026

Yes. Revolut functions as a standard Mastercard Debit at GRAI-licensed betting sites. Deposits are instant. Withdrawals take 2–24 hours typically. Monthly gambling category limits within Revolut itself may apply — check Settings → Spending limits → Gambling if deposits start declining unexpectedly.

Is Revolut accepted at GRAI-licensed betting sites?

Yes. Revolut is accepted at virtually all GRAI-licensed betting sites in Ireland for both deposits and withdrawals. It is classified as an e-money product, not a credit card, so it complies with the GRAI credit card ban.

Can I use Revolut to deposit at any Irish betting site?

Revolut is accepted at all major GRAI-licensed bookmakers. It may not be accepted at all unlicensed offshore sites, but those sites are not legally permitted to serve Irish customers.

Does Revolut have a gambling block?

Yes. Revolut includes a gambling block feature in its app settings that prevents transactions to gambling merchants. This is separate from the responsible gambling tools offered by GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

How fast are withdrawals from betting sites to Revolut?

Withdrawal processing times vary by operator. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers process withdrawals to Revolut within one to three business days. Some operators offer faster processing for e-wallet routes.
