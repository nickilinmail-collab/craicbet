What Is at Stake

The group winners are promoted to League A, the runners-up go into a promotion play-off in March 2027, and the bottom side faces a relegation play-off. With Ireland and Kosovo level on points before the window, a home win is the straightforward route to finishing above them. Exactly what each side needs will depend on Saturday’s results, and on the head-to-head record, where Kosovo hold a 1–0 lead from Pristina.

How to Price the Match Yourself

We do not publish tips, and we are not quoting a price here because odds move right up to kick-off. What you can do in a minute is check how much margin is built into the price you are offered:

  • Take the three match-result prices (home, draw, away) from your bookmaker.
  • Put them into our bookmaker margin calculator. It adds up the implied probabilities and shows the overround.
  • On a big international fixture a competitive 1X2 book is usually within a few percent of 100. If yours is much higher, the price is poor value whichever side you fancy.

The same check matters more on bet builders and accumulators, where the margin compounds with every leg you add.
