Revolut: The Fastest Option for Irish Punters

Revolut has become the preferred deposit and withdrawal method for Irish punters under 40. It is accepted at most GRAI-licensed operators, deposits are instant, and withdrawals typically process same-day. Revolut is classified as an e-money institution, not a credit card, so the GRAI’s credit card ban does not apply. Some operators have occasionally restricted Revolut deposits — check current acceptance before depositing significant funds.

Apple Pay and Google Pay: Convenience vs. Withdrawal Limitations

Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted for deposits at an increasing number of GRAI-licensed operators. The limitation: withdrawals go back to the linked underlying payment method (typically a debit card or Revolut), not directly to the digital wallet. If you use Apple Pay for deposits, factor in that your withdrawal route is effectively your debit card or Revolut, with the same withdrawal timelines.

Minimum Deposits and Fees

Most GRAI-licensed operators set minimum deposits of €5–€10. No reputable GRAI-licensed operator charges deposit fees; some charge withdrawal fees for certain methods (bank transfer at a small number of operators, e-wallet fees at others). Check the payment page before depositing to confirm there are no hidden fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are credit cards banned for betting in Ireland?

Yes. GRAI regulations ban credit card deposits at all licensed gambling sites. Debit cards, Revolut, PayPal and bank transfer are the standard alternatives.

Can I use Revolut on Irish betting sites?

Yes. Revolut is accepted at most GRAI-licensed operators for deposits and withdrawals. It is classified as e-money, not a credit card, so the GRAI ban does not apply.

Is Apple Pay accepted on betting apps in Ireland?

Some GRAI-licensed operators accept Apple Pay for deposits on iOS. Withdrawals go to the linked underlying payment method. Check the operator’s payment page for current acceptance.

What is the minimum deposit at GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

Most GRAI-licensed operators require a minimum deposit of €5–€10. Some welcome offers require a minimum qualifying deposit of €10–€20 to trigger the bonus.

Do bookmakers charge deposit or withdrawal fees?

Most GRAI-licensed operators do not charge deposit fees. Some charge small withdrawal fees for certain methods. Check the payment terms before depositing.
