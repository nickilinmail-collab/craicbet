House Edge in Blackjack

The house edge in blackjack depends heavily on the rules. Single-deck blackjack paying 3:2 with liberal rules can have a house edge under 0.5% with perfect basic strategy. Games that pay 6:5 on blackjack instead of 3:2 add roughly 1.4% to the house edge — always avoid 6:5 games.

Key Rules to Check

  • Blackjack pays: Always pick 3:2, never 6:5
  • Number of decks: Fewer decks generally better (single or double deck)
  • Dealer hits/stands on soft 17: Dealer standing on soft 17 is better for the player
  • Doubling down: More liberal rules (any two cards) favour the player
  • Re-splitting aces: Allowed is better for the player

Basic Strategy

Basic strategy is a mathematically optimal set of decisions for every hand combination. Using it correctly reduces the house edge to its minimum. Strategy cards are freely available online — most online casinos permit their use. Deviating from basic strategy (hitting on 17, not splitting 8s) increases the house's advantage.
