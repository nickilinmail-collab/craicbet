How to Self-Exclude from All Betting Sites in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Ireland’s Gambling Regulatory Authority (GRAI) operates a national self-exclusion register that lets you exclude yourself from all GRAI-licensed remote betting operators in one step. You can also self-exclude directly with individual sites or contact Gambling Awareness Trust for support.

GRAI National Self-Exclusion Register: Step by Step

The GRAI register, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, allows you to self-exclude from all licensed remote betting operators simultaneously. Here is how to use it:

  1. Visit grai.ie and navigate to the self-exclusion section
  2. Create an account using your verified identity (name, DOB, address)
  3. Select the duration: 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, or permanent
  4. Confirm your request — all GRAI-licensed operators are notified
  5. Your accounts with licensed bookmakers will be suspended within the timeframe specified in regulations

What Self-Exclusion Covers and What It Doesn’t

GRAI self-exclusion covers all remote betting operators licensed by GRAI. Unlicensed offshore sites are not bound by GRAI rules. Physical betting shops in Ireland operate under separate licensing. Self-exclusion is a tool to help you stop, not an absolute guarantee — if you use an unlicensed site, the register has no effect there.

If you are struggling with gambling, contact the Gambling Awareness Trust on 1800 753 753 (free, confidential) or visit gamblingcare.ie. Gamblers Anonymous Ireland meetings are free: gamblersanonymous.ie.

Can I reverse a GRAI self-exclusion?

During the self-exclusion period, reversal is not straightforward. After the period ends, there is typically a cooling-off period before your accounts can be reactivated. Permanent exclusion cannot be reversed under GRAI rules.
