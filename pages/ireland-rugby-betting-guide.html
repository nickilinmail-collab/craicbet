Ireland Rugby Team Betting Guide 2026/27: Six Nations & Autumn Tests

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 7 min read

The Ireland rugby team is one of the most followed sports teams in Ireland, regularly ranked among the world's best sides and generating enormous betting interest during every Test window. From the Six Nations in February–March to the Autumn Internationals in November, to the Rugby World Cup cycle, this guide explains how betting on Ireland rugby works, what markets to focus on, and how to pick a GRAI-licensed bookmaker.

Ireland in the Six Nations

The Six Nations (February–March) is Ireland's most high-profile rugby betting period of the year. Ireland face England, Scotland, Wales, France, and Italy over five rounds, playing home matches at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin and three away fixtures in a Grand Slam year. Ireland are consistently among the two or three most favoured sides in the tournament.

The outright Six Nations winner market opens in October each year. Ireland are typically priced at 5/2 to 7/2 at the start of the tournament, with the price adjusting significantly based on results and form in the opening rounds. Home/away splits matter: Ireland are considerably shorter at the Aviva than away from home — particularly in Paris, which has historically been one of the tougher venues on the tour.

For individual Six Nations matches, the handicap market is essential for Ireland fixtures where they face Italy or Scotland at home. Ireland are regularly set at -20 or greater against Italy, meaning the handicap market offers more competitive value than a match winner at 1/14 or shorter.

Autumn Internationals: November Tests

The November Autumn Internationals series brings southern hemisphere heavyweights — typically including New Zealand, South Africa, Australia, and Argentina — to European venues including the Aviva Stadium. Ireland's home record against southern hemisphere opposition at the Aviva has been among the best of any northern hemisphere nation in recent years.

For betting purposes, the Autumn Internationals are more complex than Six Nations markets because: the southern hemisphere teams arrive mid-season (some in better condition than others), European sides have played more recent rugby, and form reading requires tracking both northern and southern hemisphere data. Ireland are typically priced as slight favourites or near-evens against the top southern hemisphere nations at the Aviva.

Away summer tours (June) are less significant betting events and attract lower market volume; Ireland playing away from home in New Zealand or South Africa are typically priced as underdogs.

Rugby World Cup Betting: Ireland's 2027 Chances

The next men's Rugby World Cup is in Australia in 2027. Ireland are likely to enter the tournament as one of the five or six leading nations in the outright market, based on their current world ranking. Early prices (2–3 years before the tournament) can be attractive, but be aware these are long-range markets with significant uncertainty.

Ireland's World Cup history includes quarter-final exits on multiple occasions. This consistent knockout-stage under-performance against expectation is a key factor in how the market prices them relative to their regular-season form. Some bettors view the World Cup outright as offering value on Ireland in the early prices; others view the knockout stage history as a persistent structural factor. Both views are reasonable and neither is a guaranteed edge.

Key Markets for Ireland Rugby

Match winner is straightforward but rarely offers value for heavy-favourite Ireland home matches. The handicap is better for one-sided games. Total points is popular for Ireland fixtures where the result feels predictable but the scoring margin less so. Outright winner (Six Nations, World Cup) is worth checking early in the cycle before prices tighten. Try scorer markets carry wide margins — treat as entertainment rather than value betting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Ireland rugby ranked in the world?

Ireland are consistently ranked among the top four teams in the world by World Rugby. They have held the number one ranking at various points in recent years, making them short-priced favourites in most Six Nations fixtures and competitive in World Cup outright markets.

What are the main betting markets for Ireland rugby fixtures?

GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer match winner, handicap, total points over/under, first and anytime try scorer, half-time/full-time, winning margin, and outright Six Nations or World Cup winner markets for Ireland rugby. In-play markets are available for all televised Test matches.

Are Ireland good value in Six Nations outright betting?

Ireland's Six Nations outright price reflects their status as one of the top two or three favourites most years. The value depends on the draw and the state of competing nations. Ireland tend to be shortest for home Six Nations campaigns.

Is betting on Ireland rugby legal?

Yes. Betting on Ireland rugby matches is entirely legal in Ireland for people aged 18 and over, provided you use a bookmaker holding a GRAI remote betting licence. The GRAI framework has been in place since 1 July 2026.
