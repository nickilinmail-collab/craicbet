Best Each-Way Terms in Irish Horse Racing Betting 2026 — Where to Find Extended Places

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Each-way betting value is not just about the win price — the number of places paid and the fraction of odds offered on the place part are equally important. This guide identifies which race types and festivals offer the most generous each-way terms in Irish and British horse racing, and how to compare bookmakers before placing your each-way bets.

How Place Terms Vary by Race and Festival

Standard each-way terms are set by field size, but bookmakers regularly enhance them for major races. Here is a quick reference for the main races Irish punters target:

1/4 Odds vs 1/5 Odds: The Difference in Practice

The fraction paid on the place part matters significantly. At 1/4 odds on a 20/1 winner, the place return is 5/1. At 1/5 odds, it drops to 4/1. Over a year of each-way betting, consistently using 1/4-odds terms versus 1/5-odds terms on 20/1 runners produces meaningfully different profits. Always confirm the fraction as well as the number of places.

Extra Place Promotions

Many bookmakers offer an extra place promotion for major races — typically the Grand National, Cheltenham Festival, Galway festival and Royal Ascot. This means they will pay one more place than standard terms for that specific race. For example, in a race where standard terms are 4 places, the extra place promotion pays 5. Bookmakers vary in which races they apply these to and in which week — checking in race week is essential. Some run these as early-bird offers for ante-post bets placed before declarations.

Best Practice for Maximising Each-Way Returns

Steps to get the best each-way terms:

  • Compare in race week. Enhanced terms appear days before the race, not months ahead.
  • Read the terms precisely. Some enhanced place offers are for ante-post bets only; others apply to race-day bets. Some require a minimum odds threshold on the selection.
  • Use an each-way calculator. Our each-way calculator lets you compare exactly what different terms return at the same price.
  • Target the right races. Large-field handicaps are where place terms have the most impact. In a 10-runner conditions race, the difference between 3 and 4 places is one marginal outcome; in a 30-runner handicap, the difference between 5 and 6 places is much more significant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which race has the best each-way terms in horse racing?

The Grand National at Aintree offers among the best each-way terms in horse racing: standard 5 places at 1/4 odds, with many bookmakers extending to 6 or 7 places in race week. The Galway Plate and Irish Grand National also offer generous terms due to their large fields.

What is an extra place promotion in horse racing betting?

An extra place promotion is when a bookmaker pays one more place than the standard terms for a specified race. For example, if standard terms are 4 places and a bookmaker offers an extra place promotion, they pay 5 places. These promotions are most common for the Grand National, Cheltenham Festival, Galway races and Royal Ascot.

Is 1/4 or 1/5 odds better for each-way betting?

1/4 odds are better. At 1/4 odds on a 20/1 selection, the place part pays 5/1. At 1/5 odds, it pays 4/1. Always look for bookmakers offering 1/4 odds on the place rather than 1/5.
