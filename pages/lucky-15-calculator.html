Lucky 15 Calculator (4 Selections, 15 Bets)

What a Lucky 15 Actually Is

Four selections combined into 15 bets: 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold. A €1 unit stake costs €15 total — the most common surprise at the till. The singles mean one winner always returns something; the honest flip side is that 15 simultaneous bets carry 15 helpings of margin. Many Irish bookmakers pay a bonus (often double odds) on a one-winner Lucky 15 — check the terms, it materially changes the value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 1 euro Lucky 15 cost?

15 euro - it is 15 separate bets: 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold.

Do I get anything with one winner on a Lucky 15?

Yes. The winning single pays, and many bookmakers pay a one-winner bonus (often double odds) - check the terms.

What is the difference between a Lucky 15 and a Yankee?

A Yankee is the same four selections without the 4 singles: 11 bets. Cheaper, but one winner returns nothing.
