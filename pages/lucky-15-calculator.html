How to Calculate a Lucky 15 — Step by Step

A Lucky 15 is built from 4 selections. The name comes from the total number of bets: 4 singles + 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 four-fold = 15 bets. Here is exactly how to calculate the return manually:

Step 1: List All 15 Bet Combinations

TypeCountCombinations
Singles4A, B, C, D
Doubles6AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD
Trebles4ABC, ABD, ACD, BCD
Four-fold1ABCD

Step 2: Calculate Each Winning Bet

For each combination where all selections won, multiply the decimal odds together and multiply by the unit stake. For example with €1 stakes and odds A=3.0, B=4.0 (both win), C=2.5, D=5.0 (both lose):

  • Single A: 3.0 × €1 = €3.00
  • Single B: 4.0 × €1 = €4.00
  • Double AB: 3.0 × 4.0 × €1 = €12.00
  • Singles C, D and all combinations containing C or D = €0 (lost)

Total return: €3 + €4 + €12 = €19.00 from a €15 outlay (profit €4). The calculator above does all this instantly for any odds.

Step 3: Check for Bookmaker Bonuses

Many Irish bookmakers offer a one-winner bonus on Lucky 15 bets — often double odds on the winning single. This can significantly change the value. If the same example above had a one-winner bonus, Single A at 3.0 odds would pay at 6.0 odds (€6 instead of €3), adding €3 to the return. Always check bonus terms before placing.

What a Lucky 15 Actually Is

Four selections combined into 15 bets: 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold. A €1 unit stake costs €15 total — the most common surprise at the till. The singles mean one winner always returns something; the honest flip side is that 15 simultaneous bets carry 15 helpings of margin. Many Irish bookmakers pay a bonus (often double odds) on a one-winner Lucky 15 — check the terms, it materially changes the value.

Lucky 15 Bonuses: What to Check

Many bookmakers run Lucky 15 bonuses that pay a percentage uplift when all four selections win, or console returns when only one wins. Common formats:

  • All-four-winners bonus: often 10–25% added to the return when all selections win.
  • One winner consolation: when only one selection wins, the payout is doubled (or enhanced by some multiple). Check whether this applies to SP or BOG.
  • BOG (Best Odds Guaranteed): on horse racing Lucky 15s, your starting price is bumped to SP if SP exceeds the morning price. BOG applies per selection.

These bonuses vary considerably between operators. The all-four bonus at 10% on a €0.50 per line (€7.50 total) Lucky 15 adds up to 10% extra on what could be a large accumulated return — worth checking before placing.
