Ireland's Six Nations Recent Form

Ireland has been among the most consistent nations in the Six Nations in recent seasons, with multiple Championship wins and Grand Slam challenges. Looking ahead to 2027, the squad is expected to remain competitive at the top of the market. Home fixtures at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin provide a significant advantage, with Ireland's home record in the Championship among the best in the competition.

Main Markets for Ireland in the Six Nations 2027

  • To win the Six Nations Championship: Outright winner market — Ireland typically prices as one of the two or three favourites.
  • Ireland Grand Slam odds: Five wins from five. Ireland has claimed Grand Slams in recent years; the market will reflect current squad strength.
  • Ireland to win each match: Individual match betting across all five rounds — home and away.
  • Ireland top try scorer: Which Irish player crosses the line most times across the tournament.
  • Ireland handicap betting: When Ireland face weaker opposition (Italy, Georgia if promoted), handicap lines offer better value than the outright win market.

Key Fixtures to Watch

Ireland's toughest matches typically involve England (often in London in odd-numbered years), France (a potential title decider), and Scotland (always competitive regardless of league position). The scheduling of these fixtures within the five-round format significantly affects Ireland's chances — a kind draw in terms of home/away split can be decisive.
