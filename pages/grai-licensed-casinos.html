What Is GRAI?

The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) is Ireland's new dedicated gambling regulator, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2022. It represents the most significant reform of Irish gambling law in decades.

Before GRAI, Ireland had no modern, dedicated gambling regulator. The industry was governed by outdated legislation — some dating back to the 1930s. GRAI changes everything by creating a comprehensive regulatory framework that protects players, ensures fair play, and holds operators accountable.

GRAI's Key Powers

  • Licensing authority — GRAI issues and revokes licences for all gambling operators serving Irish players
  • Player protection — Mandatory responsible gambling tools, deposit limits, and self-exclusion systems
  • Enforcement — Power to fine operators up to €20 million or 10% of annual turnover
  • Advertising standards — Strict rules on how gambling can be marketed in Ireland
  • Complaints handling — A formal process for player complaints against licensed operators
