Racing Post Alternatives for Irish Punters 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Racing Post is the leading paid subscription service for horse racing form and betting data, but Irish punters have access to several free and lower-cost alternatives for form study, odds comparison and racing information.

Free Irish Racing Resources

  • Horse Racing Ireland (hri.ie): Official fixture lists, results, going reports and racecard downloads for all Irish fixtures
  • Sporting Life (sportinglife.com): Free form guides, results and tips for Irish racing — widely used alternative to Racing Post
  • At The Races (attheraces.com): Free form, results and tipping for UK and Irish racing with live streaming for account holders
  • Racing TV (racingtv.com): Irish racing broadcaster with free results and racecard data; full streaming requires subscription
  • GRAI register (grai.ie): Not a racing resource but essential for checking bookmaker licence status

Odds Comparison Tools

Comparing odds across GRAI-licensed bookmakers before placing a bet is one of the simplest ways to improve your returns. Free odds comparison services aggregate prices from multiple operators. The CraicBet GRAI tracker lists all currently licensed operators.

Tipster and Community Resources

Irish racing has a strong tipping community across social media, particularly Twitter/X, Telegram and racing forums. When following tipster advice, always verify the person’s credentials and remember that no tipster can guarantee profit — past performance does not predict future results.

What to Use Instead of Racing Post in Ireland

Racing Post introduced a subscription paywall on its in-depth form content in recent years. For free racecards and basic form, Sporting Life and At The Races cover all British and Irish meetings. For official Irish results and declarations, hri.ie is the authoritative free source.

  • For free racecards: Sporting Life covers all UK and Irish racing for free
  • For detailed form guides: Racing Post's free tier still shows speed figures and basic form; a subscription unlocks Spotlights and Notebook comments
  • For tissue prices / market comparisons: oddschecker.com (free) aggregates prices across all Irish bookmakers including ante-post markets
  • For Irish-specific previews: independent Irish horse racing journalists on X (Twitter) provide free analysis for major meetings
Is there a free alternative to the Racing Post for Irish racing?

Yes. Sporting Life, At The Races and Horse Racing Ireland all offer free form guides, results and racecard data for Irish racing. They are less comprehensive than the Racing Post but cover the basics for free.

Where can I check going reports for Irish racecourses?

Horse Racing Ireland (hri.ie) publishes going reports for all Irish racecourses on the morning of each meeting. Racecourses also post going updates on their own websites and social media accounts.

Are there free odds comparison tools for Irish betting?

Yes. Several free odds comparison services cover GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Comparing prices across two or three operators before placing a bet consistently improves returns over time.

Can I watch Irish racing for free online?

RTE and Racing TV broadcast some Irish racing live and free. Full live coverage of all Irish fixtures typically requires a Racing TV subscription or a GRAI-licensed bookmaker account that includes streaming.
