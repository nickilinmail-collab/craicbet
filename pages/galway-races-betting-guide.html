Galway Races Betting Guide 2027 — Festival Week, the Plate & Each-Way Strategy

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Galway Races is Ireland's most popular summer racing festival, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors over seven days in late July and early August. The highlight betting races are the Galway Plate (a Grade B chase handicap) and the Galway Hurdle (a Grade B hurdle handicap) — both large-field handicaps with big each-way opportunities. This guide covers the full betting picture for Irish punters.

The Galway Races: Festival Programme

The Galway festival runs for seven days at Galway Racecourse (Ballybrit). The first four days are the most significant for National Hunt and mixed racing; the final three days shift to the summer flat programme. The two headline betting races are:

  • Galway Plate (Wednesday, Grade B chase handicap, 2m 6f) — consistently one of the most heavily bet races in Ireland all year. Fields of 18–26 runners.
  • Galway Hurdle (Thursday, Grade B hurdle handicap, 2m) — large field, often features returning summer hurdlers. Each-way market is active.

Betting Character: Handicaps and Large Fields

Both the Plate and the Hurdle are handicaps — weights are adjusted to equalise chances. This makes them genuinely difficult to predict and means:

  • Favourites win at a lower rate than in conditions races.
  • Each-way betting at extended place terms (5–6 places) produces significant returns on mid-priced runners who finish placed.
  • Market moves in the days before the races often follow non-public information — by race week, market drifters should be treated cautiously and market movers respected.

Each-Way Terms at Galway

For the Plate and the Hurdle with fields of 18+ runners, most bookmakers offer 5 or 6 places at 1/4 odds each-way. Some run promotions with extra places or enhanced place odds in the week of the festival. With these terms, an each-way bet at 25/1 on a horse that finishes third still returns a profitable place dividend. Use the each-way calculator to plan your bets.

Other Galway Races Worth Noting

Beyond the Plate and Hurdle, Galway's flat programme in the later days of the festival includes competitive handicaps that attract international runners and significant market moves from professional betting operations. The Galway Mile and the Galway Apprentice Handicap are popular flat races with Irish punters who follow the summer flat season.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Galway Races 2027?

The Galway Races 2027 are expected in late July to early August. The exact dates will be confirmed by Horse Racing Ireland. The festival typically opens on a Monday and runs for seven days.

What is the Galway Plate?

The Galway Plate is a Grade B chase handicap run over 2m 6f at Galway Racecourse (Ballybrit) on the Wednesday of the festival. It is one of the most heavily bet races in the Irish calendar, with fields typically of 18–26 runners.

What each-way terms apply to the Galway Plate and Hurdle?

With typical large fields (18+ runners), most bookmakers offer 5 or 6 places at 1/4 odds for both races. Enhanced place terms or promotions are common in festival week — always check and compare before placing.

Is the Galway Racecourse right or left-handed?

Galway Racecourse (Ballybrit) is a right-handed, undulating circuit. The track features a significant uphill finish, which suits strong stayers and horses with proven ability to handle undulating terrain.
