Can I Use Google Pay for Betting in Ireland?

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | Gambling Awareness Trust | 1800 753 753

Short answer: Yes. Google Pay works for betting deposits at most GRAI-licensed Irish betting sites. Deposits appear instantly and you authenticate with your phone's fingerprint or PIN — no card details to type.

How Google Pay works at betting sites

Google Pay is a digital wallet that tokenises the debit card you have linked in your Google account. When you deposit at a betting site, the site processes it as a standard Visa or Mastercard transaction — it's not a separate payment network. This means:

  • Deposits are instant
  • Your actual card number is never shared with the betting site
  • Your bank still sees the transaction as a gambling payment — gambling blocks still apply
  • Withdrawals go back to your underlying bank account, not to Google Pay

Which Irish betting sites accept Google Pay?

Google Pay is now a standard deposit option at most Irish-facing bookmakers. Look for the Google Pay logo on the site's deposit screen. If it doesn't appear, the site may not support it for your card type, or you may need to use the mobile browser version rather than the app (some betting apps route payments differently).

Why is my Google Pay deposit being declined?

The most common reasons:

  • Gambling block on your bank card — check with your bank (BOI, AIB, PTSB all offer opt-in gambling blocks). Google Pay doesn't bypass your bank's restrictions.
  • 3D Secure not completed — your bank should send a push notification or SMS code. Check your phone immediately after initiating the deposit.
  • Card not set as default in Google Pay — make sure the card you intend to use is selected in the Google Pay wallet before depositing.
  • Site doesn't support Google Pay — some smaller or newer bookmakers haven't integrated it yet. Use a standard debit card instead.
  • Insufficient funds — Google Pay pulls from the linked card's available balance.

Google Pay vs Apple Pay for betting in Ireland

Both work the same way under the hood — both tokenise a card and process it as Visa/Mastercard. The only practical difference is device: Google Pay is for Android, Apple Pay for iPhone and iPad. If you're on Android, Google Pay is the equivalent option. See our Google Pay vs Apple Pay betting comparison.

Can I use Google Pay on betting apps?

It depends on the app. Most native Android betting apps support Google Pay directly in the deposit flow. Some use an in-app browser for payments, which also supports Google Pay if your Android device has it configured. If the Google Pay button doesn't appear, try depositing via the mobile browser version of the site.

Can I use Google Pay to deposit at betting sites in Ireland?
Yes. Google Pay works as a deposit method at most Irish-facing betting sites. Deposits are instant and authenticate using your phone's biometric or PIN.
Can I withdraw betting winnings to Google Pay?
No. Google Pay is deposit-only at betting sites. Withdrawals go back to the underlying bank card and arrive in your bank account within 1–5 business days.
Does Google Pay bypass my bank's gambling block?
No. Google Pay tokenises your bank card but the transaction still goes through your bank. If your bank has a gambling block enabled, it will also block Google Pay deposits to gambling sites.
