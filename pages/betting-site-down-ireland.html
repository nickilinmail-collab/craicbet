Betting Site Down in Ireland: What Happens to Your Bets?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

When a GRAI-licensed betting site goes down — whether a full outage or just the in-play/cash-out function — your existing bets remain valid and will be settled normally. Here is what to do, what your rights are, and how to minimise disruption.

Your Bets Are Safe During an Outage

Bets placed before the outage are stored on the operator’s servers and will settle based on the actual event result. An outage does not void your bets. You will not win or lose based on the site being down — only on what actually happens in the event you bet on.

Cash-Out During an Outage: Your Options

If you want to cash out during a site outage, you may not be able to. Cash-out requires real-time server communication. During an outage, cash-out is typically unavailable. This is a risk of live betting — if you are concerned about a bet, try to cash out before connectivity issues arise.

Contact the operator’s customer support (many have 24/7 Twitter/X support when apps are down) and document the time and bet reference. GRAI rules require operators to treat customers fairly in outage situations.

Checking If It’s the Site or Your Connection

  1. Try a different browser or the app (if you were on the browser)
  2. Check the operator’s Twitter/X account for outage notices
  3. Try a site like DownDetector to see if others are reporting the same issue
  4. Try on a different connection (switch Wi-Fi to 4G or vice versa)

GRAI Rights During Outages

GRAI-licensed operators must maintain service reliability as a licence condition. Persistent or repeated outages during major events can be reported to the GRAI. If an outage led to a financial loss you believe you should not have suffered (e.g., you could not cash out and the event went against you), document everything and submit a formal complaint.

Protecting Yourself During Site Outages

  • Your balance is safe. Licensed operators hold client funds separately from operating funds. A site outage does not affect your balance.
  • Bets already placed still settle. If your bet is confirmed (you have a bet reference number), it will settle even if the site goes down during the event.
  • Screenshot confirmation pages. If you place a bet just before a site goes down, screenshot the confirmation. This is your evidence if there is any dispute about whether the bet was accepted.
  • Use the mobile app as a backup. App and website often run on different infrastructure — if the website is down, the app may still work (or vice versa).
  • Contact support. GRAI-licensed operators must maintain customer service during outages. Live chat or email should still be accessible.
What happens to my bets if a betting site goes down?

Your bets are stored server-side and remain valid. They will settle based on the actual event result. An outage does not void bets.

Can I cash out if the betting site is down?

Usually no — cash-out requires real-time server communication. If the site is down, cash-out is typically unavailable. Document the outage time and your bet if you wanted to cash out.

How do I check if an Irish betting site is down?

Try the app vs browser, check the operator's Twitter/X account for outage notices, and use DownDetector to see if others report the same issue.

Can I complain to the GRAI about betting site downtime?

Yes — persistent or repeated outages during major events can be reported to the GRAI. If the outage caused a specific financial harm, document it and submit a formal complaint to the operator first.

Do I need to contact support if the site went down during my bet?

Only if you believe the outage affected your bet's outcome (e.g., prevented a cash-out you would have taken). Get the support response in writing for any potential GRAI complaint.
