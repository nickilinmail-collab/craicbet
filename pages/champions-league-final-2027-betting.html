Champions League Final 2027 — Munich Final Betting Guide for Irish Punters

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The UEFA Champions League final 2027 is scheduled to be held in Munich, Germany, in May 2027. For Irish punters, this is one of the most bet-on single matches of the year — with ante-post markets on the finalists opening from the semi-final draw and match-day markets offering comprehensive coverage.

The Munich Final — What to Expect

Munich's Allianz Arena has previously hosted Champions League finals and is one of Europe's most iconic venues. The 2027 final in Munich will be the culmination of a ten-month competition covering the league phase and five knockout rounds. By the time the final is played, bettors will have significant information about both finalists — their recent form, injury situation, tactical approach and head-to-head history.

Main Betting Markets for the Final

  • Match result (90 minutes): Straightforward 1X2, settled after 90 minutes only. Draws in knockout finals go to extra time then penalties — the 90-minute result is the standard market.
  • To lift the trophy / outright winner: Equivalent to the match winner for a final between two specific clubs.
  • First goalscorer / anytime goalscorer: Popular markets in finals; prices often reflect recent form and penalty duties.
  • Correct score: Higher-odds market; Champions League finals tend to be tighter, with fewer goals than average.
  • To go to extra time / penalties: Dedicated markets on how the match is settled.

Ante-Post Strategy Before the Final

Once the semi-final draw is known, ante-post markets on the final open — including potential finalist combinations. If a punter has backed the outright winner and their selection reaches the final, assessing cash-out versus continuing is a key decision. Carrying the bet to the final offers full odds; cashing out early locks in a portion of the profit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Champions League final 2027?

The UEFA Champions League final 2027 is scheduled to be held in Munich, Germany. The Allianz Arena is the expected venue, subject to UEFA confirmation. The final is traditionally held on a Saturday in late May.

Can I bet on the Champions League final before the finalists are known?

Yes — bookmakers offer ante-post markets on potential finalist pairings and overall winners throughout the competition. Once the semi-finals are played, the actual finalists are known and standard match betting opens.

How is the Champions League final settled for betting purposes?

The 90-minute match result market is settled after regulation time only. If the match is level after 90 minutes, it goes to extra time and potentially penalties — but the 90-minute market has already been settled. 'To lift the trophy' markets are settled on the actual winner including extra time and penalties.

Are in-play markets available for the Champions League final?

Yes — GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer extensive in-play markets on the Champions League final, including next goal, next scorer, correct score, and time of next goal. In-play betting opens at kick-off on final night.
