County Hurling Championship Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The county hurling championships run from May through to the All-Ireland final in July or August each year. Provincial championships — Munster and Leinster for hurling — are the route to Croke Park, with qualifiers offering a second chance to counties that fall in their province.

How County Hurling Championship Betting Works

Hurling championship betting follows the same structure as football: outright winner markets, match betting, handicap betting and in-play markets on selected games. Outright markets for the following summer's championship typically open at the start of the new year, often with the previous year's All-Ireland champion as the short-price favourite.

The key counties to watch in Munster hurling are historically Kilkenny, Tipperary, Cork, Clare and Limerick. In Leinster, Kilkenny are the dominant force but Galway (now competing in Leinster) and Wexford regularly provide upsets.

Market Types for Hurling

  • Outright winner: Bet on who will win the All-Ireland, Munster or Leinster title
  • Match betting: 1X2 — most hurling matches have a draw market worth considering
  • Handicap betting: Point handicaps are common in games between unevenly matched sides
  • Top scorer: Markets on the leading scorer across the championship or in a single game
  • To qualify: Betting on a county to reach a specific round

Finding Hurling Markets Online

GRAI-licensed online bookmakers carry hurling markets during the championship season, typically from May to August. Market depth is greatest for high-profile Munster and Leinster championship games and All-Ireland semi-finals and finals. For club championship matches, markets may be limited to larger club grounds fixtures.

County hurling championship is distinct from the county senior hurling championship — most betting refers to the inter-county GAA hurling championship (provincial and All-Ireland), not club-level competitions. Leinster and Munster championships are the main events before the All-Ireland series.

Markets Available on Hurling Championships

  • Match winner: Two-way market (no draw offered in some formats; check terms). Extra time and penalties may be needed to settle.
  • Half-time / Full-time: Combined result market across both halves.
  • To win provincial championship (outright): Pre-tournament odds on who wins Munster or Leinster. Good value early in the season when form is uncertain.
  • Total points (over/under): Hurling is a high-scoring sport (combined totals of 40–60+ points are common). Check market lines carefully — hurling points ≠ GAA football points.
  • Handicap: Points handicap on favourite. Common when one team is a heavy favourite (e.g. Limerick vs qualifier).
When does county hurling championship betting open?

Outright markets for the All-Ireland hurling championship typically open in January or February. Match betting for specific games opens one to two weeks before the fixture.

Can I bet in-play on hurling?

Yes, though in-play markets for hurling are more limited than for football. Major championship games such as All-Ireland semi-finals and the final typically have live betting markets. Check your GRAI-licensed bookmaker for availability.

Which counties are favourites in hurling most years?

Historically, Kilkenny, Tipperary, Limerick and Cork are the most decorated counties. Market prices reflect recent form and playing strength rather than historical records — check the latest odds at a GRAI-licensed bookmaker for current form.

Is it legal to bet on GAA games in Ireland?

Yes. Betting on GAA is legal under the GRAI framework. Bookmakers serving Irish customers must hold a GRAI Remote Betting licence. No restrictions apply to betting on specific sports including GAA.
