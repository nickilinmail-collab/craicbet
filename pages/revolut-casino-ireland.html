Revolut Casino Ireland: Does It Work?

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Can you use Revolut at an Irish casino? Revolut is one of Ireland's most popular fintech apps — but its relationship with online gambling is complicated. Here is exactly what works, what does not, and what your alternatives are.

Does Revolut Work at Online Casinos in Ireland?

It depends on your Revolut account settings and the casino's payment processor. Revolut issues Visa and Mastercard debit cards, which work at most online casinos in principle. However, Revolut has an internal gambling block that can be toggled in the app. For some accounts, gambling transactions are blocked by default.

How to Enable Gambling on Revolut

In the Revolut app: Security → Payment controls → Gambling. Toggle gambling transactions on. If your casino deposit is declining, check this setting first. This does not override the Irish credit card ban — Revolut is a debit card, so the ban does not apply to it regardless.

Why Some Casinos Do Not Accept Revolut

Some MGA and GRAI-licensed operators refuse Revolut specifically because it is classified as an e-money institution rather than a traditional bank. Their payment terms may require a traditional bank debit card for KYC and anti-money-laundering compliance. If a casino declines your Revolut card, try your AIB, Bank of Ireland, or permanent tsb debit card instead.

Revolut's Gambling Limit Feature

A genuine advantage of Revolut for gamblers: you can set a monthly spending limit on gambling inside the app under Payment controls. This adds a layer of responsible gambling control on top of the casino's own deposit limits required by GRAI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Revolut at an online casino in Ireland?

Usually yes. Revolut issues Visa and Mastercard debit cards accepted at most casinos. Check your Revolut app under Security → Payment controls → Gambling to make sure gambling transactions are enabled.

Why is my Revolut card being declined at a casino?

Two common reasons: (1) gambling transactions are blocked in your Revolut app settings — toggle them on under Security → Payment controls → Gambling; (2) the casino does not accept e-money institutions like Revolut and requires a traditional bank debit card instead.

Does the Irish credit card gambling ban affect Revolut?

No. The ban applies to credit cards only. Revolut is a debit card and e-money card, not a credit card, so it is not affected.

Can I withdraw casino winnings to my Revolut account?

Most casinos support withdrawals to the same Visa or Mastercard debit card used to deposit. Revolut card withdrawals generally work, with 1–5 business days processing time typically.

Is Revolut good for managing casino spending?

It has a useful gambling spend limit feature under Payment controls. You can cap your monthly casino spend inside the Revolut app, which adds a layer of responsible gambling control beyond the casino deposit limits.
