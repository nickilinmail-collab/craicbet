The Credit Card Ban: What It Means for Revolut Users

Ireland banned credit card gambling in 2020 under voluntary industry rules, with BoyleSports and Paddy Power stopping credit card transactions that year. When Revolut became widely used for gambling shortly after, regulators and operators noticed that credit card money was flowing into casinos via the Revolut card — effectively bypassing the ban.

Revolut's response was to implement transaction-level detection: if a gambling payment is funded from credit card rails, it is automatically blocked. The same technology had already been deployed in the UK. This was never a ban on Revolut itself — it was a targeted block on credit card money reaching gambling sites.

How to Enable Gambling on Revolut — Step by Step

Gambling payments are controlled by two separate settings in Revolut. Both must be in the right state before a casino deposit will go through.

Step 1 — Check Payment Controls (Gambling Toggle)

  1. Open the Revolut app
  2. Tap Profile (bottom right)
  3. Go to Security
  4. Tap Payment controls
  5. Find Gambling and toggle it ON

This setting controls whether your Revolut card can be used at gambling merchants at all. It is separate from the Gambling Block below.

Step 2 — Check the Gambling Block

  1. Open the Revolut app
  2. Tap Profile
  3. Go to Security
  4. Find Gambling Block
  5. If it shows as ON, toggle it off
  6. Wait 48 hours — this cooling-off period is mandatory and cannot be skipped
⚠️ Important: The 48-hour delay on disabling the Gambling Block is intentional. It was designed with GRAI to prevent impulsive decisions. If you enabled the block during a session and now want to remove it, you must wait the full two days. Plan ahead.

Step 3 — Set a Personal Gambling Spending Limit (Optional but Recommended)

  1. Go to Profile → Security → Payment controls → Gambling
  2. Tap Set spending limit
  3. Enter your monthly maximum (e.g. €100)

This cap works independently of casino deposit limits. Using both — a Revolut cap and a casino deposit limit — gives you two separate safety layers, which is exactly what GRAI's responsible gambling framework recommends.

Revolut and GRAI — The Regulatory Picture in 2026
