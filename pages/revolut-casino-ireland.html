Revolut has over 3.5 million users in Ireland, making it the country's most widely used fintech app. Naturally, many Irish casino players try to use it for deposits and withdrawals. Here is exactly what works, what does not, and how to avoid your card being declined.

Does Revolut Work at Online Casinos in Ireland?

In most cases, yes. Revolut issues Visa and Mastercard debit cards, which are accepted at the vast majority of online casinos serving Irish players. However, two things can cause a failed transaction:

  1. Gambling blocked in-app — Revolut has an internal gambling toggle that may be off by default on some accounts
  2. Casino rejects e-money institutions — Some operators' payment terms require a traditional bank debit card for KYC compliance

How to Enable Gambling on Revolut (Step by Step)

  1. Open the Revolut app on your phone
  2. Tap Security (padlock icon) in the top right
  3. Select Payment controls
  4. Find Gambling and toggle it ON
  5. Confirm your PIN or biometrics if prompted

This takes under 60 seconds and works immediately. If a deposit still fails after enabling gambling, the casino itself is blocking Revolut — not your app settings.

Why Some Irish Casinos Block Revolut

Revolut is regulated as an e-money institution by the Central Bank of Ireland (not as a traditional bank). Some casino operators' anti-money-laundering (AML) policies require a traditional bank account for KYC purposes — particularly operators that need to verify the source of funds easily. When a casino declines Revolut, it is usually this reason, not your individual account.

Operators most likely to decline Revolut are those requiring same-name bank account withdrawals or those processing under stricter jurisdictions. If this happens, your AIB, Bank of Ireland, or permanent tsb debit card will almost always work instead.

Revolut's Gambling Limit Feature — Responsible Gambling Tool

One genuine advantage of using Revolut for casino play: the in-app gambling spend limit. Under Payment controls, you can set a monthly cap on gambling transactions. Once you hit the limit, further gambling payments are automatically declined until the next calendar month.

This is separate from — and in addition to — the deposit limits that GRAI-supervised operators are required to offer Irish players. Using both gives you two independent layers of financial control.

Revolut Withdrawals from Irish Casinos

Most casinos process withdrawals to the same card used for the deposit. Revolut Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted for withdrawals at the majority of operators. Typical processing times:

Revolut shows pending transactions in real time, so you will see the incoming transfer before it fully clears.
