Half-Time Result Betting Ireland: Markets, Rules & Tips

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

Half-time result betting focuses on the scoreline at the interval rather than the full-time outcome. You bet on whether the home team, away team or neither team (draw) will be leading after 45 minutes. It is a standard 1X2 market applied to only the first 45 minutes, plus injury time added by the referee at half time.

How half-time markets differ from full-time

Half-time results show a noticeably higher draw rate than full-time results. In the Premier League, matches are level at half time in approximately 35–40% of fixtures, compared to around 24–27% at full time. This makes the draw (HT draw) the single most common half-time result in many leagues, particularly in evenly-matched fixtures. The HT draw is therefore typically priced around 2.0–2.5, reflecting its frequency. HT home win tends to be priced in the range of 2.0–3.0 for even fixtures, with the away HT win at 3.5–6.0.

Injury time and what counts as the half-time result

The half-time result is determined by the score at the official half-time whistle, including any injury time added in the first half. A goal scored in the 45+3 minute (three minutes of first-half injury time) counts for the half-time result. Goals scored after the half-time whistle (whether in warming up, the dressing room, or any delay) do not count. If the referee blows half time before a confirmed goal kick is taken, the half ends at that moment.

Combining half-time and full-time markets

Half-time betting can be combined with full-time results in an HT/FT double. This is a separate market covered elsewhere, but it is worth noting that the two markets are priced independently by most bookmakers, meaning the combined price of a HT selection and a full-time selection can be compared to the HT/FT matrix price to look for marginal differences. Frequently Asked Questions

How common is a half-time draw in football?

Matches are level at half time in approximately 35-40% of Premier League fixtures, making the HT draw the most common single half-time result. This is significantly higher than the full-time draw rate of around 24-27%, reflecting slower starts and fewer goals in the first half compared to the second.

Does first-half injury time count in half-time betting?

Yes. Any goal scored during first-half injury time counts toward the half-time result. The result is determined at the official half-time whistle, including however many minutes of injury time the referee has added.

What is first-half over/under betting?

First-half over/under markets work the same as full-match over/under totals but apply only to goals scored in the first 45 minutes. The most common line is 0.5 goals (over: at least one goal before half time; under: a goalless first half) or 1.5 goals (over: two or more first-half goals).

Are half-time bets available in-play?

Yes. Half-time result markets are available in-play during the first half. As the match progresses, odds update rapidly based on the current score and time remaining. Some punters use early goals as a signal to back the trailing team for a HT draw at higher odds.
