GAA Hurling 2027 Favourites — Championship Contenders Betting Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The All-Ireland Hurling Championship 2027 is expected to feature another tightly contested race for the Liam MacCarthy Cup among a small group of elite counties. This guide covers the likely market leaders heading into 2027 and where the best value may be found.

Limerick's Recent Dominance and the Counter-Narrative

Limerick's sustained excellence in recent seasons has set the bar for hurling championship performance. Any assessment of the 2027 market must start with the question of whether the chasing pack has closed the gap. Counties including Kilkenny, Tipperary, Galway, Clare and Cork have all shown the ability to challenge at the highest level — the question is which of them has taken the step needed to consistently match Limerick's championship intensity.

Key Differentiators in Hurling Markets

  • Munster Championship form: The Munster hurling championship is the most competitive provincial contest in Gaelic games. A county that wins Munster has overcome quality opposition and carries real championship credentials.
  • Leinster Championship form: Kilkenny and Galway are traditional Leinster powers; winning Leinster confirms form at the highest provincial level.
  • Scoring power and accuracy: Hurling is a high-scoring game — counties that maintain their scoring rate under championship pressure tend to go deeper in the competition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Limerick still favourite for the All-Ireland Hurling 2027?

Limerick's market position in 2027 will depend on squad continuity, National Hurling League form and performance in the Munster championship. Specific prices will not be published until early 2027. Limerick's sustained excellence makes them a consistent market reference point.

Which counties challenge Limerick in hurling?

Kilkenny, Tipperary, Galway, Clare and Cork are historically the most competitive All-Ireland hurling counties alongside Limerick. Waterford and Wexford have also shown potential in recent seasons. The championship remains concentrated among a group of approximately eight counties.

What is the difference between the Munster and Leinster hurling championships?

The Munster Hurling Championship features the southern counties (Limerick, Tipperary, Clare, Cork, Waterford, Kerry). The Leinster Hurling Championship features Kilkenny, Galway, Wexford, Dublin, Offaly and others. Both are provincial round-robin or knockout competitions that feed into the All-Ireland series.

Are hurling betting markets as liquid as football markets?

All-Ireland Hurling markets carry good liquidity with GRAI-licensed bookmakers, particularly during Munster and Leinster championship rounds. The All-Ireland outright is widely offered from January. Match betting during the provincial championships is most liquid around game week.
