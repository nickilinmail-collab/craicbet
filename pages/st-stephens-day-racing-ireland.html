St Stephen's Day Racing Ireland 2026: Leopardstown Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

St Stephen's Day (26 December) marks the opening day of the Leopardstown Christmas Festival, one of the most prestigious National Hunt meetings in Ireland. Grade 1 races, top trainers and thousands of racegoers make this one of the highest-betting days of the Irish racing year.

Leopardstown Christmas Festival

The Leopardstown Christmas Festival runs from 26 to 29 December. The opening day on St Stephen's Day typically features several Grade 1 National Hunt races including top novice hurdles and chases that set the form guide for Cheltenham in March. Willie Mullins and Gordon Elliott, the two dominant forces in Irish National Hunt racing, typically send strong teams.

Leopardstown Racecourse is located in south County Dublin, easily accessible from the city centre. Online betting is available for the full card at all GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

Key Races and Markets

  • Grade 1 novice hurdle: This race often features the season's leading novice hurdler ahead of Cheltenham
  • Grade 1 novice chase: New chasers are tested over Christmas fences before the spring festivals
  • Grade 2 and Grade 3 handicap chases and hurdles: Competitive betting races with less predictable outcomes

Betting Tips for St Stephen's Day

Going (ground conditions) at Leopardstown in late December is typically soft or heavy due to winter weather. Horses that handle soft ground are at a significant advantage. Check the going report on the morning of the meeting. Best Odds Guaranteed promotions from GRAI-licensed bookmakers are worth taking for each race.

When is St Stephen's Day racing at Leopardstown?

The Leopardstown Christmas Festival opens on 26 December (St Stephen's Day). Racing takes place over four days from 26 to 29 December.

What grade races are held at the Leopardstown Christmas Festival?

The festival includes multiple Grade 1, Grade 2 and Grade 3 National Hunt races. The Grade 1 novice hurdle and Grade 1 novice chase on St Stephen's Day are the flagship races.

How do I bet on the Leopardstown Christmas Festival online?

Open an account with a GRAI-licensed bookmaker, complete age verification and deposit. Find the Leopardstown card in the horse racing section. Best Odds Guaranteed is widely offered on festival racing.

What is Best Odds Guaranteed and does it apply at Christmas festivals?

Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG) means you are paid the Starting Price if it is higher than the odds you took when placing your bet. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer BOG on all Irish racing including the Christmas festivals.
