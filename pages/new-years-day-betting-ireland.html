New Year's Day Racing in Ireland

Tramore Racecourse in County Waterford traditionally hosts one of Ireland's most popular New Year's Day race meetings. The Tramore New Year's Day fixture is a flat meeting that attracts large crowds and is a social occasion as much as a sporting one. Online betting for the full card is available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

Fairyhouse Racecourse in County Meath and other Irish tracks may also hold New Year's Day fixtures. Check the Horse Racing Ireland (HRI) website for the confirmed fixture list.
