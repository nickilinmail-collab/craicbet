How No Deposit Bonuses Work

No deposit bonuses are simple in concept but have important details you need to understand:

  1. Register an account — Sign up at a casino offering a no deposit bonus. You'll need to provide valid details (name, email, date of birth).
  2. Verify your identity — Most casinos require email verification. Some also require phone verification or ID upload.
  3. Bonus is credited automatically — Free spins or free cash appear in your account. Some require a bonus code — we'll always note this.
  4. Play and try to win — Use your free spins or free cash on eligible games. Any winnings are added to your bonus balance.
  5. Meet wagering requirements — You must wager your winnings a certain number of times (e.g., 30x) before you can withdraw.
  6. Withdraw your winnings — Once wagering is complete, request a withdrawal up to the maximum cashout limit.

Wagering Requirements Explained

Wagering requirements (also called playthrough) are the most important factor in any no deposit bonus. Here's how they work:

📈 Example: 30x Wagering

You claim 50 free spins and win €10. With 30x wagering, you must bet a total of €10 x 30 = €300 before you can withdraw. You don't need to lose €300 — you just need to place €300 in total bets. With normal luck, you might have €5-€15 left after wagering.

Key wagering terms to understand:

  • Wagering multiplier (e.g., 30x): How many times you must bet your winnings. Lower is better. Under 30x is good, 20x is excellent, 40x+ is tough.
  • Max cashout: The maximum amount you can withdraw from no deposit bonus winnings. Typically €50-€200.
  • Game weighting: Slots usually count 100% towards wagering. Table games often count only 10-20%. Live casino may count 0%.
  • Time limit: You'll have 7-30 days to complete wagering. After that, bonus and winnings expire.
  • Max bet during wagering: Usually €5 per spin. Betting more can void your bonus and winnings.

No — that's the whole point. You simply register an account to receive the bonus. However, some casinos require a minimum deposit before you can withdraw winnings from a no deposit bonus. Always check the terms.
What's a good wagering requirement for a no deposit bonus?▾
For no deposit bonuses, anything under 30x is good, and 20x or below is excellent. Most no deposit bonuses have higher wagering than deposit bonuses because the casino is giving you something for free. Avoid anything above 50x — it's nearly impossible to clear.
