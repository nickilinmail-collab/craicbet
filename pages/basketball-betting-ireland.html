NBA Betting: The Main Markets

The NBA runs from October through to the Finals in June. With 30 teams and an 82-game regular season, there is betting available on virtually every day of the year from October to April, plus playoffs from April to June. The volume of fixtures means market depth is excellent for the top-market teams (the most popular franchises nationally and globally), but can be narrower for smaller-market teams.

Spread (handicap) is the most popular NBA betting market. Rather than betting on a team to simply win a game, you bet on whether they will win (or lose) by a specified margin. For example, if the Golden State Warriors are -8.5 against a weaker team, you need the Warriors to win by 9 or more points. Because basketball is a high-scoring sport (totals of 215–230 points are common in an NBA game), the spread can swing by several points late in a game, making in-play betting popular.

Totals (over/under) is the second most popular market. The bookmaker sets a line for the combined score of both teams (e.g. 224.5 total points), and you bet whether the final combined score will be over or under that line. For NBA games, totals are typically set in the 215–235 range depending on the teams and their pace of play. The over/under is a good market for bettors who want to focus on offensive vs. defensive tendencies rather than picking a winner.

Moneyline (match winner): A simple bet on which team wins the game. For closely matched NBA fixtures, the moneyline can offer similar value to the spread. For large mismatches, the spread is typically better value.

NBA Championship Outright Betting

The NBA Finals outright winner market is available from before the season starts and runs through to the conclusion of the playoffs. The outright market is typically led by three to five elite teams (usually including the previous year's finalists and the top Conference regular-season performers). The market sharpens significantly after the All-Star break in February when playoff seeding becomes clearer.

Conference winner markets (Eastern and Western Conference) are also available. These are easier to predict early in the season because team quality within each conference can vary significantly. Backing the top seed in the stronger conference early in the season has historically offered reasonable value compared to the outright Finals market, as it eliminates one round of uncertainty.

Player props: Points, rebounds, and assists markets are available for each game on major platforms. These carry wide margins and are better treated as entertainment than systematic value plays.

Irish Basketball Superleague

The Irish Basketball Superleague (men's and women's) is the domestic top division in Ireland. Coverage from GRAI-licensed bookmakers is variable — some offer match markets for high-profile fixtures while others do not carry the Superleague at all. For fans of Irish domestic basketball, check individual bookmakers for Superleague coverage.

EuroLeague basketball (the top European club competition) is well-covered by most major GRAI-licensed bookmakers, with spread, totals, and match winner markets available for all games. This may be of interest to Irish fans who follow the club game beyond the NBA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bet on the NBA in Ireland?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer extensive NBA coverage including game winner, spread, totals, player props, and outright NBA championship winner markets. All bets must be placed with a GRAI-licensed bookmaker by Irish residents aged 18 and over.

What is spread betting in basketball?

Spread betting gives the weaker team a points head start. If the Lakers are -7.5 against a weaker team, you need the Lakers to win by 8 or more points for a spread bet on the Lakers to win. This is the most popular market for NBA betting in Ireland.

Does the Irish Basketball Superleague have betting markets?

Coverage of the Irish Basketball Superleague varies between bookmakers. Some GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer match markets for high-profile Superleague fixtures. Coverage is narrower than NBA and markets are less liquid.
