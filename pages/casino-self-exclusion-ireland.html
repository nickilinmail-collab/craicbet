Casino Self-Exclusion Ireland — Complete Guide

⚠️ No GRAI casino licence exists in Ireland yet. GRAI issued only remote betting licences from July 2026. Casino licences expected 2027–2028.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Self-exclusion is the most powerful responsible gambling tool — a commitment to block access to gambling for a set period. This guide covers your options as an Irish player and how to use them effectively.

Single-Casino Self-Exclusion

All licensed casinos offer self-exclusion. Contact customer support (or find it in Account Settings → Responsible Gambling → Self-Exclusion). Choose your exclusion period: typically 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, or permanent. During exclusion, you cannot open accounts, deposit, or receive marketing. Permanent exclusion is irreversible.

GAMSTOP (UK Self-Exclusion Scheme)

GAMSTOP is the UK's national multi-operator self-exclusion scheme. Registering at gamstop.co.uk blocks you from all UKGC-licensed online casinos in a single step. It covers all online operators holding a UKGC licence — the most comprehensive single action for UKGC-licensed sites. Irish players can also register, as many Irish-facing casinos hold UKGC licences.

🔒 Licensing: No Irish casino currently holds a GRAI gaming licence. GRAI issued only remote betting licences from 1 July 2026; casino licensing is expected from 2027–2028. Irish players can legally use MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar-licensed casinos.

GRAI and National Self-Exclusion

GRAI is developing a national self-exclusion register for Ireland, to be launched when casino gaming licences are issued (expected 2027–2028). This will allow Irish players to self-exclude from all GRAI-licensed operators with a single registration. Until then, Irish players should use GAMSTOP (for UKGC-licensed casinos) and single-casino exclusion for others.

MGA Self-Exclusion

Malta's MGA operates a national register allowing exclusion from all MGA-licensed operators. Register at rcgregistration.mga.org.mt. This covers many Irish-facing casinos that hold MGA licences.

Practical Steps for Self-Exclusion

  • Use GAMSTOP for UKGC-licensed casinos
  • Use MGA's register for Malta-licensed casinos
  • Self-exclude directly at each remaining casino
  • Block gambling sites via your ISP or apps like Gamban
  • Consider blocking gambling payment processing via your bank
