Irish Gold Cup 2027 Betting Guide — The Premier Cheltenham Gold Cup Trial

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Irish Gold Cup is a Grade 1 three-mile chase at Leopardstown, run at the Dublin Racing Festival in early February. It is widely regarded as the definitive trial for the Cheltenham Gold Cup and regularly shapes the spring market. This guide covers the race, its relationship to Cheltenham, and how to approach it from a betting perspective.

The Irish Gold Cup: Race Profile

The Irish Gold Cup is a Grade 1 chase run over 3 miles at Leopardstown at the Dublin Racing Festival (early February). It draws the leading Gold Cup contenders from both Ireland and Britain. Field sizes are typically 6–10 runners — a championship quality contest, not a handicap. The left-handed Leopardstown circuit with its stiff fences in February provides a genuine stamina and jumping test.

Relationship to the Cheltenham Gold Cup

The Irish Gold Cup is considered the most reliable Irish trial for Cheltenham's main event. That said, the two races differ in important ways:

  • Distance: The Irish Gold Cup is 3 miles; the Cheltenham Gold Cup is 3m 2f. The extra distance at Cheltenham, combined with the uphill finish, is a significant additional test.
  • Course character: Leopardstown is flat and left-handed; Cheltenham is undulating and right-handed. Some horses perform significantly better or worse when the course switches.
  • Going: February at Leopardstown is usually soft or yielding; March at Cheltenham can range from good to heavy. Ground-sensitive horses may show very different form.

Ante-Post Around the Irish Gold Cup

The post-Irish Gold Cup window (from mid-February to early March) is the last significant ante-post opportunity before Cheltenham. A dominant win here often triggers a 30–50% price reduction in the Cheltenham Gold Cup ante-post market. If you are holding a selection that runs well, the resulting price compression confirms its value. If a selection underperforms, the Irish Gold Cup also provides the clearest signal to review your position.

Each-Way Betting

With a typical field of 6–10 runners, each-way terms are likely to be 3 places at 1/4 odds, or possibly 1/5 odds in a very small field. Always confirm terms before placing. In small championship fields, each-way value is narrower than in large handicaps.
