World Cup 2026 format and schedule

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico and features an expanded 48-team field for the first time, up from 32. The expanded format introduces a third-place group stage match, with the top two from each of 16 groups progressing to a 32-team knockout round. This longer tournament creates more matches and therefore more betting opportunities per team, particularly in the group stage.

Outright winner markets

World Cup outright winner markets open well before the tournament (sometimes immediately after the previous World Cup) and are updated continuously. The tournament’s historical pattern of a narrow set of winning nations (Brazil, Germany, Italy, Argentina and France have won all but four of the World Cups in the modern era) means the market is concentrated at the top. Ireland did not qualify for World Cup 2026.

Group stage betting strategy

The expanded 48-team format means weaker nations qualify for the World Cup, increasing the probability of group-stage upsets. Each group has three guaranteed matches per team. Total goals, Asian handicap and match result markets are available on every match. BTTS and over/under 2.5 are popular across the group stage given its mix of elite vs second-tier opposition in many groups.

Golden Boot and special markets

The Golden Boot (top scorer) market is popular throughout the tournament. Strikers from elite nations in advantageous groups (guaranteed progression with time to score freely) tend to have an advantage. Special markets including most yellow cards, most corners, highest-scoring day and player of the tournament are offered by most major operators. Frequently Asked Questions

How many teams are in the 2026 World Cup?

The 2026 FIFA World Cup has 48 participating nations, up from 32 at previous tournaments. It is hosted across the United States, Canada and Mexico, with the final at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey.

Did Ireland qualify for the 2026 World Cup?

Ireland did not qualify for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Irish punters will follow the tournament as neutrals, with the most popular markets typically focused on the leading nations and the Golden Boot.

When did World Cup 2026 outright markets open?

World Cup 2026 outright markets opened for major bookmakers in the period following the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, with prices continuously updated through European qualification, South American qualification and the draw for the tournament groups.
