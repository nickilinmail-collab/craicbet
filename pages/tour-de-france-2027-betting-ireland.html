Tour de France 2027 Betting — Irish Punter Guide to the World's Greatest Cycling Race

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Tour de France is the most prestigious cycling race in the world and generates growing betting interest from Irish punters. This guide covers the main markets for the 2027 edition and how to approach Tour de France betting.

Tour de France Format and Betting Structure

The Tour de France runs over 21 stages across approximately three weeks in July. The overall winner is determined by accumulated time, with bonuses for stage wins. For betting purposes, the main markets are: outright winner (the overall GC — General Classification — winner), stage winner (each of the 21 stages is a separate bet), sprint points classification, mountains classification, and best young rider.

Main Markets for 2027

  • GC outright winner: The headline market — back a rider to win the overall Tour. This market is dominated by a small number of elite stage racers capable of winning multiple Grand Tours.
  • Stage winner: Individual stage betting is available each morning. Flat stages suit sprinters; mountain stages suit climbers; time trials suit specialists. Good form and local knowledge can provide value.
  • Top sprinter / top climber: Points classification (green jersey) and mountains classification (polka dot jersey) outright markets run alongside the GC.
  • To abandon: Some bookmakers offer markets on whether specific riders will complete the tour — high attrition rate makes this a speculative but interesting market.

Irish Interest in the Tour

Irish cycling has a growing international presence. Any Irish or Irish-based riders contesting the Tour de France 2027 will attract Irish punter interest in both team and individual markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Tour de France 2027 start?

The Tour de France typically starts in late June or early July, running until the final Sunday of July on the Champs-Elysees in Paris. The 2027 start city will be announced by the Tour organisers (ASO) in 2026. Exact stage dates will be published at the official route reveal.

Is Tour de France betting popular in Ireland?

Tour de France betting has grown significantly in Ireland over the past decade. The three-week race provides daily betting opportunities for punters who follow cycling, and the outright winner market attracts interest from sports bettors who prefer longer-term event wagering.

What is a Grand Tour in cycling?

The three Grand Tours are the Tour de France (France, July), Giro d'Italia (Italy, May) and Vuelta a Espana (Spain, August-September). Each is a 21-stage, three-week race across mountainous terrain. The Tour de France is the most prestigious and highest-profile of the three.
