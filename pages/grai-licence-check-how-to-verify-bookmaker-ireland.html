How to Check a Bookmaker's GRAI Licence in Ireland

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | Gambling Awareness Trust | 1800 753 753

Since 1 July 2026, all bookmakers targeting Irish customers must hold a GRAI remote betting licence. Here's how to verify any site in under 2 minutes using the GRAI public register.

Step 1: Find the legal company name

Brand names and legal company names differ. '888sport' may be operated by '888 IE Limited'. The GRAI register is organised by company name, not brand. Find the legal name in the site's footer — typically something like 'Operated by [Company] Ltd' or in the Terms & Conditions under 'Licensing'.

Step 2: Search the GRAI register

Go to grai.ie and find the public register section. Search by the company name you found in Step 1. The register shows: licence number, company name, licence type (Remote Betting), issue date, and expiry date.

Step 3: Read the licence number format

GRAI licence numbers follow the format: GRAI-XXXX-RB-YY-ZZZZ where RB = Remote Betting, YY = year (26 = 2026). Our partner holds licence GRAI-0191-RB-26-0001. If a site displays a licence number that doesn't match this format, treat it with caution.

What about Revenue Commissioners permissions?

During the transition period from the old Betting Acts, some operators hold Revenue remote bookmaker permissions rather than a GRAI licence. These are valid while they remain unexpired. Revenue publishes the list at revenue.ie. GRAI and Revenue permissions are both legitimate during transition.

Spotting fake licence claims

Warning signs: a licence number displayed on a website that doesn't appear on the actual GRAI register; claims of a 'GRAI casino licence' (casino licences don't exist yet, expected 2027-2028); no licence number at all, only a vague 'licensed and regulated' claim. When in doubt, check the register directly — don't take the site's word for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I check if a betting site is GRAI licensed?
Go to grai.ie and search the public register by the operator's legal company name. All valid GRAI remote betting licences are listed there.
What does a GRAI licence number look like?
Format: GRAI-XXXX-RB-YY-ZZZZ. For example, GRAI-0191-RB-26-0001. The RB indicates Remote Betting, 26 indicates 2026.
Can a site have a GRAI casino licence?
No. GRAI casino licences don't exist yet — they are expected in 2027-2028. Any site claiming a GRAI casino licence is making a false claim.
What if the site's licence doesn't appear on the GRAI register?
Either they hold a Revenue Commissioners permission (check revenue.ie) or they are not licensed for Ireland. If neither register shows them, they are operating without a valid Irish licence.
Is a Revenue remote bookmaker permission the same as a GRAI licence?
No, but both are legally valid during the transition period. Revenue permissions were issued under the old Betting Acts. GRAI licences are issued under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. All operators should eventually migrate to GRAI licences.
