Can You Use Apple Pay for Betting in Ireland?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Apple Pay is accepted for deposits at a growing number of GRAI-licensed Irish betting sites, provided it is linked to a debit card or current account — not a credit card. Here is everything you need to know about using Apple Pay to bet in Ireland in 2026.

Apple Pay for Deposits: How It Works

Apple Pay for betting works through the standard card deposit flow on mobile. When you tap “Deposit,” select the card/wallet option, and choose Apple Pay if available. You authenticate with Face ID or Touch ID and the deposit is instant. No manual card number entry required.

The deposit is processed against the underlying card in your Apple Wallet. If that card is a debit card or current account, the deposit is fully GRAI-compliant. If it is a credit card, the deposit will be blocked — you need to add a debit card to Apple Pay first.

Which GRAI-Licensed Bookmakers Accept Apple Pay?

Apple Pay availability varies between operators. It is more commonly available on mobile apps than on desktop browser sites. As a quick check: open the deposit page on the iOS app and see if Apple Pay appears as an option. The list of accepting operators changes as new GRAI licences are issued and existing operators upgrade their payments infrastructure.

Apple Pay for Withdrawals

Apple Pay withdrawals are less common than Apple Pay deposits. Many operators that accept Apple Pay deposits process withdrawals back to the underlying card (Visa Debit or Mastercard Debit) rather than directly to Apple Pay. This takes the standard 1–3 business days for card withdrawals.

Apple Pay Spending Limits

Apple Pay itself does not impose specific limits on gambling deposits beyond your card’s standard limits. Your bank may have contactless or Apple Pay daily limits (often €500–€5,000 per day). Check your bank’s Apple Pay limits if you plan to make larger deposits.

How to Set Up Apple Pay for Betting in Ireland

  1. Add your debit card to Apple Wallet: Open Wallet app → tap + → follow prompts to add your Visa or Mastercard debit card. Credit cards are banned from gambling payments in Ireland.
  2. Open the betting site in Safari or the app: Apple Pay works best through Safari on iOS or the operator's native app.
  3. Select Apple Pay at checkout: On the deposit page, choose Apple Pay from payment options. It appears if the site supports it.
  4. Authenticate with Face ID / Touch ID: Confirm the payment. Done — instant deposit.

If Apple Pay does not appear as an option, the betting site does not yet support it. Use Revolut or your debit card directly instead.

Can you use Apple Pay to deposit at Irish betting sites?

Yes — Apple Pay is accepted at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers when linked to a debit card. If linked to a credit card, the deposit will be declined (credit cards are banned under GRAI rules).

Does Apple Pay count as a credit card for betting?

No — Apple Pay is a payment method, not a card type. The underlying funding source matters: debit card = allowed, credit card = blocked. Add a debit card or current account to Apple Wallet.

Can I withdraw to Apple Pay from an Irish betting site?

Less common than deposits. Most operators that accept Apple Pay deposits process withdrawals back to the underlying debit card, not directly to Apple Pay. Processing takes 1–3 business days.

Which Irish betting sites accept Apple Pay?

Availability varies and changes as operators update payment systems. Open the deposit page on the iOS app of any GRAI-licensed operator to check if Apple Pay appears as an option.

Are there limits on Apple Pay betting deposits?

Apple Pay itself has no specific gambling limits. Your bank may impose daily Apple Pay spending limits (typically €500–€5,000). Check with your bank for your specific limit.
