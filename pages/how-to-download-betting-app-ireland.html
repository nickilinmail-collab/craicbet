How to Download an Irish Betting App: Step-by-Step Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Downloading a betting app from a GRAI-licensed Irish operator is straightforward, though the process differs between iOS and Android. Some apps are on the App Store; others require a direct download or use a Progressive Web App approach. Here is a step-by-step guide for both platforms.

iOS: App Store Method

  1. Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Search for the operator by name (e.g., their brand name)
  3. Check the developer name matches the operator’s legal entity (visible under the app title)
  4. Tap Get/Download and wait for installation
  5. Open the app, log in or register, and complete KYC verification before depositing

If the app is not on the App Store, the operator likely uses a Progressive Web App (PWA). Visit their mobile site via Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen.”

Android: Google Play and Direct APK

  1. Try the Google Play Store first by searching the operator name
  2. If not on Google Play, visit the operator’s website on your Android device and look for “Download Android App”
  3. Before downloading an APK, go to Settings > Security > Unknown Sources and allow installation from that source
  4. Tap the downloaded APK file to install
  5. Re-disable Unknown Sources after installation for security

Only download APKs directly from the operator’s official GRAI-licensed website. Never download betting apps from third-party APK sites — these can contain malware.

Verifying You Have the Official App

Always verify: the app developer matches the operator’s legal entity name; the website you downloaded the APK from matches the GRAI-registered operator. If in doubt, start from grai.ie, find the operator’s registered website, and navigate directly from there.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download an Irish betting app?

On iOS: search the App Store or add via Safari 'Add to Home Screen'. On Android: check Google Play or download the APK directly from the operator's official website.

Why isn't my betting app on the App Store?

Apple requires gambling apps to have country-specific approval. Some operators skip this and use Progressive Web Apps (add to home screen via Safari) instead.

Is it safe to download a betting app APK directly?

Only if downloading from the operator's official GRAI-registered website. Never download from third-party APK sites — these may contain malware. Enable Unknown Sources temporarily, install, then disable.

How do I verify an app is from a genuine GRAI-licensed operator?

Check the developer/publisher name matches the operator's legal entity. Start from grai.ie to find the operator's official website and navigate directly from there.

Can I have multiple betting apps on my phone?

Yes — you can install and use apps from as many GRAI-licensed bookmakers as you like. Having multiple apps is common practice for comparing odds.
