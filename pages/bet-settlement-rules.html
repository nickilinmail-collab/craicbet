Bet Settlement Rules: Voids, Abandonments and Walkovers Explained

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

What happens when a match is abandoned?

When a sporting event is abandoned before completion, standard industry practice is to void all bets on that event — stakes are returned to your account. The specific settlement rules depend on the sport, the stage at which abandonment occurs, and the individual operator’s terms. Football matches abandoned after kick-off but before 90 minutes may be settled as void or at the score at the time of abandonment, depending on the operator’s stated terms. Always check these before placing a bet on any match with weather or safety uncertainty.

Postponed matches and bet carry-over

Postponed matches (rescheduled before they begin) are typically carried over by operators to the new date, without voiding. If you placed a pre-match bet on a match later postponed, the bet usually remains valid for the rescheduled fixture. If the rescheduled date falls outside a specified window (commonly 48-72 hours), the operator may void the bet and return the stake. This is particularly relevant for horse racing, where late withdrawals can trigger Rule 4 deductions rather than voids.

Walkovers and conceded matches

In some competitions (particularly knockout cups and tennis), a team or player may withdraw without competing — a walkover. Settlement on walkover results varies: in tennis, if a player retires during a match, some operators settle on the result at the time of retirement; others void in-match bets. For pre-match outright bets where a player or team withdraws without entering the competition, the standard practice is to void and return stakes.

Where to find settlement rules before betting

Every GRAI-licensed operator must publish its settlement terms clearly in its terms and conditions. For specific markets (each-way race terms, void race rules, abandoned match policy), check the market rules before placing. Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my bet if a football match is abandoned?

Standard industry practice is to void bets on an abandoned match if it was not completed according to the sport’s governing body. Operator-specific terms may differ — some settle at the score at abandonment; others void all bets. Check your operator’s terms before betting on matches with uncertain completion risk.

What is a Rule 4 deduction in horse racing?

Rule 4 deductions apply in horse racing when a non-runner withdraws after the market opens but before the off. The deduction reduces winning payouts by a percentage based on the withdrawn horse’s starting price. It is applied to protect the bookmaker from winnings inflated by the reduced field, and is a standard industry practice not specific to Irish bookmakers.

Can I dispute a bet settlement decision in Ireland?

Yes. GRAI-licensed operators must have an internal complaints process. If your complaint is unresolved, you can escalate to GRAI under the dispute resolution mechanism in the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Keep records of your bet, the event, and any operator communications.
