Six Nations 2027 Winner Betting — Outright Championship Guide for Ireland

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Six Nations Championship is one of Ireland's most watched sporting events and among the biggest betting fixtures in Irish sport. This guide covers the outright winner market for the 2027 tournament, when to back and how the competition typically unfolds for Irish punters.

Six Nations 2027: The Competition Format

The Six Nations Championship runs across five rounds in February and March 2027, with each of the six nations (Ireland, England, France, Scotland, Wales, Italy) playing five matches — one home and one away against the other five. The champion is determined by points (four for a win, two for a draw, zero for a loss), with points difference as the tiebreaker. A Grand Slam (five wins from five) is the highest achievement.

Outright Winner Market Structure

The outright winner market opens in autumn, shortly after the autumn internationals in November 2026. Prices at this stage reflect squad quality, recent form in internationals and historical performance. Ireland, England and France have been the most competitive nations in recent seasons and typically open as joint or co-favourites. Scotland and Wales carry longer prices; Italy longer still.

When to Bet on the Six Nations Winner

  • Post-autumn internationals: November 2026 provides the most recent form data before markets open fully. Teams that performed well in autumn tests (against Southern Hemisphere opposition) carry confidence into the Six Nations.
  • During the tournament: Prices shift dramatically after each round. A team that wins their first two rounds convincingly shortens significantly — backing them before Round 1 is typically better value.
  • Round 3 onwards: The title race typically clarifies after Round 3 when unbeaten teams face each other in the most critical fixtures.

Six Nations Outright Betting Strategy

The Six Nations runs February–March 2027. Outright winner markets open in the autumn and odds shorten significantly once squads are named and form becomes clear. Ireland and France have dominated since 2022 and typically open as co-favourites around 2/1–5/2 each. England offer each-way value as a third option in most ante-post markets.

  • Grand Slam market: shorter odds than the outright winner; any slip in one match eliminates that nation
  • Top points scorer / try scorer: popular long ante-post markets that open months before
  • Match betting: available from about 10 days before each round; home advantage is significant in rugby
When does the Six Nations 2027 start?

The Six Nations 2027 is expected to start in the first weekend of February 2027, running across five rounds to late March. Exact fixtures and round dates will be confirmed by World Rugby and the Six Nations board in autumn 2026.

Which team is favourite to win the Six Nations 2027?

Pre-season prices will be published in autumn 2026 following the autumn internationals. Ireland, England and France have been the dominant nations in recent seasons and are expected to open as joint favourites. No selections are specific tips — always bet based on your own research.

Is each-way betting available on the Six Nations?

Some bookmakers offer each-way terms on the Six Nations outright — typically 2 places (top two or joint champion). Check with your specific bookmaker as terms vary. The small six-team field limits each-way value compared to larger-field competitions.

What is the Grand Slam in the Six Nations?

A Grand Slam is achieved when a nation wins all five of their Six Nations matches. It is the highest individual achievement in the Championship. Grand Slam markets typically offer longer odds than the outright winner market, as not every champion wins all five games.
