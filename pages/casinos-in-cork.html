The Short Version

Cork has a small but real live-gaming scene. Like everywhere in Ireland, there is no casino licence category under current law, so venues operate as private members' clubs — join free at the door with photo ID. The scene is far smaller than Dublin's: a couple of clubs, card-room poker, and slots arcades.
