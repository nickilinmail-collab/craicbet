Each Way vs Win Bet in Horse Racing Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

An each-way (EW) bet is two bets in one: a win bet and a place bet of equal stakes. It costs twice your stated stake. A win-only bet is a single bet on the horse to win. Knowing when each-way offers better value than win-only requires understanding place terms and implied probability.

Each Way vs Win: Worked Example at 10/1

Stake: €10 each-way = €20 total (€10 win + €10 place). Field: 12 runners (1/4 odds, 4 places).

  • Horse wins: Win part returns €110 (€100 profit + €10 stake). Place part returns €35 (10/1 × 1/4 = 2.5/1 → €25 profit + €10). Total: €145 on €20 staked.
  • Horse places (not wins): Win part lost (€10). Place part returns €35. Net return: €25 on €20 staked (€5 profit).
  • Horse not placed: Both parts lost. Total loss: €20.

When Each Way Is Better Value Than Win Only

EW is better value when: the horse is at 5/1 or above (place return justifies double stake); the race has 12+ runners and 1/4 odds, 4 places; the horse is likely to run on and place even if not winning. EW is poor value at short prices (below 4/1) because the place return is very small relative to the double stake risk.

Each way bet vs win bet: when is each way worth it in Ireland

An each way (EW) bet consists of two equal stakes: one on the selection to win, one on the selection to place (finish in the specified positions). The place part is paid at a fraction of the win odds. EW betting offers value when the place odds represent genuine value relative to the selection's probability of placing — this depends on the field size and the EW terms offered.

Each way terms: what does 1/4 odds places 1-2-3-4 mean

The EW terms "1/4 odds, places 1–2–3–4" mean: the place part of your bet pays one quarter of the win price if your selection finishes in positions 1, 2, 3 or 4. Example: selection at 20/1 (21.0 decimal). EW terms: 1/4 odds, 4 places. €5 each way = €10 total stake (€5 win + €5 place).

  • If wins: win part returns €5 × 21.0 = €105. Place part returns €5 × (20 × 0.25 + 1) = €5 × 6.0 = €30. Total: €135, profit €125
  • If places 2nd/3rd/4th: only place part returns. €5 × 6.0 = €30. Loss on win part (€5). Net: +€20, profit €10
  • If falls outside places: entire €10 stake lost
Can I place each-way bets on Irish racing online?

Yes — all major GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer each-way betting on Irish and UK horse racing. EW terms are set by the bookmaker and displayed next to the race before you place your bet.
