How to Complain to the GRAI About a Gambling Operator

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

First: Does the GRAI Cover Your Problem?

The GRAI complaints function covers operators it licenses — since July 2026, that means remote betting operators on the official register. If your problem is with an offshore-licensed casino, the GRAI cannot order it to pay you; skip to the last section.

Step 1: Complain to the Operator First

Regulators everywhere expect you to exhaust the internal complaints process first. Put it in writing (live chat transcripts count — save them), state what you want, and give a reasonable timeframe to respond. Keep every email, screenshot and transaction record.

Step 2: Escalate to the GRAI

If the operator stonewalls or rejects your complaint, escalate to the regulator. The public contact route is GRAI@justice.ie and the contact section of grai.ie. Include: the operator name and licence number (from the register), your account details, a timeline of events, which rule you believe was broken, and your evidence. The enforcement and complaints provisions have been in force since 5 February 2026.

What the GRAI Can Do

The regulator can investigate, impose conditions, and apply sanctions up to €20m. Be realistic about the mechanism: the GRAI punishes rule-breaking and forces systemic fixes; it is not a small-claims court that awards you money directly — though an operator under regulatory pressure usually finds a way to settle legitimate player claims quickly.

If the Casino Is Offshore-Licensed

Complain to its regulator instead: the MGA (Malta) runs a Player Support Unit; the Curacao CGA accepts complaints under the 2024 LOK framework. Quality varies — which is exactly why checking the licence before you deposit matters more than any complaint afterwards. For gambling harm rather than a dispute, contact the Gambling Awareness Trust at 1800 753 753 or gamblingcare.ie.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact the GRAI?

Via GRAI@justice.ie or the contact section of grai.ie. Include the operator name, licence number, a timeline and your evidence.

Can the GRAI get my money back?

It sanctions operators rather than awarding compensation directly — but regulatory pressure on a licensed operator usually resolves legitimate payment disputes.

Can I complain to the GRAI about an offshore casino?

No — it handles operators it licenses. For offshore casinos, complain to their own regulator (such as the MGA in Malta).
