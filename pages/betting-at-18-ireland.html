Betting at 18 in Ireland: Everything You Need to Know

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

The minimum legal age to bet in Ireland is 18. GRAI-licensed bookmakers are legally required to verify your age before allowing any betting or deposit activity. This is one of the strictest requirements in the GRAI framework. Here is what you need to know.

Minimum Age: 18 Across All Gambling in Ireland

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, the minimum age for all gambling in Ireland is 18. This applies to online betting, casino games, in-person betting shops, and scratch cards. There are no exceptions for specific gambling types.

Attempting to open a betting account under 18 is a legal offence. For the operator, allowing underage betting is a serious licence breach that can result in fines and loss of their GRAI licence.

How GRAI-Licensed Operators Verify Age

Operators use several methods to verify that customers are 18+:

  • Document verification: Passport, national ID card, or driving licence submitted as a photo
  • Credit reference agency checks: Automated checks against databases (similar to a soft credit check — does not affect your credit score) to confirm age
  • Automated ID scanning: Machine-learning systems that read document photos and verify they are genuine

Most GRAI-licensed operators now require age verification before you can deposit, not just before your first withdrawal.

What Happens if an Underage Account is Discovered

Under GRAI rules, if an operator discovers that a customer was underage when they opened their account, the operator must: void all bets placed during the period, return all deposits made, and close the account. The operator cannot keep any winnings generated by an underage customer — they must be returned.

Turning 18 With an Existing Account

If someone opened an account claiming to be 18 but was under age, and subsequently turns 18, the operator must still take the action above when the breach is discovered. Some operators allow customers to “upgrade” their status upon turning 18 if they can show they are now of age, but this is at the operator’s discretion.

What 18-Year-Olds Should Know About Responsible Gambling

  • Deposit limits exist: Set them from day one. Most operators allow you to set daily, weekly and monthly deposit limits in account settings. These can only be tightened immediately but require a cooling-off period to increase — by design.
  • Keep records: Track your bets in a spreadsheet or notebook. Many punters overestimate their wins and underestimate their losses without records.
  • Gambling Awareness Trust Ireland: Freephone 1800 753 753, gamblingcare.ie. Free, confidential support if gambling becomes a problem. Worth knowing before you need it.
  • GRAI National Exclusion Register: If gambling becomes difficult to control, self-exclusion from all GRAI-licensed operators can be requested via the regulator.

Can I Bet Online in Ireland Under 18

No. Online betting is strictly 18+ in Ireland. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and GRAI licensing conditions require all operators to verify that customers are at least 18 before allowing any deposit or bet. Operators must complete age verification — usually by checking a passport, driving licence, or public records database — before the account is approved. Attempting to bet underage using a parent's or sibling's account is a breach of operator terms and can result in permanent account closure and forfeit of all funds.

If you are under 18 and experiencing gambling urges, Gambling Awareness Trust Ireland offers confidential support: freephone 1800 753 753.

Can a 17-year-old open a betting account in Ireland?

No. Opening an account while under 18 is an offence. GRAI-licensed operators are required to verify age and refuse accounts for anyone under 18.

Does age verification affect my credit score?

No. The automated age checks used by betting sites are soft checks that do not appear on your credit file and do not affect your credit score.
