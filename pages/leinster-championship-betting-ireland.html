Leinster Championship Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Leinster GAA Championship covers both football and hurling, with the hurling competition expanded after Galway joined the province in 2009. The Leinster football championship is one of the most followed provincial competitions, with Croke Park hosting the semi-finals and final.

Leinster Football

Leinster football has historically been dominated by Dublin, though the province has become more competitive in recent years. Meath, Kildare, Louth, Westmeath and Wexford all compete with varying degrees of success. Dublin’s strength means they are typically short-priced outright favourites, with value often found in backing Dublin to win at a price or in match handicap markets for their games.

Leinster Hurling

Galway’s switch to Leinster hurling transformed the provincial competition. Kilkenny remain the standard setters but Galway, Wexford, Dublin and Laois provide real competition. The Leinster hurling round-robin sees all counties play each other before semi-finals and a final at Croke Park.

Betting Markets

Outright Leinster championship markets are available year-round at GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Match betting opens one to two weeks before each fixture. The Leinster finals, held at Croke Park, attract large-volume betting and have in-play markets at all major operators.

Who are the favourites for the Leinster football championship?

Dublin are historically the dominant force and usually enter the championship as short-priced favourites. Check the latest odds at a GRAI-licensed bookmaker for current prices and form.

Does Galway compete in Leinster hurling?

Yes. Galway joined the Leinster hurling championship in 2009. They compete in the full Leinster round-robin alongside Kilkenny, Wexford, Dublin and others.

Where is the Leinster GAA final played?

The Leinster football and hurling finals are both played at Croke Park in Dublin.

Are there handicap markets for Leinster championship games?

Yes. Handicap markets are available for games where there is a large gap in county strength. This is particularly common in Leinster football when Dublin face smaller counties.
