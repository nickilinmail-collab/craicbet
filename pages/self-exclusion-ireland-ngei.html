Self-Exclusion Ireland 2026: NGEI, GAMSTOP & How to Take a Break from Betting

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

If gambling is becoming a problem or you simply want to take a break, Ireland now has stronger self-exclusion tools than ever before. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 created the National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGEI) — a single national system that blocks you from all licensed operators at once. This guide explains how the NGEI works, what options are available right now, and what to do if you need help.

The NGEI: Ireland's National Self-Exclusion Register

The National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGEI) is provided for under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and overseen by the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI). It is Ireland's equivalent of the UK's GAMSTOP system: instead of self-excluding from each bookmaker or casino separately, you register once and are blocked from all GRAI-licensed operators simultaneously.

Before this reform, Ireland had no centralised exclusion register at all. Players had to contact each operator individually, with no consistency. The NGEI closes that gap.

Current Status (November 2026): The NGEI is being rolled out on a phased basis under GRAI's licensing regime, which commenced 1 July 2026. Check grai.ie for the latest launch date and registration process.

How the NGEI Works

Based on the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and GRAI's published strategy:

  • Apply once centrally — submit a self-exclusion through the official GRAI process, not through each operator individually
  • Blocks all licensed operators — every GRAI-licensed remote betting and gaming operator must honour the exclusion
  • Marketing blocked — operators cannot send promotional material to a registered person
  • Data restriction — operators cannot use your data for marketing during exclusion
  • Choose a duration — options expected to range from months to permanent
  • No workarounds — the legal obligation falls on operators

Your Self-Exclusion Options Right Now

1. Contact each operator directly

Every GRAI-licensed bookmaker must provide self-exclusion. Go to the responsible gambling section of your account or contact customer support. Immediate, but only covers that one operator.

2. GAMSTOP (for UK-licensed sites)

If you use bookmakers with a UK Gambling Commission licence, GAMSTOP blocks you from all UKGC-licensed operators at once. Note: from 1 July 2026, operators serving Irish customers require a GRAI licence — many GRAI-licensed bookmakers do not hold a UKGC licence, so GAMSTOP does not cover the full Irish market.

3. Gambling Awareness Trust helpline

Freephone 1800 753 753 (available daily). Advisers can help you self-exclude and connect you with treatment services. gamblingcare.ie

Every GRAI-licensed operator must offer: deposit limits (daily, weekly, monthly); reality checks; session time limits; and cool-off periods. These can usually be set in account settings immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NGEI in Ireland?

The NGEI (National Gambling Exclusion Register) is provided for under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and overseen by the GRAI. It lets you self-exclude from all GRAI-licensed operators in one action.

Is the NGEI live yet?

The NGEI is rolling out under GRAI's phased regime, which began 1 July 2026. Check grai.ie for the current launch status.

Does GAMSTOP work in Ireland?

GAMSTOP covers UK Gambling Commission licensed operators. From 1 July 2026, Irish-facing operators require a GRAI licence. Many GRAI-licensed bookmakers do not hold a UKGC licence, so GAMSTOP will not block them. The NGEI is the Irish equivalent.

How do I self-exclude from a bookmaker right now?

Go to the responsible gambling or account settings section of your bookmaker account and request self-exclusion, or contact customer support directly. Every GRAI-licensed operator must provide this. It is immediate but only covers that one brand.

Who do I call for gambling help in Ireland?

Contact the Gambling Awareness Trust: 1800 753 753 (freephone, available daily). gamblingcare.ie. Free, confidential, no need to be in crisis.
