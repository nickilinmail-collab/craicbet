Who Are Treaty United?

Treaty United Football Club are based in Limerick city and play home matches at the Markets Field, a historic football ground in Limerick that has been renovated to serve as the base for the club's League of Ireland ambitions. The club's name references Limerick's historical connection to the Treaty of Limerick.

Treaty United represent Limerick in the League of Ireland structure, filling the gap left by the collapse of Limerick FC in 2019. The club has worked quickly to establish itself and move up through the LOI divisions, gaining promotion to the First Division and targeting a return to the Premier Division.

For bettors, Treaty United are a developing LOI club whose trajectory and ambition are clear. In their current division, they aim to compete at the top end of the table. As they grow, so too does their profile in bookmaker markets.

Treaty United in the 2026 LOI Season

The 2026 campaign has seen Treaty United working hard to consolidate their position in the League of Ireland. The Markets Field provides a home venue with genuine character — a ground that connects the club to Limerick's football history.

Away fixtures take Treaty United across the country, and the experience of competing at grounds from Donegal to Cork adds to the development of the squad and the club's understanding of what it takes to compete at the top levels of domestic Irish football.

Cup competitions offer Treaty United opportunities to test themselves against higher-division opposition, and their FAI Cup performances are worth monitoring for punters considering them in knockout betting markets.
