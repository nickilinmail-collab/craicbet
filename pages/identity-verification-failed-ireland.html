KYC Identity Verification Failed on an Irish Betting Site: Fix Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification failures are common, but most are fixable. The issue is usually a document quality problem, an address mismatch, or an expired document rather than a fundamental problem with your identity. Here is how to diagnose and resolve a failed verification at a GRAI-licensed Irish bookmaker.

Common Reasons KYC Fails

  • Blurry or cropped photo: The most common cause. Automated systems reject images where text is not clearly readable or document corners are cut off.
  • Expired document: An expired driving licence or passport is not accepted. Use a current, valid document.
  • Address mismatch: Your proof of address does not match the address on your account. Update your registered address first.
  • Document older than 3 months: Utility bills and bank statements used for proof of address must typically be less than 3 months old.
  • Unusual lighting or glare: Flash glare, shadows, or photographing through plastic covers cause failures.
  • Name mismatch: Your document name must exactly match the name on your betting account (including middle names or initials if used at registration).

What Documents Are Accepted

Identity: Irish passport, Irish driving licence, EU national ID card, UK passport or driving licence. Proof of address: Utility bill (electricity, gas, broadband), bank statement, government letter, Revenue document — all within the past 3 months and showing your full name and registered address.

How to Resubmit

Log into your account and go to the verification section. Take a new photo in good natural light, ensuring all document corners are visible and text is sharp. Use your phone’s standard camera app rather than a scanning app. Submit as a JPEG or PDF. If the portal is not accepting your documents, email them to the KYC team directly (the address is usually given in the rejection email).

Appealing a Rejection

If your documents are genuinely valid but keep being rejected, contact live chat. Explain the situation and ask for manual review. Manual reviews typically take 24–48 hours but resolve most edge cases that automated systems reject incorrectly. Under GRAI rules, you have the right to have your account access fairly assessed — if a valid verification is unreasonably refused, you can escalate to the GRAI.

Why did my identity verification fail on an Irish betting site?

Common causes: blurry photo, expired document, address mismatch, document older than 3 months, or name mismatch. Retake in good light with all corners visible and resubmit.

What ID documents are accepted for KYC in Ireland?

Passport, national ID card, or driving licence for identity. Utility bill, bank statement, or government letter (less than 3 months old) for proof of address.

How do I fix a KYC failure on a betting site?

Identify the specific reason from the rejection message. Retake document photos in natural light, ensure full document is visible, and resubmit. Contact live chat for manual review if automated checks keep failing.

How long does a manual KYC review take?

24–48 hours for most manual reviews. Contact support after 48 hours if no response — they can prioritise your case.

Can I complain to the GRAI about a KYC refusal?

If you have submitted valid documents and the operator unreasonably refuses to verify your account, you can escalate to the GRAI. This is rare — most KYC failures are documentation quality issues.
