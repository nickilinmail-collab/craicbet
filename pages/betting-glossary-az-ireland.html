Complete A-Z Betting Glossary Ireland 2026: Every Term Explained

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 November 2026⏱ 10 min read

Betting has its own language, and Irish punters encounter dozens of terms — many inherited from Victorian racing culture, others from modern financial sports betting. This glossary covers every term you are likely to encounter in an Irish betting shop or on a major betting site, explained in plain English. Use the alphabet navigation to jump to the letter you need.

Jump to: A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · J · L · M · N · O · P · R · S · T · U · V · W · Y

A

Accumulator (Acca)
A single bet combining 4+ selections from different events. All must win for the bet to pay out. Winnings from each leg roll onto the next. See also: Double, Treble.
Acca Insurance
A promotion that refunds your stake (usually as a free bet) if exactly one leg of a qualifying accumulator fails. Conditions apply — typically minimum legs, minimum odds per leg, and refund cap.
Ante-Post
Betting placed before the final declarations for a race (or before a tournament). You lock in an early price. If your selection is withdrawn, your stake is lost.
ATC (Any To Come)
An instruction on a traditional bet slip telling the bookmaker to use any winnings from the first bet to place a specified second bet. Rarely used in modern online betting.

B

Back
To place a bet on a selection to win. The conventional side of a bet, as opposed to laying.
Banker
A selection in a multiple or combination bet that you are very confident about. Used to build accumulator legs around.
Bet Builder (Same Game Multi)
A product that allows multiple selections from the same event to be combined into a single bet. All selections must win.
BOG (Best Odds Guaranteed)
A promotion where the bookmaker pays you the SP if it is higher than the early price you took. Removes the risk of an early price that shortens.
Both Teams to Score (BTTS)
A market paying out if both sides score at least one goal in a football match, regardless of who wins.

C

Cash Out
Settling an open bet before the event finishes, in exchange for a bookmaker-determined amount. The offer is typically below fair theoretical value.
CSF (Computer Straight Forecast)
The official formula used to calculate forecast dividends in horse racing and greyhound racing.
Combination Forecast
A forecast bet covering all possible first-and-second combinations from three or more selections.

D

Dead Heat
When two or more competitors finish in the same position. Payouts are reduced in proportion to the number of selections sharing the position.
Decimal Odds
Odds expressed as a decimal (e.g. 3.5) representing total return per unit staked including the stake. Standard on most online betting sites.
Double
A two-selection accumulator. Both selections must win.
Double Chance
A market covering two of three possible outcomes in a 1X2 football market (home win or draw, or draw or away win, etc.).
Draw No Bet (DNB)
A market where your stake is refunded if the match ends in a draw. Only a win or loss is recognised.
Drift
When a selection's odds lengthen (get bigger), indicating the market or bookmaker believes it is less likely to win than previously priced.

E

Each-Way (EW)
Two bets in one: a win bet and a place bet. The place portion pays at a fraction of the win odds if the selection finishes in a specified number of places. Costs double the stated stake.
Enhanced Odds (Price Boost)
A temporary increase in the available price on a selection above the standard market rate, usually for a limited stake.
Evens (EVS)
Odds of 1/1 — profit equals stake. In decimal odds: 2.0.
Exchange
A betting platform where punters bet against each other (not against a bookmaker). Users can both back and lay selections.

F

Fixed Odds
Betting where the price is agreed and locked in at the time of placing the bet, regardless of price movement before the event.
Forecast
A bet requiring you to predict the first and second place finishers in a race, in correct order.
Fractional Odds
The traditional British and Irish odds format, expressed as a fraction (e.g. 5/1). The numerator is profit per unit of denominator staked.
Free Bet
A token from a bookmaker that can be used as a stake without risking your own money. Usually SNR (Stake Not Returned) — if it wins, you receive the profit only.

G

Going
The official description of ground conditions on a racecourse (firm, good, soft, heavy). Affects horse performance and form assessment.
GRAI
The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland — the statutory body that licenses gambling operators in Ireland under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Fully operative from July 2026.

H

Handicap
A market or race where weaker competitors are given a theoretical advantage (points start, weight allowance) to level the field.
Heinz
A full cover bet on 6 selections: 15 doubles, 20 trebles, 15 four-folds, 6 five-folds and 1 six-fold accumulator (57 bets total).

I

Implied Probability
The percentage chance of an outcome implied by its odds. Calculated as 1 ÷ decimal odds. A 5.0 price implies a 20% chance.
In-Play (In-Running)
Betting placed after an event has started. Prices update continuously based on the current state of the event.

J

Jackpot
A Tote pool bet requiring the winner of each of the first six races at a meeting. Rolls over if no one is successful.
Joint Favourite
When two selections are priced equally as the market leader.

L

Lay Bet
Betting against a selection — acting as the bookmaker. You collect the backer's stake if the selection loses, but pay out at the agreed odds if it wins. Available via betting exchanges.
Liability
In lay betting, the maximum amount you stand to pay out if your selection wins. Calculated as (decimal odds − 1) × backer's stake.
Lucky 15
A full cover bet on 4 selections covering all 15 possible combinations (singles, doubles, trebles and 4-fold).

M

Margin (Overround)
The bookmaker's built-in profit — the amount by which the sum of implied probabilities across all outcomes exceeds 100%. Typically 4–10% on major football markets.
Matched Betting
A technique using free bets and a lay bet on an exchange to cover all outcomes, extracting value from promotions with limited risk. Legal but requires careful calculation.

N

Non-Runner (NR)
A horse declared to run but subsequently withdrawn before the race starts. Triggers Rule 4 deductions for day-of-race bets; ante-post bets typically lose.
NRMB (Non-Runner Money Back)
A promotional offer where your stake is refunded (usually as a free bet) if your selection is withdrawn from a race.

O

Odds-Against
When the potential profit exceeds the stake — e.g. 2/1 or above, decimal odds above 2.0.
Odds-On
When the stake exceeds the potential profit — e.g. 1/2, 4/9. Decimal odds below 2.0.
Overround
See Margin.

P

Patent
A full cover bet on 3 selections: all singles, doubles and a treble (7 bets total). Returns even if only one selection wins.
Placepot
A Tote pool bet requiring a placed horse in each of the first six races at a meeting. Carries over if unsettled.
Place Terms
The fraction of win odds paid for the place portion of an each-way bet, and how many places are paid. Determined by race type and number of runners.

R

Reverse Forecast
A forecast bet covering both possible orderings of two selections (A/B and B/A). Two bets — costs double the stated unit stake.
Rule 4 Deduction
A reduction applied to winnings when a horse is withdrawn after bets have been placed. Percentage based on the SP of the withdrawn horse.

S

SNR (Stake Not Returned)
A free bet type where the face value of the token is not included in winnings if the bet wins. Only the profit is paid.
SP (Starting Price)
The official odds returned for a horse at the start of the race, determined by on-course bookmakers.
Spread Betting
Betting on a variable outcome (total goals, corners, points) where profits and losses vary with how far the result moves from the spread. Can lose more than your stake.
SR (Stake Returned)
A free bet type where the face value is included in winnings. More valuable and less common than SNR.

T

Tote
Pool betting operated by the official Tote (or by licensed operators using Tote pools). Dividends are determined after the event based on how the pool is distributed.
Treble
A three-selection accumulator. All three must win.
Trixie
A full cover bet on 3 selections covering all doubles and the treble (4 bets). Requires 2 winners to return.

U

Under/Over
A market betting on whether a statistical total (goals, corners, points) will be under or over a specified line, e.g. Over 2.5 goals.

V

Void Bet
A bet cancelled and stake returned, typically because the event did not take place or was abandoned before the bet's conditions could be settled.
Value
When a selection's true probability of winning is higher than the bookmaker's implied probability suggests. Betting consistently at value is the theoretical basis for long-term profitability.

W

Wager
Another word for a bet.
Win Only
A bet that pays only if the selection wins outright. Contrast with each-way.

Y

Yankee
A full cover bet on 4 selections covering all doubles, trebles and the 4-fold (11 bets). Requires 2 winners to return something.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'overround' mean in betting?

The overround is the sum of implied probabilities across all outcomes in a market above 100%. A 108% overround means an 8% bookmaker margin. The higher the overround, the more expensive the market is to bet on.

What does 'ante-post' mean?

Ante-post means placing a bet before final declarations for a race. You lock in an early price. If your selection is withdrawn, your stake is lost.

What is 'each-way' in betting?

Each-way is two bets in one: a win bet and a place bet. The place portion pays at a fraction of the win odds. It costs double the stated stake.

What does 'in-play' mean in betting?

In-play (in-running or live betting) means placing a bet after the event has started. Prices update in real time based on the evolving situation.

What is 'SP' in horse racing?

SP stands for Starting Price — the official odds returned for a horse at the start of the race. When you request SP, you receive whatever price is officially declared.
