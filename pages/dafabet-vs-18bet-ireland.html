Licensing Status

Neither Dafabet nor 18Bet currently holds a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Dafabet holds licences from Malta (MGA) and other jurisdictions; 18Bet operates under an MGA or Curaçao licence (check current status). Both accept Irish registrations but cannot offer GRAI statutory protections.

Asian Handicap: Where Dafabet Excels

Dafabet originated in the Asian market and brings superior Asian handicap (AH) depth to European leagues. For football punters who use AH as their primary market, Dafabet’s margins on AH lines are typically 2–4% — tighter than most Western operators. 18Bet offers AH but with less pricing precision on smaller leagues.

Irish Sports Coverage: A Gap

Both operators are designed for a global audience and do not invest specifically in Irish markets. GAA football and hurling are absent or very thinly priced. Irish horse racing is listed but without BOG or the Irish-specific features (non-runner money back, ante-post depth) that Racing Specialist operators offer. For Irish-specific sports, a domestic GRAI operator is significantly better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dafabet available in Ireland?

Dafabet accepts Irish registrations but does not hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Irish players using Dafabet have no GRAI statutory protections.

What is Asian handicap betting?

Asian handicap eliminates the draw outcome by giving one team a head start (e.g., −0.5 goals). It reduces a three-outcome market to two outcomes, with half-ball handicaps eliminating the possibility of a push.

Does 18Bet have a licence for Ireland?

18Bet does not currently hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Check the current licence status before depositing.

Which is better for football: Dafabet or a GRAI-licensed operator?

For Asian handicap depth, Dafabet is competitive. For Irish legal protections, GAA coverage and BOG racing, a GRAI-licensed operator provides more overall value for the typical Irish punter.

Can I use crypto on these sites?

Some operators including 18Bet accept cryptocurrency deposits. GRAI-licensed operators are generally restricted to regulated payment methods; crypto is rare at GRAI-licensed operators.
