Punchestown Festival Betting Ireland 2027 — Five Days, Key Races & Each-Way Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

The Punchestown Festival is Ireland's answer to Cheltenham: five days of top-level National Hunt racing in late April and early May, run over a unique track that rewards jumping ability over raw speed. This guide explains the festival's betting character, how the track affects form, and the value angles that experienced punters target.

When Is Punchestown Festival 2027?

The Punchestown Festival typically runs in late April to early May — the 2027 dates will be confirmed by Horse Racing Ireland during the season, but historically it falls in the last week of April. It follows directly after Cheltenham (March) and the Grand National at Aintree (April), forming the closing chapter of Ireland's National Hunt season.

What Makes Punchestown Different

Punchestown runs a right-handed course with a distinctive natural double bank and stone wall — obstacles found nowhere else in professional racing. This unique terrain means:

  • Jumping matters more than at Cheltenham. Horses that are slick at fences tend to outperform their form ratings here.
  • Fresh horses have an advantage. Several trainers rest their Cheltenham runners and aim specific horses at Punchestown for the first time.
  • The ground matters. Late April can produce firm or yielding going — Punchestown's draw has seen extreme variation. Horses with a record on both ground types are more reliable each-way bets.

Each-Way Betting at Punchestown

Punchestown's championship races (8+ runners) generally pay 4 places at 1/4 odds each-way, matching Cheltenham terms. The Gold Cup equivalent often has competitive each-way markets because it genuinely produces its own result — the Cheltenham Gold Cup winner does not always show up, or shows up past their best.

Ante-Post at Punchestown

Ante-post markets for Punchestown open immediately after Cheltenham. The biggest value window is the 2–3 weeks between the festivals: the market overreacts to Cheltenham form, and horses that ran below their best at Cheltenham (unsuitable ground, travel issues, first run after a break) can be available at generous prices before fresh eyes assess their Punchestown entries.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Punchestown Festival 2027?

The Punchestown Festival typically runs in the last week of April and into early May. The 2027 exact dates will be confirmed by Horse Racing Ireland during the season. It follows Cheltenham (March) and the Grand National (April) as the final major festival of the National Hunt season.

What is the Punchestown Gold Cup and how does it differ from Cheltenham?

The Punchestown Gold Cup is run over 3 miles 1 furlong on Punchestown's right-handed course with its distinctive natural bank obstacles. The Cheltenham Gold Cup winner doesn't always run at Punchestown, and when they do, the different track conditions mean the result is far from guaranteed. The race frequently throws up fresh winners and each-way value.

Is each-way betting good at Punchestown?

Each-way betting suits Punchestown well. Standard terms are 4 places at 1/4 odds for championship races with 8+ runners. Because Punchestown regularly produces results that differ from Cheltenham (fresh horses, different track, varying ground), mid-priced each-way runners can offer genuine value. Use our each-way calculator to check exact returns.

What is the La Touche Cup at Punchestown?

The La Touche Cup is a cross-country race over 4m 2f on Punchestown's famous bank course, which features natural obstacles including stone walls, double banks, and open ditches not seen in conventional jumping. Specialist horses who have run over the course before hold a significant advantage. It is one of the most unique races in the Irish racing calendar.
