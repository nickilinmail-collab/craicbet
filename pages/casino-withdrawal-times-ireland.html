💡 Key Takeaways

  • Card and bank withdrawals add 1–5 business days of banking time on top of casino processing

  • Verify your account before your first withdrawal, not after — it is the single biggest time-saver
  • Published timeframes are operator promises; always check the current terms before you deposit

The Three Clocks of Every Withdrawal

When a casino says “withdrawals in 24 hours”, it usually means one of three different clocks. Understanding which one is quoted saves most of the disappointment:

  1. Pending time — the window between your request and the casino approving it. This is internal review time, and it is where fast and slow casinos differ most.
  2. Processing time — how long the casino takes to actually send the money after approval.
  3. Banking time — how long your bank or wallet provider takes to credit the funds. The casino does not control this clock.

KYC: The Hidden Clock Nobody Warns You About

Every licensed casino must verify your identity before paying out — usually a photo ID, a proof of address and sometimes proof of payment method. If you wait until your first withdrawal to upload documents, you add days to the process at exactly the moment you want speed.

✅ The one habit that fixes most delays

Complete verification right after registering, before you play. A verified account turns a withdrawal into a formality. An unverified one turns it into a document exchange with a support queue.

What Slows Withdrawals Down

  • Active bonuses with unmet wagering — the most common reason a withdrawal is rejected outright. Check wagering progress before requesting.
  • Weekend requests — many payment teams work business days; a Friday-night request may sit until Monday.
  • Withdrawal limits — daily, weekly and monthly caps can split a big win into several payouts over weeks. Read the limits before you deposit, not after you win.
  • Mismatched payment methods — most casinos pay back to the method you deposited with. A deposit by card and a requested payout to a wallet triggers manual review.
  • Document quality — blurry photos and cropped corners restart the KYC clock.

FAQ

Can a casino legally hold my withdrawal for weeks?

Licensed operators must follow their published terms. If the terms say 48-hour processing and weeks pass without a documented reason, that is grounds for a complaint to the operator and then the licensing regulator. Unlicensed operators, by contrast, answer to nobody — which is why the licence check matters before you deposit.

Why did my withdrawal get reversed back to my balance?

Usually either unmet wagering requirements on an active bonus, or a verification request you have not completed. Check your bonus status and your inbox for a document request.

Is there a withdrawal tax in Ireland?

Gambling winnings are generally not subject to income tax for recreational players in Ireland. Tax rules can change and individual circumstances vary — for significant amounts, confirm with a tax professional.
