What is a GRAI licence?
GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) began issuing remote betting licences on 1 July 2026 under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. A remote betting licence (licence type: RB) allows an operator to take bets from Irish customers online. No GRAI casino (gaming) licences have been issued — these are expected in 2027–2028. Until then, Irish-facing casinos operate under international licences (Malta MGA, Gibraltar, Isle of Man, UKGC, Estonian HKL/HKT).

Why GRAI Licences Matter for Irish Punters

Before GRAI, Ireland had no dedicated gambling regulator. Irish customers betting with offshore operators had limited legal recourse. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 changed this — GRAI-licensed operators must:

  • Register customers on Ireland’s National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGER)
  • Follow GRAI advertising rules, including the 9pm watershed
  • Contribute to the Social Impact Fund for problem gambling treatment
  • Handle complaints through GRAI’s dispute resolution process
  • Display responsible gambling tools on their platform

See our full GRAI guide and the daily licence register tracker for more.

Irish-facing casinos legally operate under international licences (typically Malta MGA). GRAI casino licensing is expected to begin in 2027–2028.
