2. Jammin' Jars

A vibrant, fruit-themed cluster-pays slot played on an 8x8 grid. Jam jar wilds bounce around the grid with each cascade, collecting multipliers as they go. 3. Fat Santa

A Christmas-themed slot that has become a perennial favourite during the festive season and beyond. 4. Fat Rabbit

Following the same growing-wild concept as Fat Santa, Fat Rabbit features a rabbit that eats carrot symbols to grow larger across the reels. During the Harvest feature, the rabbit can expand to fill multiple reel positions while acting as a wild, creating massive win potential. The charming farm theme and innovative mechanic make it a standout title. RTP: 96.47%.

5. Wild Swarm

A beautifully designed slot set in a meadow filled with flowers and bees. Wild Swarm's standout feature is the Swarm Mode during free spins, where bees can fill entire reels with sticky wilds. The potential for a grid full of wild symbols creates extreme win potential from what initially appears to be a gentle, nature-themed game. Maximum win: 17,829x. RTP: 96.54%.

Unique Features & Mechanics

Push Gaming is known for creating unique mechanics that feel fresh and innovative rather than iterating on existing ideas:

  • Fat Features: Push Gaming's signature mechanic where a character symbol grows larger by consuming other symbols on the grid. Used in Fat Santa, Fat Rabbit, and Fat Drac, this mechanic creates expanding wilds that can cover enormous portions of the reel set, leading to spectacular wins.
  • Mystery Stacks: Featured prominently in Razor Shark and other titles, mystery symbol stacks reveal their contents one row at a time during gameplay, building anticipation with each cascade. The reveal can show high-value symbols, multipliers, or special features.
  • Cluster Pays Innovation: Jammin' Jars and its sequel pioneered the concept of moving wild multipliers in cluster-pays slots. The jam jar wilds physically move around the grid with each cascade, collecting and combining multipliers in a way that feels dynamic and exciting.
  • Huge Max Wins: Despite their small catalogue, Push Gaming games regularly feature maximum win potentials that rival or exceed those of much larger providers. Razor Shark's 50,000x maximum and similar caps across their portfolio demonstrate their commitment to rewarding gameplay.
  • Atmospheric Design: Every Push Gaming slot feels carefully art-directed, with cohesive visual themes, original soundtracks, and attention to detail that creates genuine atmosphere rather than generic slot aesthetics.
