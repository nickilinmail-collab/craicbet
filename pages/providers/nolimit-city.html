xMechanics Explained

Nolimit City's proprietary xMechanics system is their signature innovation, comprising several interlocking mechanics that appear across their catalogue:

  • xNudge: Wild symbols nudge to fill their entire reel when they land partially visible. Each nudge increases the wild's multiplier by 1x. A wild that nudges three times becomes a 4x multiplier wild, and multiple xNudge wilds on the same spin multiply together for enormous potential payouts.
  • xWays: Mystery symbols that reveal between two and four matching symbols, dynamically expanding the number of ways to win on each spin. An xWays symbol on a reel with three positions effectively becomes a reel with four, five, or six positions, dramatically increasing win combinations.
  • xSplit: Special symbols that split every symbol on the same row into two, doubling the symbols on that row. When combined with xNudge wilds or xWays symbols, xSplit creates exponential growth in win potential.
  • xBomb: Wild bomb symbols that explode to destroy all adjacent symbols while increasing a persistent multiplier. The multiplier does not reset during cascade sequences, building higher with each explosion. This creates a snowball effect where later wins in a sequence are worth dramatically more.
  • xPays: A scatter-pays mechanic where winning combinations can form anywhere on the grid rather than following traditional paylines.
