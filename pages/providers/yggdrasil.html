About Yggdrasil Gaming

Named after the mythical Norse tree that connects the nine worlds, Yggdrasil Gaming has built a reputation for creating some of the most visually stunning online slots in the industry. Founded in Malta in 2013 by Fredrik Elmqvist, a former NetEnt CEO, the company brought a new level of graphical sophistication to online gaming from day one.

With over 100 games in their portfolio, Yggdrasil combines cutting-edge technology with artistic excellence. Their GATI (Game Adaptation Tools & Interface) framework is a groundbreaking technology platform that allows third-party studios to develop and distribute games using Yggdrasil's infrastructure, creating a collaborative ecosystem that benefits players with a wider variety of high-quality content.

Yggdrasil holds licences from the Malta Gaming Authority and the UK Gambling Commission. In 2021, the company was acquired by a major European gaming group, further expanding their reach and resources. Their games are available at many of the top online casinos serving Irish players, known for their exceptional visual quality and innovative bonus features.

Unique Features & Mechanics

Yggdrasil has developed several proprietary mechanics and technology platforms that define their offering:

  • GATI Framework: Yggdrasil's Game Adaptation Tools & Interface is a technology platform that allows third-party game studios (called YG Masters) to create and distribute games using Yggdrasil's regulatory framework, distribution network, and technical infrastructure. This has expanded the range of games available through Yggdrasil's platform significantly.
  • Splitz Mechanic: A proprietary mechanic where special Splitz symbols land on the reels and split into multiple copies of the same symbol. This can dramatically increase the number of matching symbols on the reels, creating larger winning combinations than would be possible on a standard setup.
  • Gigablox: Oversized symbol blocks that can range from 2x2 up to 6x6 in size land on the reels, covering multiple positions with a single symbol. When a high-paying Gigablox lands, it creates instant large winning combinations across multiple paylines.
  • Doublemax: A cascading wins mechanic where the win multiplier doubles with each consecutive cascade. Starting at 1x, the multiplier grows exponentially (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x, etc.), making later wins in a cascade sequence dramatically more valuable.
  • GEM (Golden Enhanced Multiplier): Found in various Yggdrasil titles, this feature enhances multiplier values during specific game states, amplifying win potential during bonus rounds and free spins.
