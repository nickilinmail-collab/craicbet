About Big Time Gaming

Big Time Gaming (BTG) is the Australian studio that changed the online slot industry forever. Founded in Sydney in 2011 by Nik Robinson, a veteran of the land-based casino gaming industry, BTG invented the Megaways mechanic — a dynamic reel system offering up to 117,649 ways to win on every spin. This single innovation has become one of the most widely adopted mechanics in online slot history.

Despite having a catalogue of only around 30 slots, BTG's impact on the industry is immeasurable. They licensed the Megaways mechanic to other providers, spawning hundreds of Megaways titles from studios like Pragmatic Play, Red Tiger, Blueprint Gaming, and more. The term "Megaways" has become synonymous with high-quality, high-volatility slot gameplay.

In 2021, Evolution Gaming Group acquired Big Time Gaming, bringing them into the same family as NetEnt and Nolimit City. BTG holds licences from the Malta Gaming Authority and the UK Gambling Commission, and their games are available at the majority of online casinos serving Irish players.

The Megaways Mechanic Explained

The Megaways mechanic is Big Time Gaming's revolutionary contribution to online slots, and understanding how it works is key to appreciating their games:

  • Dynamic Reels: Instead of fixed symbol positions, each reel displays a random number of symbols on every spin (typically between two and seven). This means the number of ways to win changes with every spin, ranging from 64 ways at minimum to 117,649 ways at maximum on a standard six-reel setup.
  • Cascading Wins: Nearly all Megaways slots use cascading (or tumble) wins. When a winning combination lands, the winning symbols are removed and new symbols fall from above. This cascade continues until no new wins form, allowing multiple wins from a single spin.
  • Win Multipliers: During free spins in most Megaways slots, each cascade increases a win multiplier by 1x. Since cascades can chain together for many consecutive wins, the multiplier can reach substantial levels, dramatically increasing the value of later wins in a sequence.
  • Licensing Model: BTG licenses the Megaways mechanic to other providers, who create their own games using the engine. This has resulted in hundreds of Megaways titles across the industry, including Gonzita's Quest Megaways (NetEnt), Gates of Olympus Megaways (Pragmatic Play), and many more.

Industry Impact

It is difficult to overstate Big Time Gaming's impact on the online slot industry. Before Megaways, slots typically offered fixed paylines or fixed ways-to-win. The dynamic reel system fundamentally changed what was possible, and the mechanic's massive popularity has led to a wave of Megaways titles from providers worldwide.

By licensing the Megaways mechanic rather than keeping it exclusive, BTG ensured that their innovation became an industry standard. This licensing model has generated significant revenue while simultaneously establishing Megaways as one of the most recognised terms in online gaming. For Irish players, this means a huge selection of Megaways games from multiple providers at almost every online casino.
