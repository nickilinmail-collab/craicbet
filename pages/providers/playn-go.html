About Play'n GO

Play'n GO is a Swedish game studio founded in 2005 that has built a reputation for delivering meticulously crafted slot games. Headquartered in Växjö, Sweden, the company takes a quality-first approach, ensuring every title in their catalogue meets exceptional standards for gameplay, art direction, and mathematical design.

With over 300 games in their portfolio, Play'n GO has become one of the most respected names in the online casino industry. They hold licences from the Malta Gaming Authority and the UK Gambling Commission, and their games are certified across more than 30 regulated markets worldwide. The company is known for creating some of the most iconic slot franchises in online gaming history.

Play'n GO's philosophy centres on creating games with depth and replay value rather than rushing to market with volume. Each title receives careful attention to theme, narrative, and mathematical balance, resulting in a catalogue where nearly every game has a distinct identity and dedicated player following.

Top 5 Play'n GO Games in Ireland

1. Book of Dead

The flagship Play'n GO slot and one of the most popular high-volatility games in Ireland. Following the adventurer Rich Wilde into ancient Egyptian tombs, this 5x3, 10-payline slot features expanding symbols during free spins that can fill entire reels. A randomly selected symbol expands to cover all positions whenever it appears, creating the potential for full-screen wins. RTP: 96.21%.

2. Reactoonz

A 7x7 grid slot featuring adorable alien creatures in a cluster-pays format. Matching groups of five or more adjacent symbols create wins, and clearing them allows new symbols to cascade into place. The Gargantoon feature deploys a massive 3x3 wild, while Quantum features add random wilds, transformations, and demolition effects. RTP: 96.51%.

3. Fire Joker

A beautifully simple 3x3, 5-payline classic fruit slot with a modern twist. The Respin of Fire triggers when two reels show matching symbols, giving the third reel a free respin. Landing a full screen of matching symbols activates the Wheel of Multipliers with prizes up to 10x your total win. Perfect for players who love retro-styled slots. RTP: 96.15%.

4. Moon Princess

An anime-inspired grid slot featuring three princess characters, each with her own special power. Love clears a set of symbols, Star adds wilds, and Storm destroys two symbol types. Clearing the entire grid triggers the Free Round with up to 20 free spins and a massive multiplier. Charming design with deep gameplay. RTP: 96.50%.

5. Rich Wilde and the Tome of Madness

The latest chapter in the Rich Wilde series takes players into Lovecraftian horror territory. This 5x5 cluster-pays slot features cascading wins that fill a portal meter, unlocking increasingly powerful Mega Wilds and the Other World free round where special portal effects transform the grid. A brilliant evolution of the franchise. RTP: 96.59%.

Unique Features & Mechanics

Play'n GO has developed several distinctive mechanics and franchise concepts that define their catalogue:

  • Book Series: Inspired by the Book of Dead mechanic, Play'n GO has created an entire series of "Book" slots including Book of Dead, Legacy of Dead, and Cat Wilde and the Eclipse of the Sun God. Each features expanding symbols during free spins and high-volatility gameplay.
  • Rich Wilde Universe: A connected franchise of adventure slots following explorer Rich Wilde (and his daughter Cat Wilde) through different historical settings and mythologies. Each game features unique mechanics while sharing the same adventurous spirit.
  • Grid Slots: Play'n GO excels at non-traditional grid formats. Reactoonz, Moon Princess, and their sequels use cluster-pays mechanics on larger grids, offering a different feel from standard reel-based slots.
  • Cascading Wins: Many Play'n GO titles use cascade mechanics where winning symbols are removed and new ones fall into place, allowing multiple wins from a single spin and building momentum toward bonus features.
  • Charge Meters: Several Play'n GO slots use charge or collection meters that fill with each win, unlocking increasingly powerful features. This creates a sense of progression within each session and rewards sustained play.

Game Quality & Art Direction

Play'n GO is widely regarded as having some of the best art direction in the industry. Their games feature distinctive visual styles that range from the pixel-art charm of Reactoonz to the cinematic polish of the Rich Wilde series. Sound design receives equal attention, with original soundtracks that enhance the theme rather than relying on generic slot music.

The company's focus on high volatility means their games tend to produce less frequent but larger wins, appealing to players who enjoy the thrill of chasing big payouts. Their mathematical models are well-respected among experienced slot players for delivering satisfying gameplay loops.

Mobile Compatibility

All Play'n GO games are developed using HTML5 and are fully optimised for mobile play. The company was an early adopter of mobile-first design, and their games deliver the same high-quality experience on smartphones and tablets as they do on desktop. Touch controls are intuitive, and the games adapt seamlessly to portrait and landscape orientations.
