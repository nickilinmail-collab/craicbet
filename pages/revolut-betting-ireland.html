Revolut Betting Ireland 2026 — Deposits, Withdrawals & GRAI Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Revolut has become one of the most popular payment methods for Irish sports bettors. This guide covers which GRAI-licensed betting sites accept Revolut deposits, why some bookmakers block Revolut transactions, and how to use Revolut to fund a betting account safely in 2026.

Does Revolut Work for Sports Betting in Ireland?

Yes — Revolut works for deposits at most major Irish-facing betting sites. However, Revolut operates a gambling merchant category block that is on by default for some account types. If your first attempted deposit to a bookmaker fails, check your Revolut app under Settings → Security & privacy → Merchant categories → Gambling and toggle it on. This is a Revolut-side control, not a bookmaker restriction.

Revolut and the Irish Credit Card Gambling Ban

Ireland's ban on credit card gambling (in force since 2024) prohibits bookmakers from accepting credit card deposits. Revolut's debit card (the standard card linked to your Revolut balance) is not affected by this ban — it is treated as a debit card by bookmakers, the same as a Visa Debit or Mastercard Debit from a traditional bank.

Revolut's credit feature (the separate credit line product, not the standard card) would be subject to the same rules as any other credit card — meaning a bookmaker should decline it. In practice, using your standard Revolut balance-linked card for betting is straightforward and fully within the rules.

Why Your Revolut Deposit Might Be Declined

  1. Gambling category blocked: Check Settings → Security & privacy → Merchant categories → Gambling in your Revolut app. Toggle on if needed.
  2. Disposable virtual card: Some bookmakers reject single-use virtual Revolut cards. Use a permanent (standard or premium) Revolut card instead.
  3. KYC not completed at Revolut: If your Revolut account identity verification is incomplete, some merchant categories including gambling may be restricted.
  4. Bookmaker's own Revolut policy: A small number of bookmakers have their own restrictions on Revolut cards. Check their banking FAQ if other methods work but Revolut doesn't.

Revolut Withdrawals from Betting Sites

Withdrawing winnings to Revolut is more straightforward than deposits for many bookmakers: they send a bank transfer to your Revolut IBAN (Irish IBANs start IE). Revolut provides a full IBAN and BIC under Profile → Account details in the app. Processing time is typically 2–5 business days, though some bookmakers are faster.

Key point: bookmakers are required to return funds to the same method used for deposit where possible. If you deposited via Revolut card, expect a return to the same card before a bank transfer is processed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Revolut work for sports betting in Ireland?

Yes. Revolut's standard debit card works for deposits at most Irish-facing betting sites. If a deposit fails, open your Revolut app → Settings → Security & privacy → Merchant categories → Gambling and make sure it is toggled on. This is a Revolut-side gambling block that is off by default on some accounts.

Is Revolut treated as a debit card or credit card by bookmakers?

Revolut's standard balance-linked card is treated as a debit card by bookmakers. The Irish credit card gambling ban does not apply to it. Only Revolut's separate credit feature (a distinct product) would be classified as a credit card.

How do I withdraw betting winnings to Revolut?

Find your Revolut IBAN under Profile → Account details in the Revolut app. Enter it as the bank transfer destination on your bookmaker's withdrawal page. Most bookmakers process SEPA bank transfers in 2–5 business days.

Why was my Revolut deposit to a betting site declined?

The most common reason is Revolut's gambling category block. Check Settings → Security & privacy → Merchant categories → Gambling in your Revolut app and toggle it on. Other causes: using a single-use disposable virtual card (switch to a permanent card), incomplete identity verification in Revolut, or the bookmaker's own restriction on certain card types.
