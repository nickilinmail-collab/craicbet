Revolut Betting Ireland 2026 — Deposit & Withdraw at GRAI Licensed Bookmakers

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

Revolut has become the most popular e-money account in Ireland, and for Irish punters it has a clear practical advantage: it is a debit instrument by default, meaning it is completely unaffected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban that came into force on 1 July 2026. GRAI-licensed bookmakers accept Revolut just like any Visa or Mastercard debit card — instant deposits, withdrawals within 1–24 hours, and a built-in gambling spending limit that makes it one of the most responsible-gambling-friendly payment methods available.

✅ Key fact: Revolut issues Visa or Mastercard debit cards. Ireland's 2026 credit card gambling ban does not apply. Revolut deposits at licensed bookmakers are permitted.

Why Revolut Is Popular for Betting in Ireland

Revolut holds an e-money licence from the Bank of Lithuania, passported across the EU including Ireland. It is not a traditional bank but functions like one for everyday spending. For betting purposes, three features make it stand out:

  • Instant deposits — funds appear in your bookmaker account immediately, the same as a standard debit card.
  • Fast withdrawals — most GRAI-licensed bookmakers process Revolut withdrawals within 1–24 hours, significantly faster than the 1–5 business days typical of traditional bank transfers.
  • Gambling spending controls — the Revolut app lets you set a monthly gambling spend cap or block gambling transactions entirely, without contacting the bookmaker.

Revolut also carries no additional fees for deposits or withdrawals at bookmakers (the bookmaker may charge a fee, but Revolut itself does not). Weekend and out-of-hours transactions process at the same speed as weekday ones.

Ireland's Credit Card Gambling Ban — Why Revolut Is Not Affected

Since 1 July 2026, GRAI-licensed gambling operators in Ireland are prohibited from accepting credit card payments for gambling transactions. This ban covers Visa Credit, Mastercard Credit, and credit facilities generally. It does not cover debit cards, prepaid cards, or e-money accounts loaded from debit funds.

Revolut operates as a debit instrument: you load money from your bank account (a debit pull) and spend from that balance. There is no credit facility involved. This means Revolut gambling deposits fall outside the ban and remain permitted. If you have linked a credit card as a Revolut funding source, some bookmakers' payment processors may flag the underlying card type — to be safe, fund your Revolut account via your bank debit card or bank transfer rather than from a linked credit card.

How to Deposit at a Betting Site Using Revolut

  1. Log in to your bookmaker account and go to Deposit.
  2. Select Debit Card / Visa / Mastercard (Revolut does not appear as a separate option — it is processed as a standard card).
  3. Enter your Revolut Visa or Mastercard card number, expiry date, and CVV from the Revolut app (Cards section).
  4. Complete 3D Secure verification — Revolut sends a push notification or SMS code to confirm the transaction.
  5. Funds appear in your betting account instantly.

For virtual cards (Revolut's disposable card numbers), the process is the same but the card details change after each use — suitable if you want an extra layer of privacy.

How to Withdraw to Revolut

Withdrawals to the same Revolut card used for deposit are standard at GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Go to Withdraw in your account, select your Revolut card, enter the amount, and confirm. Depending on the bookmaker's processing times, funds typically arrive in your Revolut account within 1–24 hours. Some bookmakers may take up to 3 business days on first withdrawal while they verify your account identity — this is a GRAI/AML requirement, not a Revolut restriction.

Setting Gambling Spending Limits on Revolut

Revolut has a built-in gambling category control in the Security section of the app. You can:

  • Block all gambling transactions — any attempt to pay at a gambling merchant will be instantly declined.
  • Set a monthly spending cap — once your chosen euro limit is reached, further gambling transactions are declined until the next calendar month.

This operates entirely within Revolut and does not interact with your bookmaker's responsible gambling tools. Using both — a Revolut cap and a bookmaker deposit limit — gives you two independent safety nets. The Revolut limit resets on the first of each calendar month; to change or remove it, you must do so in the app (there is no cooling-off delay as there is with GRAI-mandated bookmaker self-exclusion tools).

What to Do If Revolut Blocks a Gambling Transaction

If your Revolut payment at a betting site is declined, check in this order:

  1. Gambling blocked? Open Revolut app → Security & Privacy → check Gambling is not blocked.
  2. Monthly limit reached? If you set a monthly cap, check your remaining allowance.
  3. Insufficient balance? Top up your Revolut account.
  4. 3D Secure failed? Ensure push notifications are enabled so you receive the Revolut confirmation prompt.
  5. Bookmaker does not accept Revolut? Rare with GRAI-licensed sites, but confirm in the bookmaker's payment FAQ.
  6. Underlying funding source is credit? If Revolut is funded by a credit card, some processors may decline. Switch to bank transfer funding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Revolut work for betting in Ireland?

Yes. Revolut is accepted at the majority of GRAI-licensed bookmakers in Ireland. Because Revolut issues a Visa or Mastercard debit card, it is treated as a standard debit card. Ireland's credit card gambling ban (since 1 July 2026) does not affect Revolut. Deposits are instant; withdrawals typically arrive within 1–24 hours.

Can I use Revolut for gambling deposits in Ireland?

Yes, provided gambling is not blocked in your Revolut app settings. If your payment is declined, check that gambling is enabled under Security & Privacy in the Revolut app, confirm your balance is sufficient, and check you have not reached a monthly spending cap you previously set. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers accept Revolut without issue.

How do I set a gambling limit on Revolut?

Open the Revolut app → Security & Privacy → Spending controls → Gambling. You can block all gambling transactions or set a monthly euro cap. Once the cap is reached, further gambling transactions are automatically declined. The limit resets on the first of each calendar month. This is a voluntary tool and does not connect to your bookmaker account.
