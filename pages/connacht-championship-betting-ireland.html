Connacht Championship Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Connacht GAA Championship covers football (the province's primary code) and hurling. In football, Mayo and Galway are the perennial contenders, with Roscommon, Leitrim and Sligo also competing. The Connacht final at Dr Hyde Park in Roscommon or Pearse Stadium in Galway is one of the summer's major provincial occasions.

Connacht Football: The Provincial Picture

Connacht football is effectively a two-horse race between Galway and Mayo in most years, though Roscommon has mounted serious challenges in recent seasons. Leitrim and Sligo compete but are significant underdogs in most draws. Betting outrights on Connacht is a popular market because the province's structure can produce surprise semi-final upsets against the two big counties.

The Connacht Championship moves between venues including MacHale Park (Castlebar), Pearse Stadium (Galway), Hyde Park (Roscommon) and Markievicz Park (Sligo), with the final typically alternating between the main towns.

Connacht Hurling

Connacht hurling is a less prominent market but Galway — competing in Leinster hurling since 2009 — is the dominant force in the province historically. Roscommon hurling competes in lower Connacht tiers.

Betting on Connacht Championship Matches

Match betting markets for Connacht championship games typically open one to two weeks before each fixture. Outright Connacht championship winner markets are available year-round at most GRAI-licensed bookmakers. In-play betting is available on selected semi-finals and the final.

Connacht Senior Football Championship: Format

The Connacht championship uses a round-robin format for the provincial title. Connacht has 5 counties plus New York, who enter at the quarter-final stage. Galway are the dominant force with the most All-Ireland wins from the province.

Where is the Connacht GAA final played?
