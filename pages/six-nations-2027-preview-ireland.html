Six Nations 2027 Ireland Preview — Squad, Fixtures & Betting Markets

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Ireland's Six Nations 2027 campaign builds on one of the most successful periods in Irish rugby history. This preview guide covers the key elements of the squad, the fixture schedule, the main betting markets and what to watch for across the five rounds of the Championship.

Ireland's Six Nations Context Heading Into 2027

Ireland enters the 2027 Six Nations as one of the leading nations based on recent form in the Championship and in autumn internationals. The squad has significant depth in key positions, with competition for places across the starting XV. Consistency of selection and player availability — particularly in the front row and back row — will be central to Ireland's campaign.

Key Positions to Watch

  • Out-half: Ireland's kicker and decision-maker shapes the entire game plan. Performance and accuracy in the kicking game will determine points totals and territory management.
  • Back row: Ireland's breakdown work is fundamental to their defensive structure and turnover rate. Personnel in this area drives the team's work rate and competitiveness in the collision areas.
  • Centres: The midfield carries versus France and England — Ireland's direct attacking threats in the Championship — will be a key battleground.

Main Betting Markets for Ireland in 2027

  • To win the Championship: Ireland's outright price will be published in autumn 2026 and is expected to reflect them as a co-favourite.
  • Grand Slam: Five wins from five — available separately and typically at longer odds than the outright winner.
  • To win without conceding in a match: Ireland's defensive organisation makes clean-sheet props interesting in some rounds.

Ireland's Betting Markets and Recent Form

Ireland won the Six Nations Grand Slam in 2023 and have been among the favourites in every subsequent tournament. Under Andy Farrell's management, Ireland play a structured, forward-dominant game that suits Aviva Stadium's conditions. Home matches in Dublin — particularly against France and England — attract the biggest betting interest from Irish punters.

  • Match handicap: Ireland typically give 5–10 points when hosting lower-ranked nations; watch for value laying the handicap against Scotland or Italy at Aviva
  • First try scorer: prop tries are rare — back wing or centre runners who feature in set-piece moves
  • Winning margin: Ireland's home wins tend to be 10–20 points; value may be on the "11–20 points" band rather than a narrow handicap
When is Ireland's first Six Nations 2027 match?

Ireland's first Six Nations 2027 match is expected in the first weekend of February 2027. The full fixture list will be released in autumn 2026. Ireland typically alternate home and away openers based on the fixture rotation.

What are Ireland's main threats in the Six Nations 2027?

The key threats to Ireland in the 2027 Championship are expected to be France and England, who have been the most competitive nations alongside Ireland in recent seasons. Away fixtures at the Stade de France or Twickenham are historically Ireland's most difficult matches.

Where do Ireland play their home Six Nations matches?

Ireland play all Six Nations home matches at the Aviva Stadium (also known as the Aviva) in Dublin 4, with a capacity of approximately 51,700. The stadium is owned by the IRFU and has hosted major international rugby since 2010.
