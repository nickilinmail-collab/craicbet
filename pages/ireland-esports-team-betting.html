Ireland Esports at National Level

Ireland sends national teams to international esports events through organisations including the Irish Esports Association and participation in the European Esports Championships. The team competes across multiple titles, with CS2 and League of Legends the most prominent internationally.

At club level, Irish esports organisations compete in European and global league structures for games like VALORANT, Rocket League and FIFA/EA Sports FC. Some Irish clubs have competed in top-division European leagues.

Is esports betting legal in Ireland under GRAI?

Yes. Esports is treated as a sport under the GRAI framework, and esports betting is fully regulated. Bookmakers must hold a GRAI Remote Betting licence to offer esports markets to Irish customers.

Which esports titles have the most betting markets in Ireland?

CS2, League of Legends, Dota 2, VALORANT and Rocket League are the most widely covered esports titles at GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

Does Ireland compete in the European Esports Championships?

Ireland participates in European esports competition at both national and club level. The Irish Esports Association manages national team participation.

How do I find esports markets at GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

Look for an Esports section in the sports menu at your chosen GRAI-licensed bookmaker. Major events including ESL Pro League (CS2), Worlds (LoL) and The International (Dota 2) are typically featured.
