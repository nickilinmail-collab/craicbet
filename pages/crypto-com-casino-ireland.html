How to Withdraw from Crypto.com to Your Bank in Ireland

This is the most common question Irish players ask. Here is the step-by-step process:

  1. Open the Crypto.com app → tap Accounts → Fiat Wallet
  2. Select EUR → tap Withdraw

  4. Enter amount (minimum usually €50) and confirm with 2FA
  5. Funds arrive via SEPA transfer in 1–3 business days

Once the EUR is in your bank or Revolut, you can use your debit card to deposit at any online casino in Ireland as normal.

Tax: Do I Pay Tax on Crypto Casino Winnings in Ireland?

Gambling winnings in Ireland are not subject to income tax. However, if you sell or exchange cryptocurrency (including converting to EUR in Crypto.com), Capital Gains Tax (CGT) may apply to any gain from the crypto's increase in value since you acquired it. Revenue Ireland treats cryptocurrency as a chargeable asset. Keep records of when you bought your crypto and at what price — your accountant or Revenue's myAccount portal can help calculate any CGT due.

Sending Casino Winnings Back to Crypto.com

  3. Wait for blockchain confirmations (Bitcoin: ~10–60 min; Ethereum: ~5–15 min)
  4. Funds appear in your Crypto.com wallet — you can hold, convert to EUR, or use on the Crypto.com card
