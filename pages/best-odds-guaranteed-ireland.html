Best Odds Guaranteed Ireland 2026

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | Gambling Awareness Trust | 1800 753 753

Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG) is one of the most valuable features a horse racing punter can have on their account. It means you can take the early morning price on a horse and, if the price drifts (gets bigger) by the time the race starts, you're paid at the Starting Price (SP) instead — automatically, with no extra action needed.

Example: You back a horse at 5/1 (early price) for €20. By the off it has drifted to 8/1 (SP). With BOG you are paid at 8/1 — €180 profit instead of €120. Without BOG, you receive your original 5/1.

How BOG works

When you take a fixed (early) price on a horse race, you lock in those odds. With BOG:

  • If SP at the off is bigger than your early price → you receive SP
  • If SP at the off is shorter than your early price → you receive your early price
  • You always get the better of the two — hence “Best Odds Guaranteed”

BOG is automatic at participating bookmakers — you don't need to request it or use a code. It applies to cash bets on eligible races (typically UK and Irish horse racing) placed during BOG hours (usually from 8am or 9am on race day).

What BOG does not cover

  • Free bets and bonus tokens — excluded at virtually all bookmakers
  • Enhanced prices — promotional boosted odds are usually excluded
  • Ante-post bets — bets placed days or weeks before the race
  • Cashed-out bets — if you cash out before the race, you lose BOG entitlement
  • Greyhounds, football, and other sports — horse racing only

When BOG is most valuable

BOG delivers the most value at major festivals — Cheltenham, Grand National, Leopardstown Christmas, Punchestown — where markets are open for hours before the off and significant market movements are common. If you regularly back horses each-way at festivals, BOG can materially improve your returns over a season.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG)?
BOG means if you take an early price on a horse and the Starting Price is bigger when the race goes off, you are paid at the higher SP. You always receive the better of your early price or SP.
Does BOG apply to free bets?
No. BOG at most bookmakers applies to cash bets only. Free bet tokens and bonus bets are excluded.
Does BOG apply to Cheltenham?
Yes. BOG is particularly valuable at Cheltenham where horses frequently drift before the off. Most bookmakers extend BOG to all UK and Irish racing including festival days.
Does BOG apply to ante-post bets?
No. BOG applies to same-day early prices on race day only. Ante-post bets placed days or weeks in advance are not covered.
