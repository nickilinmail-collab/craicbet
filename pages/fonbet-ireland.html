What Is Fonbet?

Fonbet is one of Russia’s leading online and retail bookmakers, with a large customer base and a wide range of sports markets including Russian Premier League football, KHL ice hockey, basketball, and international events. It operates under Russia’s SRO-based regulatory model and is regulated by the Federal Tax Service, rather than under an MGA, UKGC, or any EU-equivalent licence.

Fonbet is primarily designed for Russian-speaking markets. Its website and customer service are predominantly in Russian, and its payment methods are oriented towards Russian banking systems (Qiwi, YooMoney, etc.) rather than Irish banking infrastructure.

Fonbet’s GRAI Status in Ireland

As of November 2026, Fonbet does not appear on the GRAI public register. Without a GRAI licence, Fonbet is not authorised to operate under Ireland’s regulatory framework. Irish players using Fonbet would receive no GRAI-mandated protections. The Russian regulatory framework under which Fonbet operates does not provide EU or GRAI-equivalent consumer protection standards.
