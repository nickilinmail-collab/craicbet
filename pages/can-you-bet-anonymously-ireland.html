Can You Bet Anonymously in Ireland After the GRAI?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

No — anonymous betting is not possible at GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers. Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, all operators must verify customer identity before allowing betting or withdrawals. This applies to every GRAI-licensed bookmaker and means your name, address, and age must be confirmed. Here is what this means in practice.

Why Anonymous Betting Is No Longer Possible

The GRAI framework mandates KYC (Know Your Customer) verification as a licence condition. This exists for three main reasons: (1) age verification — ensuring all customers are 18+; (2) anti-money-laundering requirements under Irish financial crime law; (3) problem gambling identification — enabling the National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGER) to function across all operators.

Under the old Revenue permissions system (before July 2026), some operators had lighter verification requirements. The GRAI framework harmonised this upward — all operators must now meet the same minimum standard.

What Information You Must Provide

  • Full legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Home address
  • Valid photo ID (passport, driving licence, national ID)
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement within 3 months)
  • Payment method registered in your name

What About Privacy?

Your personal data at a GRAI-licensed operator is protected by GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Operators cannot share your data with third parties for marketing without consent. You can request deletion of non-AML-essential data after account closure. The GRAI itself does not receive your personal gambling transaction data beyond what is needed for licence compliance.

Offshore “Anonymous” Betting: The Real Risks

Some offshore sites advertise minimal KYC. These operators are not GRAI-licensed and have no payout obligation under Irish law. If they refuse to pay out, you have no domestic recourse. Crypto-based gambling sites that claim anonymity typically still have blockchain-traceable transactions. The anonymity is illusory and the consumer protection is nil.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you bet anonymously in Ireland?

No — GRAI-licensed operators must verify every customer's identity (KYC) as a licence condition under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Anonymous betting at licensed sites is not permitted.

What personal information do Irish betting sites require?

Full name, date of birth, address, photo ID (passport or driving licence), and proof of address (utility bill within 3 months). Payment methods must also be in your name.

Is my betting data private at a GRAI-licensed site?

Protected by GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Operators cannot share your data for marketing without consent. You can request data deletion after account closure (AML records may be retained up to 7 years).

What about crypto betting sites that don't require ID?

These are typically offshore and unregulated. No GRAI protection, no payout obligation under Irish law. Blockchain transactions are publicly traceable — anonymity is not genuine, and consumer protection is absent.

When does KYC verification happen on Irish betting sites?

Most GRAI-licensed operators now require verification before you can deposit. At minimum, KYC is required before your first withdrawal. Some operators verify at a specific deposit threshold.
