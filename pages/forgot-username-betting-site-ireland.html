Forgot Your Username on an Irish Betting Site? Recovery Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Most GRAI-licensed Irish betting sites use your email address as your username, so a forgotten username typically means a forgotten registered email. If you remember the email but not the password, recovery is straightforward. If you remember neither, you will need to verify your identity through the operator’s support team. Here is how to handle both scenarios.

If You Remember Your Email

Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers use your email address as the account identifier. Go to the login page and try “Forgot Password” with your email. If the email is registered, you will receive a reset link. This is the fastest route and works for the majority of cases.

If You Have Forgotten Your Registered Email

Contact the operator’s customer support (live chat is fastest) and provide:

  • Your full name as registered on the account
  • Date of birth
  • Last deposit amount and method (if known)
  • Registered address

Support can look up your account via these identifiers. You will then be asked to verify identity — typically providing a photo of your ID document via email. Under GRAI KYC requirements, operators must verify your identity before giving access to account details, which protects you from someone else recovering your account.

Accounts Created via Social Login

Some operators offer registration via Google or Apple accounts. If you registered this way and have lost access to that Google or Apple account, recovery becomes more complex. Contact the operator first; they can usually manually link a new email address after full identity verification.

If You Cannot Access the Account at All

Provide as much identifying information as possible to support. GRAI rules require operators to verify your identity before providing account access — this is a consumer protection measure. Processing a manual identity verification typically takes 2–5 business days. Ensure you have a valid ID document ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to Recover a Forgotten Betting Site Username

  1. Go to the site's login page and look for 'Forgot username?' or 'Forgot login?' link.
  2. Enter the email address you registered with — most sites use email as your login rather than a separate username.
  3. Check your inbox for an email with your username or a reset link.
  4. If you no longer have access to the registered email, contact customer support with your name, date of birth, and last 4 digits of your registered payment method for identity verification.
  5. Once verified, support can update your registered email address so you can regain access.

Preventing Login Issues in Future

  • Use a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, Apple Keychain) to store your login credentials securely.
  • Register with an email address you will keep long-term — avoid disposable email addresses.
  • Enable two-factor authentication if the site offers it — this protects your account and also gives you an alternative way to verify your identity.
  • Keep a note of which email you registered with for each betting site — a simple spreadsheet (without passwords) is useful.
How do I recover my betting account username in Ireland?

Most sites use your email as username. Try 'Forgot Password' with your email. If you've forgotten your email too, contact support with your name, date of birth, and address for identity verification.

Can support find my account without my username?

Yes — provide your full name, date of birth, registered address, and optionally last deposit details. After identity verification, support can retrieve your account details.

How long does account recovery take at an Irish betting site?

If you have your email: instant via password reset. Manual identity verification through support: 2–5 business days. Provide documentation promptly to speed up the process.

Is it safe to send my ID to betting site support?

GRAI-licensed operators must follow data protection rules (GDPR) for all personal data. Sending ID via secure email to official support channels is standard KYC procedure and is safe at licensed operators.

What if the email address on my account no longer exists?

Contact support and explain. After full identity verification (which may require a video call or in-person document review in some cases), operators can update the email address on your account.
