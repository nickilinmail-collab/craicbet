Crash games are a relatively new casino game format that exploded in popularity from 2020 onward. The concept is simple: a multiplier rises from 1x and can "crash" at any point. Cash out before the crash to win. Wait too long and you lose.

How Crash Games Work

You place a bet before the round starts. A multiplier begins at 1x and grows. At any moment, you can cash out — winning your stake multiplied by the current value. The crash point is determined in advance by a provably fair algorithm. If you haven't cashed out when the crash occurs, you lose your bet.

Provably Fair: What It Means

Many crash games use provably fair algorithms — the crash point is determined before betting opens using a cryptographic hash. Players can verify that the outcome wasn't manipulated after the fact. This is a transparency feature, not a guarantee of favourable outcomes. The house edge is built into the algorithm.

Crash Game House Edge

Aviator's RTP is stated at 97% (3% house edge). This is competitive for a casino game. The key difference from slots is the interactive element — you decide when to cash out. This creates an illusion of skill, but mathematically, the house edge applies regardless of your strategy over a long run.

Auto Cash-Out

Crash games typically offer an auto cash-out feature — set a multiplier and the game automatically cashes out when reached. This removes emotional decision-making and enforces discipline. Useful for players who find the manual cash-out tempting them to hold too long.
