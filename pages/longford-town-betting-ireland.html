Who Are Longford Town?

Longford Town Football Club are based in Longford town and play home matches at Bishopsgate, a tidy ground that serves as the focal point of football in Co. Longford. The club draws support from across the county and the wider midlands region.

Longford Town have experienced genuine highs in their history, including FAI Cup wins and periods of Premier Division football. The club has at various points competed against some of Europe's finest clubs in UEFA competition — a source of great pride for Longford football supporters.

For bettors, Longford Town are typically considered an underdog in LOI fixtures against the top-flight's established names. Their home record at Bishopsgate is the most useful data point when assessing them — they can be a stubborn side to beat on their own ground, particularly in cup football.

Longford Town in the 2026 LOI Season

Longford Town's 2026 campaign depends on which division they are competing in. When in the Premier Division, the club targets survival first and cup runs as a secondary objective. When in the First Division, they aim for promotion.

Regardless of division, Longford Town carry the pride of a small county town competing in national football, and their supporters travel well for such a small catchment area. This loyalty and spirit can translate to something tangible on the pitch, particularly in derby fixtures against other midlands clubs.

At Bishopsgate, Longford Town are always at their best. The tight pitch dimensions and passionate local atmosphere can make them more than a match for bigger-name opponents — a factor worth noting in any betting assessment, especially in cup competitions.
