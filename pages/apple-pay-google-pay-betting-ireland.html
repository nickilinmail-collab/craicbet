Apple Pay & Google Pay Betting Ireland 2026 — How to Deposit at Bookmakers

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Apple Pay and Google Pay are increasingly popular for funding betting accounts in Ireland — instant, no card details entered, and already on your phone. This guide covers which betting sites accept Apple Pay and Google Pay in Ireland, how deposits work, and the one important limit to know.

Does Apple Pay Work for Betting in Ireland?

Apple Pay works at betting sites that accept Visa or Mastercard — since Apple Pay is processed through the card network linked to your Apple Wallet. The bookmaker sees a standard card transaction; the fact that you tapped with Face ID rather than entering a 16-digit number makes no difference on their end. As long as your linked card is a debit card (not a credit card), it works.

Does Google Pay Work for Betting in Ireland?

Google Pay works the same way — it tokenises your linked debit card and processes through Visa or Mastercard. Any bookmaker that accepts debit cards will generally accept Google Pay on mobile, provided their payment page is Google Pay-enabled (most modern betting sites are, especially on Android).

The Credit Card Ban — What It Means for Apple/Google Pay

Ireland's credit card gambling ban (in force since 2024) prohibits bookmakers from accepting credit card deposits. If your Apple Pay or Google Pay is linked to a credit card, that deposit will be declined. The bookmaker's system reads the card type on the token — a credit card linked to Apple Pay is still a credit card.

Solution: ensure your default Apple/Google Pay card for gambling is a debit card — Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit, or Revolut (which is treated as debit). See our full credit card gambling ban guide.

How to Deposit via Apple Pay at a Betting Site

  1. Open the bookmaker's app or mobile site on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Deposit and select Card or Apple Pay as the payment method.
  3. Enter the amount. Apple Pay will prompt you to confirm with Face ID or Touch ID.
  4. The deposit appears in your betting account instantly.

Minimum deposits are set by the bookmaker, not Apple Pay. Most Irish betting sites set minimums of €5–€10. Our €5 minimum deposit guide lists operators accepting the smallest amounts.

Withdrawals — Apple Pay and Google Pay

Withdrawal via Apple Pay or Google Pay directly is not typically available at betting sites. Most bookmakers return withdrawals to the card used for deposit (i.e. the underlying Visa or Mastercard debit card), or via bank transfer to your IBAN. This means your winnings go back to your bank account rather than appearing in Apple or Google Wallet directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Apple Pay to deposit at betting sites in Ireland?

Yes — Apple Pay works at betting sites in Ireland when linked to a debit card. The credit card gambling ban means Apple Pay linked to a credit card will be declined. Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit, and Revolut all work.

Does Google Pay work at Irish betting sites?

Yes. Google Pay works at most modern Irish betting sites on Android, using your linked debit card. As with Apple Pay, the credit card gambling ban means a credit card linked to Google Pay will be declined — use a debit card.

Why was my Apple Pay deposit declined at a bookmaker?

The most common reasons are: (1) your Apple Pay is linked to a credit card — this is blocked under Ireland's credit card gambling ban; switch to a debit card. (2) The bookmaker's payment page doesn't support Apple Pay — try entering your card details manually. (3) Your Revolut gambling category is blocked — enable it in Revolut app settings.

Can I withdraw to Apple Pay or Google Pay from a betting site?

Direct Apple Pay or Google Pay withdrawal is not typically available at Irish betting sites. Withdrawals return to the underlying debit card linked to your digital wallet, or via SEPA bank transfer to your IBAN. Most bookmakers process card withdrawals in 2–5 business days.
