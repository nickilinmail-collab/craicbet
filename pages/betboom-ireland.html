What Is BetBoom?

BetBoom is an online sports betting and gaming platform primarily targeting CIS markets (Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan). It holds a Curacao Gaming Authority licence for its international operations and Russian regulatory authorisation for its domestic market. BetBoom is known for its esports and cybersport coverage alongside traditional sports markets including football, basketball, and hockey.

The Curacao licence provides basic operational standards but does not meet the consumer protection requirements of EU regulators such as the MGA or GRAI. BetBoom’s platform is primarily designed for Russian-speaking users, with Russian-language customer service and CIS-oriented payment methods.

BetBoom’s GRAI Status & Ireland

As of November 2026, BetBoom does not appear on the GRAI public register. Without a GRAI licence, BetBoom is not authorised to operate under the Irish regulatory framework. The combination of a Curacao licence (lower-tier regulation) and no GRAI authorisation means Irish players using BetBoom would have minimal formal protections under any recognised Irish or EU regulatory framework.
