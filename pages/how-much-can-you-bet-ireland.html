How Much Can You Bet in Ireland? Stake and Deposit Limits Explained

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

There is no government-imposed maximum stake for betting in Ireland. However, GRAI-licensed operators must offer deposit limit tools, and individual bookmakers set their own maximum stake and payout rules. Here is what you need to know about betting limits in 2026.

No Statutory Betting Limit for Punters

Under Irish law, there is no legal maximum you are allowed to bet. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and the GRAI framework focus on consumer protection and responsible gambling tools — they do not cap individual bets for recreational punters. Any limit you encounter comes from the individual bookmaker, not Irish law.

GRAI Deposit Limit Rules

GRAI-licensed operators are required to offer customers the ability to set their own deposit limits on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis. These limits must take effect immediately when lowered. Increases must be delayed by at least 24–48 hours to prevent impulsive decisions. Operators must offer this tool to all customers — it is a licence condition, not an optional feature.

You can set a deposit limit at any time through your account settings. This is a responsible gambling tool, not a restriction imposed on you — you choose the amount.

Bookmaker Maximum Stake Rules

Individual operators set their own maximum stakes per bet and per market. Common limits:

  • Football: €5,000–€50,000 on major markets (varies hugely by operator and event)
  • Horse racing: Often lower, particularly for smaller meetings
  • Enhanced odds markets: Usually capped at €5–€25
  • Accumulators: Max payout limits typically €500,000–€1,000,000

Account Restrictions and “Gubbing”

Bookmakers can restrict your account’s maximum stake if you are consistently profitable. This is legal — bookmakers are private businesses that can choose what bets to accept. The GRAI does not currently require bookmakers to accept all bets. If your stakes are restricted, your options are limited: you can try other operators or accept the reduced limits. Is there a maximum bet limit in Ireland?

No statutory maximum for punters. Limits come from individual bookmakers (who can restrict your stakes) and from your own voluntary deposit limits. The GRAI requires operators to offer deposit limit tools.

Are deposit limits mandatory on Irish betting sites?

GRAI-licensed operators must offer daily, weekly, and monthly deposit limit tools. Setting them is optional for customers, but the tool must be available and must work immediately when you lower limits.

Can a bookmaker limit how much I can bet?

Yes — bookmakers can restrict your maximum stake. This is legal under Irish law. The GRAI does not currently require operators to accept bets of any particular size.

How do I set a deposit limit on an Irish betting site?

Go to your account settings and look for Responsible Gambling or Safer Gambling tools. Set your preferred daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limit. Reductions take effect immediately; increases are typically delayed 24–48 hours.

Can I self-exclude from all Irish betting sites at once?

The GRAI's National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGER) covers all GRAI-licensed operators. Once fully operational, self-excluding through one operator will notify all participating GRAI licensees.
