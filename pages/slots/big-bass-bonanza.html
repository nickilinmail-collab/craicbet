Big Bass Bonanza Overview

Big Bass Bonanza from Pragmatic Play's Reel Kingdom studio is the fishing slot that started a franchise. Released in 2020, this 5-reel, 3-row slot with 10 fixed paylines has hooked Irish players with its straightforward gameplay and rewarding free spins mechanic. The premise is simple: cast your line, reel in some fish, and let the fisherman wild symbol collect money values for you.

While the max win of 2,100x is more modest compared to some Pragmatic Play titles, Big Bass Bonanza makes up for it with a generous RTP of 96.71% and a satisfying collecting feature during free spins that keeps players coming back. The game's success has spawned numerous sequels including Big Bass Splash, Bigger Bass Bonanza, and Big Bass Bonanza Megaways, but the original remains a fan favourite.

Bonus Features and Free Spins

The heart of Big Bass Bonanza lies in its Free Spins feature, triggered by landing three or more scatter symbols (the fishing reel). Three scatters award 10 free spins, four award 15, and five scatters deliver 20 free spins.

During the free spins round, the fisherman symbol takes centre stage as a wild. When the fisherman lands on the same spin as any money fish symbols, he collects all their values and adds them to your total win. Money fish symbols display random cash values, and the fisherman scoops up every single one visible on the reels.

The real magic happens when multiple fisherman symbols appear during free spins. Each fisherman collects all money values independently, effectively multiplying the collection. Landing three fisherman wilds alongside several money fish can produce the game's biggest payouts. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger extra rounds, extending the collecting opportunity.

Theme and Design

The lakeside setting features a tranquil backdrop of calm waters, green hills, and a wooden dock. Symbols include various fish species (bass, perch, and pike), tackle boxes, dragonflies, and standard card values. The fisherman character is the standout symbol, animated with a casting motion when he appears to collect money values. The audio design is cheerful and relaxed, with splashing sound effects accompanying wins and a gentle country-style tune in the background. It is a laid-back, enjoyable atmosphere that suits extended playing sessions.

Verdict

Big Bass Bonanza is a masterclass in simple, engaging slot design. It does not need complex grid mechanics or dozens of features to deliver an entertaining experience. The collecting mechanic during free spins creates genuine excitement with every fisherman landing, and the strong 96.71% RTP gives players fair value. While the 2,100x maximum win is not the highest around, the medium-high volatility means you will encounter rewarding free spin rounds more regularly than on extreme high-volatility slots. For Irish players who enjoy straightforward gameplay with a fun theme, Big Bass Bonanza is an excellent choice.

Big Bass Bonanza FAQ

During free spins, money fish symbols appear on the reels with random cash values.
