What Is First Person Roulette?

First Person Roulette by Evolution is an RNG European Roulette game featuring the company's distinctive Go Live button. Rather than a live studio stream, the game uses high-quality CGI graphics to simulate the roulette experience, with the option to switch to a live dealer table at any moment via the Go Live button.

First Person Roulette occupies a bridge position between RNG casino games and live casino — it delivers standard European Roulette mechanics and RTP without requiring a live stream, while giving players the option to transition directly to a live dealer table if they choose.

RTP & How the House Edge Works

First Person Roulette carries a published RTP of 97.3%. RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Go Live button is the game's key feature: pressing it at any time transitions the player directly to a live Lightning Roulette, Auto Roulette or Immersive Roulette table in one click, without navigating through the casino lobby. The transition is seamless and the bet size carries over.

The core mechanics are standard European Roulette: 37 numbers (0–36), all standard bet types (straight-up 35:1, splits 17:1, streets 11:1, corners 8:1, six-lines 5:1, dozens and columns 2:1, even-chance bets 1:1). The wheel spins and the ball is determined by RNG. The 97.3% RTP is identical to standard European Roulette.

Because it uses RNG rather than a live stream, First Person Roulette can be played at any pace — the player controls when to spin, with no waiting for other players or live dealer timing. This makes it suitable for players who prefer autonomous pacing or want to understand roulette mechanics before playing live.

Presentation & Design

A photorealistic 3D CGI roulette environment: a detailed European Roulette wheel, a green baize betting table with all standard bet positions, and a cinematic camera angle that shifts on spin. The visual quality is high — Evolution's CGI work gives the impression of a physical environment.
