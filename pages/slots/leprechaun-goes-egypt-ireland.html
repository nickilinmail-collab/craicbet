What Is Leprechaun Goes to Egypt?

Leprechaun Goes to Egypt by Play'n GO is a creative Irish-Egyptian crossover slot released in 2016 that transplants the classic leprechaun character from the Irish countryside to an ancient Egyptian tomb setting. The mashup concept delivers a 25-payline game with free spins, expanding wilds and two distinct symbol sets blending Irish and Egyptian iconography.

Play'n GO built Leprechaun Goes to Egypt as a deliberate genre crossover — taking the most recognisable figure from Irish-themed slots and placing him in the Egyptian mythology setting that dominated another entire slot sub-genre. The concept is whimsical and self-aware, and the mechanical execution is solid if conventional.

Leprechaun Goes to Egypt carries a published RTP of 95.03%. Medium volatility means the game balances win frequency and payout size.

Key Features

Expanding Wild symbols are triggered when the leprechaun Wild symbol lands on reels two, three or four. When activated, the leprechaun expands to cover the entire reel height, creating full-reel wild coverage on the triggering reel. When multiple reels show expanding wilds simultaneously, the combination across 25 paylines is highly rewarding.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. During free spins, expanding wilds trigger more frequently, increasing the probability of multiple full-reel wild reels appearing within the same spin. The expanding-wild-during-free-spins combination is the game's high-value moment.

Two symbol sets appear across the reels: traditional Irish icons (shamrocks, harps, gold coins, horseshoes) and Egyptian icons (ankhs, scarab beetles, hieroglyphs, the Eye of Horus). Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 25-payline structure is standard for Play'n GO slot design of this era.

Theme & Design

A gleaming Egyptian tomb populated by an incongruous but cheerful leprechaun in his green hat and suit, surrounded by ancient Egyptian artefacts, gold sarcophagi, hieroglyph-covered walls and hidden treasure. The colour palette contrasts Irish greens with Egyptian golds and blues. The design is playful and the concept is executed with genuine visual wit.

Verdict

Leprechaun Goes to Egypt is an enjoyable novelty slot that works better than the concept might suggest. The 95.03% RTP is below what higher-value alternatives offer, and the medium volatility makes for a moderate session experience. For Irish players who want an Irish-themed slot with an unusual twist, it is worth a few sessions.
