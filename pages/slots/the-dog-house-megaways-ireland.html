Key Features

Wild symbols are sticky during free spins and each has a multiplier value (1x, 2x or 3x). When a wild is part of a winning combination, its multiplier is applied to the win. During free spins, all sticky wilds remain in position and new wilds can land with their own multipliers, creating an accumulating board of multiplied wilds.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the grid. The more scatters, the more free spins awarded. During the bonus, the sticky wild multipliers interact with the Megaways way-count on each spin — when a large Megaways grid aligns with multiple sticky multiplier wilds, the results can be spectacular.

The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing chain wins from one paid spin in both base game and free spins. The variable Megaways reel heights shift with each tumble, keeping the way-count dynamic throughout the bonus.

Theme & Design

Cartoon dogs — a French bulldog, a dachshund, a beagle, a spaniel and a poodle — across a doghouse backyard setting. The design is colourful, cheerful and high-quality. Bone symbols, paw prints and dog toys fill the lower-value positions. The playful soundtrack matches the lighthearted aesthetic.
