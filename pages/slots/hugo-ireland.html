What Is Hugo Slot?

Hugo by Play'n GO is a slot based on the beloved Danish TV character Hugo the Troll, known to generations of Irish viewers who grew up watching the classic children's programme. The 2016 release combines a fun family-friendly troll aesthetic with high-volatility mechanics and an extraordinary 500,000x theoretical maximum win driven by its unique Money Lock feature.

Despite the light-hearted animated character heritage, Hugo the slot is emphatically a high-volatility game. The base gameplay features standard 5-reel, 20-payline mechanics, but the Money Lock feature — where multiplied coin symbols fill a locking grid for accumulated prizes — drives a maximum win that places Hugo among the most ambitious Play'n GO titles.

RTP & Volatility

Hugo Slot carries a published RTP of 96.74%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Money Lock feature triggers when six or more coin symbols land on the reels. All coin symbols freeze in position and three re-spins begin. Each new coin landing resets the counter to three. At the end of re-spins, all coin values are totalled. Special multiplied coin symbols (5x, 10x, 100x) dramatically inflate the total payout.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. During free spins, coin symbols land more frequently and multiplied coin values are higher. The free-spins version of the Money Lock feature has access to larger multiplier coin values than the base game, making the bonus round significantly more valuable.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. Hugo himself can appear as a special wild that carries a multiplier applied to any wins he contributes to. The combination of a high-multiplier Money Lock and Hugo's wild multiplier during free spins creates the conditions for the 500,000x theoretical maximum.

Theme & Design

The cheerful green-and-brown cartoon troll Hugo is the game's star, surrounded by his friends and nemeses from the Danish TV show. The visual design is bright, colourful and immediately recognisable to fans of the original series. Play'n GO has reproduced the character faithfully and the animations are expressive and endearing.

Verdict

Hugo is a surprising package — a cheerful children's TV character driving a high-volatility slot with one of the highest theoretical maximum wins in Play'n GO's catalogue. The 96.74% RTP is excellent, the Money Lock feature creates dramatic coin-collection moments, and the 500,000x ceiling gives it extraordinary theoretical ambition. For Irish players who remember Hugo from TV, it carries additional charm.
