What Is Rise of Olympus?

Rise of Olympus by Play'n GO is a 5x5 grid slot set in ancient Greek mythology that became one of the most acclaimed grid slots of its era. The three Greek gods — Zeus, Poseidon and Hades — each bring unique powers to the grid, and the Wrath of Olympus free-spins round with a God Meter creates one of the most dramatic free-spin experiences in Play'n GO's catalogue.

The game's mechanics are built around individual god powers that can trigger at any time during base game and become central to the free-spins round. Each god alters the grid in a distinct way, and the free-spins God Meter tracks how many times each god has acted, filling toward a final Wrath activation.

RTP & Volatility

Rise of Olympus carries a published RTP of 96.60%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Zeus strikes the reels with lightning, transforming random low-value symbols into wilds. Poseidon sends a wave across the grid, transforming one symbol type into another at random. Hades sucks low-value symbols into the underworld, destroying them from the grid. Each god can activate individually in the base game, or all three can activate in sequence during free spins.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During the bonus, all three gods can appear each spin. The God Meter on the side fills with each god's appearance. When a god's meter is full, that god unleashes a more powerful version of their ability. When all three meters are full simultaneously, the final Wrath of Olympus event triggers — a dramatic full-grid clear and wild drop.

During the base game and free spins, winning clusters remove matched symbols and trigger a tumble, allowing multiple wins per spin. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of three distinct god powers, the God Meter escalation and tumble cascades gives Rise of Olympus extraordinary session variety.

Theme & Design

Ancient Greek marble columns frame a 5x5 grid populated by mythological symbols — the trident of Poseidon, the helmet of Hades, Zeus's thunderbolt, an amphora, a shield, a laurel wreath and playing card ranks. The god characters are dramatically animated. Play'n GO's art direction is excellent — rich golds, deep blues and atmospheric stone textures.

Verdict

Rise of Olympus is one of Play'n GO's finest grid slots. The 96.60% RTP is competitive, the three gods create session variety absent in most single-mechanic slots, and the Wrath of Olympus free-spins escalation delivers genuine drama. For Irish players who enjoy high-volatility grid slots with mechanical depth, it is essential.
