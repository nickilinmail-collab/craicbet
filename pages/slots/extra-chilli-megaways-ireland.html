Key Features

During free spins, the progressive multiplier increments by 1x with each winning tumble cascade. There is no cap. By the time five or six tumble chains occur on a single free-spin turn, the multiplier can reach double figures or beyond. The multiplier does not reset between individual free spins — it carries the full session accumulation.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. The unique feature is a player-choice mechanic: before free spins begin, the player can gamble one free spin at a time to potentially receive more. Each gamble is a 50/50 proposition — winning adds more spins, losing removes one. Players choose when to stop gambling and start the bonus.

The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of a Megaways reel structure offering over 100,000 ways, unlimited multiplier accumulation and player-choice free-spin gambling is what gives Extra Chilli its distinctive character.
