What Is Big Bass Bonanza Keeping It Reel?

Big Bass Bonanza Keeping It Reel by Pragmatic Play is a 2022 entry in the Big Bass franchise that introduces a Reel Respin mechanic alongside the series' signature fisherman free spins. When three or more fish symbols land during the base game, a Reel Respin is triggered — only the reels with fish symbols respin while the others hold, adding a base-game win-enhancement layer.

The Big Bass series has expanded consistently since the original 2020 release. Keeping It Reel differentiates itself through the Reel Respin mechanic — a base-game feature absent from the original Big Bass Bonanza — that adds more engagement per session rather than purely waiting for the free-spins trigger.

Key Features

The Reel Respin mechanic activates when three or more fish symbols land during a base-game spin. The reels that contain fish symbols hold in place while all other reels respin. Additional fish symbols caught during the respin accumulate their monetary values, which are paid out at the end of the respin sequence when no new fish land.

The fisherman Wild free-spins system from the original carries over: three Scatter symbols trigger 10 free spins. During free spins, Wild fisherman symbols collect the monetary values of fish symbols they land adjacent to. The more fish the fisherman collects, the higher the cumulative prize. Additional scatters retrigger extra free spins.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the 10 paylines. The base game's fish symbols carry monetary values in the standard Big Bass format. The combination of the Reel Respin mechanic and the free-spins fishing system gives the game a broader engagement range than the original.

Theme & Design

The same lakeside fishing aesthetic as the original Big Bass Bonanza: the fisherman, the lake, lures, tackle boxes, rubber boots, and bass fish carrying monetary value across the 10-payline grid. The colour palette is warm woodland greens, blues and oranges. The familiar design communicates continuity with the franchise.
