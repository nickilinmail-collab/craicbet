What Is Sugar Rush?

Sugar Rush by Pragmatic Play is a candy-themed 7x7 cluster-pays slot that takes the sweet aesthetic of Sweet Bonanza and rebuilds it on a larger grid with a distinct multiplier trail mechanic. The 2022 release has become a popular alternative to Sweet Bonanza among Irish players who enjoy the candy theme but want a different set of mechanics.

The key difference from Sweet Bonanza is the Sugar Rush Multiplier Trail: a vertical progress bar on the side of the grid that climbs as wins accumulate during both base game and free spins, with multiplier boosts triggered at milestone levels. This gives Sugar Rush a persistent engagement layer absent in most cluster-pays slots.

RTP & Volatility

Sugar Rush carries a published RTP of 96.5%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Multiplier Trail fills with each winning cluster. At milestone levels, all wins on the current spin receive an increased multiplier. Reaching the top of the trail applies a maximum multiplier to any wins. This trail resets after free spins conclude.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the 7x7 grid. During free spins, the Multiplier Trail fills faster and the milestone multipliers are higher than in the base game. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger extra spins.

A Buy Bonus option is available in permitted jurisdictions. The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing multiple wins per paid spin. The 7x7 grid (49 symbols versus 30 in a standard 5-reel game) creates more cluster-formation opportunities per spin.

Theme & Design

A vivid candy world of lollipops, gummy bears, chocolate bars, candy floss, cake slices and boiled sweets across a pastel-pink grid. The design is brighter and more modern than Sweet Bonanza, with a clean UI that communicates the Multiplier Trail progress clearly. The upbeat confectionary soundtrack matches the aesthetic.

Verdict

Sugar Rush is an effective evolution of the Pragmatic Play candy theme. The 96.5% RTP is competitive, the Multiplier Trail adds persistent engagement absent in Sweet Bonanza, and the 7x7 grid creates more cluster opportunities. For Irish players who enjoyed Sweet Bonanza and want something familiar but different, Sugar Rush delivers.
