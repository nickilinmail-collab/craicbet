What Is Le Bandit?

Le Bandit by Hacksaw Gaming is a 2022 Wild West heist-themed slot featuring a distinctive Spins Reloaded mechanic. After any win in the base game, players can gamble their free-spins trigger state — choosing to 'reload' the scatter count at the cost of winning symbols — creating a unique risk/reward decision layer absent from standard slots.

Le Bandit is built around player agency: the Spins Reloaded mechanic gives players an active choice during the base game rather than simply waiting for mechanical outcomes. The outlaw protagonist, wanted posters and Old West train robbery narrative give the game strong thematic identity beyond its mechanical design.

RTP & Volatility

Le Bandit carries a published RTP of 96.1%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Spins Reloaded mechanic allows players to gamble after landing two Scatter symbols. By accepting the reload, winning symbols are cleared from the grid and the remaining scatter(s) hold while all other reels respin. If a third Scatter lands on the respin, free spins trigger. If not, the base game continues. This is an optional risk decision.

Free spins trigger with three or more Scatter symbols, awarding between 10 and 20 free spins depending on the number of scatters. During free spins, Wild symbols expand to cover the full reel height when they land. Expanded Wilds remain sticky for the duration of the free-spins round.

Wild symbols appear on all reels in both base game and free spins, substituting for all non-scatter symbols across the full ways-to-win format. During free spins, expanded sticky Wilds can cover multiple positions across all reels simultaneously, driving the 20,000x maximum potential.

Theme & Design

An Old West train robbery aesthetic: a masked outlaw protagonist, wanted posters, sheriffs, desert canyons, steam locomotives, gold bars and Western typography across the 5x4 grid. The colour palette is warm sands, deep reds and whiskey golds. Hacksaw Gaming's production quality is high throughout.
