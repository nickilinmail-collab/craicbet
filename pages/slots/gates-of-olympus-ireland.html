What Is Gates of Olympus?

Gates of Olympus by Pragmatic Play is a 6x5 cluster-pays slot set in the realm of Zeus that became one of the standout titles of the early 2020s and a firm favourite at Irish online casinos. Tumble mechanics and random Zeus multipliers drive its high-volatility gameplay toward a 5,000x maximum win.

Unlike traditional payline slots, Gates of Olympus pays whenever eight or more matching symbols connect anywhere on the grid. When a cluster wins, the symbols are removed and new ones tumble in, allowing multiple wins from one paid spin. Zeus multipliers — which can appear during base game and free spins — stack additively to amplify any win on that spin.

RTP & Volatility

Gates of Olympus carries a published RTP of 96.5%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience and a sufficient bankroll.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

Zeus himself can randomly scatter multiplier symbols (2x–500x) across the reels at any point. When a win occurs, all multipliers on the grid are added together and applied to the win total. If a 50x and a 100x land on the same spin, the combined 150x is applied to the winning cluster value.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter (golden coin) symbols land anywhere on the grid. Four scatters award 15 spins, five award 20, six award 25. Zeus multipliers continue during free spins, and crucially they do not reset between tumbles within a single free-spin turn — they accumulate progressively.

A Buy Bonus option is available in permitted jurisdictions. During free spins, landing three additional scatter symbols retriggers the bonus. The combination of non-resetting multipliers and tumble cascades during free spins is what makes the theoretical 5,000x max achievable.

Theme & Design

Zeus sits atop Mount Olympus as the gates glow with divine light. The 6x5 grid is set against golden columns and swirling clouds, with symbols including Pegasus, an owl, a ring, a chalice, a watch and Zeus himself. Production is rich and atmospheric, with animated winlines and dramatic symbol explosions on big clusters.

Verdict

Gates of Olympus earns its popularity. The 96.5% RTP, tumble mechanic, stacking multipliers and free-spins round create a compelling high-volatility experience. Sessions can be dry before multipliers align, but when they do, 5,000x is genuinely achievable. Essential for high-volatility slot fans in Ireland.
