What Is Chaos Crew?

Chaos Crew by Hacksaw Gaming is a vibrant 7x7 cluster-pays slot featuring a masked street art crew. Released in 2021, it became one of Hacksaw Gaming's most popular titles and introduced many Irish players to the studio's distinctive style: bold character design, cluster mechanics and a free-spins round with wild multipliers.

Hacksaw Gaming built their reputation on distinctive aesthetics and mechanics, and Chaos Crew exemplifies this. The street art crew characters serve as the high-value symbols, and the game's mechanics centre on a cluster-pays grid with tumble cascades and a multiplier-driven free-spins round.

RTP & Volatility

Chaos Crew carries a published RTP of 96.4%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

During the base game, winning clusters are removed and replaced by tumbling new symbols. Any Bomb symbols that land during the base game can destroy adjacent symbols, clearing space and extending tumble chains. The Bomb mechanic adds a base-game destruction element absent from most cluster pays slots.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land on the 7x7 grid. During free spins, wild symbols appear with multiplier values. Each wild multiplier that contributes to a winning cluster applies its value to the win. Additional wild multipliers can land during free spins, and their values combine on any win they both contribute to.

The combination of cluster pays mechanics, Bomb symbols, and multiplier wilds in free spins creates a game with multiple paths to substantial wins. The 10,000x maximum win sits at the upper end for Hacksaw Gaming's portfolio.

Theme & Design

A street art graffiti world: masked characters in hoodies, spray cans, skateboards and bold typography across a 7x7 grid. The colour palette is saturated urban — bold yellows, greens, reds and purples. Hacksaw Gaming's production quality is high, with energetic animations and a hip-hop-influenced soundtrack.

Verdict

Chaos Crew is a solid cluster-pays slot from Hacksaw Gaming with a distinctive aesthetic and effective mechanics. The 96.4% RTP is competitive, the Bomb mechanic adds base-game variety, and the multiplier wilds in free spins create meaningful win potential. For Irish players who want an alternative to Pragmatic Play cluster pays, Chaos Crew delivers.
