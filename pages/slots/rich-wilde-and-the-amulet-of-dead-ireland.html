What Is Rich Wilde and the Amulet of Dead?

Rich Wilde and the Amulet of Dead by Play'n GO is a 2019 entry in the Rich Wilde Egyptian archaeology series, sharing the same 10-payline expanding-symbol free-spins framework as Book of Dead and Legacy of Dead. The Amulet of Dead features a Charged Wild system where wilds accumulate charges across spins to trigger enhanced multiplier wild transformations.

The Rich Wilde series is Play'n GO's flagship intellectual property — the archaeologist character who traverses various ancient civilisations. Amulet of Dead continues the Egyptian chapter with a Charged Wild mechanic that adds a base-game progression layer absent from the simpler Book of Dead structure.

Key Features

The Charged Wild mechanic works as follows: each Wild symbol that lands in the base game adds a charge to a counter. When the counter reaches a threshold, the charged wild transforms into an Enhanced Wild that carries a multiplier value for the spin it appears on. This base-game progression rewards extended sessions.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. As in other Rich Wilde Egyptian titles, one symbol is designated as the expanding symbol for the entire bonus round. Winning spins featuring the expanding symbol result in full-reel coverage. Enhanced Wilds can also appear during free spins with their multiplier values active.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the standard 10 paylines. The base game's Charged Wild counter resets at the start of each free-spins bonus. The combination of expanding symbol free spins and Enhanced Wild multipliers drives the 5,000x theoretical maximum.

Theme & Design

The Egyptian archaeology setting with Rich Wilde himself rendered as the character in the high-value symbol positions — his fedora, leather jacket and magnifying glass are consistent across the series. Golden amulets, scarab beetles, hieroglyphs and golden statues appear across the 10-payline grid. Play'n GO production quality is excellent.

Rich Wilde and the Amulet of Dead is a well-constructed entry in the Play'n GO Egyptian series that earns its place through the Charged Wild progression mechanic. The 96.37% RTP is competitive, the Charged Wild adds a base-game engagement layer, and the expanding symbol free-spins system is proven. For Irish fans of the Book of Dead style who want additional mechanics, it is recommended.
