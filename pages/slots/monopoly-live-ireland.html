What Is Monopoly Live?

Monopoly Live by Evolution is a licensed live casino game show released in 2019, based on the iconic Hasbro Monopoly board game. It uses the Dream Catcher money wheel format enhanced with two bonus game triggers — 2 Rolls and 4 Rolls — that activate an AR 3D Monopoly board where multipliers accumulate.

The 3D Monopoly board bonus, in which a CGI Mr. Monopoly walks around the game board collecting prizes, is one of the most sophisticated AR features ever built into a live casino product.

RTP & How the House Edge Works

Monopoly Live carries a published RTP of 96.23% (overall). RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The main money wheel has 54 segments: numbers 1, 2, 5 and 10 for direct multiplier wins, plus 2 Rolls and 4 Rolls bonus trigger segments, and one Chance segment. When 2 Rolls or 4 Rolls is hit, the 3D Monopoly bonus board activates.

During the 3D bonus, Mr. Monopoly rolls the dice and walks around the Monopoly board collecting rent from properties, bonus spaces and multiplier fields. 2 Rolls gives two dice rolls; 4 Rolls gives four. Rolling doubles during the bonus extends the number of rolls.

The Chance segment on the wheel reveals a random multiplier bonus for all Chance bets. These multipliers vary in value and add an additional segment type beyond the standard number and roll segments.

Presentation & Design

The full Monopoly property board in CGI form — Mayfair, Park Lane, Bond Street, the green squares, the railways, Jail and Free Parking — rendered as a 3D animated overlay above the physical money wheel studio. Mr. Monopoly himself is the CGI character who moves around the board.
