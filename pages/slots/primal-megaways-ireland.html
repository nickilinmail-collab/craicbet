What Is Primal Megaways?

Primal Megaways by Blueprint Gaming is a prehistoric jungle-themed Megaways slot released in 2019. Blueprint Gaming licensed the BTG Megaways engine and built their unlimited multiplier free-spins format around it, delivering one of their most popular Megaways titles with a dinosaur and jungle theme well-suited to high-energy gameplay.

Blueprint Gaming has established one of the strongest Megaways portfolios outside BTG themselves. Primal Megaways is an early example of their approach: the BTG Megaways engine combined with Blueprint's own free-spins escalating multiplier system and a Reaction mechanic that removes winning symbols for tumble cascades.

RTP & Volatility

Primal Megaways carries a published RTP of 96.26%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

Key Features

The Reaction mechanic (Blueprint's name for the tumble cascade) removes winning symbols from the reels and replaces them with new falling symbols, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin. During free spins, each successive Reaction increments a progressive multiplier by 1x with no cap.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. During free spins, the unlimited progressive multiplier applies to all wins, incrementing with each Reaction. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger extra spins. The escalating multiplier carrying across all free spins is what drives the 50,000x theoretical maximum.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The BTG Megaways variable row heights shift with each spin, delivering between a minimum and maximum of 117,649 ways to win. Blueprint Gaming's implementation is faithful to the BTG template while adding their own visual identity.

Theme & Design

A prehistoric jungle populated by dinosaurs: a T-Rex, a Triceratops, a Brachiosaurus, Pterodactyls and tropical vegetation across the Megaways reels. The colour palette is vivid jungle greens with golden highlights. Blueprint Gaming's production quality is high, with dynamic dinosaur animations on significant wins.
