What Is Immortal Romance?

Immortal Romance by Microgaming is one of the most beloved vampire-romance themed slots ever made, released in 2011 and remaining consistently popular in 2026. It features four progressive free-spins chambers that unlock in sequence — Amber, Troy, Michael and Sarah — each with different mechanics, following a gothic vampire love story narrative.

Immortal Romance's enduring popularity owes much to its storytelling approach — the four chambers represent four different characters in the vampire romance narrative, and each chamber unlocks a mechanically distinct free-spins experience. No other slot from 2011 (and very few from any era) has sustained this level of narrative investment alongside its mechanical design.

RTP & Volatility

Immortal Romance carries a published RTP of 96.86%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Wild Desire feature can trigger randomly on any spin, transforming one to five reels entirely into Wild symbols. This base-game feature can deliver extraordinary wins when four or five full reels become wild simultaneously, and its random trigger makes it the game's most exciting base-game moment.

The Chamber of Spins opens progressively as the free-spins trigger accumulates: the first triggering opens Amber's chamber (10 free spins, 5x multiplier), the fifth opens Troy's (15 spins, Rolling Reels with 2x–5x multiplier trail), the tenth opens Michael's (20 spins, randomly transformed Wild reels) and the fifteenth onwards opens Sarah's (25 spins, Rolling Reels with 2x–10x multiplier trail).

243 ways to win means any adjacent combination of matching symbols in adjacent reels creates a win, without specific payline alignment. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of Wild Desire random wilds, 243 ways and four distinct progressive free-spins chambers creates exceptional replay depth.

Theme & Design

Gothic vampire romance: Sarah's castle, the four vampire characters in their individual chamber cards, ornate candelabras, red roses, blood-red stained glass, ravens, velvet curtains and gothic script across the 243-ways grid. The cinematic orchestral soundtrack is atmospheric and appropriate. The design is classic 2011 Microgaming quality.

Verdict

Immortal Romance is a timeless Microgaming slot that earns its continued presence in Irish casinos through the progressive Chamber of Spins narrative structure, the Wild Desire random feature and an exceptional 96.86% RTP. For Irish players who want historical depth, narrative engagement and genuine mechanical variety, it remains one of the best slots ever made.
