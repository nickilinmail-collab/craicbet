Key Features

Stacked Wild symbols are the game's primary base-game feature: the leprechaun Wild symbol can appear stacked across full reels, dramatically increasing coverage on any spin where it lands. When stacked wilds align across multiple reels simultaneously, the resulting combinations can be highly rewarding on a 20-payline grid.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels, awarding 10 free spins. During free spins, stacked wilds become more prevalent, increasing the probability of multiple-reel stacked-wild alignment. The free-spins round is retrigger-eligible when additional scatters land during the bonus.

The base game and free spins share the same 20-payline structure. No pick-and-click bonus rounds are present — the game is focused on the stacked wild and free-spins mechanics, keeping it simple and accessible for casual players who prefer straightforward gameplay.
