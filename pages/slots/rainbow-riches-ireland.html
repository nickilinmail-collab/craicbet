What Is Rainbow Riches?

Rainbow Riches by Barcrest is one of the most recognisable and beloved Irish-themed slot games ever made, originally created as a British pub slot machine in 2006 before its digital transition. It remains enormously popular across Irish and British online casinos in 2026 for its Road to Riches, Wishing Well and Pots of Gold bonus features.

Rainbow Riches achieved iconic status not through high RTP or extreme volatility mechanics but through three distinct bonus rounds that Irish players have been enjoying for two decades. The Road to Riches, Wishing Well and Pots of Gold bonuses each offer a different form of bonus entertainment, all accessible from a simple 20-payline base game.

RTP & Volatility

Rainbow Riches carries a published RTP of 95%. Medium volatility means the game balances win frequency and payout size.

Key Features

Road to Riches is the game's signature feature: landing three or more Leprechaun Scatter symbols triggers a spinning bonus wheel that moves a player token along a winding road. The further the token travels, the higher the multiplier applied to the player's bet. Reaching the end of the road awards the top multiplier prize.

Wishing Well is a pick-and-click bonus: three Wishing Well Scatter symbols trigger a selection from three wells, each hiding a multiplier prize. Pots of Gold is triggered by three Pots of Gold symbols anywhere on the reels, launching a spinning wheel of gold, silver and bronze pots. The spinning wheel selects the prize pot.

Standard Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the 20 paylines. The base game is deliberately simple — it is a vehicle for the three bonus rounds, which are the game's core appeal. At 95% RTP, Rainbow Riches sits below the ideal for extended play but remains the benchmark Irish-themed slot for nostalgia reasons.

Theme & Design

The classic leprechaun and rainbow iconography: a smiling leprechaun, pots of gold, rainbows, shamrocks, wishing wells and green hills across a 20-payline reel grid. The design is cheerful and characterful, rooted in the original pub machine aesthetic. The jaunty Irish folk music is iconic to the game.

Verdict

Rainbow Riches is an Irish slot classic that belongs in the experience of any player who wants to understand the history of the genre. The 95% RTP is below what many modern alternatives offer, and the low maximum win limits upside — but no other slot carries the same combination of three distinct bonus rounds, pub machine history and Irish cultural resonance. Play it for the experience.
