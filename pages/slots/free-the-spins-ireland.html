What Is Free the Spins?

Free the Spins by Thunderkick is a prison-break-themed slot released in 2021 with a published RTP of 96.31%. The game's central mechanic is a lock and key system for free spins — symbols are locked in jail cells during the base game and must be collected to trigger free spins, creating an engaging and unusual build-up to the bonus.

Thunderkick is a Swedish independent studio known for distinctive visual design and mechanical creativity. Free the Spins exemplifies their approach: a recognisable base theme executed with strong visual design and a mechanical concept — the locked free spins accumulation — that makes the base game actively engaging rather than simply a waiting room for the bonus.

Key Features

The Lock and Key mechanic works as follows: Key symbols land on the reels during the base game and are assigned to padlocked cells. The cells contain free spin symbols — when enough keys are collected to open the cells, the locked free spins are released. The number of free spins released depends on how many cells are successfully opened.

During free spins, Wild symbols can land and are locked in position (Sticky Wilds). Each new Sticky Wild adds to the coverage for the remaining spins. Scatter symbols during free spins retrigger additional spins. The combination of an escalating Sticky Wild count and free-spin retriggering drives the 20,000x theoretical maximum.

The base game uses Wild substitution on 5 reels with 243 ways to win. The Lock and Key mechanic is the base game's primary engagement layer — rather than waiting for scatter symbols, the Key collection system gives every base-game spin a progress dimension. Players can see how close they are to triggering free spins at all times.

Presentation & Design

A stylised Swedish prison setting — stone walls, iron bars, padlocked cells, an escape rope, blueprints and prison uniforms rendered in Thunderkick's distinctive illustrative style. The colour palette is desaturated greys and blues with flashes of orange.
