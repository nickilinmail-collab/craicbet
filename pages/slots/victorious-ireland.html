What Is Victorious?

Victorious by NetEnt is a Roman Empire-themed slot released in 2012 that remains one of NetEnt's highest-RTP titles at 96.89%. The 5-reel, 243-ways slot is designed for low-volatility players who want steady entertainment with solid theoretical value and an occasional free-spins boost.

Like Blood Suckers and Jack Hammer 2, Victorious earns its place in an Irish player's consideration primarily through its RTP. At 96.89%, it delivers theoretical value comfortably above the standard for a low-volatility title. The Roman Legion theme is polished for its era and the free-spins round with 3x multiplier provides the game's peak moments.

RTP & Volatility

Victorious carries a published RTP of 96.89%. Low volatility means wins arrive frequently but tend to be smaller in size, suiting longer sessions.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols (eagle standards) land. Three scatters award 10 free spins, four award 15, five award 20. During free spins, all wins are multiplied by 3x. The combination of a 243-ways structure and a 3x multiplier across all paylines during free spins is what creates the conditions for the 10,000x theoretical maximum.

Wild symbols (a golden arch) substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 243-ways structure means any adjacent combination of matching symbols in adjacent reels pays, without the need for specific payline paths. This gives Victorious one of the most generous base-game coverage structures for a low-volatility title.

There is no additional bonus pick round, no charge meter and no special collection mechanic beyond the free spins. Victorious is straightforward: solid base-game play with a 3x multiplier free-spins round as the bonus peak. For players who want a no-nonsense low-volatility slot with excellent RTP, this structure is a feature.

Theme & Design

Roman legionnaires, centurion helmets, laurel wreaths, eagles, shields and gold coins fill the reels against a Mediterranean stone wall backdrop. The design is polished for 2012 and communicates the Roman theme clearly, if not innovatively. NetEnt's reliable production values deliver clean animations and clear symbol differentiation.

Verdict

Victorious is a low-volatility gem for Irish players who prioritise RTP. At 96.89%, it is among NetEnt's best-value titles. The 243-ways structure gives it a good hit rate, and the 3x multiplier free spins provide reliable bonus peaks. For value-conscious players who prefer low volatility, Victorious and Blood Suckers are the two NetEnt titles worth bookmarking.
