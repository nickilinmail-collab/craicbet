What Is Sweet Bonanza?

Sweet Bonanza by Pragmatic Play is a candy-coloured 6x5 cluster-pays slot that became one of the most played games in Ireland within months of its 2019 release. The colourful fruit and sweet symbols, tumble mechanics and free-spin multiplier bombs give it a 21,100x maximum win that remains among the highest of any mainstream slot.

Sweet Bonanza pays via cluster mechanics — eight or more matching symbols anywhere on the 6x5 grid create a win, those symbols are removed and new ones tumble in for potential follow-up wins. The free spins round features multiplier bomb symbols that multiply together (not add) on any winning spin, creating extraordinary potential.

RTP & Volatility

Sweet Bonanza carries a published RTP of 96.51%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience and a sufficient bankroll.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

Scatter symbols (lollipop) trigger free spins when four or more land. Four scatters award 10 free spins, five award 12, six award 14, rising to 20 for a full six. During free spins, multiplier bomb symbols appear carrying values from 2x to 100x.

Crucially, bomb multipliers multiply together rather than add. Two 100x bombs give a 10,000x multiplier on a single win — not 200x. Combined with a large winning cluster, this is how the theoretical 21,100x maximum is reached. It is rare, but it is real.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. An Ante Bet option in some regions increases scatter frequency for a 25% extra stake cost. During free spins, landing four or more scatters retriggers additional spins.

Theme & Design

Sweet Bonanza is set in a colourful candy world, with strawberries, watermelons, grapes, plums, apples, bananas, hearts and lollipops tumbling across a bright confectionary background. The design is exuberantly cheerful, with bouncing animations on wins and satisfying sugar-themed sound effects.

Verdict

Sweet Bonanza delivers. The 96.51% RTP is solid, the candy theme is irresistible, and the multiplicative bomb system in free spins makes for genuinely heart-pounding moments. The 21,100x maximum win is achievable in theory and verifiable in reality through published big-win records. High volatility means patience, but Sweet Bonanza rewards it.
