What Is Annihilator?

Annihilator by Play'n GO is a heavy metal-themed slot inspired by the Canadian thrash metal band of the same name. Released in 2020, the branded slot combines a Charge Meter with multiple escalating power features, free spins and a highly distinctive rock-and-metal aesthetic that sets it apart visually from virtually every other slot on the market.

The game's high-volatility structure is reflected in its metal heritage: heavy riffs, big hits and long builds toward enormous payouts. The Charge Meter fills during the base game, triggering special features at four levels before culminating in the Free Spins bonus, which begins with a Charge Level amplified from wherever the base-game meter reached.

RTP & Volatility

Annihilator carries a published RTP of 96.74%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Charge Meter has four levels, each triggering a different feature when reached: Level 1 adds extra wilds to the reels; Level 2 transforms low-value symbols into higher-value ones; Level 3 upgrades all symbols on the grid to the highest-value category; Level 4 triggers free spins with an elevated starting multiplier.

Free spins begin at whatever Charge Level was reached when the trigger landed. During free spins, winning combinations add to a progressive multiplier that is applied to subsequent wins. The multiplier does not reset between free spins — it carries forward throughout the entire bonus, potentially building to substantial values.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 20-payline structure gives the game a reasonable base-game hit rate before the Charge Meter features amplify wins. The combination of level-up features and a carrying multiplier in free spins drives the 10,000x theoretical maximum.

Theme & Design

The Annihilator band aesthetic is consistent throughout: dark stage lighting, Marshall amplifier stacks, guitar shapes, skull motifs and the band's own iconography. Play'n GO licensed the band's visual identity comprehensively. The soundtrack features actual Annihilator music tracks, making it one of the most authentically branded music slots available.

Verdict

Annihilator is an excellent branded slot that earns its place on musical merit alone — the genuine heavy metal soundtrack is a strong differentiator. The 96.74% RTP is very competitive, the Charge Meter creates escalating base-game engagement, and the carrying multiplier in free spins creates real big-win potential. Recommended for metal fans and high-volatility slot enthusiasts alike.
