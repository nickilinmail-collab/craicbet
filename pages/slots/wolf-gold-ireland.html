What Is Wolf Gold?

Wolf Gold by Pragmatic Play is a classic American wilderness slot that introduced many Irish players to Pragmatic Play when it launched in 2017. The 5-reel, 25-payline game combines base gameplay with two separate bonus rounds — free spins and a Money Re-spins jackpot feature — giving it broad appeal across player types.

Wolf Gold strikes a balance between volatility levels. The base game and free spins deliver medium-volatility entertainment, while the Money Re-spins jackpot feature introduces the potential for life-changing wins up to 10,000x stake. This combination of steady base gameplay and jackpot ambition is a large part of Wolf Gold's enduring popularity.

RTP & Volatility

Wolf Gold carries a published RTP of 96.01%. Medium volatility means the game balances win frequency with payout size, sitting between the extremes.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

Three Scatter symbols trigger 5 free spins, with retriggering available. The key free-spins feature is the Giant Symbol: a 3x3 giant symbol can randomly appear on reels 3, 4 and 5, acting as nine symbols of the same type and dramatically increasing the probability and size of winning combinations.

The Money Re-spins jackpot triggers when six or more coin symbols land simultaneously. All coin symbols freeze and three re-spins begin. Each new coin landing resets the counter to three. At the end, all frozen coin values are added up — plus Mini, Minor, Major or Grand jackpot prizes if the corresponding positions fill.

The Grand Jackpot requires all 15 reel positions to be filled with coin symbols. Jackpot values are fixed (not progressive) in many configurations, but can still produce significant wins relative to the bet.

Theme & Design

A moonlit North American landscape with wolves, bison, eagles and mustangs across the reels. The Grand Jackpot screen fills with golden coins against a dramatic sky. Wolf howls and an atmospheric western soundtrack give the game a cinematic quality.

Verdict

Wolf Gold delivers two experiences in one package: medium-volatility entertainment in the base game and free spins, plus jackpot ambition through the Money Re-spins. At 96.01% RTP it is fair. Its 2017 vintage shows in the graphics but not in the gameplay.
