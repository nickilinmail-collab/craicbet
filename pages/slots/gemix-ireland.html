What Is Gemix?

Gemix by Play'n GO is an 8x8 grid cluster-pays slot featuring gemstone symbols and a unique Planet feature with four distinct special effects. Released in 2016, it was an early template for the kind of mechanically varied grid slot that the industry has since embraced widely.

The Planet Charge meter fills with each winning cluster. When fully charged, one of four Planet features activates at random: Crystal Strike (destroys symbols in a cross pattern), Nova Blast (clears symbols in a circular area), Star Fall (transforms a cluster of symbols into the highest-value gem), and Crystal Warp (transforms lower-value gems into higher-value gems throughout the grid).

RTP & Volatility

Gemix carries a published RTP of 96.70%. Medium volatility means the game balances win frequency with payout size, sitting between the extremes.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Each Planet feature alters the grid in a distinct way, creating high variety across sessions. Crystal Strike and Nova Blast are removal mechanics that create space for new symbols from above; Star Fall and Crystal Warp are transformation mechanics that upgrade existing symbols to higher values. Each can combine with the tumble cascade for multiple wins.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land on the 8x8 grid. During free spins, the Planet feature activates at the start of each spin rather than requiring a full charge, ensuring consistent feature engagement throughout the bonus round.

The tumble mechanic removes all winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing chain wins from one paid spin. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 8x8 grid of 64 positions creates more cluster-formation opportunities than a standard 5-reel layout.

Theme & Design

Eight different coloured gemstone symbols — including diamonds, rubies, sapphires and emeralds — fill an 8x8 grid against a magical cosmic background. Planet icons in the corners represent the four feature types. The colour palette is vivid and jewel-toned. The design is clean and communicates the Planet Charge progress clearly.

Gemix is a solid medium-volatility grid slot with a good RTP of 96.70% and a genuinely interesting four-planet feature system. For Irish players who want a medium-volatility grid slot that provides variety without extreme patience requirements, Gemix is an excellent choice.
