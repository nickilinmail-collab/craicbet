Key Features

Wild symbols in Wild West Gold carry a multiplier (1x, 2x or 3x) and are sticky during free spins. When a wild with a multiplier is part of a winning combination, its multiplier is applied to the win. During free spins, sticky multiplier wilds remain on the reels and new wilds can land with their own multipliers.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols (a sheriff's badge) land anywhere on the reels: four gives 8 free spins, five gives 15, six gives 20. During free spins, multiplier wilds are sticky and additional scatters retrigger extra spins.

A Money Collect feature can appear during the base game, where certain symbols carry cash values and a Collect symbol sweeps the board, claiming all visible cash values in one go. The combination of sticky multiplier wilds and cash collect creates multiple paths to substantial wins.

Theme & Design

Dusty desert landscapes, saloon fronts and adobe buildings frame the five reels. High-value symbols include a sheriff, an outlaw, a horse and a bull skull. Playing card ranks make up the lower values. Dynamic cinematic animations for big wins and a southern twang soundtrack create strong thematic coherence.
