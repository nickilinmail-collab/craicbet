What Is Fire in the Hole?

Fire in the Hole by Nolimit City is a mining-themed extreme high-volatility slot using the xBomb mechanic — a unique explosive symbol that destroys adjacent symbols, creates wild multipliers and escalates multiplier values through chain explosions. Released in 2021, its mining theme and explosion-driven mechanics give it a distinctive character in Nolimit City's catalogue.

The xBomb mechanic is Fire in the Hole's signature. xBomb symbols are explosive wilds: when an xBomb lands, it destroys adjacent symbols, creating wilds and applying a multiplier to any wins on that spin. When multiple xBombs land simultaneously, their multipliers interact, escalating the total applied to any winning combination.

Key Features

The xBomb creates wilds from adjacent symbol destruction and applies a multiplier. When an xBomb is part of a winning combination directly, its multiplier is applied. When a chain of adjacent xBombs all detonate, their multipliers compound. During free spins, xBombs land more frequently and carry higher base multiplier values.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the xBomb frequency increases and chain detonations become more common. The combination of multiple chaining xBombs during free spins, each carrying elevated multipliers that interact, is what drives the 50,000x theoretical maximum.
