What Is Mega Moolah?

Mega Moolah by Microgaming is the most famous progressive jackpot slot in the world, released in 2006 and holder of the Guinness World Record for the largest online progressive jackpot ever paid (€18.9 million in 2018). The base game is an African safari theme with standard free-spins mechanics, but the game is played primarily for its four-tier Jackpot Bonus.

The 88.12% base RTP is significantly below industry standard. This figure reflects the progressive jackpot contribution — a portion of every bet across the entire global Mega Moolah network funds the four jackpot tiers. Players who choose Mega Moolah are explicitly trading base-game value for participation in the progressive jackpot network.

Key Features

The Jackpot Bonus triggers randomly at the end of any spin, more likely at higher stakes but accessible at any bet level. A jackpot wheel is spun with 20 segments. The outer segments award Mini, Minor and Major jackpots; the innermost segment awards the Mega jackpot — the life-changing prize that has broken records multiple times.

The base game's free-spins feature awards 15 free spins when three or more Scatter symbols land, with all wins tripled (3x multiplier) during the bonus. Wild symbols are doubled (2x multiplier) and substitute for all non-scatter symbols. Combined with the standard 25-payline structure, the free-spins round is straightforward.

The four jackpot tiers — Mini, Minor, Major and Mega — are all guaranteed at their minimum values. The Mega jackpot has a minimum guaranteed level and grows with every bet across the entire Mega Moolah network. The specific current levels are displayed at the casino offering the game.

Theme & Design

An African wildlife safari: lions (wild), elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffalo, hyenas and safari jeeps across the 25-payline grid. The colour palette is golden savannah tones. The design is Microgaming's 2006-era style — characterful and clean, if dated by modern standards. The jackpot wheel overlay is visually distinct from the base game.

Verdict

Mega Moolah is played for the jackpot, not the base game. The 88.12% RTP is the lowest on this list by a significant margin — explicitly disclosed and structurally necessary to fund the global jackpot network. Irish players who choose Mega Moolah do so for the possibility of life-changing wins across the progressive jackpot tiers, understanding that base-game session value is the tradeoff.
