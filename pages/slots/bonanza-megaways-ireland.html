What Is Bonanza Megaways?

Bonanza Megaways by Big Time Gaming is the original Megaways slot — the game that introduced the patented variable-ways engine to the world in 2016. Set in a gold mine, Bonanza Megaways remains one of the most important and influential slots ever created, with an unlimited multiplier free-spins system that every subsequent Megaways game has referenced.

Bonanza's historical significance is inseparable from its appeal: every Megaways slot released by any studio is a direct descendant of this 2016 release. Playing Bonanza is playing the original. The unlimited progressive multiplier during free spins, the tumble cascade mechanic and the scatter anywhere trigger remain compelling in 2026.

RTP & Volatility

Bonanza Megaways carries a published RTP of 96%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The unlimited progressive multiplier works as it has since the original: each winning tumble during free spins increments the multiplier by 1x, with no cap. The multiplier carries across all spins in the bonus. By the time the multiplier reaches double figures on a decent tumble chain, the wins can be extraordinary.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols spell G-O-L-D anywhere on the reels (one letter per reel). During free spins, the unlimited multiplier system applies. Additional scatter landings during free spins retrigger extra spins.

An additional horizontal reel runs above the main six reels, providing extra symbols on every spin. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 117,649 Megaways maximum ways count is delivered through variable 2–7 row heights on six reels.

Theme & Design

An underground gold mine: pickaxes, dynamite bundles, gold nuggets, mine carts and six-sided dice — the original Bonanza symbols are now iconic within the industry. The design is functional rather than spectacular, characteristic of BTG's production style in 2016. The cheerful country music soundtrack is fondly remembered by veteran players.

Verdict

Bonanza Megaways is a slot everyone should play at least once. The 96% RTP is fair, the unlimited multiplier free-spins system is still compelling a decade later, and its historical importance makes it essential context for any Irish player who wants to understand where modern high-volatility slots come from. The low €20 maximum bet is its main practical limitation.
