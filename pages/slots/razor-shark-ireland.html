What Is Razor Shark?

Razor Shark by Push Gaming is a deep-sea slot that has become one of the most admired high-volatility titles at Irish casinos since its 2019 release. Its unique dig mechanic, stacked symbols and enormous 50,000x maximum win have earned it a dedicated following among experienced slot players.

The slot's distinctive mechanic is the Razor Reveal: mystery symbols on the reels can be dug away to reveal multiplied wilds or free spins, adding anticipation to every spin. Combined with rare but potentially huge free-spin wins, Razor Shark sits at the top of Push Gaming's catalogue.

RTP & Volatility

Razor Shark carries a published RTP of 96.70%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience and a sufficient bankroll.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

Mystery symbols marked with a question mark can appear on the reels during both base game and free spins. When a spin resolves, they are dug away to reveal either a multiplied wild (1x–20x), a free spin or an upgrade to a standard symbol. The dig-reveal animation creates genuine visual tension.

Razor Scatter symbols trigger free spins when three or more land: three gives 7 spins, four gives 11, five gives 16. During free spins, multiplied wilds from Razor Reveals carry forward rather than resetting, stacking to produce the conditions for truly enormous wins. These accumulated multipliers drive the 50,000x theoretical maximum.

In the base game, the reef row at the top of the reels contains stacked symbols. When a full row of matching stacked symbols locks, they shift down to the main reel area, often covering large portions of the grid and triggering substantial wins.

Theme & Design

A sunlit underwater reef populated by sharks, tropical fish, coral and oceanic creatures. The colour palette is vivid blues and greens. The shark itself — sharp, sleek and predatory — is the highest-value symbol. The dig animations are satisfying and the atmospheric sound design is excellent.
