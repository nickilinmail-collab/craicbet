What Is Wild Water?

Wild Water by NetEnt is a surfing-themed slot from 2013 that features a Walking Wilds mechanic in its free-spins round. This medium-volatility 5-reel, 20-payline slot appeals to players who want progressive wild coverage during their bonus rounds without the extreme variance of high-volatility titles.

The Walking Wild mechanic is Wild Water's defining feature and gives it specific appeal for free-spins round strategy. When a Wild surfer symbol lands during free spins, it walks one position to the left with each subsequent free spin. Multiple surfer wilds landing during the same free-spins round can create a conveyor of left-moving wilds across the reels.

Key Features

During free spins, Wild surfer symbols land on the reels and progress left by one reel position with each spin. If a surfer is on reel 5 when free spins begin, it will be on reel 4 on the next spin, reel 3 on the spin after, and so on until it falls off the left edge. Multiple surfers landing in the same round stack their progressive left movement.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. The number of scatters determines the number of free spins awarded. During free spins, the Walking Wild mechanic means the bonus round starts relatively clear but builds in wild coverage as surfers accumulate and migrate.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols in the base game on a standard basis. The 20-payline structure gives a reasonable base-game hit rate. Medium volatility balances win frequency and size within the standard spin cycle.

Theme & Design

A tropical surf beach setting: sun, waves, palm trees and surfers on colourful boards across the reels. The highest-value symbol is the surfer dude Wild himself. Coral, fish, surfboards and tropical fruit fill the lower-value positions. The colour palette is vivid beach blues, yellows and whites. The relaxed surf soundtrack matches the theme perfectly.
