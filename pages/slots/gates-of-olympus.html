Gates of Olympus Overview

Gates of Olympus from Pragmatic Play transports you to the realm of the Greek gods, where Zeus himself presides over a 6x5 grid filled with colourful gems and divine symbols. Released in 2021, this slot quickly became one of the most popular titles in Irish online casinos thanks to its thrilling tumble mechanic and the potential for massive multiplier wins.

Unlike traditional payline-based slots, Gates of Olympus uses a scatter pays system. Winning combinations are formed by landing eight or more matching symbols anywhere on the reels, regardless of position. After every winning combination, the tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and drops new ones into place, giving you multiple chances to win from a single spin.

Bonus Features and Free Spins

The real excitement in Gates of Olympus comes from the multiplier orbs that Zeus hurls down onto the reels during both base play and free spins. These multipliers can reach values of 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, and even 500x. During a tumble sequence, all multiplier values are added together and applied to the total win for that round.

Landing four or more scatter symbols triggers the Free Spins round, awarding 15 free spins. During free spins, the multiplier does not reset between tumbles — it accumulates across the entire round, which is where the game's biggest wins materialise. You can retrigger additional free spins by landing more scatters during the bonus round.

For players who prefer to skip straight to the action, Gates of Olympus includes a Buy Bonus feature. This allows you to purchase direct entry into the free spins round for 100x your stake. While it is a significant investment, the accumulated multiplier potential during free spins makes this option appealing for high-volatility enthusiasts.

Theme and Design

The visual presentation is impressive, with a backdrop of golden clouds and classical columns framing the reels. Zeus stands to the left, animated with lightning effects whenever big wins occur. The soundtrack blends orchestral and mythological tones that build tension during winning sequences. Symbols include crowns, hourglasses, rings, and chalices alongside the lower-paying gem symbols in blue, green, red, yellow, and purple.

Verdict

Gates of Olympus is a high-volatility slot that rewards patience and bankroll management. The tumbling mechanic with accumulating multipliers creates genuinely exciting gameplay, especially during free spins where wins can escalate rapidly. With an RTP of 96.5% and a maximum win of 5,000x your stake, this remains one of the best Greek mythology slots available to Irish players in 2026.
