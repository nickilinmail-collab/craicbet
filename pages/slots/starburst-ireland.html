What Is Starburst?

Starburst by NetEnt is the best-known online slot in the world and the standard title for free-spins welcome bonuses at Irish casinos. Launched in 2012, its simple gem-and-jewel format, low volatility and distinctive both-ways payline structure make it one of the most accessible slots for players of all experience levels.

The appeal is in its simplicity: Starburst does not have scatter free spins, bonus rounds or complex cascades. It focuses on one clean mechanic — the expanding Starburst Wild re-spin — delivered with crisp NetEnt production values and a satisfying cosmic soundtrack.

RTP & Volatility

Starburst carries a published RTP of 96.09%. Low volatility means wins arrive frequently but tend to be smaller in size, suiting longer, steadier sessions.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

The Starburst Wild appears only on reels 2, 3 and 4. When it lands, it expands to cover the entire reel and triggers a free re-spin. If another Wild lands during the re-spin it also expands and triggers another re-spin. Up to three consecutive re-spins are possible if Wilds land on all three middle reels.

Paylines run both ways — left to right and right to left — meaning winning combinations pay from either direction. This unusual structure is part of Starburst's enduring charm and helps maintain a healthy hit rate from a simple 5-reel, 3-row layout.

There are no scatter free spins, no multipliers, no bonus picks. Starburst is exactly what it presents: a fast-paced, honest low-volatility slot where the expanding wild re-spins are the entire source of excitement. For players who prefer substance over complexity, that is the point.

Theme & Design

Bright gem shapes — bar, seven, diamond and four coloured gems — glow against a deep space background. The Starburst Wild is a radiating multicoloured star that flares dramatically when it expands. The design remains clean and visually appealing after 14 years, with a dreamy electronic soundtrack.

Verdict

Starburst may no longer be the flashiest slot on the market, but its 96.09% RTP, both-ways paylines and reliable expanding wild re-spins make it a solid, honest low-volatility choice. Its ubiquity in Irish casino bonuses means free spins are easy to come by. For a relaxed session or a first experience with online slots, it is hard to beat.
