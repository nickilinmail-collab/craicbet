What Is Jammin' Jars?

Jammin' Jars by Push Gaming is a vibrantly coloured 8x8 cluster-pays slot that introduced many Irish players to grid-based gaming when it launched in 2018. Its Rainbow Wild multiplier mechanic, cascading tumbles and free-spins round with growing multiplier wilds remain compelling years after release.

The 96.83% RTP is among the highest in Push Gaming's catalogue and makes Jammin' Jars genuinely attractive on pure return-to-player terms. The high volatility means wins are irregular, but the grid's size (64 symbols versus a standard 15) and the multiplier wild system give it a distinct character that has aged very well.

RTP & Volatility

Jammin' Jars carries a published RTP of 96.83%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience and a sufficient bankroll.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing and treat any win as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Key Features

Wild symbols in Jammin' Jars are Rainbow Wilds. When a Rainbow Wild is part of a winning cluster, it moves one position in a random direction and its multiplier value increases by 1x. Each subsequent win it participates in grows it by another 1x, allowing a single wild to accumulate a large multiplier over multiple tumbles.

Free spins are triggered by landing five or more Scatter symbols anywhere on the 8x8 grid. During free spins, Rainbow Wilds land more frequently and retain their position and multiplier for the duration of the bonus. Multiple growing wild multipliers across the large grid drive the 20,000x theoretical maximum.

The tumble mechanic removes all winning symbols and replaces them with new ones from above, allowing chain wins from a single paid spin. This applies in both base game and free spins, extending the duration of lucky spins and amplifying the effect of positioned multiplier wilds.

Theme & Design

A party scene built around colourful jam jars, fruits and vegetables — strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, watermelons, oranges, kiwis, aubergines and bananas — across a vibrant 8x8 grid. The design is exuberantly colourful, the animations are fluid, and the funky electronic soundtrack is appropriately celebratory.

Verdict

Jammin' Jars remains one of the best-value high-volatility slots available to Irish players. The 96.83% RTP is exceptional, the Rainbow Wild multiplier system creates unique progression, and the 8x8 grid gives the game a distinctive feel. It is not a slot for those seeking frequent wins, but for high-variance enthusiasts it is essential.
