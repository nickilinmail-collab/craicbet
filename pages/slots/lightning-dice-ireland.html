What Is Lightning Dice?

Lightning Dice by Evolution is a live casino dice game released in 2018 that applies the Lightning multiplier mechanic from Lightning Roulette to a three-dice total format. Players bet on outcomes from three dice dropped through a glass tower, while the Lightning mechanic randomly strikes one to three totals with multipliers of 50x to 1,000x.

Lightning Dice adapts the Lightning multiplier concept to a dice format. The three dice produce totals from 3 to 18, and players bet on which total they expect. Before each drop, one to three totals are struck by lightning and assigned enhanced multipliers.

RTP & How the House Edge Works

Lightning Dice carries a published RTP of 96.2% (overall). RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Before each dice drop, the Lightning mechanic randomly selects one to three totals from 3 to 18 and assigns multipliers of 50x, 100x, 200x, 300x, 400x or up to 1,000x to those totals. Standard (non-struck) totals pay at their natural dice probability odds.

Three physical dice drop through a glass Lightning Dice tower filled with metalite pegs that physically randomise the dice trajectories. The fall takes several seconds and the trajectory through the tower is visually engaging — a physical randomisation mechanism rather than an RNG.

Players can bet on individual total outcomes (3 through 18) with varying natural odds reflecting their dice probability. Even/Odd side bets are available with near 50/50 probability.

Presentation & Design

The Lightning Dice glass tower studio: a tall transparent column with metalite pegs, dramatic blue and yellow Lightning branding, and the live dealer operating the dice drop. The glass tower is a physical mechanical object — not CGI — giving the game tactile authenticity.
