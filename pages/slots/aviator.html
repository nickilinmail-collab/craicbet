How the Crash Game Mechanic Works

Each round of Aviator begins with a betting phase where you place your wager. You can actually place two separate bets simultaneously, giving you the flexibility to cash out one early for a safe return while letting the other ride for a potentially larger multiplier.

Once the round starts, the multiplier begins at 1.00x and increases rapidly. The multiplier curve is not linear — it accelerates, meaning the number climbs faster the higher it goes. The plane can crash at any moment, and the outcome of each round is determined by a provably fair algorithm that neither the casino nor the players can predict or manipulate.

The provably fair system uses a combination of server seed, client seed, and a hashed result that players can independently verify after each round. This transparency is one of the reasons Aviator has gained such a loyal following among Irish players who value fair play.

Multipliers can theoretically reach up to 10,000x your stake, though in practice, most rounds crash somewhere between 1x and 10x. Rounds where the multiplier exceeds 100x are rare but do occur, and they create memorable moments for anyone who manages to hold their nerve long enough.

This feature is invaluable for players who want to employ a consistent strategy.
