What Is Starlight Princess?

Starlight Princess by Pragmatic Play is an anime-inspired 6x5 cluster-pays slot that became one of Pragmatic Play's most popular releases of 2022. Its celestial fantasy setting, multiplier system and free-spins round parallel the Gates of Olympus formula, applied to a visually striking Japanese anime aesthetic.

Like Gates of Olympus, Starlight Princess pays via cluster mechanics on a 6x5 grid with tumble cascades. The critical difference is in the multiplier system: instead of Zeus dropping multipliers, Starlight Princess features a Gate Charge mechanic that fills a progress bar and triggers a random multiplier applied to any win on the charging spin.

RTP & Volatility

Starlight Princess carries a published RTP of 96.5%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure calculated across millions of spins — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Gate Charge mechanic fills with each winning tumble during base game and free spins. When fully charged, it triggers a random multiplier applied to the next win on that spin. The multiplier values are not disclosed in advance — they are revealed when the charged spin resolves.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the grid. During free spins, the Gate Charge multiplier effect is enhanced, with higher multiplier values available. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger extra spins.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions, allowing direct purchase of the free-spins round. The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing multiple wins from one paid spin in both base game and free spins.

Theme & Design

A celestial realm populated by an anime-style princess in ornate pink and gold robes, surrounded by cherry blossoms, stars, celestial orbs and sparkles. The colour palette is soft pinks, purples and gold against a twilight sky. The visual execution is appealing and distinctive — Pragmatic Play's highest-quality aesthetic for the anime market segment.

Starlight Princess is an excellent addition to the Pragmatic Play high-volatility catalogue. The 96.5% RTP matches Gates of Olympus, the Gate Charge mechanic adds a base-game engagement layer, and the anime aesthetic gives it strong individual identity. For Irish players who enjoy Pragmatic Play cluster pays slots, Starlight Princess is worth exploring alongside Gates of Olympus.
