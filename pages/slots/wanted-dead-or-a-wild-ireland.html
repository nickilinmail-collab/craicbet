What Is Wanted Dead or a Wild?

Wanted Dead or a Wild by Hacksaw Gaming is a western-themed slot with one of the most dramatic free-spins executions in modern slots. Its Shootout free-spins round, multiplier bandana wilds and an intensity that escalates with every spin have made it one of Hacksaw Gaming's signature titles since its 2022 release.

The game's very high volatility is core to its identity — the original Dead or Alive 2 is a natural comparison point. Where Dead or Alive 2 uses sticky wilds, Wanted Dead or a Wild builds its free-spins system around multiplier bandana wilds and a Wanted Poster wild-multiplier escalation that can grow dramatically across consecutive wins.

Key Features

During the base game, Wild symbols (bandana-masked outlaws) appear on the reels with or without multipliers. When a wild multiplier is part of a winning combination, its multiplier is applied to the win. Wild multiplier values in the base game range from 2x upward.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols (Wanted Posters) land. During free spins, the Wanted Poster Shootout begins. All wild multipliers that land are retained as sticky wilds. Each subsequent win that involves an existing sticky wild increases that wild's multiplier by 1x. This progressive escalation is what drives the 25,000x maximum.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. The 10-payline structure means wins must align across specific paths, keeping the game's focus on the sticky multiplier wild system rather than broad coverage. Very high volatility means long base-game sessions before the free-spin trigger lands.

Theme & Design

A cinematic Wild West setting: sheriff stars, six-shooters, whiskey bottles, Wanted posters, Stetson hats and cacti across a dusty frontier background. The highest-value symbol is the outlaw himself. Dark, atmospheric colour palette of ochres, browns and reds. The gunshot sound effects and western soundtrack are cinematic and immersive.

Wanted Dead or a Wild is a masterpiece of high-volatility slot design from Hacksaw Gaming. Very high volatility means this is strictly for experienced players — but those players will find it unmissable.
