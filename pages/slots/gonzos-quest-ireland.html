What Is Gonzo's Quest?

Gonzo's Quest by NetEnt is one of the most historically significant slots ever made, released in 2011 as the first major slot to introduce the Avalanche (cascade/tumble) mechanic and a 3D animated character front-and-centre in the game frame. The game features the Spanish conquistador Gonzo on his quest for El Dorado, with Avalanche mechanics and free-fall multipliers.

Gonzo's Quest is credited with introducing the Avalanche mechanic to mainstream slots — a system where winning symbols explode and are replaced with new falling symbols from above, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin. This mechanic has since been adopted by hundreds of slots across the industry. Playing the original is playing a piece of slot history.

Key Features

The Avalanche mechanic removes winning symbol combinations and drops new symbols from above to fill the spaces. Each consecutive Avalanche within a single paid spin increments a multiplier: 1x, 2x, 3x and 4x in the base game. Free falls multipliers reach 3x, 6x, 9x and 15x for four or more consecutive Avalanches during the bonus.

Free falls (free spins) trigger when three or more Free Fall symbols land anywhere on the reels, awarding 10 free falls. During the bonus, the Avalanche multiplier sequence applies with enhanced values: 3x, 6x, 9x and 15x for four or more consecutive Avalanche wins. Additional Free Fall symbols during the bonus retrigger extra free falls.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the 20 paylines. The stone block symbols on the reels can be unlocked by landing the same symbol in the same position across multiple consecutive Avalanches, contributing to the visual storytelling of Gonzo unlocking the city of El Dorado.

Theme & Design

The Aztec/El Dorado setting rendered in NetEnt's pioneering 2011 3D animation style: the lovable Spanish conquistador Gonzo, stone carved temple symbols, golden statues, ancient script, jungle ruins and the face of El Dorado in the background. Gonzo reacts to wins with exuberant celebrations. The design has dated somewhat but remains charming.

Verdict

Gonzo's Quest is essential playing for any Irish slots player who wants to understand the history of the medium. The 96% RTP is fair, the Avalanche mechanic with free-fall multipliers is still compelling, and the game's historical importance — as the originator of the cascade mechanic used in hundreds of modern slots — makes it the most historically significant game on this list after Bonanza Megaways.
