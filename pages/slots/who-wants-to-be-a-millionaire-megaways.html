Key Features

The 50:50 Lifeline removes half of the low-value symbols from the reels, increasing the concentration of higher-value symbols on subsequent spins. Phone a Friend activates a multiplier wild for a limited number of spins. Ask the Audience temporarily boosts the number of ways to win by forcing maximum reel heights.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the unlimited progressive multiplier system applies — each winning tumble increments the multiplier by 1x with no cap. The multiplier does not reset between individual free spins, carrying the full accumulated value.

The Lifeline features can trigger at any time during the base game, adding an unpredictable enhancement layer to standard Megaways gameplay. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of Megaways variable ways, Lifeline enhancements and the unlimited multiplier free-spins system drives the 50,000x maximum.
