What Is Bridesmaids Slot?

Bridesmaids by Microgaming is a licensed slot released in 2015 based on the 2011 Universal Pictures comedy film. It features one of the highest published RTPs of any branded slot at 97.09%, and offers five bonus features that reflect the film's key characters and moments — including free spins with multipliers and two pick-and-click bonus rounds.

The 97.09% RTP is the defining characteristic of Bridesmaids for value-conscious Irish players. Key Features

Five bonus features: Free Spins (up to 25 spins with up to 10x multiplier), Wilds on Wheels (randomly triggered wilds), Friendship Feature (triggered randomly, awards coin prizes to all players at the table simultaneously), Cake feature (pick-and-click to reveal a prize from a wedding cake) and the Spin My Shoes mini-game.

The Free Spins feature is triggered by scatter symbols and awards between 10 and 25 free spins depending on the number of scatters, with multipliers from 3x to 10x applied. The combination of up to 25 spins and a 10x multiplier is what gives Bridesmaids its bonus ceiling despite the low volatility.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the 40 paylines. The 40-payline structure provides excellent base-game coverage and a high hit rate consistent with the game's low-medium volatility positioning.

Theme & Design

The five main characters from the Bridesmaids film — Annie, Lillian, Megan, Becca and Helen — are the primary symbols, alongside wedding dresses, rings, cakes and other wedding iconography. The colour palette is pinks, creams and golds consistent with the film's visual language. The design is faithful to the brand.

Bridesmaids is an excellent-value branded slot for Irish players. The 97.09% RTP is exceptional for a licensed game, the five bonus features provide genuine variety, and the low-medium volatility gives consistent sessions. For players who want branded content with the best available RTP, Bridesmaids is a standout recommendation.
