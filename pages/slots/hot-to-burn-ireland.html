What Is Hot to Burn?

Hot to Burn by Pragmatic Play is a classic-style fruit slot that strips everything back to basics: five reels, five paylines, fruit symbols and a single bonus feature in the form of free spins. Despite its retro simplicity, its 96.74% RTP is one of the highest in Pragmatic Play's entire catalogue.

In an era where slots compete on feature complexity, Hot to Burn's appeal lies in its honesty. No tumbles, no multipliers, no bonus picks — just fast-paced fruit machine gameplay with a free-spins round that is easy to understand. RTP & Volatility

Hot to Burn carries a published RTP of 96.74%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

Key Features

Three or more Scatter symbols (a star) trigger free spins: three awards 5 spins, four awards 15, five awards 25. During free spins, symbols are more frequently stacked, increasing the probability of winning combinations across the five paylines.

Wild symbols (a burning 7) substitute for all other symbols to complete winning combinations. As a pure 5x3 grid game, the wild's impact is immediate — a burning 7 in the centre column can connect symbols across all five paylines at once.

There are no bonus pick rounds, no multipliers and no special collection mechanics. Hot to Burn is exactly what its interface shows: a clean, fast fruit machine where the scatter free-spins round is the sole bonus mechanic.

Theme & Design

Bold reds and yellows dominate the flaming fruit machine aesthetic. Classic symbols — cherries, lemons, oranges, plums, watermelons and lucky 7s — are styled with flame effects, and the high-temperature name is reflected in every visual element. The design is clean, retro and visually punchy.
