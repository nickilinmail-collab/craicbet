What Is Crazy Time?

Crazy Time by Evolution is a live casino game show released in 2020, based on a money wheel format with four interactive bonus games: Cash Hunt, Pachinko, Coin Flip and the namesake Crazy Time bonus. It is one of the highest-grossing and most-watched live casino products ever created.

Crazy Time is Evolution's most ambitious game show production — a full studio built around a giant illuminated money wheel operated by a live host, with four different bonus game rooms each featuring unique mechanics. No two sessions look identical because the four bonus games each deliver a completely different form of gameplay.

RTP & How the House Edge Works

Crazy Time carries a published RTP of 96.08% (overall). RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The money wheel has 54 segments: 23 for number 1, 15 for 2, 7 for 5, 4 for 10, one each for the four bonus games, and two Crazy Time segments. Players bet on number segments or bonus game segments. When a bonus game is hit, it activates that game's room.

Cash Hunt fires 108 random multipliers across a giant screen and players shoot one to reveal their prize. Pachinko is a giant physical Pachinko wall — a puck drops through pegs to land on a multiplier. Coin Flip spins two sides of a coin to reveal gold or silver multipliers. The Crazy Time bonus is a giant wheel with sections that double, triple or spin again.

The Top Slot mechanic randomly selects a colour and multiplier before each spin. All segments of that colour on the main wheel are multiplied by the Top Slot value for that round, dramatically amplifying certain segments when the main wheel stops on them.

Presentation & Design

A vast custom-built live studio with a giant illuminated money wheel, a live host in a colourful suit, four separate bonus game rooms, and Evolution's signature production quality. The energy is deliberately high-entertainment.
