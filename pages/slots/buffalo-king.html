Buffalo King Overview

Buffalo King from Pragmatic Play is a wildlife-themed slot that takes place on the sweeping plains of North America, where majestic bison roam alongside eagles, cougars, moose, and wolves. Playing on a 6x4 grid with 4,096 ways to win, this high-volatility slot is built for players who chase life-changing payouts. With a staggering maximum win of 93,750x your stake, Buffalo King sits among the most explosive slots available to Irish players.

The 4,096 ways to win system means there are no traditional paylines. Instead, wins are formed by landing matching symbols on consecutive reels from left to right, regardless of their position on each reel. With four symbol positions per reel across six reels, the sheer number of possible combinations creates frequent opportunities for multi-way wins, particularly when stacked symbols align across the grid.

Buffalo King draws clear inspiration from the classic buffalo-themed slots that have been popular in land-based casinos for decades, but Pragmatic Play has added their own twist with stacked wilds and a free spins mechanic that can deliver truly extraordinary payouts.

Stacked Wilds

The wild symbol in Buffalo King is represented by the golden sunset icon, and it can appear stacked on any of the six reels. When stacked wilds land, they substitute for all symbols except the scatter, creating multiple winning combinations simultaneously across the 4,096 ways to win system.

A fully stacked wild reel covers all four positions on that reel, effectively making every symbol on adjacent reels part of a potential winning combination. When two or more reels display fully stacked wilds, the results can be spectacular, as every possible way-to-win combination is activated at once. This is particularly potent in the free spins round where additional wild reels are added to the mix.

Free Spins with Extra Wilds

Landing three or more scatter symbols triggers the free spins round. Three scatters award 8 free spins, four scatters award 15 free spins, five scatters award 25 free spins, and the rare six-scatter landing awards a massive 100 free spins. The number of free spins you receive significantly impacts your potential returns, as more spins mean more opportunities for stacked wilds to cover the reels.

During the free spins bonus, extra wild reels are added to the game. Depending on the number of scatters that triggered the feature, additional reels become wild reels that are fully stacked with wild symbols on every spin. This dramatically increases the frequency and size of wins throughout the bonus round.

With four, five, or six scatter triggers, multiple reels can become permanently wild for the entire free spins duration. Combined with natural stacked wilds landing on the remaining reels, this creates scenarios where nearly the entire grid is filled with wilds and high-value symbols, producing the enormous wins that give Buffalo King its legendary 93,750x maximum payout.

The free spins round can be retriggered by landing additional scatters during the bonus, extending the round and keeping those extra wild reels active for even longer. Extended free spins sessions with multiple wild reels active are where Buffalo King's most extreme wins occur.

Theme and Design

Buffalo King features a vast North American prairie landscape as its backdrop, complete with rolling grasslands, distant mountains, and a dramatic sky that shifts between golden sunsets and stormy atmospheres. The visual design captures the raw beauty and grandeur of the American wilderness.

The high-value symbols are beautifully rendered wildlife — a powerful bison, a soaring eagle, a stalking cougar, a stoic moose, and a howling wolf. Lower-value symbols use stylised card values (A, K, Q, J, 10, 9) that blend into the natural colour palette. The buffalo symbol is the highest-paying regular symbol, and landing six across the reels with multiplied ways delivers premium payouts.

The soundtrack uses sweeping orchestral arrangements reminiscent of nature documentaries, building to dramatic crescendos during big wins. Animal sound effects are woven into the audio design, with the thundering hooves of a buffalo stampede accompanying the largest payouts.
