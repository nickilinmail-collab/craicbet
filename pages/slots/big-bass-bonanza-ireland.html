What Is Big Bass Bonanza?

Big Bass Bonanza by Pragmatic Play launched in 2020 and quickly became a phenomenon at Irish online casinos, spawning one of the most prolific slot series in history. The fishing-themed 5-reel, 10-payline slot combines straightforward mechanics with an enormously entertaining free-spin round driven by cash-collecting Fisherman symbols.

The Big Bass franchise now spans well over a dozen titles, but the original holds its own against its sequels. The 96.71% RTP is excellent for a high-volatility slot, and the game's combination of accessible gameplay and big free-spin potential has kept it popular with Irish players who want excitement without overly complex mechanics.

Big Bass Bonanza carries a published RTP of 96.71%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience and a sufficient bankroll.

Key Features

The Fisherman symbol is the game's hero. During the base game, landing a Fisherman alongside a Fish symbol with a cash value triggers a collection mechanic — the Fisherman collects that cash amount. Multiple Fishermen on the reels simultaneously multiply the value collected.

Three or more Scatter symbols (a fishing magazine) trigger free spins: three gives 10 spins, four gives 15, five gives 20. During free spins, Fisherman symbols expand to fill an entire reel and collect values from all visible Fish symbols in one sweep, with multipliers (1x, 2x or 3x) amplifying the collected amount.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. During free spins, additional scatter landings can retrigger extra spins. The combination of expanding multiplied Fishermen and a full board of valued Fish symbols drives the slot's most memorable wins.

Theme & Design

A lakeside fishing spot under a clear sky. The reels feature the Fisherman, cash-valued Fish, a fishing rod, rubber boots, a tackle box and playing card symbols. The cheerful country soundtrack and laid-back aesthetic belie the high-volatility nature of the game.
