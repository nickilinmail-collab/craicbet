What Is Emerald King?

Key Features

Wild symbols (the emerald crown) substitute for all non-scatter symbols to complete winning combinations across the 20 paylines. The wild's coverage on a 5-reel, 3-row grid with 20 paylines gives it meaningful impact when it appears on the central reels.

Three or more Scatter symbols (a pot of gold) trigger free spins: three gives 10 spins, four gives 15, five gives 25. During free spins, symbols are more frequently stacked and special expanding symbols can appear, increasing the probability of full-reel wins across multiple paylines.

A bonus wheel feature can trigger at the end of a free-spin sequence, awarding multiplied wins or additional free spins. The combination of stacked symbols and the bonus wheel creates the conditions for the 1,000x maximum win.

Theme & Design

Lush green Irish countryside, rainbows, pots of gold, lucky horseshoes and the green-robed Emerald King himself feature across the reels. The colour palette is vivid emerald greens and golds. The cheerful Celtic-inspired soundtrack and dynamic animations give the game a festive, approachable character.
