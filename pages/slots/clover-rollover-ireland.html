What Is Clover Rollover?

Clover Rollover by NextGen Gaming is a classic Irish-themed slot released in 2010 with one of the highest published RTPs of any Irish-themed game at 97.03%. Key Features

The Clover Rollover mechanic is the game's signature feature: when a full screen of matching symbols appears (all 15 positions on the five-reel, three-row grid showing the same symbol), a Rollover triggers — the reels spin again with the same symbol stacked across the entire grid. If another full screen appears, it rolls over again, with no limit on the number of Rollover repetitions.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels, awarding 10 free spins. During free spins, stacked symbols appear more frequently, increasing the probability of a Rollover trigger. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger further bonus spins.

Stacked symbols in the base game appear on all five reels — not just the Wild symbol, but regular value symbols too. When multiple reels show stacked versions of the same symbol, the combination of 25 paylines and stacked coverage creates strong base-game win potential even without triggering free spins.

Theme & Design

Classic Irish countryside: four-leaf clovers, gold coins, horseshoes, rainbows, leprechaun hats and shamrocks across a five-reel grid with green rolling hills in the background. The design is clean and functional, characteristic of NextGen's 2010-era production style. The traditional Irish music is warmly familiar.
