What Is Moon Princess?

Moon Princess by Play'n GO is a magical girl anime-inspired 5x5 grid slot that introduced the Power Charge mechanic to Play'n GO's portfolio. Released in 2017, its combination of three individual character power meters, a Trinity feature when all three charge, and a free-spins round with wild-generating princesses make it one of the more mechanically interesting Play'n GO grid slots.

The three Moon Princess characters — Love (pink), Star (yellow) and Storm (blue) — each have their own charge meter. Each fills as that character's symbol appears in winning clusters. When a character's meter fills, she activates her power. When all three fill simultaneously, the Trinity feature triggers.

RTP & Volatility

Moon Princess carries a published RTP of 96.51%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Love's power converts all hearts symbols into wilds. Star's power converts all star symbols into wilds. Storm's power destroys all low-value symbols from the grid. When all three powers activate in one spin sequence (Trinity), all symbols are destroyed and wild symbols are dropped onto the grid for a guaranteed major win.

Free spins trigger when a Scatter symbol lands on the grid. The player chooses one of the three princesses to accompany the bonus. The chosen princess applies her power at the start of each free spin, potentially converting or destroying symbols before each spin resolves. This gives the player a degree of agency in shaping the bonus.

During free spins, winning clusters of four or more matching symbols automatically generate a wild symbol in the win location. This wild remains for subsequent spins, gradually filling the grid with wild symbols if wins continue. The combination of the princess power and accumulating wilds is what drives the theoretical 4,000x maximum.

Theme & Design

An anime-styled magical girl world of pink cherry blossoms, stars, hearts, lightning bolts and rainbow orbs. The three princesses are richly animated, each with distinct visual styling and power animations. Play'n GO's production quality is high throughout — fluid animations, expressive character reactions and a dreamy magical soundtrack.

Verdict

Moon Princess is a mechanically interesting Play'n GO grid slot with strong anime identity. The 96.51% RTP is solid, the Trinity feature creates dramatic all-clear moments, and the choice of princess in free spins gives players agency. For Irish fans of anime-themed slots, it is a natural recommendation alongside Starlight Princess.
