What Is Dazzle Me?

Dazzle Me by NetEnt is a gem-themed slot with an unusual asymmetric reel structure — the reels widen from left to right, with reel 1 having 3 rows, reel 2 having 4 rows, reel 3 having 4 rows, reel 4 having 5 rows and reel 5 having 5 rows. This unusual structure creates 76 paylines and a visually distinctive playing field.

The asymmetric reel layout is Dazzle Me's most distinctive mechanical feature. The widening reel structure means the rightmost reels contribute disproportionately to winning combinations, and wild symbols on reels 4 and 5 cover more positions than on reels 1 and 2. This structural asymmetry gives the game a different feel from standard 5x3 layouts.

RTP & Volatility

Dazzle Me carries a published RTP of 96.07%. Low volatility means wins arrive frequently but tend to be smaller in size, suiting longer sessions.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Linked Reels feature is Dazzle Me's key bonus mechanic. During any spin, two or three reels can link together to show identical symbols in all linked positions. When three full reels show the same symbol type due to Linked Reels, the winning combination coverage across the asymmetric 76-payline structure can be enormous.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the Linked Reels feature is more frequent, and more reels can link simultaneously. The combination of three or four linked reels during a free spin creates the conditions for multi-payline wins across the entire asymmetric grid.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 76-payline structure gives a high base-game hit rate. The low volatility means consistent small wins with the Linked Reels feature providing the notable peaks.

Theme & Design

A glittering showgirl aesthetic: sequins, sparkles, diamond rings, gold bars and gem-studded symbols across the asymmetric widening reels. The colour palette is saturated pinks, purples and golds. The design is brash and celebratory, capturing a Las Vegas revue aesthetic. NetEnt's production quality is consistent.

Verdict

Dazzle Me earns its place through its unique asymmetric reel structure and 76 paylines. The 96.07% RTP is fair, the low volatility suits casual play, and the Linked Reels feature adds the game's most engaging moments. For Irish players who want a structurally different NetEnt experience, Dazzle Me is worth trying.
