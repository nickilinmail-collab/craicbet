What Is Jurassic World Slot?

Jurassic World by Microgaming is a licensed slot released in 2016 based on the 2015 Universal Pictures Jurassic World film. It features three distinct free-spins modes — Gyrosphere Valley, Creation Lab and Indominus Rex Wilds — each with different mechanics and volatility characteristics, alongside a 243-ways structure.

The Jurassic World licence is authentically deployed — the Indominus Rex, the Gyrosphere vehicles, the creation lab and the film's key characters are all present. The three free-spins modes differentiate themselves mechanically in a way that reflects distinct aspects of the film's narrative, giving players real choice.

RTP & Volatility

Jurassic World Slot carries a published RTP of 95.45%. Medium volatility means the game balances win frequency and payout size.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Three free-spins modes accessible from the Feature Choice Bonus: Gyrosphere Valley (5 free spins, stacked wilds with sticky respin), Creation Lab (10 free spins, random extra wilds added each spin) and Indominus Rex Wilds (5 free spins, Indominus Rex wild transformation feature with multipliers).

The Feature Choice Bonus also includes a Giganotosaurus feature where the dinosaur walks across the reels, randomly transforming symbols into wilds, wild multipliers or scatter symbols. This random base-game feature can trigger significant wins when multiple wild transformations occur simultaneously.

243 ways to win means any adjacent combination of matching symbols across adjacent reels creates a win. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of a base-game random feature, three distinct free-spins modes and 243 ways provides varied session experiences.

Theme & Design

The Jurassic World film locations and creatures: the Indominus Rex, the Velociraptor squad, the Pteranodon, the Gyrosphere, the Creation Lab, Owen Grady and Claire Dearing — all rendered in high-quality Microgaming style. The film's John Williams-inspired soundtrack plays during gameplay. The visual design is a faithful recreation of the film's aesthetic.
