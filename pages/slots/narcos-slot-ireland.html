What Is Narcos Slot?

Narcos by NetEnt is a branded slot based on the Netflix Narcos TV series, featuring characters and aesthetics from the show including Pablo Escobar and DEA agents. Released in 2019, the 243-ways, 5-reel slot combines recognisable brand identity with NetEnt's reliable production quality and a Drive-By feature that adds explosive action to any spin.

The branded content is well-integrated — Netflix's visual assets are reproduced faithfully, and the slot captures the tension of the TV series through its feature design. The Drive-By feature is the game's most memorable mechanic and can trigger at any point during base game or free spins.

RTP & Volatility

Narcos Slot carries a published RTP of 96.23%. Medium-High volatility means the slot sits between medium and high, offering a reasonable hit rate alongside larger payouts.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Drive-By feature triggers randomly. A car drives across the reels and fires bullets at the grid, transforming random symbol positions into wilds. The more bullet hits, the more wild symbols are created. This random wild-injection mechanic adds unpredictability to any spin and can significantly boost wins when it fires.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the Drive-By feature is more frequent, wild symbols are more prevalent, and Walking Wilds can appear — wild symbols that move one position to the left with each free spin, staying on the grid until they fall off the left edge. Multiple Walking Wilds across the reels at the same time create significant coverage.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 243-ways structure means winning combinations pay from left to right across any positions in adjacent reels without fixed payline paths. The combination of random Drive-By wilds, Walking Wilds during free spins and 243 ways gives the game a decent base-game hit rate.

Theme & Design

1980s Colombia: Pablo Escobar, DEA agents Steve Murphy and Javier Pena, cocaine bricks, police badges, stacks of cash and the Narcos show logo. The colour palette is vivid tropical greens and golds. NetEnt's production quality is high — character animations are fluid and the licensed content is integrated seamlessly.

Verdict

Narcos is a solid medium-to-high volatility branded slot that delivers on its TV series premise. The 96.23% RTP is fair, the Drive-By mechanic adds excitement to any spin, and the Walking Wilds in free spins create consistent coverage. For Irish fans of the Narcos series who want a slot that reflects their favourite show, it is a natural choice.
