Book of Dead Overview

Book of Dead by Play'n GO is one of the most iconic online slots ever created and remains a staple at Irish online casinos nearly a decade after its 2016 release. Following the adventurer Rich Wilde on his journey through ancient Egyptian tombs, this 5-reel, 10-payline slot delivers classic high-volatility gameplay with a focus on one explosive bonus feature: expanding symbols during free spins.

Book of Dead belongs to the beloved "book" slot genre, where a single special symbol acts as both wild and scatter, and where free spins revolve around a randomly chosen expanding symbol. The format has been imitated countless times, but Play'n GO's original remains one of the best and most widely played versions available to Irish players.

Bonus Features and Free Spins

The Book of Dead symbol is the key to everything in this slot. It serves as both the wild symbol, substituting for all other symbols to complete winning combinations, and the scatter symbol that triggers the Free Spins round. Landing three or more Book of Dead symbols anywhere on the reels awards 10 free spins.

Before the free spins begin, a special expanding symbol is randomly selected from the paytable. Whenever this chosen symbol appears during free spins, it expands to fill the entire reel. If the expanding symbol lands on enough reels to form a winning combination across your active paylines, the results can be spectacular. Landing Rich Wilde as the expanding symbol across multiple reels on a 10-payline setup can produce the game's maximum 5,000x payout.

Book of Dead also features a Gamble option after any win, where you can risk your winnings on a card colour or suit prediction. Correctly guessing the colour doubles your win, while guessing the suit quadruples it. This optional feature adds an extra layer of risk-reward for players who enjoy pushing their luck.

Theme and Design

The Egyptian theme is executed with polish and atmosphere. The reels are set inside a golden-framed tomb, with hieroglyphic walls visible in the background. High-value symbols include Rich Wilde himself, the golden bird of Horus, Anubis, Osiris, and the scarab, while the lower-value symbols are traditional playing card values styled in an Egyptian aesthetic. The animations are smooth, with satisfying reel spin effects and dramatic reveals during the expanding symbol feature. The soundtrack evokes mystery and adventure with Middle Eastern-inspired instrumentation.

Verdict

Book of Dead has earned its legendary status for good reason. The high volatility means patience is required, but when the expanding symbol feature delivers, the results can be extraordinary. The 96.21% RTP is solid, the gameplay is intuitive, and the gamble feature adds optional excitement. After a decade, Book of Dead remains one of the most-played slots in Ireland, and it continues to be a go-to title for both newcomers and experienced players. If you have never tried a "book" slot, this is the definitive place to start.
