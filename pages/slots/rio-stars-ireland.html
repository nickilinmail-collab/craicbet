What Is Rio Stars?

Rio Stars by Play'n GO is a Brazilian carnival-themed slot that combines vibrant festival aesthetics with a multiplier wild system and a free-spins round where multipliers grow progressively. The 2020 release offers high-volatility gameplay with a 20,000x maximum win and an excellent 96.74% RTP.

The game's carnival setting provides an energetic backdrop for its multiplier-based mechanics. Wild symbols in Rio Stars carry multiplier values (1x–5x in the base game, escalating further in free spins), and the free-spin round features a multiplier ladder that climbs with each winning spin.

RTP & Volatility

Rio Stars carries a published RTP of 96.74%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Wild symbols appear on reels 2, 3, 4 and 5. Each wild carries a multiplier value. When a wild substitutes in a winning combination, its multiplier is applied to the win total. During free spins, wild multiplier values start higher and increase with each winning spin — a 2x wild in the base game might be a 6x or 8x in later free spins.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. During free spins, all wild multiplier values increase with each successive winning spin. The escalating wild multipliers combined with 20 paylines and a healthy reel coverage create the conditions for the 20,000x theoretical maximum.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. Wild multipliers and the free-spins escalation ladder are the primary mechanics — there are no secondary bonus pick rounds or complex charge meters. Rio Stars is mechanically cleaner than some Play'n GO titles, focusing on its multiplier wild system.

Theme & Design

A Rio de Janeiro carnival setting bursting with colour: feathered headdresses, samba dancers, carnival masks, tropical birds and sparkling stars fill the reels. The colour palette is saturated reds, yellows and oranges. The samba soundtrack is appropriately celebratory and the animation quality is high.

Verdict

Rio Stars is a solid high-volatility slot from Play'n GO with an excellent 96.74% RTP and a multiplier wild system that creates genuine escalation potential. The 20,000x maximum win is ambitious, the carnival theme is vibrant, and the mechanics are approachable. For Irish players who want a high-RTP high-volatility title with a lively aesthetic, Rio Stars delivers.
