What Is Mental?

Mental by Nolimit City is a psychedelic extreme high-volatility slot set in a surreal asylum world. Released in 2021, it uses the xSplit mechanic alongside a distinctive multiplier ladder system in its free-spins round, with a thematic setting that is entirely unique in the online slots market.

The xSplit mechanic is Mental's signature mechanic. xSplit symbols create split versions of existing symbols, doubling their coverage. When xSplit affects a high-value symbol in a winning position, it effectively doubles the win contribution of that position. Multiple xSplits in one spin can multiply coverage exponentially.

Key Features

During the base game, xSplit symbols can appear on the reels and immediately double the coverage of adjacent symbols. The split symbols act as two full instances of the original symbol type, contributing to winning combinations across the 10 paylines as if each were a separate independent symbol.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the xSplit mechanic is more frequent, and a multiplier ladder escalates with each winning spin. The escalating multiplier combined with exponential xSplit coverage is what drives the 100,000x theoretical maximum.

The xBet option allows players to increase scatter frequency for an additional cost per spin. The base 10-payline structure gives the game standard coverage for the non-xSplit portion of each spin. Very high volatility means extended base-game sessions are typical before free-spin triggers.

Theme & Design

The colour palette is saturated pinks, purples and yellows against clinical white backgrounds. Nolimit City's distinctive artistic vision is at its most extreme here.
