What Is Thunderstruck II?

Thunderstruck II by Microgaming is a Norse mythology slot released in 2010 and widely regarded as one of the greatest sequels in slot history. It introduced the Great Hall of Spins — a progressive free-spins system with four chambers representing four Norse gods (Valkyrie, Loki, Odin and Thor) — that influenced slot design for a decade.

Thunderstruck II's Great Hall of Spins is the benchmark progressive free-spins chamber system. Each of the four chambers is unlocked as the scatter trigger accumulates across sessions, providing mechanically distinct bonuses from the relatively straightforward Valkyrie chamber through to the exceptional Thor chamber unlocked on the fifteenth scatter trigger.

RTP & Volatility

Thunderstruck II carries a published RTP of 96.65%. Medium-High volatility means the slot sits between medium and high, offering a reasonable hit rate alongside larger payouts.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Great Hall of Spins unlocks progressively: Valkyrie (first trigger, 10 spins, 5x multiplier), Loki (fifth trigger, 15 spins, Wild Magic feature), Odin (tenth trigger, 20 spins, Ravens wild-substitution reel feature) and Thor (fifteenth trigger onwards, 25 spins, Rolling Reels with 5x multiplier). Thor's chamber is the game's high-value mode.

The Wildstorm feature can trigger randomly in the base game, turning up to five reels entirely into Wild symbols — similar in concept to Immortal Romance's Wild Desire. The 243 ways structure means Wild reels covering multiple positions create extensive coverage across adjacent symbol combinations.

243 ways to win means any adjacent combination of matching symbols in adjacent reels creates a win. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The combination of the Wildstorm random feature, four progressive chambers and 243 ways gives Thunderstruck II exceptional replay depth and session variety.

Theme & Design

Norse mythology at its cinematic best for 2010: Thor with his hammer Mjolnir, Loki the trickster, Odin and his ravens, the Valkyrie on horseback — all rendered in Microgaming's detailed illustrative style against stormy Nordic skies. The heavy rock soundtrack references the AC/DC song from which the IP derives. The design retains its quality in 2026.

Verdict

Thunderstruck II is a slot that every serious Irish player should play. The 96.65% RTP is competitive for a 2010 release, the Great Hall of Spins progressive chamber system is the design template that Immortal Romance and later games followed, and the Norse mythology execution remains compelling. The €15 maximum bet is a significant limitation for high-stakes play.
