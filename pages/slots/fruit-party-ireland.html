What Is Fruit Party?

Fruit Party by Pragmatic Play is a 7x7 grid cluster-pays slot that takes the classic fruit slot aesthetic and rebuilds it with modern mechanics. The 2020 release combines a tumble cascade system with randomly placed multipliers that double in value each time they participate in a winning tumble.

The game's most distinctive feature is the multiplier doubling mechanic: multiplier symbols can land randomly on the grid and their values double each time the tumble cascade passes through them. Starting multipliers of 2x can grow to 4x, 8x, 16x and beyond if the tumbles keep chaining, contributing to the 5,000x maximum win.

RTP & Volatility

Fruit Party carries a published RTP of 96.47%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger when they land, rewarding patience.

Key Features

Multiplier symbols land randomly on the 7x7 grid during both base game and free spins. When a winning cluster includes or is adjacent to a multiplier, that multiplier's value doubles. If the winning tumble produces another cluster that also involves the multiplier, it doubles again. This compounding effect is Fruit Party's most exciting mechanic.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land anywhere on the grid. During free spins, multipliers appear more frequently and their doubling effect is more pronounced. Additional scatters during free spins retrigger extra spins.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. The tumble mechanic removes winning clusters and replaces them from above, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin. The 7x7 grid creates 49 symbol positions, increasing cluster formation opportunities versus a standard 5-reel layout.

Theme & Design

A colourful retro-style party featuring classic fruit symbols — cherries, lemons, oranges, watermelons and grapes — alongside stars and lucky 7s, all rendered with modern gloss and animation. Colourful streamers, confetti and balloon effects pop on big wins. The design is cheerful and vibrant.
