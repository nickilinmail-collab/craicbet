What Is Fat Santa?

Fat Santa by Push Gaming is a festive cluster-pays slot that became a Christmas favourite at Irish casinos from its 2019 release. Despite its seasonal theme, Fat Santa's mechanics — a stash-and-collect system combined with sticky multiplier wilds in free spins — make it entertaining year-round for high-volatility fans.

The game's Christmas setting features Santa collecting gifts, but the real draw is the mechanical structure: tumble cascades, a stash mechanic where Fat Santa builds up bonus values, and a free-spins round where multiplier wilds remain sticky throughout. The 96.56% RTP and 20,000x maximum win give it serious numbers alongside the seasonal aesthetic.

Key Features

The Stash feature is Fat Santa's heart. During the base game, Fat Santa carries a sack that fills with collected symbol values as winning combinations pay and symbols are removed. This stash carries forward until free spins, where it is released back onto the reels.

Scatter symbols trigger free spins when four or more land. During free spins, multiplier wild symbols are sticky — they remain in position for the rest of the bonus. Each multiplier wild that lands stays put, and when a win occurs across a sticky wild, its multiplier value is applied to the win total.

The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them with new falling symbols, allowing multiple consecutive wins from a single paid spin. The combination of tumbles, sticky multiplier wilds and the released stash value in free spins drives the 20,000x theoretical maximum.
