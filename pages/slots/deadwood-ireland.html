What Is Deadwood?

Deadwood by Nolimit City is one of the studio's earliest and most celebrated extreme high-volatility titles. Released in 2020, this Wild West slot preceded Tombstone RIP and established Nolimit City's western aesthetic. It uses a free-spins round with sticky multiplier wilds and escalating spin multipliers that drive a 150,000x theoretical maximum win.

Deadwood introduced Nolimit City's approach to sticky wild mechanics to a wide audience. The free-spins round features sheriff and outlaw wild symbols that become sticky on landing and carry individual multiplier values. As more sticky wilds accumulate, their combined coverage and individual multipliers interact with the escalating spin multiplier.

Key Features

During the base game, Wild symbols land on the reels in two types: Sheriff wilds (lower multipliers, more frequent) and Outlaw wilds (higher multipliers, rarer). Both types carry multiplier values applied to any wins they contribute to.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, both Sheriff and Outlaw wild types become sticky. An escalating spin multiplier increments with each free spin. The combination of sticky wild coverage, individual wild multipliers and an escalating spin multiplier is what drives the 150,000x maximum.

The xBet option is available to increase scatter frequency. The base 10-payline structure is standard. Very high volatility means that Deadwood requires significant patience in both base game and free-spins setup before the full sticky wild and spin multiplier combination produces the game's most extreme wins.

Theme & Design

A classic Wild West frontier town at dusk: saloon fronts, wanted posters, sheriff's badges, pistols, dynamite and poker cards across the reels. The colour palette is dusty ochres and deep blues. Nolimit City's production quality, while earlier than Tombstone RIP, is still high and the town aesthetic is well-executed.

Verdict

Deadwood established Nolimit City as a premier extreme high-volatility studio and its mechanics still hold up in 2026. The 96.5% RTP is competitive, the sticky wild dual-type system is engaging, and the 150,000x maximum win is achievable. For Irish players who want to explore Nolimit City's catalogue chronologically, Deadwood is the essential starting point.
