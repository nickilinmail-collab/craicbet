What Is Funky Time?

Funky Time by Evolution is a 2023 live game show built around a 1970s disco aesthetic, serving as a spiritual sequel to Crazy Time. It features four bonus games — Disco, Starstruck, Bar and VIP Disco — each with different mechanics and win potentials, alongside a main money wheel format.

Funky Time is Evolution's most recent flagship game show, designed to bring the production scale and four-bonus-game format of Crazy Time to a new visual aesthetic. The four bonus games each have distinct mechanics that differentiate them meaningfully from one another.

RTP & How the House Edge Works

Funky Time carries a published RTP of 96.08% (overall). RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The main money wheel has number segments (1, 2, 3, 5, 10) and four bonus trigger segments (Disco, Starstruck, Bar, VIP Disco). Number segments multiply player bets. Each bonus trigger activates that game's dedicated studio room.

Disco bonus: players choose a number target on a disco-themed spinning wheel; landing on the target awards a multiplier. Starstruck bonus: a star field of multipliers is revealed and players pick their star for the prize — with top multipliers reaching 20,000x. Bar bonus: a bar setting mini-game awarding multiplier prizes from bottles. VIP Disco: a premium version of Disco with higher baseline multipliers.

The Top Slot mechanic from Crazy Time is also present: before the main wheel spins, the Top Slot randomly selects a colour and multiplier. All segments of that colour on the main wheel are multiplied by the Top Slot value for that round.

Presentation & Design

A spectacular full 1970s disco-themed live studio: mirrored ball ceiling, gold and silver decor, wide-lapel-suit-wearing live host, era-appropriate furniture, funky synthesiser soundtrack and the visual language of Studio 54 at its peak.

Verdict

Funky Time is an excellent Crazy Time-era Evolution game show for players who appreciate the formula but want a fresh aesthetic and different bonus mechanics. The 96.08% overall RTP matches Crazy Time and the Starstruck bonus ceiling of 20,000x is ambitious.
