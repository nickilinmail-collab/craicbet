What Is Stick 'Em?

Stick 'Em by Hacksaw Gaming is a fruit-themed slot with a unique sticky symbol mechanic that gives it one of the highest theoretical maximum wins in the Hacksaw Gaming catalogue at 250,000x. The 2021 release uses a 5x5 grid where fruit symbols can become sticky during free spins, with the stickiest fruits carrying multipliers.

The game's distinctive mechanic is the Sticky Symbols system. During both base game and free spins, any fruit symbol that lands with a multiplier value can become sticky on the grid. As additional matching or multiplied symbols land, the sticky cluster grows and its multiplier value compounds, potentially reaching extraordinary heights.

Key Features

In the base game, Multiplier symbols land on the reels carrying values from 2x upward. When a Multiplier symbol is adjacent to a matching fruit symbol, it attaches and applies its multiplier to any wins involving that position. During free spins, multiplied sticky symbols remain on the grid and can grow through new landings.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, the Sticky Symbol mechanic is enhanced — multiplied sticky symbols are more frequent and their values escalate with each contributing win. The combination of a growing sticky cluster and escalating multipliers drives the 250,000x theoretical maximum.

A Buy Bonus feature is available in permitted jurisdictions. The 5x5 grid structure gives the game a cluster-pays-like feel despite the sticky mechanic's focus on individual symbol positions rather than pure cluster formation.

Theme & Design

A retro-inspired fruit machine aesthetic: cherries, lemons, oranges, watermelons and grapes styled with bold colours and clean lines against a simple background. The simplicity of the visual design belies the mechanical complexity of the sticky multiplier system underneath.
