What Is Jammin' Jars 2?

Jammin' Jars 2 by Push Gaming is the 2021 sequel to the beloved Jammin' Jars, expanding the grid from 8x8 to an 8x8 format with added mechanics including a Cluster Charge meter, Giga Wilds and a Super Free Spins mode. It is one of the highest-ceiling cluster pays slots available, with a theoretical maximum of 20,000x stake.

The sequel significantly expands on the original with the Cluster Charge meter — a progressive mechanic that fills with each winning cluster, triggering increasingly powerful bonus features when it reaches thresholds. The Giga Wild that fills a 3x3 block of positions is the most visually dramatic feature.

RTP & Volatility

Jammin' Jars 2 carries a published RTP of 96.4%. Very High volatility means wins are rare but the payout potential is extreme — for experienced players only.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Cluster Charge meter fills with each winning cluster. At tier thresholds, it triggers: a Giga Wild (3x3 block of Wild symbols), a Multiplier Wild (single Wild with an escalating multiplier), or a Super Jam (all same-colour fruit across the grid become sticky Wilds). These triggered features stack with the base cluster pays system.

Free Spins trigger with four or more Scatters, awarding 10 spins. During free spins, the Cluster Charge meter still fills and triggers bonus features, with an additional Super Free Spins mode that activates after the Cluster Charge fills completely during the bonus.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols in cluster formations. The 8x8 grid has 64 positions — the largest grid of any game on this list — creating extraordinary coverage per spin when the Giga Wild or Super Jam features activate.

Theme & Design

An expanded psychedelic jam-band festival setting with more vibrant colours and character than the original: fruit characters in festival attire, neon music effects, dancing jars and an expanded cast of tropical fruit symbols. The colour palette is bold neons and saturated festival colours. Push Gaming's visual quality is excellent throughout.

Verdict

Jammin' Jars 2 is an outstanding sequel that meaningfully improves on the original without losing its essential character. The 20,000x maximum win is extraordinary for a cluster pays format, the Cluster Charge meter adds progressive engagement, and the Giga Wild feature is visually spectacular. For Irish players who want the highest possible ceiling from cluster pays mechanics, this is the definitive version.
