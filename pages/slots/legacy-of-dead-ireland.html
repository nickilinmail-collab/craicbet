What Is Legacy of Dead?

Legacy of Dead by Play'n GO is a 2020 Egyptian mythology slot that serves as a companion title to Book of Dead, sharing similar mechanics — a single expanding symbol in free spins, 10 paylines, and an ancient Egyptian theme — but differentiating through a Golden Bet feature and slightly higher RTP.

Legacy of Dead follows the Book of Dead template closely: a 10-payline Egyptian slot with one designated expanding symbol per free-spins round and a simple, clean base game. The Golden Bet feature is the key differentiator — by doubling the stake per spin, players unlock the ability to have two expanding symbols active simultaneously during free spins.

RTP & Volatility

Legacy of Dead carries a published RTP of 96.58%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land, awarding 10 free spins. One symbol is randomly selected as the expanding symbol for the entire bonus round. All winning spins featuring the expanding symbol result in that symbol expanding to cover the full three rows of its reel, potentially covering multiple reels simultaneously.

The Golden Bet feature costs double the standard stake per spin. When activated, it increases the probability that two symbols are active as expanding symbols simultaneously during free spins, rather than the standard one. With two expanding symbols active, the coverage available across 10 paylines is dramatically amplified.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols on the standard 10 paylines. The base game is deliberately minimal — the game's depth is in the free-spins expanding symbol mechanic, consistent with Play'n GO's Egyptian slot design philosophy.

Theme & Design

Ancient Egyptian temples and tombs rendered in Play'n GO's high-quality illustrative style: pharaohs, Anubis, scarab beetles, Horus eyes, lotus flowers and canopic jars across the 10-payline grid. The colour palette is rich golds, lapis blue and sandy stone. The design is a faithful, well-executed companion to Book of Dead.

Verdict

Legacy of Dead is a solid companion slot to Book of Dead that justifies its separate existence through the Golden Bet mechanic and its marginally higher RTP. The 96.58% RTP is competitive for the Egyptian high-volatility category, the Golden Bet gives players an optional stake-based way to amplify bonus potential, and the two-expanding-symbol possibility is a genuine differentiator.
