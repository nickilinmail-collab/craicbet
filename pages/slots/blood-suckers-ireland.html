Key Features

The Bonus Game is Blood Suckers' primary feature. When three or more Coffin symbols land on the reels, the player enters a pick-and-click bonus round. Coffin lids are opened to reveal sleeping vampires. Some vampires yield coin prizes; others are already dead and end the bonus. The more vampires staked before hitting an end-bonus, the larger the total prize.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land. During free spins, a 3x multiplier is applied to all wins. The 3x free-spin multiplier applied to 25 paylines creates the conditions for the most significant wins in the game, reaching the 7,000x theoretical maximum.

Wild symbols (a bat) substitute for all non-scatter, non-coffin symbols. The game's medium volatility means wins are reasonably frequent in the base game, with the bonus game and free spins delivering the larger prizes. The 25-payline structure gives a good base-game hit rate.

Theme & Design

Gothic Victorian aesthetic: vampire characters, coffins, garlic, crosses, blood and bats fill the reels against a midnight-blue backdrop. The design is authentic to the horror genre and atmospheric despite its age. NetEnt's 2009 production values were strong for their era and the game still functions cleanly.

Verdict

Blood Suckers is not the most glamorous slot on the market, but no other slot offers 98% RTP to Irish players who care about theoretical value. The medium volatility means steady base-game activity, the bonus game is engaging, and the free spins with 3x multiplier provide the biggest wins. For RTP-aware players in Ireland, Blood Suckers is essential.
