Key Features

The xWays mechanic allows certain symbols to expand and reveal two to five of any matching symbol beneath. When an xWays symbol reveals matches that form a winning combination, the coverage is dramatically amplified. During free spins, xWays are more frequent and reveal higher numbers of matching symbols.

The xSplit mechanic splits existing symbols into two or more symbols of matching type. Combined with xWays, this creates exponential symbol multiplication potential on a single spin. During Tombstone RIP's free-spins round, xWays and xSplit mechanics interact to drive the most extreme wins.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, a multiplier increments with each spin that produces a win. The combination of xWays symbol expansion, xSplit multiplication and an escalating spin multiplier during free spins is what drives the 150,000x theoretical maximum.
