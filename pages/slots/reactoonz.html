Reactoonz Overview

Reactoonz from Play'n GO is one of the most beloved cluster pays slots in the Irish online casino market. Set in a quirky alien laboratory, this 7x7 grid slot combines cascading wins with a layered feature system that builds towards the spectacular Gargantoon feature. Its distinctive one-eyed alien characters and engaging gameplay loop have earned it a dedicated following since its release.

The cluster pays mechanic requires five or more matching symbols to connect horizontally or vertically to form a winning combination. After each win, the winning symbols are removed and new ones cascade down from above, potentially creating chain reactions of consecutive wins from a single spin. This cascading mechanic is the foundation upon which Reactoonz builds its more complex features.

What sets Reactoonz apart from other cluster pays slots is its layered bonus system. Rather than relying on a single bonus trigger, the game features multiple interconnected features that charge up through gameplay, creating a sense of progression and building anticipation as you play.

Fluctuation Feature

Before each spin, one of the one-eyed alien symbols is randomly selected as the Fluctuation symbol, highlighted by a glowing marker. When this symbol is part of a winning cluster, it leaves behind one or two wild symbols after being removed. These wilds remain on the grid for the subsequent cascade, potentially connecting otherwise separate clusters into larger winning groups.

The Fluctuation feature triggers frequently and provides a steady stream of additional wins. While individual Fluctuation wilds may not create massive payouts, they contribute to charging the Quantum Leap meter, which drives the game's bigger features.

Quantum Leap Features

Every winning cascade charges the Quantum Leap meter on the left side of the grid. As the meter fills, it unlocks progressively more powerful features. There are five Quantum features in total, each triggered when the meter reaches a specific level:

Implosion: Transforms three to six random symbols into wilds and destroys all adjacent symbols, creating gaps for new symbols to cascade in.

Alteration: Selects one random one-eyed symbol type and transforms all instances of it into another symbol, potentially creating large clusters of matching symbols.

Demolition: Destroys all one-eyed symbols and symbols of one randomly selected type, clearing a significant portion of the grid for new cascades.

Incision: Places a wild symbol in the centre of the grid and draws a cross shape, turning all symbols along the cross into the same randomly selected type.

If the meter charges through all four features without the cascade sequence ending, the fifth and most powerful feature activates: the Gargantoon.

The Gargantoon Feature

The Gargantoon is a massive 3x3 wild symbol that appears on the grid when the Quantum Leap meter is fully charged. This giant wild connects with surrounding symbols to form enormous winning clusters. After the first cascade, the Gargantoon splits into two 2x2 wilds, and after the next cascade, these further divide into nine individual wild symbols scattered across the grid.

The progressive splitting of the Gargantoon creates multiple cascading wins, each potentially larger than the last as the wilds spread across the grid and connect with different symbol groups. This is where Reactoonz's biggest wins occur, and triggering the Gargantoon is the aspiration of every session.

Theme and Design

Reactoonz features a charmingly quirky alien theme. The one-eyed alien symbols come in various colours and expressions, each with its own personality. The grid sits within what appears to be an alien laboratory or containment unit, with electrical effects and glowing energy surrounding the play area.

The visual design is distinctive and immediately recognisable. The aliens are designed with character and humour, making them memorable compared to the generic symbols found in many slots. Sound design is equally strong, with electronic sound effects and escalating audio cues that build tension during cascade sequences. The overall aesthetic has a retro-sci-fi charm that appeals to a broad audience.
