Starburst Overview

Starburst by NetEnt is, quite simply, the most popular online slot ever made. Released in 2012, it has maintained its position at the top of casino charts worldwide for over a decade, and it remains one of the most played slots among Irish players in 2026. Its enduring appeal lies in its elegant simplicity: a clean space-gem theme, frequent small wins, and the exciting Starburst Wild feature that keeps every spin interesting.

Played on a traditional 5-reel, 3-row grid with 10 paylines, Starburst pays both ways — meaning winning combinations count from left to right and right to left. This effectively doubles the number of winning opportunities on every spin compared to a standard left-to-right slot. With low volatility, Starburst is designed to deliver steady, frequent payouts rather than occasional massive wins.

Starburst Wild and Respins

The signature feature of Starburst is the expanding Starburst Wild symbol. This rainbow-coloured gem can appear on reels 2, 3, and 4. When it lands, it expands to fill the entire reel and triggers a free respin. If another Starburst Wild appears during the respin, it also expands and grants another respin. You can get up to three consecutive respins if a Starburst Wild lands on each of the three middle reels.

When all three middle reels are filled with expanded wilds, the result is typically the game's biggest payout. With the both-ways pay system creating winning combinations from both directions simultaneously, a fully wild centre section generates wins across numerous paylines at once. While the maximum win of 500x is modest by modern standards, these moments are genuinely thrilling and occur more frequently than bonus features in high-volatility slots.

Notably, Starburst does not have a traditional free spins round, scatter symbols, or bonus games. The expanding wild with respins is the only feature, and it is all the game needs. This simplicity is a deliberate design choice that makes Starburst immediately accessible to new players while remaining satisfying for experienced ones.

Theme and Design

The cosmic theme features a deep purple-blue space backdrop filled with twinkling stars and gentle nebula effects. Symbols are polished gemstones in seven colours — yellow, orange, green, blue, purple, red, and the multi-coloured Starburst Wild. The design is timeless in its minimalism, avoiding the cluttered interfaces that many modern slots favour. The electronic soundtrack is upbeat and energetic without being intrusive, building in intensity during winning combinations and wild expansions. Every element of the presentation contributes to a relaxing yet engaging experience.

Verdict

Starburst proves that a slot does not need dozens of features to be great. Its low volatility delivers frequent wins that keep your balance steady, the both-ways pay structure doubles your opportunities, and the expanding wild respins provide just enough excitement to keep you spinning. The 500x maximum win will not change your life, but the journey is consistently enjoyable. There is a reason this slot has been the world's favourite for over a decade — it simply gets everything right. For Irish players looking for a relaxed, reliable slot with proven entertainment value, Starburst is an essential play.
