What Is Temple of Treasure Megaways?

Temple of Treasure Megaways by Nolimit City combines the BTG Megaways engine — licensed to Nolimit City — with the studio's signature high-volatility mechanics and an ancient temple exploration theme. Released in 2020, it blends Megaways variable ways with the xWays and wild multiplier system Nolimit City developed independently.

The combination of two distinct mechanical systems — BTG's Megaways variable row heights and Nolimit City's xWays expansion mechanics — gives Temple of Treasure a unique mechanical depth. xWays symbols expand to reveal two to five of any matching symbol, dramatically amplifying the already-high coverage from the Megaways grid.

RTP & Volatility

Temple of Treasure Megaways carries a published RTP of ~96%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

xWays symbols appear during base game and free spins, expanding to reveal multiple matching symbols within their position. On a Megaways grid that already delivers variable ways to win, the xWays expansion adds another layer of potential coverage per spin — a single xWays reveal can multiply the effective symbol count in a position by up to five.

Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land. During free spins, xWays symbols appear more frequently and an escalating multiplier builds with each winning spin. The combination of xWays expansion on a Megaways grid with an escalating multiplier is what creates the conditions for the 20,000x theoretical maximum.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols in the standard way across the Megaways grid. The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin in both base game and free spins.

Theme & Design

An ancient Egyptian or Aztec temple setting — stone columns, golden relics, hieroglyphs, gemstone artefacts and exploration equipment across the variable-height Megaways reels. The colour palette is warm golds and earthy stone tones. The cinematic production quality is consistent with Nolimit City's standard.

Verdict

Temple of Treasure Megaways is an interesting hybrid title that combines two strong mechanical systems. The approximately 96% RTP is competitive, the xWays-on-Megaways combination creates unique coverage amplification, and the 20,000x maximum is achievable in favourable conditions. For Irish players who enjoy both Megaways and Nolimit City mechanics, it is worth exploring.
