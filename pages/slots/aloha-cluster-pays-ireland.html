What Is Aloha! Cluster Pays?

Aloha! Cluster Pays by NetEnt is a tropical Hawaiian-themed slot released in 2016 that uses a cluster pays mechanic on a 6x4 grid rather than traditional paylines. Wins form when clusters of nine or more matching symbols connect horizontally or vertically, creating a very different win geometry from standard slot formats.

The cluster pays format with a 6x4 grid means the full grid has 24 symbol positions available each spin, creating much more coverage than a typical 5x3 slot with traditional paylines. Combined with low volatility and a 96.42% RTP, the game is well-suited to longer sessions at modest stakes.

RTP & Volatility

Aloha! Cluster Pays carries a published RTP of 96.42%. Low volatility means wins arrive frequently but tend to be smaller in size, suiting longer sessions.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Sticky Win respin mechanic activates after any cluster win: winning symbols freeze in place (become sticky) while all non-winning symbols respin. The respin continues as long as new clusters form. This chain mechanic is what gives the game its win potential beyond the base cluster size.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land, awarding 10 free spins. During free spins, special Coin symbols can appear carrying monetary values. At the end of the bonus, all Coin symbol values are summed and paid out as a lump win.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols in cluster formations. The tropical 6x4 grid includes regular and special symbols across all 24 positions, with clusters of nine or more same symbols required for a base pay.

Theme & Design

A vivid Hawaiian tropical paradise: palm trees, hula dancers, pineapples, tropical fish, flowers, surfboards and tiki masks across the 6x4 grid. The colour palette is bright tropical greens, blues, yellows and reds. The cheerful Hawaiian soundtrack and sunny beach animations are well-executed.

Verdict

Aloha! Cluster Pays is a distinctive NetEnt low-volatility slot that provides a genuinely different win geometry through its cluster pays format and Sticky Win respin mechanic. The 96.42% RTP is excellent, the 6x4 grid gives good coverage, and the tropical theme is cheerfully executed. For Irish players who want lower volatility with more session consistency than high-volatility alternatives, it is recommended.
