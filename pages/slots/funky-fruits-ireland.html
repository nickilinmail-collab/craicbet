What Is Funky Fruits?

Funky Fruits by Playtech is one of the highest-RTP slots in the Playtech catalogue at 97.01%, a fruit-themed 243-ways slot released in 2017 that appeals to value-conscious Irish players who want a low-volatility title with solid theoretical return.

At 97.01% RTP, Funky Fruits sits in the same tier as NetEnt's Blood Suckers and Jack Hammer 2 as one of the few widely available slots to publish an RTP above 97%. Combined with low volatility and 243 ways to win, it is a strong choice for extended casual play sessions.

RTP & Volatility

Funky Fruits carries a published RTP of 97.01%. Low volatility means wins arrive frequently but tend to be smaller in size, suiting longer sessions.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Free spins are the game's primary bonus: three or more Scatter symbols trigger 10 free spins. During free spins, multiplier values up to 5x can apply, with the multiplier level determined by the number of scatters that triggered the bonus. Five scatters trigger a 5x multiplier, four scatters a 3x multiplier, and three scatters a 1x multiplier.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The 243-ways structure means any adjacent combination of matching symbols in adjacent reels creates a win, without specific payline alignment. Combined with the low volatility, this gives the game a healthy hit rate across extended base-game sessions.

The jackpot feature can trigger at random on any spin. A jackpot wheel spins to reveal one of four levels: Mini, Minor, Major or Grand. While jackpot values are smaller than the Age of the Gods series, the random trigger adds an extra dimension to the base game.

Theme & Design

A retro disco-inspired fruit machine aesthetic: cherries, lemons, oranges, watermelons, grapes, coconuts, bananas and pineapples styled with bright, bold colours and a fun, funky visual treatment. The colour palette is saturated tropical. The upbeat funk soundtrack matches the fruit disco aesthetic.

Verdict

Funky Fruits is an excellent value choice for Irish players who prioritise RTP. At 97.01%, the theoretical return is exceptional, the low volatility gives consistent results, and the 243-ways structure provides a good hit rate. The €50 maximum bet limits high-stakes play, but for casual players who understand RTP mathematics, Funky Fruits is one of the most honest slots available.
