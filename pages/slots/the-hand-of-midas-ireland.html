What Is The Hand of Midas?

The Hand of Midas by Pragmatic Play is a 2020 Greek mythology slot themed around King Midas and the golden touch. It features the Golden Touch mechanic — a randomly triggered feature where the golden hand transforms base-game symbols into higher-value golden alternatives — and an Ante Bet option that doubles the free-spins trigger probability.

The 15-payline structure keeps the game's mechanics clean and readable, while the Golden Touch random feature and Ante Bet option give players meaningful options to control their session. The Greek mythology execution is rich with golden visual design that captures the Midas legend's aesthetic.

RTP & Volatility

The Hand of Midas carries a published RTP of 96.76%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

The Golden Touch feature triggers randomly in the base game. When activated, a golden hand reaches across the reels and transforms a selection of symbols into gold versions of higher-value alternatives. The transformation increases the value of winning combinations using the upgraded symbols for that spin.

Free spins trigger when three or more Scatter symbols land, awarding 10 free spins. During free spins, an additional Golden Touch transformation occurs at the start of each spin, with the golden hand upgrading multiple positions before the spin resolves. This enhanced trigger rate significantly amplifies the bonus potential.

The Ante Bet option costs 25% of the standard bet (1.25x total). When active, the probability of triggering free spins from scatter symbols is doubled, trading slightly reduced base-game value for substantially more frequent free-spins entry. For high-volatility players who want more bonus entries, it is a useful option.

Theme & Design

Ancient Greek opulence: King Midas himself with his golden touch, Greek temple columns, amphoras, olive wreaths, laurel crowns and the mythical golden artefacts that the king's touch creates. The colour palette is rich golds, deep blues and marble whites. The design is detailed and consistent with the Midas legend's visual language.

For Irish players who enjoy Pragmatic Play's high-volatility output with an Ante Bet option, it delivers.
