What Is Aztec Bonanza?

Aztec Bonanza by Pragmatic Play is a 2020 Aztec-themed slot that uses a tumble (cascade) mechanic on a pyramid-shaped reel grid. The pyramid structure has three rows on the outer reels and more rows in the centre, creating an unusual win geometry. The tumble mechanic removes winning symbols and replaces them from above, allowing multiple consecutive wins from one paid spin.

The Aztec Bonanza pyramid grid is the game's visual and mechanical centrepiece: the outer two reels have fewer active rows, while the central reels have more rows, creating a diamond/pyramid shape. This unusual grid structure affects the coverage available per spin — different positions carry different win potential based on their position in the pyramid.
