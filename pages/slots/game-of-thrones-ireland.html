What Is Game of Thrones Slot?

Game of Thrones by Microgaming is a licensed slot released in 2014 based on the HBO television series. It offers two play modes — 15 paylines or 243 ways — and features four House free-spins bonuses (Stark, Lannister, Baratheon and Targaryen) each with distinct mechanics that reflect the characteristics of the respective House in the TV series.

The Game of Thrones licence is well-integrated: the four Houses determine which free-spins mechanic activates, and each mechanic reflects the House's character — Targaryen's Fire and Blood mechanic features fire-spreading wilds, for example. The brand recognition is universal and the mechanical execution is genuine.

RTP & Volatility

Key Features

The Four Houses free-spins system: House Stark (8 spins, 3x multiplier), House Lannister (10 spins, 5x multiplier on select wins), House Baratheon (8 spins, tripled random wilds) and House Targaryen (18 spins, fire-spreading wilds). Three Scatter symbols trigger the free-spins round and the player selects which House to enter.

The 243 ways mode covers more combinations per spin than the 15-payline mode at the same stake, providing better coverage for the same bet. The 15-payline mode supports lower minimum effective bets. Both modes share the same free-spins system and House mechanics.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols. The Iron Throne is the game's highest-value symbol. The branded symbols include key character and House iconography from the series, all rendered in Microgaming's detailed illustrative style.

Theme & Design

The Red Keep, the Iron Throne, the Night's Watch ravens, House sigils — the Direwolf, the Lion, the Stag and the Three-Headed Dragon — and the faces of key Game of Thrones characters across the reel grid. The detailed design captures the series' visual language. The iconic Game of Thrones theme music is reproduced in the soundtrack.
