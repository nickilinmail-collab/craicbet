What Is Fire Portals?

Fire Portals by Play'n GO is a high-octane fantasy-themed slot released in 2021 built around a portal system that dynamically alters the reel grid. The game features an expanding grid mechanic where portal symbols open new reel sections, potentially transforming a standard 5x3 grid into a massive multi-reel configuration with an enormous number of ways to win.

The portal mechanic is Fire Portals' defining feature: when portal symbols appear, they open additional reel spaces on the grid, expanding the playing field and dramatically increasing the coverage per spin. At maximum expansion, the grid grows substantially beyond the base configuration, creating win combinations that are impossible in the standard layout.

RTP & Volatility

Fire Portals carries a published RTP of 96.20%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Portal symbols appear on specific reels and when they land, they activate additional grid expansion for that spin. The expanded grid creates additional symbol positions across the activated reels, multiplying the effective coverage. The more portals that activate simultaneously, the greater the grid expansion.

Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols across the expanded grid. Free spins trigger when enough Scatter symbols land, awarding a bonus round with enhanced portal activation probability. During free spins, portals trigger more frequently, increasing the probability of maximum grid expansion.

Multiplier values can attach to portal activations during the bonus, applying to wins made on the expanded grid sections. The combination of maximum grid expansion and stacked multipliers drives the 10,000x theoretical maximum achievable during the free-spins round.

Theme & Design

A dark elemental fantasy setting — volcanic landscapes, arcane fire portals carved from obsidian stone, animated fire runes and mythical creatures silhouetted against a glowing red-orange sky. The colour palette is deep blacks, dark reds and orange flames. Play'n GO's production quality is high throughout.

Verdict

Fire Portals is a distinctive Play'n GO slot that justifies its place in the studio's portfolio through the portal grid expansion mechanic. The 96.20% RTP is competitive for high-volatility, the grid expansion creates dramatic visual moments, and the 10,000x maximum is achievable in favourable conditions. For Irish players who want Play'n GO's quality in a more mechanically inventive framework than Book of Dead, it delivers.
