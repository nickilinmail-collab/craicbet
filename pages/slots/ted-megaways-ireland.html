What Is Ted Megaways?

Ted Megaways by Blueprint Gaming is a branded Megaways slot based on the Universal Pictures Ted movies, featuring the talking teddy bear character. Released in 2020, it combines Blueprint Gaming's Megaways implementation with five distinct bonus features that players can choose between, drawing on the film's humorous tone.

The branded content is well-integrated — Ted the talking bear is the slot's highest-value symbol and his irreverent personality is reflected in the bonus feature design. The player-choice bonus feature system is the game's key differentiator from standard Megaways titles.

RTP & Volatility

Ted Megaways carries a published RTP of 96.3%. High volatility means wins are less frequent but can be significantly larger, rewarding patience.

RTP is a theoretical long-run figure — it does not predict outcomes in any single session. Set a budget before playing.

Key Features

Five distinct bonus features are available: Couch Potato Free Spins (standard Megaways free spins with unlimited multiplier), Bong Rip Spins (enhanced wilds), Psychedelic Spins (randomised symbol upgrades), Thunder Buddies Free Spins (enhanced scatter triggers) and Movie Night Spins (a mix of the above mechanics).

During Couch Potato Free Spins — the base free-spins mode — the standard BTG unlimited progressive multiplier applies. Each winning Reaction cascade increments the multiplier. The other bonus modes provide different enhancement types that trade some multiplier depth for different forms of guaranteed symbol coverage.

Free spins trigger when four or more Scatter symbols land. The player selects which of the five bonus modes to activate. Each mode has a different balance of base wins versus enhancement mechanics. Wild symbols substitute for all non-scatter symbols in the standard Megaways format.

Theme & Design

Ted the foul-mouthed stuffed bear in his apartment setting: sofas, beer cans, bongs, movie tickets, playing cards and Ted himself across the Megaways reels. The design is faithful to the film's low-brow humour aesthetic. Blueprint's production quality is consistent and the character animations capture Ted's personality.

Verdict

Ted Megaways is a strong branded Blueprint Megaways slot. The 96.3% RTP is competitive, the five-mode bonus selection gives genuine player agency, and the Ted brand is beloved by its fan base. The low maximum bet is consistent with Blueprint's portfolio. For Irish fans of the Ted films who want a slot that actually reflects the source material, it delivers.
