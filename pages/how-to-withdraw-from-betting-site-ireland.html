How to Withdraw From an Irish Betting Site: Complete Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Withdrawing from an Irish betting site is straightforward once your account is verified. This step-by-step guide covers the process for all payment methods available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers, including the same-method rule and what to do if something goes wrong.

Step-by-Step: How to Request a Withdrawal

  1. Log in and go to your account balance or cashier. Look for “Withdraw” or “Cash Out” in the main menu or profile section.
  2. Select your withdrawal method. Choose the same method you deposited with for the fastest, complication-free processing.
  3. Enter the amount. Must be above the site’s minimum withdrawal threshold (typically €10–€20).
  4. Confirm your payment details. Card number, Revolut username, bank account number — ensure these match your verified identity.
  5. Submit the request. You will receive an email or in-app confirmation.
  6. Wait for processing. E-wallets: same day. Revolut/debit card: 1–3 days. Bank transfer: 1–3 days.

The Same-Method Rule

Under GRAI anti-money-laundering requirements, you must typically withdraw to the same method you deposited with, up to the amount deposited. For example, if you deposited €100 by Visa debit card and want to withdraw €250, the first €100 goes back to your Visa card and the remaining €150 can go to a bank account. This applies at most GRAI-licensed operators.

Fees on Withdrawals

Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer at least one free withdrawal method per month. Additional withdrawals may incur small fees (€1–€3). E-wallet providers sometimes charge their own fees for transferring funds from the e-wallet to your bank. Check the cashier section for your operator’s specific fee schedule.

If Your Withdrawal Does Not Arrive

Wait the full processing time for your method before contacting support. Card withdrawals sometimes take 5 business days with certain banks. If the funds have not arrived after the stated timeframe, contact the operator with your withdrawal reference number. If unresolved, the GRAI complaints process is available as a last resort.

Step-by-Step: Withdrawing From a Betting Site

  1. Log in to your account and ensure your balance shows available funds (not bonus funds).
  2. Go to My Account → Withdraw (or Cashier → Withdraw).
  3. Select your preferred payment method — the same method you deposited with is usually required first.
  4. Enter the withdrawal amount — check the minimum and maximum for your chosen method.
  5. Confirm the withdrawal — some sites send a confirmation email or SMS for large amounts.
  6. Wait for the pending period (24–48 hours for most sites) then the payment provider's processing time.

What to Do If Your Withdrawal Is Delayed

  • Check your email for any verification requests from the site — they often send document requests that you may have missed.
  • Log in and check the Withdrawal History section — a 'pending' status means it's in the internal review queue.
  • Contact live chat and quote your withdrawal reference number — this speeds up manual review.
  • If a withdrawal has been pending for more than 5 business days without explanation, file a formal complaint through the site's complaints procedure, then escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie.
How do I withdraw money from an Irish betting site?

Log in, go to Cashier/Withdraw, select your payment method (same as you deposited with), enter the amount, confirm details and submit. Processing takes 1–3 business days for cards, same day for e-wallets.

What is the same-method rule for withdrawals?

GRAI anti-money-laundering rules require you to withdraw back to the same method you deposited with, up to the deposited amount. Any profit above that can go to a bank account.

Can I withdraw to a different card than I deposited with?

Usually only after the deposited amount has been returned to the original card. Contact support to arrange alternative withdrawal routes for excess funds.

Are there fees for withdrawing from Irish betting sites?

Most operators offer at least one free withdrawal per month. Some charge €1–€3 for additional withdrawals or specific methods. Check the cashier page of your operator.

How long does a bank transfer withdrawal take from an Irish betting site?

1–3 business days from when the operator dispatches the payment. Weekend requests usually queue until Monday. SEPA transfers are typically credited within 1 business day of dispatch.
