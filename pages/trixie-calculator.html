Trixie Calculator (3 Selections, 4 Bets)

What is a Trixie bet?

A Trixie is a full-cover multiple bet from 3 selections: 3 doubles and 1 treble, totalling 4 bets. There are no singles, which makes it the stripped-down version of a Patent. At a €1 unit stake, a Trixie costs just €4 — the cheapest full-cover multiple that includes a treble. The key limitation: you need at least 2 winners for any return. One winner from three returns nothing. The Trixie suits punters who are confident in most of their selections and want to maximise returns without the cost of singles.

Trixie vs Patent: what's the difference?

Both use 3 selections, but a Patent adds 3 singles to make 7 bets total. This means a Patent costs 75% more (€7 vs €4 at €1 unit stakes) but gives you insurance: even one winner returns something. A Trixie is better value when you're confident at least 2 selections will win — you're not paying for 3 singles you don't need. If there's any doubt about one of your selections, the Patent's singles provide coverage. For short-priced selections where singles won't return much, the Trixie's focus on combinations often makes more sense.

When to use a Trixie

A Trixie is ideal when you have 3 selections you rate highly and want to maximise returns without the extra cost of singles. It works especially well at longer odds — if each selection is priced at 3.0 or more, even two winners together can return several times the outlay. It's also the go-to for punters who've narrowed a card down to 3 confident picks for horse racing or football. If you have any concern about one selection, consider upgrading to a Patent — the singles it adds provide a safety net for just €3 more at €1 stakes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bets in a Trixie?

4 bets — 3 doubles and 1 treble. There are no singles in a Trixie.

How many winners do you need on a Trixie?

At least 2 winners are needed to win any return from a Trixie, as the smallest bet is a double. With just 1 winner you lose your entire stake.

What is the difference between a Trixie and a Patent?

A Patent uses the same 3 selections but adds 3 singles, making 7 bets total. It costs more (7 stakes vs 4) but returns something with just 1 winner. A Trixie is cheaper but needs 2 winners.

How much does a €1 Trixie cost?

€4 total — 4 separate bets at €1 each (3 doubles and 1 treble).

What is the difference between a Trixie and a Lucky 15?

A Trixie uses 3 selections (4 bets), while a Lucky 15 uses 4 selections (15 bets including singles). They are different sizes of full-cover multiple bet.
