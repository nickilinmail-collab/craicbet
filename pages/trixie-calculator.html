What is a Trixie bet?

A Trixie is a combination bet covering 3 selections across all possible doubles and one treble — 4 bets in total. Unlike a Patent, it contains no singles, so you need at least 2 of your 3 selections to win before you see any return. The advantage is a lower total outlay compared to a Patent (4 units vs 7 units) with higher potential returns when two or more selections come in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Trixie bet?

A combination bet on 3 selections covering all doubles and the treble — 4 bets total. No singles, so you need at least 2 winners for any return.

How many bets in a Trixie?

4 bets: 3 doubles (AB, AC, BC) and 1 treble (ABC).

What is the difference between a Trixie and a Patent?

A Patent adds 3 singles to bring the total to 7 bets. A single winner returns something on a Patent; on a Trixie you need at least 2 winners.

Does a Trixie include singles?

No. To include singles, you need a Patent (7 bets total).

How much does a Trixie cost?

A €1 Trixie costs €4 total (4 bets × €1 unit stake). A €5 Trixie costs €20.
