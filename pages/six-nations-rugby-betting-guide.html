Six Nations Rugby Betting 2027: Guide for Irish Punters

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Six Nations outright markets

Six Nations outright markets (championship winner and Grand Slam) open before Christmas the previous year and attract significant betting from Irish rugby fans. Ireland are perennial title contenders and generate the most betting activity of any Six Nations nation in Ireland. Championship outrights typically see five nations priced between 3/1 and 10/1, with one clear favourite depending on recent autumn international form and squad health going into the tournament.

Grand Slam betting

The Grand Slam (winning all five matches) is a separate market to the championship winner. A team can win the championship without winning every match if other results go their way. Grand Slam odds are longer than championship winner odds because all five matches must be won. Ireland, France and England have all won Grand Slams in the past decade, and their prices for the next edition are always closely followed.

Match betting in the Six Nations

Individual Six Nations match betting follows standard rugby union format: match result, handicap (points start), total points and first try scorer. Away match betting in the Six Nations is tighter than domestic competition because all six teams are closely matched in quality. Ireland at home in Dublin are a different proposition from Ireland away in Paris or London, which the handicap line reflects.

In-play Six Nations betting

In-play betting during Six Nations matches is extensive on televised fixtures. Live points-spread and next-score markets move rapidly with try scoring and yellow-card-related momentum shifts. The final 20 minutes of a close Six Nations match produce some of the most volatile in-play prices of any rugby event. Frequently Asked Questions

When is the 2027 Six Nations?

The 2027 Six Nations Championship runs from late January to mid-March 2027. The exact schedule is confirmed by World Rugby and the participating unions in autumn 2026.

How does Grand Slam betting differ from championship winner?

The Grand Slam market requires a team to win all five matches in the championship. The championship winner market only requires the most points accumulated across all results. A team can win the championship with one loss if other results give them the best overall record. Grand Slam odds are longer because all five matches must be won.

Can I bet on individual Six Nations matches?

Yes. All Six Nations matches are well-covered with match result, handicap, total points, first try scorer and in-play markets from GRAI-licensed bookmakers.
