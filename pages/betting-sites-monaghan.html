The GRAI Framework for Monaghan Punters

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 brought Irish online betting regulation up to modern standards. All previous Revenue Commissioners permissions expired on 30 June 2026, replaced by the GRAI’s licensing regime. For Monaghan residents, this means every legal online bookmaker must now:

  • Hold a valid GRAI Remote Betting (RB) licence, verifiable at grai.ie
  • Verify your age before accepting bets
  • Ban credit card deposits (debit cards, Revolut and bank transfer are allowed)
  • Provide mandatory responsible gambling tools including deposit limits and self-exclusion
  • Answer to a formal GRAI complaints process if disputes arise

How to Verify Any Bookmaker in 2 Minutes

Before depositing with any betting site, check its licence status on the GRAI public register at grai.ie:

  1. Find the legal company name in the footer of the betting site (not the brand name)
  2. Search for that company name on the GRAI register
  3. Confirm RB (Remote Betting) appears in the licence number
  4. Check the status is Active and the expiry date is in the future

Alternatively, our GRAI licence tracker is updated daily and flags any changes, new licences issued or suspensions.

Local Sport in Monaghan: What to Bet On

Monaghan GAA is one of the most competitive Ulster football counties, regularly contesting Ulster and All-Ireland championships. The county has a strong GAA culture centred around Clones and Monaghan town.

All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry markets on GAA matches at both club and county level, League of Ireland fixtures, National Hunt and flat racing from Irish tracks, and all major international sports. Markets for intercounty GAA matches typically open one to two weeks before the fixture, with in-play betting available on selected games.

Online vs Physical Betting Shops in Monaghan

Physical betting shops in Ireland operate under a separate GRAI retail licence framework. Applications for retail licences opened in late 2026. In the meantime, the shops that have been operating continue under transitional arrangements. For most Monaghan punters, online betting via a GRAI-licensed site offers better odds, a wider range of markets, full app support and responsible gambling tools that may not be available trackside or in a shop.

Payment options at GRAI-licensed online sites include Revolut, Visa debit, Mastercard debit and bank transfer. Credit cards are banned for all gambling deposits under GRAI rules.

Responsible Gambling in Monaghan

All GRAI-licensed operators are required to offer deposit limits, cool-off periods and self-exclusion. The national self-exclusion register, once fully operational, will allow you to exclude yourself from all licensed sites with a single registration. For support with problem gambling, the Gambling Awareness Trust helpline is available free on 1800 753 753 and at gamblingcare.ie.
