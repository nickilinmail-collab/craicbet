GRAI — How Ireland’s New Regulator Will Change the Landscape

🇮🇪 The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI)

Ireland’s gambling landscape is undergoing its biggest transformation in decades. The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI), established under the Gambling Regulation Bill, is set to become the country’s first dedicated gambling regulator.

For Irish players, this means:

  • Mandatory licensing — All operators targeting Irish players will need a GRAI licence to operate legally
  • Stronger player protections — Standardised responsible gambling measures, deposit limits, and self-exclusion tools
  • Advertising restrictions — Tighter rules on how casinos can market to Irish players
  • Dispute resolution — A clear process for Irish players to resolve complaints against operators
  • Fair bonus practices — Potential regulation of wagering requirements and bonus terms

New casinos launching now will need to factor GRAI compliance into their operations from the start. This is actually a positive sign — casinos built with Irish regulation in mind are likely to offer better player protections than those built purely for unregulated markets.
