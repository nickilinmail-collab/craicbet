How CraicBet Reviews Betting Sites

Published 5 November 2026 · Methodology version 2.0

In brief

Every site we rate must hold an active GRAI remote betting licence (verified on grai.ie). We then score 6 weighted categories on a 0–100 rubric. Sites scoring below 70 are not recommended. Affiliate arrangements play no role in scores.

Why Methodology Matters

Irish punters are now protected by the strongest gambling consumer framework in Europe — the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, enforced by the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) from 1 July 2026. But regulation doesn't prevent bad operators from offering poor terms or slow withdrawals. CraicBet's role is to cut through the marketing and report what actually matters to players.

Step 1: Licence Verification (Mandatory Gate)

1 GRAI Register Check

No score is assigned and no recommendation is made until we verify the operator holds an Active Remote Betting (RB) licence on the official GRAI public register at grai.ie. We check the legal company name, licence type, licence number, issue date, and expiry date.

An operator that appears to be licensed but whose legal entity name does not match the GRAI register is flagged and not recommended until resolved.

Our GRAI tracker is updated daily. If a licence status changes between quarterly reviews, affected pages are updated within 24 hours.

Step 3: Specific Checks

3a Bonus Maths

We calculate the actual expected value of a welcome offer, not just quote the headline figure. For a "€50 free bet with 5x wagering at minimum 1.5 odds", we show the realistic cost to unlock the bonus, the percentage of bets that must win for you to break even, and the expected monetary value.

We never describe a bonus as "free money" or imply it's risk-free when it isn't.

3b Withdrawal Timeline Verification

We document operator-stated withdrawal timeframes from published terms and cross-reference with verified player reports where available. We distinguish between processing time (operator's side) and banking time (payment provider).

Under GRAI rules, operators must process withdrawals promptly. Any documented pattern of delays is reflected in the Withdrawals score.

3c Odds Comparison

We sample overround (margin) on at least 3 markets per operator: a standard 90-minute match result for a Premier League game, a GAA county championship match, and a Grade 1 horse race. Overround is calculated as (sum of implied probabilities − 1) × 100. Lower overround = better value for punters.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't accept payment to change editorial scores or rankings.
  • We don't use operator-supplied marketing copy as fact without verification.
  • We don't claim any casino holds a GRAI licence — GRAI issues remote betting licences only. Casino licences are expected from 2027–2028; zero have been issued as of November 2026.
  • We don't invent RTP figures, withdrawal speeds, or market depth data.
  • We don't publish reviews of operators we cannot verify on the GRAI register.

How We Calculate Overround

Overround (margin) is calculated from a standard 3-way football market: sum of (1/decimal_odds) for all outcomes × 100 − 100. A 5% overround means the implied probabilities add to 105%. We sample at least three markets per site — a Premier League match, a GAA county game, and a Grade 1 horse race — and use the average as our odds score input.
