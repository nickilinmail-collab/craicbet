How We Review & Rate Online Casinos

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 October 2026 ⏱ 8 min read

Anyone can copy a casino's feature list from their website. We don't do that. Every casino reviewed on CraicBet goes through our rigorous 12-step testing process — using real accounts, real money, and real gameplay. Here's exactly how we do it.

💡 Why This Matters

Most casino review sites never actually sign up or play. They rewrite marketing copy and call it a review. We believe you deserve better. Our process costs us real money — but it means every word we publish is based on first-hand experience.

Our 12-Step Review Process

Step 1: Sign Up with Real Accounts

We register a genuine account at every casino we review, going through the full sign-up process just like any Irish player would. We note how long registration takes, what documents are required, and whether the KYC (Know Your Customer) process is smooth or frustrating.

Step 2: Deposit Real Money

We deposit between €50 and €200 of our own money using payment methods popular with Irish players — Visa, Mastercard, Revolut, Apple Pay, and others. We test which methods are accepted, how quickly funds appear, and whether there are any hidden fees.

Step 3: Test the Bonus Claiming Process

If the casino offers a welcome bonus, we claim it. We read every line of the terms and conditions, check the wagering requirements, note any game restrictions, and see whether the bonus is genuinely worth taking or just a marketing trap.

Step 4: Play 50+ Games Minimum

We play at least 50 different games across categories — slots, table games, live dealer games, and any specialty titles. We're checking for game variety, software quality, loading speed, and whether the casino actually carries games from the providers they advertise.

Step 5: Test Customer Support

We contact customer support through every available channel — live chat, email, and phone where offered. We ask real questions and time the responses. We note whether agents are knowledgeable, helpful, and available during Irish hours.

Step 6: Make a Real Withdrawal

This is where many casinos fall down. We request a genuine withdrawal and document the entire process: what verification was needed, how straightforward the request was, and whether any obstacles were placed in our way.

Step 7: Time the Withdrawal

We record exactly how long it takes from withdrawal request to money landing in our account. We test multiple withdrawal methods where possible and report the actual times — not the casino's claimed processing times.

Step 8: Check the Mobile Experience

Most Irish players gamble on their phones. We test each casino on both iOS and Android, checking the mobile site or app for speed, usability, game selection, and whether all features (deposits, withdrawals, support) work properly on mobile.

Step 9: Verify Licence and Security

We confirm the casino's licensing directly with the regulatory authority — not just by looking at a logo on their website. We check for SSL encryption, data protection policies, and any history of regulatory action or player complaints.

Step 10: Evaluate Responsible Gambling Tools

We test every responsible gambling feature: deposit limits, loss limits, session time limits, cooling-off periods, and self-exclusion options. A casino that doesn't take responsible gambling seriously won't score well with us, regardless of how good their games are.

Step 11: Score Across 12 Criteria

Each casino receives a score out of 10 across our twelve scoring criteria (detailed below). The final rating is a weighted average that reflects what matters most to Irish players.

Step 12: Re-Test Quarterly

A review is only as good as its last update. We re-visit every reviewed casino at least once per quarter to check for changes — new bonuses, updated terms, different withdrawal times, or changes in game selection. Our reviews reflect the casino as it is today, not as it was six months ago.

What We Look For

Beyond the numbers, here are the qualities that separate great casinos from average ones in our experience:

  • Fast, hassle-free withdrawals — Nothing matters more than being able to get your money out quickly and without jumping through hoops
  • Fair bonus terms — Low wagering requirements (under 35x), no maximum win caps on deposit bonuses, and clear terms written in plain English
  • Strong licensing — We favour casinos licensed by reputable authorities like the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA), UK Gambling Commission (UKGC), or Gibraltar Licensing Authority
  • Irish payment support — Accepting methods that Irish players actually use, including Revolut, Visa Debit, and Apple Pay
  • Genuine customer care — Support that's available during Irish hours, staffed by people who can actually solve problems
  • Responsible gambling commitment — Meaningful tools, not just tick-box compliance

Our Commitment to Independence

CraicBet earns revenue through affiliate commissions — we're transparent about that. But here's our promise: affiliate relationships never influence our ratings. We've given low scores to casinos we have partnerships with, and high scores to casinos we don't. The review always comes first.

If you ever feel a review doesn't match your experience, let us know. We take reader feedback seriously and will re-investigate any casino where our findings are challenged.
