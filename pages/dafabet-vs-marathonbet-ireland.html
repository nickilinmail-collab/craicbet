Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dafabet or Marathonbet better for football?

Both offer competitive football margins (3.5–5%). Dafabet leads on Asian handicap; Marathonbet leads on European league breadth. Neither offers GRAI Irish protections.

Do these bookmakers have high limits?

Both Dafabet and Marathonbet are known for higher limits than mainstream operators. This is attractive for professional bettors but comes without Irish legal protections.

Is Dafabet good for Asian football?

Yes. Dafabet has strong pricing on Asian football leagues and excellent Asian handicap lines. It is well-regarded in the professional betting community for this reason.

What is the GRAI?

The GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) is the Irish gambling regulator, issuing remote betting licences since July 2026. GRAI-licensed operators must pay out winnings as a licence condition.

Are professional punters restricted by GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

Some mainstream GRAI-licensed operators restrict winning accounts. High-limit operators like Dafabet and Marathonbet are more tolerant of professional bettors, though they lack GRAI Irish protections.
