Is Crypto Betting Legal in Ireland in 2026? GRAI's Rules on Bitcoin Gambling

Updated 5 November 2026 · CraicBet Editorial

✅ Key point: Crypto betting is legal in Ireland if the operator holds a GRAI licence. The currency used doesn't determine legality — the operator's licence does.

GRAI's Position on Cryptocurrency Gambling

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 does not specifically prohibit cryptocurrency as a payment method. GRAI-licensed operators may accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins and other cryptocurrencies, provided they comply with:

  • Anti-money laundering (AML) requirements — Know Your Customer (KYC) verification, source of funds checks for large deposits
  • Consumer protection rules — deposit limits, self-exclusion, responsible gambling tools must function regardless of payment method
  • Credit card ban compliance — the ban applies to credit cards, not crypto

In practice, most of the 39 GRAI-licensed operators (as of November 2026) do not advertise cryptocurrency as a primary deposit method, but some do accept it on request or through third-party crypto processors.

The Stake.com Case: Crypto ≠ Exempt from Licencing

Stake.com — the world's largest crypto gambling platform by volume — was geo-blocked from accepting Irish customers in September 2026. This case illustrates the key principle clearly:

🚫 Using crypto does not exempt an operator from GRAI licensing requirements. Stake was blocked not because it used crypto, but because it had no GRAI remote betting licence. The payment method is irrelevant to the licencing obligation.

Why Some Players Use Unlicensed Crypto Sites

Irish players who use unlicensed crypto gambling sites often cite: anonymity, faster withdrawals with no bank involvement, and access to casino games not yet available in Ireland (casino licences are expected 2027–2028). These are real advantages — but they come with the significant risk of having no recourse if something goes wrong.

⚠️ Crypto transactions are irreversible. If you deposit crypto at an unlicensed site and the site freezes your account or refuses withdrawal, there is no chargeback mechanism and no GRAI to complain to. The funds are effectively gone.

Tax Implications of Crypto Betting Winnings

Gambling winnings in Ireland are generally not subject to income tax for recreational players. However, if you hold cryptocurrency that appreciates in value between deposit and withdrawal, Capital Gains Tax (CGT) may apply on the crypto appreciation — not on the gambling winnings themselves. Consult a tax professional if you're making significant crypto gambling transactions.

Tax on Crypto Betting Winnings in Ireland

  • Gambling winnings are not taxed for recreational Irish punters, whether in euros or crypto.
  • Capital Gains Tax (CGT) at 33% may apply to any gain on the cryptocurrency asset itself — if BTC appreciates between your deposit and withdrawal, that gain is a CGT event.
  • Record the euro value of crypto at the point of deposit and withdrawal to calculate any CGT liability.
  • Consult a tax advisor if you hold significant crypto gambling activity.
