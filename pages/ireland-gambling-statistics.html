Who the Spending Comes From

The most important finding in the ESRI research is concentration. The 3.3% with problem gambling account for 28.3% of all gambling expenditure (~€1.6bn), spending on average more than €1,000 per month. Widen the lens to everyone with problem gambling or moderate evidence of it — 10.4% of adults — and that group funds 47% of total spend (~€2.6bn). Nearly half the industry's money comes from one adult in ten. Even "moderate" gamblers (7%+ of adults) average around €70 a week — €3,640 a year.

The Ten-Times Correction

Before the ESRI's landmark study (commissioned during the establishment of the GRAI), Irish problem gambling was officially estimated at roughly a tenth of the current figure. The 3.3% finding — one adult in thirty — is about ten times higher than previously thought, and is the single statistic that most shaped the Gambling Regulation Act's player protections, from the credit card ban to the National Exclusion Register and the Social Impact Fund.

Online vs In-Person, and What Moves Spending

Reported spending splits roughly evenly between online and in-person channels, with 35% of adults gambling online in a given month. A joint GRAI–ESRI experiment also found that inducements — free bets and moneyback offers — increased spending by over 10%, evidence that sits directly behind the Act's inducement restrictions. Self-reported figures understate true losses — concealment is real and the ESRI tested for it — so the €5.5bn estimate is best read as a floor.

What This Page Is For

Ireland has no routine annual national account of gambling participation and spend, so the numbers above — ESRI survey research plus the GRAI's first annual report — are the best public picture as of 2026. Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Irish people spend on gambling?

ESRI research estimates ~€5.5bn of adult spend a year; industry gross revenue was estimated at €1.17–€2.57bn in 2025 (GRAI annual report).

How many problem gamblers are there in Ireland?

ESRI estimates 3.3% of adults — about 130,000 people — with a further ~279,000 showing moderate evidence. Roughly ten times earlier official estimates.

What share of gambling revenue comes from problem gamblers?

28.3% of all spend from the 3.3% with problem gambling; 47% from the 10.4% with PG or moderate evidence of it (ESRI).
