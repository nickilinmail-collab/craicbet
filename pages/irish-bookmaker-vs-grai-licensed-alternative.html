Odds Margins: Where the Real Difference Is

Odds margins are the percentage built into every market price that guarantees the bookmaker a profit long-term. On a standard Premier League match, the leading Irish operator typically runs a combined margin of 5–6%, meaning for every €100 staked the bookmaker retains €5–6 on average. The GRAI-licensed alternative regularly offers tighter markets at 4–5% on the same fixtures — a meaningful difference if you bet consistently throughout a season.

On horse racing the gap narrows: both operators compete aggressively on domestic Irish and UK racing, where Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG) promotions are standard. Neither is notably better on GAA, where thin liquidity keeps margins wide across the industry at 7–10%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BoyleSports GRAI-licensed?

Yes. BoyleSports holds a current GRAI remote betting licence. You can verify any operator at the official GRAI register at grai.ie.

What is a GRAI betting licence?

A GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) remote betting licence is the legal requirement for any bookmaker to accept bets from Irish residents online. It replaced the old Revenue Commissioners system from 1 July 2026.

Can I use both bookmakers at the same time?

Yes. There is no restriction on holding accounts with multiple GRAI-licensed operators. Many punters use two or three to compare odds and claim welcome offers on each.

Are free bets legal under GRAI rules?

Yes. Free bets and welcome bonuses are still legal. The GRAI restricts how they are advertised (watershed rules, no targeting of self-excluded players), but the offers themselves remain available.

What payment methods are accepted by GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

All GRAI-licensed operators must accept debit card deposits. Most also accept Revolut, PayPal and bank transfer. Credit card deposits are banned under GRAI rules.
