Who Are UCD AFC?

University College Dublin Association Football Club — known as UCD AFC or simply UCD — are the football club of University College Dublin, Ireland's largest university. The club plays home matches at the UCD Bowl, a purpose-built sports complex on the UCD campus in Belfield, south Dublin.

UCD AFC occupy a unique position in Irish football. Their university connection means they have access to a constant stream of young talent, and the club has a strong reputation for player development. Several top LOI and international players have come through the UCD system before moving on to bigger clubs in Ireland and abroad.

For bettors, UCD are an interesting club. They are typically not among the title favourites in the LOI, but their distinctive home venue at the UCD Bowl, their young squad, and their development-oriented style of play can make them unpredictable in individual fixtures.

UCD AFC in the 2026 LOI Season

The 2026 LOI campaign has seen UCD AFC competing in their current division with a squad that blends university-linked players with signed LOI professionals. The club's unique structure means squad composition can change more rapidly than at other clubs, as student players graduate and move on.

At the UCD Bowl, the club benefits from their familiar home surface and the support of the university community. The venue is somewhat off the beaten track compared to other Dublin LOI grounds, which can reduce the away crowd but also makes it a distinctive and sometimes intimidating home environment.

UCD's youth-focused ethos means they are often the story of a cup upset — energetic young sides with nothing to lose against larger, more established Premier Division clubs. Their FAI Cup performances are always worth watching.
