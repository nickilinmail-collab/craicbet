Why Casino Licences Come Last

The GRAI chose to license the biggest existing market first. Betting already operated under Revenue excise licences, so migrating bookmakers was the fastest way to bring regulated operators under the new rules. Online gaming has never been licensed in Ireland — the GRAI is building that framework from scratch, which is why it takes longer.

What It Means for Players Right Now

Every online casino accepting Irish players today operates under a foreign licence — typically MGA (Malta), Curacao or Anjouan. That is not illegal for you as a player, but protections vary enormously by licence. Our licence comparison and 2-minute licence check show how to tell them apart.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a casino hold a GRAI licence in 2026?

No. The GRAI has issued remote betting licences only since 1 July 2026. Online casino licences are expected between late 2026 and 2028. Any casino claiming a GRAI casino licence today is misleading you.

Is it legal to play at an online casino in Ireland before GRAI licenses them?

Playing is not a criminal offence for the player — the licensing obligation falls on operators. Protections depend on the foreign licence, so check it before depositing.

Where can I see which operators are licensed?
