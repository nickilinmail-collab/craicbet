GRAI vs UKGC: The Jurisdictional Difference

The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) was established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and covers online gambling in the Republic of Ireland only. It is now licensing remote betting operators serving the Republic, with casino licences expected from 2027–2028.

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) was established under the Gambling Act 2005 and covers all online gambling activity in Great Britain and Northern Ireland. Any operator that accepts wagers from UK residents — including those in Belfast — is required to hold a UKGC licence.

These are two entirely separate regulatory systems. A GRAI licence means nothing in Northern Ireland; a UKGC licence means nothing in the Republic. If you live in Belfast, the UKGC is your regulator.

What to Look for as a Belfast Casino Player

When choosing an online casino as a Belfast player, the most important trust signal is a UKGC licence. Here is how to verify one:

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the casino's website and find the licence information — UKGC-licensed sites must display their licence number
  2. Visit gamblingcommission.gov.uk and use the licence register to confirm the number is active
  3. Check the licence type covers remote casino gaming (not just remote betting)

Beyond UKGC licensing, look for casinos that also hold an MGA (Malta Gaming Authority) or Gibraltar licence — these jurisdictions provide additional player protections and have strong track records on withdrawals.

GamStop: Available to Belfast Players

GamStop is the UK's national online self-exclusion scheme. As a Belfast resident, you can register at gamstop.co.uk to block yourself from all UKGC-licensed online gambling sites simultaneously. Registration is free and takes effect within 24 hours. Minimum exclusion period is 6 months; you can choose longer, up to permanent exclusion.

Note: GamStop covers only UKGC-licensed operators. Sites operating under other licences (MGA, Curaçao, etc.) without a UKGC licence are outside GamStop's reach. For comprehensive protection, choose only UKGC-licensed casinos and use GamStop alongside direct per-operator self-exclusion.
