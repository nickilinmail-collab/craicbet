Can You Have Two Betting Accounts in Ireland?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

The rule is simple: one account per bookmaker, but you can have accounts at as many different GRAI-licensed bookmakers as you like. Having multiple accounts at the same operator to claim bonuses twice is against every operator’s T&Cs and will get both accounts closed. Here is the full picture.

One Account Per Bookmaker

Every GRAI-licensed bookmaker explicitly prohibits multiple accounts per customer. This is enforced through KYC checks — because operators verify your identity against a government-issued document, duplicate accounts are caught when the second account’s KYC uses the same name, date of birth, address, payment method, or device.

Getting caught with duplicate accounts results in both being closed, all bonuses voided, and in some cases winnings from the bonus activity being confiscated under the operator’s terms. It can also result in a permanent ban from that operator.

Having Accounts at Multiple Different Bookmakers: Perfectly Fine

Having accounts at 5, 10, or 20 different GRAI-licensed bookmakers is entirely legal and common practice among regular punters. This is called “line shopping” — comparing odds across operators to find the best price. It is not considered multi-accounting because each account is at a different company.

Welcome bonuses can be claimed once at each operator (once per customer per bookmaker). Claiming each bookmaker’s welcome offer as a new customer is legitimate — this is distinct from using multiple accounts at the same operator.

Matched Betting and Multiple Accounts

Matched bettors routinely hold accounts at many different bookmakers simultaneously. This is legal and not banned by GRAI rules. What eventually happens is that profitable matched bettors get their accounts restricted (“gubbed”) by individual bookmakers, but holding multiple accounts across different operators remains legitimate.

Signing Up on Behalf of Someone Else

You cannot open a betting account using someone else’s identity, even with their permission. KYC verification requires the account holder to submit their own documents. Opening an account in another person’s name is fraudulent misrepresentation and potentially a criminal matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you have two accounts at the same betting site in Ireland?

No — one account per customer per operator. Duplicate accounts are caught via KYC identity checks. Both accounts will be closed and bonuses voided if discovered.

Can you have accounts at multiple different Irish bookmakers?

Yes — there is no limit to how many different GRAI-licensed bookmakers you can hold accounts with. Having accounts at 10+ different operators is common and entirely legitimate.

Can you claim a welcome bonus at every Irish bookmaker?

Yes — each operator's welcome offer can be claimed once as a new customer. This is different from multi-accounting (which means multiple accounts at the same operator).

What happens if I accidentally created a duplicate account?

Contact support immediately and explain the situation. If you act in good faith quickly, some operators will simply close one account and merge the histories. If you have claimed bonuses on both, those bonuses will likely be voided.

Can matched bettors have accounts at many bookmakers?

Yes — holding accounts at many different operators is legal and common for matched bettors. What eventually happens is individual accounts get restricted by profitable bookmakers, but multi-site use is legitimate.
