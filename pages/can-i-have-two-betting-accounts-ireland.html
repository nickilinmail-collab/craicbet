Can I Have Two Betting Accounts at the Same Site in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Having two accounts at the same bookmaker is prohibited under virtually every bookmaker’s terms and conditions. Known as “duplicate accounts,” they are detected through shared device IDs, payment methods, addresses, or identity documents. The consequences can be severe.

Consequences of Duplicate Betting Accounts in Ireland

If a bookmaker detects that you hold two accounts, they may: void all bets placed on the duplicate account; confiscate any winnings earned through the duplicate account; close both accounts permanently; flag you as a problem customer across the industry. Under GRAI rules, licensed operators are required to verify identity, and duplicate accounts are a KYC violation.

What to Do If You Accidentally Have Two Accounts

Contact the bookmaker’s customer support proactively. Explain the situation and request that the duplicate account be closed and balances merged or refunded. Acting before the bookmaker detects the duplicate is significantly better than waiting — self-reporting is usually treated more leniently. Withdraw any balance from both accounts before closing, and obtain written confirmation of the closure.

Can I have two betting accounts at the same bookmaker in Ireland

No. Under the terms and conditions of all GRAI-licensed bookmakers, each person may hold only one account per operator. This is also required by GRAI regulations to enable proper responsible gambling controls and accurate self-exclusion enforcement. Creating a second account at the same operator (using a different email, name or address) is a breach of terms and may constitute fraud.

What happens if a bookmaker finds duplicate accounts

If an operator identifies duplicate accounts, they will typically: void all bets placed from the second account, close the duplicate account, retain any winnings from voided bets, and may close both accounts in serious cases. The original account may be restricted. Under GRAI rules, operators are required to perform identity checks precisely to prevent this. All pending withdrawals from a closed duplicate account may be delayed pending investigation.

Having accounts at multiple different operators is completely legal and very common in Ireland. Many experienced punters maintain accounts at several GRAI-licensed bookmakers to access the best prices on each event. This is not restricted by GRAI.

Can I have accounts at different bookmakers in Ireland?

Yes — having accounts at multiple different bookmakers is completely allowed and common practice. The prohibition is only on having more than one account at the same bookmaker.
