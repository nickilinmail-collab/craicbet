Deposit Limits for Betting in Ireland: What GRAI Requires

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Deposit limits are a mandatory responsible gambling tool for all GRAI-licensed bookmakers in Ireland. This guide explains what deposit limits are, what GRAI requires operators to provide, how to set a limit, and what happens when you reach it.

What Are Deposit Limits?

A deposit limit is a maximum amount you can deposit to a betting account over a defined period — daily, weekly, or monthly. Once you reach the limit, the bookmaker's system automatically declines any further deposit until the period resets.

Deposit limits serve as a responsible gambling safeguard. They help bettors control their spending and prevent impulsive or excessive deposits made during a losing streak. Under GRAI regulations, all licensed operators must offer deposit limit tools and must make them easy to find and use.

Deposit limits are in addition to any limits your bank may apply to debit card spending. The two systems operate independently.

GRAI Mandatory Deposit Limit Rules

The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) issues remote betting licences and sets the conditions operators must meet. GRAI's responsible gambling framework includes requirements around deposit limits. Key rules under the framework include:

  • All GRAI-licensed bookmakers must offer deposit limit functionality to customers.
  • Deposit limits must be easy to set and must take effect promptly.
  • Increasing a deposit limit must include a cooling-off period — typically 24 hours or more — before the higher limit takes effect. This prevents impulsive decisions to raise limits during a session.
  • Decreasing a deposit limit must take effect immediately — there is no delay when tightening your own controls.
  • Operators must not use marketing that encourages customers to increase or remove their deposit limits.

How to Set a Deposit Limit

Setting a deposit limit is straightforward at any GRAI-compliant bookmaker:

  1. Log in to your betting account.
  2. Go to your account settings or the responsible gambling / safer gambling section.
  3. Select "Deposit limits" or "Spending limits".
  4. Choose daily, weekly or monthly limit and enter the amount.
  5. Confirm — the limit takes effect immediately (or at the next period reset if you are reducing an existing limit that was already in effect).

Many bookmakers also prompt new customers to set a deposit limit during the registration process. If you skip this step during signup, you can return to your account settings at any time.

If you are concerned about your gambling, consider also setting a time limit or loss limit, or contacting the Gambling Awareness Trust on 1800 753 753 or visiting gamblingcare.ie.

What Happens If You Hit Your Limit

When you reach your deposit limit:

  • Further deposit attempts are automatically declined by the bookmaker's system.
  • You can still place bets with your existing account balance — the limit applies to new deposits, not your current funds.
  • You can still withdraw winnings from your balance at any time.
  • The limit resets at the start of the next period (e.g., midnight for a daily limit, Monday for a weekly limit — the exact reset time varies by operator).
  • If you need to increase your limit, the new higher limit will only apply after a mandatory cooling-off period (typically 24 hours). You cannot immediately override a deposit limit.

If you want to restrict your access further, most GRAI-licensed operators also offer self-exclusion, which prevents you from accessing your account for a defined period. For longer-term self-exclusion, the national self-exclusion scheme (when available through GRAI) will cover all licensed operators simultaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are deposit limits mandatory at Irish betting sites?

Yes. All GRAI-licensed bookmakers are required to offer deposit limit tools to customers. This is a condition of holding a GRAI remote betting licence. The tools must be easy to access and must take effect promptly once set.

Can I increase my deposit limit immediately?

No. GRAI rules require a cooling-off period before a higher deposit limit takes effect — typically at least 24 hours. Decreasing your limit takes effect immediately. This asymmetry is intentional: it protects you from making impulsive decisions to raise your limits in the heat of the moment.

What should I do if I am gambling more than I want to?

Start by setting or reducing your deposit limit in your account settings — this takes effect immediately. For more support, contact the Gambling Awareness Trust on 1800 753 753 or visit gamblingcare.ie. GRAI-licensed bookmakers must also offer self-exclusion options if you need a complete break.
