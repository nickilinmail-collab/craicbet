World Cup 2026 Final — MetLife Stadium Final Betting Guide for Ireland

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The FIFA World Cup 2026 final is scheduled for MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey on 19 July 2026. It is expected to be the most watched sporting event of the year globally and one of the biggest betting occasions in Irish sport. This guide covers all the main markets for the final.

The MetLife Final — What to Expect

MetLife Stadium, home of the New York Giants and New York Jets NFL teams, is a modern covered stadium with a capacity of approximately 82,500. Its NFL configuration will be adapted for the World Cup final. The match will kick off in the afternoon local time — which translates to late evening in Ireland, making it one of the most accessible World Cup finals for Irish viewers and punters in terms of timing.

Main Markets for the World Cup Final

  • Match winner (90 minutes): Standard 1X2 market settled after regulation time. If level, extra time and penalties follow but the 90-minute market is settled at 90 minutes.
  • To lift the trophy: Winner including extra time and penalties — the same as 'outright World Cup winner' once the finalists are known.
  • First goalscorer / anytime goalscorer: Highly popular for World Cup finals; prices will reflect recent form and penalty duties in the knockout stages.
  • Total goals: World Cup finals have historically been tight affairs; over 2.5 goals is not the default expectation.
  • Correct score: Higher-odds market; the most common World Cup final scores historically have been 1-0 and 2-1.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the World Cup 2026?

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is hosted across 16 stadiums in the United States, Canada and Mexico. The tournament runs from June to July 2026, with the final expected at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on 19 July 2026.

How many teams are in the World Cup 2026?

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the first to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. The group stage features 12 groups of four teams, with the top two from each group and eight best third-placed teams advancing to the round of 32.

What is the best ante-post market for the World Cup?

The outright winner market offers the best long-term value for the World Cup. Early prices on the eventual champion can be significantly longer than closer to the tournament.
