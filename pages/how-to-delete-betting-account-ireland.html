How to Delete a Betting Account in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

You have the right to close and delete your betting account at any GRAI-licensed operator. Under both GRAI rules and GDPR, you can request account closure and deletion of your personal data. Here is the step-by-step process and what to expect.

Step 1: Withdraw Your Balance First

Before requesting account closure, withdraw your full balance. Once the account is closed, the operator should still return any outstanding funds, but it is simpler to withdraw first. Use the standard withdrawal process and wait for the funds to clear before initiating closure.

Step 2: Use Any Outstanding Bonuses or Request Their Removal

Decide whether to use any outstanding free bets or bonus funds before closing. Once the account is closed, unused bonuses are forfeited. If you are closing specifically because you want to stop gambling (self-exclusion), do not use the bonuses — request immediate closure instead.

Step 3: Request Account Closure

Most GRAI-licensed operators offer account closure via:

  • Account settings > Responsible Gambling > Close Account
  • Live chat or email support
  • A formal closure form in the responsible gambling section

If you are closing for responsible gambling reasons, say so — the operator must process this under GRAI rules and must not try to retain you with bonus offers.

Data Protection: Requesting Deletion of Personal Data

Under GDPR (which applies in Ireland), you have the “right to erasure” (also called the right to be forgotten). You can request that the operator deletes your personal data after account closure. However, operators are permitted to retain transaction and identity records for AML compliance (typically 7 years) — they can decline to delete these specific records even after a deletion request, but must delete non-essential data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to Close a Betting Account: Step-by-Step

  1. Withdraw all funds first. Initiate a withdrawal of your full balance via the cashier. Ensure it has settled to your bank/card before proceeding.
  2. Use any remaining free bets or bonuses. Active bonuses are usually forfeited on account closure — if they have value, use them first (or accept they will be lost).
  3. Contact support to request closure. Most operators require you to contact live chat or email — there is usually no self-service delete button. State clearly: "I wish to permanently close my account."
  4. Confirm whether it is a temporary or permanent closure. Some operators ask if it is for responsible gambling reasons or simply no longer using the service. Answer honestly — this affects whether a cooling-off period applies.
  5. Receive confirmation. You should receive an email confirming closure. Keep this for your records.

How do I delete my betting account in Ireland?

Withdraw your balance first, then go to Account Settings > Responsible Gambling > Close Account, or contact live chat. GRAI-licensed operators must process closure requests promptly.

Do I get my money back when I close a betting account?

Yes — your balance must be returned when you close an account. This is a GRAI licence condition. Withdraw before closing for the simplest process.

Can I request deletion of my personal data from a betting site?

Yes — under GDPR you can request data deletion. Operators may retain AML-required records (up to 7 years) but must delete non-essential personal data.

Can a bookmaker try to persuade me not to close my account?

Under GRAI responsible gambling rules, operators cannot use inducements or high-pressure tactics to retain customers seeking to close for responsible gambling reasons. Report any such behaviour to the GRAI.

How long does account deletion take?

Account closure typically takes 1–5 business days. GDPR data deletion requests must be responded to within 30 days under Irish data protection law.
