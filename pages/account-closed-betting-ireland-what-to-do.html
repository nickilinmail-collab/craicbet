Betting Account Closed in Ireland: Your Rights and Next Steps

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

If a GRAI-licensed bookmaker closes your account, they must return your balance. This is a licence condition. The reasons for closure, your rights, and what comes next vary depending on whether it was a commercial decision, a compliance issue, or a self-exclusion. Here is the full breakdown.

Why Bookmakers Close Accounts

Commercial closure: The operator has decided you are not a profitable customer (consistently wins, matched betting patterns). Your balance is returned and you can open accounts elsewhere. Compliance/KYC failure: Identity verification issues, suspected fraud, or AML concerns. Balance may be temporarily held pending investigation. Bonus/T&C violation: Multiple accounts, bonus abuse. Bonus winnings may be voided; real-money balances must be returned. Regulatory: GRAI-mandated closure of accounts with certain patterns (e.g., markers of harm).

Getting Your Balance Back

Under GRAI licence conditions, operators must return your balance upon account closure. This is a legal requirement. If you have an outstanding balance after closure, the operator must pay it out via the same method you last deposited with, or via bank transfer if that method is no longer available. If the operator refuses to return your balance without a valid legal reason, escalate to the GRAI.

The GRAI Complaints Process for Account Closures

  1. Get the closure reason in writing from the operator
  2. Request your balance withdrawal
  3. If the balance is withheld without valid reason, submit a formal complaint to the operator
  4. If unresolved in 8 weeks, file a complaint with the GRAI at grai.ie

Cooling-Off vs Permanent Ban vs Self-Exclusion

A cooling-off period is a temporary restriction you can request at any time (typically 24 hours to 6 weeks). A permanent account closure is usually at operator request. Self-exclusion is a request you make to stop gambling — under GRAI rules this feeds into the National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGER). Self-exclusion cannot be reversed within the exclusion period — this is intentional.

How to Retrieve Your Balance if Your Account Is Closed

  1. Contact support immediately — ask for a written explanation of why the account was closed.
  2. If a balance remains, request it be returned to your original deposit method (the site is legally required to return funds).
  3. If the site refuses to return a balance without adequate justification, file a formal complaint to the site's complaints team.
  4. Escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie if the complaint is unresolved after 8 weeks.
  5. For large amounts, consider consulting a solicitor — bookmakers cannot retain your funds without a legally valid reason.

  • If your account was closed for self-exclusion purposes, consider whether you need a break from gambling rather than immediately opening elsewhere. The Gambling Awareness Trust (1800 753 753) can help.
Can a GRAI-licensed bookmaker close my account?

Yes — operators can close accounts for commercial or compliance reasons. They must return your balance under GRAI licence conditions.

What happens to my money if my betting account is closed?

Your balance must be returned — this is a GRAI licence condition. The operator should contact you about withdrawal within a reasonable timeframe. If they don't, escalate to the GRAI.

Can I open a new account after being closed by a bookmaker?

At the same operator: usually no (you are banned). At other GRAI-licensed operators: yes, unless you are on the National Gambling Exclusion Register.

What is the GRAI complaints process for account closures?

Submit a formal complaint to the operator first. If unresolved after 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie. The GRAI can investigate and mandate balance returns where operators have breached licence conditions.

How long does a closed account balance take to be returned?

Operators should process this within the standard withdrawal timeframe (1–5 business days depending on method). If no response within 10 business days, contact support and reference your account closure date.
