Google Pay Betting Ireland 2026 — Android Betting Apps Accepting Google Pay

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Google Pay gives Android users the same instant, one-tap deposit experience that Apple Pay offers on iOS. It is accepted at an increasing number of GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers and has one meaningful edge over Apple Pay: it works in Chrome on any device, not just on Apple hardware. The same 2026 credit card gambling ban rules apply — the transaction is accepted or refused based on the underlying card type linked to your Google Wallet, not on Google Pay itself.

✅ Key fact: Google Pay works in Chrome on Android, Windows, and Mac. The credit card gambling ban applies to the underlying card in your Google Wallet — debit cards are permitted, credit cards are blocked.

How Google Pay Works at Betting Sites

Google Pay tokenises your card in the same way as Apple Pay — the bookmaker receives a device-specific payment token, not your real card number. The deposit flow on Android:

  1. Select Google Pay in the deposit screen (available in the bookmaker's Android app or in Chrome on the mobile/desktop website).
  2. The Google Pay payment sheet appears with the amount and merchant details.
  3. Authenticate with your fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN.
  4. Funds appear in your betting account instantly.

Google Pay also works in Chrome on desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux) if you have Google Pay set up and a card stored in your Google account. This cross-platform availability is its main advantage over Apple Pay.

Google Pay and Ireland's Credit Card Gambling Ban

The same rules that apply to Apple Pay apply to Google Pay. Since 1 July 2026, GRAI-licensed gambling operators cannot accept credit card payments. Google Pay passes the underlying card type to the payment processor — if that card is a credit card, the transaction will be declined under the ban.

Permitted: Google Pay linked to a Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit, Revolut debit card, or any debit instrument stored in Google Wallet.

Blocked: Google Pay linked to a Visa Credit or Mastercard Credit card.

To check which card Google Pay is using, open the Google Wallet app, select your default payment card, and confirm it is labelled as a debit card. If you have multiple cards in Google Wallet, select your debit card as the default before depositing.

Deposit and Withdrawal Speed

Google Pay deposits are instant — the same speed as a standard debit card, since they use the same card networks (Visa and Mastercard). Withdrawals are processed back to the underlying card and follow that card's withdrawal timeframe: typically 1–5 business days for debit cards at GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

Neither Google Pay nor Apple Pay offers faster withdrawals than the underlying card — the wallet is a deposit mechanism only. For faster withdrawals, Revolut's 1–24 hour turnaround (using their own processing) is worth considering.

Setting Up Google Pay for Betting

If you have not used Google Pay before, the setup takes under two minutes:

  1. Download the Google Wallet app (Android) or visit wallet.google.com.
  2. Tap Add to Wallet and select Payment card.
  3. Add your debit card details (Visa or Mastercard debit).
  4. Verify the card — usually a small test charge or SMS code from your bank.
  5. Set the debit card as your default payment method in Wallet.

Once set up, Google Pay appears automatically as a payment option on supported bookmaker sites in Chrome and in Android apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Google Pay work for betting in Ireland?

Yes. Google Pay is accepted at a number of GRAI-licensed bookmakers in Ireland, via Android apps and the Chrome browser. The underlying card in your Google Wallet must be a debit card to comply with Ireland's 2026 credit card gambling ban. Deposits are instant; withdrawals follow the underlying card's timeline (typically 1–5 business days).

Is Google Pay accepted at Irish bookmakers?

Google Pay is available at a growing number of GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Check the deposit section of the bookmaker's website or app. If not listed, Revolut (processed as a standard Visa or Mastercard debit card) is accepted at nearly all GRAI-licensed sites and works on any device or browser.

Android or iPhone — which is better for mobile betting payments?

Both are equally fast and secure for betting deposits. The key difference: Google Pay works in Chrome on any operating system, while Apple Pay is restricted to Safari. If you use Chrome on a Windows PC, Google Pay has the broader reach. If you are entirely within the Apple ecosystem, Apple Pay is marginally more convenient. For the most platform-neutral option, Revolut works identically on all devices.
