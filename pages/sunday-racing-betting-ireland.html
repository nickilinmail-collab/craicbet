Sunday Racing Betting in Ireland: What to Know

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Sunday racing in Ireland

Sunday racing in Ireland spans Flat, National Hunt and mixed cards across venues including Leopardstown, Navan, Fairyhouse and Cork throughout the season. Sunday fixtures often feature competitive handicaps and listed races, drawing larger fields than weekday cards. The Curragh and Leopardstown host many of the most high-profile Sunday meetings, particularly in summer.

Key differences from midweek racing

Sunday cards attract larger fields because trainers target the bigger prize money and more competitive conditions on offer. Larger fields mean more non-runners and more scope for surprise results. Each-way betting is popular on Sundays precisely because field sizes often exceed eight runners, triggering place terms. Know your place terms before betting: eight runners might give three places at 1/4, while fifteen-plus runners can give four places.

How to approach a Sunday card

Rather than betting every race, identify the two or three races where you have a genuine opinion and focus your stake there. Check the going report — Irish weather means conditions can change significantly between Thursday declarations and Sunday morning. The track report and overnight declarations on Saturday evening are the most useful planning tool for Sunday punters.

In-play and best odds guaranteed

Many GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer best odds guaranteed (BOG) on Irish racing, which means if the starting price is longer than your early price, you receive the SP. BOG applies to win bets on most Irish tracks and is worth using if you bet before the off. In-play betting is available on most televised Sunday fixtures, with live prices from the first flight or fence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which racecourses host racing on Sundays in Ireland?

Leopardstown, Navan, Fairyhouse, Cork, the Curragh, Tipperary and Galway all host Sunday fixtures depending on the season. Leopardstown is the most prominent Sunday venue, particularly for winter National Hunt racing.

What is best odds guaranteed and does it apply on Sundays?

Best odds guaranteed (BOG) means if the starting price (SP) on race day is longer than the price you took early, you receive the SP. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer BOG on Irish racing including Sunday fixtures. Check your operator's terms as conditions can vary.

Are Sunday racing fields larger than midweek?

Generally yes. Sunday fixtures attract more entries because prize money tends to be higher and trainers target weekend cards. Larger fields increase each-way opportunities but also increase the risk of a wide-open race where form is harder to assess.
