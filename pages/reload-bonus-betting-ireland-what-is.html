What Is a Reload Bonus on an Irish Betting Site?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A reload bonus is a recurring promotional offer for existing customers — as opposed to a one-time welcome offer. At GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers, reload bonuses typically take the form of weekly free bets, odds boosts, profit insurance, or money-back specials. Here is what to expect and how to get value from them.

Common Types of Reload Bonus in Ireland

  • Weekly free bet: Place a certain amount in bets during the week and receive a free bet (e.g., place €25 in acca bets, get a €5 free bet). Typically credited on Monday or Tuesday.
  • Odds boost tokens: Credit given each week to boost odds on a selected market by a fixed percentage or amount.
  • Profit boost: An extra percentage added to winnings on a qualifying bet (e.g., 20% profit boost on selected markets, up to €25).
  • Insurance / money-back specials: Stake refunded as a free bet if a specific result occurs (e.g., money back if a named player scores first).

Typical Weekly Reload Value

Across GRAI-licensed bookmakers in 2026, a regular bettor who qualifies for all available weekly reload promotions can typically extract €5–€30 per month in SNR free bet value per operator — before converting at approximately 50–60% to actual cash, so €2.50–€18 per operator per month in real terms. Across 10 operators with active accounts, this is €25–€180 per month.

Qualifying Conditions

Reload bonuses almost always require you to place qualifying bets at minimum odds (typically 1/1 to 4/5) and within a specific window (usually Monday–Sunday). Missing the qualifying period means no reload that week. Some operators require a minimum deposit to trigger the reload; others require a minimum number of bets.

GRAI Inducements Rules on Reload Offers

Reload offers, like welcome bonuses, must comply with GRAI inducements regulations: clearly disclosed terms, no targeting of self-excluded players, no pre-watershed TV advertising. Operators who send targeted reload offers to customers who have set deposit limits must comply with responsible gambling restrictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a reload bonus on a betting site?

A recurring promotional offer for existing customers — typically a weekly free bet, odds boost, or profit insurance in return for a qualifying bet amount during the week.

How much can you make from reload bonuses in Ireland?

€5–€30 per operator per month in free bet value, converting to roughly €2.50–€18 in real cash per operator. Across multiple active accounts, this adds up meaningfully.

Do reload bonuses count as bonus abuse?

No — using legitimately offered reload promotions exactly as intended is not abuse. Abuse means creating multiple accounts or exploiting technical loopholes, not simply claiming every available promotion.

How do I qualify for a reload bonus?

Usually by placing a minimum amount in qualifying bets (at minimum specified odds) during the qualifying period. Check your promotions page or the weekly email for specific requirements each week.

Are reload bonuses regulated under GRAI rules?

Yes — GRAI inducements regulations require reload bonuses to have clearly disclosed terms and prohibit targeting self-excluded or deposit-limited players with promotional offers.
