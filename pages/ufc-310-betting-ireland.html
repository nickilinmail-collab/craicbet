UFC Betting Ireland 2027 — How to Bet on MMA Events Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

UFC events are one of the fastest-growing betting markets in Ireland. With multiple major events throughout 2027, this guide covers how UFC betting works, the main market types and what Irish punters need to know.

How UFC Betting Works

UFC (Ultimate Fighting Championship) fights can be bet on across multiple markets. The main match result market is a simple win/loss — which fighter wins the bout by any method. Beyond the win market, specific method of victory and round betting add layers of complexity and potential value for punters who follow MMA closely.

Main UFC Betting Markets

  • Fight winner: Back one fighter to win — typically the most popular market. Odds reflect recent form, stylistic matchups and official world rankings.
  • Method of victory: KO/TKO, submission or decision. Each method has distinct odds reflecting each fighter's finishing tendencies.
  • Round betting: Which round the fight ends — available as over/under rounds or specific round winner combinations.
  • To go the distance: Whether the fight reaches a decision or ends early. Useful when both fighters have defensive styles.
  • Main event winner: The headline fight on each card is the most heavily traded market.

UFC Events to Watch in 2027

UFC holds approximately 40 events per year including numbered PPV events, Fight Nights and international shows. Looking ahead to 2027, numbered PPV events (UFC 300+) are the most prominent betting occasions. Title fights generate the most market liquidity. Specific cards and opponent matchups for 2027 events will be announced by the UFC typically 4–8 weeks before each event.
