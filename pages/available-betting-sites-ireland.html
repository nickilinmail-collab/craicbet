Betting Sites Available to Irish Players in 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 3 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Ireland's online betting landscape changed significantly on 1 July 2026 when the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) began issuing licences under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Irish players now have a new layer of legal protection — but a large number of established international operators were already available and remain so. This guide explains who accepts Irish players, what licences matter, and what to look for before you sign up.

Who Can Irish Players Use?

Irish residents can legally bet online with:

  • GRAI-licensed bookmakers — operators who have obtained a remote betting licence from Ireland's own regulator. These must follow strict Irish rules on advertising, self-exclusion, and player protection. Our recommended operator holds licence GRAI-0191-RB-26-0001. See the full list of GRAI licensed bookmakers.
  • UKGC-licensed operators — UK Gambling Commission licensees such as bet365, William Hill, Betfair, and Betdaq have long served Irish customers. The UKGC is a well-regarded regulator and these operators offer strong consumer protections, though Irish-specific GRAI rules do not apply to them directly.
  • MGA-licensed operators — Malta Gaming Authority licensees are another common option. Many large internationally recognised brands hold MGA licences. Standards are broadly comparable to the UKGC for betting.

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, operators who actively market to Irish customers are expected to hold a GRAI licence. For the highest level of Irish-law protection, a GRAI-licensed bookmaker is the recommended choice.

GRAI Licences: What They Mean

GRAI began issuing remote betting licences from 1 July 2026. It is important to understand what this means:

  • Only remote betting licences exist under GRAI so far. No casino gaming licences have been issued — those are expected from 2027–2028 at the earliest.
  • A GRAI betting licence covers sports betting, horse racing, virtual sports, and similar wagering products.
  • GRAI-licensed operators must comply with advertising watershed rules (no gambling ads before 9 pm), mandatory self-exclusion, and enhanced affordability checks.
  • The licence register is publicly available on the GRAI website, allowing players to verify any operator's status. Read our full GRAI licence guide for more.

Payment Methods for Irish Bettors

Since January 2022, credit card deposits for gambling are banned in Ireland. The following methods remain widely accepted:

  • Visa / Mastercard debit cards — accepted at virtually all licensed operators. Deposits are usually instant; withdrawals take 1–3 business days.
  • Bank transfer (SEPA) — slower (1–3 business days) but widely supported. Good for larger withdrawals.
  • PayPal — fast and familiar for many Irish users. Not available at every operator. Check the cashier before signing up.
  • Skrill and Neteller — popular e-wallets. Note that many operators exclude e-wallet deposits from welcome bonuses.
  • Revolut — Revolut's Visa/Mastercard debit card works at most operators if gambling transactions are enabled in the app (Security → Payment controls → Gambling). See our Revolut guide.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay — available at a growing number of operators; linked to your underlying debit card.

Which Betting Sites Are NOT Available to Irish Players

A small number of operators do not serve Irish customers. This is typically because:

  • They hold a licence only in a jurisdiction that has restricted Ireland as a market (for example, certain US-state-licensed operators).
  • They are in the process of applying for a GRAI licence and have paused Irish customer onboarding in the interim.
  • They are locally-licensed operations in another country and have not sought international licences.

Always check the 'Restricted territories' section of an operator's terms and conditions before registering. If Ireland is listed, the operator will not accept your account and you should not attempt to use a VPN to circumvent this — doing so typically voids any winnings and can result in account closure.

  • Customer support — live chat and email support during Irish hours is important, particularly for withdrawal queries.
  • Responsible gambling tools — deposit limits, reality checks, self-exclusion, and time-out features are required of GRAI licensees and most reputable operators.

FAQ

Which betting sites are available in Ireland?

Irish residents can legally use betting sites licensed by GRAI, UKGC, or MGA that accept Irish customers. Large operators such as bet365, Paddy Power, William Hill, and Betfair all accept Irish players under their respective licences. For full Irish-law protection, use a GRAI-licensed operator.

What is a GRAI licence and why does it matter?

GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) is Ireland's new independent gambling regulator under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Since 1 July 2026 it has issued remote betting licences. A GRAI-licensed bookmaker must follow Irish player protection rules including watershed advertising restrictions and self-exclusion requirements. Only remote BETTING licences have been issued so far — no casino licences exist under GRAI yet.

Can I use a betting site not licensed in Ireland?

It is not illegal for an Irish resident to place bets at a site licensed in another reputable jurisdiction such as the UK or Malta. However, for maximum protection under Irish law, choose a GRAI-licensed operator.

What payment methods work for Irish bettors?

Visa and Mastercard debit cards are the most widely accepted. Bank transfers (SEPA), PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Revolut (where gambling is enabled in the app) also work at most licensed sites. Credit card deposits for gambling are banned in Ireland since January 2022.

Are there any betting sites that do NOT accept Irish players?

A small number of operators, particularly some US-facing or locally-licensed sites, do not accept Irish customers. Always check the operator's terms and conditions under 'Restricted territories' before registering. Do not use a VPN to bypass geographic restrictions — this typically voids any winnings.

GRAI Licence Number: How to Check Ireland
