What App Ratings Actually Measure

App Store ratings aggregate all reviews, so a bookmaker with millions of users can maintain a 4.5 rating despite a poor in-play experience simply through volume of satisfied casual bettors. Read recent 1- and 2-star reviews instead: they surface recurring problems (bet-slip bugs, withdrawal delays, app crashes during peak events). Our testing found that all major GRAI-licensed apps are stable on iPhone 14 and later; older Android devices (pre-2020) show more variance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which betting app is best for iPhone in Ireland?

Apps from major GRAI-licensed operators score 4.3–4.6 on the iOS App Store. All support Face ID and Touch ID. Test two or three apps before choosing — the best UX is partly personal preference.

Can I deposit with Apple Pay on betting apps?

Some GRAI-licensed operators now support Apple Pay deposits on their iOS app. Check the operator’s payment page. Under GRAI rules, credit cards are banned; Apple Pay linked to a debit card is permitted.

Do betting apps work offline?

No. Betting apps require an internet connection for real-time odds and bet placement. You can browse static content (account history, upcoming fixtures) while offline on some apps.

Are betting apps safe to use on public Wi-Fi?

Reputable GRAI-licensed operators use TLS encryption on all connections. Using a VPN adds a layer of protection on public networks. Never share your login credentials.

Can I self-exclude via a betting app?

Yes. All GRAI-licensed operators are required to offer self-exclusion tools in their apps. Under GRAI rules, exclusion is effective immediately and marketing must stop promptly.
