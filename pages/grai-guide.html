GRAI Guide: Everything You Need to Know About Ireland's Gambling Regulator

The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) is Ireland's new independent gambling regulator, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. It began issuing remote betting licences on 1 July 2026 and will oversee casino licensing once gaming regulations are finalised, expected in 2027–2028. GRAI also operates the national self-exclusion register and enforces advertising rules.
