GRAI Blacklisted & Unlicensed Gambling Sites Ireland 2026

Updated 5 November 2026 · CraicBet Editorial

⚠️ No published blacklist yet. GRAI has the powers but has not publicly released a list of blocked domains as of November 2026. The authority has stated that unlicensed operators are under investigation. Using unlicensed sites leaves you with zero consumer protections.

GRAI's Power to Block Sites

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, GRAI can apply to the High Court for blocking orders requiring internet service providers (ISPs) to restrict access to unlicensed gambling websites targeting Irish consumers. This follows a model used in several EU countries and replaces the patchwork of voluntary arrangements that existed before July 2026.

The process works like this:

  • GRAI identifies a site operating without an Irish licence
  • GRAI applies to the High Court for a blocking injunction
  • If granted, major ISPs (Eir, Sky, Virgin, Vodafone) must block the domain
  • GRAI can also direct Google/Apple to remove app store listings

Why There Is No Public Blacklist

GRAI has stated that publishing a blacklist can inadvertently advertise illegal sites to consumers. Instead, the authority is pursuing a "white list" approach: promote the licensed register and enforce against non-compliant operators through the courts without publicising individual domain names. This may change as enforcement matures in 2027.
