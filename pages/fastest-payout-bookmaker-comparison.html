What Causes Withdrawal Delays?

Most legitimate delays fall into three categories: (1) KYC verification — first withdrawals trigger identity and address verification; have your passport/driving licence and a utility bill ready; (2) Pending bonus wagering — if you have an active bonus with unfulfilled wagering, withdrawals may be held until it is complete; (3) Manual review — large withdrawals (over €5,000) often trigger a manual check. These are normal and not signs of a problem.

GRAI Rules on Withdrawals

Under the GRAI framework, withholding a legitimate withdrawal is a licence condition breach. If a GRAI-licensed operator delays or refuses a withdrawal without valid grounds (outstanding verification, active bonus terms), you can escalate directly to the GRAI’s formal complaints process. File a complaint with the operator first; if unresolved in 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI. This statutory complaints route is a key advantage of using a GRAI-licensed bookmaker over an unlicensed offshore site.

Tips for Faster Withdrawals

Complete KYC verification before your first deposit, not after a win. Use the same payment method for deposits and withdrawals — most operators route withdrawals back to the original deposit source. Withdraw via Revolut or PayPal rather than bank transfer for faster processing. If a withdrawal is not processed within the operator’s stated timeframe, contact support and reference the GRAI licence conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do bookmaker withdrawals take in Ireland?

With GRAI-licensed operators: e-wallets (Revolut, PayPal) typically same day; debit card 1–2 business days; bank transfer 1–3 business days. First withdrawals may take longer due to identity verification.

Can a GRAI-licensed bookmaker refuse to pay out?

GRAI-licensed operators can withhold withdrawal for legitimate reasons (outstanding verification, bonus terms). Refusing a valid withdrawal is a licence condition breach. Escalate unresolved disputes to the GRAI.

What is KYC verification?

Know Your Customer (KYC) is the identity verification process all GRAI-licensed operators must complete. You need to provide a photo ID (passport, driving licence) and proof of address. Complete this before your first withdrawal to avoid delays.

Is Revolut the fastest withdrawal method?

Revolut and similar e-wallets are typically the fastest withdrawal method at GRAI-licensed bookmakers, often processing same-day. Bank transfer is usually the slowest at 1–3 business days.

How do I complain about a delayed withdrawal?

Contact the operator’s support first. If unresolved within 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI’s formal complaints process at grai.ie. GRAI-licensed operators must maintain an internal complaints procedure as a licence condition.
