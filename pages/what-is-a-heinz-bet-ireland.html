What Is a Heinz Bet in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A Heinz is a full-cover multiple bet on 6 selections. It consists of 57 bets — every possible combination of doubles, trebles, four-folds, five-folds, and one six-fold accumulator. The name comes from Heinz’s famous “57 varieties.”

Heinz Cost and When to Use It

At €0.10 per line, a Heinz costs €5.70. At €1 per line, it costs €57. The bet is popular for horse racing accumulators across a full day's card. You need at least 2 winners to see a return, but with 6 selections all winning at reasonable prices, the return can be very large.

A Lucky 63 is a Heinz with 6 singles added (63 lines total). If you want some return even from just one winner, Lucky 63 is safer; Heinz is cheaper if you are confident in getting at least 2 winners.

How many winners do you need for a Heinz to profit?

You need at least 2 winners to get any return. For profit above stake, you typically need 3–4 winners at decent odds, depending on prices. With all 6 winning at evens or above, the Heinz can return many times the stake.
