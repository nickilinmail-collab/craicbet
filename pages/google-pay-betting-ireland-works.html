Does Google Pay Work for Betting in Ireland?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Google Pay is accepted at some GRAI-licensed Irish betting sites, primarily through Android apps. Like Apple Pay, it must be linked to a debit card or current account to comply with the GRAI credit card ban. Here is the current status and what to expect in 2026.

Google Pay for Betting Deposits

On Android, Google Pay for betting deposits appears as a wallet option in the deposit flow on apps and mobile sites that support it. Tap the payment option, select Google Pay, and authenticate with your phone’s biometrics or PIN. Deposits are instant and processed against the underlying card in your Google Wallet.

As with Apple Pay: the underlying card must be a debit card, not a credit card. Any credit card in your Google Wallet that is used to fund a gambling deposit will be declined under GRAI rules.

Google Pay vs Apple Pay in Ireland

Both services work similarly for betting deposits. Google Pay has slightly wider acceptance in some global markets due to Android’s larger global market share, but in Ireland the two are roughly comparable in availability across GRAI-licensed operators. iOS users cannot use Google Pay; Android users cannot use Apple Pay. Each has its own payment ecosystem.

Google Pay on Desktop

Google Pay for desktop betting deposits is available on Chrome browser with a saved Google Pay method. This extends availability beyond mobile-only betting. Availability at GRAI-licensed operators for desktop Google Pay varies — check the cashier section of your specific operator.

Google Pay Withdrawal Availability

Google Pay withdrawals from betting sites are even less common than Apple Pay withdrawals. Most operators process withdrawals to the underlying debit card rather than directly through Google Pay. The same 1–3 business day debit card timeline applies.

Does Google Pay work for betting in Ireland?

Yes — at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers on Android. Must be linked to a debit card; credit cards are blocked under GRAI rules. Check the cashier page of your specific operator for availability.

Is Google Pay the same as a credit card for betting purposes?

No — Google Pay is a payment method. The underlying card type matters. Debit card in Google Wallet = allowed. Credit card = blocked by GRAI rules.

Can I use Google Pay on a desktop betting site?

Some operators support Google Pay on Chrome desktop. Check the cashier section — availability for desktop Google Pay is less universal than mobile.

Can I withdraw to Google Pay from an Irish betting site?

Rarely — most operators process withdrawals to the underlying debit card rather than directly through Google Pay. Standard debit card processing times of 1–3 days apply.

Which Irish betting sites support Google Pay?

Varies and changes over time. Open the deposit section of any GRAI-licensed operator's Android app to see if Google Pay appears as an option.
