Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular bet type in Ireland?

The accumulator is Ireland’s most popular bet type. Multiple selections are combined on one slip, with all legs needing to win for a payout. The appeal is a large headline return from a small stake — though the compounded house margin makes accas significantly more expensive per bet than singles. See our full accumulator guide for the maths.

What is the difference between a single and an accumulator?

A single is one bet on one outcome. An accumulator combines two or more selections — the return from each winning leg rolls into the next. Singles carry the bookmaker’s margin once; accumulators compound that margin across every leg. The comparison page singles vs accas shows the full expected-value breakdown.

What is lay betting?

Lay betting means betting against an outcome rather than for it — you act as the bookmaker. If the outcome you laid does not occur, you collect the backer’s stake. If it does occur, you pay out at the agreed odds. Lay betting is only available on betting exchanges, not traditional bookmakers. Full rules and liability maths in the lay betting guide.

Can I bet on correct scores in Ireland?

Yes. Correct score markets are offered on most GRAI-licensed betting sites for football matches. You predict the exact final scoreline. Odds are substantially longer than match winner markets because of the precision required. Settlement rules around extra time and abandoned matches vary — our correct score guide covers them fully.

What is the safest bet type for beginners?

Singles on well-understood markets carry the lowest house edge and are the most transparent starting point. Each-way betting on horse racing builds in a safety net by paying out on placed finishes. Full cover bets like the Lucky 15 offer multiple chances to return a profit from four selections. Avoid accumulators and correct score markets until you understand how the margins compound. All betting carries risk — never stake more than you can afford to lose.
