What Is a Goliath Bet in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A Goliath is a full-cover bet on 8 selections consisting of 247 individual bets: 28 doubles, 56 trebles, 70 four-folds, 56 five-folds, 28 six-folds, 8 seven-folds, and 1 eight-fold accumulator. It is the largest standard full-cover bet offered by Irish bookmakers.

Goliath Bet Cost at Different Stakes

At €0.10 per line: 247 × €0.10 = €24.70 total stake. At €0.50 per line: €123.50. At €1 per line: €247. The minimum return requires at least 2 of your 8 selections to win, which pays out the one double that covers those two selections.

Goliath vs Super Heinz: Which to Choose

A Super Heinz covers 7 selections in 120 bets (minimum stake at €0.10 = €12). A Goliath adds an 8th selection and 127 more bets. Use a Goliath only when you have high confidence in all 8 selections, as the cost is significant and returns require multiple winners to generate meaningful profit. For casual use, a Lucky 15 or Heinz is typically more cost-effective.
