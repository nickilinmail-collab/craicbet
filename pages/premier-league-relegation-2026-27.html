Premier League Relegation Betting 2026/27 — Which Clubs Are at Risk?

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Relegation betting is one of the most accessible outright markets in football — identifying which three clubs will drop to the Championship from the Premier League 2026/27 season. This guide covers the market types available, the factors that determine relegation risk, and how Irish punters can find value in these markets.

Understanding Relegation Markets

Three clubs are relegated from the Premier League each season. The main betting markets are: "To Be Relegated" (back a specific club to finish in the bottom three), "First Team Relegated" (which club drops first), and "Relegation Play" (yes/no on whether a specific club avoids the drop).

Newly promoted clubs are typically shortest in the relegation market — their squads are sized for the Championship and the Premier League's intensity represents a significant step up. However, established clubs that suffer managerial instability, poor transfer business or early-season injuries can quickly become genuine candidates.

Factors That Drive Relegation Risk in 2026/27

  • Promoted clubs: The three clubs promoted in spring 2026 begin as favourites to return to the Championship. Squad depth and financial backing are the key differentiators.
  • Managerial changes: Clubs that sack managers mid-season often experience chaotic periods — historically associated with relegation battles.
  • Injury to key players: Small squads with one or two irreplaceable players are most vulnerable to a prolonged injury crisis.
  • Early fixture difficulty: A run of away games against top-six clubs in the first 10 rounds can leave a vulnerable side short of points before Christmas.

Ante-Post Timing for Relegation Markets

The best prices on promoted clubs come immediately after the Championship play-off final in May — bookmakers price them quickly and the market is relatively illiquid. As pre-season progresses and squads are assessed, prices on promoted sides typically shorten. For established clubs, backing them "to be relegated" at the start of the season carries most risk — waiting for a poor October can offer better odds on the same club.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many teams are relegated from the Premier League each season?

Three teams are relegated from the Premier League each season, dropping to the EFL Championship. The bottom three clubs by points at the end of 38 gameweeks go down, with goal difference used as the first tiebreaker.

When is the best time to bet on relegation?

Immediately after the play-off final in May offers the longest pre-season prices on newly promoted clubs. For established clubs, waiting until after a poor start to the season in October or November can provide better odds on a side that has shown genuine vulnerability.

Are there each-way terms on relegation markets?

Relegation markets are typically settled as win-only bets — your club either gets relegated or it does not. Some operators offer markets such as 'bottom six' or 'survival odds' that function differently. Always check market terms before placing.

What happens to my bet if the club is deducted points?

Points deductions — for financial irregularities, insolvency or rule breaches — directly affect where a club finishes and therefore your relegation bet. Most bookmakers settle on the final verified league table. Check individual operator terms regarding deducted points scenarios.
