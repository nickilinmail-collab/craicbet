Last updated: 5 November 2026

The All-Ireland Senior Football Championship is Ireland's most followed GAA betting market. The provincial structure — Leinster, Munster, Connacht and Ulster — determines the route to the All-Ireland final at Croke Park each August.

The Structure of the Football Championship

The championship begins in May with provincial rounds. Ulster is typically the most competitive province, with counties like Donegal, Monaghan, Tyrone, Armagh and Derry regularly contesting. Connacht is dominated by Mayo and Galway. Leinster has seen increased competition with Dublin remaining a consistent force but other counties such as Meath, Kildare and Louth mounting stronger challenges. Munster sees Kerry and Cork as the primary contenders.

Counties that lose in provincial rounds enter the All-Ireland qualifiers, giving a second path to Croke Park. This format makes it difficult for bookmakers to set accurate outright prices until the draw has been made.

Key Betting Markets

  • All-Ireland outright: The headline market; prices typically open in January
  • Provincial outright: Useful for identifying value in perceived weaker provinces
  • Match betting (1X2): Draws are significant in GAA football — always price the draw
  • Handicap: Common when there is a large gap in county strength
  • First scorer / anytime scorer: Available on selected high-profile matches

When does All-Ireland football betting open?

Outright All-Ireland football betting typically opens in January, shortly after the previous year's final. Provincial championship betting opens closer to the draw in spring.

Do draws matter in Gaelic football betting?

Yes, significantly. GAA football has a higher draw rate than soccer, particularly in evenly matched provincial games. Always check the draw price before placing a match bet.

Can I bet on qualifier draw matches?

Yes. Once the qualifier draw is made, GRAI-licensed bookmakers open match betting markets for qualifier games, usually available from the day after the draw.

What is handicap betting in GAA football?

A points handicap is applied to level the market between unevenly matched sides. For example, a weaker county might receive a 7-point start. If that county scores within 7 points of the stronger side, a handicap bet on them wins.
