World Cup 2026 Knockout Stage Betting — Round of 32 to Final Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The FIFA World Cup 2026 knockout stages are the most watched and most bet-on phase of the tournament. With the new 48-team format creating a round of 32 for the first time, there are more knockout matches than ever — and more betting opportunities from the last 32 through to the final.

The New Knockout Structure

The World Cup 2026 features a round of 32 (16 matches) before the traditional round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final. This adds an extra knockout round for every team and means the eventual champion must win six or seven matches from group-stage opener to final. Each round provides discrete betting markets.

Main Knockout Stage Markets

  • To win each match / round: Individual knockout match betting. 90-minute result, extra time and penalty shoot-out markets separate.
  • To reach each round: Which nations will make the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final. Available as ante-post throughout the tournament.
  • Penalty shoot-out betting: Whether the match will reach penalties; the winner of a potential shoot-out. Higher variance but popular markets.
  • Total goals — knockout ties: Over/under on goals across each match. Knockout matches tend to be tighter than group stage.

The 2026 World Cup introduces a Round of 32 for the first time, as 48 teams means the top two plus some best third-placed teams from groups advance — 32 teams total enter the knockout stage. This is different from previous tournaments where 16 teams entered the last 16 directly.

Knockout Stage Betting Approaches

  • To qualify from group: Markets on which 2 of 3 teams advance. Available pre-tournament and during the group stage.
  • To reach each round: To reach the quarter-finals, semis, final. Useful for identifying value on nations likely to have a good run without necessarily winning.
  • Match result with extra time / penalties: Knockout matches can go to extra time (AET) and penalties. Know how your bookmaker settles — many pay on 90 minutes, some on the full 120. Check the specific market rules.
  • Penalty shootout markets: Some operators offer "to win on penalties" — extremely volatile markets; approach carefully.
When is the World Cup 2026?

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is hosted across 16 stadiums in the United States, Canada and Mexico. The tournament runs from June to July 2026, with the final expected at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on 19 July 2026.

How many teams are in the World Cup 2026?

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the first to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. The group stage features 12 groups of four teams, with the top two from each group and eight best third-placed teams advancing to the round of 32.

What is the best ante-post market for the World Cup?

The outright winner market offers the best long-term value for the World Cup. Early prices on the eventual champion can be significantly longer than closer to the tournament.
