Grand National Week Betting Guide Ireland 2026: Aintree Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Aintree Grand National Festival in April is the second-biggest betting event of the year for Irish punters, with the Grand National itself the single most bet-on race globally. The three-day festival runs from Thursday to Saturday.

Thursday: Becher Chase Day

The festival opens with the Manifesto Novices’ Chase and the Maghull Novices’ Chase. The card includes several high-quality National Hunt races and is a full day of competitive betting. Irish trainers typically send strong teams to the Aintree Festival.

Friday: Mildmay Day

Friday is highlighted by the Melling Chase and the Liverpool Hurdle. Grade 1 racing fills the card with championship-level competition. This is a strong betting day with well-established form horses competing.

Saturday: Grand National Day

The Grand National is run on Saturday afternoon over 4 miles 2.5 furlongs with 30 fences. Fields of up to 40 runners make this the most chaotic and unpredictable race of the year — and one of the most bet-on. GRAI-licensed bookmakers open ante-post markets months in advance and carry extensive in-play markets on the day.

Irish-trained horses have won the Grand National many times and Irish owners and trainers dominate the ante-post market most years.

The Grand National is a 4m2f handicap over 30 fences at Aintree. With fields of 40 runners, it is statistically the hardest race to win in UK/Irish racing. Each-way is essential - bookmakers offer 1/5 odds, 5-8 places on the National. Each-way value often exists at prices of 20/1-40/1 for well-handicapped runners with strong Cheltenham form. Irish trainers (Willie Mullins, Gordon Elliott) have excellent modern records. 18+. No pronostic is guaranteed.

When is the Aintree Grand National Festival?

The Aintree Grand National Festival takes place in April, typically the first or second weekend. The Grand National itself is on the Saturday.

How many fences are in the Grand National?

The Grand National course has 30 fences over two circuits of approximately 2 miles 2.5 furlongs. The race is approximately 4 miles 2.5 furlongs in total.

Do GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer non-runner money back for the National?

Many GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer non-runner money back (NRMB) or similar offers for the Grand National. Check the terms and conditions of your chosen operator.

Can I bet in-play on the Grand National?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry in-play markets for the Grand National. Given the chaotic nature of the race, in-play odds can move dramatically at the early fences.
