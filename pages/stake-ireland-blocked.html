Stake Blocked in Ireland — What Happened & What to Do Next

Last updated: November 2026. In September 2026, Stake geo-blocked access for all Irish users following engagement with the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI). If you try to visit Stake.com from Ireland, you will see: "Due to licensing restrictions, access to Stake from your current location is not available."

This page explains why it happened, how to recover your funds, and which GRAI-licensed alternatives you can use safely and legally.

⚠️ Affected Irish Stake customer? You are entitled to a full refund of your deposited balance. The GRAI confirmed it is working with Stake to ensure Irish customers receive their money. Contact Stake support directly through your account dashboard.

Why Did Stake Block Ireland?

Ireland's Gambling Regulation Act 2024 required all operators targeting Irish customers to hold a remote betting licence issued by the GRAI from 1 July 2026 onwards. Stake — registered in Curaçao and operating without an Irish licence — blocked access for Irish users following GRAI intervention, in order to avoid formal enforcement action.

The GRAI confirmed that Stake was one of multiple platforms to geo-block Ireland, alongside prediction market operators Polymarket and Kalshi. The regulator is investigating more than 100 unlicensed operators serving Irish customers.

Will Stake Come Back to Ireland?

Yes, possibly. A Stake spokesperson confirmed: the platform "closed registrations in the Irish market" but intends to "apply for a licence to operate under the new regime." If Stake is granted a GRAI remote betting licence, it could legally re-enter the Irish market. The GRAI has issued licences to 32 operators as of September 2026, with more applications under review.

How to Get Your Money Back from Stake

  • Log in to your Stake account (access to the account dashboard may still be available even if betting is blocked)
  • Go to the wallet section and request a withdrawal of your remaining balance
  • If you cannot access your account, email Stake's customer support directly
  • The GRAI is monitoring the refund process — if you experience difficulties, you can file a complaint at grai.ie

GRAI-Licensed Alternatives for Irish Players

Rather than waiting for Stake to return, Irish punters can bet legally with any of the 32 operators holding a valid GRAI remote betting licence. These operators have passed GRAI consumer protection checks, hold segregated player funds, and are required to offer responsible gambling tools.

To verify any operator's licence, download the official licence register at grai.ie/register.
