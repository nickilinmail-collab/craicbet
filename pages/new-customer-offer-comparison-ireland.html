Calculating Real Welcome Offer Value

For a €30 SNR free bet (stake not returned): place it on 2.0 odds (evens). If it wins, you receive €30 profit (the stake is not returned). The expected value of the free bet at fair 2.0 odds is €15 (50% chance of winning €30). To maximise: use the free bet on odds as close to 2.0 as possible on a near-50/50 market to minimise variance. Using it on a 1.1 shot (90% probability) nets only €3 expected value. Our free bets guide includes a full value calculator.

The GRAI’s Inducements Rules and Welcome Offers

Since July 2026, GRAI regulations restrict where and how welcome offers can be advertised: no TV ads before the 9pm watershed, no targeting of self-excluded players, no inducements in communications not specifically requested. The offers themselves remain legal and available. The practical impact: welcome offers are harder to find passively but still available to customers who actively seek them.

Terms to Check Before Claiming

Before claiming any welcome offer: (1) minimum qualifying deposit amount; (2) minimum qualifying odds for the first bet; (3) wagering requirement and qualifying odds for the wagering; (4) maximum withdrawal from welcome bonus; (5) expiry clock (usually 7–30 days). Missing any of these can invalidate the bonus entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a free bet SNR?

SNR means ‘stake not returned’. If your free bet wins, you receive only the profit — not your stake back. A €30 SNR free bet at 2.0 (evens) wins €30 profit; a €30 SR (stake returned) bet wins €60 total.

What is the real value of a €30 free bet?

An SNR free bet placed on 2.0 (evens) odds has an expected value of approximately €15 (50% × €30 profit). The closer the odds are to evens, the closer you get to 50% of the headline value.

Are welcome offers still available after GRAI rules?

Yes. Welcome offers are legal under GRAI licensing. The GRAI’s inducements rules restrict advertising (watershed, targeting), not the offers themselves.

What wagering requirement should I expect?

For deposit match bonuses, wagering requirements of 5–10x the bonus amount are common. This means a €50 bonus at 5x wagering requires €250 in qualifying bets before withdrawal.

Can I claim welcome offers at multiple bookmakers?

Yes. Each GRAI-licensed operator can offer a separate welcome bonus for new customers. There is no restriction on claiming bonuses at multiple operators.
