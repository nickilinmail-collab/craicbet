Europe's 16 Qualifiers

UEFA's allocation for the 2026 World Cup is 16 teams. This includes the traditional powerhouses — the main European footballing nations — alongside several smaller nations who qualify through the UEFA qualifying rounds. The draw determines which European nations face easier or harder paths through the group stage and into the knockouts.

Main European-Specific Markets

  • Best European nation (highest finisher): Which of the 16 European teams goes furthest in the tournament.
  • To be the last European team standing: Equivalent to reaching the final or winning from the European cohort.
  • European nation to win the tournament: European teams have won the majority of World Cup titles historically; this market tracks the European contingent in the outright winner market.
  • Individual European nation markets: Per-country outright winner, to reach the final, to reach the semi-final — available for all 16 European qualifiers.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the first to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. The group stage features 12 groups of four teams, with the top two from each group and eight best third-placed teams advancing to the round of 32.

Top European Contenders for 2026

  • Traditional heavyweights: France, Germany, Spain, England, Portugal — all likely to be near the top of outright markets. Post-2022, France and Brazil are historically the most backed nations.
  • Dark horses: Netherlands, Belgium (aging squad by 2026), Croatia, and rising nations like Austria and Scotland (if they qualify) may offer outright value.
  • Republic of Ireland: Ireland are in UEFA qualifying. Their outright odds for tournament glory are long (100/1+) — focus on group stage betting if they qualify.

Betting Strategy for European Nations at World Cup 2026

  • Ante-post value: Place early outright bets on European nations before the draw — the draw can dramatically change a team's odds, and early prices are often generous.
  • Group stage betting: With 48 teams in 16 groups of 3, the top 2 from each group plus 8 best third-placed qualify — group stage exits are less likely for major European nations.
  • Each-way tournament outright: Backing a European nation to reach the final (each-way, top 2) can offer better value than backing them to win outright.
