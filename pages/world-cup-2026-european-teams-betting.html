World Cup 2026 European Teams Betting — Which Nation Goes Furthest?

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Europe sends 16 teams to the World Cup 2026 — the largest continental allocation. For Irish punters, tracking European performance is natural given the familiarity with European club football. This guide covers the betting markets for European nations in the 2026 tournament.

Europe's 16 Qualifiers

UEFA's allocation for the 2026 World Cup is 16 teams. This includes the traditional powerhouses — the main European footballing nations — alongside several smaller nations who qualify through the UEFA qualifying rounds. The draw determines which European nations face easier or harder paths through the group stage and into the knockouts.

Main European-Specific Markets

  • Best European nation (highest finisher): Which of the 16 European teams goes furthest in the tournament.
  • To be the last European team standing: Equivalent to reaching the final or winning from the European cohort.
  • European nation to win the tournament: European teams have won the majority of World Cup titles historically; this market tracks the European contingent in the outright winner market.
  • Individual European nation markets: Per-country outright winner, to reach the final, to reach the semi-final — available for all 16 European qualifiers.
