Betting App Not Working in Ireland? How to Fix Common Issues

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

When a betting app stops working during a live event, every second counts. This guide covers the most common causes of Irish betting app failures and the fastest fixes for each one.

Quick Fixes: Try These First

  1. Force-close and reopen the app. On iOS: swipe up and hold, swipe the app off the top. On Android: square button > swipe away. Reopen.
  2. Check your connection. Switch from Wi-Fi to 4G/5G or vice versa. Test another app (browser) to confirm it’s not a network issue.
  3. Clear the app cache. On Android: Settings > Apps > [Betting app] > Clear Cache. On iOS, delete and reinstall (settings and account are stored server-side).
  4. Update the app. Go to App Store / Play Store and check for updates. Outdated apps frequently break after platform OS updates.

VPN Conflicts

If you have a VPN active, disable it. Many GRAI-licensed betting apps block connections from known VPN IP addresses. Even a VPN enabled for another purpose (privacy, streaming) can prevent the betting app from connecting. Toggle the VPN off, then reopen the app.

Login Issues

If the login screen loads but credentials fail: check Caps Lock is not on, verify you are using the correct email, and try a password reset. If the account is locked after too many failed attempts, contact support via the browser-based site (not the app) to unlock it.

Specific Feature Not Loading (Cash-Out, In-Play)

Cash-out and in-play features are data-intensive. If the rest of the app works but specific features fail: (1) try a full refresh; (2) check the operator’s Twitter/X account for service outage notices; (3) use the mobile browser version as a temporary alternative. Under GRAI rules, operators must inform customers of significant service disruptions.

If the App Is Completely Down

Check the operator’s social media channels and their website for outage notifications. If you have pending live bets and cannot access cash-out, see our guide on what to do when a betting site is down. GRAI-licensed operators must maintain service reliability as a licence condition.

Android vs iOS App Issues: Differences

Android APK apps (most Irish betting apps) require manual updates — the app does not update automatically through the Play Store. When an app starts misbehaving, the fix is usually to redownload the latest APK from the betting site's official page and reinstall. Uninstall the old version first, then install the new APK.

iOS apps update through the App Store. If an iOS betting app is misbehaving, go to the App Store, search the app, and check for pending updates. iOS 17+ may background-refresh apps less frequently, meaning the app version you have open may be outdated — a fresh open after an update resolves most issues.

Why is my betting app not working in Ireland?

Common causes: poor connection, VPN active, outdated app version, corrupted cache, or server-side outage. Try force-closing, switching networks, disabling VPN, and updating the app.

How do I clear the cache on a betting app?

Android: Settings > Apps > [app name] > Clear Cache. iOS: delete and reinstall the app (your account is stored server-side, so you just log back in).

Can a VPN stop my betting app from working?

Yes — many GRAI-licensed operators block VPN IP addresses. Disable any active VPN and reopen the app.

What do I do if cash-out is not working on my betting app?

Refresh the bet slip, switch from app to mobile browser, or check if the operator has announced a service issue on social media. Cash-out is a feature, not a right — operators can suspend it during certain events.

What if the app is down and I have live bets?

Open the mobile browser version as a backup. If still inaccessible, contact support immediately. GRAI-licensed operators must maintain service continuity as a licence condition; persistent outages can be reported to the GRAI.
