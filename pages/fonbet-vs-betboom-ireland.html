Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fonbet safe for Irish punters?

Fonbet carries substantial risk for Irish punters: Curaçao licensing, no GRAI Irish protections, uncertain payment availability and primarily Russian-market focus. We recommend GRAI-licensed alternatives.

Is BetBoom available in Ireland?

BetBoom is primarily a Russian-market operator. Irish payment methods may not be accepted and withdrawal reliability in Ireland is uncertain.

What is Curaçao licensing?

Curaçao is considered the weakest mainstream gambling licensing jurisdiction. Its regulatory framework offers minimal player protections compared to MGA or GRAI licensing.

Russian-origin bookmakers vary significantly. Those operating primarily in Russian markets without Western European licences carry higher risk for Irish punters regarding payment reliability and dispute resolution.
