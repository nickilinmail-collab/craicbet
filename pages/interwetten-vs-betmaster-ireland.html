Interwetten: European Sportsbook Heritage

Interwetten’s longevity (over 35 years) gives it credibility in European sportsbook markets. It holds an MGA licence and an Austrian licence — both reputable regulators. Its pricing on football is competitive (4.5–5.5% margins) and it covers a wide range of European leagues. For Irish punters, the absence of a GRAI licence remains the key gap.

Betmaster: Crypto-First Approach

Betmaster targets crypto bettors, accepting Bitcoin and several altcoins for deposits and withdrawals. Its Curaçao licence offers minimal player protections by global regulatory standards. Irish punters using Betmaster have no meaningful complaint escalation route.

The GRAI Context

Both operators fail the primary criterion for Irish punters who want statutory protection: a GRAI remote betting licence. Interwetten’s MGA licence provides more protection than Betmaster’s Curaçao, but neither matches the Irish-law protections of a GRAI-licensed operator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Interwetten licensed in Ireland?

Interwetten does not hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. It operates under Malta MGA and Austrian licences.

Does Betmaster accept Irish players?

Betmaster accepts international registrations including from Ireland, but holds only a Curaçao licence — one of the weakest regulatory frameworks globally.

Is Curaçao licensing reputable?

Curaçao licensing is considered a low-bar regulatory framework with limited player protections. It is significantly weaker than MGA or GRAI licensing.

Can I use crypto on these bookmakers?

Betmaster accepts cryptocurrency deposits. Interwetten does not. GRAI-licensed operators are generally restricted to regulated payment methods.

How old is Interwetten?

Interwetten was founded in 1990, making it one of the oldest online bookmakers in Europe. Its longevity and MGA licence make it a relatively trustworthy international operator, though it lacks GRAI Irish protections.
