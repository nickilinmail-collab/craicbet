The Ireland women’s rugby team competes in the Women’s Six Nations and World Cup qualifying. The team is ranked in the top 10 globally and has become a more prominent betting market as professional structures grow in Irish women’s rugby.

Ireland Women’s Rugby in 2026

The Women’s Six Nations is the primary annual competition for Ireland women, running in April and May. The championship features six nations (Ireland, England, France, Wales, Scotland, Italy) competing in a home-and-away round-robin format. England and France are historically the dominant sides, but Ireland have become more competitive in recent seasons.

The Rugby World Cup qualifying cycle also generates betting interest, particularly in the knockout rounds that determine which teams represent their nations at the World Cup.

Betting Markets

  • Women’s Six Nations outright winner: Available from January each year
  • Match result (1X2): Available for all Six Nations fixtures
  • Handicap: Points handicaps are common in rugby betting
  • Over/under total points: Total points market for each match

Tips for Betting Ireland Women’s Rugby

Squad depth and player availability are key factors. The transition to professional structures means player movement between provinces, overseas clubs and the national squad is worth monitoring. Ireland’s home advantage at Musgrave Park or the RDS should be factored into handicap assessments.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Women's Six Nations?

The Women's Six Nations runs in April and May each year. Some editions have been moved to autumn. The exact schedule is published by World Rugby and the Six Nations organisation.

Can I bet on Ireland women's rugby internationally?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry Women's Six Nations betting markets throughout the tournament. Match betting, outright winner and handicap markets are all available.

How does handicap betting work in rugby?

A points handicap is applied to level the market. For example, if Ireland receive a 10-point start against England, an Ireland handicap bet wins if Ireland lose by 9 points or fewer, or win outright.

Are there in-play markets for Ireland women's rugby?

In-play markets for the Women's Six Nations are available at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Availability is lower than for the men's Six Nations.
