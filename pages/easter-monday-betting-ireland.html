Easter Monday Betting Ireland: Fairyhouse National Meeting Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Easter Monday is one of the most important days in the Irish betting calendar, primarily because Fairyhouse Racecourse hosts the Irish Grand National — the most bet-on single horse race in Ireland each year.

Irish Grand National at Fairyhouse

The Irish Grand National is a Grade A handicap chase run over approximately 3 miles 5 furlongs at Fairyhouse Racecourse in County Meath. It attracts some of the biggest fields of the National Hunt season and is the highlight of the Easter racing programme. The race is typically run on Easter Monday and draws enormous betting volumes, surpassed only by Cheltenham week.

The Irish Grand National is a staying handicap, which means the weights are designed to give every horse a theoretical chance. This makes it one of the more difficult races to win at short prices and historically produces good-value winning bets. Field sizes are large — typically 25 to 30 runners.

Fairyhouse Easter Festival

The Irish Grand National is the centrepiece of a three-day Easter festival at Fairyhouse, with Grade 1 racing also featuring on Saturday and Sunday. The full festival provides multiple days of premium betting.

When is the Irish Grand National?

The Irish Grand National is run at Fairyhouse on Easter Monday each year. The exact date varies with the Easter calendar, typically falling in March or April.

How many runners are in the Irish Grand National?

The Irish Grand National typically has a field of 25 to 30 runners, one of the largest fields in National Hunt racing. The large field increases the importance of a good draw and clean jump at the first few fences.

What is the distance of the Irish Grand National?
