Our Methodology — How We Review Operators

Every operator review on CraicBet is evaluated against six criteria, applied consistently across all reviews:

1
Valid GRAI licence verified on grai.ie

We check the GRAI public licence register at grai.ie before publishing any review. No GRAI licence means no review and no link. We only promote operators with a valid, current remote betting licence. Licence type, number and expiry date are displayed in every review.

2
Responsible gambling tools

We test whether deposit limits, session limits, cooling-off periods, and self-exclusion are genuinely accessible — not buried in settings menus. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 mandates these tools for all GRAI-licensed operators. We verify compliance and note any gaps.

3
Payment method speed — tested withdrawal times

We test withdrawal speeds using real accounts where possible, and collect reader-reported experiences. Quoted withdrawal times in reviews reflect tested or consistently reported times, not the best-case times quoted in operator marketing. Debit card, bank transfer and e-wallet speeds are assessed separately.

4
Bonus terms — wagering requirements, minimum odds, SNR explained honestly

We read the full terms before describing any bonus. Wagering requirements are stated as a total amount (e.g., "wager €30 bonus × 30 = €900 before withdrawal") rather than just the multiplier. Stakes-not-returned (SNR) each-way promotions are explained. Minimum odds requirements are highlighted. We do not describe bonuses as "free money".

5
Customer support response time and quality

We contact support via live chat and email and report realistic response times. We note whether live chat is staffed by humans or bots, and whether complaint escalation paths are clear.

6
Mobile experience

Betting is predominantly mobile in Ireland. We assess apps (iOS and Android) and mobile browser performance on betting slip usability, in-play load times, and deposit/withdrawal flow. App store ratings are noted but not substituted for direct assessment.

What We Don't Do

  • No fake reviews — every operator assessment is based on real testing or verified reader reports
  • No invented statistics — if a figure cannot be sourced, we don't use it; if it's an estimate, we say so
  • No "guaranteed wins" — no betting system guarantees profit; we explain the mathematics honestly
  • No brand names in headlines — operator brands do not appear in our URLs, page titles, H1/H2 headings, meta descriptions or schema markup
  • No casino licence claims — GRAI has issued zero casino licences as of November 2026; we never claim otherwise
  • No unlicensed operator promotion — we only link to operators with a valid GRAI remote betting licence
