How to Reopen a Closed Betting Account in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Whether you can reopen a closed betting account depends entirely on why it was closed in the first place. A cooling-off period expires automatically; a commercial closure is usually permanent at that operator; a self-exclusion cannot be reversed during the exclusion period under GRAI rules. Here is how each type works.

Cooling-Off Period: Automatic Reopening

A cooling-off period is a temporary restriction you apply to your own account. Under GRAI responsible gambling requirements, once a cooling-off period expires, your account should automatically become accessible again — or you may need to log in and confirm you wish to resume. GRAI rules require that increasing limits (and ending cooling-off) has a mandatory reflection period of at least 24–48 hours.

Temporary Suspension: Contact Support

If the operator suspended your account for investigation (unusual activity, payment issues), contact customer support with your account details and a request to reinstate. You will likely need to provide KYC documentation if the suspension was ID-related. Processing typically takes 3–10 business days.

Self-Exclusion: Cannot Be Undone Within the Period

Self-exclusion under GRAI rules is intentionally irreversible during the exclusion period. If you self-excluded for 6 months, you cannot reopen the account for 6 months. This applies to all GRAI-licensed operators enrolled in the National Gambling Exclusion Register (NGER). Once the exclusion period ends, there is typically a mandatory 24-hour reflection period before reactivation.

If you are struggling with gambling and have broken your self-exclusion, contact the Gambling Awareness Trust at 1800 753 753 or gamblingcare.ie for support.

Commercial Closure: Usually Permanent at That Operator

If a bookmaker closed your account for commercial reasons (you are too profitable) or for T&C violations, reopening with that specific operator is very unlikely. Your options are other GRAI-licensed operators, which are unaffected by one operator’s commercial decision.

How to Reopen a Voluntarily Closed Account

  1. Contact the site's customer support via live chat or email.
  2. State your registered email address and that you wish to reopen the account.
  3. If a cooling-off period was set (e.g. 30 days), you must wait until it has fully elapsed — sites legally cannot reopen accounts early for responsible gambling closures.
  4. Confirm you are aware of responsible gambling resources and wish to continue betting.
  5. You may be asked to re-verify your identity if documents have expired.

GAMSTOP and National Self-Exclusion

If your account was closed because you registered with GAMSTOP (the UK-wide self-exclusion scheme) or the Irish National Gambling Exclusion Register, GRAI-licensed operators are legally required to maintain your exclusion for the duration you selected (6 months, 1 year, or 5 years for GAMSTOP). No operator covered by these schemes can reopen your account early. When your exclusion expires, contact GAMSTOP to confirm it has lifted before attempting to reopen accounts. If you're concerned about your gambling, speak to the Gambling Awareness Trust: 1800 753 753.

Can you reopen a closed betting account in Ireland?

It depends on why it was closed. Cooling-off periods expire automatically. Self-exclusions cannot be reversed within the exclusion period. Commercial closures are usually permanent at that operator.

How do I reopen an account after a cooling-off period?

Log in when the period expires — you may need to confirm you wish to resume. Under GRAI rules, there is a mandatory 24–48 hour reflection period before the account is fully reactivated.

Can I reopen an account if I self-excluded?

Not during the exclusion period. GRAI rules make self-exclusion irreversible for the specified duration. After it expires, contact the operator to initiate reactivation with the mandatory reflection period.

What if I want to reopen an account that was closed for a T&Cs violation?

Very difficult — operators typically maintain permanent bans for T&Cs violations. You can try contacting support, but most will not reopen accounts for bonus abuse or multi-accounting.

Does closing one account affect my accounts at other bookmakers?

No — each bookmaker is independent. A commercial closure at one operator does not affect your accounts at others, unless you are on the NGER (National Gambling Exclusion Register) for self-exclusion reasons.
