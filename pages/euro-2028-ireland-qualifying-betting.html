Euro 2028 Ireland — Host Nation Betting & Tournament Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Republic of Ireland qualifies automatically for UEFA Euro 2028 as one of the co-host nations. This guide covers what automatic qualification means for Irish football fans and bettors, and the key markets surrounding Ireland's participation in a home tournament.

What Automatic Qualification Means

As a co-host, the Republic of Ireland is guaranteed a place in the UEFA Euro 2028 tournament without needing to progress through the standard qualifying rounds. This is a significant structural advantage — Ireland's squad will have more competitive preparation time and the manager can plan for tournament football without the pressure of qualification results. It also allows resources to be focused on squad building and tactical preparation rather than qualifying campaign survival.

Ireland's Tournament Markets for 2028

  • To progress from the group stage: Ireland's most realistic initial target. Home matches at the Aviva will boost crowd support; advancing from the group would be a significant achievement.
  • Outright winner: Ireland will likely open at long odds for the tournament winner — but home advantage, automatic qualification and two or three years of squad development between now and 2028 could improve their market position.
  • Top scorer — Ireland: Which Irish player scores most across the tournament. Popular market once squads are known.
  • Ireland performance props: To reach the last 8 / last 4; to concede more/fewer than X goals; to score in every group match etc.

Building Toward 2028

With automatic qualification secured, the Republic's focus between now and Euro 2028 is squad development. Nations League results and friendly matches provide the competitive base for a squad that must be ready to perform at a home tournament. This context makes Ireland's October–November 2026 matches significant for early 2028 market formation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Ireland have to qualify for Euro 2028?

No — the Republic of Ireland qualifies automatically as one of five co-host associations for UEFA Euro 2028. The other co-hosts are England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. No qualifying rounds are required for host associations.

Which Irish players are likely in the Euro 2028 squad?

The 2028 squad will be selected closer to the tournament — it is too early to name specific selections. Ireland's squad has significant depth in certain positions, particularly in the Premier League and Championship. The manager's selection philosophy over the 2026-27 and 2027-28 seasons will shape the tournament squad.

When will Ireland play their home Euro 2028 matches?

The Euro 2028 match schedule and venue allocation will be confirmed by UEFA and the five host associations. Ireland's home matches are expected at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, with capacity approximately 51,700. Specific fixtures and opponents will only be known after the tournament draw.

What are Ireland's realistic Euro 2028 expectations?

As a host nation, Ireland faces elevated expectations from home fans. Realistic objectives are qualifying from the group stage and potentially reaching the quarter-finals. Any advance beyond that would be a remarkable achievement. Specific predictions are beyond the scope of this pre-tournament guide — no outcome is guaranteed.

Euro 2028 Ireland Qualifying: Group and Format

Euro 2028 is hosted across the UK and Ireland (joint bid confirmed). As co-hosts, Ireland receive automatic qualification — no qualifying campaign needed. The Republic of Ireland will be in Pot 1 for the group stage draw as a host nation.
