Betting Sites in Ireland: The Post-GRAI Guide for 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

Betting Just Became Ireland's Only Fully Regulated Vertical

Since 1 July 2026, every bookmaker serving Irish customers online needs a GRAI remote betting licence — the first gambling products in Irish history with modern domestic regulation. Casino licensing is still ahead, which makes betting the one place where "Irish-licensed" already means something you can verify.

What a GRAI Betting Licence Guarantees You

A licensed bookmaker must verify age, pay out winnings as a licence condition, refuse credit cards, follow the advertising rules, and answer to the GRAI complaints process — with fines up to €20m behind all of it. An unlicensed book offering odds to Irish customers is now committing a criminal offence.

How to Verify Any Bookmaker in 2 Minutes

Find the company name in the site footer (the company, not the brand), open the official GRAI register, and search it. Licence numbers follow a readable pattern — RB in the number means remote betting. We log every register change on our daily tracker, including new licensees the day they appear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online betting legal in Ireland?

Yes — fully regulated since July 2026. Bookmakers serving Irish customers need a GRAI remote betting licence; operating without one is a criminal offence.

How do I know if a betting site is licensed in Ireland?

Search the company name in the official GRAI register (RB in the number = remote betting), or check our daily tracker.

Do I pay tax on betting winnings in Ireland?

No — winnings are tax-free for players; the 2% betting duty is paid by operators. Details: gambling tax guide.
