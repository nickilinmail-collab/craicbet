Can You Withdraw Free Bet Winnings in Ireland?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Yes — you can withdraw free bet winnings in Ireland, but only the profit portion, not the free bet stake itself, in most cases. Understanding the difference between SNR and SR free bets is essential before you claim any welcome offer.

SNR vs SR Free Bets: The Key Difference

SNR (Stake Not Returned) is the most common type. If you place a €10 SNR free bet at odds of 3.0 (2/1), your return is €20 total — but the €10 "stake" was the free bet token, so you receive only €10 profit in cash. The free bet token itself does not come back to you.

SR (Stake Returned) free bets work like real money. If you place a €10 SR free bet at 3.0, you receive the full €30 — €20 profit plus the €10 stake back as withdrawable cash. SR free bets are rarer and represent genuinely better value.

Wagering Requirements and Free Bet Winnings

Some welcome offers add a wagering requirement on top of the SNR/SR structure. For example, a "€50 free bet with 5x wagering" means you must bet €250 total (5 × €50) before winnings from the free bet are withdrawable as cash. Always read the full terms before claiming.

Under GRAI transparency rules, operators must display wagering requirements clearly alongside any bonus offer. If the terms are buried or unclear, that is worth flagging to the GRAI.

How to Calculate the Real Value of a Free Bet

For an SNR free bet, the real cash value is roughly (odds − 1) ÷ odds × stake. At odds of 2.0 (evens), a €10 SNR free bet is worth only €5 in real cash on average — a 50% conversion rate. At higher odds (3.0+), conversion improves. At odds-on (below 2.0), it worsens. This is why matched bettors target specific odds ranges when converting free bets.

Expiry: How Long Do Free Bets Last?

Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers set free bet expiry at 7–30 days from issue. The clock typically starts when the bonus is credited, not when you claim it. If a free bet expires unused, it is forfeit — you cannot claim it back. GRAI rules require operators to notify you before expiry, but check your promotional emails and account notifications.

When Winnings Cannot Be Withdrawn Immediately

  • Acca boost / enhanced odds offers: Sometimes the extra winnings from a boosted bet are credited as bonus funds, not cash. Read the promo terms before placing.
  • Wagering applied to winnings: Some less-common offers apply wagering to the winnings from a free bet. These are inferior offers — the standard is that free bet winnings are cashable immediately.
  • Pending bonus: If you have an active casino or sports bonus running simultaneously, withdrawing may forfeit it. Manage bonuses separately — only run one at a time if you plan to cash out.
Can you withdraw free bet winnings in Ireland?

Yes — you can withdraw the profit from a free bet. With SNR free bets (most common), the token stake is not returned, only the winnings. With SR free bets, both the stake and winnings are withdrawable.

What does SNR mean on a free bet?

Stake Not Returned. If you win with an SNR free bet, you receive the profit but not the original free bet amount back as cash.

What does SR mean on a free bet?

Stake Returned. You receive both the profit and the free bet amount back as withdrawable cash if your bet wins.

Do free bet winnings have wagering requirements?

Sometimes. Check the full T&Cs of each offer. GRAI rules require operators to display wagering requirements clearly. If a wagering requirement applies, you must bet that amount before winnings become withdrawable.

How long do free bets last on Irish betting sites?

Typically 7–30 days from when the free bet is credited to your account. Check your account notifications — expiry dates are shown in the bonus/promotions section.
