Boku is a mobile billing service that lets you deposit at online casinos by charging the amount to your phone bill or prepaid credit — no bank details, no card number, just your mobile number. It's one of the most private deposit methods available to Irish players, though it comes with low daily limits and no withdrawal capability.

How Boku Works at Irish Online Casinos

Boku acts as a bridge between the casino and your mobile network operator. Here's the process step by step:

  1. In the casino cashier, select Boku or "Pay by Mobile"
  2. Enter your Irish mobile number and choose the deposit amount
  3. Receive a confirmation SMS from Boku
  4. Reply "YES" (or tap the confirmation link) to authorise the charge
  5. The deposit appears in your casino account instantly
  6. The charge is added to your next phone bill or deducted from your prepaid balance

The two-step SMS confirmation means that even if someone knows your mobile number, they cannot trigger a Boku deposit without access to your phone.

Boku Deposit Limits — The Key Constraint

Boku's low limits are set by Irish mobile network operators, not the casinos. Typical limits:

📲 Boku Limits at Irish Casinos

  • Minimum deposit: €5–10
  • Maximum single deposit: €10–30
  • Daily limit: €30 (most networks)
  • Monthly limit: may vary by network contract
  • Withdrawals: Not supported

These limits make Boku unsuitable for high-volume play. It's best suited to players who want a small, controlled deposit with no bank account exposure.

Privacy Benefits

Boku's main advantage is privacy. No card number, sort code, or bank account number is ever shared with the casino. The deposit shows on your phone bill as a third-party service charge, which is less identifiable than a direct casino debit. For players on prepaid SIMs who don't use a bank account for gambling, Boku offers a rare card-free deposit route.

Irish networks Eir, Three, and Vodafone all support Boku billing. If you're on a smaller MVNO, check the casino's cashier to confirm your network is listed.

Withdrawals — You'll Need a Different Method

Boku cannot process withdrawals. Mobile billing infrastructure only flows in one direction — from customer to merchant. Before you deposit via Boku, register a withdrawal method in the casino cashier: a Visa or Mastercard Debit card, Revolut, e-wallet (Skrill, Neteller), or your bank IBAN. This ensures you can cash out without delays when you're ready.

Can I withdraw using Boku at an Irish casino?▾
No. Boku is a deposit-only payment method. You cannot withdraw winnings back to your phone bill. You must register a separate withdrawal method — such as a debit card, Revolut, or bank transfer IBAN — before requesting any payout.
What is the Boku deposit limit at Irish casinos?▾
Boku deposit limits are set by your mobile network operator, typically €10–30 per day. This is significantly lower than card or e-wallet limits. The low cap reflects the fact that charges appear on a phone bill rather than a dedicated payment account.

Boku supports major Irish networks including Eir, Three, and Vodafone. Availability at a specific casino depends on the casino's integration. Always check the cashier to confirm Boku is available and that your network is listed before attempting to deposit.
Is Boku safe to use at online casinos?▾
Yes. Boku is a well-established payment processor used by major online platforms globally. Your bank details and card numbers are never shared with the casino — only your mobile number. All transactions require SMS confirmation from your registered number, adding a layer of security.
