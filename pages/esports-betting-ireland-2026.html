Esports Betting Ireland 2026: CS2, LoL & Dota Under GRAI

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Esports betting is fully regulated under the GRAI framework in Ireland as of 2026. Irish punters can legally bet on CS2, League of Legends (LoL), VALORANT, Dota 2, Rocket League and other titles through GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

GRAI Framework for Esports Betting

The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 covers betting on any event of uncertain outcome, explicitly including esports as a regulated sport for betting purposes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers are permitted to offer esports betting markets, and the same protections apply to esports bettors as to traditional sports bettors: licensed payout obligations, age verification, credit card ban and responsible gambling tools.

Key Esports Markets

  • CS2 (Counter-Strike 2): Match winner, map winner, pistol round winner, in-play round betting. Major events: ESL Pro League, BLAST Premier, IEM tournaments
  • League of Legends: Match winner, game winner, outright tournament winner. Major events: Worlds, MSI, LEC (Europe)
  • VALORANT: Match winner, map winner. Major events: VCT (VALORANT Champions Tour)
  • Dota 2: Match winner, game winner. Major events: The International, Dota Pro Circuit events
  • Rocket League: Match winner, outright tournament winner

Responsible Gambling in Esports

Esports audiences skew younger than traditional sports. The GRAI’s age verification requirements apply equally to esports betting. All GRAI-licensed bookmakers require age verification before allowing any betting activity, including esports. The Gambling Awareness Trust helpline (1800 753 753) covers problem gambling across all forms including esports.

Is esports betting legal in Ireland under GRAI?

Yes. Esports is explicitly covered under the GRAI framework as a regulated sport for betting purposes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers can legally offer CS2, LoL, VALORANT, Dota 2 and other esports markets.

Which esports titles are covered at GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

CS2, League of Legends, VALORANT, Dota 2 and Rocket League are the most commonly featured titles. Some operators also cover FIFA/EA Sports FC, Fortnite and other popular competitive games.

Can I bet in-play on esports matches?

Yes. In-play betting is widely available for CS2 and League of Legends at major GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Round-by-round betting in CS2 and game-by-game betting in LoL are popular in-play markets.

How do I find esports markets at GRAI-licensed bookmakers?

Look for an Esports section in the sports menu at your chosen GRAI-licensed bookmaker. Major events including ESL Pro League (CS2), Worlds (LoL) and The International (Dota 2) are typically featured.
