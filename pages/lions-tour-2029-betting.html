British & Irish Lions Tour 2029 — Early Betting Guide for Irish Punters

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The British and Irish Lions tour 2029 is the next scheduled Lions tour, with the destination to be confirmed in the coming years. For Irish punters, the Lions generate significant interest given the number of Irish players who historically feature in the touring squad.

What Is the British and Irish Lions?

The British and Irish Lions is a combined rugby union team drawn from the best players of England, Scotland, Wales and Ireland. The team tours a Southern Hemisphere nation (Australia, New Zealand or South Africa) every four years, playing regional matches and a three-test series against the host nation. It is one of rugby union's most prestigious touring traditions, dating back to 1888.

Lions 2029 — What We Know

The Lions tour schedule follows a four-year cycle. With the 2025 tour to Australia completed, the next Lions tour is expected in 2029. The host nation for 2029 will be announced by World Rugby. The two most likely destinations are New Zealand (the All Blacks) or South Africa (the Springboks) — both destinations carry enormous historical significance for the Lions tradition.

Main Betting Markets for Lions Tours

  • Test series winner: Which team — Lions or the host nation — wins the three-test series. This is the flagship market.
  • Individual test results: Match betting on each of the three tests separately.
  • Top try scorer for the Lions: Which Lions player scores most tries across the tour — popular novelty market.
  • To be named Lions captain: Pre-tour selection market — Irish players have captained recent Lions tours.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the next British and Irish Lions tour?

The next British and Irish Lions tour after 2025 is expected in 2029, following the four-year cycle. The host nation for 2029 has not yet been officially confirmed. New Zealand and South Africa are the most likely destinations based on the rotation cycle.

How many Irish players are typically in the Lions squad?

The number of Irish players in a Lions squad varies based on form at the time of selection. Recent tours have included a significant Irish contingent — sometimes as many as 10 or more of the 36-player touring squad — reflecting Ireland's strong period of international rugby. Ireland players have captained recent tours.
