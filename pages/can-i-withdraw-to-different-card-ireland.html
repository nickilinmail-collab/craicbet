Can I Withdraw to a Different Card Than I Deposited in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

In most cases, Irish bookmakers require you to withdraw winnings to the same card or payment method you used to deposit. This is an Anti-Money Laundering (AML) requirement, not an arbitrary rule. However, there are legitimate solutions when you cannot withdraw to the original card.

Why Withdrawal Must Match Deposit Method

AML regulations require bookmakers to return funds to their source. If you deposited by Visa, the site must return funds to that Visa card before allowing withdrawal to a bank account or e-wallet. This prevents illicit funds from being laundered through betting accounts. GRAI-licensed operators are legally required to follow these procedures.

How to Request an Alternative Withdrawal

Contact customer support with: proof of identity (passport or driving licence), proof that the original payment method is no longer available (bank letter or screenshot), and your preferred alternative withdrawal method (bank account IBAN). Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers have a manual review process for genuine cases. Response times are typically 2–5 business days.

Can I withdraw to a different card than I deposited with in Ireland

Under GRAI anti-money laundering rules, Irish bookmakers must return funds to the same payment method used to deposit. This "source of funds" principle prevents money laundering by ensuring the transaction trail is clear. If you deposited by Visa debit, your withdrawal must return to that Visa card, not to a different card or bank account.

Exceptions are possible when the original card is no longer valid, but require documentation. The operator will typically require you to contact customer support, verify your identity, and provide proof of card ownership or cancellation. Withdrawals to bank transfer (SEPA) are usually available as an alternative once KYC is fully verified.

What if my deposit card has expired

If your original deposit card has expired, the card number typically remains the same while only the expiry date and CVV change. Many banks will process the withdrawal to your expired card and automatically credit your new replacement card. If your card was actually cancelled (account closed), contact the bookmaker's customer support team directly with proof of the cancellation and your new payment details.

Under GRAI licensing conditions, operators must handle these requests within a reasonable timeframe and cannot withhold funds indefinitely. If you encounter unreasonable delays, you can file a complaint with GRAI at grai.ie.

Can I withdraw to a bank account if I deposited by card?

Usually only after the full deposited amount has been withdrawn to the original card first. Once the original deposit amount is returned to the card, any remaining winnings can often be withdrawn via bank transfer. This varies by operator.
