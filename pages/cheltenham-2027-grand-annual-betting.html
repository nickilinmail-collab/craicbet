Cheltenham 2027 Grand Annual Chase — Handicap Chase Betting Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Grand Annual Chase is one of the most anticipated races of the Cheltenham Festival and a popular betting target for Irish punters from the moment autumn form unfolds. This guide covers the market, ante-post strategy and how to approach Grand Annual Chase betting in 2027.

About the Grand Annual Chase

Run on Gold Cup Day (Friday) of the Cheltenham Festival, the Grand Annual Chase is a 2 mile Grade 3 handicap chase race. The Grand Annual Chase is a 2 mile handicap chase run at the festival's highest pace. It regularly produces long-priced winners from the deep fields of 20+ runners. The race is run on the new course and demands a quick, accurate jumping style rather than raw stamina.

Main Betting Markets

  • Win market: Outright winner — the most popular market. Prices typically available from the autumn.
  • Each-way betting: 4 places at 1/4 odds for large-field races. Each-way value exists when field sizes reach 12 or more runners.
  • Ante-post: Pre-festival markets available months in advance. Best value typically before the Dublin Racing Festival in February.
  • To be placed: Some bookmakers offer 'each-way only' or place-only markets for punters who want lower-risk exposure to competitive races.

Ante-Post Strategy

Irish-trained horses are consistent Cheltenham performers across multiple races. Looking at the form guide in autumn — first-season novices and Grade 2 winners — often surfaces potential Cheltenham contenders before the market has fully priced them. The Dublin Racing Festival in February is the last major reference point before the spring ante-post market becomes fully focused on specific Cheltenham prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Cheltenham Festival 2027?

The Cheltenham Festival 2027 is expected to take place in the third week of March 2027, running Tuesday to Friday. Exact dates will be confirmed by the Jockey Club. The Gold Cup is traditionally run on the Friday.

Is ante-post betting available for individual Cheltenham races?

Yes — ante-post markets for most of the 28 Cheltenham Festival races are available from the autumn onwards. Some races have markets open from the previous season. Non-runner risk applies — if your selection is withdrawn, most ante-post bets are lost unless non-runner money-back is offered.

What are typical each-way terms at Cheltenham?

Standard each-way terms at Cheltenham are 4 places at 1/4 odds for championship races with 8 or more runners. Terms vary by race type and field size — always check with your bookmaker before placing.

Which Cheltenham races attract the most Irish betting?

Irish punters bet most heavily on races with strong Irish-trained runners: the Champion Hurdle, Cheltenham Gold Cup, Supreme Novices' Hurdle, Arkle Trophy and the novice hurdle races. St Patrick's Thursday is the peak day for Irish betting volume.

Grand Annual Betting Tips

  • Minimum trip chase — fast, athletic horses with speed rather than stamina excel
  • Large field (12–18 runners); low-weight horses often competitive
  • Nicky Henderson, Paul Nicholls and Irish yards all competitive in this race
  • Price range: 8/1–20/1 for most winners — favourites at 4/1 occasionally land it but not reliably
