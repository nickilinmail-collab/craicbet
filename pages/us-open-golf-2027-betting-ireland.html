US Open Golf 2027 Betting — Major Championship Guide for Irish Punters

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The US Open Golf Championship is one of the four major golf championships and a major betting event for Irish punters. Known for its notoriously difficult setup, the US Open rewards precision and consistency over power — which creates a distinctive betting profile compared to other majors.

US Open Format and Betting Context

The US Open is a 72-hole strokeplay championship played over four days in June. The USGA deliberately sets up the course to be the most demanding of all the majors — narrow fairways, thick rough and fast greens create a scoring environment where even the best players struggle to reach double digits under par. This setup tends to produce winners from a specific profile: consistent ball-strikers who avoid big numbers.

Main Betting Markets for the US Open

  • Outright winner: Back a player to win the 72-hole tournament. Prices range from 10/1 for the strongest favourites to 300/1 or longer for genuine outsiders.
  • Each-way betting: Most bookmakers offer 5 places at 1/5 odds for major championships. This is the most popular format for US Open betting — 5 places allows investment in longer-priced players who have realistic top-five prospects.
  • Top nationality: Which player from a specific country (e.g. top Irish player) finishes highest.
  • Top 10 / top 20 finish: Lower-odds markets for backing strong players to perform without backing them to win outright.

Irish Interest in the US Open

Irish golfers have strong US Open records. Historically significant victories and consistent Irish representation at the top of the leaderboard make this a natural betting target for Irish punters.

US Open Betting Strategy for Irish Punters

The US Open is set up harder than any other major — narrow fairways, thick rough, and fast greens. The USGA designs the course to produce high scoring and penalise wayward shots. Accurate drivers and iron players historically outperform long-distance bombers unless the course rewards length (Shinnecock, Pebble Beach).

  • Each-way value: with 156 players and places paid to top 8, each-way bets on 40/1–80/1 shots can offer genuine returns without needing a win
  • Irish contenders: Rory McIlroy and Shane Lowry are regularly near the top of the market; check their recent US Open form and iron play statistics before betting
  • Course form: look for players with strong records at tight, demanding US Open-style setups (past US Open courses or Muirfield, Oakmont equivalents)
  • Market margin warning: outright golf markets have a combined overround of 15–25% across the field; each-way offers better value than backing individual players at win-only
When is the US Open Golf 2027?

The US Open is traditionally held in June, over the weekend of Father's Day in the United States (third Sunday of June). The 2027 venue will be confirmed by the USGA. Past venues include Oakmont, Pebble Beach, Shinnecock Hills, Torrey Pines and The Country Club.

What each-way terms are available for the US Open?

Standard each-way terms for major golf championships are 5 places at 1/5 odds. Some bookmakers run enhanced each-way promotions (6 or 7 places) during major championship week. Always check terms before placing — enhanced terms can significantly improve returns on longer-priced selections.

Which Irish golfers have won the US Open?

Several Irish golfers have won the US Open across different eras. Rory McIlroy won at Congressional in 2011. Shane Lowry and other Irish players have competed at the top of recent US Opens. For current Irish prospects in 2027, assess world rankings and recent form closer to the event.

How is the US Open course setup different from other majors?

The USGA deliberately makes the US Open course the most difficult of the four majors through long rough, tight fairways, fast greens and challenging pin positions. Par is considered a good score. This setup rewards accuracy from the tee, iron play into greens and putting touch on fast surfaces — a different skillset from power-focused Augusta or links-focused Open Championship.
