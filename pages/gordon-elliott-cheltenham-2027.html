Gordon Elliott Cheltenham 2027: Horses and Tips

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Gordon Elliott is one of the dominant forces at the Cheltenham Festival. His yard in Longwood, Co. Meath typically sends 30–40 horses to the meeting, targeting a spread of novice and championship races. Here is what Irish punters need to know about Elliott's 2027 runners.

Gordon Elliott's Cheltenham Record

Elliott has trained multiple Cheltenham winners across every grade. His operation excels in novice hurdles and novice chases, where younger horses from his yard frequently dominate. He also sends competitive runners in the Champion Hurdle and Gold Cup divisions via top-level recruits.

Gordon Elliott Cheltenham 2027: What to Watch

Elliott's main stable jockey partnerships and his record of running horses in conditions they're suited to make his team among the safest to follow at ante-post stage. Key factors to monitor:

  • Festival entries published approximately 6 weeks before Cheltenham (late January 2027)
  • Elliott often runs horses in multiple entries — final declarations (5 days before each race) confirm runners
  • Watch for horses who won at the Dublin Racing Festival (early February) — these frequently go on to Cheltenham
  • Elliott's novice chasers from the previous season are often best value at 10/1+ ante-post

Ante-Post Betting on Gordon Elliott Horses

Taking ante-post prices on Elliott runners carries non-runner risk — if a horse is withdrawn before the race, your stake is lost unless the bookmaker offers NRNB (Non-Runner No Bet) terms. Always check whether the offer is NRNB before committing at ante-post prices.

Best strategy: wait for the Dublin Racing Festival (early February 2027) confirmation before placing ante-post bets on Elliott's key novices. The festival often clarifies which horses are on target and in what form.
