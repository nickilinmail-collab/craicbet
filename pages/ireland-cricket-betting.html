Last updated: 5 November 2026

Ireland cricket achieved Full Member status of the International Cricket Council (ICC) in 2017, granting them Test match status. The national team now competes at the highest level of the game, creating a new betting market for Irish sports fans.

Ireland Cricket in 2026

Ireland compete in Test matches, One Day Internationals (ODIs) and T20 Internationals (T20Is) throughout the international cricket calendar. Home fixtures are typically played at Malahide Cricket Club ground in Dublin or the Civil Service Cricket Club ground. Away tours take the team to major cricket-playing nations.

Ireland are still developing as a Test nation and are not yet favourites in most matchups against established cricket powers. This creates asymmetric odds where betting on Ireland at longer prices can represent value when they are playing below the standard favourites.

Betting on Ireland Cricket: What to Know

Ireland is a Full Member of the ICC, playing Test cricket since 2018 and regularly competing in ODI and T20 World Cups. While Ireland are rarely favourites in major tournaments, they are capable of causing upsets against top-ranked nations — most famously defeating Pakistan at the 2007 ODI World Cup and England in 2011.

  • Market availability: Ireland matches in ICC events get full coverage on GRAI-licensed sites; bilateral home series have more limited markets
  • Top players to watch: Paul Stirling (top-order batter), Lorcan Tucker (wicket-keeper), and Barry McCarthy (pace bowling) are key market factors
  • In-play betting: cricket is excellent for in-play — odds shift dramatically ball-by-ball; watch for Ireland batting collapses when they are favourites
Is cricket popular in Ireland?

Cricket has a niche but loyal following in Ireland. Following Test status in 2017, media coverage has grown significantly and betting interest has increased alongside it.

Can I bet in-play on Ireland cricket matches?

Yes. Cricket is one of the best sports for in-play betting due to its long format and changing dynamics. GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry extensive in-play markets for Ireland Test and ODI fixtures.

Where does Ireland play home cricket?

Ireland's main home ground is Malahide Cricket Club in north County Dublin. Some matches are also played at the Civil Service Cricket Club ground in Stormont, Belfast (Northern Ireland).

Are there markets specifically for Irish cricket?

Yes. Ireland-specific markets include top Ireland batsman, top Ireland bowler and Ireland innings total at major bookmakers.
