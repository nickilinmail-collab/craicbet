What is a Goliath bet?

A Goliath is the largest standard named full-cover combination bet, covering 8 selections across 247 bets — from doubles all the way up to the eight-fold accumulator. It includes no singles, so at least 2 winners are needed for any return. Despite the high outlay, many punters place Goliaths at small unit stakes (20p or less) during major racing festivals such as Cheltenham or the Galway Races to cover a wide range of selections while keeping the total cost manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Goliath bet?

A Goliath covers 8 selections across 247 bets (28 doubles through to 1 eight-fold). No singles. At least 2 winners needed for any return. It is the largest standard named bet.

How many bets in a Goliath?

247 bets: 28 doubles + 56 trebles + 70 four-folds + 56 five-folds + 28 six-folds + 8 seven-folds + 1 eight-fold.

How much does a Goliath cost?

A €1 Goliath costs €247. A 20c Goliath costs €49.40.

How many winners do you need in a Goliath?

At least 2 winners — since the smallest bet type is a double. More winners dramatically increase returns.

What is the difference between a Super Heinz and a Goliath?

A Super Heinz covers 7 selections in 120 bets; a Goliath adds an 8th, bringing the total to 247 bets — more than double the outlay for the same unit stake.
