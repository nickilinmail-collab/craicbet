What is a Goliath bet?

A Goliath is a full-cover multiple bet using 8 selections and 247 bets. It covers every possible combination of 2 or more selections from your 8 picks: 28 doubles, 56 trebles, 70 four-folds, 56 five-folds, 28 six-folds, 8 seven-folds and 1 eight-fold. No singles are included. At a €1 unit stake, the total outlay is €247 — making the Goliath the largest widely accepted standard named bet in betting. The payoff for getting 7 or 8 winners can be enormous, but even getting 5 or 6 of 8 right can produce a healthy profit from the fold combinations.

When to use a Goliath

A Goliath is for the rare occasion when you feel genuinely confident across a large programme of selections — think a full day of racing at Cheltenham or a full programme of Champions League matches — and you want complete mathematical coverage of every possible combination of winners without having to pick a reduced set. The 247-bet cost is a barrier at anything above a micro-stake, but the full-cover structure means that even if 2 or 3 selections let you down, the winning combinations from the rest still pay out. For most punters, the Heinz (57 bets, 6 selections) or Super Heinz (120 bets, 7 selections) offer similar coverage at a lower cost. Goliath example

Imagine eight race selections at a festival, each at modest odds. With a €0.10 unit stake (total outlay: €24.70), if 5 of the 8 win, the calculator collects on C(5,2)=10 doubles, C(5,3)=10 trebles, C(5,4)=5 four-folds and C(5,5)=1 five-fold — 26 winning bets in total. The return from those 26 bets can easily cover the outlay and produce a profit even if none of the accumulators above 5-fold come in. That is the appeal of the Goliath: depth of coverage. At €1 per bet the numbers scale 10×, so plug your own odds into the calculator above.

Frequently asked questions

How many bets in a Goliath?

247 bets: 28 doubles, 56 trebles, 70 four-folds, 56 five-folds, 28 six-folds, 8 seven-folds and 1 eight-fold. No singles.

How much does a €1 Goliath cost?

€247 total — 247 bets at a €1 unit stake. A €0.50 unit stake costs €123.50; a €0.10 unit stake costs €24.70.

How many winners do you need for a Goliath return?

At least 2 — to land one of the 28 doubles. With fewer than 2 winners, the entire stake is lost.

Is a Goliath the biggest named bet?

Yes — the Goliath (8 selections, 247 bets) is the largest standard named full-cover bet that most bookmakers accept. Some offer non-standard larger bets but they are not widely available.

What is the difference between a Goliath and a Super Heinz?

A Super Heinz uses 7 selections (120 bets); a Goliath uses 8 selections (247 bets). The Goliath adds 7 seven-folds and 1 eight-fold plus 127 additional combinations across the smaller folds.
