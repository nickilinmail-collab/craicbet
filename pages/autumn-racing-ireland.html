Autumn Racing Ireland 2026: Champions Weekend & October Festivals

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Irish autumn racing centres on the Irish Champions Weekend at the Curragh and Leopardstown in September, followed by a series of National Hunt meetings that build through October and November towards the Christmas festival season.

Irish Champions Weekend

Irish Champions Weekend takes place over two days in September, split between Leopardstown Racecourse (Saturday) and the Curragh (Sunday). The weekend features the richest prize money in Irish racing, with top flat horses competing in the Irish Champion Stakes, the Matron Stakes, the National Stakes and other Group 1 races. The weekend is Ireland’s equivalent of France’s Prix de l’Arc de Triomphe day and attracts international competition.

See our dedicated Irish Champions Weekend betting guide for full detail.

National Hunt Return

October marks the return of National Hunt racing in earnest, with novice hurdlers and chasers beginning their campaigns. Tracks including Tipperary, Wexford, Sligo and Roscommon host autumn meetings. The National Hunt ante-post market for Cheltenham begins to form in October as trainers map out campaigns.

Key October/November Races

  • Leopardstown October meeting (Grade 2 and 3 hurdles and chases)
  • Down Royal Festival of Racing (Grade 1 chases in late October/November)
  • Punchestown November meeting
  • Navan National Hunt opening festival

Autumn Ante-Post Strategy for Irish Racing

Autumn marks the end of the flat season and the ramp-up of National Hunt racing. Leopardstown's late October/November fixtures are key trials for Cheltenham — horses that run well here often shorten in the Cheltenham outrights over winter.

  • Champion Stakes day at Leopardstown (Sep): top flat horses; watch for Breeders' Cup-bound runners who may be half-ready
  • November NH trials: Graded hurdle and chase form here directly predictor of Cheltenham festival prices
  • Going matters: Irish tracks tend to be softer than UK in autumn — check the going before backing flat-track speedsters
When is Irish Champions Weekend?

Irish Champions Weekend takes place in September, split between Leopardstown on Saturday and the Curragh on Sunday. Exact dates vary by year.

What races are on at Irish Champions Weekend?

The Irish Champion Stakes, Matron Stakes, National Stakes and other Group 1 flat races are the flagship events. The weekend is the climax of the flat turf season in Ireland.

When does National Hunt racing return in autumn?

National Hunt racing in Ireland typically returns in earnest in October after the summer flat season concludes. Cheltenham Festival ante-post markets begin forming from October.

Can I bet ante-post on Cheltenham from October?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers open Cheltenham Festival ante-post markets from autumn, often from October or November. Ante-post bets carry the risk of non-runner void if your horse does not run.
