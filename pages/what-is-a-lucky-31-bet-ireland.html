What Is a Lucky 31 Bet in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A Lucky 31 is a full-cover bet on 5 selections consisting of 31 bets: 5 singles, 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-folds, and 1 five-fold accumulator. The key advantage over a straight five-fold accumulator is that you get a return even if only one selection wins.

Lucky 31 Bonus Rules

Many bookmakers apply consolation bonuses to Lucky 31 bets: if only 1 selection wins, the single bet return is often doubled. If all 5 win, the five-fold return may be enhanced by 25% or more. Bonus percentages vary by bookmaker — check terms before placing.

Lucky 31 vs Lucky 15 vs Heinz

Lucky 15 covers 4 selections in 15 bets (cost at €1/line = €15). Lucky 31 covers 5 selections in 31 bets (cost at €1/line = €31). Heinz covers 6 selections in 57 bets but has no singles. The Lucky 31 sits between these — the singles make one winner sufficient for a return, while five combinations mean bigger profits when several win. At €0.50/line, a Lucky 31 costs €15.50 — comparable to a Lucky 15 at €1/line.
