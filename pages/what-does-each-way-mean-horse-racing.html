What Does Each-Way Mean in Horse Racing? A Complete Beginner's Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

An each-way bet is two bets in one: a win bet and a place bet. If your horse wins, both bets pay. If it places (finishes in the top 2, 3, or 4 depending on the race), you receive the place portion. If it neither wins nor places, both parts of your bet lose. Here is a plain-English explanation with worked examples.

How Each-Way Betting Works

When you place an each-way bet, your stake is doubled — half goes on the win, half on the place. Example: a €10 each-way bet actually costs €20 (€10 win + €10 place).

Place terms are set by the bookmaker before each race and determine: (1) how many places pay, and (2) the fraction of the win odds paid for a placed horse.

Worked Example

€10 each-way on a horse at 10/1 in a 10-runner race (1/4 odds, 3 places). Total stake: €20.

Horse wins: Win part: €10 × 10/1 = €100 profit + €10 stake = €110. Place part: €10 × (10/1 × 1/4) = €10 × 2.5/1 = €25 profit + €10 stake = €35. Total return: €145 (€125 profit on a €20 stake).

Horse places (2nd or 3rd but not 1st): Win part: loses (€10 gone). Place part: €25 profit + €10 stake = €35. Total return: €35 on a €20 stake (€15 profit).

Horse unplaced: Both parts lose. Total loss: €20.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does each-way mean in betting?

An each-way bet is two bets — a win bet and a place bet. If the horse wins, both pay. If it places (but doesn't win), only the place part pays. If it's unplaced, both bets lose.

How much does an each-way bet cost?

Double your stated stake. A €10 each-way bet costs €20 — €10 on the win and €10 on the place.

What are place terms in each-way betting?

Place terms define how many places pay and at what fraction of the win odds. Common: top 3 at 1/4 odds for 8+ runner races. Bookmakers set terms before each race.

Is each-way betting good value?

It depends on the race type and odds. Each-way value is best for outsiders in big-field handicaps (16+ runners, 4 places). For short-priced favourites in small fields, an each-way bet is poor value because the place fraction adds little.

What happens if a race is void after I've placed an each-way bet?

If the race is void, both the win and place parts of your each-way bet are voided and stakes returned. If your selected horse is a non-runner, the same applies — stakes returned.
