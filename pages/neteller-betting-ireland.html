Neteller is one of the most widely supported e-wallets at betting sites available to Irish players. How Neteller Works for Betting in Ireland

Neteller is a separate digital wallet regulated by the FCA (UK). To use it for betting:

  1. Create a Neteller account at neteller.com and verify your identity.
  2. Fund your Neteller wallet via bank transfer or debit card.
  3. At your betting site's cashier, select Neteller and enter your account email and Secure ID.
  4. Deposits are instant from your Neteller balance.
  5. Withdrawals return to your Neteller wallet (usually within hours).

The Net+ Prepaid Mastercard

Neteller's Net+ prepaid Mastercard is a significant differentiator from other e-wallets. Once you receive the card, your Neteller wallet balance is accessible at any Mastercard-accepting merchant and ATM. This means your betting winnings are accessible as cash or for purchases without needing to wait for a bank transfer. ATM withdrawals carry a small fee (typically €1.75 per transaction).

Bonus Eligibility — The Key Restriction

The most important thing to know: many bookmakers exclude Neteller deposits from welcome offer eligibility. This applies to both new customer and reload bonuses at a significant proportion of operators. The practical advice: use a debit card for your first deposit to claim any welcome bonus, then switch to Neteller for the privacy and fast withdrawal benefits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Neteller work at betting sites in Ireland?

Yes. Neteller is widely accepted at betting sites serving Irish players. It offers instant deposits and fast withdrawals. Note that many bookmakers exclude Neteller deposits from welcome offer eligibility.

Are Neteller deposits excluded from welcome betting bonuses?

At many bookmakers, yes. Use a debit card for your first deposit to claim a welcome bonus, then switch to Neteller for ongoing play. Always check the specific bonus terms and conditions.

What is the Net+ Mastercard and can I use it to access betting winnings?

Net+ is a prepaid Mastercard linked to your Neteller wallet. You can use it at ATMs and merchants to access funds directly — no bank transfer needed. There is a small ATM fee (typically €1.75 per transaction).

Is Neteller affected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban?

No. Neteller is an e-money wallet, not a credit product, and is unaffected by the ban. If you fund your Neteller account using a credit card, that is subject to Neteller's own policies.

Is Neteller Available for Betting in Ireland

Neteller is available as a deposit method at many international betting sites, but Irish players should be aware that some sites exclude Neteller deposits from welcome bonus eligibility — the same restriction that applies to Skrill. For withdrawals, Neteller is typically fast (within 24 hours) once your account is verified. The minimum deposit is usually €10 and withdrawals €5–€10 depending on the site.

Neteller Deposit Bonus Exclusion Ireland: How to Work Around It

Most sites that exclude Neteller from bonus eligibility do so because e-wallets allow easy chargebacks and account switching. The workaround is straightforward: make your first bonus-qualifying deposit via debit card, then switch to Neteller for future deposits once the bonus is active. Alternatively, check the site's T&Cs — some sites distinguish between Neteller-funded bonuses and Neteller as a withdrawal method, allowing you to deposit by card and withdraw to Neteller.
