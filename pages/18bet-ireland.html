What Is 18Bet?

18Bet is an online betting and casino platform operating under a Curacao Gaming Authority licence. It offers sports betting across football, tennis, basketball, ice hockey, and other markets, as well as a casino product. 18Bet is primarily active in CIS and Eastern European markets.

Curacao-licensed operators operate under a relatively permissive regulatory regime compared to MGA or UKGC licensees. The Curacao licence requires basic operational standards but does not provide the same depth of consumer protection requirements as EU or UK regulators.

18Bet’s GRAI Licence Status in Ireland

As of November 2026, 18Bet does not appear on the GRAI public register. Without a GRAI licence, 18Bet is not authorised to operate under the Irish regulatory framework. Irish players using 18Bet would not receive GRAI-mandated protections, including NGEI self-exclusion, mandatory deposit limits, or a formal Irish complaints route. The Curacao licence additionally provides fewer consumer protections than the MGA or GRAI frameworks.
