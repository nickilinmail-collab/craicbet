Newest Online Casinos Ireland: Licensing Under the GRAI 2026 Framework

Ireland's gambling regulation changed fundamentally in 2026. Casino (gaming) licences have not yet been issued — they are expected in 2027–2028. As of November 2026, zero GRAI casino licences exist.
