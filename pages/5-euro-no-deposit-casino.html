The Short Version

Cash no-deposit bonuses of €5 do exist, but they are rare, small-print-heavy, and often fake as advertised. The common real form of a no-deposit offer in Ireland is free spins on registration (10–50 spins), not cash. Anything promising "free €5, withdraw anytime" is not how this industry works.

How a Real €5 No-Deposit Offer Works

You register, verify your email or phone, and receive €5 in bonus funds or equivalent spins. Then the terms kick in: wagering of 40x–60x (so €200–€300 in stakes from a €5 start), a win cap of typically €50–€100, a short expiry (often 24–72 hours), and withdrawal only after identity verification. It is a free test drive of a casino — genuinely worth taking — but it is not free money.

Spotting the Fakes

Red flags, in order of reliability: the offer appears only on an affiliate page and nowhere on the casino's own promotions page; the "claim" button leads to a deposit screen; the terms are unfindable; the casino has no verifiable licence (run the 2-minute check). A fake no-deposit offer is a recruitment trick, and casinos that recruit with tricks pay out with tricks.
