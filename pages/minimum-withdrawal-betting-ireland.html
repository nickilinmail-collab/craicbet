Minimum Withdrawal Amounts on Irish Betting Sites

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Every GRAI-licensed bookmaker sets minimum withdrawal thresholds. For most Irish punters using Revolut or debit cards, the minimum is €10–€20. Here is what to expect across each payment method in 2026.

Typical Minimum Withdrawals by Method

  • Debit card (Visa/Mastercard): Usually €10–€20
  • Revolut: Treated as a prepaid debit card; typically €10
  • Bank transfer: Often €20–€50 due to processing costs
  • E-wallets (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller): Usually €10–€20

These figures are typical across GRAI-licensed operators in November 2026, but individual sites set their own minimums. Always check the cashier or payments page of your specific operator.

Why Do Minimum Withdrawals Exist?

Processing a payment costs money — card networks and bank wires both charge per-transaction fees. Setting a minimum withdrawal threshold ensures the operator is not paying processing fees on €0.50 withdrawals. For punters, the practical implication is that if your balance falls below the minimum, you may need to bet it up before you can cash out.

Revolut Withdrawal Minimums in Detail

Revolut is classified as an e-money or prepaid debit card under Irish financial regulations. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers treat it identically to a standard Visa or Mastercard debit card for withdrawal purposes. The minimum is typically €10, and processing takes 1–2 business days. Revolut itself does not charge a fee for incoming transfers.

Can You Withdraw Less Than the Minimum?

No — withdrawals below the stated minimum will be rejected by the system. If your balance is below the threshold (say €7 when the minimum is €10), your options are to top it up, place a small bet to try to reach the threshold, or leave it in your account. Contact support if you are trying to close your account and withdraw a small residual balance — operators often make an exception for account closure.

What Delays a Withdrawal?

  • Pending winnings review: Large wins (often over €5,000) trigger a manual review. This is standard for licensed operators and is disclosed in terms.
  • KYC documents not submitted: If you have never submitted ID and proof of address, the withdrawal will be held until you do. Submitting proactively after sign-up avoids this.
  • Wagering requirements: If a bonus is active and wagering is not complete, funds are locked. Check your bonus status before requesting withdrawal.
  • Return-to-source: You must withdraw to the same method you deposited with, up to the amount deposited. Excess to card or bank.

How Much Is the Minimum Bet in Ireland

The minimum stake at most Irish online betting sites is €0.10–€0.50 for singles. Accumulator minimum stakes are typically the same. For some markets (Asian handicaps, exotic horse racing bets), minimums may be higher at €1–€2. Phone betting and in-shop betting minimums vary by operator. There is no legal minimum — it is set entirely by the operator.
