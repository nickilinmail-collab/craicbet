Licensing and Legal Status for Irish Punters

As of November 2026, neither Marathonbet nor Betsson holds a GRAI remote betting licence. Both operate under international licences (Marathonbet under a Gibraltar licence; Betsson under Malta MGA). Irish residents using unlicensed operators have no GRAI complaint route, no statutory payout protection under Irish law, and no access to the GRAI’s player fund scheme. GRAI-licensed operators must pay out as a licence condition; unlicensed operators do not.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Marathonbet licensed in Ireland?

Marathonbet does not currently hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. It operates under a Gibraltar licence. Irish residents using it have no GRAI complaint route.

Is Betsson available in Ireland?

Betsson operates under a Malta MGA licence and does not currently hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. MGA-licensed operators offer some player protections but not under Irish law.

What is the difference between a GRAI licence and an MGA licence?

A GRAI licence is issued by the Irish regulator and gives Irish players statutory protections under Irish law, including the GRAI’s formal complaints route. An MGA licence is Maltese and gives no Irish legal protections.

Can I bet with Marathonbet in Ireland legally?

It is not illegal for an individual Irish resident to place a bet with an unlicensed operator. However, the operator is committing an offence by accepting Irish bets without a GRAI licence, and you have no Irish legal protections.

Which is better for football betting: Marathonbet or Betsson?

Marathonbet typically offers tighter margins on football, particularly Eastern European leagues. Betsson has broader sports coverage. Neither offers Irish GRAI protections — a GRAI-licensed operator at 4–5% football margin provides both competitive odds and legal protection.
