💡 Quick Guide to Free Spins

Not all free spins are equal. The value depends on three things: the spin value (how much each spin is worth), the wagering requirement on winnings, and which slots they’re valid on. 50 free spins at €0.10 each with no wagering are far more valuable than 200 free spins at €0.01 with 50x wagering.

Types of Free Spins

Given as part of a welcome bonus when you make your first deposit. Usually 50–200 spins paired with a deposit match. Most common type.

🎁 No Deposit Free Spins

The holy grail. Winnings from these spins are credited as real cash you can withdraw immediately. Rare but incredibly valuable.

📅 Daily Free Spins

Ongoing promotions where you receive free spins every day or on specific days. Usually for existing players who deposit regularly.

🔄 Reload Free Spins

Bonus spins offered on your second, third, or subsequent deposits. Often part of a multi-deposit welcome package.

How Free Spins Work

🎲 Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Claim the offer — Register or deposit to activate your free spins bonus
  2. Open the eligible slot — Free spins are usually locked to specific games
  3. Spin at the set value — Each spin has a fixed bet amount (typically €0.10–€0.20)
  4. Winnings credited as bonus — Unless it’s a no-wagering offer, winnings go to your bonus balance
  5. Complete wagering — Bet through the wagering requirement on eligible games
  6. Withdraw — Once wagering is complete, bonus funds convert to real cash

FAQ — Free Spins Ireland

Can I really win money from free spins?▾
Yes, but it depends on the terms. No-wagering free spins give you real cash winnings. Standard free spins with wagering requirements mean you need to bet through your winnings before withdrawal. The lower the wagering, the more realistic your chance of cashing out.
What are no wagering free spins?▾
No wagering free spins (also called “wager-free spins”) credit your winnings as real cash instead of bonus funds. You can withdraw immediately without any playthrough requirements. These are the most valuable type of free spins.
How much are free spins worth?▾
Each free spin has a fixed bet value, typically €0.10 per spin. So 100 free spins at €0.10 each = €10 total value in bets. Your actual winnings depend on what the slot pays out during those spins. Average expected returns are around 95–97% of the bet value (the RTP).
Can I choose which slot to use free spins on?▾
Usually no. Most free spins offers specify which slot(s) they can be used on. Some casinos offer “any game” free spins, but these are rare. The assigned slots are typically popular titles like Starburst, Book of Dead, or Gates of Olympus.
Do free spins expire?▾
Yes. Most free spins must be used within 24 hours to 7 days of being credited. Unused spins are forfeited. Additionally, any winnings from free spins usually have a separate wagering deadline (typically 7–30 days). Always check both expiry dates.
