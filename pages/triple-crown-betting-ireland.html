Last updated: 5 November 2026

The Triple Crown is awarded to the Home Nation (England, Ireland, Scotland or Wales) that beats all three of the other Home Nations in a single Six Nations Championship. For Irish punters, the Triple Crown is a separate betting market alongside the outright Six Nations Championship winner.

What is the Triple Crown?

The Triple Crown requires Ireland (or any Home Nation) to beat England, Scotland and Wales in the same Six Nations. It is a subset of the Grand Slam — a Grand Slam means beating all five opponents; the Triple Crown requires beating only the other three Home Nations. Ireland can win the Triple Crown without winning the Championship if they lose to France or Italy.

Ireland have won the Triple Crown on multiple occasions, most recently in years when they have been strong championship contenders.

Betting on the Triple Crown

Dedicated Triple Crown betting markets are available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers from the opening of Six Nations markets in autumn. Prices update after each round of fixtures based on how the Home Nations perform. Ireland winning the Triple Crown typically prices up at longer odds than winning the Grand Slam if France are strong favourites for the overall Championship.

Six Nations Triple Crown: What It Is

The Triple Crown is an unofficial prize awarded to the Home Nation (England, Ireland, Scotland, or Wales) that beats all three other Home Nations in the Six Nations Championship. It is not a trophy given by the Six Nations organisation — it is a historic concept tracked by fans and media.

How many times have Ireland won the Triple Crown?

Ireland have won the Triple Crown on multiple occasions in the Six Nations era. Check the latest IRFU and Six Nations website for the full record.

Is the Triple Crown the same as the Grand Slam?

No. The Grand Slam requires a team to beat all five opponents. The Triple Crown only requires beating the other three Home Nations (England, Scotland and Wales for Ireland).

Where can I bet on the Six Nations Triple Crown?

Dedicated Triple Crown betting markets are available at all major GRAI-licensed bookmakers from the autumn before the tournament.

Can Ireland win the Triple Crown without winning the Championship?

Yes. If Ireland beat England, Scotland and Wales but lose to France or Italy, they can still win the Triple Crown without winning the overall Championship.
