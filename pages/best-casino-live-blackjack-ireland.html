Side Bets in Live Blackjack

Perfect Pairs and 21+3 are common side bets. Perfect Pairs pays if your first two cards form a pair (3:1 to 25:1 depending on colour/suit). 21+3 uses your two cards and the dealer upcard to form poker hands. Both have house edges significantly higher than the base game (3–10%) — they're entertainment bets.

Basic Strategy Still Applies

Basic strategy (the mathematically optimal decision for every hand) works in live blackjack exactly as in RNG blackjack. Using it reduces the house edge to approximately 0.5%. Strategy cards are permitted at online live tables. Learning basic strategy for the specific rules used (number of decks, hit/stand on soft 17) is worth doing for regular players.

Live Blackjack Strategy for Irish Casino Players

Standard Classic Blackjack with basic strategy gives one of the lowest house edges in a live casino — around 0.4–0.5%. Basic strategy means always following the mathematically optimal play (hit, stand, double, split) based on your hand and the dealer's upcard. Deviating from basic strategy is the single biggest mistake live blackjack players make.

  • Always split: Aces and 8s are always split; never split 10s or 5s
  • Always double: hard 10 or 11 when dealer shows 2–9; hard 9 when dealer shows 3–6
  • Never take insurance: insurance pays 2:1 but the true odds are roughly 2.17:1 against — it's a losing bet in the long run
  • Avoid Lightning Blackjack: multipliers look attractive but the mandatory fee raises the house edge from 0.5% to around 1.5%
