Player Props Betting: Individual Player Markets Explained

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

What are player props?

Player props (player proposition bets) are wagers on individual player performance in a match, independent of the team result. Common football player props include: anytime goalscorer, player to have a shot on target, player to be carded, player to make an assist, number of passes completed and number of tackles won. American sports (NFL, NBA, MLB) have the widest range of player props; European football markets have expanded significantly in recent years.

Football player props in depth

Anytime goalscorer is the most popular football player prop, priced at the probability of a player scoring at least one goal during the match. Strikers in form are priced around 1.70-2.50; creative midfielders who score regularly can be 3.00-5.00. Shot on target props are increasingly available on major games. Assist markets work similarly but carry slightly wider margins, as assists are harder to price accurately given their dependence on teammates’ finishing.

NBA and American sports player props

The NBA offers the widest and most liquid player props market in sports: points, rebounds, assists, three-pointers made and fantasy score combinations are all standard. NFL props cover receiving yards, touchdown scorer, longest completion and many others. These markets are well-developed and priced by sophisticated models, with the same margin challenges as other markets.

Combining player props with match markets

Player prop combinations are popular in-play and pre-match, particularly scorecast (first scorer and correct score combined) and wincast (anytime scorer and match winner). These combination markets carry compound margins from both individual markets. Frequently Asked Questions

What player props are available in football betting?

Anytime goalscorer, first goalscorer, shot on target, player to be carded, player to make an assist, number of passes completed and player performance score markets are the most common football player props available from GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

Are player props available on GRAI-licensed sites?

Yes. Major GRAI-licensed operators offer player props on Premier League, Champions League and other top football competitions. The range varies by operator, with some offering only goalscorer markets and others providing comprehensive props including shots, assists and cards.

What sports have the widest player props markets?

The NBA and NFL have the most liquid player props markets globally. Football (soccer) has expanded significantly in recent years. Golf and tennis also offer player-level markets (finishing position, sets won) that function similarly to player props in other sports.

Key Rules for Player Prop Bets

  • Void if player doesn't start: most operators void player props if the named player doesn't take the field
  • Void if match abandoned: all player props are void if match is abandoned before completion
  • Injury updates: watch teamsheet releases — Irish bookmakers typically allow bets up to kick-off on props
  • Overtime inclusion: NBA props typically include overtime; check the specific market rules
