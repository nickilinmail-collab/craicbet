How Wagering Requirements Work — Calculator

🧮 Wagering Requirement Calculator

Use this formula to work out exactly how much you need to bet:

Total Wager = Bonus Amount × Wagering Multiplier

Example 1: €100 bonus × 10x wagering = €1,000 total bets needed

Example 2: €100 bonus × 35x wagering = €3,500 total bets needed

Example 3: €100 bonus × 50x wagering = €5,000 total bets needed

Some casinos apply wagering to bonus only (better for you), while others apply it to bonus + deposit (much harder). Always check which type applies:

Bonus-only wagering: €100 bonus × 30x = €3,000

Bonus + Deposit wagering: (€100 bonus + €100 deposit) × 30x = €6,000

Key takeaway: Bonus-only wagering at 30x is equivalent to bonus + deposit wagering at 15x. Always read the fine print.

FAQ — Low Wagering Casinos

What is a good wagering requirement?▾
Anything under 30x is considered good. Under 20x is excellent. The industry average is 35x–40x. Wagering requirements above 50x make it extremely difficult to ever withdraw bonus winnings, and we generally don’t recommend those bonuses.
Can I withdraw before completing wagering?▾
Usually no. Most casinos require you to complete all wagering before any withdrawal. Some casinos forfeit the bonus and associated winnings if you attempt to withdraw early, while others simply cancel the bonus and let you keep your deposited funds.
Is bonus-only or bonus+deposit wagering better?▾
Bonus-only wagering is always better for players. With bonus-only 30x on a €100 bonus, you wager €3,000 total. With bonus+deposit 30x, you wager €6,000. That’s double the amount for the same bonus.
Do free spins have wagering requirements?▾
Yes, in most cases. Free spins winnings are usually credited as bonus funds with their own wagering requirements. Some casinos offer “no wagering free spins” where winnings are credited as cash. See our free spins guide →
How long do I have to complete wagering?▾
Most casinos give you 7–30 days to complete wagering requirements. If you don’t finish in time, the bonus and any associated winnings are forfeited. Always check the time limit before accepting a bonus.
