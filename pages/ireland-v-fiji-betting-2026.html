Ireland v Fiji: Kick-Off Time, TV Channel & Betting Markets

Team News (12 November)

Ireland are without several front-liners. Dan Sheehan is ruled out under the 12-day concussion protocol after the Argentina game (he is hoped to return for South Africa on 21 November), Sean Jansen is out of the rest of the Nations Championship with a knee injury, and Caelan Doris, Jack Conan and Garry Ringrose were already unavailable. Rob Baloucoune and Josh van der Flier carried knocks early in the week but were expected to be fit, and Bundee Aki returns after illness. Andy Farrell names his team on Thursday afternoon — with the back row stretched, selection there is the one to watch before betting any player markets. Sources: Rugby365 (9 Nov), Balls.ie (11 Nov).

Head-to-Head

Ireland have won the last three meetings with Fiji, most recently 35–17 in November 2022 in Dublin, and before that 23–20 in 2017 — a reminder that Fiji have pushed Ireland close at the Aviva within the last decade. Fiji arrive as the strongest Pacific nation in the new championship and traditionally produce more offloads and line breaks per carry than any tier-one defence enjoys facing.

The Betting Markets That Matter
