Ryder Cup 2027 Betting — Bethpage Black Guide for Irish Punters

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Ryder Cup 2027 returns to the United States, to be played at Bethpage Black in New York. It is one of the most anticipated events in golf and generates significant betting interest from Irish punters given the strong Irish representation in the European team.

Ryder Cup Format

The Ryder Cup is a team match play competition between Europe and the United States, played over three days. Friday and Saturday feature foursomes and fourball sessions (morning and afternoon) with 8 points available each day. Sunday features 12 singles matches worth 1 point each. The first team to 14.5 points wins; Europe holds the cup if the match ties 14-14 (the defending champions retain).

Main Betting Markets

  • Outright winner (Europe vs USA): The headline market. Bethpage Black is a notoriously tough course that has historically suited low-scoring precision golf.
  • Session betting: Which team wins each session of foursomes or fourballs — popular daily markets.
  • Individual match betting: Head-to-head results for each of the 28 matches across the three days.
  • Winning margin: By how many points will the winning team prevail — typically available as a range (1-2.5, 3-4.5, 5+ etc).
  • Top points scorer: Which player accumulates the most Ryder Cup points across all sessions.

Irish Interest in the Ryder Cup

Irish golfers have been central to recent European Ryder Cup teams, with multiple Irish players typically in the 12-man squad. This creates strong Irish betting interest in both the outright market and individual player-focused props. Home advantage for the USA at Bethpage — a New York-area course with a partisan crowd — is a real factor in the outright market.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Ryder Cup 2027?

The Ryder Cup 2027 is scheduled at Bethpage Black in New York. The exact dates will be confirmed by the PGA of America and Ryder Cup Europe. Ryder Cups are typically held in late September, running Thursday to Sunday.

Where is Bethpage Black?

Bethpage Black is a public golf course in Farmingdale, New York, on Long Island. It has previously hosted the US Open and the PGA Championship and is known for its challenging layout and large, boisterous New York gallery.

How many Irish players are typically in the European Ryder Cup team?

The number of Irish players in the European team varies year to year based on world rankings and form. Recent European teams have typically included two to four Irish players. The European captain selects six automatic qualifiers and six captain's picks.

Can I bet on individual Ryder Cup matches?

Yes — bookmakers offer individual head-to-head match markets for all foursomes, fourballs and singles matches. These markets open once pairings are announced on match mornings, with singles matches available once the final draw is made on Sunday morning.
