1xBet Ireland Review 2026: What You Need to Know Before Betting

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

1xBet is one of the world's largest sports betting platforms, operating in over 140 countries and now with a visible Irish presence following a sponsorship deal with Gowran Park racecourse signed in July 2026. For Irish punters who have searched for 1xBet, this review covers the platform's offering, its licence status under Ireland's new GRAI regime, and the honest trade-offs.

Licence Status for Irish Players (November 2026)

From 1 July 2026, operators serving Irish residents require a remote betting licence from the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI). As of November 2026, 1xBet does not appear on the GRAI public register of licensed operators. Despite the Gowran Park racecourse sponsorship announced in July 2026, marketing activity in Ireland does not substitute for regulatory authorisation. 1xBet holds a Curaçao licence (eGaming Curaçao) and operates in many markets, but does not currently hold a GRAI licence. It is classified as an offshore betting site for Irish purposes — accessible, but outside the GRAI framework. Odds Quality

1xBet is often cited in odds comparison tools for competitive pricing, particularly on football. Overrounds on Premier League 1X2 markets frequently fall below 5%, and the site runs regular enhanced-odds promotions. This comes at a trade-off: the breadth of markets means some niche events carry higher margins.

Deposits and Withdrawals

Irish players can use Visa/Mastercard debit (credit cards banned for gambling since February 2026), Neteller, Skrill, Paysafecard, bank transfer and a wide range of cryptocurrencies. Withdrawals are processed promptly on e-wallets; standard bank transfer takes 3–5 business days. No withdrawal fees are charged by 1xBet itself.

Gowran Park Sponsorship (July 2026)

In July 2026, 1xBet signed a sponsorship deal with Gowran Park racecourse in Co. Kilkenny, giving the brand visibility at one of Ireland's historic racing venues. Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1xBet legal in Ireland?

1xBet can be accessed by Irish residents but does not hold a GRAI remote betting licence as of November 2026. It operates outside the GRAI framework — meaning GRAI player protections, dispute processes and fund segregation requirements do not apply.

Does the Gowran Park sponsorship mean 1xBet is licensed in Ireland?

No. 1xBet signed a sponsorship deal with Gowran Park in July 2026, but this is a commercial arrangement. Holding a GRAI remote betting licence is a separate legal requirement that 1xBet does not currently meet.

Does 1xBet have a GRAI licence?

No. As of November 2026, 1xBet does not appear on the GRAI public register of licensed remote betting operators. The operator holds a Curaçao licence for global operations.

What sports can I bet on at 1xBet?

1xBet covers 40+ sports with thousands of daily events. It is particularly strong in football (Premier League, Champions League, League of Ireland), horse racing (including Gowran Park), esports and cricket.

Can I use a credit card on 1xBet from Ireland?

No. Since February 2026, Irish banks block credit-card gambling transactions. Use Visa/Mastercard debit, Neteller, Skrill, Paysafecard or cryptocurrency instead.
