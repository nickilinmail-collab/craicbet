Plinko is an arcade-style casino game inspired by the classic TV game show format. A ball drops through a grid of pegs, bouncing randomly left or right until landing in a multiplier slot at the bottom. Simple to understand, it has become one of the most popular instant games at licensed online casinos. This guide explains how it works and what Irish players should know in 2026.

How Online Plinko Works

  1. Select your bet size.
  2. Choose the number of rows (typically 8–16 — more rows = more possible landing positions).
  3. Choose a risk level: Low, Medium or High.
  4. Drop the ball — it bounces through the peg grid.
  5. The ball lands in one of the bottom slots; each slot has a multiplier value.
  6. Your bet is multiplied by the slot value.

Outcomes are determined by a certified RNG (random number generator) in licensed games. The visual of the ball bouncing is a representation of the RNG result.

RTP and Honest Maths

Well-made Plinko games from licensed developers (Spribe, BGaming, Hacksaw) publish RTPs typically in the 97–99% range. This is higher than most slot games. However, as with all casino games, RTP is a long-run statistical average and no individual session is guaranteed to match it. High-risk settings increase variance significantly — the same RTP but distributed over a wider range of outcomes.

Plinko vs Crash Games

  • Plinko: You set your bet and drop the ball. The outcome is instant and fully determined on that drop. No decision point during the game.
  • Crash games (Aviator, JetX): A multiplier grows from 1x upward. You must decide when to cash out before it crashes. The timing of the crash is predetermined by RNG before you play.

Both are legitimate RNG-based casino products at licensed operators. Neither allows skill to influence the outcome.
