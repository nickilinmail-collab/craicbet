How Progressive Jackpots Work

A progressive jackpot grows with every bet placed on the network until someone hits it, then resets to a seed value. Network jackpots pool wagers across many casinos, which is how prizes reach into the millions of euro. The trade-off is baked into the maths: a slice of every spin feeds the jackpot, so the base game usually pays back less than a comparable non-progressive slot.

Should You Chase a Progressive?

Go in with clear eyes. The jackpot odds resemble a lottery ticket attached to a slot; the entertainment comes from the base game you will actually be playing for 99.99% of your session. Three rules keep it sane:

  • Check the qualifying bet. Some progressives only open the jackpot wheel at higher stakes — playing below it means feeding a pot you cannot win.
  • Compare the base RTP. Progressive slots often run several points below the ~96% norm; the in-game info panel shows the split.
  • Set a budget for the session, not the dream. The pot does not know how long you have been playing — every spin has the same microscopic chance.
