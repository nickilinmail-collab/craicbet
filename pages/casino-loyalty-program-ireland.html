Casino loyalty programmes (also called loyalty schemes, rewards programmes, or comp systems) reward regular players with points that convert to bonuses, cash, or other benefits. Here's how they work and what to look for.

How Loyalty Points Work

Most programmes award points based on the amount wagered. A typical structure: 1 point per €10 wagered on slots; fewer points (or no points) on table games and live casino. Points accumulate in your account and can be exchanged for bonus funds or real cash, or used to advance through VIP tiers.

Point Conversion Rates: The Key to Real Value

The conversion rate is critical. 100 points = €1 at Casino A is worth dramatically more than 10,000 points = €1 at Casino B. Always calculate the actual cash value per €100 wagered. Many programmes offer less than €0.10 per €100 wagered — an effective cashback of 0.1% or less.

Tiered Loyalty Systems

Most loyalty programmes have multiple tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond). Each tier offers incrementally better benefits: higher point-to-cash conversion rates, weekly cashback, faster withdrawals, dedicated support, exclusive bonuses. Moving between tiers requires meeting activity thresholds within a qualifying period.

Loyalty vs Cashback

Some casinos offer cashback instead of points-based loyalty. Weekly cashback (% of net losses returned) can be more straightforward and genuinely valuable — especially at no-wagering cashback. If cashback has 0x wagering, €10 weekly cashback is worth more than 1,000 points that might convert to €0.50.

Responsible Gambling and Loyalty

Loyalty programmes reward volume of play. This creates an incentive to gamble more than you otherwise might. Set a session budget and stick to it regardless of loyalty goals — VIP status isn't worth losing more than your entertainment budget. UKGC rules require VIP programmes to have enhanced responsible gambling checks.

How Casino Loyalty Points Work

Loyalty points (also called comp points or reward points) are earned for every real-money wager at eligible games. Points accumulate in your account and can be converted to bonus cash or free spins at a fixed rate — typically 100 points = €1, though the earn rate varies widely.

Redeeming Points: Real Value Calculation

  • If you earn 10 points per €10 wagered (1 point per €1) and redeem at 100 points = €1, your loyalty return rate is 1% of wagers.
  • Compare this to the house edge: blackjack at 0.5% house edge means your loyalty return nearly cancels the house advantage. On slots at 4–5% house edge, the 1% return is meaningful but far from offsetting losses.
  • Tiered programmes accelerate earn rates at higher levels — Gold or Platinum tiers may earn 2× or 3× the base rate.

Loyalty Scheme Red Flags to Watch

  • Points expiry: Many schemes expire points after 30–90 days of inactivity — check the terms before accumulating a large balance.
  • Withdrawal of rewards: Some sites require wagering on redeemed points (e.g. 3× wagering on the bonus cash). This can eliminate much of the value.
  • Game exclusions: If your preferred game contributes 0% to loyalty points, the scheme has no value for you.
