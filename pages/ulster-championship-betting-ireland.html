Ulster Championship Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Ulster Football Championship is widely regarded as the most physically demanding and unpredictable provincial competition in Gaelic football. Counties including Donegal, Tyrone, Armagh, Monaghan, Derry, Fermanagh, Cavan, Antrim, Down and Armagh compete in a knockout format.

Why Ulster Betting Offers Value

The knockout format in Ulster means any county can exit at the first hurdle, regardless of their All-Ireland ambitions. This creates value betting opportunities, particularly in first-round games where a county with All-Ireland credentials faces a motivated smaller county at home. Ulster upsets are historically more common than in other provinces.

Recent Ulster winners have included Donegal, Monaghan, Tyrone, Armagh and Derry. The province sends strong representatives into the All-Ireland series each year, often providing Sam Maguire contenders.

Key Ulster Championship Venues

Clones (St Tiernach’s Park) has traditionally hosted the Ulster final but Croke Park has also been used. Fitzgerald Stadium and MacHale Park are other venues used for Ulster games. County grounds including Healy Park (Omagh), Athletic Grounds (Armagh) and Clones host earlier rounds.

Betting Markets for Ulster

Outright Ulster championship winner markets are available year-round at GRAI-licensed bookmakers. First-round match betting is available one to two weeks after the draw. The Ulster final typically has strong trading volume with in-play markets at major bookmakers.

Which counties have won the Ulster football championship most recently?

Donegal, Tyrone, Derry, Monaghan and Armagh have all won the Ulster title in recent years. The province is highly competitive with no single dominant county in the way that Dublin were in Leinster.

Is the Ulster final at Croke Park?

The Ulster final is traditionally held at St Tiernach's Park in Clones, County Monaghan, though some recent finals have been played at Croke Park. The venue is confirmed by Ulster GAA ahead of the final.

Can I bet on Ulster championship games from Northern Ireland?

If you are resident in Northern Ireland (part of the United Kingdom), UK Gambling Commission licensed bookmakers apply. The GRAI framework applies only to residents of the Republic of Ireland.

Are Ulster championship matches available for in-play betting?

In-play markets are available on the Ulster final and selected semi-final fixtures at GRAI-licensed bookmakers. First and second round games may have more limited in-play availability.
