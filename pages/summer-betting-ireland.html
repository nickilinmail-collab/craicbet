Summer Betting Ireland 2026: Galway, Tramore & Festival Season Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Irish summer betting runs from May to September and is dominated by flat horse racing festivals — most notably the Galway Races summer festival — alongside the GAA championship season and League of Ireland football.

The Galway Races

The Galway Races festival in late July is Ireland’s most attended racing event of the year, running for seven days at Ballybrit Racecourse. The Galway Plate (a Grade B handicap chase) and the Galway Hurdle (a Grade A handicap hurdle) are the flagship races and among the most bet-on races in the Irish summer. The festival atmosphere, large fields and competitive handicap racing make Galway a favourite event for punters.

Tramore Summer Festival

Tramore Racecourse in County Waterford hosts a popular Bank Holiday weekend flat racing festival in August, one of the busiest summer seaside meetings in Ireland. The carnival atmosphere and competitive racing attract large crowds and healthy betting volumes.

GAA Championship Betting

The GAA All-Ireland football and hurling championships reach their climax in the summer, with quarter-finals, semi-finals and All-Ireland finals at Croke Park in July and August. These are some of the highest-volume GAA betting occasions of the year.

League of Ireland Summer Run-In

The League of Ireland Premier Division enters its crucial phase from July to September, with the title race, European places and relegation battle all relevant betting narratives.

When are the Galway Races?

The Galway Races summer festival takes place in late July each year, running for seven days at Ballybrit Racecourse near Galway City.

What are the main races at Galway?

The Galway Plate (Grade B handicap chase) and the Galway Hurdle (Grade A handicap hurdle) are the two feature races. Both are competitive handicaps with large fields.

When does Tramore hold its summer festival?

Tramore Racecourse hosts a Bank Holiday weekend meeting typically in August. Check the Horse Racing Ireland fixture list for the confirmed dates.

Can I bet on the GAA championship in summer?

Yes. All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry extensive GAA championship betting markets throughout the summer season, including quarter-finals, semi-finals and All-Ireland finals.
