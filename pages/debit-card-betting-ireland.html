Debit Card Betting in Ireland: Visa, Mastercard and What to Know

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Debit cards are the most widely used payment method for sports betting in Ireland. This guide explains why only debit cards work (not credit cards), how Visa and Mastercard compare for betting deposits, what deposit limits apply, and how long withdrawals take.

Why Only Debit Cards (Not Credit Cards) Work

Since 2024, Irish-licensed gambling operators have been prohibited from accepting credit card deposits. This ban applies to all credit cards issued by any bank or card network — Visa Credit, Mastercard Credit, and any other form of revolving credit. The prohibition was introduced to reduce problem gambling fuelled by borrowed money.

Debit cards, which draw directly from your bank account balance, are fully permitted. This covers:

  • Standard bank-issued Visa Debit and Mastercard Debit (AIB, Bank of Ireland, PTSB, KBC, An Post Money, etc.)
  • Revolut's standard card (linked to Revolut balance — treated as a debit card)
  • N26 Mastercard Debit
  • Prepaid Mastercard/Visa cards loaded from your own funds

Both Visa Debit and Mastercard Debit are accepted at virtually all GRAI-licensed betting sites. The practical differences for Irish bettors are minor:

Deposit Limits on Debit Cards

Deposit limits for debit card betting come from two places: your bookmaker's minimum and maximum per-transaction limits, and your bank's daily spending controls.

Bookmaker minimums: Most Irish bookmakers set a minimum card deposit of €5 to €10. Some premium products require €20 as a minimum.

Bank daily limits: Irish banks typically apply a daily debit card spending limit — commonly €2,000–€5,000, though this varies by bank and account type. If you need a higher limit for a larger deposit, contact your bank to request a temporary increase. Revolut users can adjust their spending limits in the Revolut app.

Bookmaker-set deposit limits (responsible gambling): Separately, all GRAI-licensed bookmakers must offer deposit limit tools, and under GRAI rules, customers can set their own daily, weekly or monthly deposit limits. See our deposit limits guide for how these work.

Withdrawal Times Back to Debit Card

Withdrawals back to a debit card take longer than deposits. Card credits processed through the Visa or Mastercard network typically take 1–5 business days to appear in your bank account after the bookmaker releases the funds. This is a card network processing time, not a bookmaker delay.

Bookmakers are required to return funds to the same method used for deposit where possible. If you deposited by debit card, expect your withdrawal to go back to the same card. Processing time at the bookmaker side is typically 0–48 hours before the card credit is initiated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a credit card for betting in Ireland?

No. Irish-licensed gambling operators are prohibited from accepting credit card deposits since 2024. Only debit cards (and other non-credit methods such as bank transfers, PayPal, and e-wallets) are accepted. Revolut's standard card is a debit card and is not affected by the ban.

Is Visa or Mastercard better for betting deposits in Ireland?

Both are equally well supported. Virtually every GRAI-licensed bookmaker accepts both Visa Debit and Mastercard Debit. The choice of card issuer (which bank your card is from) is more likely to cause a difference than the network itself.

How long does a debit card withdrawal from a bookmaker take?

Typically 1–5 business days after the bookmaker initiates the card credit. The bookmaker's own processing usually takes 0–48 hours before the funds are sent to the card network. If speed matters, an e-wallet (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller) is a faster alternative.
