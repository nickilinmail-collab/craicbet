Football Coverage

Both operators offer standard Western European football coverage (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Champions League). 18Bet additionally covers American sports and esports. Neither operator invests significantly in Irish-specific markets (GAA, League of Ireland). Margins are broadly similar at 4.5–6% on Premier League.

Irish Punters: Key Consideration

Neither SportBet nor 18Bet holds a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Without this, Irish punters using these operators have no statutory complaint route under Irish law, no GRAI payout protection and no access to Irish responsible gambling mechanisms. For equivalent sports coverage and competitive margins, a GRAI-licensed bookmaker is the safer choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SportBet available in Ireland?

SportBet accepts international registrations but does not hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Irish players have no GRAI protections.

Does 18Bet have a GRAI licence?

18Bet does not currently hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. Check current status before depositing.

Which has better football odds?

Both offer broadly similar Premier League margins (4.5–6%). Neither offers GRAI Irish protections or strong Irish domestic sports coverage.

Is 18Bet a trusted bookmaker?

18Bet holds an MGA or Curaçao licence depending on the entity. MGA-licensed operations have more regulatory teeth than Curaçao. Neither provides Irish GRAI protections.

What is the safest alternative for Irish punters?

A GRAI-licensed bookmaker provides Irish statutory protections, a formal Irish complaint route and equivalent sports coverage. Use the GRAI register at grai.ie to verify any operator.
