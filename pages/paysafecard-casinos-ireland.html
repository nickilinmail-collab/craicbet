Paysafecard for Responsible Gambling & Budgeting

Paysafecard is arguably the best payment method for responsible gambling, and here's why:

  • Fixed budget: You can only spend what's on your voucher. When it's gone, it's gone — no temptation to chase losses with more money
  • Physical purchase barrier: To add more funds, you must physically go to a shop to buy another voucher. This creates a natural pause that helps prevent impulsive deposits
  • No bank connection: Your bank account and cards are completely separate from your casino activity, removing the risk of overspending
  • Cash-based control: If you buy vouchers with cash, your gambling spend doesn't appear on any bank statement

FAQ — Paysafecard Casinos Ireland

Where can I buy Paysafecard in Ireland?▾
Paysafecard vouchers are sold at thousands of locations across Ireland, including Spar, Centra, most newsagents, An Post offices, and many petrol stations (Topaz, Circle K, Applegreen). You can also buy PINs online at paysafecard.com. Vouchers come in €10, €25, €50, and €100 denominations. Use the Paysafecard store locator to find your nearest stockist.
Can I withdraw casino winnings to Paysafecard?▾
No. Paysafecard is a deposit-only method at all online casinos. To withdraw your winnings, you'll need an alternative method such as bank transfer, PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, or Revolut. We recommend setting up a withdrawal method when you first register to avoid delays when cashing out.
Is Paysafecard anonymous for casino deposits?▾
Paysafecard offers a high degree of privacy. When you buy a voucher with cash at a shop, no personal information is required (for standard amounts). When you deposit at a casino, only the 16-digit PIN is transmitted — no bank or card details. However, the casino itself will require KYC verification for withdrawals, so complete anonymity isn't possible if you plan to cash out winnings.
What are the Paysafecard deposit limits at Irish casinos?▾
Individual Paysafecard vouchers come in denominations of €10, €25, €50, and €100. Most casinos allow you to combine multiple voucher PINs in a single deposit, effectively letting you deposit up to €250–€1,000 per transaction (depending on the casino's policy). With a My Paysafecard account, limits may be higher. The minimum deposit at most casinos is €10 (some accept €5).
Are there fees for using Paysafecard at casinos?▾
No fees are charged by casinos for Paysafecard deposits. Paysafecard itself doesn't charge a purchase fee for the voucher either. However, there are two fees to be aware of: a maintenance fee of €3/month is applied after 12 months of inactivity on unused voucher balances, and a currency conversion fee of approximately 2% applies if you use the voucher in a different currency than the one you purchased it in (not an issue at EUR casinos).
