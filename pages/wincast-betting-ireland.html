Wincast Betting Ireland: What Is a Wincast

A wincast is a combined bet on a team to win a match and a specific player to score during that match. Both parts must come true for the bet to win. It's simpler than a scorecast (which requires a correct score) but still delivers boosted odds compared to betting on each outcome separately.

How Wincast Odds Are Calculated in Ireland

Bookmakers calculate the wincast price by multiplying the implied probabilities of the team winning and the player scoring, then converting back to odds. The resulting price is usually slightly lower than the theoretical combination due to the bookmaker margin, but still significantly higher than backing either selection alone.

Wincast Settlement Rules Ireland

Key rules that apply to wincasts on Irish-licensed betting sites: the player must score (own goals don't count); the team must win in 90 minutes (extra time goals usually don't count unless specified); if the player doesn't take part in the match, most sites void the entire wincast and return the stake. Always check the specific T&Cs before placing, as settlement can vary between operators.

What does DNB mean in betting?

Draw No Bet — if you back a team and the match draws, your stake is returned. Only a loss results in losing your bet. The draw is removed as a result that costs you money.

Is DNB the same as Asian Handicap 0?

Yes — mathematically identical. AH 0 and Draw No Bet have the same settlement rules: draw = stake returned, win = win, loss = lose. The notation differs by market style.

Are DNB odds lower than outright win odds?

Yes — because DNB includes draw insurance. The odds are lower to reflect the fact that you can't lose your stake in the event of a draw.

When should I use Draw No Bet?

When you're confident in a team but worried about a draw. DNB reduces profit on a win but provides a safety net. Worth using when draw probability is high and the team's win margin is uncertain.

What happens to a DNB bet in an accumulator if one game draws?

The DNB leg is treated as a void — stake for that leg is returned and it's removed from the acca. The remaining legs continue at adjusted odds.
