Live Casino vs RNG Games — Which is Better?

Both live dealer games and standard RNG (Random Number Generator) casino games have their place. Choosing between them depends on what you value most in your gaming experience:

Choose Live Casino if you value:

  • Authenticity — Watching a real dealer handle real cards or spin a real wheel eliminates any doubt about fairness. Many players simply feel more comfortable seeing the game unfold physically.
  • Social interaction — The chat function lets you communicate with dealers and fellow players. Dealers are trained to be engaging and personable, creating a social atmosphere that RNG games can't match.
  • Atmosphere — HD streams with multiple camera angles, professional dealers, and sophisticated studio designs create an immersive experience that captures the feel of a luxury casino.
  • Strategy games — For games like blackjack where strategy matters, live play gives you time to think and provides a more natural rhythm than the rapid-fire pace of RNG versions.

Choose RNG Games if you value:

  • Speed — RNG games run at your pace. You can play hundreds of blackjack hands per hour versus 50-60 in a live setting.
  • Lower stakes — RNG table games typically start at €0.10-1.00 per hand, while live dealer minimums are usually €1-5 (and €10+ at premium tables).
  • Privacy — No cameras, no chat, no interaction. Some players prefer the solitary experience of playing against software.
  • Availability — RNG games never have full tables, waiting lists, or maintenance windows. They're available instantly, 24/7.

Our recommendation: Try both. Many Irish players enjoy slots and RNG table games for quick sessions, then switch to live casino for a more relaxed, social evening of play. The best online casinos offer strong options in both categories.
