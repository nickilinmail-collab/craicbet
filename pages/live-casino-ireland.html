What Is Live Casino?

Live casino is a category of online casino gaming where games are hosted by human dealers in a purpose-built studio (or occasionally a real casino floor), filmed with multiple high-definition cameras, and streamed to players in real time via the internet. Players interact through an on-screen interface — placing bets, making decisions — while watching the dealer shuffle cards, spin the wheel, or deal the dice.

The key difference from standard online casino games is that live casino games use physical cards and wheels rather than a software random number generator (RNG). Many players prefer this because they can see the outcome unfolding rather than trusting an algorithm they cannot observe. The games are also typically social — a live chat function lets players interact with the dealer and sometimes other players.

Live casino has grown substantially in Ireland since broadband penetration made reliable video streaming accessible nationwide. The Irish market is predominantly mobile, so the best live casino sites are optimised for smartphones and tablets as well as desktop.

What to Look for in a Live Casino Site

1. Licence strength

Since no GRAI casino licence exists, look for MGA (Malta) or UKGC (UK) licences as the first filter. Both require studios to meet technical certification standards for game integrity. Verify the licence on the regulator's own register, not just the casino's footer claim.

2. Table limits that suit your bankroll

Live casino tables typically have minimum and maximum bet limits that differ from online slots. Standard tables may start at €1 minimum; VIP tables can require €25 or €100 minimums. For recreational players, finding a site with low-limit live tables is important. The best sites offer a range from micro-stakes tables to high-limit VIP rooms.

3. Stream quality on your device

HD video streaming on mobile requires good data or WiFi. The best live casino sites offer adaptive streaming that reduces quality on weak connections rather than dropping frames. Test on mobile before committing to a site if mobile is your primary device.

Beyond roulette and blackjack, a quality live casino library includes baccarat, live poker variants (Three Card Poker, Casino Hold'em), and craps. Sites powered by Evolution also offer game shows (Crazy Time, Deal or No Deal Live, etc.). Game variety matters if you plan to spend extended time in the live casino section.
