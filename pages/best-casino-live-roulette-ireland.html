Lightning Roulette: Is It Worth Playing?

Lightning Roulette adds excitement through multipliers but adjusts base payouts to 29:1 instead of 35:1 on straight-up bets to fund the multipliers. The house edge is approximately 3.6% — higher than standard European roulette (2.7%). The multipliers add variance, not an improved expected value.

Choosing the Best Live Roulette Table in Ireland

For pure value, French Roulette with La Partage (half your even-money stake returned when the ball lands on zero) gives the lowest house edge at just 1.35% on red/black and odd/even bets.
