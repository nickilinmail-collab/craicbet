When the App Wins

Push notifications are the app’s biggest practical advantage: price boosts, early prices on your favourite teams and in-play goal alerts all arrive as instant notifications rather than requiring you to open a browser. For punters who want to maximise daily promotional value (price boosts, early prices), the app is essential. Touch ID and Face ID login via the app is faster than re-entering credentials in a browser. In-play betting loads slightly faster on the app as data is cached locally.

When the Mobile Site is Enough

The mobile site is adequate for: placing pre-match bets on markets you have already found, withdrawals and account management, one-off users who do not want to install multiple apps. It is always current without updates and requires no storage space. For punters who bet infrequently or do not use promotional offers, the mobile site is fully functional.

Android: Sideloading Considerations

Some GRAI-licensed operators do not list their Android betting app on Google Play due to Play Store policy restrictions. These apps must be downloaded directly from the operator’s website (sideloading, requiring enabling “install from unknown sources” in Android settings). Only download apps directly from GRAI-licensed operators’ official websites — third-party APK download sites carry malware risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use the app or mobile site for betting?

The app is better if you want push notifications (price boosts, in-play alerts, results). The mobile site is adequate for occasional, pre-match betting. Both are functionally complete for core betting.

Are betting apps available on iPhone?

Yes. Major GRAI-licensed operators have iOS apps available on the App Store. All support Face ID and Touch ID login.

Why isn't the betting app on Google Play?

Google Play has historically restricted real-money gambling apps in some regions. Some GRAI-licensed operators require their Android app to be sideloaded (downloaded from their website). Only download from the operator’s official site.

Do betting apps work better than mobile sites?

Apps load faster, offer push notifications and have smoother in-play interfaces. For heavy users and promotion chasers, the app advantage is meaningful. For occasional bettors, the mobile site difference is minimal.

Is it safe to install a betting app?

Apps from GRAI-licensed operators are safe to install from official sources (App Store, Google Play, or the operator’s official website). Never install a betting app from a third-party APK site.
