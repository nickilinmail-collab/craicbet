What the Licence Number Tells You

Every GRAI remote betting licence follows the format GRAI-NNNN-RB-26-0001:

  • GRAI — issued by the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland
  • NNNN — operator's unique identifier number
  • RB — Remote Betting (RBI = Remote Betting Intermediary; IPB = In-Person Betting)
  • 26 — year issued (2026)
  • 0001 — licence sequence number

You can verify any licence number directly at grai.ie.

Operators That Chose Not to Apply

Several large international operators chose to geo-block Irish users rather than apply for a GRAI licence. These include Stake, Betfred, Kalshi and Polymarket. Irish players attempting to access these sites are now redirected or blocked.

What GRAI Licences Do Not Cover Yet

The current GRAI register covers betting only. Online casino licences (remote gaming licences) are a separate category. Applications open in a later phase, with licences expected in 2027–2028. No GRAI casino licence has been issued yet. See our no GRAI casino Ireland explainer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many operators hold a GRAI remote betting licence?

as of 12 November 2026, 39 companies hold 41 GRAI remote betting or betting intermediary licences (37 RB + 4 RBI), trading under 50+ brands. The list is maintained at grai.ie.

How do I check if a bookmaker has a GRAI licence?

Check the footer of the betting site for a GRAI licence number in the format GRAI-NNNN-RB-26-0001. You can verify it directly at grai.ie. All licensed operators are required to display their licence number.

What does a GRAI remote betting licence mean for players?

A GRAI licence means the operator is legally authorised to accept bets from Irish players, must pay out winnings, cannot accept credit card deposits, must offer deposit limits and self-exclusion, and is subject to Irish law enforcement by GRAI.

Do GRAI licences cover online casinos?

No. GRAI has issued remote betting licences only. Online casino licences (remote gaming licences) are a separate category, with applications expected to open later. No GRAI casino licence has been issued yet.

Which bookmakers are not GRAI licensed?

Stake, Betfred, Kalshi and Polymarket chose to geo-block Irish players rather than apply for a GRAI licence. Irish players cannot access these sites. Always verify a licence before depositing.
