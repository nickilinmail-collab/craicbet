Who Are Drogheda United?

Drogheda United Football Club are based in Drogheda, Co. Louth, and have been a fixture in the League of Ireland Premier Division for many years. The club plays home matches at Head in the Game Park (formerly United Park), a ground that has seen its fair share of memorable LOI moments over the decades.

Drogheda United have a proud history in domestic football, including league title success, and have participated in European competition. The club carries strong support from the Drogheda community and the wider Louth area, giving home matches a passionate local flavour that can unsettle visiting teams.

For bettors, Drogheda United represent a solid mid-table LOI side. Their fixtures are covered by GRAI-licensed bookmakers with standard LOI markets, and matches against local rivals Dundalk attract particular attention and can offer interesting betting propositions when form is taken into account.

Drogheda United in the 2026 LOI Season

The 2026 Premier Division campaign has seen Drogheda United working hard to establish themselves in the top half of the table. Consistency is the key challenge for clubs of their size in the LOI — squad depth over a long season from spring to autumn is crucial, and form can fluctuate.

The Co. Louth derby against Dundalk FC remains the standout fixture on the Drogheda United calendar. These meetings generate significant local interest and are well covered by bookmakers, with enhanced markets often available around the time of the fixture.

At Head in the Game Park, Drogheda have traditionally been a tough side to beat. The home crowd factor and the familiarity with the pitch dimensions can give them an edge against sides who have not played there before. When assessing Drogheda in any betting market, their home record is a key data point.
