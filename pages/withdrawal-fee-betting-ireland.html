Hidden Costs: E-Wallet to Bank Transfer Fees

This is the fee most punters miss. Even if the bookmaker charges nothing to withdraw to your Skrill account, Skrill itself charges a fee (around 1–1.9%) when you then move funds from Skrill to your bank account. If you use Skrill or Neteller purely as a pass-through, factor this cost in. PayPal to Irish bank transfers are typically free within the EU.

Inactivity Fees

Some operators charge a monthly fee on dormant accounts (no login for 12–24 months). This is not a withdrawal fee per se, but can erode a small balance over time. Check your operator’s terms. Under GRAI rules, operators must clearly disclose inactivity fees in their T&Cs.

How to Avoid Withdrawal Fees

  • Use your debit card or Revolut as the primary withdrawal method — free at nearly all GRAI-licensed operators
  • Withdraw directly to your bank account via SEPA if the operator offers it fee-free
  • If using Skrill or Neteller, transfer directly to your bank rather than keeping funds in the e-wallet
  • Avoid multiple small withdrawals if the operator charges per transaction; batch them into one

When Fees Apply and How to Avoid Them

  • Inactive accounts: Some sites charge a monthly inactivity fee (€5–€10) if you haven't logged in for 12+ months. Withdraw your balance before going inactive.
  • Too-frequent withdrawals: A handful of sites limit fee-free withdrawals per month (e.g. one free per month, then €1–€2 per request). Batch your withdrawals if possible.
  • Currency conversion: If your account is in euros but your bank card is sterling (e.g. Northern Ireland), conversion fees may apply — confirm the currency at registration.
  • Minimum withdrawal: Withdrawing below the minimum (usually €5–€20) may incur a fee or be blocked entirely.

Your Rights on Withdrawal Fees Under GRAI

GRAI-licensed operators must clearly disclose all fees in their terms and conditions before you deposit. If a fee appears that was not disclosed, you can raise a complaint formally with the site's customer service, then escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie if unresolved. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 gives the GRAI powers to sanction operators who withhold funds or charge undisclosed fees.

Do Irish betting sites charge withdrawal fees?

Most GRAI-licensed operators offer at least one free withdrawal method (usually debit card or Revolut). Some charge €1–€3 for bank transfers or additional withdrawals in a month.

Are there fees to withdraw to Revolut from a betting site?

No — most GRAI-licensed bookmakers treat Revolut as a standard debit card and do not charge a fee. Revolut itself does not charge for incoming transfers.

Do e-wallets charge fees for betting withdrawals?

The bookmaker usually does not charge. But Skrill and Neteller may charge 1–1.9% when you transfer funds from your e-wallet to your bank account. PayPal bank transfers within the EU are typically free.

What is an inactivity fee on a betting account?

A monthly charge applied to accounts that have not been used (no login) for 12–24 months. GRAI rules require this to be clearly disclosed in the T&Cs. Avoid by logging in occasionally or withdrawing your balance.

How can I avoid withdrawal fees on Irish betting sites?

Use debit card or Revolut as your primary withdrawal method, both free at nearly all operators. Batch withdrawals rather than making multiple small requests if your operator charges per transaction.
