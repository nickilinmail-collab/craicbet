GRAI Interoperable Exclusion Register Ireland

Last updated: November 2026

Ireland is building a national interoperable gambling exclusion register under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. When live, one registration will exclude you from every GRAI-licensed betting site and shop in the country simultaneously — online and retail.

Current status (November 2026)

GRAI is established and issuing remote betting licences since 1 July 2026. The interoperable exclusion register is in development but is not yet live as a single national system. This is a significant piece of infrastructure that requires all licensed operators to connect to a central database — it takes time to build correctly.

Until it launches, the options available to Irish players are:

  • Self-exclude from each operator individually — every GRAI-licensed site must offer this under their responsible gambling tools
  • Contact GamblingCare.ie at 1800 753 753 — they can help coordinate exclusions and provide support
  • Use your bank's gambling block — Bank of Ireland, AIB and others offer opt-in gambling transaction blocks

What the interoperable register will do

Once live, the GRAI exclusion register will:

  • Allow you to self-exclude from all GRAI-licensed operators with a single registration
  • Cover online betting, online casino (once casino licences issue), and retail betting shops
  • Include a mandatory minimum exclusion period (expected to be at least one year)
  • Prevent operators from knowingly allowing a registered person to bet
  • Include a cooling-off period before an exclusion can be lifted

How to self-exclude right now (operator by operator)

Every GRAI-licensed bookmaker must offer self-exclusion as a player protection tool. The process varies slightly per site but generally:

  1. Log into your account
  2. Go to Account Settings → Responsible Gambling (or similar)
  3. Select Self-Exclusion
  4. Choose your exclusion period (minimum 6 months at most sites)
  5. Confirm — the exclusion takes effect immediately and cannot be reversed during the period

You will need to repeat this at each site where you hold an account.

Your bank's gambling block: an immediate option

While the national register is in development, your bank offers a faster blanket solution:

  • Bank of Ireland — opt-in gambling block available in the BOI app under Controls
  • AIB — contact customer service to enable a gambling restriction
  • Revolut — Settings → Security → Gambling block
  • N26 — card limits available in the app

Bank blocks prevent gambling transactions at the payment level — they work even at unlicensed sites. See our Bank of Ireland gambling block guide for full instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GRAI interoperable exclusion register?
It is a national self-exclusion system under development by GRAI. When live, a single registration will exclude you from all GRAI-licensed gambling operators in Ireland simultaneously.
Is the GRAI exclusion register live in 2026?
Not yet as a full national system as of November 2026. GRAI is developing it under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Individual operator self-exclusion tools are available now.
How do I self-exclude from all betting sites in Ireland now?
Self-exclude from each operator individually through their responsible gambling tools. Also contact GamblingCare.ie on 1800 753 753 for support. Consider enabling your bank's gambling block for immediate coverage.
Can I reverse a GRAI self-exclusion?
Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, self-exclusions will have a mandatory cooling-off period before they can be lifted. This prevents impulsive reversals. The exact period will be set in GRAI regulations.
