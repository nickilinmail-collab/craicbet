Round Robin Calculator (3 Selections, 10 Bets)

What is a Round Robin bet?

A Round Robin is a 10-bet combination from 3 selections. It includes 3 doubles, 1 treble and 3 up-and-down (single stakes about) pairs. Each up-and-down pair is 2 bets — one per direction — giving 6 bets from the pairs plus 4 from the doubles and treble, totalling 10. The unique feature of a Round Robin is the up-and-down element: if both selections in a pair win, the return is double what a simple double would pay. If just one wins from a pair, you get your stake back. This makes the Round Robin more protective than a Patent (which uses standard singles) for shorter-priced selections.

How up-and-down bets work

An up-and-down (also called a single stakes about or SSA) between selections A and B works like this: if A wins, the stake plus winnings from that bet ride on B. If B also wins, you collect on that too. If B loses, the bet is void — you recover the stake that went on B. The same applies in the other direction: if B wins, its return rides on A. The result: both win → you receive double the double return; one wins → stake refunded on the losing direction; neither wins → both bets lose. This structure suits markets where you expect both selections to win but want some protection if one doesn't.

When to use a Round Robin

A Round Robin works best when you have 3 selections at short to medium odds that you expect to win, but want some protection built in. The up-and-down pairs give you a stake refund if one of a pair fails, which softens the blow compared to losing a standard double. It's popular in horse racing when punters have strong fancies for a card but want the extra insurance of the SSA structure. At longer odds, the extra cost of the up-and-down bets may not be worth it — a Trixie at lower cost covers the same combinations (minus the SSA protection).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bets in a Round Robin?

10 bets — 3 doubles, 1 treble and 3 up-and-down pairs (each pair = 2 bets).

What is an up-and-down bet?

An up-and-down (single stakes about) is a pair of bets where each selection's return rides on the other. Both win → you collect double the double; one wins → stake refunded; neither wins → lose both stakes.

How many winners do you need for a Round Robin return?

Just 1 winner can return a stake refund via the up-and-down bets. With 2 or 3 winners, the doubles, treble and up-and-downs combine for a larger return.

What is the difference between a Round Robin and a Patent?

A Patent has 7 bets using standard singles. A Round Robin has 10 bets, replacing the 3 singles with 3 up-and-down pairs (6 bets). The Round Robin costs more but the SSA structure can produce bigger returns when both selections in a pair win.

How much does a €1 Round Robin cost?

€10 total — 3 doubles (€3), 1 treble (€1) and 3 up-and-down pairs (€6) = €10.
