About the League of Ireland First Division

The League of Ireland First Division is the second tier of domestic football in the Republic of Ireland, sitting below the Premier Division in the LOI pyramid. The First Division features clubs that have been relegated from the Premier Division, clubs that have climbed up from the amateur and regional leagues, and ambitious new clubs targeting a first ever top-flight campaign.

The First Division season runs in parallel with the Premier Division, from early spring through to autumn. At the end of the season, the top-finishing clubs earn promotion to the Premier Division, while the bottom Premier Division clubs drop down. The exact promotion/playoff structure varies and is confirmed by the FAI each season.

For bettors, the First Division is a less well-covered competition than the Premier Division, but it still attracts betting markets from GRAI-licensed bookmakers, particularly for well-known clubs that have recently been relegated.

Promotion Race: Outright Betting

Outright betting on LOI First Division promotion is available from some GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Key considerations:

  • Clubs recently relegated from the Premier Division often have the squad quality to bounce back immediately and are frequently installed as favourites for promotion
  • Clubs with strong financial backing and clear promotion ambitions are worth noting in outright markets
  • Home form is particularly important at First Division level, as the gap between home and away performance can be more pronounced than in the Premier Division
  • Clubs with experienced management and stable squads tend to outperform over a full season

First Division vs Premier Division Betting

Bookmaker coverage differs between the two LOI divisions. Premier Division matches typically offer a broader range of markets and more competitive odds than First Division fixtures. For First Division matches:

  • Market availability may be limited to match result (1X2) and over/under goals for some operators
  • Odds may be less competitive due to lower volumes of bets placed on these matches
  • Local knowledge is particularly valuable: punters who follow a First Division club closely have a bigger information advantage relative to bookmakers
  • FAI Cup fixtures involving First Division clubs can produce upsets against Premier Division opposition and may offer value in cup betting markets
