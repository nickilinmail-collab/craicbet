Each-Way Calculator: What Your Bet Really Pays

An each-way calculator splits your stake in two: half on the win, half on the place. Enter the odds, the place terms (such as 1/4 odds, 3 places), and your stake — the calculator shows your win return, your place return, and your total payout. It is essential for horse racing bets where the place portion is easy to miscalculate.

Work Out Any Each-Way Bet

How the Maths Works

€10 each-way is two €10 bets (total €20). The win part pays full odds; the place part pays the odds divided by the place terms (1/4 or 1/5). At 8/1 with 1/5 terms: a win returns €10×8 + €10×1.6 plus both stakes back — €116; a place-only returns €26, a €6 profit on the €20 outlay. The break-even point for the place part alone is odds of 4/1 at 1/4 terms or 5/1 at 1/5 — below that, "placing" still loses money overall, which is the most misunderstood fact in each-way betting.

Rule 4, Built In

If a horse is withdrawn after you bet, a Rule 4 deduction (5p–90p per euro, by the withdrawn horse's odds) comes off winnings — not stakes. Set the slider to see the real payout after a deduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 10 euro each-way bet cost?

€20 — it is two bets: €10 the win, €10 the place.

At what odds does an each-way place make a profit?

Above 4/1 at 1/4 terms, above 5/1 at 1/5 terms — below that a place still loses overall.

Does Rule 4 reduce my stake?

No — deductions apply to winnings only.
