Mobile Betting on the Move Ireland 2026: Apps & Lifestyle Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The majority of online betting in Ireland now takes place on mobile devices. GRAI-licensed bookmakers all offer dedicated mobile apps or mobile-optimised websites that allow you to bet from anywhere — at home, on a commute, at a racecourse or while watching a game live.

GRAI-Licensed Betting Apps in Ireland

All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer native iOS and Android apps. These apps support the full range of betting markets, live in-play betting, quick deposit via Revolut or debit card, and responsible gambling tools including deposit limits and self-exclusion. Features vary by operator but typically include:

  • Push notifications for odds movement, results and promotions
  • Live streaming (where available under the licence)
  • Cash-out functionality for in-play bets
  • Touch ID or Face ID login for quick access
  • Bet tracker for monitoring your open bets

Betting at Racecourses and Stadiums

Using your phone to bet at an Irish racecourse or stadium is entirely legal under the GRAI framework as long as you are using a licensed bookmaker. Wi-Fi is available at Leopardstown, the Curragh, Punchestown and other major venues; 4G/5G coverage is generally reliable at Croke Park, the Aviva Stadium and most League of Ireland grounds.

Data Usage and Connectivity

Live in-play betting uses more data than standard browsing. Most betting apps use less than 5MB per hour of in-play use. Ensure your mobile data plan supports this if you are relying on 4G/5G rather than venue Wi-Fi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are GRAI-licensed bookmaker apps available on iOS and Android?

Yes. All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer apps for both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play or direct APK download). Some operators offer a mobile web version as an alternative.

Can I bet in-play on a mobile device in Ireland?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmaker apps fully support in-play betting. Live markets update in real time and cash-out is available on selected in-play bets.

Is mobile betting legal at an Irish racecourse?

Yes. Placing bets on your phone using a GRAI-licensed bookmaker app while at an Irish racecourse is fully legal. On-course bookmakers are separate and operate under their own transitional arrangements.

Do betting apps work on slow mobile connections?

Most GRAI-licensed betting apps are optimised for mobile data connections. Live in-play streaming may require a stronger connection, but standard match betting and market browsing works on 3G or better.
