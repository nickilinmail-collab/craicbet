SportPesa Ireland 2026: GRAI Licence Status & What Irish Players Need to Know

SportPesa is a major sports betting operator with roots in Kenya and a significant African market presence, now also operating in Europe under an MGA licence. As of November 2026, SportPesa does not appear on the GRAI public register. Here is what Irish players should know.

What Is SportPesa?

SportPesa was founded in Nairobi, Kenya in 2014 and rapidly became one of Africa’s most prominent sports betting brands, with significant sponsorship deals including the Everton FC shirt sponsorship. The company holds a Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) licence for its European operations and a Kenyan BCLB licence for its core African market. It operates a sportsbook covering football, basketball, rugby, and other sports.

SportPesa has had a complex regulatory history, including a temporary suspension of operations in Kenya in 2019 due to a tax dispute, before resuming. Its MGA licence covers European operations to EU regulatory standards, but this does not extend to Irish GRAI authorisation.

SportPesa’s GRAI Status in Ireland

As of November 2026, SportPesa does not appear on the GRAI public register of licensed remote betting operators. Without a GRAI licence, SportPesa is not authorised to operate under Ireland’s specific regulatory framework. Irish players would not receive GRAI-mandated protections. SportPesa’s MGA licence provides EU-standard protections, but these are not equivalent to Irish GRAI requirements for Irish residents.
