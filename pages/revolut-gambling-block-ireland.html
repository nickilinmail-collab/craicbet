Revolut Gambling Block Ireland: How to Turn It Off

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Revolut’s gambling block is a voluntary spending control that blocks transactions at merchants classified under MCC (Merchant Category Code) 7995 — which covers most online betting and casino sites. Here is how to turn it off, what it covers, and alternatives if you are still declined.

How to Turn Off the Revolut Gambling Block in Ireland

  1. Open the Revolut app on your phone
  2. Tap the card icon or go to “Cards” in the bottom menu
  3. Select the card you use for deposits (usually your main Revolut card)
  4. Scroll to “Controls” (or “Spending controls”)
  5. Find “Gambling” or “Betting & gambling” and toggle it off
  6. The change takes effect immediately — retry your deposit

Still Declined After Disabling the Block?

Some betting sites decline Revolut regardless of the gambling block. This can happen because the site itself does not accept prepaid or e-money cards, or because Revolut’s issuer (prepaid MasterCard) is blocked by that specific bookmaker. If declined after disabling the block: try depositing via bank transfer instead; use a different card (debit card linked to your bank account); or use a different e-wallet such as Skrill or PayPal.

Revolut Premium and Metal cards may have different acceptance rates at some bookmakers, but there is no guarantee. The underlying issue is often the site’s payment processing policy, not Revolut’s settings.

How to turn off the gambling block on Revolut Ireland

Revolut allows you to toggle gambling transaction blocks within the app. To disable the block: open the Revolut app → tap your profile icon (top left) → go to Security → find Gambling transactions → toggle off. The change takes effect immediately for new transactions. If the toggle re-enables automatically, check if parental controls or a subscription plan feature is overriding it — some Revolut plans enforce this by default for under-18 accounts.

Why is Revolut blocking my betting deposit in Ireland

Revolut uses merchant category code (MCC) 7995 to classify betting and wagering transactions. Even with the in-app toggle off, some betting sites may be classified under slightly different MCCs or as general e-commerce, which can cause inconsistent blocking. If a specific betting site deposit fails with Revolut even after toggling the block off, try a different payment method: standard debit cards from AIB, Bank of Ireland, Permanent TSB or Ulster Bank, or PayPal linked to your bank account. PayPal transactions to betting sites may appear as PayPal payments (MCC 6012) rather than gambling, bypassing some blocks.

Can I turn the Revolut gambling block back on?

Yes — toggle it back on in the same Controls menu at any time. It re-activates immediately. This is a responsible gambling tool — use it whenever you want to restrict your spending at betting sites.
