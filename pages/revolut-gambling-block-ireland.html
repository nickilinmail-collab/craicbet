Revolut Gambling Block in Ireland: Where It Is and the 48-Hour Wait

Last updated: 12 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 12 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Revolut’s gambling block is an optional safety setting that stops card payments to bookmakers, online casinos and betting apps. It switches on instantly, but switching it off takes up to 48 hours. That delay is the most common reason an Irish Revolut card is still declined after the toggle has been changed. Details below were checked against Revolut’s own help pages on 12 November 2026.

Revolut Gambling Block: The Short Version

How to Find the Setting

  1. Open the Revolut app and tap your profile icon at the top left of the Home screen.
  2. Open Security (labelled “Security & privacy” in some app versions; the toggle may sit under Payments).
  3. Find Gambling block and toggle it.
  4. Tap Yes to confirm.

Menu labels move between app versions. If you cannot find the toggle, search “gambling block” in the in-app Help.

Why the 48-Hour Wait Exists

The block is a safer-gambling tool, and the delay is deliberate: it stops the block being removed in the heat of the moment. Nothing speeds it up. If you switched the block on because gambling was costing more than you wanted, treat the wait as the tool working. The self-exclusion guide covers stronger options, and the Gambling Awareness Trust (1800 753 753, gamblingcare.ie) offers free, confidential support.

Declined, but the Block Was Never On?

Because the block is off by default, a declined deposit often has another cause:

  • The site does not accept the card. Some bookmakers decline certain card types regardless of your settings.
  • Credit cards are not allowed. Irish law bans gambling with credit cards; see the credit card ban explained. Revolut debit cards are not affected by this rule.
  • The operator is not licensed for Ireland. Payments to unlicensed sites fail more often, and you have no Irish regulator behind you if something goes wrong. Check the licence number on the GRAI register tracker.
  • Card security checks. An unapproved 3-D Secure prompt or a frozen card produces the same decline message.

For how Revolut deposits and withdrawals work at licensed bookmakers, see betting with Revolut in Ireland.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the gambling block in the Revolut app?

Tap your profile icon at the top left of the Home screen, open Security (shown as Security & privacy in some app versions) and find Gambling block. Toggle it and confirm.

How long does it take to turn the Revolut gambling block off?

Up to 48 hours. Switching the block on works immediately, but switching it off starts a cooling-off period of up to 48 hours before gambling payments are allowed again.

Is the Revolut gambling block on by default?

No. Revolut says the block is disabled by default on new accounts. If your card is declined at a betting site and you never switched the block on, the cause is something else.

Does the gambling block stop bank transfers?

No. It applies to card payments that Revolut identifies as gambling through the merchant category code. Revolut says it does not affect other payment types such as bank transfers.

Should I turn the block off?

If you switched it on to control your gambling, the 48-hour wait is there to give you time to reconsider. Free, confidential support is available from the Gambling Awareness Trust on 1800 753 753 and at gamblingcare.ie.
