How to Claim a Casino Bonus in Ireland

⚠️ No GRAI casino licence exists in Ireland yet. GRAI issued only remote betting licences from July 2026. Casino licences expected 2027–2028.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Claiming a casino bonus in Ireland is straightforward once you know the steps. This guide walks you through the process from start to finish, covering what to watch out for at each stage.

Step 1: Choose a Licensed Casino

Select a casino with a valid licence from the MGA (Malta), UKGC (UK), or Gibraltar. Verify the licence number on the regulator's website — don't rely solely on the casino's own claim. No Irish casino currently holds a GRAI gaming licence; casino licences are expected 2027–2028.

Step 2: Register Your Account

Fill in the registration form with accurate details — name, address, date of birth, email. Providing false information will cause problems during withdrawal verification (KYC). Use your real details from the outset.

Step 3: Check the Bonus Terms Before Depositing

Before making your deposit, read the full bonus terms and conditions: wagering requirement, eligible games, maximum bet while bonus is active, time limit, withdrawal cap, and any game restrictions. The most critical terms are wagering (how much you must bet) and eligible games (whether your preferred games count).

🔒 Licensing: No Irish casino currently holds a GRAI gaming licence. GRAI issued only remote betting licences from 1 July 2026; casino licensing is expected from 2027–2028. Irish players can legally use MGA, UKGC, or Gibraltar-licensed casinos.

Step 4: Enter a Bonus Code (If Required)

Some bonuses require a promo code entered during registration or in the cashier. If a code is required and you miss this step, the bonus won't be credited. Check the bonus landing page or T&Cs for the exact activation method.

Step 5: Make Your Deposit

Deposit the minimum qualifying amount (usually €10–€20). Many bonuses require a specific minimum to unlock the full bonus. Depositing below the minimum may result in a reduced bonus or none at all.

Step 6: Meet the Wagering Requirements

Play through the required wagering amount on eligible games. Track your progress in the casino's bonus section or account page. Stay within the maximum bet limit (usually €5/spin or €5/round) while the bonus is active — exceeding this can void the bonus at some casinos.

Step 7: Withdraw

Once wagering is complete, the bonus converts to real cash. Complete identity verification (KYC) if not already done and request your withdrawal. Processing time depends on the method and casino.

Step-by-Step: Claiming a Casino Bonus in Ireland

  1. Register and verify identity — GRAI-compliant sites require ID before bonus activation.
  2. Opt in — most bonuses require you to tick a box or enter a promo code during deposit.
  3. Meet the minimum deposit — usually €10–€20; depositing via e-wallets may exclude you.
  4. Check your bonus balance — bonus funds typically appear in a separate 'bonus' wallet.
  5. Read the game weighting — slots count 100%; table games may count 10–20% or be excluded.
  6. Complete wagering before withdrawing — bonus + deposit multiplied by the wagering requirement.

Common Mistakes When Claiming Bonuses

  • Depositing via e-wallet: Revolut, PayPal and Skrill deposits often disqualify you from welcome bonuses — use Visa/Mastercard or bank transfer.
  • Playing excluded games: If you play a game weighted at 0% while a bonus is active, it won't count toward wagering and may forfeit the bonus.
  • Exceeding the max bet: Placing a bet above the maximum allowed stake (often €5) during bonus wagering can void the entire bonus.
  • Letting the timer expire: Most bonuses expire in 7–30 days — set a reminder to complete wagering in time.
