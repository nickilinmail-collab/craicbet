Bank of Ireland Gambling Block — How to Stop Gambling Transactions

The GRAI encouraged all Irish banks to introduce gambling blocking tools, and Bank of Ireland launched its gambling block facility in 2025. It joins AIB, EBS and Revolut in giving Irish customers the ability to block gambling transactions directly from their banking app or online account.

This page explains how each bank's gambling block works, how to activate it, and what else you can do if you want to limit or stop your gambling spending in Ireland.

How to Activate the Bank of Ireland Gambling Block

  1. Log in to Bank of Ireland 365 Online or the Bank of Ireland mobile app
  2. Go to My Accounts → select your card → Card Controls or Spending Controls
  3. Find the Gambling category and toggle it to block
  4. The block applies immediately to debit and credit card transactions at gambling merchants
  5. If you cannot find the option, call Bank of Ireland at 0818 365 365 and request the block by phone
💡 Important: A bank gambling block stops card payments to gambling sites but does not block other payment methods such as e-wallets, cryptocurrency, or bank transfers in some cases. For comprehensive blocking, combine it with the national self-exclusion register.

How to Activate the AIB Gambling Block

  1. Open the AIB Mobile Banking app
  2. Go to Cards → select your card → Card Controls
  3. Toggle off Gambling & Lottery to block all transactions in this category
  4. The change takes effect immediately

How to Activate the Revolut Gambling Block

  1. Open the Revolut app
  2. Tap your Card → Control your card
  3. Toggle off Gambling under spending categories
  4. Revolut's block is widely used as it covers a broad range of online gambling merchants

Gambling Block vs Self-Exclusion — What's the Difference?

A bank gambling block prevents card payments but does not stop you from visiting gambling websites or using other payment methods. Self-exclusion is a stronger step — it removes your ability to access gambling accounts directly.

For the most effective protection, use both: activate your bank's gambling block and register with Ireland's national self-exclusion scheme through the GRAI.
