Can You Bet on Your Phone in Ireland? Mobile Betting Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Yes — mobile betting is fully legal in Ireland and accounts for the majority of online bets placed at GRAI-licensed bookmakers. You can bet via a dedicated app, through a mobile website, or via a progressive web app. Here is everything Irish punters need to know about mobile betting in 2026.

Apps vs Mobile Browser: Which to Use

Dedicated app: Usually offers the best in-play experience, push notifications for price changes and bet settlement, and faster loading because it caches content locally. Preferred for regular betting.

Mobile browser: No download required. Works on any device. Some operators offer mobile-optimised sites that are almost as fast as their apps. Good if you bet occasionally or do not want to install multiple apps.

Progressive Web App (PWA): A middle ground — a web page you add to your home screen that behaves like an app. Used by operators who have not pursued App Store approval. Functionally equivalent to a native app for most betting purposes.

iOS vs Android: Key Differences

iOS: Apple requires gambling apps to have country-specific approval. Some GRAI-licensed operators have App Store apps; others use PWAs. Apple Pay is natively integrated on iOS for seamless deposits.

Android: More permissive for gambling apps. Most operators offer Android APKs directly from their website if not on Google Play. Google Pay works for deposits on Android.

Data Usage and Connectivity

In-play betting apps, particularly those with live video streaming, can use significant mobile data. A live-streamed Premier League match can use 300–500MB per hour. If you are on a limited data plan, prefer Wi-Fi for streaming; in-play betting without streaming uses minimal data (under 10MB per hour).

Mobile Responsible Gambling Tools

Under GRAI requirements, all responsible gambling tools available on desktop must also be accessible on mobile. You can set deposit limits, request cooling-off periods, and access self-exclusion from within any GRAI-licensed mobile app or site.

How to Download a Betting App in Ireland

  1. iOS: Search the App Store for the site's official app, or tap the app download link on the site's mobile page. Some Irish betting apps are available directly in the App Store; others redirect to a dedicated download page.
  2. Android: Google Play Store restricts gambling apps in some regions. Download the APK file from the site's official 'Download App' page. Go to Settings → Security → Allow unknown sources (or equivalent on your device), then install the APK file.
  3. Verify it's official: Only download from the site's own domain — third-party APK sites may contain modified or malicious versions.

Mobile Betting Features to Look For

  • Live in-play: Real-time markets updating during the event — essential for in-play bettors.
  • Cash out: Settle a bet early for a guaranteed return — available in the app before the event ends.

  • Responsible gambling tools: Deposit limits, session time reminders, and self-exclusion access — all accessible within the app under 'My Account' or 'Responsible Gambling'.
Is mobile betting legal in Ireland?

Yes — fully legal. Mobile betting at GRAI-licensed operators is legal and regulated under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Mobile accounts for the majority of online bets in Ireland.

Do I need to download an app to bet on my phone in Ireland?

No — you can bet through your mobile browser on any GRAI-licensed bookmaker's mobile site. Apps provide a better in-play experience but are not required.

What is a Progressive Web App for betting?

A mobile website that you add to your home screen, behaving like a native app. Used by operators who have not pursued App Store approval. Functionally equivalent for most betting purposes.

Does mobile betting use a lot of data?

In-play betting without streaming uses very little data (under 10MB/hour). Live video streaming uses 300–500MB/hour. Use Wi-Fi for streaming; mobile data is fine for odds-only in-play betting.

Are GRAI responsible gambling tools available on mobile?

Yes — GRAI rules require all responsible gambling tools (deposit limits, self-exclusion, cooling-off) to be accessible on mobile. Any GRAI-licensed app or mobile site must include these.
