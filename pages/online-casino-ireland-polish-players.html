Online Casino in Ireland for Polish Players: What You Need to Know

Ireland is home to around 120,000 Polish-born residents, making Poles one of the largest communities in the country. If you have moved to Ireland from Poland and want to play at an online casino, the rules are very different from what you were used to back home. The short version: once you live in Ireland, Irish law governs your gambling activity — not Polish law. That means no gambling tax on winnings, access to international casinos, and straightforward euro payments via Revolut or your Irish bank card.

Irish Law Applies, Not Polish Law

Poland runs one of the most restrictive online gambling regimes in the EU. Online casino games are a state monopoly (Total Casino), and playing at unlicensed foreign sites from Polish territory is prohibited for players. Poland also maintains a register of blocked domains.

None of that applies to you once you are resident in Ireland. Irish law is based on where you are physically located and resident, not on your citizenship or country of birth. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 — the foundation of Ireland's new regulatory framework — places the licensing obligation on operators, not players. As an Irish resident, you are legally free to play at any casino licensed by a reputable regulator such as the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) or the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) that accepts Irish customers.

Poland's domain-blocking register and player prohibitions are tools of Polish territorial law. They cover gambling activity taking place from Polish territory. If your connection and residence are in Dublin, Cork or Galway, you are outside the jurisdiction of Polish gambling regulation.

💡 Key Legal Takeaway

As an Irish resident, your gambling rights are defined by the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and overseen by GRAI (the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland). Polish gambling restrictions do not follow you across the border.

GRAI Licensing: What Polish Players Should Know

The Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) is Ireland's new gambling regulator, established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. GRAI began issuing remote betting licences in July 2026 — these cover sports betting only. As of November 2026, no GRAI casino licence has been issued anywhere in Ireland. Casino licences are a separate category and are expected to follow in 2027–2028.

What this means for Polish players in Ireland:

  • Any casino claiming to hold a "GRAI casino licence" is making a false claim — zero have been issued.
