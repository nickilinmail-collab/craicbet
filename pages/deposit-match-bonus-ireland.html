Deposit Match Bonus Ireland: How It Works

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A deposit match bonus doubles your deposit up to a set limit. If you deposit €100 with a 100% match up to €200, you receive €100 bonus on top of your €100 deposit. However, the bonus comes with wagering requirements that determine its real value.

How a Deposit Match Bonus Works

Step 1: Deposit the qualifying amount. Step 2: Bonus is credited (usually as bonus funds, not withdrawable cash). Step 3: Wager the bonus (and sometimes deposit) the required number of times across qualifying games. Step 4: Once wagering is met, remaining balance converts to withdrawable cash.

Deposit Match Bonus: What Wagering Requirement Means

A 30× wagering requirement on the bonus means you must stake the bonus amount 30 times before it converts to cash. At typical slot RTP of 96%, each €100 wagered loses approximately €4 on average. To wager €3,000 at 96% RTP costs approximately €120 in expected losses. The €100 bonus received is worth less than the €120 expected wagering cost — meaning the bonus has negative expected value at 30× wagering on most standard slots.

When Deposit Match Bonuses Have Positive Value

  • Wagering requirement is 10× or below — rarely offered
  • High-RTP games (98%+) contribute 100% — reduces expected wagering loss
  • No deposit or bonus funds required for wagering — only the bonus portion
  • No-wagering bonuses (bonus converts directly to cash) — best case scenario
