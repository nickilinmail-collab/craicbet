Betting Site Comparison Ireland 2026: How to Compare GRAI-Licensed Bookmakers

Updated: November 2026 • Reading time: 11 min
Quick Summary: Comparing betting sites goes beyond welcome bonuses. This guide gives you a structured framework — six core areas — to evaluate any GRAI-licensed bookmaker and find the right fit for how you bet. No bookmaker scores best on every dimension; the best site for you depends on your specific priorities.

The Six-Area Comparison Framework

When comparing Irish betting sites, assess each site across these six areas:

  1. Licence and regulatory standing
  2. Odds quality and margin
  3. Market depth and coverage
  4. Mobile experience and app quality
  5. Payments for Irish customers
  6. Responsible gambling toolkit

Weighting these depends on your usage pattern. A professional bettor placing 100 bets a month weights odds quality heavily. An occasional weekend bettor may care more about mobile UX and payment speed. Someone with concerns about gambling habits should weight RG tools above all else.

2. Odds Quality and Margin

Convert the odds for the same event across multiple sites into implied probabilities and compare. For a football match with three outcomes, the site with the lowest total implied probability across all three outcomes is offering the best value.

Typical margins by sport:

  • Football (major leagues): 5–8%
  • Horse racing: 15–25% (pre-race); much higher in-running
  • Tennis (ATP/WTA major events): 4–6%
  • Golf (each-way markets): 15–20%

Sites that consistently offer margins at the lower end of these ranges are better value over time.

3. Market Depth and Coverage

Coverage matters as much as headline odds. Consider:

  • Irish sports: GAA (all grades), League of Ireland, Irish horse racing — does the site offer county-level GAA and lower-division horse racing markets, or only the headline events?
  • Live betting: how many markets stay open in-play? Can you cash out in-play? How does the platform behave during high-traffic events?
  • Bet builder / same-game multis: available on which sports and competitions?
  • Niche sports: Rugby union (Pro14/United Rugby Championship), darts, snooker, cricket, MMA — assess coverage for the sports you actually bet on.

4. Mobile Experience

Key questions when evaluating mobile:

  • Is there a dedicated iOS and Android app, or is it mobile browser only?
  • How many steps does it take to place a bet from the app home screen?
  • Does it remain fast and stable during major events (Grand National, Champions League finals)?
  • Is cash-out available on mobile and does it work reliably?

5. Payments for Irish Customers

Key comparison points on payments:

  • Revolut acceptance (not all sites treat Revolut as a debit card — some block it)
  • Withdrawal processing time (same day, 1–3 business days, or longer?)
  • Minimum withdrawal amount
  • Withdrawal method options (must you withdraw to the same method you deposited?)

6. Responsible Gambling Tools

All GRAI-licensed sites must offer deposit limits, time limits, reality checks, cooling-off and self-exclusion. Beyond compliance, assess:

  • How easy are the tools to access? (Account settings, number of clicks)
  • Is Gambling Awareness Trust contact (1800 753 753) prominently displayed?
  • Does the site have a proactive safer gambling team (or just a tick-box compliance approach)?

Quick Odds Comparison Method

  • Pick any Premier League match at the weekend and check the 1X2 odds on two sites
  • Add the implied probabilities (1 ÷ each decimal odd) — the lower the total, the lower the margin
  • A total of 1.055 = 5.5% margin; 1.08 = 8% margin — 2.5 percentage points difference adds up over a season
  • BOG (best odds guaranteed) on horse racing is a free upgrade — always use it when available
