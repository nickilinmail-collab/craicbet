Lucky 31 Calculator (5 Selections, 31 Bets)

What is a Lucky 31 bet?

A Lucky 31 is a full-cover multiple bet using 5 selections. It gets its name from the total number of bets — 31. Because singles are included, one winning selection is enough to get a return. The bet is popular for horse racing, particularly festival days when punters have five fancied runners across different races. At €1 per bet the total outlay is €31; serious festival punters often use €0.50 or €0.25 unit stakes to manage costs while still covering all five selections.

Lucky 31 vs Lucky 15: scaling up

A Lucky 15 uses 4 selections and 15 bets. A Lucky 31 adds a fifth selection, which more than doubles the bet count to 31. The extra selection creates 16 more combinations (5 more singles, 4 more doubles, 6 more trebles, 4 more four-folds, 1 five-fold). The key difference in practice: a Lucky 31 rewards moderate strike rates more generously because there are more winning combinations available. If you back five 3/1 shots and three win, the return on a Lucky 31 significantly outperforms a Lucky 15 on four of those selections.

Lucky 31 example

Say you back five horses at odds of 3.0, 4.0, 2.5, 5.0 and 3.5 (decimal) with a €0.50 unit stake (total outlay: €15.50). Three of the five win — selections 1, 2 and 5. Winning bets: 3 singles (1, 2, 5), 3 doubles (1×2, 1×5, 2×5) and 1 treble (1×2×5). Calculate each: single 1 = €1.50, single 2 = €2.00, single 5 = €1.75, double 1×2 = €6.00, double 1×5 = €5.25, double 2×5 = €7.00, treble = €21.00. Total return: €44.50 — a profit of €29 from a €15.50 outlay. Use the calculator above for instant results on any odds.

One-winner bonuses on Lucky 31

A common feature with named bets in Ireland is a one-winner consolation. If only one of your five selections wins, some bookmakers will pay the single at enhanced odds — often double the SP (starting price) or a fixed bonus such as 25% extra on the return. This materially changes the value of a Lucky 31 when you have one unlucky afternoon. Always check the specific bonus terms of the bookmaker you are using before placing, as the consolation rules vary. The calculator above shows the standard return without any bonus applied.

Frequently asked questions

How many bets in a Lucky 31?

31 bets from 5 selections: 5 singles, 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-folds and 1 five-fold.

How much does a €1 Lucky 31 cost?

€31 total — 31 bets at €1 each. A €0.50 unit stake costs €15.50.

Do I get a return with one winner on a Lucky 31?

Yes — the winning single pays out. Many Irish bookmakers also offer a one-winner consolation bonus. Check the terms before placing.

What is the difference between a Lucky 31 and a Lucky 15?

Lucky 31 uses 5 selections (31 bets); Lucky 15 uses 4 selections (15 bets). Lucky 31 offers more coverage but costs more than twice as much.

What is the difference between a Lucky 31 and a Canadian?

A Canadian (Super Yankee) uses 5 selections but has 26 bets — no singles. Lucky 31 adds 5 singles (31 total), so one winner still returns something.
