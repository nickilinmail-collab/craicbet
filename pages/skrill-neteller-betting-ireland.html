Skrill & Neteller Betting Ireland 2026 — E-Wallet Deposits, Withdrawals & Bonuses

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Skrill and Neteller are the two most widely supported e-wallets at betting sites available to Irish players. Both offer fast deposits and withdrawals, keep gambling transactions off your bank statement, and are unaffected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban. This guide explains how each works, how they compare, and what Irish punters need to know in 2026.

Skrill for Betting in Ireland

Skrill (formerly Moneybookers) is a digital wallet based in the UK and regulated by the FCA. It works as a separate account from your bank: you load funds into Skrill via bank transfer or debit card, then use the Skrill balance to fund betting accounts. Withdrawals go back to Skrill first, then to your bank.

  • Deposits: Instant once Skrill is funded. Minimum typically €1–5.
  • Withdrawals: Back to Skrill wallet quickly (hours, not days). Transfer to bank typically 1–3 business days.
  • Fees: Skrill charges a fee to transfer from wallet to bank account (typically 1.45%+). Deposits to bookmakers are usually free.
  • Bonus eligibility: Many bookmakers exclude Skrill (and Neteller) deposits from welcome offer eligibility. Check T&Cs before depositing via Skrill to claim a bonus.
  • Verification: Skrill requires identity verification before allowing withdrawals.

Neteller for Betting in Ireland

Neteller is owned by the same parent company as Skrill (Paysafe Group) and works similarly — a separate wallet you fund and use across betting sites. Neteller also issues a Net+ Mastercard prepaid card that lets you spend your Neteller balance directly.

  • Deposits: Instant. Neteller is accepted at a wide range of betting sites available to Irish customers.
  • Withdrawals: Back to Neteller wallet quickly; Net+ card available for spending.
  • Fees: Similar to Skrill — wallet-to-bank transfers carry a fee. Check current Neteller fee schedule.
  • Bonus eligibility: Same issue as Skrill — many operators exclude Neteller from welcome offers. Use debit card for bonus activation if needed.

Are Skrill and Neteller Affected by the Credit Card Ban?

No. Ireland's credit card gambling ban applies to credit cards, not to e-wallets. Skrill and Neteller are classified as electronic money instruments (like a prepaid account), not credit products. Deposits via Skrill or Neteller at Irish-facing bookmakers are not affected by the ban. However, if you fund your Skrill or Neteller wallet using a credit card, that may be blocked at the wallet level — Skrill and Neteller have their own policies on this.

Practical Tip: Use Debit for Bonuses, E-Wallet for Privacy

The most practical approach for Irish punters: use your debit card for your first deposit at a new bookmaker (to maximise welcome bonus eligibility), then switch to Skrill or Neteller for subsequent deposits if you want your gambling transactions kept off your bank statement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Skrill work at betting sites in Ireland?

Yes. Skrill is accepted at a range of betting sites available to Irish customers. Note that many bookmakers exclude Skrill (and Neteller) deposits from welcome offer eligibility — check the bonus terms before depositing via Skrill if you want to claim a new customer offer.

Does Neteller work for betting in Ireland?

Yes. Neteller is widely accepted at betting and casino sites available to Irish players. Like Skrill, Neteller deposits are frequently excluded from welcome offers — use a debit card for your first deposit if you want bonus eligibility. Neteller's Net+ prepaid Mastercard lets you spend your wallet balance directly without transferring to a bank.

Are Skrill and Neteller affected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban?

No. Ireland's credit card gambling ban applies to credit cards specifically. Skrill and Neteller are e-money wallets and are not classified as credit products — they are unaffected by the ban. If you fund your Skrill or Neteller wallet using a credit card, that is a separate transaction subject to the wallet's own policies.

Which is better for betting in Ireland — Skrill or Neteller?

Both offer similar functionality — instant deposits, fast withdrawals, transactions kept off your main bank statement. Neteller has the advantage of the Net+ prepaid Mastercard for spending winnings. Skrill may have slightly wider acceptance at some bookmakers. The practical advice: use a debit card for your first deposit to claim welcome offers, then use whichever e-wallet suits you.
