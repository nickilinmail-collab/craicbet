Betting with Revolut in Ireland: What You Need to Know in 2026

Does Revolut work for online betting in Ireland?

Revolut is one of the most widely used digital payment methods in Ireland, with millions of Irish accounts. For betting, Revolut functions as a Visa or Mastercard depending on your card type, which means it is accepted at any GRAI-licensed bookmaker that accepts Visa or Mastercard deposits. In practice, most major licensed Irish bookmakers accept Revolut deposits, though success rates depend on Revolut’s own gambling transaction settings.

Revolut’s gambling controls

Revolut includes in-app controls allowing users to block gambling transactions. If you have this setting enabled in your Revolut app, gambling deposits will be declined. To check: open the Revolut app, go to Settings › Security › Spending controls. If the gambling category is blocked, toggle it off to allow transactions. Revolut also occasionally flags gambling transactions for security review, which can cause temporary delays.

Revolut withdrawal and processing times

Withdrawals from betting sites to Revolut typically process within 1-3 working days, the same as standard Visa/Mastercard withdrawals. Some operators process same-day withdrawals to cards; others have a 24-hour processing window before the funds appear in your Revolut balance. If a withdrawal is delayed beyond your operator’s stated processing time, contact their support team.

Alternatives if Revolut is blocked

If Revolut declines a transaction and you do not wish to change the in-app settings, alternatives include: a traditional Irish bank debit card (AIB, Bank of Ireland), PayPal (accepted by most major operators), or a dedicated prepaid card for gambling. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Revolut to deposit at Irish betting sites?

Yes, in most cases. Revolut cards (Visa or Mastercard) are accepted at the majority of GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers. Transactions may be declined if Revolut’s in-app gambling block is enabled in your account settings.

How do I enable gambling transactions on Revolut?

Open the Revolut app, go to Settings (profile icon) then Security or Spending controls. Find the Gambling category and toggle it on to allow gambling transactions. The specific menu path may vary slightly with app updates.

How long do Revolut betting withdrawals take?

Revolut withdrawals from betting sites typically take 1-3 working days, the same as other Visa/Mastercard withdrawals. Some operators process withdrawals faster — check your operator’s stated withdrawal processing time for card payments.
