All-Ireland Final Weekend Betting 2026: GAA August Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The All-Ireland GAA Finals weekend in August is the most watched domestic sporting event in Ireland. The All-Ireland hurling final and football final are played at Croke Park in Dublin, attracting a combined attendance of over 160,000 across two days and enormous national betting interest.

All-Ireland Hurling Final

The All-Ireland hurling final is traditionally played on the first Sunday in August. It is one of the most prestigious sporting events in Ireland, with the two provincial champions or best qualifier sides competing for the Liam MacCarthy Cup. Hurling finals are frequently decided by narrow margins and are among the highest-scoring GAA games of the season.

All-Ireland Football Final

The All-Ireland football final takes place two to three weeks after the hurling final, in mid to late August. It is the most bet-on domestic sporting event in Ireland each year. Match betting, handicap, first scorer and in-play markets all trade at peak volume.

Betting Markets for Finals Weekend

  • Match betting (1X2): Finals can go to replays; check the specific settlement rules
  • Handicap: When one side is a strong favourite, the handicap market offers better value
  • First scorer / anytime scorer: Top players in both games attract significant betting
  • Winning margin: Predict the margin of victory
  • In-play: Full in-play trading on all final fixtures

When is the All-Ireland GAA Final?

The All-Ireland hurling final is traditionally on the first Sunday in August. The football final follows two to three weeks later. Exact dates are confirmed by the GAA.

Can the All-Ireland final go to a replay?

The All-Ireland finals can go to extra time, but replays were abolished in 2022. All matches are settled on the day via extra time and, if necessary, a penalty shootout.

What is the capacity of Croke Park?

Croke Park has a capacity of approximately 82,300, making it one of the largest sports stadiums in Europe. It is sold out for All-Ireland finals.

How do I find the best odds for the All-Ireland final?
