Underage Betting Account Discovered in Ireland: Operator Obligations

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

If a GRAI-licensed bookmaker discovers that a customer was under 18 when they opened an account, specific legal obligations are triggered. All bets must be voided, all deposits returned, and the account closed. Here is what happens in detail.

The Operator’s Legal Obligations

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, allowing underage gambling is a serious licence breach. When an operator discovers or is notified that a customer was underage when they opened an account, they must:

  • Immediately close the account
  • Void all bets placed during the period of underage use
  • Return all deposits made to the original payment method
  • Not retain any winnings generated during the underage period
  • Report the breach to the GRAI as a compliance incident

How Operators Discover Underage Accounts

Most commonly: through ID verification revealing a date of birth showing the customer was under 18 at account creation. Sometimes through reports from parents or family members. Occasionally through GRAI audits. Under GRAI rules, operators must conduct KYC on all new accounts and must age-verify before any gambling activity is permitted.

What the Customer Receives

All deposits must be returned, regardless of whether they were used for bets. Winnings from bets are not returned — the bets are voided. For example, if a customer deposited €100, bet it all and won €500, the €100 deposit is returned but the €500 is not, because the bets were void.

Family Members or Guardians

If a parent or guardian discovers their child has opened a betting account, they should report it to the operator directly (and provide evidence of age). The operator’s compliance obligations then apply as above. Parents can also report ongoing concerns to the GRAI at grai.ie.

How Bookmakers Verify Age in Ireland

  • All GRAI-licensed bookmakers must verify age before allowing any bets or deposits
  • Methods: passport, driving licence, national ID card — uploaded during registration (KYC)
  • Passive checks: name and date of birth against credit reference databases
  • Enhanced verification triggered by withdrawal requests over a threshold
  • If in doubt about age verification, contact the operator — it's not an optional step

What happens if an underage betting account is found in Ireland?

The operator must void all bets, return all deposits, close the account, and report to the GRAI. This is a legal requirement under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024.

Does an underage bettor keep their winnings?

No — bets are voided, so winnings are not paid. Only deposits made are returned. The operator cannot keep the deposits either.

What should a parent do if their child opened a betting account?

Report it to the operator immediately with proof of age. The operator must close the account, void all bets, and return all deposits. You can also report concerns to the GRAI at grai.ie.

Can a GRAI-licensed operator be fined for allowing underage betting?

Yes — allowing underage gambling is a serious licence breach. The GRAI can investigate and impose sanctions including fines and licence suspension.

What is the minimum age to bet in Ireland?

18. This applies to all forms of gambling — online betting, casino, in-person shops, and lotteries — under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024.
