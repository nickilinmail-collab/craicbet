How to Set Deposit Limits on Irish Betting Sites: Step-by-Step

Updated: November 2026 • Reading time: 8 min
Quick Summary: Deposit limits are one of the most effective responsible gambling tools available. Under GRAI rules, all licensed Irish betting and casino sites must offer them. Reductions take effect immediately; increases require a waiting period of at least 24 hours. This guide walks you through the process and explains the rules.

What Are Deposit Limits?

A deposit limit restricts the total amount of money you can transfer into your gambling account over a specified period — daily, weekly or monthly. Once you hit the limit, the site blocks further deposits until the period resets.

Deposit limits are distinct from loss limits (which cap how much you can lose) and spending limits (which cap total wagers). Some sites offer all three; deposit limits are the most universally available because they are the most straightforward to implement and enforce.

Setting a deposit limit does not affect your ability to withdraw funds, play with existing balance, or manage your account. It only blocks new money going in beyond your chosen amount.

GRAI Rules on Deposit Limits

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, all GRAI-licensed operators are required to:

  • Offer deposit limit tools to all customers.
  • Apply reductions immediately — there is no delay on lowering a limit.
  • Apply a cooling-off period (minimum 24 hours) before increases take effect, giving customers a chance to reconsider.
  • Not process deposits that would exceed a customer's self-set limit.
  • Not increase limits on behalf of customers without an active customer request.

Sites that offer deposit limits beyond the minimum (such as 72-hour cooling-off periods on increases) are going above the regulatory floor — this is a positive sign when evaluating a site's responsible gambling credentials.

How to Set Deposit Limits: General Steps

The exact path varies slightly by site, but the process is broadly consistent across GRAI-licensed operators:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Go to your account settings or profile (usually accessed via a user icon or your name in the top-right corner).
  3. Find the Responsible Gambling, Safer Gambling, or Player Protections section. This may be directly in account settings, or in a dedicated dropdown.
  4. Select Deposit Limits.
  5. Set your preferred daily, weekly, and/or monthly limit. Enter the maximum amount in euro you want to be able to deposit in each period.
  6. Confirm the change. Reductions apply immediately. You may receive an email confirmation.

Tips for Setting Effective Limits

  • Set limits before you start a session, not during one when you may be in a heightened state.
  • Start conservative: it is easy to request an increase later (with a 24-hour wait); it is harder to enforce discipline if you set a limit too high to be meaningful.
  • Think monthly first: decide on a total monthly entertainment budget for gambling, then divide by 4 for a weekly limit and by 30 for a daily limit.
  • Set limits on every site you use, not just your primary one. Limits on one site don't automatically apply to another.
  • Write down your limits and review them every three months. If you are regularly hitting your limit, it may be worth reducing it further.

Beyond Deposit Limits: Other Tools

Deposit limits are most effective when combined with other responsible gambling tools:

  • Time limits / reality checks: notifications after a set session duration reminding you how long you've been playing and how much you've spent.
  • Loss limits: cap the amount you can lose in a session, day or week.
  • Cool-off / take-a-break: temporary account suspension for 24 hours, a week or a month.
  • Self-exclusion: longer-term or permanent account suspension. See our self-exclusion guide for details.
