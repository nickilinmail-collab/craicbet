Revolut Blocking Gambling Transactions in Ireland: Why It Happens

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Revolut's gambling block is one of the most common reasons Irish bettors see a declined deposit. This guide explains why Revolut blocks gambling transactions, how to toggle the setting, and what to do when Revolut still won't work.

Why Revolut Blocks Gambling Payments

Revolut applies a merchant category block to gambling transactions. This is a risk-management feature built into the Revolut app — not a decision by the bookmaker. When the block is active, any card transaction sent to a gambling merchant code (MCC 7995) is automatically declined before it reaches the bookmaker's payment processor.

Revolut introduced default-on gambling blocks across many account types as part of its responsible gambling approach. The block applies to both physical Revolut cards and standard virtual cards. Disposable virtual cards may behave differently — see below.

This is separate from Ireland's credit card gambling ban, which applies to credit cards only. Revolut's standard debit card is not a credit card, so the ban does not affect it — but Revolut's own block still might.

How to Enable Gambling in Revolut Settings

Disabling Revolut's gambling block takes about 30 seconds in the app:

  1. Open the Revolut app and tap your profile icon (top left).
  2. Go to Security & privacy.
  3. Tap Merchant categories.
  4. Find Gambling and toggle it on (green means permitted).
  5. Retry your deposit — it should go through instantly.

If you cannot find this menu, your app may need updating. The exact path has varied across app versions; searching "gambling" in the Revolut app's search bar will also locate the toggle.

When Revolut Still Declines (Merchant Category)

Even with the gambling toggle enabled, some deposits may still fail. Common reasons include:

  • Disposable virtual card: Revolut's disposable virtual cards (single-use) are frequently blocked by bookmakers regardless of your Revolut settings. Switch to your permanent Revolut card (physical or standard virtual) for gambling deposits.
  • Incomplete KYC at Revolut: If your identity verification at Revolut is incomplete or under review, gambling and other high-risk merchant categories may be restricted. Complete Revolut's identity check before attempting a gambling deposit.
  • Bookmaker's own policy: A small number of Irish-facing bookmakers restrict Revolut specifically — this is unusual but does happen. If your Revolut toggle is on and your card is permanent, check the bookmaker's payment FAQ or try a bank transfer to the same bookmaker.
  • Spending limits: If you have set a Revolut spending limit that excludes gambling merchants, that limit will override the category toggle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Revolut blocking my gambling deposit?

Revolut applies a gambling merchant category block by default on some account types. Open the Revolut app → Security & privacy → Merchant categories → Gambling, and toggle it on. This is Revolut's own control, not the bookmaker's. If the toggle is already on, check whether you are using a disposable virtual card — these are frequently declined for gambling merchants.

Does the Irish credit card ban affect Revolut?

No. Ireland's ban on credit card gambling applies to credit cards only. Revolut's standard card (linked to your Revolut balance) is a debit card and is not affected by the ban. Only Revolut's separate credit feature would be caught by the credit card ban.

My Revolut gambling toggle is on but deposits still fail — what next?

Switch from a disposable virtual card to your permanent Revolut card. If that doesn't help, check your Revolut identity verification is complete. If still failing, the bookmaker may have its own Revolut restriction — try a bank transfer or a bank-issued Visa/Mastercard Debit instead.
