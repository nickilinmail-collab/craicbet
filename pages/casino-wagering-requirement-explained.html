Casino Wagering Requirements Explained

Wagering requirements (also called playthrough requirements) are the most important casino bonus term to understand. They determine whether a bonus can realistically convert into withdrawable cash.

What is a Wagering Requirement?

A wagering requirement is a multiplier applied to your bonus amount (sometimes bonus + deposit) that you must bet before withdrawing bonus-related funds. Example: €100 bonus with 30x wagering = €3,000 in bets required before withdrawal.

Bonus-Only vs Deposit+Bonus Wagering

This distinction matters significantly. Bonus-only wagering: 30x on a €100 bonus = €3,000. Deposit+bonus wagering: 30x on €100 deposit + €100 bonus = €6,000. Always check whether the requirement applies to the bonus alone or to the combined deposit+bonus amount.

What Counts as Low, Average, or High Wagering

Under 20x is excellent and uncommon. 20x–30x is good. 30x–40x is average. 40x–60x is high. Over 60x is very high and rarely worth claiming unless the bonus amount is small. Zero-wagering (cashback, real cash) bonuses are the most player-friendly.

The Real Cost of Wagering

At a slot with 96% RTP, the expected loss per €1 wagered is €0.04. To wager €3,000, expected losses = €120. If your bonus was worth €100, you'd expect to lose €20 net on the wagering alone — before considering your own playing variance. Wagering requirements are rarely positive-EV for the player.
