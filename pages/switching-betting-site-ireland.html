Switching Betting Sites in Ireland: How to Move & What to Check

Updated: November 2026 • Reading time: 10 min
Quick Summary: Switching betting sites is straightforward but involves a few important steps — withdrawing your balance, settling pending bets, closing your account correctly, and setting up responsible gambling tools at the new site. This guide covers each step and what to check at the new site before depositing.

Common Reasons to Switch

Irish bettors switch betting sites for a range of practical reasons:

  • Better odds on the sports and markets they bet on most.
  • GRAI licensing now available at a site previously using an EU-only licence.
  • Payment method changes — Revolut support, faster withdrawals.
  • Mobile app improvements at competing sites.
  • Better responsible gambling tools or customer support quality.
  • Account restrictions or limitations imposed by the current site (stake restrictions, reduced limits).

Whatever the reason, the switch is typically straightforward if done in the right order.

Before You Close Your Old Account

  1. Withdraw your entire balance. Do not leave funds in an account you are about to close. Request a withdrawal and confirm it has cleared to your bank or Revolut before proceeding.
  2. Wait for pending bets to settle. Any unsettled bets should be allowed to run to their conclusion. Closing an account with active bets can complicate payout processing.
  3. Use up any free bets or bonus funds. These expire on account closure. Use them on a qualifying market before you close — but do not place bets purely to use bonus funds if the market or odds are unsuitable.
  4. Download your bet history if you want records. Some sites allow you to export your wagering history. This can be useful for personal record-keeping, particularly if you track results for tax purposes or personal analysis.

How to Close Your Account

At most GRAI-licensed sites, the process is:

  1. Log in and go to Account Settings.
  2. Find "Close Account," "Deactivate Account," or "Request Account Closure."
  3. If the option is not available directly, contact customer support (live chat or email) and explicitly request account closure. Keep a copy of your request.
  4. Confirm you have no pending bets or outstanding balance when making the request.

GRAI-licensed operators must action account closure requests promptly. If a site delays, refuses, or creates friction around closing your account, file a complaint via GRAI's formal complaints process.

Important: Self-Exclusion and Account Closure Are Different

Closing an account is not the same as self-excluding. If you close an account for responsible gambling reasons, you should also formally self-exclude — otherwise the site may allow you to re-register. If you have a NGER (National Gambling Exclusion Register) registration in place, that continues regardless of account status and will block re-registration at all participating GRAI-licensed operators for the duration of the exclusion.

Setting Up at the New Site

  1. Verify the licence at grai.ie before registering.
  2. Complete KYC during registration if prompted. Having ID and proof of address ready avoids withdrawal delays later.
  3. Set responsible gambling tools immediately — deposit limits, session time limits and reality check intervals — before making your first deposit. This should be the first thing you do, not an afterthought.
  4. Check the welcome bonus terms before depositing if you plan to claim an offer. Minimum deposit, eligible markets, minimum odds and wagering requirements all affect real value.
  5. Make a test withdrawal before depositing significant funds. This confirms the payment process works as expected and flags any verification issues before they delay a larger withdrawal.
