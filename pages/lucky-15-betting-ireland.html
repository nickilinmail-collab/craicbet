Lucky 15 Betting Guide for Ireland

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A Lucky 15 covers 4 selections in 15 individual bets — 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold accumulator. It is one of the most popular full-cover bets in Ireland, particularly for horse racing, because the inclusion of singles means even 1 winner gets you a return. At €1 per bet the total stake is €15.

Lucky 15 Bet Breakdown

Here is exactly what a Lucky 15 contains:

Bet typeNumber of betsHow it works
Singles4Each selection wins alone
Doubles6Every combination of 2 from 4
Trebles4Every combination of 3 from 4
Four-fold accumulator1All 4 must win
Total15—

The maths: choosing 2 from 4 gives 4×3÷2 = 6 doubles. Choosing 3 from 4 gives 4 trebles. The name “Lucky 15” comes from the 15 total bets, while “Lucky” refers to the bonus rules most bookmakers attach.

Lucky 15 Bonus Rules

The word “Lucky” in Lucky 15 refers specifically to the consolation bonuses that most bookmakers apply:

  • One winner consolation: Most bookmakers pay double the odds on your single winner if that is the only selection to win. So if your 1 winner was 3/1, you receive a return as if the odds were 6/1. Terms vary — always check before placing.
  • All four winners bonus: If all 4 selections win, some bookmakers add a percentage bonus (typically 10–25%) to your overall return. Again, terms differ by operator.
  • All losers consolation: Some books pay a small consolation if all 4 lose (rarely offered).

These bonuses make the Lucky 15 more punter-friendly than a straight Yankee on difficult racing days. The bonus for a sole winner can turn a loss into a small profit depending on the price.

Worked Example: Horse Racing Lucky 15

Suppose you pick 4 horses at €1 per bet (€15 total stake), and 2 of them win:

  • Horse 1: 4/1 → decimal 5.0 (wins)
  • Horse 2: 7/2 → decimal 4.5 (wins)
  • Horse 3: 3/1 → decimal 4.0 (loses)
  • Horse 4: 5/2 → decimal 3.5 (loses)

With 2 winners you collect from: 2 singles (H1 and H2) and 1 double (H1×H2). The trebles and four-fold all require 3 or 4 winners and are lost. Single H1 returns: €1 × 5.0 = €5.00. Single H2 returns: €1 × 4.5 = €4.50. Double H1×H2 returns: €1 × 5.0 × 4.5 = €22.50. Total return: €32.00 on a €15 stake — a profit of €17.00. If only 1 winner had landed, the consolation double-odds rule would have softened the blow.

The Yankee costs €4 less per €1 unit stake because it drops the 4 singles. That saves money, but means you need at least 2 winners before seeing any return. For punters who back longer-priced horses where 1 winner on a bad card is realistic, the Lucky 15's singles component and consolation bonus give meaningful insurance.

Why Lucky 15s Are Popular in Ireland

Horse racing culture in Ireland is deeper than almost anywhere else in the world. Festival meetings like Cheltenham, Punchestown and the Galway Races produce natural Lucky 15 betting patterns — punters identify four fancies across a card, build a Lucky 15, and the singles mean even a tricky card where only one horse wins still generates a return (and potentially a doubled-odds bonus). The structure is also well understood by betting shop staff across Ireland, making it easy to place in person or by phone as well as online.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bets is a Lucky 15?

A Lucky 15 consists of 15 bets from 4 selections: 4 singles, 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold accumulator.

What is the Lucky 15 bonus rule?

Most bookmakers apply a consolation bonus if only 1 of your 4 selections wins — typically double the odds on that single. Some also pay a bonus if all 4 selections win, adding a percentage to the four-fold return. Terms vary by bookmaker, so check before placing.

What is the difference between a Lucky 15 and a Yankee?

Both cover 4 selections. A Yankee has 11 bets (6 doubles, 4 trebles, 1 four-fold) with no singles. A Lucky 15 adds 4 singles to become 15 bets — meaning even 1 winner gets you a return.

How much does a €1 Lucky 15 cost?

A Lucky 15 at €1 per bet costs €15 in total. At 50c per bet it costs €7.50.

Is a Lucky 15 good for horse racing?

Yes — Lucky 15s are particularly popular in Ireland for horse racing. The singles component means even one winner returns something, the bonus for a sole winner softens the blow on difficult cards, and four selections across a Cheltenham or Punchestown card is a natural fit.
