Both Teams to Score Betting: Statistics, Strategy & Best Markets

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

What is BTTS betting?

Both teams to score (BTTS) is a market predicting whether both teams in a match will score at least one goal each, regardless of the final result. If both teams score, BTTS Yes wins. If either team fails to score, BTTS No wins. The market is binary with no draw outcome, making it one of the most straightforward football betting markets. BTTS is available pre-match and in-play on most football fixtures.

BTTS statistics by league

BTTS Yes lands in roughly 50-55% of Premier League matches, slightly less in La Liga (around 48-52%), and higher in leagues with weaker defences such as the Bundesliga (sometimes 55-60%). These rates vary year to year. The key inputs are both teams’ goals-scored and goals-conceded averages — a fixture between two teams each averaging over 1.5 goals per game and over 1.0 conceded is a natural BTTS candidate.

When BTTS is most defensible

BTTS Yes is most defensible when both teams have scored in a high proportion of their recent matches, have porous defences and are playing in a fixture with no major defensive motivation. BTTS No is defensible when at least one team has a strong defensive record (keeping multiple clean sheets), is facing a low-scoring opponent, or has specific motivation to protect a lead. Neither side of the market eliminates the bookmaker’s margin.

BTTS in accumulators

BTTS accumulators are popular because each leg offers a near-50/50 proposition at odds of roughly 1.7-1.9 per leg. However, combining six BTTS legs at 1.75 gives combined odds of around 28/1, with a true probability closer to 60/1 once the margin on each leg is accounted for. Frequently Asked Questions

How often does BTTS Yes land in the Premier League?

BTTS Yes lands in approximately 50-55% of Premier League matches, though this varies by season and the specific teams involved. Teams with high-scoring averages and leaky defences produce the highest BTTS Yes rates; those with strong defensive records tend to produce more BTTS No results.

What odds is BTTS typically priced at?

BTTS Yes is typically priced at 1.70-1.90 (evens to 9/10) depending on the fixture. BTTS No is priced at 1.90-2.20 in most fixtures. The combined implied probability of both options typically sums to more than 100%, reflecting the bookmaker's margin.

Can I combine BTTS with a match result?

Yes. Some operators offer a combined ‘BTTS and match result’ market, letting you pick, say, Home Win and BTTS Yes in a single selection. This is essentially a scorecast without the specific score and carries a combined margin from both markets.
