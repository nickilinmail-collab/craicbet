SSE Airtricity League Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The SSE Airtricity League Premier Division is the top flight of Irish football, featuring ten clubs competing from March to November. Clubs like Shamrock Rovers, Bohemians, St Patrick's Athletic, Shelbourne, Drogheda United and Derry City provide the main championship and European qualification betting narratives.

Premier Division Structure and Betting Opportunities

The Premier Division operates a full league format with home and away fixtures. The title race, European qualification places (top two typically enter European qualifying), and relegation battle provide betting narratives throughout the long season. The FAI Cup and League Cup run alongside the league, offering additional match betting.

Key Clubs and Betting Narratives

Shamrock Rovers have been the dominant force in Irish football in recent years and are typically the short-priced league title favourites. Bohemians and St Patrick's Athletic regularly challenge for European places. Derry City are a strong force from the northwest. Smaller clubs fight relegation, with the bottom one or two facing the drop.

Betting Markets

  • Outright league winner: Available at the start of the season in February or March
  • Top four (European places): Season-long market
  • Relegation: Which clubs will be relegated
  • Match betting (1X2), over/under goals, both teams to score: Available for all fixtures
  • Top scorer (Golden Boot): Available at major bookmakers

SSE Airtricity League: Key Facts

  • Season runs approximately March to November — summer football, different from European leagues
  • Premier Division: 10 teams, 36-match season (each team plays others 4 times)
  • Shamrock Rovers are the dominant team — 5 consecutive titles 2020–2024
  • Dundalk, Shelbourne, Bohemians, Derry City, St Patrick's Athletic are consistent top-half sides
  • Goals per game average: 2.8–3.2 in recent seasons — higher than many European leagues
  • Broadcast: LOI TV (subscription streaming), RTE Radio Sport, some matches on Virgin Media

When does the SSE Airtricity League season run?

The Premier Division season runs from late February or early March to late October or early November each year, with the FAI Cup final typically in November.

Can I bet in-play on League of Ireland Premier Division matches?

Yes. All major GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry in-play markets for Premier Division matches. Live match betting includes next goal, match winner and total goals markets.

How does the League of Ireland relate to European competition?

The top two or three clubs in the Premier Division qualify for European competition the following season. Clubs enter the UEFA Conference League qualifying rounds, with the possibility of reaching the group stages.
