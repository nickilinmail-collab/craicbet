Why Margin Is the Number That Matters

The bookmaker margin calculator reveals how much profit a bookmaker builds into a set of odds. Enter the odds for all outcomes of an event and it calculates the overround — the percentage above 100% that represents the bookmaker's edge. A margin of 5% means for every €100 bet across all outcomes, the bookmaker expects to keep €5.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bookmaker margin or overround?

The amount by which the implied probabilities of all outcomes exceed 100%. It is the bookmaker's built-in edge on the market.

What is a good betting margin?

5% or under on football 1X2 is competitive. Above 8%, you are paying heavily for every bet.

How do I calculate implied probability?

100 divided by the decimal odds: odds of 2.0 imply 50%.

How to Use the Calculator on Football Accumulators

For an accumulator, calculate the margin on each leg separately. The combined effect compounds: a 5-leg acca where every leg has a 5% margin carries a total effective margin of roughly 1 − (0.95)⁵ = 22.6% on the acca. That is why accumulators represent the highest-margin product at most bookmakers, despite looking like good value due to the big advertised odds.

Price-boosted accas are the exception: when a bookmaker guarantees a minimum enhanced return, the effective margin on that promoted acca may be zero or even negative. The key is to check whether the boosted odds represent genuine enhancement versus what you would compile yourself — paste the individual leg prices into this calculator, then compare to the offered acca price.

Bookmaker Margin Calculator: How to Calculate the Vig

The bookmaker margin (or vig) is the percentage by which the total implied probability of all outcomes exceeds 100%. It represents the bookmaker's built-in profit edge. You can calculate it by converting each set of odds to implied probability, adding them together, and subtracting 100 — the remainder is the margin.
