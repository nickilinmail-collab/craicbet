Bookmaker Margin Calculator: See the Real Price of a Market

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Measure Any Market’s Overround

Why Margin Is the Number That Matters

Add up the implied probabilities (100 ÷ decimal odds) of every outcome in a market. A fair book sums to 100%; everything above that is the bookmaker’s built-in edge. A typical football 1X2 runs 4–7%; racing markets often run far higher. Margin is the real price of betting — and it compounds over every bet you place.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bookmaker margin or overround?

The amount by which the implied probabilities of all outcomes exceed 100%. It is the bookmaker's built-in edge on the market.

What is a good betting margin?

5% or under on football 1X2 is competitive. Above 8%, you are paying heavily for every bet.

How do I calculate implied probability?

100 divided by the decimal odds: odds of 2.0 imply 50%.
