Crypto Betting: What It Means in Practice

Both operators primarily target crypto-native bettors. Bitcoin, Ethereum and other cryptocurrencies are accepted for deposits and withdrawals. For Irish punters who hold crypto and want to bet, the deposit process is straightforward — but neither operator provides GRAI Irish legal protections. Dispute resolution is via Curaçao jurisdiction: slow and limited.

Sports Coverage

Sportsbet.io has invested more in esports coverage (CS:GO, League of Legends, Dota 2) than Betmaster. For traditional sports (football, tennis, basketball), both offer comparable coverage at similar margins. Neither focuses on Irish-specific sports (GAA, Irish racing).

Irish Punters and Crypto Betting

GRAI-licensed operators do not widely accept cryptocurrency. For Irish punters who want both crypto deposits and Irish legal protections, no current GRAI-licensed operator satisfies both criteria. The choice is: crypto at Curaçao-licensed operators (no Irish protections) or fiat at GRAI-licensed operators (full Irish protections). For most Irish punters, fiat with GRAI protection is the safer default.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bet with Bitcoin in Ireland?

Some international operators (Betmaster, Sportsbet.io) accept Bitcoin. GRAI-licensed operators do not widely accept crypto. Using crypto with unlicensed operators means no Irish GRAI protections.

Is Sportsbet.io licensed in Ireland?

Sportsbet.io does not hold a GRAI Irish remote betting licence. It operates under Curaçao jurisdiction.

Are crypto bookmakers safe?

Crypto bookmakers vary in safety. Curaçao-licensed operators offer minimal regulatory protections. For Irish punters, GRAI-licensed operators provide significantly stronger protections even though they typically use fiat currency.

Does Sportsbet.io cover esports?

Yes. Sportsbet.io has one of the broader esports offerings including CS:GO, League of Legends and Dota 2. For traditional sports, coverage is standard.

What is the safest way to bet with crypto in Ireland?

Currently, there is no GRAI-licensed operator that fully supports crypto betting. If using crypto is essential, an MGA-licensed operator provides better protections than Curaçao while still not matching GRAI Irish rights.
