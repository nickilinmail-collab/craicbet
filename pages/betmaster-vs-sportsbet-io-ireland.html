What a GRAI Licence Means in Practice

Since 1 July 2026, an operator offering betting to Irish customers needs a GRAI licence under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. With a licensed bookmaker these Irish rules apply:

  • Deposit limits: GRAI licensees must apply the deposit-limit rules set under Irish regulations.
  • National self-exclusion: licensees must integrate with the National Gambling Exclusion register.
  • Complaints: a dispute that cannot be resolved with the operator can be escalated to the GRAI.
  • Credit card ban: licensees may not accept credit cards for gambling.

With an operator that holds no GRAI licence, none of these Irish protections apply to your account.

What the Licence Does Not Cover

The licence held by BM Solutions Limited covers remote betting only. The GRAI has not issued any gaming (casino) licences yet; they are expected in 2027–2028.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Betmaster licensed in Ireland?

Yes. Betmaster is operated by BM Solutions Limited, which holds GRAI remote betting licence GRAI-0735-RB-26-0001, issued 1 July 2026 and valid until 30 June 2029.

Is Sportsbet.io licensed in Ireland?

Sportsbet.io does not appear in our list of GRAI licences, which we maintain from the official register. Check the register at grai.ie for the current position.

What do Irish punters lose with an operator that has no GRAI licence?

The Irish rules on deposit limits, national self-exclusion, complaints escalation to the GRAI and the credit card ban apply only to GRAI licensees.
