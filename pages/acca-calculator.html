Frequently Asked Questions

How are accumulator odds calculated?

Multiply the decimal odds of every leg together, then by your stake. Four legs at 1.8, 2.1, 1.5 and 3.0 = 17.01 combined odds.

What happens if one leg of my acca loses?

The whole bet loses. Every leg must win; one beaten favourite sinks the lot.

Do bookmaker margins stack in an acca?

Yes. Roughly 5% per leg compounds: a five-fold can carry a ~23% house edge against you.
