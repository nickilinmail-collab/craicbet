NBA betting basics for Irish punters

The NBA season runs from October to June, covering 82 regular season games per team plus playoffs. This volume creates betting opportunities every night of the week, making the NBA one of the most heavily traded basketball competitions among Irish bookmakers. Standard markets include moneyline (match winner), point spread (handicap), total points (over/under) and game props. Championship outrights are available throughout the season.

Moneyline vs point spread in the NBA

The moneyline is a straight win bet. For lopsided matchups, the favourite may be priced at 1.20-1.30, with the underdog at 4.00-6.00. The point spread levels the game: a favourite at −8.5 must win by 9 or more points; the underdog at +8.5 wins the bet if they lose by 8 or fewer. Standard spread bets are priced at −110 on each side (you bet €11 to win €10), reflecting the bookmaker’s margin.

Total points (over/under) betting

Total points betting asks whether the combined score of both teams will be over or under a specified line. NBA totals lines typically range from 210 to 240+ depending on the teams involved. High-pace offensive teams produce higher totals; strong defensive teams produce lower ones. Rest and back-to-back schedule factors also influence NBA scoring. Teams playing their second game in two nights (back-to-back) often score slightly fewer points.

NBA outright and playoff betting

Championship outright markets are open from the start of the season, with the perceived favourites priced at 5/1 to 10/1 and lesser contenders at much longer odds. Conference winner markets (Eastern and Western Conference) offer an intermediate outright bet. Frequently Asked Questions

What NBA betting markets are available from Irish bookmakers?

Moneyline (match winner), point spread, total points (over/under), player props (points, rebounds, assists), quarter and half betting, and outright championship and conference winner markets are available from most GRAI-licensed bookmakers covering the NBA.

What is the NBA point spread?

The point spread gives one team a notional lead to level the bet. If a team is −8.5, they must win by 9 or more points for a spread bet to win. The standard price on each side of an NBA spread is typically −110 (American) or 1.909 (decimal), representing the bookmaker's margin.

When does the NBA season start and end?

The NBA regular season runs from October to April, followed by playoffs from April to June, culminating in the NBA Finals in June. Championship outright markets are available from October, with prices updating after every game.
