What is a Super Heinz bet?

A Super Heinz is a full-cover multiple bet using 7 selections and 120 bets. It covers every combination of 2 or more selections from your 7 picks — 21 doubles, 35 trebles, 35 four-folds, 21 five-folds, 7 six-folds and 1 seven-fold. There are no singles, so you need at least 2 winners to see any return. At a €1 unit stake the total outlay is €120, making it one of the more expensive standard named bets. The appeal is breadth of coverage: if 5 or 6 of your selections come in, the returns can be substantial.

Super Heinz example

Say you pick 7 horses at a festival — odds of 3.0, 4.0, 2.5, 5.0, 3.5, 2.0 and 6.0 — with a €0.50 unit stake (total outlay: €60). If selections 1, 2 and 3 win, you collect on 1 double (1×2), 1 double (1×3), 1 double (2×3) and 1 treble (1×2×3). Double 1×2: 3.0×4.0×€0.50 = €6.00. Double 1×3: 3.0×2.5×€0.50 = €3.75. Double 2×3: 4.0×2.5×€0.50 = €5.00. Treble 1×2×3: 3.0×4.0×2.5×€0.50 = €15.00. Total return €29.75 — a loss of €30.25 from the €60 outlay. The Super Heinz rewards volume winners; three of seven is not enough.

Is a Super Heinz worth it?

A Super Heinz makes sense when you are confident in a large number of selections — say, five or more from a programme of seven races — and want full cover without placing singles. The wide spread means a high number of winners generates outsized returns through the larger accumulators. However, the £120 (or €120) outlay is significant, and the lack of singles means two winners are the minimum to see any return. Most casual punters would be better served by a Lucky 15 (four selections, singles included) or a Heinz (six selections, €57). Frequently asked questions

How many bets in a Super Heinz?

120 bets: 21 doubles, 35 trebles, 35 four-folds, 21 five-folds, 7 six-folds and 1 seven-fold. There are no singles.

How much does a €1 Super Heinz cost?

€120 total — 120 bets at a €1 unit stake each. A €0.50 unit stake costs €60.

How many winners do you need for a Super Heinz return?

At least 2 — to land one of the 21 doubles. Fewer than 2 winners means the entire stake is lost.

What is the difference between a Super Heinz and a Heinz?

A Heinz uses 6 selections (57 bets); a Super Heinz uses 7 selections (120 bets). Both include no singles.

What comes after a Super Heinz?

The Goliath — 8 selections and 247 bets, the largest standard named bet in betting.
