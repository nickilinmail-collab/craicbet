What is a Super Heinz bet?

A Super Heinz is a full-cover combination bet on 7 selections, covering every possible multi-selection combination from doubles to the seven-fold accumulator — 120 bets in total. It does not include singles. You need at least 2 winners before any return is paid, and the potential returns can be very large if most selections win. The Super Heinz sits between the Heinz (6 selections, 57 bets) and the Goliath (8 selections, 247 bets) in the family of named full-cover bets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Super Heinz bet?

A Super Heinz covers 7 selections across 120 bets: 21 doubles, 35 trebles, 35 four-folds, 21 five-folds, 7 six-folds and 1 seven-fold. No singles. At least 2 winners needed for any return.

How many bets in a Super Heinz?

120 bets: 21 doubles + 35 trebles + 35 four-folds + 21 five-folds + 7 six-folds + 1 seven-fold.

How much does a Super Heinz cost?

A €1 Super Heinz costs €120. A 50c stake costs €60.

What is the difference between a Heinz and a Super Heinz?

A Heinz covers 6 selections in 57 bets; a Super Heinz adds a 7th selection bringing the total to 120 bets.

Does a Super Heinz include singles?

No — only doubles through to the seven-fold accumulator.
