Autumn Internationals 2026 Betting — November Rugby Tests Guide for Ireland

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Autumn Internationals in November 2026 are the final major international rugby window before the Six Nations 2027. For Irish punters, Ireland's home tests at the Aviva Stadium are among the most popular betting fixtures of the year.

Autumn Internationals Format

The Autumn International window typically runs across three consecutive weekends in November. Each Tier 1 nation hosts three or four visiting Southern Hemisphere or Northern Hemisphere opponents. For Ireland, the Aviva Stadium hosts three matches — typically against opponents from Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Argentina, Fiji or similar.

Main Betting Markets for the Autumn Internationals 2026

  • Match result: Win/draw/win for each individual test. Southern Hemisphere opposition provides genuine competitive tests for all Northern Hemisphere nations.
  • Handicap betting: When Ireland face teams significantly below their current ranking, handicap lines provide better value than the outright win market.
  • Total points over/under: Autumn internationals are often high-scoring as teams experiment with squad rotation and attacking intent.
  • Try scorer markets: Anytime try scorer and first try scorer for each match — popular with punters who follow both squad selections closely.

Six Nations 2027 Implications

Autumn International results carry significant betting implications for the Six Nations 2027 ante-post market. Nations that perform strongly against Southern Hemisphere opposition in November typically see their Six Nations prices shorten in December and January. Poor autumn form can lengthen Six Nations prices before the tournament begins.

The Autumn Internationals (November Test window) brings major Southern Hemisphere nations (New Zealand, Australia, South Africa) to European grounds. Ireland's series at the Aviva Stadium, Dublin, is Ireland's biggest home fixture window outside the Six Nations and attracts significant betting volume.

  • Southern Hemisphere form: the All Blacks, Springboks and Wallabies arrive at the end of their own season and may be fatigued after Super Rugby; check recent Test results before backing them at big prices

When are the Autumn Internationals 2026?

The Autumn Internationals 2026 typically take place over three weekends in November — the first, second and third weekends of the month. Ireland's home fixtures at the Aviva Stadium are usually spread across these three windows. Exact dates will be confirmed by World Rugby.

Who does Ireland play in the Autumn Internationals 2026?

Ireland's Autumn International opponents for 2026 will be confirmed by the IRFU, typically by mid-2026. Standard opposition includes nations from Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Argentina and Pacific Island nations. The draw creates different market profiles for each fixture.

Are the Autumn Internationals a good indicator of Six Nations form?

Autumn International results provide useful form guides but should not be over-weighted. Clubs use the November window to rotate squads and try new combinations, and Southern Hemisphere opposition at the end of their own season is not always at peak form. The results are useful context but not definitive predictors of Six Nations performance.
