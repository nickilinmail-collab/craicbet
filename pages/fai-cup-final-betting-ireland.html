FAI Cup Final Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The FAI Cup Final at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin is the showpiece domestic cup event in Irish football, bringing together the finalists from the season-long knockout competition. For punters, it is one of the highest-profile single League of Ireland betting events of the year.

About the FAI Cup

The FAI Cup is a knockout competition open to clubs from all tiers of Irish football, including League of Ireland Premier and First Division sides, as well as lower league amateur clubs. Giant-killing is a real possibility in the early rounds, but by the final, it is typically two Premier Division clubs competing.

The final is played at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, usually in November. It is a one-off fixture with no replay provision — if the game is level after 90 minutes, extra time and a penalty shootout determine the winner.

Betting Markets

  • Match result (90 minutes): This is typically offered rather than the outright winner market, as extra time is possible
  • Outright winner (including extra time and penalties): The full result market
  • Both teams to score: FAI Cup finals tend to be cagey; research recent head-to-head
  • First scorer / anytime scorer: Available at major bookmakers for cup finals
  • In-play: Full in-play markets available for the final

Outright FAI Cup Winner

Outright markets for the FAI Cup winner are available from the start of the season or after the draw is made for each round. Research shows Shamrock Rovers, Bohemians, St Patrick's Athletic, Shelbourne and Derry City are the most frequent contenders.

Where is the FAI Cup Final played?

The FAI Cup Final is played at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, the national football stadium with a capacity of approximately 51,000.

Does the FAI Cup Final include extra time and penalties?

Yes. If the match is level after 90 minutes, extra time is played. If still level, a penalty shootout determines the winner. Bet on the outright winner (including extra time and penalties) if you want your bet to cover the full result.

Can I bet on the FAI Cup from the early rounds?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer match betting for FAI Cup rounds from the third round onwards. The final and semi-finals have the deepest markets.

Which club has won the FAI Cup most often?

Shamrock Rovers hold the most FAI Cup titles historically. However, the competition has had varied winners in recent years. Check current odds at a GRAI-licensed bookmaker for the latest favourite.
