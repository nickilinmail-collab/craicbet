What Is a Rollover Requirement on a Betting Bonus?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A rollover requirement and a wagering requirement are the same thing — different terms used by different operators for the same mechanic: the number of times you must bet through a bonus before withdrawing the resulting funds. Here is a plain-English explanation with examples.

Rollover = Wagering: Same Term, Different Labels

US-facing sites and casino-first platforms tend to use “rollover.” UK and Irish bookmakers more commonly use “wagering requirement” or “playthrough.” If you see any of these, the mathematics are identical: Bonus × Multiplier = Total bets required before withdrawing.

Calculating Your Total Rollover

Examples with a €50 bonus:

  • 5x rollover: €50 × 5 = €250 total bets
  • 10x rollover: €50 × 10 = €500 total bets
  • 20x rollover: €50 × 20 = €1,000 total bets
  • 35x rollover: €50 × 35 = €1,750 total bets

At a 5% bookmaker margin (typical for Premier League football), you are expected to lose €12.50 clearing a €250 rollover and €87.50 clearing a €1,750 rollover. The difference between 5x and 35x is not 7 times harder — it is the difference between a genuinely profitable bonus and one with negative expected value.

What Is a Good Rollover for Sports Betting?

For sports free bets: 1–10x is good; 10–20x is acceptable; above 20x and the bonus value erodes significantly. For casino-style deposit bonuses with slot wagering, even 20x is aggressive because slot margins are higher (3–10% RTP shortfall per spin).

Deposit + Bonus vs Bonus-Only Rollover

Some casino bonuses require wagering of both the deposit and the bonus, multiplied. “Deposit €50, receive €50 bonus, 20x on deposit + bonus” means €2,000 total wagering (€100 × 20). This is much more demanding than a 20x on the bonus alone (€50 × 20 = €1,000). Read the T&Cs carefully to know what the multiplier applies to.

Is a rollover requirement the same as a wagering requirement?

Yes — they are identical. Rollover is the term used by US/casino sites; wagering requirement is used by UK and Irish bookmakers. Both mean: bet through the bonus this many times before withdrawing.

How do I calculate my total rollover?

Multiply the bonus amount by the rollover multiplier. €50 bonus × 10x = €500 total bets required before the bonus converts to withdrawable cash.

What is a good rollover requirement for sports betting?

1–10x is good value. 10–20x is acceptable. Above 20x for sports bonuses, the expected cost of clearing the wagering begins to exceed the bonus value.

Does rollover apply to the bonus or the deposit + bonus?

Check the T&Cs — it varies. Some bonuses apply the multiplier to the bonus only. Others apply it to the deposit + bonus combined, which doubles or triples the total wagering requirement.

Can I clear rollover requirements with any bet?

Not always. Most bonuses specify minimum odds (typically 2.0+) and may exclude certain bet types (each-way, cash-out bets) or sports. Check the qualifying bets section of the T&Cs.
