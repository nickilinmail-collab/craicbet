Live Casino vs RNG Games — Which Should Irish Players Choose?

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 October 2026 ⏱ 8 min read

Online casinos offer two fundamentally different ways to play table games: live casino with real human dealers streamed in HD, and RNG (Random Number Generator) games powered entirely by software. Both are regulated and fair, but the experience couldn't be more different. Here's how they compare.

🎬 Quick Answer

Live casino is the closer experience to a real casino — real dealers, real cards, social chat, and authentic atmosphere. RNG games are faster, cheaper to play, available in demo mode, and better for learning. Choose live for the experience, RNG for practice and convenience.

What Is Live Casino?

Live casino games are streamed in real time from professional studios (or sometimes real casino floors) using multiple HD cameras. A real human dealer runs the game — shuffling cards, spinning the roulette wheel, or dealing baccarat hands — and you interact through a digital interface.

Major live casino providers include Evolution, Pragmatic Play Live, and Playtech Live. Games typically include blackjack, roulette, baccarat, poker, and game shows like Crazy Time and Dream Catcher.

You can chat with the dealer and sometimes other players, making it the most social form of online gambling. For a deeper dive, see our live casino guide for Irish players.

What Are RNG Games?

RNG (Random Number Generator) games are fully software-based. A computer algorithm determines every outcome — card draws, roulette spins, dice rolls. The visuals are animated graphics, not a live video feed.

RNG games include digital versions of blackjack, roulette, baccarat, poker, and all online slots. The RNG is regularly tested and certified by independent labs like eCOGRA, iTech Labs, and GLI to ensure genuine randomness.

Every outcome is independent and unbiased — exactly as in live casino. The difference is presentation and pace, not fairness.

The Social Experience

Live casino's biggest selling point is the human element. You see a real dealer, hear the cards being dealt, and watch the roulette ball spin in real time. Most tables have a live chat where you can talk to the dealer and other players.

Dealers are trained professionals who keep the game moving, respond to chat messages, and create a welcoming atmosphere. It's the closest thing to sitting at a real casino table from your sofa.

RNG games have no social element. You're playing against software in isolation. For some players, that's a drawback. For others who prefer to focus and play at their own pace, it's an advantage.

Speed & Pace of Play

RNG games are significantly faster. A hand of RNG blackjack takes seconds. A live blackjack hand takes 30–60 seconds because the dealer physically deals cards and waits for all players to act.

Live roulette has a spin cycle of about 60–90 seconds. RNG roulette lets you spin as fast as you click. If you want to play a lot of hands quickly (or if you're clearing a bonus), RNG is far more efficient.

However, the slower pace of live casino can be a benefit — it gives you time to think, reduces the speed at which you burn through your bankroll, and makes the experience feel more deliberate and less mechanical.

Bet Limits — Who Has the Edge?

Live casino tables generally have higher maximum bets, with some VIP tables accepting wagers of €50,000 or more per hand. This makes live casino the preferred choice for high rollers.

RNG games have lower minimum bets, often starting at €0.10 or €0.20. This makes them more accessible for casual players and those with smaller bankrolls. Live table minimums are typically €0.50 to €5.00, and some tables start even higher.

Demo Mode — Practice Before You Play

One of RNG games' biggest advantages is demo mode (also called free play or practice mode). You can play with virtual credits to learn the rules, test strategies, and get comfortable before wagering real money.

Live casino games never have demo mode. Because real dealers are involved and tables operate in real time, you can't spectate or practice for free. You need to place a real bet to play. This makes RNG games the better starting point for beginners learning table games.

Fairness — Both Are Equally Regulated

A common question is whether one format is "fairer" than the other. The answer: both are equally fair when you play at licensed casinos.

  • RNG games use certified random number generators, tested by independent labs. Every outcome is statistically random and independent.
  • Live casino uses physical randomness (real cards, real wheels) with multiple camera angles and sophisticated shuffling procedures. Results are monitored and recorded.

The house edge is determined by the game's rules, not the format. European roulette has a 2.70% house edge whether it's live or RNG. Blackjack with basic strategy returns about 99.5% either way.

Live Casino Game Shows — A Unique Category

One area where live casino truly stands alone is game shows. These are live-hosted games that don't exist in RNG format:

  • Crazy Time — A giant money wheel with four bonus rounds, hosted by energetic presenters. Multipliers can reach 25,000x.
  • Dream Catcher — A simpler money wheel game with multiplier segments. Great for beginners.
  • Monopoly Live — Combines a money wheel with an augmented reality Monopoly board. Multipliers up to 10,000x.
  • Lightning Dice — Three dice with random lightning multipliers. Fast-paced and exciting.
  • Funky Time — A disco-themed show with multiple bonus games. Pure entertainment.

These game shows represent the most innovative category in online gambling and are exclusive to live casino. They combine gambling with entertainment TV, and have become hugely popular with Irish players.

Mobile Experience

RNG games are generally better optimised for mobile play. They load quickly, use minimal data, and work perfectly on any screen size. Most modern slots are designed mobile-first.

Live casino works on mobile but requires more from your device. You need a stable data connection for HD streaming, and the interface can feel cramped on smaller screens. That said, all major live casino providers have optimised their mobile experience significantly, and playing on a modern smartphone is perfectly viable over Wi-Fi.

If you primarily play on your phone with a mobile data connection, RNG games will give you a smoother, more reliable experience. On Wi-Fi at home, live casino works beautifully on tablets and larger phones.

What's More Popular in Ireland?

Slots remain the most-played casino game category by a significant margin, accounting for roughly 70–80% of online casino revenue globally. However, live casino has been the fastest-growing segment for several years running.

Irish players have shown strong appetite for live casino, particularly live roulette and game shows. Evolution (the market leader in live casino) has reported consistent double-digit growth, and most casinos now feature extensive live lobbies with dozens of tables.

The trend is clear: live casino will continue to grow as technology improves and more players discover the experience. But slots aren't going anywhere — the two formats serve different needs and attract different moods, even from the same player.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are live casino games rigged?▾
No. Live casino games at licensed casinos use real cards, real wheels, and real dealers, with multiple HD cameras recording every action. Games are operated by licensed studios (like Evolution and Pragmatic Play Live) that are regularly audited. The results are as random as a physical casino — arguably more transparent because everything is recorded on video.
Can I try live casino games for free?▾
No, live casino games do not offer demo or free play mode. Because real dealers operate the tables in real time, you need to place real money bets to participate. If you want to learn the rules first, play the RNG version of the same game in demo mode, then switch to live when you're comfortable.
Do live casino games have better odds than RNG games?▾
The odds are typically the same for equivalent games. European roulette has a 2.70% house edge whether live or RNG. Blackjack basic strategy yields about 99.5% RTP in either format. The game rules determine the odds, not whether it's live or software-based. Always check the specific rules of each table, as some variants have slightly different rules that affect the house edge.
Why are live casino minimum bets higher?▾
Running a live casino table is expensive — it requires trained dealers, studio space, cameras, production staff, and streaming infrastructure. These costs mean casinos need higher minimum bets to make live tables profitable. RNG games have minimal running costs after development, so they can offer much lower minimums. Some live tables do start at €0.50, but most begin at €1.00–€5.00.
Is my internet connection good enough for live casino?▾
Live casino streams in HD video, so you need a stable internet connection of at least 5 Mbps for a smooth experience. Wi-Fi is usually fine, but mobile data can be unreliable. If your connection drops during a hand, the game continues and any bets stand — the result is determined regardless. RNG games need much less bandwidth and are more forgiving of connection issues.
