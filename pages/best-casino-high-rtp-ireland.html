RTP (Return to Player) is a frequently misunderstood casino concept. This guide clarifies what RTP means, why it's a game-level figure not a casino-level one, and how to use it practically when choosing what to play.

What RTP Actually Means

RTP is the theoretical percentage of all wagered money that a game returns to players over millions of rounds. A 96% RTP slot returns €96 for every €100 wagered on average — over the long run, across all players. In any single session, results vary enormously from this theoretical average.

RTP is Set by the Game, Not the Casino

The same slot title has the same published RTP whether you play it at Casino A or Casino B — the game's RTP is set by its developer and can't be altered by the casino. This means comparing casinos by "site RTP" is misleading. What matters is finding high-RTP individual games.

Some Casinos Offer Alternate RTP Versions

A minority of casinos negotiate with providers to run a different RTP variant of a game. For example, a slot might be available in 94%, 96%, and 97% configurations. If a casino publishes its game-specific RTP configuration, that's worth checking — but most casinos don't disclose this. The default configuration from the provider is what you'll encounter at most casinos.

RTP vs Volatility

High RTP doesn't mean frequent wins. High-volatility slots can have respectable RTPs but infrequent large pays. Low-volatility slots have smaller, more frequent wins. Choose volatility based on your session goals and bankroll, not just RTP.

What RTP Actually Means for Your Bankroll

RTP (Return to Player) is the theoretical percentage of all wagers returned to players over millions of spins. A 97% RTP slot returns €97 for every €100 wagered — in theory, over a very long run. In a single session, variance swamps the RTP: a low-volatility 97% game can still produce a £200 swing in either direction within 100 spins.

High-RTP slots help over long play periods, but the best use is to satisfy wagering requirements on casino bonuses — where the lower house edge reduces your average cost to clear the bonus.
