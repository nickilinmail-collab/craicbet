Why golf is well-suited to each-way betting

Golf tournaments feature 60 to 156 players competing over four rounds. No other sport offers this many possible ‘losers’ in a single event, which is why each-way betting dominates the market. Place terms of top 5, top 8 or top 10 at 1/4 or 1/5 of the win odds are standard for major events. A player priced at 40/1 to win the Masters pays 10/1 for a top-5 each-way bet at 1/4 odds — a meaningful return even without winning the tournament.

Major tournament markets

The Masters, US Open, The Open Championship and PGA Championship are the four annual golf majors and attract the widest market range from Irish bookmakers: outright winner, top 5, top 10, top 20, top European/Irish, group betting (choose the best-placed of three named players), first round leader and head-to-head matchups. Ryder Cup years also generate significant Irish interest, with outright and session betting widely available.

Reading golf form correctly

Golf form is contextual: a player who performs well on links courses (The Open setting) may struggle on the tight tree-lined courses of Augusta or the US Open rough. Check recent form on similar course types, in similar conditions. World ranking is a useful starting point but does not capture recent form trajectory — a player ranked 80th but finishing top-10 in their last three events may be better value than their ranking suggests.

Place terms comparison and where to look

Place terms vary between operators for the same tournament — one may offer top 6 at 1/4 while another offers top 8 at 1/5. The best terms depend on the player’s price: at short odds (under 10/1), more places beats a higher fraction; at long odds (25/1+), the fraction becomes more important. Frequently Asked Questions

How does each-way betting work in golf?

An each-way golf bet has two parts: a win bet and a place bet. If your player wins, both parts pay. If they finish in the top 5 (or whatever the place term is), only the place part pays at the stated fraction of the win odds (usually 1/4 or 1/5). If they finish outside the places, both bets lose.

What are typical golf tournament place terms?

Major tournaments typically offer each-way terms of top 5 or top 6 at 1/4 odds, or top 8 at 1/5 odds. Regular tour events may offer top 5 at 1/4 for 72-player fields or fewer. Terms vary by operator and event size — always check before placing.

Is group betting a good market in golf?

Group betting asks you to pick which of three named players finishes highest. This simplifies the choice and removes the field, though bookmaker margins are still present. Group bets can be a useful alternative to outright betting when you have a strong relative view on two or three players but are uncertain about the wider field.

Each-Way Golf Betting: How Terms Work

Golf is one of the best sports for each-way value. Typical each-way terms:

  • Standard: 1/4 odds, top 5 places — most tournaments at GRAI-licensed sites
  • Enhanced: 1/5 odds, top 8 places — majors and WGC events often have extra places
  • Example: backing a player at 20/1 each-way, 1/4 for top 5 → place part pays at 5/1 (20/1 ÷ 4)
  • A player finishing 4th at 20/1 returns: €10 win stake lost + €10 × (5/1 + 1) = €60 place return
