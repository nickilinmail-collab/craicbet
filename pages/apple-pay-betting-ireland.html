Apple Pay Betting Ireland 2026 — GRAI Licensed Bookmakers Accepting Apple Pay

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Apple Pay is the fastest deposit method available at GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers that support it — a single Face ID or Touch ID confirmation and funds appear instantly in your betting account. For iPhone and iPad users in Ireland, it removes the friction of typing card numbers on a mobile screen. The key thing to understand in 2026: Apple Pay is a payment wrapper, not a card — whether it is affected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban depends entirely on which card you have linked to your Apple Wallet.

✅ Key fact: Apple Pay linked to a debit card is unaffected by Ireland's credit card gambling ban. Apple Pay linked to a credit card will be blocked at GRAI-licensed bookmakers.

How Apple Pay Works at Betting Sites

Apple Pay tokenises your card details — the bookmaker never receives your actual card number. Instead, a one-time device-specific token authorises the payment. For the punter, the experience is:

  1. Select Apple Pay in the deposit screen (available on Safari on iOS and in the bookmaker's native app).
  2. The Apple Pay payment sheet appears with the amount and merchant name.
  3. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone/iPad passcode.
  4. Funds are credited to your betting account instantly.

The same process applies on Mac with Touch ID when using Safari. Apple Pay is not available in Chrome or Firefox on any platform — only Safari and apps that have implemented Apple's payment API.

Apple Pay and Ireland's Credit Card Gambling Ban

Since 1 July 2026, GRAI-licensed gambling operators cannot accept credit card payments. This ban catches Apple Pay transactions where the underlying card is a credit card. The bookmaker's payment processor receives card type information at the token level and applies the same rules as it would to a physical credit card.

Permitted: Apple Pay linked to a Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit, Revolut debit card, or any other debit instrument.

Blocked: Apple Pay linked to a Visa Credit, Mastercard Credit, or any other credit facility.

To check which cards are in your Wallet, open the Wallet app on your iPhone — each card shows its type (Debit or Credit) below the card name. If you have multiple cards, you can set a debit card as the default for Apple Pay in Wallet settings.

Speed: Apple Pay vs Other Methods

Apple Pay deposits are as fast as a standard debit card — they hit the same processing rails. The difference is in authentication speed: no typing card numbers, no SMS codes (Face ID/Touch ID replaces that). Withdrawals go back to the underlying card and follow that card's withdrawal timeline, typically 1–5 business days for debit cards.

iOS Only — The Main Limitation

Apple Pay works exclusively within the Apple ecosystem: iPhone, iPad, Mac (Touch ID models), and Apple Watch. If you use an Android phone or a Windows PC, Apple Pay is not available. For cross-platform payment convenience, Revolut (card works everywhere) or Google Pay (Android and Chrome) are better alternatives. If you switch between iOS and Android devices, you will need a fallback payment method registered on the bookmaker for use on Android.
