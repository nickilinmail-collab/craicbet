Paysafecard Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | Gambling Awareness Trust | 1800 753 753

Paysafecard is a prepaid voucher payment method available at newsagents and convenience stores across Ireland. You buy a voucher for a fixed amount (€10, €25, €50 or €100) and use the 16-digit PIN to deposit at betting sites — no bank account or card details needed.

Key point: Paysafecard is deposit only. Withdrawals must go via bank transfer or verified debit card — you cannot withdraw winnings back to a Paysafecard voucher.

Who accepts Paysafecard for betting in Ireland?

Not all GRAI-licensed bookmakers accept Paysafecard. The GRAI licensing regime that began July 2026 tightened AML requirements, and some sites have removed Paysafecard because its anonymous nature makes compliance harder. Check the deposit page of your specific site — look for the Paysafecard logo in the payment methods section.

Why some sites restrict Paysafecard

GRAI-licensed operators must verify player identity (KYC) under AML regulations. Paysafecard purchased with cash in a shop doesn’t leave a paper trail back to the customer, creating compliance gaps. Some bookmakers accept it for deposits but require a verified card or bank transfer before processing any withdrawal.

How to use Paysafecard at a betting site

  1. Buy a Paysafecard voucher at a Spar, Eurospar, or other stockist
  2. Log in to your verified betting account
  3. Go to Deposit → select Paysafecard
  4. Enter the 16-digit PIN from your voucher
  5. Enter the deposit amount (up to your voucher balance)
  6. Confirm — funds appear instantly

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Paysafecard for betting in Ireland?
Yes, at betting sites that accept it. It is a prepaid voucher system — buy a voucher in a shop and use the 16-digit PIN to deposit. Not all GRAI-licensed sites accept it.
Can I withdraw winnings to Paysafecard?
No. Paysafecard is deposit only. Withdrawals must go via bank transfer or a verified debit card.
Where can I buy Paysafecard in Ireland?
Paysafecard vouchers are sold at Spar, Eurospar, and other newsagents across Ireland in denominations of €10, €25, €50 and €100. You can also buy online at paysafecard.com.
Does Paysafecard show up on bank statements?
If bought with cash in a shop, no. If bought online with a card, your bank statement shows a Paysafe purchase — not the betting site name. Many punters prefer Paysafecard for this privacy.
