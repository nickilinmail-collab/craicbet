How Wagering Requirements Work — A Worked Example

Wagering requirements (also called "playthrough") are the number of times you must bet the bonus amount before you can withdraw winnings. They're the single most important factor in determining a bonus's real value.

🧮 Worked Example: €100 Bonus with 35x Wagering

Bonus received: €100
Wagering requirement: 35x
Total bets needed: €100 × 35 = €3,500
If playing slots (96% RTP): Expected loss: €3,500 × 4% = €140
Expected value of bonus: €100 − €140 = −€40

In this example, the expected value is actually negative — meaning on average, you'd lose money clearing the bonus. This is why lower wagering requirements (20x–30x) are so much more valuable. At 20x wagering, the same €100 bonus would require €2,000 in bets with an expected loss of only €80, giving you a positive expected value of €20.

Key takeaway: Always divide the bonus by the wagering requirement and multiply by the house edge. If the expected loss exceeds the bonus value, it's a bad deal. As a rule of thumb, 35x or lower is fair, 20x–25x is excellent, and anything above 50x is tough to clear.

6 Tips for Claiming Casino Bonuses in Ireland

  1. Always read the full T&Cs. Pay special attention to wagering requirements, max bet limits, game restrictions, and expiry dates. A €5 max bet with a bonus active means one accidental €6 spin could void your entire bonus.
  2. Check the wagering calculation method. Does the casino apply wagering to the bonus only or bonus + deposit? "30x bonus" is far better than "30x (bonus + deposit)" — the latter effectively doubles the requirement.
  3. Play high-RTP slots to clear wagering. Choose slots with 96%+ RTP to minimise losses while meeting playthrough. Avoid low-RTP games and jackpot slots, which often contribute 0% towards wagering.
  4. Watch the clock. Most bonuses expire within 7–30 days. Ireland's GRAI licensed casino gives you 14 days, which is tight for a €1,050 bonus with 50x wagering. Set a reminder and pace yourself.
  5. Don't chase losses with bonus funds. If you've burned through most of your bonus before meeting wagering, it's usually better to forfeit the bonus and keep your deposited funds than to deposit more trying to chase it.
