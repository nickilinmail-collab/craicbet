How Do Enhanced Odds Work? Price Boosts Explained Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 November 2026⏱ 6 min read

Enhanced odds — also called price boosts, odds boosts or boosted prices — are one of the most visible promotions on Irish betting sites. A team is normally priced at 2/1 and suddenly a bookmaker is offering 4/1 for the same outcome, usually for a limited stake and a limited time. They look like free money. Sometimes they genuinely are better value than the standard market. But the conditions attached to most boosts mean the real benefit is often less than the headline figure suggests.

What Is an Enhanced Odds Offer?

An enhanced odds offer (or price boost) temporarily raises the available price on a specific outcome above the standard market rate. A typical boost might offer a Premier League team at 7/1 instead of their standard 4/1, or an accumulator with one leg artificially inflated. You can bet on this boosted price, usually up to a capped maximum stake (commonly €5–€25), and if the selection wins you are paid at the boosted price.

The key detail: the extra profit above the standard price is typically paid in cash for new customer enhanced odds offers, but ongoing promotional boosts from some operators pay the boost-portion as a free bet token (which is then subject to free bet terms). This distinction significantly affects real value.

Calculating the Real Value of a Price Boost

The genuine value of a boost depends on three factors: (1) the size of the boost relative to the standard price; (2) the stake cap; and (3) whether the extra profit is paid in cash or as a free bet.

Example: a selection boosted from 2/1 (3.0 decimal) to 4/1 (5.0 decimal) with a €10 max stake, profit paid in cash.

  • Standard price win: €10 × 2.0 (profit) = €20 profit
  • Boosted price win: €10 × 4.0 (profit) = €40 profit
  • Extra from boost: €20 extra profit if it wins
  • EV of boost (at 33% true probability): 0.33 × €20 = €6.60 expected value from the boost

That €6.60 expected value from a €10 stake is genuinely good — if you can find bets where the true probability is higher than the standard odds imply, the boost multiplies that edge. Even if you have no edge on the standard price, the boost creates positive expected value at the boosted price.

Stake Caps and Their Impact

Most boosts have stake caps of €5–€25, rarely up to €50. This limits the total benefit to the punter significantly. Even the most generous boost at a 100% odds increase with a €10 stake cap gives you at most a few euros of EV per boost. For recreational punters this is a nice bonus. As a primary betting strategy, the volume of boosts and the small cap sizes mean the absolute returns are limited.

When Are Enhanced Odds Genuine Value?

Enhanced odds are genuine value when: (1) the boosted price represents better odds than any other market; (2) the extra profit is paid in cash rather than as a free bet; (3) you were planning to bet the selection anyway. Using a boost on a selection you have assessed independently as good value at standard prices makes the boost a true benefit.

Enhanced odds are poor value when: they push you to bet on a selection you would not have backed at standard price (you are essentially manufacturing a bet to use the promotion); when the extra profit is as a free bet with restrictions; or when the selection is at odds so short that the boost barely moves the return. 18+ — Gambling Awareness Trust: 1800 753 753, gamblingcare.ie.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an enhanced odds / price boost offer?

A price boost temporarily raises the odds on a selection above the standard market price, for a limited stake. The extra profit is typically paid as cash or as a free bet.

Are the extra winnings from a price boost paid in cash?

Usually yes for new customer offers. Some ongoing promotions pay the boost portion as a free bet token. Always check the specific terms.

Why is there always a stake cap on enhanced odds?

Stake caps limit the bookmaker's maximum exposure on the boosted price. Without a cap, a punter could bet large amounts at a price below fair market value.

Can I always find enhanced odds on major events?

Most bookmakers offer regular boosts on major football, horse racing and other events. The specific selection, boost size and stake cap vary daily.

Are enhanced odds available to all customers?

New customer boosts are available once per new account. Ongoing enhanced odds vary. Customers with restricted accounts may find enhanced odds also restricted.

Enhanced Odds: What to Watch Out For

  • Stake limits: Most boosts cap bets at €5–€10, so the absolute extra value is small
  • One per customer: Usually limited to one bet per enhanced offer per account
  • Time limits: Offers are usually valid for a few hours only
  • Specific selections only: The boost applies to one exact selection, not related bets
  • May count toward wagering: Check if stakes on enhanced odds count toward any wagering requirement
  • Not available to all accounts: Gubbed or restricted accounts often excluded from promotions
