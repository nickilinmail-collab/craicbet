Why This Comparison Matters

A large share of the casinos accepting Irish players run on Curaçao licences — so understanding how that regime compares with what the GRAI is building tells you exactly what protections you have today, and what you will gain when Irish casino licensing opens.

Is a Curaçao Casino Automatically Unsafe?

No — but the licence does less of the work, so your own checks matter more. Curaçao’s 2024 reform replaced the old master-licence system with direct licensing and a central regulator, which improved transparency. Still, the practical protections — dispute resolution, fund segregation enforcement — are weaker than mature regimes like Malta’s. Verify the licence at the source, test a small withdrawal early, and read our licence check guide before depositing.

The Bottom Line

Today the comparison is lopsided: the GRAI does not license casinos at all, so Curaçao-licensed sites are a daily reality for Irish players rather than a choice against an Irish alternative. When GRAI gaming licences open, that calculus flips — and we expect licensed status to become the single biggest differentiator between casinos serving Ireland. Should Irish Punters Avoid Curaçao-Licensed Sites?

  • Curaçao is used by many crypto casinos and offshore bookmakers — not inherently scam sites, but far less regulated
  • No mandatory dispute resolution if the operator refuses to pay
  • No GRAI complaint escalation available for Curaçao-licensed sites
  • For Irish residents, GRAI-licensed or MGA-licensed sites offer significantly more protection
