Revolut is the most popular payment method at Irish online casinos. Fast, familiar, and widely accepted — but not every casino processes Revolut withdrawals at the same speed. Here's what Irish players need to know.

Using Revolut at Online Casinos

Revolut works like a standard debit card (Visa or Mastercard). Deposits at casinos that accept Revolut are instant. Withdrawals back to Revolut typically take 1–4 hours at the fastest casinos, though 1–2 business days is more common.

Why Some Casinos Block Revolut

Some casinos block Revolut deposits due to its built-in gambling block feature. Revolut allows users to restrict gambling transactions. When this feature is active, the deposit is declined. If your Revolut deposit is blocked, check your Revolut app settings under Cards or Security — a gambling block may be enabled by default.

Revolut Gambling Block: Useful or Frustrating?

Revolut's gambling block is a responsible gambling tool. If you've previously enabled it and want to deposit at a casino, you can disable it in-app. However, Revolut implements a 48-hour cooling-off period after disabling the block before gambling transactions are permitted — by design, to discourage impulsive decisions.

Revolut vs Traditional Banking for Casinos

Revolut offers near-instant deposits (same as a debit card) and generally faster withdrawals than traditional Irish banks. It also provides better privacy — your casino transactions appear as "Card Purchase" on Revolut rather than named casino transactions on a bank statement.

Revolut Limits at Casinos

Standard Revolut accounts have spending limits (varies by plan). If your casino deposits are declined, check your Revolut plan limits. Premium and Metal plans have higher limits. Some casinos also impose per-deposit or per-day limits.
