Leopardstown Racecourse Betting Guide — Ireland's Premier Track

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Leopardstown is Ireland's most important dual-purpose racecourse, staging both flat and National Hunt racing at the highest level. Located in south Dublin, it hosts several Grade 1 races throughout the year including the Dublin Racing Festival (February), the Leopardstown Christmas Festival (December) and significant summer flat meetings. This guide covers everything an Irish punter needs to know about betting at Leopardstown.

The Track: Characteristics That Affect Betting

Leopardstown is a flat, left-handed oval circuit with a home straight of about 2 furlongs. Key features:

  • Fair track. The flat circuit rewards horses without particular biases for or against high draws in most conditions.
  • Going varies. The Dublin climate means ground ranges from firm in summer to heavy in winter. Many high-profile National Hunt races are run on soft or yielding ground.
  • Stiff fences. National Hunt fences at Leopardstown are considered stiff; novice chasers who make errors here tend to find the experience costly.

Key Meetings at Leopardstown

The meetings that drive the most betting interest are:

  • Dublin Racing Festival (February): The Irish Champion Hurdle, Irish Gold Cup and Dublin Chase — definitive spring trials. Major ante-post moves happen after this meeting.
  • Leopardstown Christmas Festival (26–29 December): Four days of Grade 1 National Hunt racing; see our Christmas Festival guide.
  • Flat summer meetings (June–September): Significant flat prize money; Irish Oaks, Tattersalls Gold Cup, Champions Weekend.

Ante-Post from Leopardstown

Both the Christmas Festival and the Dublin Racing Festival serve as ante-post trigger points for Cheltenham and Punchestown. Watching which horses perform well at Leopardstown — and then placing before the market fully reacts — is one of the core timing strategies experienced Irish punters use. The post-Christmas window (late December to late January) is often the best period for Cheltenham ante-post value.

Each-Way Betting at Leopardstown

Grade 1 fields at Leopardstown are typically 8–14 runners, attracting standard 4-place each-way terms at 1/4 odds. For handicaps and bumpers — often larger fields — extended each-way terms (5+ places) can be available from some bookmakers. Use our each-way calculator to check potential returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Leopardstown Racecourse?

Leopardstown Racecourse is located in Foxrock, south County Dublin, approximately 10km from Dublin city centre. It is accessible by public transport, with Luas (Sandyford) and bus services running from the city.

Does Leopardstown host flat and National Hunt racing?

Yes. Leopardstown is a dual-purpose track hosting major flat meetings in summer and National Hunt (hurdles and chases) in autumn and winter. The Dublin Racing Festival in February and the Christmas Festival in December are the two biggest National Hunt events.

What is the Dublin Racing Festival at Leopardstown?

The Dublin Racing Festival is a two-day meeting at Leopardstown in early February featuring the Irish Champion Hurdle, the Irish Gold Cup and the Dublin Chase. It is the most important trial meeting for the Cheltenham Festival and produces significant ante-post movements.

What each-way terms are available at Leopardstown?

For Grade 1 races with 8+ runners, standard terms are 4 places at 1/4 odds. Terms vary with field size and race type. Always confirm each-way terms with your bookmaker before placing.
