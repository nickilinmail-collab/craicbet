Yankee Calculator (4 Selections, 11 Bets)

What is a Yankee bet?

A Yankee is a full-cover multiple bet using 4 selections and 11 bets: 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold accumulator. Unlike a Lucky 15, there are no singles — this means a Yankee is cheaper (11 unit stakes instead of 15) but you need at least 2 winners for any return. The Yankee is popular for horse racing and football accumulators where punters are confident in multiple selections but want to cap their outlay.

Yankee vs Lucky 15: which is better?

Both use 4 selections, but the key difference is singles. A Lucky 15 adds 4 singles to the same 11 Yankee bets, bringing the total to 15. This costs €4 more per unit stake but means one winner always pays something — often with a bookmaker one-winner bonus on top. A Yankee suits punters who are confident enough to risk the "2 winners needed" threshold in exchange for a lower total outlay. For festival betting or when each selection is at short odds, the Lucky 15 often makes more sense.

Yankee bet example

Say you back 4 horses at odds of 3.0, 4.0, 5.0 and 6.0, with a €1 unit stake (total outlay: €11). If selections 1 and 2 win (odds 3.0 and 4.0), only one double pays: 3.0 × 4.0 × €1 = €12. Return €12, loss €1 on the full bet. If selections 1, 2 and 3 all win, you collect: doubles AB (€12), AC (€15), BC (€20), treble ABC (€60). Total return: €107 from an €11 outlay — profit of €96. When to use a Yankee

A Yankee works well when you have 4 selections and are reasonably confident at least 2 will win, but want a lower outlay than a Lucky 15. It suits punters backing longer-priced horses or football teams where the return from 2 winners alone would cover the stake multiple times over. At shorter prices (under 2.0), even 3 winners may not cover the full 11-stake outlay — in that case a Lucky 15 with singles gives better protection. Frequently Asked Questions

How many bets in a Yankee?

11 bets — 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold. There are no singles in a Yankee.

How many winners do you need for a Yankee?

At least 2 winners are needed to win any return from a Yankee, as the smallest bet is a double. With just 1 winner you lose your entire stake.

What is the difference between a Yankee and a Lucky 15?

A Lucky 15 uses the same 4 selections but adds 4 singles, making 15 bets total. It costs more (15 stakes vs 11) but returns something with just 1 winner.

How much does a €1 Yankee cost?

€11 total — 11 separate bets at €1 each (6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold).

Can I do a Yankee each-way?

Yes — an each-way Yankee doubles the bets to 22 (11 win + 11 place), with win and place parts calculated separately at the place fraction and number of places.
