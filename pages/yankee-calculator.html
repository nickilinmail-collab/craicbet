What is a Yankee bet?

A Yankee is a combination bet on 4 selections covering every possible double, treble and the four-fold accumulator — 11 bets in total. It does not include singles, so you need a minimum of 2 winners before any return is paid. The name has been used in Irish and British horse racing betting since at least the 1950s. A Yankee is popular because it offers wide coverage across four selections without the outlay of a Lucky 15 (which adds four singles to bring the total to 15 bets).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Yankee bet?

A combination bet on 4 selections covering all doubles, trebles and the four-fold — 11 bets total. At least 2 winners needed for any return.

How many bets in a Yankee?

11 bets: 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 four-fold accumulator.

What is the difference between a Yankee and a Lucky 15?

A Lucky 15 adds 4 singles to a Yankee, giving 15 bets in total. With a Lucky 15, a single winner returns something; with a Yankee you need at least 2 winners.

How much does a Yankee cost?

A €1 Yankee costs €11 (11 bets × €1). A €5 Yankee costs €55.

Does a Yankee include singles?

No. To include singles, choose a Lucky 15 (15 bets total).
