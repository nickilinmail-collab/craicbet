How to Spot Genuine No-Wagering Offers

Key phrases in terms to look for: "winnings credited as cash," "no wagering on free spin winnings," "play-through free." Red flags: "wagering applies to winnings" or "bonus funds subject to 30× wagering" — these indicate the spins are not truly wagering-free. Always read the full T&Cs, not just the headline.

No-Wagering Free Spins: What to Expect

  • Typically available on specific slot titles only (usually Starburst or one designated game)
  • Win cap often applies — e.g. maximum €20 withdrawable from free spin winnings
  • Time-limited — usually must be used within 24–72 hours of being credited
  • Often lower value per spin (€0.10–€0.20) compared to standard free spins
  • Genuinely no-wagering offers are worth taking even at lower total value because the cash conversion is 1:1
