League of Ireland First Division Betting 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The League of Ireland First Division is the second tier of Irish football, feeding into and from the Premier Division. It is a competitive ten-team division where promotion to the Premier Division and relegation to the intermediate level are the primary betting narratives across a full season.

How the First Division Works

The First Division is a full league format with home and away fixtures across the season, running from March to November. The top two or three places earn promotion to the Premier Division, while the bottom teams face relegation to the third tier. Play-off scenarios create additional betting interest towards the end of the season.

Clubs in the First Division include a mix of former top-flight sides rebuilding, ambitious new clubs and consistent mid-table sides. Because the clubs are smaller and media coverage is limited, there is often value available for punters who follow the division closely.

Betting Markets

  • Outright winner (First Division title): Available at the start of the season
  • Promotion (top 2 or top 3 depending on season rules): Popular markets throughout the season
  • Relegation: Which club will be relegated; often long odds early in the season
  • Match betting: 1X2 available for all league fixtures
  • Top scorer: Available at selected bookmakers

Finding First Division Odds

League of Ireland First Division markets are available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers throughout the season. For the best odds, compare prices across two or three operators. Market depth is lower than for Premier Division games, so prices can move significantly closer to kick-off.

Betting on the First Division: What to Look For

  • Match result markets: Available at most bookmakers, though liquidity is thin. Prices may move significantly on team news.
  • Outright winner: Odds available pre-season and during the campaign. Early-season prices before form is established can offer value.
  • Promotion markets: To finish in top 2 or top 3 (playoff spot). These markets often close in September — back early if you have a view.
  • BTTS and over/under: First Division matches vary significantly in quality and style. Check head-to-head records and home/away stats for specific fixtures.
  • Local knowledge edge: National bookmakers price First Division matches with less precision than Premier Division or European leagues. Local knowledge of clubs' form, squad and venue is a genuine edge.
Which clubs are in the League of Ireland First Division?

The First Division changes each season due to promotion and relegation. The SSE Airtricity League website (sseairtricityLeague.ie) publishes the current season's participating clubs.

Can I bet in-play on First Division matches?

In-play markets for First Division games are less widely available than for Premier Division fixtures. Some GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry live markets for First Division games; check your preferred operator.

Is promotion from the First Division automatic?

The promotion rules can change. Historically the top two sides gain automatic promotion, with a possible play-off for a third promotion place. Check the League of Ireland rules for the current season.

How does wagering on the League of Ireland differ from the English Premier League?

Odds markets are thinner for League of Ireland than for the English Premier League, meaning larger bets can move prices. In-play markets are more limited. The benefit is that dedicated followers of Irish football may have an information advantage over bookmaker oddsmakers.
