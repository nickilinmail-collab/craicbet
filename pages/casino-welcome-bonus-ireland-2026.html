Wagering Cost Worked Example: 30× vs 50×

You deposit €100 and claim a 100% match bonus = €100 bonus. Wagering required on bonus + deposit:

  • At 30× on bonus only: wager €100 × 30 = €3,000. At 96% average slot RTP, expected loss = €3,000 × 4% = €120. Net: you lose €120 in wagering to clear a €100 bonus. The bonus has negative expected value.
  • At 40× on bonus + deposit: wager €200 × 40 = €8,000 expected wagering. Expected loss at 96% RTP = €320. Even worse.
  • At 1× wagering (rare): wager €100. Expected loss = €4. The €100 bonus is worth ~€96. Good value.

Red Flags to Check Before Claiming

  • Wagering above 35× on bonus + deposit — almost always negative EV
  • Slot contribution below 100% (e.g. table games at 10% mean you need 10× more play)
  • Win cap lower than the bonus amount (e.g. maximum withdrawal from bonus = €50 on a €200 bonus)
  • Expiry of less than 7 days — insufficient time to complete wagering
