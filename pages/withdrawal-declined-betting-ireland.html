Withdrawal Declined on an Irish Betting Site: Causes and Fixes

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

A declined withdrawal is frustrating, but in most cases it is fixable. Under GRAI rules, licensed operators must pay out winnings as a licence condition — so a persistent refusal without valid reason is a matter you can escalate. Here are the most common causes and how to resolve each.

Most Common Reasons for a Declined Withdrawal

1. KYC verification outstanding. The most common cause. If you have not fully verified your identity and address, the operator cannot legally process a withdrawal under GRAI rules. Upload clear photos of your passport or driving licence plus a utility bill and resubmit.

2. Bonus wagering not completed. If you claimed a welcome bonus with a wagering requirement, the operator will hold your funds until the requirement is met. Check your bonus status in the promotions section.

3. Same-method rule. You are trying to withdraw to a different method than you deposited with. Return the deposit amount to the original card first, then withdraw the balance elsewhere.

4. Payment method not supported for withdrawals. Some deposit methods (prepaid vouchers, some crypto routes) are deposit-only. Contact support to arrange a bank transfer alternative.

5. Account under review. Unusual betting patterns or a large withdrawal can trigger a temporary security hold. This is usually resolved within 48 hours.

What GRAI Rights Do You Have?

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, GRAI-licensed operators must follow a formal complaints procedure. If your withdrawal is declined without a valid reason: (1) submit a formal complaint through the operator's complaints form; (2) if unresolved in 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie. The GRAI can investigate and mandate payouts where an operator has breached its licence conditions.

Escalation Checklist

  1. Get the decline reason in writing from support (email or in-app message)
  2. Resolve any outstanding KYC or bonus requirements
  3. If no valid reason given, submit a formal complaint to the operator
  4. If unresolved after 8 weeks, file a complaint at grai.ie
  5. Keep all screenshots and correspondence

Step-by-Step: What to Do When a Withdrawal Is Declined

  1. Check your email for a message from the site explaining the reason — most declines trigger an automated email.
  2. Log into your account and check the Withdrawal History — the decline reason may appear there.
  3. Contact live chat immediately with your withdrawal reference number — agents can often process manually or escalate.
  4. If KYC is the issue, upload your documents and request the withdrawal be re-processed once verified.
  5. If no reason is given and the site is unresponsive, raise a formal complaint in writing via their complaints procedure (usually on the website footer).
  6. If the complaint is unresolved after 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie.

Your Rights if a Withdrawal Is Wrongly Declined

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, GRAI-licensed operators must process withdrawal requests in a reasonable time and must not withhold funds without documented justification. If you believe your withdrawal has been wrongly refused, you have the right to file a complaint with the GRAI. Retain all correspondence with the site (screenshots of chat, email confirmations) as evidence for any dispute.

Why was my withdrawal declined on an Irish betting site?

Most common reasons: KYC verification outstanding, bonus wagering requirements not met, trying to withdraw to a different method than you deposited with, or an account security review.

Can a GRAI-licensed bookmaker refuse to pay out my winnings?

No — paying out is a GRAI licence condition. An operator can delay while verifying your identity or checking bonus compliance, but an outright refusal without valid cause is a potential licence breach.

What do I do if a withdrawal is refused without explanation?

Get the reason in writing, resolve any KYC/bonus issues, then submit a formal complaint. If unresolved after 8 weeks, escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie.

How long does a withdrawal hold for security review last?

Typically 24–72 hours. For large withdrawals or unusual patterns, up to 5 business days. Contact support with your bet ID and withdrawal reference for a status update.

Can I complain to the GRAI about a declined withdrawal?

Yes. If the operator's internal complaints process fails to resolve the issue within 8 weeks, you can escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie. The GRAI has powers to investigate licence breaches.
