What is a Lucky 63 bet?

A Lucky 63 is a full-cover combination bet on 6 selections covering singles, doubles, trebles, four-folds, five-folds and the six-fold accumulator — 63 bets in total. Because singles are included, one winner returns something. The Lucky 63 is essentially a Heinz (57 bets, no singles) with 6 singles added. It is especially popular during the Cheltenham Festival and the Galway Races when punters want maximum coverage across a card of selections.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Lucky 63 bet?

A Lucky 63 covers 6 selections across 63 bets: 6 singles, 15 doubles, 20 trebles, 15 four-folds, 6 five-folds and 1 six-fold. One winner returns something.

How many bets in a Lucky 63?

63 bets: 6 singles + 15 doubles + 20 trebles + 15 four-folds + 6 five-folds + 1 six-fold.

What is the difference between a Lucky 63 and a Heinz?

Both cover 6 selections. A Heinz has 57 bets and no singles — 2 winners needed. A Lucky 63 adds 6 singles (63 bets) so one winner returns something.

How much does a Lucky 63 cost?

A €1 Lucky 63 costs €63. A 25c Lucky 63 costs €15.75.

Does a Lucky 63 include bonus terms?

Many bookmakers offer a bonus — enhanced returns if all 6 win, or boosted odds if only one wins. Terms vary by operator; always check before placing.
