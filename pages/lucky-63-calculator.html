Lucky 63 Calculator (6 Selections, 63 Bets)

What is a Lucky 63 bet?

A Lucky 63 is a full-cover multiple combining 6 selections into 63 bets. It includes every possible combination from singles up to the six-fold — 2⁶ − 1 = 63 bets in total. Because it includes 6 singles, a single winner always pays out. The Lucky 63 is the most expensive of the named Lucky bets and is typically used by experienced horse racing punters on major festival days who have six confident selections across a card. At €0.50 per bet the outlay is €31.50 — a manageable stake for the full coverage it provides.

Lucky 63 vs Heinz

Both use 6 selections, but they differ on singles. The Heinz has 57 bets — no singles — meaning you need at least two winners to see any return. The Lucky 63 adds 6 singles for a total of 63 bets, so one winner still pays the winning single. For a €1 unit stake the Heinz costs €57 and the Lucky 63 costs €63 — a €6 difference that buys the safety net of those 6 singles. If you are confident of hitting 3 or more winners the Heinz is marginally more efficient per bet; if there's any chance of a one-winner day, Lucky 63 is the better choice.

Lucky 63 example

Say you back six horses at decimal odds 3.0, 4.0, 2.5, 5.0, 3.5 and 6.0 with a €0.50 unit stake (total outlay: €31.50). Three win — selections 1, 2 and 3. Winning bets: 3 singles (1, 2, 3), 3 doubles (1×2, 1×3, 2×3) and 1 treble (1×2×3). Single 1 = €1.50, single 2 = €2.00, single 3 = €1.25, double 1×2 = €6.00, double 1×3 = €3.75, double 2×3 = €5.00, treble 1×2×3 = €15.00. Total return: €34.50 — a small profit of €3 from €31.50 outlay. Five winners would produce a dramatically larger return via the five-folds and six-fold accumulation. Is a Lucky 63 worth it?

The honest answer depends on your selections and strike rate. At €1 per bet the total outlay is €63 — not trivial. With three winners at short prices (around 3.0–4.0) you are likely to roughly break even. The bet starts paying handsomely with four or more winners at decent prices. At €0.50 or €0.25 unit stakes the Lucky 63 becomes more accessible while maintaining full coverage. Many serious horse racing punters use small unit stakes on Lucky 63 bets during festivals specifically because the upside when things go right is very large. It is not a bet for single-race dabbling — it rewards preparation across a full card of confident selections.

Frequently asked questions

How many bets in a Lucky 63?

63 bets: 6 singles, 15 doubles, 20 trebles, 15 four-folds, 6 five-folds and 1 six-fold.

How much does a €1 Lucky 63 cost?

€63 total. A €0.50 unit stake costs €31.50; €0.25 costs €15.75.

What is the difference between a Lucky 63 and a Heinz?

Heinz = 57 bets, no singles (need 2 winners for any return). Lucky 63 = 63 bets including 6 singles, so one winner still pays out.

Do I need all 6 to win on a Lucky 63?

No — even one winner returns the winning single. More winners activate more combinations and larger returns.

What is the biggest named bet?

The Goliath uses 8 selections and 247 bets (no singles). Lucky 63 is the largest in the Lucky series. Super Heinz covers 7 selections (120 bets, no singles).
