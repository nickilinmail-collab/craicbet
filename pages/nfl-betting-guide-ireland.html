NFL betting basics for Irish punters

The NFL season runs from September to February, culminating in the Super Bowl. Irish bookmakers offer comprehensive NFL markets, including the point spread (handicap), moneyline (match result at true odds), totals (over/under a points line) and Super Bowl outright winner. The NFL is the most bet-on American sport in Ireland, partly because games air on Sky Sports and streaming services and partly because the Super Bowl has become a major betting event in its own right.

Understanding the point spread

The point spread is the standard NFL betting format in the US and is widely available on GRAI-licensed sites. If the Kansas City Chiefs are –7.5, they must win by 8 or more points. The underdog at +7.5 wins the bet if they lose by 7 or fewer points, draw (not possible in NFL regular season) or win outright. The –110 price on each side (you bet €11 to win €10) reflects the bookmaker’s margin on a balanced line.

Moneyline betting in the NFL

The moneyline is simply a straight win bet at true odds. For a lopsided game, the favourite might be priced at 1.25 (American –400) and the underdog at 4.00 (+300). Unlike the spread, you are not betting on a margin. Moneyline is simpler than spread betting and suits punters who want to back a team outright without worrying about point margins. The tradeoff is that heavy favourites offer poor value at short moneyline prices.

Super Bowl outright and futures betting

Super Bowl outright markets open in September at the start of the season, with 32 teams listed from long (100/1 or longer for teams expected to struggle) to short (5/1 to 10/1 for the season’s leading contenders). Value-hunting in outright markets requires genuine knowledge of roster quality, coaching and schedule difficulty. Betting on a conference winner (NFC or AFC) is a popular halfway house between outright and match-by-match betting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the point spread in NFL betting?

The point spread is a handicap applied to both teams to level the betting. If a team is –7.5, they must win by 8 or more points. If they are +7.5, they can lose by up to 7 points and still win the spread bet. This creates near-50/50 bet opportunities even on lopsided matchups.

Can I bet on NFL matches from Ireland?

Yes. NFL markets are widely available from GRAI-licensed bookmakers operating in Ireland. The main markets offered are point spread, moneyline and totals (over/under) on weekly fixtures, plus Super Bowl outright and conference winner futures markets.

When is the Super Bowl and can I bet ante-post?

The Super Bowl is played in early February each year. Outright betting markets open in September at the start of the NFL regular season, allowing ante-post bets on team to win the Super Bowl at longer prices than available in the playoffs.
