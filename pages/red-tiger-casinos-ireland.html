Daily Jackpots: How Red Tiger's Network Works

Red Tiger's Daily Jackpot is a network of linked jackpots that have a "must-drop before midnight" mechanic. The jackpot rises through the day and guaranteed drops before 00:00. This means:

  • Evening play improves jackpot size: The pot accumulates through the day — evening sessions offer a larger jackpot pool than morning sessions (though win probability is the same per spin).
  • Many small jackpots, not one large one: Daily jackpots are typically €500–€5,000, not life-changing sums. The appeal is frequency of winning, not size.
  • RTP impact: Daily jackpot contribution comes from the base game RTP. Games with daily jackpots may have slightly lower base game returns than standalone versions of the same title.
