Cheltenham Festival 2027 Betting Guide — Dates, Key Races & Irish Punter Strategy

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Cheltenham Festival remains the pinnacle of National Hunt racing and the most-bet event of the year for Irish punters. This guide covers how the festival works, the betting markets Irish fans focus on, and the strategies experienced punters use — without invented tips or specific selections.

When Is the Cheltenham Festival 2027?

The Cheltenham Festival takes place over four days in mid-March each year. The 2027 festival is expected to run in the third week of March — exact dates will be confirmed by the Jockey Club. The four days are Champion Day (Tuesday), Ladies Day (Wednesday), St Patrick's Thursday, and Gold Cup Day (Friday). Irish punters travel in large numbers, particularly for St Patrick's Thursday, which produces some of the most heavily backed Irish-trained runners of the entire year.

Key Races by Betting Interest

Each of the four days carries a championship race. These attract the biggest pools and the longest ante-post queues:

  • Champion Hurdle (Tuesday) — 2m hurdle, often the season's most dominant hurdler. Short market; each-way value rare.
  • Queen Mother Champion Chase (Wednesday) — 2m chase, the festival's fastest race. Market often polarised between two or three horses.
  • Stayers' Hurdle (Thursday) — 3m hurdle, often produces the biggest-priced winner of the festival's main races.
  • Cheltenham Gold Cup (Friday) — 3m 2f chase, the sport's most prestigious title. All-weather betting interest; ante-post markets form from October.

Subsidiary races — the Supreme Novices' Hurdle, the Arkle Trophy, the Ryanair Chase — carry significant betting volume too, particularly for Irish-trained runners.

Ante-Post Betting at Cheltenham

Ante-post markets for Cheltenham open the week after the previous year's festival — over eleven months before the event. The best-value windows are generally:

  • Autumn (October–November): Before the new season's form is established; long-odds selections available on future festival entries.
  • December–January: After Christmas bumpers and novice hurdles; promising horses available at prices that shorten significantly by February.
  • Post-Leopardstown (early February): Irish Champion Hurdle and Dublin Racing Festival often clarify Champion Hurdle and Gold Cup market leaders.

Ante-post betting carries non-runner risk. If a horse is withdrawn before the race, most bookmakers will not refund ante-post bets unless non-runner money-back is offered on that specific market.

Each-Way Betting at the Festival

Standard each-way terms at Cheltenham are 4 places at 1/4 odds for championship races with large fields. In smaller fields (under 8 runners), terms drop to 3 places or 1/5 odds — always check terms before placing. The Cheltenham Festival is one of the few events where each-way betting produces consistent long-term interest because:

  • Fields are large (up to 25+ runners in some races)
  • Results are often non-predictable — Irish-trained horses sometimes dominate, British-trained occasionally sweep
  • Ground conditions vary significantly year to year (soft to good, sometimes heavy)

Responsible Betting at Cheltenham

The Cheltenham Festival concentrates significant betting activity into four days. Set a total budget for the festival before it starts, divide it across the four days, and stick to your allocation regardless of results. Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Cheltenham Festival 2027?

The Cheltenham Festival 2027 is expected to take place in the third week of March 2027, running Tuesday to Friday. Exact dates will be confirmed by the Jockey Club. The Gold Cup is traditionally run on the Friday.

Is ante-post betting worth it for Cheltenham?

Ante-post betting can offer better prices than race-day markets, but carries non-runner risk. If your horse is withdrawn before the race, most ante-post bets are lost unless the bookmaker is offering non-runner money-back on that specific market. Always read the terms before placing.

What are the each-way terms at Cheltenham?

Standard terms for championship races at the Cheltenham Festival are 4 places at 1/4 odds, subject to field size of 8 or more runners. Terms can vary between bookmakers and by race — always confirm terms before placing your bet.

Which races attract the most betting from Irish punters?

Irish punters focus most heavily on races with strong Irish-trained contenders: the Champion Hurdle, Queen Mother Champion Chase, Cheltenham Gold Cup, and the novice races (Supreme, Arkle, Ballymore). St Patrick's Thursday is the peak day for Irish betting volume.
