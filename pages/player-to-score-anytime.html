Player to Score Anytime: How Goalscorer Markets Work in Football

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 4 November 2026⏱ 6 min read

What Is Anytime Scorer Betting?

Anytime scorer betting asks you to pick a player who will score at least one goal at any point during the match. It does not matter when the goal is scored or what the final result is. As long as your chosen player scores at least once, the bet wins. This is one of the most popular football betting markets in Ireland due to its simplicity and the relatively high probability of top strikers scoring in any given match.

First Scorer vs Anytime Scorer: What’s Different

First scorer requires your selected player to score the very first goal of the match. Last scorer requires them to score the final goal. These markets carry significantly higher risk than anytime scorer because only one player can score first or last — even if your selection scores, they lose the first scorer bet if another player scored before them.

Anytime scorer odds are always shorter than first or last scorer odds for the same player because the anytime condition is easier to satisfy. The difference in odds reflects the probability difference between scoring at all vs scoring specifically first.

How Goalscorer Odds Are Priced

Goalscorer odds reflect a player’s expected number of shots, their shooting conversion rate, and the match context (home advantage, opponent defensive quality). A striker who averages 0.6 goals per match in a typical game against average opposition might be priced at approximately 11/10 anytime scorer. The same player against a weaker defence might shorten to 4/6; against a strong back line they might lengthen to 7/4.

Bookmakers apply significant margin to goalscorer markets. A game might have 22 players available to score anytime; the sum of implied probabilities across all players typically exceeds 100% significantly. This means goalscorer markets carry higher house edges than 1X2 markets.

Value in Goalscorer Markets

Value in goalscorer markets can come from identifying players whose role or recent run of form is not yet reflected in the price. A player returning from injury who the market still prices on historical metrics, a winger who has started playing as a striker with higher shot volume, or a central midfielder arriving late into the box who the market prices too cheaply relative to their actual xG contribution — these are the types of edges goalscorer specialists seek. 18+ — Gambling Awareness Trust: 1800 753 753, gamblingcare.ie.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a penalty count in anytime scorer?

Yes. Goals scored from the penalty spot count for anytime scorer bets unless the specific market rules state otherwise.

Does an own goal count for my selection?

No. Own goals do not count for goalscorer markets. Your selection must directly score or be credited with the goal.

Can I do an anytime scorer double?

Yes. Combining two anytime scorer picks in a double is common. Both players must score at least once for the double to win.
