Last updated: 5 November 2026

Sligo in Sligo is one of Ireland's established racecourses, hosting flat racing throughout the year. Whether you are heading to the track for the first time or betting online from home, this guide explains how to find GRAI-licensed bookmakers, understand the racing calendar, and bet responsibly.

About Sligo

Sligo Racecourse at Cleveragh Park hosts flat meetings during the summer season. The course has a strong local following and is particularly popular during the Sligo summer festival.

Horse Racing Ireland (HRI) publishes the full fixture list at hri.ie. For the latest going reports, declarations and results, the Racing Post and Racing TV are the primary reference sources for Irish racing.

How to Bet on Sligo Racing Online

  1. Open an account with a GRAI-licensed bookmaker and complete age verification
  2. Deposit using Revolut, debit card or bank transfer (credit cards banned under GRAI rules)
  3. Find the Sligo card in the Horse Racing section
  4. Select your bet type: win, each-way, forecast or one of the exotics (trifecta, Jackpot)
  5. Check the terms of any Best Odds Guaranteed offer before placing

Each-way betting divides your stake in two: half on the horse to win, half on it to place. The place terms (usually 1/4 or 1/5 of the win odds) and number of places paid vary by race and field size. Our each-way guide explains the maths.

Tips for a First Visit to Sligo

If you are attending in person, the racecard (available on the HRI app or at the gate) is essential reading. It shows each horse’s recent form, trainer and jockey. Going (ground conditions) matters significantly in Irish racing — soft or heavy ground suits different horses. Check the going report on the morning of the meeting.

Bring a debit card for on-course purchases; most tracks have card facilities at bars and food outlets. Betting with on-course bookmakers is typically cash-based — there are ATMs at most venues.

  • Sligo's sharp turns and undulating track strongly favour horses with previous course form
  • Field sizes at Sligo are often smaller than the big tracks — research is easier but prices are tighter

Can I bet online on Sligo races?

Yes. All GRAI-licensed online bookmakers carry markets on Sligo meetings. Online betting typically offers better odds than on-course bookmakers, plus the ability to bet in-play on selected races.

When does Sligo hold its main meetings?

Sligo hosts its main fixtures during summer season. Check the Horse Racing Ireland (HRI) website for the full fixture list and confirmed raceday dates.

Is betting on horse racing legal in Ireland?

Yes. Horse racing betting is regulated by the GRAI since July 2026. Online bookmakers must hold a GRAI Remote Betting (RB) licence. All on-course bookmakers operate under transitional arrangements while the GRAI retail licence process completes.

What is Best Odds Guaranteed and does it apply at Irish tracks?

Best Odds Guaranteed (BOG) means if the Starting Price (SP) for your horse is higher than the odds you took when placing your bet, you are paid at the SP. Many GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer BOG on Irish racing — check the terms before placing your bet.
