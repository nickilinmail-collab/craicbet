Betting on Elections in Ireland 2026: Political Betting Under GRAI

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Political betting — wagering on the outcome of elections, referenda and political events — is fully legal in Ireland under the GRAI framework. It is one of the most popular niche betting markets, particularly around general elections, presidential contests and by-elections.

Is Election Betting Legal Under GRAI?

Yes. The Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and the GRAI framework do not restrict the subject matter of bets — sports events, political outcomes and other events of uncertain result are all permissible markets for GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Election betting is an established, legal part of the Irish betting market.

Key Political Betting Markets in Ireland

  • Irish General Election: Party seat counts, next Taoiseach, majority/coalition outcomes
  • Irish Presidential Election: Outright winner market (held every 7 years)
  • By-elections: Constituency-level seat markets
  • UK General Election: Popular among Irish punters given the strong cross-border interest
  • US Presidential Election: One of the most bet-on global political events

How Political Betting Markets Work

Political betting markets work the same way as sports markets. You bet on an outcome at given odds; if that outcome occurs, you win. Odds reflect the implied probability of each outcome based on the market’s collective opinion, polling and punter money. Political markets can be less liquid than sports markets, so prices may move more with individual large bets.

Fairness and Integrity

Political betting raises questions about whether people with inside knowledge (politicians, campaign staff) have an unfair advantage. GRAI-licensed bookmakers take standard measures against insider abuse, including stake limits on unusual bet sizes around elections.

  • GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer Irish general election markets from writ day to close of polls
  • Election betting is classed as a novelty/specials market – higher margins than sports (10–15%)

  • Responsible gambling: never bet money you need on political outcomes

How Irish Election Betting Works

  • Irish general elections use PR-STV (proportional representation with single transferable vote) — counts can take 2–4 days
  • Markets settle on the official declared result — not on exit polls or projections
  • Presidential elections (every 7 years) and by-elections also have betting markets
  • EU Parliament elections (every 5 years) offer Irish constituency markets
  • Opinion polls are frequently used to price election markets — beware late-campaign swings
  • Betting on elections is legal in Ireland; responsible use means treating it as entertainment, not financial prediction

Is political betting legal in Ireland?

Yes. Betting on political events including elections is legal in Ireland under the GRAI framework. GRAI-licensed bookmakers are permitted to offer political betting markets.

Can I bet on the next Irish General Election?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry Irish General Election betting markets including next Taoiseach, party seat counts and coalition outcomes. Markets open when an election is called or in anticipation.

Are election betting winnings taxable in Ireland?

No. Betting winnings are tax-free for all players in Ireland regardless of the event bet on, including political events.

Do bookmakers have stake limits on election bets?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers typically apply stake limits to political betting markets, particularly as results approach. This is standard practice to manage risk and prevent potential insider advantage.
