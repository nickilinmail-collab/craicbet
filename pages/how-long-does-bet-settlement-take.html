How Long Does Bet Settlement Take on Betting Sites?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

The vast majority of bets on GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers settle within minutes of the final whistle or result being confirmed. But certain bet types, markets, and circumstances can push settlement to hours or even the next day. Here is what to expect.

Standard Settlement Timelines

For mainstream pre-match bets on football, horse racing, and GAA, settlement typically happens within 5–15 minutes of the event result being confirmed. In-play bets usually settle immediately once the relevant market closes. Same-game accumulators settle when all legs have completed.

Overnight settlement is most common with:

  • Outright futures bets (league winners, top scorers) — these settle at the end of the season
  • Results-dependent markets that require official confirmation (e.g., Player of the Match, official scorer attribution)
  • Live events where the feed was delayed and results need verification

Abandoned and Void Matches

If a match is abandoned before 90 minutes (or the minimum playing time specified in the operator's rules), bets are typically voided and stakes returned. The exact rule varies by operator — some void all bets, others settle on the score at the time of abandonment if a certain percentage of play has occurred (commonly 55–60 minutes for football).

Always read the operator's House Rules for the specific sport. This information must be clearly displayed under GRAI transparency requirements.

Disputed Settlements

If you believe a bet has been settled incorrectly, contact the operator's support team with the bet ID and your reasoning. Under GRAI rules, operators must have a formal complaints process. If unresolved through internal channels, you can escalate to the GRAI. Keep screenshots of the market at settlement time as evidence.

Accumulator Settlement

Accas settle as each leg completes. If an early leg is voided (e.g., match abandoned), that selection is typically removed from the acca and the remaining legs continue at adjusted odds. A single loss in an acca voids the entire accumulator — there is no partial payout unless you have specific insurance promotions active.

Why Bets Are Sometimes Delayed

  • Results under review: Stewards enquiries in horse racing or VAR checks in football can delay official results by 30–60 minutes, and bookmakers wait for official confirmation before settling.
  • Abandonments: If a match is abandoned before completion, settlement rules vary — typically 'void' if less than 55–60 minutes played in football, settled on the official result if completed in horse racing.
  • Manual review: Bets placed at odds that appear to be an error (palpable errors) may be reviewed and voided by the bookmaker.
  • Large winning bets: Some sites manually review large payouts before releasing funds.

What To Do If Your Bet Hasn't Settled

  1. Wait the standard settlement window (see table above) before contacting support.
  2. Check the site's Bet History — if it shows 'Open' it's still settling; 'Pending' may indicate a review.
  3. Use live chat and quote your bet ID and event name for the fastest resolution.
  4. If a bet remains unsettled after 48 hours past the event, raise a formal complaint.
How quickly are bets settled on Irish betting sites?

Most pre-match bets settle within 5–15 minutes of the final result. In-play bets settle as soon as the market closes. Futures and outrights settle at season end.

What happens to my bet if a match is abandoned?

Bets are usually voided and stakes returned if the match does not reach the minimum playing time (typically 55–60 minutes for football). Check your operator's House Rules for the exact threshold.

Why is my bet showing as unsettled the next day?

Likely causes: the result needs official confirmation (e.g., scorer attribution), a leg of your acca is still in play, or the operator flagged the market for manual review. Contact support with your bet ID if it remains unsettled after 24 hours.

Can a bookmaker change a settlement after paying out?

Yes, in limited circumstances — if the official result is subsequently reversed (e.g., a goal is disallowed by official review) or if there was a data error. GRAI-licensed operators must follow transparent rules on resettlement.

Do accumulators settle when the last leg finishes?

Yes. The full acca settles once all legs are complete. If one leg is voided (match abandoned), that selection is removed and the acca recalculates at adjusted odds.
