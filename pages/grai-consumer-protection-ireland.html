Your Consumer Rights with GRAI-Licensed Betting Sites in Ireland

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Betting on a GRAI-licensed site gives you a set of enforceable consumer rights you do not have on unregulated platforms. These rights exist because the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and GRAI licensing conditions impose specific obligations on operators about how they treat customers. This page summarises your key rights as a customer of a GRAI-licensed Irish betting site.

The Right to Clear Terms Before You Bet

GRAI licensing requires operators to present their terms and conditions in clear, plain language. This includes:

  • Betting rules and how odds are calculated and paid.
  • Withdrawal timescales and any conditions on withdrawals.
  • Bonus terms, including wagering requirements, minimum odds and time limits, presented upfront — not only in a full terms document linked from small print.
  • Circumstances in which an operator can void bets or withhold payments.

If terms are unclear, unreasonably long, or use deliberately confusing language designed to obscure customer rights, that is a potential GRAI licensing breach. You can raise this via GRAI's complaint process.

The Right to Responsible Gambling Tools

As a customer of a GRAI-licensed betting site, you have the right to:

  • Set deposit limits (daily, weekly, monthly) that take effect immediately on reduction.
  • Set session time limits and receive reality check notifications.
  • Self-exclude from one site or all GRAI-licensed sites via the National Gambling Exclusion Register.
  • Have those limits and exclusions respected — operators cannot ignore or circumvent them.
  • Not receive marketing communications designed to increase your spending if you have set responsible gambling limits.

See our dedicated pages on deposit limit rules and self-exclusion for full details.

The Right to a Formal Complaint Process

Every GRAI-licensed operator must have an accessible, documented internal complaint process. As a customer, you have the right to:

  • Submit a formal written complaint and receive a formal written response within a reasonable time.
  • Have your complaint reviewed by someone senior if your initial contact does not resolve it.
  • Receive a "final position" response that you can use as the basis for an escalated complaint if you disagree.
  • Escalate to the operator's ADR scheme and, for regulatory breaches, to GRAI.

See our step-by-step guide on how to make a formal GRAI complaint for the full process.

Protection of Your Funds

GRAI licensing conditions include requirements for operators to maintain arrangements that protect customer funds from the operator's own business finances. This matters if an operator becomes insolvent — your deposits and winnings should not simply disappear as unsecured creditor claims.

The specific protection arrangements (and the level of security they provide) vary by operator. Operators must disclose their fund protection arrangements to customers. When reviewing betting sites, it is worth checking how customer funds are protected — this information should be accessible in the site's terms or responsible gambling section.

Fund protection on unlicensed sites is typically absent or unverified. This is a risk Irish players bear when using unregulated platforms.

The Right to Fair and Non-Discriminatory Treatment

GRAI licensing conditions prohibit operators from using terms or practices that unfairly discriminate against customers or arbitrarily deny them access to services they have paid for. This includes:

  • Voiding winning bets without clear contractual justification.
  • Restricting accounts in ways not disclosed in terms.
  • Applying bonus terms retrospectively in ways that disadvantage customers.
  • Making withdrawal processes materially harder than deposit processes without regulatory justification.

None of these prohibitions prevent operators from managing risk legitimately (e.g. limiting stakes from consistently profitable customers) — but they must do so within the terms disclosed upfront and cannot act arbitrarily.

Your Rights vs. Rights on Unlicensed Sites

The contrast with unlicensed offshore sites is stark. On an unlicensed site:

  • Terms can be changed at any time without notice.
  • Winnings can be withheld or voided with no regulatory recourse for you.
  • No formal complaint process is required or enforceable.
  • No responsible gambling tools are mandated.
  • GRAI has no jurisdiction to investigate your complaint.
  • Your funds may not be segregated from the operator's business finances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What consumer rights do I have with a GRAI-licensed betting site?

You have: the right to clear terms before you bet, the right to responsible gambling tools, the right to a formal complaint process, fund protection, fair treatment, and the ability to escalate regulatory breaches to GRAI.

Are my funds protected if a GRAI-licensed betting site goes out of business?

GRAI licensing conditions include customer fund segregation requirements. The level of protection varies by operator and their specific arrangements — check each site's terms for details. This protection does not exist on unlicensed sites.

Can a GRAI-licensed operator change bonus terms after I have accepted a bonus?

No. Operators cannot materially change the terms of a bonus after a customer has accepted it. Retrospective changes to wagering requirements, minimum odds or time limits are not permitted.

Can a betting site withhold winnings without explanation?

GRAI-licensed operators must provide a clear reason for any delay or withholding of winnings. Withholding winnings without explanation or recourse is a breach of GRAI consumer standards.

What is the fastest route to resolving a dispute with a GRAI-licensed betting site?

Start with the operator's internal complaint process. If unsatisfied, escalate to the operator's ADR scheme, then to GRAI for regulatory breaches. The Financial Services and Pensions Ombudsman may also have jurisdiction for financial disputes.
