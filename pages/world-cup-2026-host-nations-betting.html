World Cup 2026 Host Nations Betting — USA, Canada & Mexico Market Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is hosted across the United States, Canada and Mexico — the first World Cup with three co-hosts. Each nation has automatically qualified and each will benefit from home advantage in at least some matches. This guide covers the betting markets for the three host nations and what home advantage means in a 48-team tournament.

Home Advantage at the World Cup

Host nations in World Cup history have generally outperformed their pre-tournament market expectations. The host nation receives guaranteed home-country matches in the group stage, reducing travel fatigue and maximising crowd support. Historical data suggests host nations advance from the group stage more often than their FIFA ranking would predict. However, the degree of advantage diminishes against the elite global nations.

The Three Host Nations: Expectations and Markets

  • United States: The most competitive of the three hosts in global rankings, with strong MLS development and several players at top European clubs. Expected to progress from the group stage and potentially reach the round of 16 or beyond.
  • Mexico: A regular World Cup participant with a strong footballing tradition, though historically exit at the round of 16. Home advantage could push them deeper in 2026.
  • Canada: Qualified for the 2022 World Cup for the first time since 1986 and has an improving football culture. Home advantage provides a boost; expectations are modest but hopeful for group-stage qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the World Cup 2026?

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is hosted across 16 stadiums in the United States, Canada and Mexico. The tournament runs from June to July 2026, with the final expected at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey on 19 July 2026.

How many teams are in the World Cup 2026?

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the first to feature 48 teams, expanded from 32. The group stage features 12 groups of four teams, with the top two from each group and eight best third-placed teams advancing to the round of 32.
