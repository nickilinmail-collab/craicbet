Cheltenham 2027 Gold Cup Day — Full Friday Race Card Betting Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Gold Cup Day is the pinnacle of the Cheltenham Festival — the Friday featuring the Cheltenham Gold Cup, the Grand Annual Chase, the Triumph Hurdle, the County Hurdle and the Festival Plate. For Irish punters, it is the most heavily bet day of the year. This guide covers the full day's card and how to approach it strategically.

The Gold Cup Day Race Card

Gold Cup Day typically features six or seven races, culminating in the Gold Cup in the late afternoon. The day includes a mix of Grade 1 championship races, handicaps and novice contests — providing multiple entry points for different betting styles. The Gold Cup is the most prestigious race in National Hunt racing and the festival's showpiece event.

The Cheltenham Gold Cup

The Gold Cup is a 3 mile 2 furlong Grade 1 chase with a typical field of 15–20 runners. It is both the culmination of the entire festival and the target for the sport's best staying chasers. The favourite has a reasonable (but not dominant) win rate — there are regular upsets and priced-up results. Each-way betting at 4 places 1/4 odds is widely available and historically rewarding given the competitive field.

Full Day Strategy

  • Set a Gold Cup Day budget before the first race and divide it across the card. Do not allocate everything to the Gold Cup.
  • Handicap races (Grand Annual, County, Festival Plate) offer each-way opportunities with large competitive fields.
  • Grade 1 races (Gold Cup, Triumph Hurdle) have smaller fields; win-only or each-way 3 places terms are more common.
  • Avoid chasing losses in the later races after a poor start to the day — the Gold Cup carries the highest stakes and deserves clear-headed analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Cheltenham Festival 2027?

The Cheltenham Festival 2027 is expected to take place in the third week of March 2027, running Tuesday to Friday. Exact dates will be confirmed by the Jockey Club. The Gold Cup is traditionally run on the Friday.

Is ante-post betting available for individual Cheltenham races?

Yes — ante-post markets for most of the 28 Cheltenham Festival races are available from the autumn onwards. Some races have markets open from the previous season. Non-runner risk applies — if your selection is withdrawn, most ante-post bets are lost unless non-runner money-back is offered.

What are typical each-way terms at Cheltenham?

Standard each-way terms at Cheltenham are 4 places at 1/4 odds for championship races with 8 or more runners. Terms vary by race type and field size — always check with your bookmaker before placing.

Which Cheltenham races attract the most Irish betting?

Irish punters bet most heavily on races with strong Irish-trained runners: the Champion Hurdle, Cheltenham Gold Cup, Supreme Novices' Hurdle, Arkle Trophy and the novice hurdle races. St Patrick's Thursday is the peak day for Irish betting volume.
