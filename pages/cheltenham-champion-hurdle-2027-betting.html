Champion Hurdle 2027 Betting Guide — The Festival Opener Explained

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Champion Hurdle is the opening championship race of the Cheltenham Festival, run on Tuesday over 2 miles. It is the definitive test of speed over hurdles, and Irish-trained horses have dominated the race in recent decades. This guide covers the market character, ante-post strategy, and each-way considerations for the 2027 renewal.

The Champion Hurdle: Race Profile

The Champion Hurdle is a Grade 1 two-mile hurdle race run at Cheltenham on the opening day of the festival (Champion Day, Tuesday). Key characteristics:

  • Speed-biased. At two miles, it rewards horses with exceptional pace. Stayers rarely win.
  • Often a small field. Championship quality limits the pool; fields of 8–14 are typical. Smaller fields affect each-way terms.
  • Favourite-friendly. Historically, the race produces fewer big-priced winners than the Gold Cup or Stayers' Hurdle — the dominant two-miler of the season tends to win if fit and well.

Ante-Post Strategy

The Champion Hurdle ante-post market is active from November once the new season's leading two-milers establish form. The key trials used as reference points are the Fighting Fifth Hurdle (Newcastle, November), the Christmas Hurdle (Kempton, December) and the Irish Champion Hurdle (Leopardstown, February). After the Irish Champion Hurdle, the market is typically set and prices for the front two or three are short. Ante-post value exists in October–December on emerging horses before they prove their form.

Each-Way Considerations

Because Champion Hurdle fields are often 10 or fewer runners, each-way terms can drop to 3 places at 1/5 odds in a small field. Always check the declared field size and confirmed each-way terms before placing. In a field of 12+, standard 4-place 1/4 odds apply and each-way interest increases for mid-priced runners. Use the each-way calculator to compare returns.

Familiar Patterns and Exceptions

Favourite-backing is statistically more reliable in the Champion Hurdle than in longer races, but upsets do occur. Season-long dominance can be misleading if a horse has not faced a true stamina or ground test. The race has also been won by horses making only their third or fourth hurdle start. As with all races, no result is guaranteed and form from trials is an indicator, not a prediction.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Champion Hurdle 2027?

The Champion Hurdle is run on the first day of the Cheltenham Festival — Champion Day, Tuesday. The 2027 festival falls in mid-March; exact dates will be confirmed by the Jockey Club.

What distance is the Champion Hurdle?

The Champion Hurdle is run over 2 miles (approximately 3,200m) at Cheltenham, making it the festival's principal speed test over hurdles.

What are the each-way terms for the Champion Hurdle?

Terms depend on field size. With 12+ runners, expect 4 places at 1/4 odds. Smaller fields may attract 3-place terms at 1/5 odds. Always check the declared runners and confirmed terms before placing.

What is the Irish Champion Hurdle?

The Irish Champion Hurdle is run at Leopardstown in early February at the Dublin Racing Festival. It is the principal Irish trial for the Cheltenham Champion Hurdle. The two races share many of the same leading contenders.
