First Club Relegated from Premier League 2026/27 — Betting Market Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The "first team relegated" market is a pure long-shot bet on which club will be mathematically confirmed as relegated from the Premier League before the other two. It is distinct from the "to be relegated" market and carries unique timing dynamics worth understanding before placing.

How the First Relegated Market Differs

Mathematical relegation is confirmed when a club has accumulated so few points that it cannot mathematically escape the bottom three regardless of remaining results. This typically happens in late April or May — but in extreme seasons, early confirmation can come in March. Clubs that have no form, no confidence and difficult remaining fixtures can be confirmed relegated weeks before the season ends.

The key distinction from the "to be relegated" market: a club can be relegated but not be the first. If two clubs are confirmed simultaneously (rare but possible), bookmakers have specific settlement rules — always check these before placing.

Who Is Typically at Risk?

  • Newly promoted clubs: Starting the season with a Championship squad and no Premier League experience is the highest risk profile.
  • Financial constraint clubs: Clubs under transfer embargos or with significant financial pressure cannot reinforce in January to escape trouble.
  • Managerial instability: Clubs that sack managers in October or November and struggle to appoint a replacement quickly.

Timing the Bet

The first relegated market is most useful for punters who want to back a specific club to drop before it becomes mathematically confirmed. The best prices come at the start of the season before the promoted clubs have demonstrated their Premier League quality. If a club loses its first four games, prices on "first relegated" shorten dramatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'first relegated' mean in betting?

The 'first relegated' market settles on whichever club is first mathematically confirmed as one of the three Premier League clubs that will drop to the Championship. Mathematical confirmation typically occurs in April when a club's points tally cannot be matched even with maximum remaining points from other clubs in the bottom three.

Is the first relegated market available all season?

Yes — the first relegated market typically runs from pre-season right through to mathematical confirmation. Prices shift significantly based on form, with promoted clubs and struggling established sides shortening after poor starts.

What if two clubs are relegated at the same time?

If two clubs are mathematically relegated simultaneously, bookmakers typically settle the market on the club that is mathematically confirmed first by date and time. If truly simultaneous, most operators will apply dead heat rules. Always check individual bookmaker settlement terms.

Can I combine first relegated with other bets?

Some bookmakers allow first relegated to be included in accumulator bets alongside other football outrights. This is a speculative combination and all legs of an acca must win for a return. The long timeframe means significant exposure across multiple markets.
