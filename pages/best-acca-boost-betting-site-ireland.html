How Acca Boosts Work

A standard acca boost adds a percentage to your winnings (not your stake) based on the number of legs in your accumulator. Example: a 5-team acca at 10/1 pays €1,100 on a €100 stake without boost; a 10% boost pays €1,110. The boost is applied after the acca odds are calculated — it does not change the underlying odds.

The Real Maths: What a Boost Is Actually Worth

To calculate the monetary value of a boost: (1) calculate your acca winnings at the base odds, (2) apply the boost percentage to the profit (not the total return). For a €10 stake 5-team acca returning €100 at 9/1: profit = €90. A 10% boost = €9 extra. The boost effectively reduces the compounded margin on the acca by roughly 0.5–1% per leg — meaningful but not transformative.

Minimum Odds: Where Operators Differ Most

The minimum qualifying odds per leg is the most impactful condition. An operator requiring 1.40 (2/5) per selection excludes odds-on favourites. An operator allowing 1.30 (3/10) lets you include short-priced European football or strong favourites. If your acca strategy involves including one near-certainty at 1.3, the minimum odds condition matters significantly.

Free Bet Accas and Boosts

Most operators do not apply acca boosts to free bet stakes. This is standard practice — the boost is a cash-bet feature. Some operators offer a separate “free acca” promotion (stake-not-returned free bet on accas) which is distinct from the boost and has its own conditions. Read both sets of T&Cs before combining offers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an acca boost?

An acca boost is a percentage added to your winnings when your accumulator wins and contains a minimum number of legs. It is applied to the profit portion of your return, not the total stake+return.

Are acca boosts worth using?

Yes, if you would have placed the acca regardless. The boost adds real value. Building an acca purely to trigger the boost adds more legs than your analysis justifies and reduces expected value.

What is the typical minimum odds for an acca boost?

Most GRAI-licensed operators require each leg to be at minimum odds of 1.30–1.40 (3/10–2/5). Check the specific T&Cs before building your accumulator.

Can I use an acca boost with a free bet?

Usually no. Acca boosts typically apply to cash stakes only. Free bet accas are a separate promotion type. Check each operator’s terms before attempting to combine offers.

How many legs do I need for an acca boost?

Most operators start acca boosts from 4 legs, with increasing percentages for 5, 6, 7 and 8+ legs. The maximum boost (50% at some operators) typically requires 8 or more legs.
