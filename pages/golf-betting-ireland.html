Golf Betting in Ireland

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Golf betting is a natural fit for Irish punters. Ireland has one of the strongest golfing traditions in the world — multiple Major winners, world-class links courses, and a Ryder Cup hosting record that underlines the country's connection to the game at the highest level. The structure of golf betting — large fields, four-round tournaments, and the dominance of each-way markets — rewards patience and research in ways that fast-paced sports do not.

Golf's Major Tournaments for Betting

The Masters (Augusta National, Georgia, April) is the most prestigious Major and the one with the most distinct course characteristics. Augusta rewards power and a high ball flight; familiarity with the course matters, and players with multiple previous starts there have a track record punters can study. Outright markets open months before and attract substantial betting.

The Open Championship (UK links, July) has the strongest resonance for Irish punters, rotating between legendary links courses — St Andrews, Royal Liverpool, Royal Portrush, Muirfield. Irish and Northern Irish players have historically performed well at The Open, and the links conditions are familiar to any Irish player. The weather unpredictability of UK links golf in July adds variance that can create value in pre-tournament markets.

The US Open (June) is the most demanding Major in terms of course setup, with narrow fairways and thick rough designed to make par an achievement. Players who trend towards accuracy over distance are historically well-suited here.

The PGA Championship (May) is the year's second Major and rounds out the spring Major season.

Main Golf Betting Markets

Outright winner: Bet on who wins the tournament. With fields of 100–150+ players, top-ranked players might be priced at 8/1 or 10/1 and rank outsiders at 500/1. This is the core golf market, and each-way terms make it more accessible.

Top-5/Top-10/Top-20: Finish markets pay out if your player finishes in the stated position range, regardless of the winner. Top-20 at 1/4 of the stated odds for a mid-price player is a popular lower-risk golf bet. Each-way terms vary by bookmaker and event — check before placing.

Head-to-head: Which of two named players finishes higher in the tournament? The two-way market eliminates the complexity of picking from a full field. If either player withdraws, the bet is typically void. Popular as a companion to outright bets.

First round leader: Who leads after day one? Early tee times and weather conditions create information advantages for punters who track the starting times and forecast carefully. Usually available as straight win or each-way.

Each-Way Betting: Why It Dominates Golf

Each-way betting is more central to golf than any other sport in the Irish betting market. The reason is simple: a 150-player field means even the favourite has at most a 15–20% chance of winning. Each-way terms at 1/5 the odds for a top-5 or 1/4 the odds for a top-10 mean a good-but-not-winning performance can still return a meaningful profit.

The key calculation is whether the each-way place terms offer genuine value at the odds available. At very short prices (say 6/1 and shorter), each-way bets have poor value because the place return is small relative to the win price reduction. At longer odds (25/1 and above), each-way bets can be excellent value if top-5 or top-10 finish is achievable. Use our each-way calculator to work out exactly what you are getting.

Ryder Cup Betting: A Different Market

The Ryder Cup is Europe vs USA in a team match play format held every two years. It is a genuinely different betting market from strokeplay golf. The main market is which team wins (or a tie/halved result). Individual session markets (foursomes, fourballs, singles) are also available on match day. The home advantage effect in the Ryder Cup has historically been significant — the host continent has won the last several editions, which is now well-understood by the market and reflected in the prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is each-way betting so popular in golf?

Golf fields of 150+ make winning bets very difficult. Each-way terms (typically 1/4 or 1/5 odds for top-5 or top-10) give meaningful place returns at long odds without requiring an outright win. Use our each-way calculator to check the value.

What are the golf Majors and when do they take place?

The Masters (Augusta, April), US Open (June), The Open Championship (UK links, July) and the PGA Championship (May). The Open Championship rotates between famous UK links courses including Royal Portrush in Northern Ireland.

What is a head-to-head bet in golf?

A head-to-head bet pits two named players against each other — you bet on which finishes higher in the tournament. If either player withdraws, the bet is usually void. Popular because it isolates one decision from the complexity of a full field.

How does Ryder Cup betting differ from normal golf betting?

The Ryder Cup is a team match play event — no individual winner. The main market is which team (Europe or USA) wins the overall points total. Individual session and match markets are also available.

What is the first round leader market in golf?

A bet on who leads after the first 18 holes. Early tee times and weather conditions create information advantages. Usually available as straight win or each-way.
