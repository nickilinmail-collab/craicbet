The Ireland Under-20 rugby team competes in the Six Nations Under-20 Championship and the World Rugby Under-20 Championship. For punters, the Under-20 competition produces the next generation of Irish rugby stars and offers a niche betting market away from the mainstream senior game.

Ireland Under-20 Rugby in 2026

The Six Nations Under-20 Championship runs in parallel with the senior Six Nations, providing six rounds of fixtures between Ireland, England, France, Wales, Scotland and Italy Under-20 sides. Ireland have been competitive in recent years, with the Under-20s providing a pathway into the full Leinster, Munster, Connacht and Ulster squads and ultimately the senior Ireland team.

The World Rugby Under-20 Championship is held annually and features teams from across the globe. It is a higher-profile competition than the Six Nations for the Under-20 level, with New Zealand, South Africa and France regularly dominant.

Betting Markets

  • Match result: Available for all Six Nations U20 fixtures
  • Six Nations U20 outright winner: Available from January each year
  • Handicap: Points handicap betting for all matches
  • Over/under total points: U20 rugby can be high-scoring
  • World Rugby U20 Championship winner: Available during the tournament

Betting Considerations

Player availability and Provincial Academy selections can change quickly at Under-20 level. Research the latest squad selections before placing bets. The Under-20s often have mixed results against France and England, who invest heavily in their age-grade programmes.

Betting on Ireland U20 Rugby: What to Know

Ireland U20 have been a competitive side in the U20 Six Nations and regularly qualify for the top division of the World Rugby U20 Championship. Markets are more limited than senior internationals but match winner and handicap betting is available on most GRAI-licensed sites for Six Nations fixtures.

  • Market depth: fewer markets than senior rugby — stick to match winner and handicap; total points and first try scorer may not be offered
  • Odds movement: U20 markets move on team news and weather; check lineups before the morning of the game
  • World Rugby U20 Championship: outright winner markets open months ahead — Ireland are rarely favourites but can offer value in the top-4 each-way markets
When is the Under-20 Six Nations?

The Under-20 Six Nations Championship runs alongside the senior Six Nations, in February and March each year.

Can I bet on Ireland Under-20 rugby internationally?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry Six Nations Under-20 betting markets throughout the tournament. Match betting and outright winner markets are available.

Which nations are strongest in Under-20 rugby?

France, England, New Zealand and South Africa are historically the most successful nations at Under-20 level. Ireland are competitive but face a stiff challenge from the more resourced rugby nations.

Are there in-play markets for Under-20 Six Nations games?

In-play markets for the Under-20 Six Nations are available at some GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Availability is lower than for the senior Six Nations.
