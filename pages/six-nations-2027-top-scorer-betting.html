Six Nations 2027 Top Scorer Betting — Most Points Market Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Six Nations top scorer market covers the player who accumulates the most points across all five rounds — a combination of tries, conversions, penalties and drop goals. For Irish punters, this market offers an interesting angle on the Championship that rewards analysis of team tactics and individual kicking reliability.

Who Scores Most Points in the Six Nations?

The top scorer is almost always a kicker who also carries a threat from open play — typically a fly-half or full-back who takes all conversions and penalties for their nation and can also score tries. Pure strikers rarely win the top scorer award, as points from tries (5 each) are supplemented by far more points from successful kicks (2 for conversions, 3 for penalties). A reliable kicker with a 75%+ conversion rate across the tournament who plays in a nation that scores frequently is the ideal profile.

Key Considerations for 2027

  • Kicking accuracy: Points from penalties and conversions dominate the total. A player with 85% kicking accuracy in a team that generates many kickable chances is better than a less accurate kicker from a higher-scoring side.
  • Playing time: A player injured mid-tournament loses the opportunity to accumulate points. Injury risk is relevant for ante-post backing.
  • Try scoring contribution: Some kickers also score tries regularly, boosting their total beyond pure kicking points. These dual-threat players are most valuable in this market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who typically wins the Six Nations top scorer award?

The Six Nations top scorer is almost always a kicker from one of the leading nations. Fly-halves and full-backs who take their team's penalties and conversions dominate the market. Players from Ireland, France and England have provided the most recent top scorers given those nations' success.

What is a typical top scorer total in the Six Nations?

A winning Six Nations top scorer typically accumulates between 60 and 80 points across five rounds, though exceptional seasons have seen 90+ points. The total depends on team performance (number of tries and kickable positions) and individual kicking accuracy.

Can a try scorer win the Six Nations top scorer award?

It is theoretically possible but very rare. Tries score 5 points each, while a good kicker can accumulate 15–20 points per match from conversions and penalties. A try scorer would need an extraordinary number of tries to overtake a consistent kicker.
