Bankroll Management for €5–€20 Deposits

A small deposit works best when your bet sizes are proportional. As a rough guide, stake no more than 1–2% of your session bankroll per spin or hand. On a €5 session that is €0.05–€0.10 per spin — which rules out most table games (minimum bets usually €0.50–€1) but is achievable on low-volatility slots.

High-volatility slots (big max wins, long dry spells) are risky with small bankrolls because the variance can wipe a €5 deposit before the game pays out at its stated RTP. Low-volatility slots or classic 3-reel games are better suited for play on a small budget.

Setting a time and loss limit before you start is good practice regardless of deposit size. Responsible gambling tools (deposit limits, session timers, cooling-off periods) are available on GRAI-licensed sites.
