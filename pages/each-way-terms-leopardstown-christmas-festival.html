Each-Way Terms at Leopardstown Christmas Festival 2026

Last updated: November 2026 — 18+ | Gambling Awareness Trust | 1800 753 753

The Leopardstown Christmas Festival runs from 26-29 December and is one of the highlights of the Irish jumps racing calendar. Each-way betting is especially popular here — here's how to understand the terms on offer.

Standard each-way terms at Leopardstown

Each-way place terms at Leopardstown depend on the number of runners in the race. The standard structure at most bookmakers is:

  • 5-7 runners: 2 places, 1/4 odds
  • 8+ runners: 3 places, 1/5 odds (most bookmakers) or 1/4 odds (some each-way specialists)
  • Handicaps with 12+ runners: typically 4 places, 1/5 odds
Always check the specific terms at your bookmaker before placing — BOG and each-way terms can vary between sites.

Best Odds Guaranteed and each-way at Leopardstown

BOG applies to both the win and place parts of an each-way bet at most bookmakers. If you take an early price on a Leopardstown runner and the SP is bigger at the off, you receive SP for both components. This can significantly improve your return on drifting horses in competitive festival fields.

Extra place offers at Christmas

Several bookmakers run extra place promotions during the Christmas Festival — paying 4 or 5 places instead of the standard 3, often at standard 1/5 odds. These are promoted as 'Extra Place' or '5-Place Racing'. Check promotional pages at participating bookmakers in the week before the Festival.

Key races and typical field sizes

The Leopardstown Christmas Festival features both Graded hurdle and chase races (typically 6-12 runners, standard 3-place terms) and handicaps (often 15-24 runners, 4-place terms at most bookmakers). The Grade 1 races on Day 2 (27 December) and the Leopardstown Chase on Day 3 attract the biggest fields and most competitive each-way markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many places do bookmakers pay at Leopardstown?
Typically 3 places for non-handicap races with 8+ runners, and 4 places for handicaps with 12+ runners, at 1/5 odds. Check each bookmaker's terms as they vary.
What are the each-way place fractions at Leopardstown?
1/5 odds at most bookmakers for 3-place races. Some each-way specialists offer 1/4 odds. For handicaps, 1/5 is standard.
Do extra place offers apply at Leopardstown Christmas?
Yes, most major bookmakers run extra place promotions during Christmas racing. Check promotional pages in the days before the Festival.
Does BOG apply to each-way bets at Leopardstown?
Yes at most bookmakers. BOG applies to both the win and place parts of your each-way bet if you take an early price and the SP is bigger.
