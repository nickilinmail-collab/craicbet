Champions League Semi-Final Betting 2027 — Last Four Market Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The UEFA Champions League semi-finals in April–May 2027 are among the most watched and most bet-on football matches of the year. With only four clubs remaining and the final in Munich just weeks away, every match carries maximum drama and betting intensity.

Semi-Final Structure

Two semi-final ties determine the two Munich finalists. First legs are typically played in the final week of April; second legs in the first week of May, leaving two or three weeks before the final. The draw is unseeded — the two semi-final ties can include any combination of the four remaining clubs.

Main Market Types at the Semi-Final Stage

  • To reach the final: The headline market — back a club to win their semi-final tie and advance to Munich.
  • To win the Champions League: The outright market now covers only four clubs. Prices are significantly shorter than earlier in the competition; value depends on the specific matchup.
  • Individual match betting: Full first-leg and second-leg markets with 1X2, over/under, BTTS and scorer markets.
  • To go to extra time / penalties: Popular prop at this stage as ties are closely contested.

Strategy Notes

Semi-final second legs are some of the most dramatic in football. Clubs defending a first-leg lead often adopt a more conservative approach — which affects over/under and BTTS markets. Clubs that must chase the game after a deficit are more likely to open up and create goal-laden second legs. This tactical context is important for second-leg match betting.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the Champions League semi-finals 2027?

The Champions League semi-finals 2027 are expected in late April and early May 2027. First legs are typically played on consecutive Tuesday/Wednesday sessions in the last week of April; second legs the following week. The final is then usually held in late May.

How often do Champions League semi-finals go to extra time?

Champions League semi-final ties rarely go to extra time — most are decided on aggregate over two legs before requiring 30 extra minutes. When they do go to extra time, it typically reflects a very tight and high-quality contest. Historical data suggests roughly 10–15% of two-legged semi-finals require extra time.

Is the semi-final draw seeded?

No — the Champions League semi-final draw is unseeded. Any two of the four remaining clubs can be paired together. The draw determines which club plays the first leg at home.

Should I cash out my outright bet at the semi-final stage?

Whether to cash out depends on your original stake, the current cash-out offer and your assessment of the remaining rounds. If your selected club faces a difficult semi-final, cashing out locks in a profit while reducing risk. If you believe in your selection, carrying the bet to the final offers the full return.

CL Semi-Final Format and Betting Strategy

Champions League semi-finals are two-legged ties (home and away). Key betting considerations:

  • Away goal rule was abolished in 2021 — ties level on aggregate go to extra time and penalties, not away goals
  • First-leg results heavily influence second-leg dynamics — teams with a lead often defend; teams trailing attack
  • Squad rotation is minimal at this stage — expect near-full strength teams
  • To qualify bets cover both legs combined, paying out before the second leg's kick-off result
