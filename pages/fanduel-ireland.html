FanDuel Ireland 2026: Why It’s Not Available & What Irish Punters Can Use Instead

FanDuel is one of the biggest sports betting brands in the United States, owned by Flutter Entertainment — a company headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Despite that Irish connection at the ownership level, FanDuel does not operate in Ireland, holds no GRAI licence, and cannot be used by Irish players. Here is exactly why, and what GRAI-licensed alternatives are available.

💡 Key Facts

  • FanDuel is a US-only sports betting brand — it does not operate in Ireland or any other non-US market
  • FanDuel is owned by Flutter Entertainment plc, which is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland — but this does not make FanDuel available in Ireland
  • FanDuel does not hold and has never applied for a GRAI remote betting licence

  • Since 1 July 2026, any operator targeting Irish customers must hold a GRAI licence under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024

What Is FanDuel?

FanDuel is one of the dominant online sports betting platforms in the United States. It was founded in 2009 as a daily fantasy sports company and pivoted to legal sports betting following the Supreme Court’s 2018 Murphy v. NCAA decision, which allowed individual US states to legalise sports wagering. FanDuel now operates in the majority of US states that have legalised sports betting, covering markets from football and basketball to baseball and golf.

The brand is owned by Flutter Entertainment plc, the global gambling group headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Flutter holds a majority stake in FanDuel and has positioned it as the centrepiece of its North American strategy. FanDuel is currently the market leader in US online sports betting by revenue.

Despite this significant US presence, FanDuel operates exclusively in the American market. It is not available in Europe, Ireland, or any jurisdiction outside the United States. The Flutter ownership link sometimes creates confusion for Irish users searching for FanDuel, but the brand itself has no Irish presence.

Is FanDuel Available in Ireland?

No. FanDuel is not available in Ireland and does not accept Irish players. FanDuel is a US-only product, designed for and licensed within the United States market. Irish residents cannot open a FanDuel account, make deposits, or place bets through the FanDuel platform.

This is a deliberate market decision by Flutter Entertainment. Flutter operates in the Irish market through its established Irish brands, which have been active in Ireland for many years and have obtained GRAI licences under Ireland’s new regulatory framework. FanDuel is not part of Flutter’s Irish strategy — it is their North American brand, tailored to the specific regulatory and commercial conditions of US sports betting.

⚠️ The Flutter Confusion Explained

Flutter Entertainment is an Irish company — it was formed from the merger of Irish brands and is listed on the Dublin and New York stock exchanges. But owning FanDuel does not make FanDuel an Irish product. Think of it like any multinational: a Dublin-headquartered company can own a US brand that only sells in the US. That is the relationship between Flutter and FanDuel. Irish players looking for Flutter’s Irish operations should look to Flutter’s GRAI-licensed Irish brands, not FanDuel.

Does FanDuel Have a GRAI Licence?

No. FanDuel has never held, applied for, or been issued a GRAI remote betting licence. Because FanDuel does not operate in Ireland, it has no requirement to do so. You can confirm this yourself by checking the official GRAI public register at grai.ie — FanDuel does not appear on it.

Ireland’s Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI) began issuing remote betting licences on 1 July 2026 under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024. Any operator wishing to legally target Irish customers must hold one of these licences. As FanDuel neither targets Irish customers nor operates here, the question of a GRAI licence does not arise for this brand.

It is worth being precise: the absence of a GRAI licence does not mean FanDuel is an illegitimate operator. Within the United States, FanDuel holds state-by-state sports betting licences and operates under robust regulatory oversight. The GRAI framework is specifically an Irish requirement for operators targeting the Irish market — and FanDuel simply is not such an operator.

Flutter Entertainment and Ireland

Flutter Entertainment plc is a Dublin-headquartered global gambling company and one of the largest betting and gaming groups in the world. Its origins are Irish — the business traces its Irish heritage through decades of operation in the Irish market — and its corporate headquarters remain in Dublin.

In Ireland specifically, Flutter operates through brands that hold GRAI remote betting licences. Those brands have been granted licences under Ireland’s Gambling Regulation Act 2024 and appear on the GRAI public register. Flutter’s Irish operations are therefore properly authorised for Irish players under the current regulatory framework.

FanDuel, however, is not part of Flutter’s Irish operation. It is Flutter’s North American brand, acquired as Flutter expanded aggressively into the US market following sports betting legalisation. FanDuel holds US state licences for the states where it operates, but no European or Irish authorisation. The two parts of Flutter’s business — its Irish brands and FanDuel — operate in entirely separate regulatory jurisdictions with no overlap for Irish consumers.

There is no publicly announced plan to bring FanDuel to Ireland or any European market. Flutter’s strategy appears to maintain distinct brand identities for different markets rather than unifying under a single global brand.
