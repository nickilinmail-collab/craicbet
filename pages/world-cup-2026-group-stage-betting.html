World Cup 2026 Group Stage Betting — 48-Team Format Guide for Ireland

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The FIFA World Cup 2026 group stage introduces a new format with 48 teams across 12 groups — the largest World Cup in history. For Irish punters, the expanded format means more matches, more betting opportunities and more potential value in group-stage markets.

The New 48-Team Format Explained

For the first time, the World Cup features 48 nations split into 12 groups of four. The top two teams from each group advance automatically to the round of 32. The eight best third-placed teams also qualify — meaning 32 of 48 nations advance from the group stage. This reduces the elimination rate in the group stage compared to previous 32-team tournaments and changes the risk calculus for group winner and qualification markets.

Main Group Stage Betting Markets

  • Group winner: Back a nation to finish first in their group and claim the top seed for the round of 32 draw.
  • To qualify from the group: Lower-odds alternative covering both the group winner and runner-up positions, plus the possibility of advancing as a third-placed team.
  • Individual match betting: 48 group-stage matches offer week-by-week betting across June 2026.
  • To be eliminated in the group stage: Back a nation to finish third or fourth in their group without qualifying. Useful for nations considered vulnerable despite high pre-tournament profiles.
