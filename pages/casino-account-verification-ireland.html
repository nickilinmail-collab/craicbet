Account verification (KYC — Know Your Customer) is a mandatory process at all licensed online casinos. It confirms your identity and protects against fraud, money laundering, and underage gambling. Here's what Irish players can expect.

Why KYC is Required

KYC is a legal requirement under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations. All casinos holding EU-jurisdiction licences (MGA, Gibraltar) or UKGC licences must verify player identity before allowing withdrawals above minimal thresholds. It's not optional — refusing to provide documents will result in withdrawal delays or account closure.

Documents Typically Required

  • Proof of identity — Passport, driver's licence, or national ID card
  • Proof of address — Utility bill, bank statement, or government letter (usually dated within 3 months)
  • Proof of payment method — Photo of card (front, with middle 8 digits obscured), or bank statement showing the account used
  • Source of funds — For large deposits or wins, payslips, bank statements, or other proof of income

When to Complete KYC

Complete KYC as soon as you open your account — before you need to withdraw. Casinos that have already verified you process withdrawals faster. Waiting until you need to withdraw adds unnecessary delays (often 24–72 hours for document review).

KYC Timelines

Simple verifications (clear ID document, matching address) are often processed within 1–24 hours at efficient casinos. Complex cases (multiple address changes, source of funds queries) can take 2–5 business days. Most casinos will notify you by email when verification is complete.

If KYC is Rejected

Rejection reasons: image quality too low, document expired, name/address doesn't match account details, or document type not accepted. Re-submit with a clearer image. If rejected again, contact customer support directly. If your account details don't match your documents (e.g. you used a nickname or different address), this needs to be corrected.
