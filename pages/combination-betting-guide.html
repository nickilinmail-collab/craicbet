Combination Betting in Ireland: Full Cover Bets and How They Work

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 4 November 2026⏱ 6 min read

What Is Full Cover Betting?

Full cover betting means placing every possible combination of doubles, trebles and accumulators across a set of selections. This is different from a standard accumulator, which requires all selections to win. With a full cover bet, partial returns are possible even if only two of four or five selections win.

The most common full cover bets are the Trixie (3 selections: 3 doubles + 1 treble = 4 bets), the Patent (3 selections: 3 singles + 3 doubles + 1 treble = 7 bets), the Yankee (4 selections: 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 fourfold = 11 bets) and the Lucky 15 (4 selections + 4 singles = 15 bets).

Trixie, Patent, Yankee: When to Use Each

The Trixie (no singles) requires at least two of three selections to win to generate any return. The Patent (includes singles) generates a return if even one selection wins, but costs 7 units vs 4. The Yankee on four selections gives multiple return paths: if only two of four win, one or more doubles pay. The Lucky 15 (four selections with singles) gives a return even if only one selection wins.

Use a Trixie or Yankee when you are confident enough in your selections that you do not need single insurance. Use a Patent or Lucky 15 if you want some return even from a single winner.

How Full Cover Bets Protect You

The protection in a full cover bet comes from multiple independent bets all rolled into one stake. If you have four selections and three win, a Yankee returns winnings from the three doubles plus any trebles containing winning selections. An accumulator on the same four returns nothing if one selection loses. This downside protection costs you in total stake: a Yankee costs 11 units vs 1 for an accumulator.

Full Cover vs Accumulator: Which Is Better?

An accumulator offers the highest potential return for a given stake but the lowest chance of any return. A full cover bet offers lower maximum return (the same stake is spread across many smaller bets) but higher frequency of partial returns. Neither is objectively better — it depends on your confidence level and risk preference. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Trixie bet?

A Trixie is a full cover bet on three selections consisting of 3 doubles and 1 treble (4 bets). At least two selections must win for any return.

What is a Yankee bet?

A Yankee is a full cover bet on four selections: 6 doubles, 4 trebles and 1 fourfold accumulator (11 bets). At least two must win for any return.

Is a Lucky 15 the same as a Yankee?

A Lucky 15 covers four selections like a Yankee but adds 4 singles, making 15 bets total. It costs more but returns something even if only one selection wins.
