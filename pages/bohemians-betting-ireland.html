Who Are Bohemians FC?

Bohemian Football Club — known universally as Bohs — are one of the founding pillars of Irish football. Founded in 1890, the club is based in Phibsborough, Dublin 7, and has played home matches at Dalymount Park for well over a century. That ground, with its terraces and atmosphere, remains one of the most atmospheric venues in the League of Ireland.

Bohemians have always carried a proudly working-class Dublin identity, and their fanbase reflects that: loyal, loud, and fiercely attached to the club's independence and community roots. The club has won the League of Ireland title multiple times and has been a consistent presence in the Premier Division for generations.

For bettors, Bohs represent a team with genuine quality when firing on all cylinders — but, like all League of Ireland sides, form can be difficult to predict over a long season. Understanding the club's rhythms, home record at Dalymount, and key fixtures is essential before placing any bet.

Bohemians in the 2026 LOI Season

The 2026 League of Ireland Premier Division campaign has seen Bohemians feature among the contenders at the sharp end of the table. Shamrock Rovers remain the benchmark for consistency in recent seasons, but Bohs and Shelbourne have both pushed hard for silverware.

Dalymount Park provides a genuine home advantage: the ground's compact size and vocal support can unsettle visiting sides, particularly those less accustomed to the atmosphere of an LOI night match. When assessing Bohemians in any betting market, their home record is a key factor.

The Dublin Derby: Bohemians vs Shelbourne

The fixture between Bohemians and Shelbourne FC — the Dublin derby — is the most emotionally charged match in the League of Ireland calendar. The two north Dublin clubs have contested this fixture for generations, and the intensity rarely drops regardless of league position.

From a betting perspective, the derby brings higher attention from bookmakers and therefore more market depth. It also brings an element of unpredictability: form guides carry less weight when the bragging rights of an entire city are at stake. Historical head-to-head records are worth studying, but upsets happen regularly in derby football.

The derby against Shamrock Rovers is another major fixture on the Bohemians calendar, attracting significant interest and coverage.
