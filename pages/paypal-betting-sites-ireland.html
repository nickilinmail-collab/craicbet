PayPal Betting Sites Ireland 2026 — Which GRAI Licensed Bookmakers Accept PayPal?

Updated 5 November 2026 · CraicBet Editorial

✅ PayPal is a legal and widely accepted payment method at GRAI-licensed betting sites in Ireland. Here's what you need to know about using it after the July 2026 regulatory changes.

PayPal and Irish Gambling Law in 2026

PayPal permits gambling transactions in Ireland, subject to the merchant holding a valid gambling licence. Since 1 July 2026, that means a GRAI remote betting licence for betting operators. PayPal's own terms require gambling merchants to be appropriately licensed in the customer's jurisdiction.

In practice, this means:

  • GRAI-licensed bookmakers can accept PayPal from Irish accounts ✅
  • Unlicensed operators targeting Ireland cannot legally process PayPal gambling payments ❌
  • If you try to deposit at an unlicensed site via PayPal, the transaction may be declined or reversed

Credit Card Ban — Does It Affect PayPal?

Ireland's credit card gambling ban took effect on 1 July 2026. This bans licensed operators from accepting deposits made via credit cards. The ban does not affect:

  • Debit card payments
  • PayPal funded from a debit card or bank account
  • Bank transfers
  • Digital wallets (Skrill, Neteller, etc.) funded from debit accounts
⚠️ Exception: If your PayPal account's primary funding source is a credit card, the transaction from your bank/card provider to PayPal may be flagged as a gambling-related credit transaction. Avoid this by ensuring PayPal is funded from your debit card or bank account.

Setting PayPal Gambling Controls

PayPal offers its own gambling controls separate from your bank or GRAI obligations:

  • Log into your PayPal account → Settings → Payments → Manage payment permissions
  • You can block gambling merchants from your PayPal account entirely
  • This is an additional responsible gambling tool on top of GRAI-mandated deposit limits

What to Do If PayPal Doesn't Work

If you experience declined PayPal gambling transactions:

  1. Confirm the betting site holds a valid GRAI licence (check grai.ie)
  2. Verify your PayPal account isn't funded primarily by credit card
  3. Check if you've previously set gambling restrictions on your PayPal account
  4. Contact PayPal support referencing your GRAI-licensed merchant's details
  5. As a fallback, use debit card or bank transfer directly
