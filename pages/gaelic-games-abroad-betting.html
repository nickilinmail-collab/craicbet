Gaelic Games Abroad Betting Guide 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The GAA oversees Gaelic games across the world through its network of county boards in Britain, North America, Europe, Asia, Australasia and other regions. For punters interested in the GAA diaspora, overseas championship events including the Connacht championship in New York and the provincial competitions in Britain attract betting interest.

Gaelic Games Beyond Ireland

The Connacht championship has a unique feature: New York GAA competes in the Connacht football championship, with an Irish county travelling to Gaelic Park in the Bronx for a first-round game each year. This fixture is one of the most unusual in the GAA calendar and attracts betting interest because of the home advantage New York enjoy on their own patch.

The All-Britain GAA championships cover football and hurling for clubs across Great Britain. Counties in Britain field teams in provincial structures, with an All-Britain final each year. These competitions are less prominently featured in betting markets but major bookmakers may carry some markets on request.

Overseas GAA Competitions

  • New York in Connacht championship: One first-round game per year at Gaelic Park, Bronx
  • London in Connacht championship: London GAA also competes in Connacht football
  • Club Championships: Club World Games and international club competitions

Betting on the New York Game

The New York vs Connacht province game is widely available at GRAI-licensed bookmakers when confirmed. New York are significant underdogs but home advantage in the Bronx is a genuine factor — Irish clubs have lost there before. Research the travelling county’s recent form and player availability before betting.

Betting on Gaelic Games Abroad

  • The main betting market for overseas Gaelic games is the London GAA in the All-Ireland Championship — London play in the Connacht Championship and can cause upsets
  • New York enter the Connacht Senior Football Championship at the quarter-final stage — a popular market for Irish emigrants
  • The Fitzgibbon Cup (colleges hurling) and Sigerson Cup (colleges football) attract betting interest in January–March

Does New York play in the GAA championship?

Yes. New York GAA competes in the Connacht football championship, with an Irish county travelling to Gaelic Park in the Bronx for a first-round fixture each year.

Can I bet on Gaelic games overseas?

GRAI-licensed bookmakers carry betting markets on New York and London fixtures in the Connacht championship. Markets for other overseas GAA competitions are more limited.

When is the New York GAA championship game?

The New York game in the Connacht championship takes place in May, typically in the opening rounds of the provincial championship. Exact dates are confirmed by Connacht GAA.

Which Connacht county plays in New York?

The draw determines which Connacht county travels to New York. The draw is made in spring and is covered on GAA media channels and betting sites.
