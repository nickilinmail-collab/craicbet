Euro 2028 Betting: Early Markets and Ireland’s Tournament Odds

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Euro 2028 host nations and format

Euro 2028 is to be hosted across the United Kingdom and Ireland, with matches planned at venues including Wembley, Aviva Stadium in Dublin and grounds in Cardiff, Edinburgh and Belfast. The host nation advantage is relevant for betting — home crowds, familiar pitches and no travel burden historically benefit host nations in major tournaments, though this effect is not uniform across all hosts.

Ireland as host and potential qualifier

As host, Ireland qualifies automatically for Euro 2028 without needing to go through the UEFA qualifying rounds. This removes a significant barrier for a nation that has struggled to qualify via the competitive route in recent years. Ireland’s squad development through the Stephen Kenny era and its successor will determine whether automatic qualification translates into genuine competitiveness in the tournament itself.

Early outright odds for Euro 2028

Early outright markets open months or years before the tournament and are dominated by the established European powers: France, Germany, Spain and England are typically grouped as the shortest-priced contenders. Ireland’s automatic entry and home advantage may be reflected in a price shorter than pure ability would warrant. These long-horizon prices carry enormous uncertainty and should only be treated as entertainment-grade speculation.

When to bet Euro 2028 outrights

Early outright prices carry the highest uncertainty and are priced conservatively by bookmakers to manage their risk. The best value window for outright betting on major tournaments is typically 6-12 months before the tournament, when squad shape is clearer, the draw has been made and qualifying performance is known. Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Euro 2028 being held?

Euro 2028 is being held across the United Kingdom and Ireland, with venues including Wembley Stadium in London, the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, the Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Hampden Park in Glasgow and Casement Park in Belfast.

Does Ireland qualify automatically for Euro 2028?

Yes. As a co-host nation, Ireland qualifies automatically for the tournament without going through the UEFA European Championship qualifying rounds.

When do Euro 2028 outright markets open?

Early outright winner markets opened at some bookmakers shortly after the host nation announcement. Full market depth typically develops in the 12-18 months before the tournament begins, once qualifying is complete and squads are more clearly defined.
