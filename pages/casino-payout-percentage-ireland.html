Casino Payout Percentage Explained — Ireland Guide

Casino payout percentage and game RTP are related but different concepts. Understanding both helps you evaluate casinos and game choices more accurately.

Casino-Wide Payout Percentage

Some casinos publish an overall payout percentage — typically 95%–98%. This is calculated across all games played at the casino over a period (usually monthly or annually). It's an average of all game RTPs weighted by the volume of play on each game.

Why Casino Payout % Is Less Useful Than You Think

A casino's overall payout percentage of 97% doesn't mean every game returns 97%. If 90% of players play slots with an average 96% RTP and 10% play high-RTP blackjack, the blended average will be pulled toward the slot average. The payout % tells you about the casino's player mix, not necessarily the best games to play.

What to Use Instead: Game-Level RTP

Game-level RTP (found in each game's info screen) is far more useful. If you want to minimise the house edge, choose games with the highest published RTPs: video poker (99%+), blackjack with basic strategy (~99.5%), baccarat banker bet (98.9%), and high-RTP slots.

How Casino Payout Percentages Are Calculated

Legitimate casinos have their payout percentages audited by independent testing laboratories — eCOGRA, iTech Labs, GLI, or BMM. Monthly audit reports are sometimes published on the casino's website or the testing lab's website. If a casino claims a payout % without an independent audit to back it up, treat the claim with scepticism.

Certified vs Uncertified Payout Claims

A certified payout percentage has been independently audited. An uncertified figure is simply the casino's own claim. Reputable MGA and UKGC-licensed casinos submit to regular independent audits. Look for eCOGRA or iTech Labs seals (usually in the footer) as indicators of independent certification.
