How Live Casino Works

Live casino studios are purpose-built facilities housing multiple tables — roulette wheels, blackjack tables, baccarat shoes, and game show sets. HD cameras film the action from multiple angles. The live stream is transmitted to players via their browser or app. Players bet through an on-screen interface that overlays the live video. Results are determined by the physical equipment (the ball, the cards, the wheel) — not software.

Live Casino vs RNG Casino

Standard online casino games use an RNG (Random Number Generator) to determine outcomes. Live casino uses physical equipment operated by real dealers. Both are audited for fairness. Live casino has a more social, immersive feel; RNG games are faster and can be played in demo mode (live tables cannot).
