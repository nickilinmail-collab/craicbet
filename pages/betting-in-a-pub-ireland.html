Betting in a Pub in Ireland 2026: Phone Betting & Live Odds Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Watching sport in an Irish pub and betting on your phone has become a natural part of the sporting experience. GRAI-licensed mobile betting means you can follow live odds in real time, place in-play bets on the game showing on the screen, and cash out before the final whistle — all legally and safely.

How Pub Betting Works Under GRAI

Using a GRAI-licensed bookmaker app on your phone in a pub is entirely legal. You are simply using an online service from your mobile device — the pub’s licensed premises does not affect your ability to bet online. The pub itself is not a betting venue and does not need a GRAI licence for you to use your phone while there.

In-Play Betting While Watching the Game

In-play betting while watching a live game on a pub screen is one of the most popular forms of mobile betting in Ireland. Key considerations:

  • Pub screens may have a 10-15 second delay compared to the live event — in-play markets update faster than pub broadcasts, so prices may already reflect events you are still waiting to see
  • Push notifications from your betting app can alert you to price movements or score changes in real time
  • Cash-out is available during in-play betting if you want to lock in a profit or limit a loss before the game ends

Responsible Gambling in a Social Setting

Social pressure and alcohol can affect betting decisions. Set a budget before you go out and stick to it. Use the deposit limit tools at your GRAI-licensed bookmaker to cap the amount you can spend in a day, week or month. Never bet more than you can comfortably afford to lose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal to bet on your phone in an Irish pub?

Yes. Using a GRAI-licensed bookmaker app on your phone anywhere in Ireland, including a pub, is fully legal. The pub's licence covers alcohol service, not gambling activity by its customers.

Are pub TV screens delayed compared to in-play betting markets?

Yes. Broadcast feeds to pub screens typically have a 10-30 second delay compared to the live event. In-play betting markets update from live data feeds that are closer to real time, so a goal may already be reflected in prices before you see it on screen.

Can I place bets at the pub if the pub has betting screens?

Some Irish pubs have betting terminal partnerships. Terminals in pubs operate under separate GRAI or transitional licensing arrangements. Using your own GRAI-licensed app is a separate activity.

How do I cash out an in-play bet from my phone?

Open your betting app, go to 'My Bets' or 'Open Bets', and select the bet you want to cash out. The app will show you the current cash-out value. Confirm the cash-out to settle the bet immediately at that value.
