Premier League Christmas Number One 2026 — Which Club Leads at Xmas?

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Christmas number one market — which club tops the Premier League table on 25 December 2026 — is one of the season's most popular novelty outrights. With a long history of the Christmas leader going on to win the title (though not perfectly), it generates significant Irish betting interest and is widely covered through November and December.

The Christmas Leader Market Explained

Bookmakers settle the market on whichever club leads the Premier League table at midnight on Christmas Day (25 December). In most seasons, 16–17 gameweeks have been played by this point, meaning the standings carry genuine significance. If two clubs are level on points, goal difference is used as the primary tiebreaker — exactly as the official table uses.

Does the Christmas Leader Go On to Win the Title?

Historically, the club leading the Premier League at Christmas has won the title in the majority of seasons — but it is far from automatic. There are well-documented examples of clubs who led at Christmas eventually finishing second, third or even outside the top four. The market is therefore best treated as an interesting short-to-medium term outright rather than an infallible predictor of the eventual champion.

Strategy for the Christmas Number One Market

  • Pre-season backing: Title contenders typically carry the shortest Christmas number one prices too. Early backing on a genuine title contender can offer marginal value over waiting.
  • Post-October backing: If a club is four or more points clear after 10 gameweeks and faces a relatively clear November schedule, mid-season backing can still provide value at shorter but more reliable odds.
  • Dark horse selection: Clubs with a relatively easy November/December fixture list can build a temporary advantage — this is where value sometimes hides.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Premier League Christmas number one market settled?

The market is settled on the club leading the Premier League table at midnight on Christmas Day, 25 December 2026. If clubs are level on points, official goal difference from the Premier League table is used as the tiebreaker.

Does the Christmas number one always win the Premier League?

No — while the Christmas leader goes on to win the title in most seasons, it is not guaranteed. There are multiple examples of clubs who led at Christmas ultimately finishing second or lower. The market is best treated as a medium-term outright, not a title bet.

When do Christmas number one markets open?

Markets typically open in July or August alongside the main outright winner market. Prices mirror the title market closely, though fixture scheduling can create small divergences — a club with an easy November–December run may trade slightly shorter for Christmas leader than their title odds suggest.

Can I bet on the Christmas number one in-play during the season?

Yes — the market remains live throughout the season and updates after each round of fixtures. The best liquidity is in October and November when the Christmas period is approaching and the standings are becoming clearer.
