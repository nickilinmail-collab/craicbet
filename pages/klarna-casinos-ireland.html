What is Klarna and Why It Doesn't Work for Gambling

Klarna is a buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) service that lets shoppers purchase items on credit and pay later — in a lump sum or in instalments. That credit element is precisely why it cannot be used for gambling.

There are two separate prohibitions in play:

  • Irish credit card gambling ban (February 2026): Irish regulations prohibit online casinos from accepting credit-based payment methods. Klarna's "Pay Later" and "Pay in 3" products are credit instruments and fall within this ban.
  • Klarna's own merchant policy: Klarna explicitly classifies gambling as a prohibited merchant category. Even if a casino tried to accept it, Klarna would decline the transaction.

This isn't unique to Ireland — Klarna excludes gambling across all markets where it operates. The same applies to Clearpay, Afterpay, and other BNPL services.

The Irish Credit Card Casino Ban — What It Covers

Since February 2026, Irish-facing online casinos have been required to block credit card deposits. This brought Ireland in line with the UK, which introduced a similar ban in 2020. The rule covers any payment that extends credit to the player, including:

  • Visa Credit and Mastercard Credit cards
  • American Express cards
  • Buy-now-pay-later services (Klarna, Clearpay, etc.)
  • Overdraft-funded bank transfers

FAQ — Klarna at Irish Online Casinos

Can I use Klarna to deposit at an online casino in Ireland?▾
No, in practice. Klarna is a buy-now-pay-later credit service, and Irish online casinos do not accept credit-based payment methods since the credit card ban came into force in February 2026. Even before this, Klarna's own merchant policy explicitly excludes gambling transactions.
Why is Klarna not accepted at online casinos?▾
Two reasons. First, Klarna is a credit product — it extends a line of credit to pay for purchases later. Using borrowed money to gamble is prohibited under responsible gambling rules, and Irish regulations banned credit card gambling payments in February 2026. Second, Klarna's own terms of service classify gambling as a prohibited merchant category.
What payment methods can I use instead of Klarna at Irish casinos?▾
The best alternatives are debit cards (Visa Debit or Mastercard Debit), Revolut, e-wallets (Skrill, Neteller), PayPal where accepted, Paysafecard prepaid vouchers, or bank transfer. All of these use funds you already own rather than credit.
Does the Irish credit card casino ban affect Klarna?▾
Yes. Ireland's credit card gambling ban, which took effect in February 2026, prohibits all credit-based payment methods from being used for gambling. Klarna's Pay Later and Pay in 3 products are credit products and therefore covered by this prohibition.
Are there any BNPL services accepted at Irish online casinos?▾
No. Buy-now-pay-later services — including Klarna, Clearpay and similar products — are credit instruments and are not accepted at licensed Irish online casinos. If a casino claims to accept Klarna for gambling deposits, treat this as a red flag and check its licence carefully.
