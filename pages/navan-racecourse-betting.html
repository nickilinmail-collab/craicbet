Navan Racecourse Betting Guide — County Meath's Premier Track

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Navan Racecourse is a dual-purpose track in County Meath, one of Ireland's best-attended provincial courses and home to some significant National Hunt racing in the autumn and winter season. Its proximity to Dublin (approximately 50km north-west) and the strong local racing community in Meath make it a well-supported course with competitive markets throughout the year.

Navan Racecourse: Overview

Navan is a left-handed, undulating oval with a 3-furlong straight and a home bend that makes it a sharp, turning track. Field sizes at Navan tend to be medium (10–18 runners in most races). The undulating character — there is a noticeable rise and fall through the back straight — means horses need to handle varied terrain. Good jumpers and adaptable flat horses tend to perform consistently here.

Key National Hunt Meetings at Navan

Navan's autumn National Hunt card — typically in November — is one of the best-attended race days in Leinster outside the Dublin festivals. Key races include:

  • Navan Novice Hurdle series: Attracts promising young hurdlers from leading yards.
  • Lismullen Hurdle: A Grade 2 hurdle that serves as a Leopardstown Christmas Festival pointer.
  • Fortria Chase: Grade 2 chase for two-mile chasers; can serve as an early season Cheltenham QMCC trial.

Flat Racing at Navan

Navan flat cards (May–September) feature competitive maiden and handicap fields. The course is a popular early-season destination for Leinster trainers. The draw can be a factor at Navan in sprint distances — statistics from recent seasons should be checked closer to the event as draw biases evolve with track maintenance.

Each-Way Betting at Navan

Standard each-way terms at Navan for fields of 8–14 runners are 3 places at 1/4 odds for conditions races. Handicaps with 12+ runners typically attract 4 places at 1/4. Use our each-way calculator to plan stakes and returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Navan Racecourse?

Navan Racecourse is located in County Meath, approximately 50km north-west of Dublin city centre.

Does Navan host both flat and National Hunt racing?

Yes. Navan stages flat racing from May to September and National Hunt racing from October to May, making it a dual-purpose course.

What is the Lismullen Hurdle at Navan?

The Lismullen Hurdle is a Grade 2 hurdle race at Navan, typically run in November. It is a recognised pointer for the Leopardstown Christmas Festival Grade 1 races and the Cheltenham Champion Hurdle.

What each-way terms are offered at Navan?

For conditions races with 8–14 runners, standard terms are 3 places at 1/4 odds. Larger handicaps attract 4 places at 1/4 odds. Always confirm before placing.
