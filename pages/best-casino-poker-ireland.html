Casino poker differs from poker rooms: you play against the house, not other players. Games like Casino Hold'em, Three Card Poker, and Caribbean Stud are dealt by the casino — your skill affects outcomes, but the house always has a mathematical edge.

Casino Poker vs Poker Rooms

Casino poker games (house-banked) are available at almost all online casinos. Poker rooms (player vs player) are a separate product. This guide covers casino poker; if you're looking for tournament or cash-game poker against other players, you'll need a dedicated poker site.
