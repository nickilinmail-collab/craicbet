What Is Esports Betting?

Esports betting involves wagering on competitive video gaming events. Professional players and teams compete in games such as CS2 (Counter-Strike 2), Dota 2, League of Legends, Valorant and FIFA at tournaments with prize pools ranging from thousands to millions of euros. Bookmakers offer betting markets on match winners, map/round scores, tournament outrights and in-game statistics.

Esports has grown rapidly as a betting category and is now available at most major GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers. Betting markets cover global events including ESL Pro League (CS2), The International (Dota 2), Worlds (League of Legends) and Valorant Champions.

Main Esports Markets Available in Ireland

Match winner: which team wins an individual match. Map winner (CS2, Valorant): which team wins a specific map or round. Tournament outright: which team wins the overall championship. Handicap: adjusted head-to-head on map or round count. Total maps: over/under on the number of maps played in a match. First blood, first to 10 kills and similar in-game specific markets are available at specialist esports bookmakers.

Reading Esports Form

Esports form assessment requires understanding team rosters (player substitutions are common and can significantly alter team strength), recent tournament results, head-to-head records, regional vs international performance, and patch updates (game updates can fundamentally change team strength in games like Dota 2 and LoL where meta shifts favour different playstyles). Data sites specific to each game provide detailed statistics for serious esports bettors.

GRAI and Esports: How It’s Regulated

Esports betting falls under GRAI’s remote betting licence category, the same as traditional sports betting. The key distinction from real sports is that match integrity risks differ — individual player throw events and match-fixing in esports have occurred at grassroots level globally. GRAI-licensed operators are required to participate in sport integrity programmes and report suspicious betting patterns. Frequently Asked Questions

Is esports betting available in Ireland?

Yes. Most major GRAI-licensed Irish bookmakers offer esports betting markets on CS2, Dota 2, League of Legends, Valorant and FIFA.

What is a map bet in esports?

In CS2 or Valorant, matches are played across multiple maps. A map bet asks which team wins a specific map within a match, rather than the overall match result.

How often do esports rosters change?

Esports rosters can change frequently, sometimes mid-season. Always check the current roster before betting as a team’s recent form may not reflect their lineup for the upcoming match.
