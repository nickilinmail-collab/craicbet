How Megaways Works

Standard slots have fixed paylines (10, 20, 243). Megaways reels change height on every spin — typically 2–7 symbols per reel. With 6 reels at maximum height (7 each), that's 7⁶ = 117,649 ways to win. The changing reel sizes are determined by an RNG on each spin.

Megaways Volatility

Megaways slots are typically high to very high volatility. The expanding reel mechanic creates the potential for enormous winning spins (Bonanza can produce 10,000x+ pays) but also means long dry spells. Your bankroll needs to be sufficient to ride variance to the bonus round. Low bankrolls are quickly depleted on high-volatility Megaways games.

Buy Bonus Feature

Many Megaways games include a "Buy Bonus" or "Feature Buy" option, letting you skip to the free spins round for 50x–100x your stake. This provides instant access to the high-variance bonus. Some regulators (notably the UKGC for UK players) have restrictions on bonus buys — check if the feature is available in Ireland at your chosen casino.

Megaways Licence: BTG and Third-Party Developers

Big Time Gaming licenses the Megaways mechanic to other developers (Pragmatic Play, Red Tiger, Blueprint). This means Megaways is now available from dozens of developers. Quality and RTP vary — the BTG originals remain among the best-regarded.
