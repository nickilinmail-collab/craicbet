How Free Bet Value is Calculated

A free bet’s real cash value depends on the odds you use it at. At longer odds, the profit is bigger but less likely to land. The formula below calculates the expected value — what the free bet is worth on average, factoring in the probability of winning:

SNR Free Bet Value
Cash Value = Free Bet × (Decimal Odds − 1) / Decimal Odds

Example: €10 free bet at odds 3.00
Cash Value = €10 × (3.00 − 1) / 3.00 = €10 × 2/3 = €6.67

SR Free Bet Value (full odds)
Cash Value = Free Bet × Decimal Odds / Decimal Odds = Free Bet amount
Why odds matter: At odds 2.00 (evens), a €10 SNR free bet is worth €5. At odds 4.00, it’s worth €7.50. The higher the odds, the higher the potential return — but also the lower the probability of winning. For matched betting, odds around 3.00–5.00 are typically the sweet spot to extract value while keeping risk manageable.
