Starburst RTP Paytable Explained

Starburst (NetEnt) has an RTP of 96.09% and low volatility — it pays frequently but in small amounts. It uses a 5×3 reel layout with paylines paying left-to-right AND right-to-left (10 total). Wild Starburst symbols expand to cover an entire reel and trigger a free re-spin (up to 3 times). Maximum win is 500× stake — the lowest of major slots, but Starburst's value is consistent, low-variance entertainment rather than jackpot chasing.
