22Bet Ireland Review 2026: What Irish Punters Need to Know

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

22Bet launched in 2018 and operates in over 190 countries, including Ireland. The site attracts Irish punters who want wider sports coverage than the local high-street names — especially lower-division football, GAA markets, esports and cricket. This review covers what 22Bet offers, its licence position under the 2026 GRAI regime, and the honest trade-offs.

Licence Status for Irish Players (November 2026)

From 1 July 2026, every operator taking bets from Irish residents legally requires a remote betting licence from the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI). As of November 2026, 22Bet does not appear on the GRAI public register. It is therefore an offshore betting site for Irish purposes — accessible, but operating outside the GRAI framework. This means no GRAI player protections apply, no GRAI dispute process, and customer funds are not subject to GRAI segregated-account requirements. Sports Coverage

22Bet covers 40+ sports. Highlights for Irish punters: Premier League, Champions League and League of Ireland football; GAA football and hurling during championship season; Irish and UK horse racing; esports (CS2, Dota 2, LoL) with in-play markets; and cricket including IPL with live stats. Lower-division European football is a particular strength — markets often appear same-day.

Odds and Margins

The overround on Premier League 1X2 markets typically falls between 5–7%, competitive with other offshore bookmakers. For major Champions League fixtures the margin often drops to 4–5%. Accumulator margins compound per leg — always check individual odds before building a parlay.

Deposits and Withdrawals

Irish players can deposit via Visa/Mastercard debit (credit cards are banned for gambling in Ireland since February 2026), Neteller, Skrill, bank transfer and cryptocurrency. E-wallet withdrawals typically process within 24 hours; bank transfers take 3–5 business days. 22Bet itself charges no withdrawal fee, though your bank may.

Mobile

22Bet offers an Android APK from its own website (not Google Play) and an iOS app. The interface is functional with a large sports menu; in-play betting and live streaming are available on selected markets.

22Bet suits Irish punters who want market breadth and are comfortable with an offshore operator.
