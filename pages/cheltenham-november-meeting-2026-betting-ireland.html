Cheltenham November Meeting 2026: Betting Guide for Irish Punters

Last updated: 12 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 12 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

The November Meeting, Friday 13 – Sunday 15 November 2026, is the first big weekend of the jump season at Cheltenham — three days of racing on the Festival track, and the traditional first look at Cheltenham Festival 2027 clues. For Irish punters it is also the first serious raid of the season: Irish yards routinely send runners across for the big Saturday handicaps.

Saturday Feature: the Big Handicap Chase

Saturday's feature — the Paddy Power Gold Cup (2.20pm, per the published racecard) — drew 44 entries at the initial stage. The sponsors installed Meetmebythesea (Ben Pauling, in the JP McManus colours) as 4/1 favourite, ahead of Blueking D'Oroux at 6/1 and last season's Festival Plate winner Madara at 8/1, with Dan Skelton responsible for seven of the entries. Irish-trained interest at the entry stage came from Henry de Bromhead (July Flower, Zurich) and Paul Nolan (Ifiwerearichman, Release The Beast). Final declarations land in race week — check the racecard before betting, and note each-way terms of 1/4 odds, 4 places are standard unless a bookmaker pays extra places. Entries and prices via Sporting Life, 27 October 2026.

How Irish Punters Should Bet the November Meeting

The big Saturday handicaps are deep, competitive races — typically 15+ runners, so each-way betting comes into its own. Look for bookmakers paying extra places on the feature races; on an 18-runner handicap chase, 5 or 6 places instead of 4 meaningfully changes your expected return. Our each-way value guide explains the maths.

Early-season form is unreliable: many runners are having their first start since spring, so fitness and course form at Cheltenham count for more than summer jumping form. The form guide covers how to read a seasonal debut.

Festival 2027 Clues

The November Meeting traditionally launches ante-post moves for March. If a novice wins impressively here, its Cheltenham Festival 2027 price will shorten within hours — which is exactly why we urge caution with ante-post betting this far out: non-runner-no-bet concessions usually don't appear until much closer to March.

Irish Trainers to Watch

Irish stables treat this meeting as an early statement weekend. Follow the declared runners from the major Irish yards in the week of the meeting via the racecards — we don't guess at entries before declarations are published, and neither should you: ante-post lists before final declarations carry real non-runner risk.

What's Next on the Irish Calendar

The following weekend brings a home double-header: the Punchestown Premiere Weekend (21–22 November) and Ireland v South Africa at the Aviva on the Saturday. Then it's Leopardstown at Christmas.
