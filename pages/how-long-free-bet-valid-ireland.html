How Long Are Free Bets Valid on Irish Betting Sites?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Free bets on GRAI-licensed Irish betting sites typically expire between 7 and 30 days after they are credited to your account. Miss the deadline and the tokens are forfeited. Here is everything Irish punters need to know about free bet expiry in 2026.

Typical Free Bet Validity Periods

  • Welcome free bets: 7–14 days (most common)
  • Reload / weekly offers: 7 days
  • Enhanced odds free bets: Sometimes as short as 24–48 hours
  • Refer-a-friend bonuses: 14–30 days

The clock typically starts when the free bet is credited to your account, not when you opt in or when you meet the qualifying conditions. This distinction matters: if there is a 24-hour delay between meeting the qualifying deposit requirement and the free bet being credited, your effective window may be shorter than advertised.

GRAI Transparency Requirements on Free Bet Expiry

Under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024, GRAI-licensed operators must clearly display all bonus terms, including expiry dates, in a transparent and accessible manner. If the expiry date is buried in terms or hard to find, that is a potential breach you can report. The GRAI’s inducements guidelines require operators to show key terms (wagering requirements, minimum odds, expiry) alongside the headline bonus figure in all marketing.

What Happens When a Free Bet Expires?

Expired free bet tokens are removed from your account automatically. You cannot claim them back after expiry, and operators are not required to extend them. Some operators send an email or push notification as the expiry approaches (often 24–48 hours before) — check your notifications settings are on.

How to Avoid Losing Free Bets

  • Read the expiry date in the bonus terms when you claim
  • Set a calendar reminder for 2 days before the expiry
  • Enable email and push notifications from the operator
  • Use the free bet on a suitable event within the first few days rather than waiting for the perfect match
