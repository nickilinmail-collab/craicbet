Hurling All-Ireland Quarter-Final Betting Ireland 2026

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The All-Ireland hurling quarter-finals take place in July at Croke Park, bringing together the two provinces’ best sides with qualifier runners. For punters, the quarter-finals offer a full week of hurling betting with in-play markets, enhanced odds promotions and the first chance to properly weigh the All-Ireland contenders.

How the Quarter-Finals Work

The All-Ireland hurling series brings together Munster and Leinster provincial runners-up and qualifier sides for the last eight. The two provincial champions go directly to semi-finals. Quarter-final fixtures are determined by draw, meaning you may get a Munster vs Leinster cross-provincial clash or two sides from the same province meeting.

Betting Markets for All-Ireland Hurling Quarter-Finals

  • Match betting (1X2): Win, draw or loss. Draws in hurling are possible but less common at this stage.
  • Handicap: Points handicap markets are common when two unevenly matched counties meet
  • Total points (over/under): Hurling is a high-scoring game; total points markets are popular
  • First scorer / anytime scorer: Available at major bookmakers for All-Ireland fixtures
  • In-play: Live betting is widely available for All-Ireland quarter-final fixtures

Preparing to Bet on the Quarter-Finals

Key factors to research before placing a bet: recent form in the provincial championship and qualifiers, fitness reports (available from county management press conferences), home/away advantage (all played at neutral Croke Park), and head-to-head record between the two counties. The GAA betting guide covers these factors in detail.

Hurling Quarter-Final Betting Strategy

  • Form going into the quarter-finals matters enormously. Teams peaking after a tough qualifier route are often better prepared than a provincial winner who had an easy run. Look at match sharpness, not just results.
  • Injuries and suspensions: Key player availability is a bigger factor in hurling than in some sports — 15-player teams where one forward can account for 15–20% of scoring output make injury news crucial.
  • Venue effect: Croke Park neutralises home advantage, but counties with large Dublin-based supporter bases can create a de facto home atmosphere.
  • Odds movement: Markets open 48–72 hours before the game and move significantly on team news (starting 15 announced, training reports). Early prices on perceived value are taken quickly.
When are the All-Ireland hurling quarter-finals?

The quarter-finals take place in July, typically on the two weekends after the provincial championships and qualifiers conclude. Exact dates are set by the GAA and published on gaa.ie.

Are there enhanced odds for hurling quarter-finals?

GRAI-licensed bookmakers regularly run enhanced odds promotions for All-Ireland hurling fixtures. Check the promotions section of your chosen operator. Note that wagering requirements may apply to enhanced odds offers.

What is the total points market in hurling?

The total points market involves betting on whether the combined score of both teams will be above or below a bookmaker-set line, for example over or under 45 points. Hurling is a high-scoring game and total points markets require research into the form of both forward lines.

Can I cash out on hurling quarter-final bets?

Many GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer a cash-out feature on in-play hurling bets. This allows you to take a guaranteed return before the game ends, either to lock in a profit or limit a loss. Cash-out availability varies by market and operator.
