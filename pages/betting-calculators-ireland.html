Which calculator do I need?

If you're betting on horse racing, start with the Each-Way Calculator for most bets and the Lucky 15 Calculator for the classic festival multiple. The Patent and Heinz are useful for race day accumulators.

For football, the Accumulator Calculator handles everything from a double to a 10-fold. The Cash-Out Calculator tells you whether the offer from the bookmaker is actually fair before you accept it.

If you've claimed a welcome bonus, use the Free Bet Calculator to see the real value and the Wagering Calculator to understand what you need to stake to withdraw any winnings.

For strategy, the Odds Converter is essential if your bookmaker shows fractional odds but you think in decimal (or vice versa). The Kelly Criterion Calculator helps size your bets correctly if you believe you have an edge. The Arbitrage Calculator and Dutching Calculator are for more advanced punters managing multiple bookmaker accounts.

Frequently asked questions

Are these betting calculators free to use?
Yes — all 13 calculators on CraicBet are completely free, run in your browser, and require no sign-up or personal details.
Which betting calculator should I use for horse racing?
For horse racing in Ireland, the most useful calculators are Each-Way (place terms vary by race), Lucky 15 (the most popular horse racing multiple), Odds Converter (fractional to decimal), and Patent (3-selection multiple with singles).
How do I calculate an accumulator return?
Use the Acca Calculator: enter your stake and the decimal odds for each selection (up to 10 legs). The calculator multiplies all the odds together and shows your potential return.
What is the difference between a Lucky 15 and a Heinz?
A Lucky 15 uses 4 selections and 15 bets (including 4 singles). A Heinz uses 6 selections and 57 bets (no singles). The Heinz covers more combinations but needs at least 2 winners for any return.
Can I use these calculators on mobile?
Yes — all calculators are fully responsive and work on any smartphone or tablet without downloading an app.
