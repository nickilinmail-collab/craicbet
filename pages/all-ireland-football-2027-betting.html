All-Ireland Football Championship 2027 Betting — Sam Maguire Outright Guide

📝 Mark Craic, editor📅 Updated November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Gaelic Football Championship is one of Ireland's defining sporting competitions, drawing passionate betting interest from counties across the country. This guide covers the key markets for the Gaelic Football 2027 season, how the market is structured and what experienced punters look for across the summer campaign.

Championship Format Overview

The All-Ireland Gaelic Football Championship runs from provincial championships in spring through to the All-Ireland final in summer. The province winners and wild card teams contest the All-Ireland series in a round-robin All-Ireland preliminary quarter-final and quarter-final format before the semi-finals and final at Croke Park. Provincial results feed directly into form assessment for All-Ireland betting.

Main Outright Markets

  • All-Ireland Gaelic Football winner: The headline market — back a county to lift the Liam MacCarthy (hurling) or Sam Maguire (football) Cup at Croke Park.
  • Provincial winner: Leinster, Munster, Connacht and Ulster championship winner markets run separately and feed into the All-Ireland draw.
  • To reach the All-Ireland final: Lower-odds alternative to the outright winner — back a county to reach the final regardless of the result.
  • To reach the All-Ireland semi-final: Available for counties with realistic but limited All-Ireland winning prospects.

Traditional Powerhouses and Rising Contenders

Dublin has dominated Gaelic football in recent seasons, though the gap to the chasing pack has narrowed. Kerry remains a constant contender with deep tradition. Galway, Tyrone and Roscommon have emerged as genuine championship threats. Looking ahead to 2027, the market is expected to reflect recent All-Ireland form and league performance as the primary indicators of championship readiness.

Ante-Post Strategy

All-Ireland Gaelic Football markets typically open in January or February. The best prices on traditional powerhouses come immediately — counties like Dublin, Kerry and Galway typically shorten after any form win in the league season. If backing a county that tends to come good in summer, waiting until after the spring league can offer a better price with more information.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the All-Ireland Football final 2027?

The All-Ireland Senior Football Championship final 2027 is expected to be held at Croke Park in late July or August 2027. Exact dates will be confirmed by the GAA. The final traditionally takes place on the last Sunday of July or first Sunday of August.

Which county is favourite for the All-Ireland Football 2027?

Market prices for 2027 will be published in early 2027 based on league form and squad assessments. Dublin, Kerry and Galway have been the most consistent All-Ireland contenders in recent seasons, though any of a dozen counties carry realistic aspirations.

Are provincial championship markets available separately?

Yes — Leinster, Munster, Connacht and Ulster football championship markets are available separately from the All-Ireland outright. Provincial winners are well-known in advance of the All-Ireland series and carry better liquidity.

How does GAA betting work for Irish punters?

GRAI-licensed bookmakers offer full GAA betting markets including outrights, match betting, handicaps and specials. Markets typically open in January and become most active during the provincial championships in May and June.

Strongest Counties and Historical Context

Dublin have won 9 of the last 12 All-Ireland titles (2011–2023) but Kerry ended their run in 2022, with Galway winning in 2023. For 2027 betting, consider:

  • Dublin, Kerry, and Galway historically the shortest prices early in the season
  • Ulster teams (Tyrone, Armagh, Donegal, Derry) regularly reach the last four
  • Connacht championship winner often emerges as an outsider value pick
  • All-Ireland final crowds at Croke Park average 82,000 — neutral venue for both finalists
