Six Nations 2027 Betting: Outright Winner, Match and Player Markets

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

The Six Nations Championship is rugby union’s premier annual international competition and one of the biggest betting events on the Irish calendar. Six teams — Ireland, England, France, Scotland, Wales and Italy — each play five matches between late January and mid-March. The tournament generates enormous betting interest in Ireland, where rugby has a substantial following across all provinces. This guide covers what to know for 2027 as the season approaches.

Six Nations 2027 Dates

The Six Nations Championship traditionally begins in late January or early February and runs to Super Saturday in mid-March, when three matches are played simultaneously and the title is frequently decided. For 2027, the tournament is expected to follow the same window — starting around 30 January or 6 February 2027 and concluding around 20 March 2027. Official fixtures are confirmed by Six Nations Rugby and published at sixnationsrugby.com.

Outright betting markets for 2027 open immediately after the 2026 tournament, and prices evolve through the autumn internationals (November 2026) as form and squad strength become clearer.

Outright Winner Betting

The outright championship market is the most popular single betting proposition for the Six Nations. Ireland, France and England typically enter each tournament as the most prominent contenders, with the market influenced heavily by the World Cup cycle and autumn international results.

Key considerations for outright betting:

  • Home advantage. Every team plays three home matches and two away. Teams benefit meaningfully from home crowd support; the scheduling of home and away fixtures against stronger opponents matters enormously for championship prospects.
  • Squad form coming in. November international results give a live read on squad depth and form under new or continuing coaches. Watch for injuries to key players in the autumn series.
  • Grand Slam market. Separate from the championship winner, this pays out only if a team wins all five matches. It is priced at longer odds and is a more difficult outcome to achieve. Ireland, France and England have all achieved Grand Slams in recent years.
  • Wooden Spoon market. Italy and Scotland have historically been the main contenders for the bottom position, though Scotland have shown improvement. The wooden spoon market offers different dynamics from the top-of-table competition.

Ireland's Chances in Six Nations 2027

Ireland have been one of the top-ranked nations in world rugby and have delivered competitive Six Nations campaigns in recent years. The key variables going into 2027 are squad depth in the tight five and back row following the autumn series, the fitness of core players, and the fixture schedule (specifically whether Ireland’s toughest away match falls early or late in the tournament).

Irish bettors should be aware that Ireland often trade at short odds given the volume of domestic support in the betting market. This means Ireland’s price may not always reflect cold value. France and England can sometimes represent longer prices than their realistic probability warrants in Irish-dominated markets.

In-Play and Match Betting Markets

Match betting for individual Six Nations fixtures generates very high liquidity. Available markets include:

  • Match result (handicap). When one team is a heavy favourite, handicap betting creates a more balanced market. Handicap lines are closely watched, as the spread can vary significantly between bookmakers on the same match.
  • Total points (over/under). Rugby union scoring patterns are relatively predictable at international level. High-scoring teams and muddy February conditions both influence this market.
  • First try scorer and anytime try scorer. High-volume markets. Wing and full-back positions carry the highest try-scoring rates; this market is statistically tractable for systematic bettors.
  • Half-time result. Six Nations matches can swing dramatically at half-time as coaches adjust. The first-half market is sometimes priced independently from the full-match winner.
  • In-play betting. Six Nations fixtures attract very high in-play volumes. Odds move rapidly when tries are scored or when a team has a numerical disadvantage (yellow cards). Managing in-play stakes carefully is important.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Six Nations 2027 start?

The Six Nations Championship traditionally begins in late January or early February and runs to mid-March. The 2027 tournament is expected to begin in late January or early February 2027. Official fixture dates are announced by Six Nations Rugby — check sixnationsrugby.com for confirmation.

Can I bet on individual player markets in the Six Nations?

Yes. Top points scorer, top try scorer and first try scorer per match are widely available. Player of the Tournament outright markets are also offered by most bookmakers. These markets attract high liquidity given the profile of the competition.

What is the Grand Slam and how does it affect betting markets?

A Grand Slam means winning all five matches in the tournament. It is priced as a separate outright market. The odds of winning the outright championship and winning the Grand Slam differ, since it is possible to win the championship with one draw or defeat if other results go your way.
