Six Nations 2027 Betting: Ireland Open and Close at the Aviva

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated 1 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

The 2027 Guinness Six Nations runs from 5 February to 13 March 2027, and the fixture computer has been kind to Irish punters' nerves twice over: Ireland open and close the championship at the Aviva. This preview sets out the schedule, the markets that matter, and — in our usual style — where the prices tend to be hype rather than value.

Ireland's 2027 schedule

Ireland start with a Friday-night blockbuster against England in Dublin on 5 February (8.10pm), then travel to Italy in Rome on 13 February and Wales on 20 February. After the rest week it's Scotland at Murrayfield on Friday 5 March (8.10pm), before the finale at home to France on Saturday 13 March at 8.10pm — the last match of Super Saturday. Three of five games under lights, and the two hardest opponents on home soil.

Why fixture order moves the market

Home games against England and France are the single biggest structural edge a Six Nations side can get, and bookmakers price it in: expect Ireland to be prominent in the outright market before a ball is kicked. The flip side is three away trips in the middle — Rome has stopped being a gimme, and Friday night at Murrayfield is a classic banana skin. The market tends to price Ireland's ceiling; it is the floor that decides outright bets.

Grand Slam vs outright: know the difference

The outright (championship winner) pays on points, with ties split on points difference — a team can lose a match and still win it. Grand Slam markets need five from five and are priced accordingly. The honest maths: if a side is around 11/8 to win the title, the same side's Slam price must absorb the probability of any single slip across five games. Slam odds look generous precisely because slams are rare — there is no hidden value in the bigger number.

When prices are value vs hype

Six Nations outright markets are shortest in the week after the autumn internationals, when recency bias peaks. Historically the better moments to bet are early summer (before hype builds) or after Round 1, when one result swings prices further than one match of evidence justifies. A Friday-opener between the two pre-tournament favourites — exactly what 5 February serves up — is the kind of match that overcorrects the whole market overnight.

Match-level angles

Handicaps, not match winners, are where Six Nations betting really lives — favourites in this tournament are often 1/5 or shorter head-to-head. Our rugby betting guide covers handicap lines, total points and the cards/discipline markets that have grown with rugby's sin-bin era.

A note on discipline

Five rounds over six weekends invites betting every match out of habit. Pick spots instead — and remember every outright or Slam bet is money locked up until mid-March.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the 2027 Six Nations start and finish?

From Friday 5 February to Saturday 13 March 2027. Ireland open against England in Dublin on the Friday night and close the tournament at home to France on Super Saturday.

What home games do Ireland have in the 2027 Six Nations?

Two, but the big two: England (5 February) and France (13 March), both at the Aviva. The away trips are Rome, Wales and Murrayfield.

Is a Grand Slam bet better value than the outright?

Usually not. The outright can survive a defeat; the Slam cannot. The longer Slam price is the market charging you fairly for five-from-five — the bigger number is not hidden value.
