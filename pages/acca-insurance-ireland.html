Acca Insurance Ireland: How It Works

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Acca insurance (also called acca protection or accumulator insurance) is a promotion where if one leg of your accumulator loses, you receive your stake back as a free bet. It is designed to give you a second chance when your acca falls at the last leg.

Typical Acca Insurance Conditions

ConditionTypical Requirement
Minimum legsUsually 5 legs (some offer 4+)
Minimum odds per legUsually 1.3 (3/10) or higher per selection
Minimum acca oddsOften 3.0 or higher total
Refund typeFree bet (stake not returned) in almost all cases
Refund limitUsually capped at €10–€25
Free bet expiryUsually 7 days

Acca Insurance: Worked Example

You place a 5-leg acca at €10. Four legs win, one leg loses at the last moment. With acca insurance, you receive €10 back as a free bet. The free bet is SNR (stake not returned), meaning if you win with it at 3.0, you receive €20 profit (€10 × 3.0 minus the €10 stake = €20). The real value of the free bet depends on the odds you use it on.

Is Acca Insurance Worth It

Acca insurance provides genuine psychological value but limited mathematical value. The refund is a free bet (not cash), capped at a small amount, and requires you to build a qualifying acca to access it. The best use: take acca insurance as a standard feature when it comes at no additional cost (most bookmakers include it in their regular promo suite). Do not change which acca you build in order to qualify — build the acca you want and accept the insurance as a bonus if it applies.
