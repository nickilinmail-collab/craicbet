KYC Verification — How to Speed Up Your First Withdrawal

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is required by all licensed online casinos before processing your first withdrawal. This anti-money laundering requirement is standard across the industry and will become even more rigorous when GRAI licensed casinos begin operating for online gaming.

What you'll need to provide:

  • Photo ID — A valid passport, Irish driving licence, or national identity card. The document must be in date and clearly legible.
  • Proof of Address — A utility bill (electricity, gas, broadband), bank statement, or government correspondence dated within the last 3 months. The name and address must match your casino account details.
  • Payment Verification — A photo of the front of your debit card (you can cover the middle 8 digits) or a screenshot of your Revolut account showing your name.

Tips to speed up the process:

  • Submit your documents immediately after registering — don't wait until your first withdrawal. Many casinos allow early verification.
  • Take clear, well-lit photos. Avoid glare, shadows, and cut-off edges. A blurry document will be rejected and require resubmission.
  • Ensure the name on your casino account exactly matches your ID. Even small differences (e.g., "Padraig" vs "Patrick") can cause delays.
  • Use the casino's mobile app to upload documents — phone cameras typically produce better quality than webcams.
  • Check your email for verification requests. Some casinos won't notify you within their platform — the request may come via email only.

At the fastest casinos we've tested, KYC verification is completed within 1-2 hours. Average verification time across all casinos is 24-48 hours. Once verified, your account is cleared for instant processing on future withdrawals.
