Bohemian FC: Club Profile for Bettors

Bohemian Football Club — known as Bohs — are one of the founding pillars of Irish football, founded in 1890 and based in Phibsborough, Dublin 7. Their home ground, Dalymount Park, is one of the most historic and atmospheric football venues in Ireland, with a compact layout and terracing that creates a distinctive atmosphere on match nights.

For punters, Bohs represent a club with strong market recognition. Their history, Derby match pedigree, and consistent Premier Division presence mean their fixtures are well covered by GRAI-licensed bookmakers. Understanding their patterns — home dominance at Dalymount, the intensity of derby fixtures, and their European match performances — is key to making informed betting decisions.

No prediction is guaranteed. The LOI is a competitive league and results are often determined by fine margins. Use the information in this guide alongside current form and team news before making any betting decisions.

Bohemian FC Home Advantage: Dalymount Park Analysis

Dalymount Park is one of the factors that makes Bohemians a genuinely interesting betting proposition at home. The ground's compact dimensions can disrupt the rhythm of visiting sides accustomed to larger pitches. The vocal and passionate Phibsborough crowd — particularly in evening fixtures and derbies — creates an atmosphere that visiting teams can find intimidating.

When assessing Bohs at home, look at:

  • Their home record over the last 10 LOI matches at Dalymount
  • The head-to-head record at Dalymount against the specific opponent
  • How often home matches at Dalymount produce goals — both for and against
  • Whether the fixture is a weeknight under lights (typically more intense) or a weekend afternoon

The Dublin Derby: Betting Tips

The Dublin derby between Bohs and Shelbourne is the fixture that generates the most betting interest in the League of Ireland calendar alongside Shamrock Rovers derbies. For punters, the derby brings both opportunity and risk:

  • Derby form is often independent of league form — a mid-table Bohs side can outperform expectations against a top-two Shelbourne side
  • Goals are not always guaranteed — early-season derbies can be cautious and tight; later in the season they may open up as stakes increase
  • Bookmakers typically offer enhanced markets for derbies: anytime scorer, correct score, and half-time/full-time are often available where they might not be for a standard LOI fixture
  • Red cards are more frequent in high-intensity derbies — this can affect in-play markets significantly
