Free Bet Expired on an Irish Betting Site: Can You Recover It?

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

When a free bet expires at a GRAI-licensed Irish betting site, the token is automatically removed from your account. In most cases it is not recoverable. However, there are limited circumstances where contacting support before expiry may help. Here is the full picture.

What Happens When a Free Bet Expires

At midnight (or the specific expiry time stated in the T&Cs), expired free bet tokens are automatically removed by the operator’s system. Your account balance will not decrease — free bets are tokens, not cash — but the tokens disappear from your bonus/promotions section. This happens without notice at the moment of expiry.

Can You Recover an Expired Free Bet?

Once expired, free bets are almost never reinstated. Operators are not legally required to extend or refund expired bonuses — expiry dates are part of the agreed T&Cs. However:

  • If you contact support before expiry and have a genuine technical reason you could not place the bet (site down, payment method issue), some operators will grant a short extension as goodwill.
  • If you can demonstrate the free bet was not credited correctly or the expiry was not clearly communicated, you can raise a complaint under GRAI rules.
  • Simply forgetting to use it is not grounds for reinstatement.

GRAI Requirements on Free Bet Expiry Communication

GRAI inducements transparency rules require operators to clearly disclose expiry dates alongside any bonus offer. If the expiry date was not visible or communicated, that is a potential T&Cs transparency breach. You can raise a formal complaint with the operator, and escalate to the GRAI at grai.ie if unresolved.

How to Avoid Losing Free Bets

  • Read the expiry date in the offer terms when you claim the bonus
  • Turn on push notifications and email from the operator
  • Set a personal calendar reminder 2–3 days before expiry
  • Use the free bet as soon as a suitable event is available rather than waiting for the ideal match

Free Bet Expired: What You Can Do

  • Contact support immediately: Some operators restore expired free bets as a one-off goodwill gesture, especially for long-standing customers. Live chat is fastest.
  • Check terms first: Confirm the free bet was credited and has actually expired — sometimes "not yet credited" looks like "expired" in the UI.
  • Ask for a reload offer instead: If reinstatement is refused, ask whether a current promotion can compensate. Many operators have ongoing reload offers.
  • Note for future: Set a phone calendar reminder for 48h before the free bet expiry date. Most operators email when a free bet is credited — check the email for expiry date.
What happens when a free bet expires?

The free bet token is automatically removed from your account at the expiry time. Your cash balance is unaffected. The token is forfeited.

Can I get my expired free bet back?

Usually no. Contact support before expiry if you had a genuine technical issue. After expiry, reinstatement is at the operator's discretion and rarely granted for simply forgetting.

How much notice should I get before a free bet expires?

GRAI transparency rules require expiry dates to be clearly disclosed. Many operators send an email or push notification 24–48 hours before expiry, but check your notification settings.

Can I complain to the GRAI about an expired free bet?

If the expiry date was not clearly disclosed or communicated, you have grounds for a formal complaint to the operator, with GRAI escalation if unresolved. If you simply forgot, the GRAI is unlikely to intervene.

How long are free bets valid on Irish betting sites?

Typically 7–14 days for welcome offers, 7 days for weekly promotions, 24–48 hours for enhanced odds free bets. Check the T&Cs of each specific offer.
