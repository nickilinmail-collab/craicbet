The Troytown: Ireland's Big November Handicap

The Troytown is to the Irish November what the Paddy Power Gold Cup is to Cheltenham's: a valuable, fiercely competitive handicap chase that attracts a huge field of staying chasers — 29 acceptors stood their ground at the equivalent 2025 stage, and 20-plus runners is normal. Gordon Elliott has dominated the modern race (a seven-time winner since 2014, and in some renewals responsible for over half the field), so expect the Cullentra yard mob-handed again; Answer To Kayf took the 2025 renewal.

Betting Angles

  • Each-way terms are the edge: in a 20-plus runner handicap, the difference between 4 places at 1/4 odds and 6 places at 1/5 odds is often worth more than a point of price. Shop the extra-place offers on the morning — our each-way guide covers the maths.
  • One-yard dominance cuts both ways: when a single trainer saddles a third of the field, the stable's lesser-fancied runners historically outrun their odds more often than the shortest-priced one. Don't assume the market has the yard's pecking order right.
  • Fortria as a watching brief: Grade 2 fields here are small and the favourite often cramped. Seasonal-debut fitness is the unknown — trainers' first-time-out records matter more than last spring's form.
  • The triple-header Saturday: Navan, Cheltenham and the rugby all on one day. Set one combined budget for the day, not three — see our staking basics.

Getting There / Watching

Navan Racecourse is at Proudstown, just north of Navan town, County Meath. The racecourse publishes first-race times and the final running order in race week; feature races are live on Racing TV — see where to watch Irish racing.
