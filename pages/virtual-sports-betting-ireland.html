Virtual Sports Betting in Ireland

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 November 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Virtual sports betting lets you place bets on computer-simulated sporting events that run 24 hours a day, every day of the year. The events — football matches, horse races, greyhound races, and more — look like animated broadcasts but are determined entirely by a certified Random Number Generator. This guide explains exactly how they work, what to expect, and how they compare to betting on real sports.

🎮 Key Point: Virtual Sports Are RNG, Not Real

Virtual sports have no connection to real teams, horses, or athletes. The team names and horse names may look familiar, but outcomes are purely mathematical. No form guide, injury news, or tactical analysis applies. The simulation is generated after the RNG determines the result — not the other way around.

How Virtual Sports Work

Virtual sports events are generated and resolved in one to three minutes. At any point in the day or night, a slate of upcoming virtual events is available to bet on. You browse the markets, place your bets, and watch a short animated simulation before the result is confirmed. The house margin is built into the odds, just as with real sports betting.

The most common virtual sports available to Irish players include:

  • Virtual football — Simulated matches with scorelines, goal scorers, and half-time/full-time results. Betting markets typically cover match result, both teams to score, over/under goals, and correct score.
  • Virtual horse racing — Simulated races with named horses, win and place markets, and each-way options. The familiar betting structure makes this the most popular virtual sport among Irish players with a horse racing background.
  • Virtual greyhound racing — Six or eight dog races with trap draw and win/place markets. Fast format — races complete in under 30 seconds of simulated running.
  • Virtual tennis, cycling, and other sports — Available at some operators, though selection varies.

24/7 Availability: The Core Appeal

The primary practical advantage of virtual sports is availability. Real football fixtures are concentrated in certain hours and certain days. During international breaks, the summer close season, or simply at 3am on a Tuesday, there may be no live fixtures to bet on. Virtual sports fill that gap — a new event starts every few minutes around the clock.

For Irish players, this makes virtual sports a supplement to — not a replacement for — real sports betting. They are available through the sportsbook section of most online bookmakers, often in a dedicated ‘Virtuals’ tab.

The Difference from Real Sports Betting

Betting on real sports involves skill in some measure: form analysis, team news, tactical knowledge, understanding of odds movements. These inputs can generate value for knowledgeable bettors. None of that applies to virtual sports. The RNG makes every event statistically independent of every other. There is no edge to be found through research because there is no information with predictive value.

This matters for Irish players who are experienced real sports bettors: the mental framework of ‘doing your homework’ that applies to football or horse racing has zero transfer value in virtuals. Treat them as a fixed-odds casino game, not as a betting skill game.

House Edge and Odds Margin

The house edge on virtual sports is embedded in the odds, just as with any sportsbook market. The overround (the amount by which the implied probabilities across all outcomes exceed 100%) on virtual sports markets tends to be higher than on competitive real sports markets — often in the 10–20% range. This compares unfavourably to well-priced real sports markets but is broadly comparable to fixed-odds casino table games. No guarantee of winning exists; virtual sports carry a consistent long-run mathematical edge for the operator.

FAQ — Virtual Sports Betting Ireland

What are virtual sports?▾
Virtual sports are computer-simulated sporting events — typically football, horse racing, greyhound racing, tennis, and cycling — that run on a Random Number Generator (RNG). They are not based on real matches or races. Events complete in one to three minutes and run continuously 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
Are virtual sports outcomes random?▾
Yes. Virtual sports outcomes are determined entirely by a certified RNG. No form guides, team statistics, or real-world factors influence the results. The simulation visuals are generated after the outcome is determined. Any strategy based on past results or virtual team ‘form’ has no effect on future outcomes.

Can I bet on virtual sports during the football off-season?▾
Yes. Virtual sports run 24/7 regardless of the real-world sporting calendar. This makes them available during international breaks, summer close seasons, and overnight when real fixtures are limited. However, the RNG-based outcomes mean virtual sports do not replicate the experience of betting on real matches.
What is the house edge on virtual sports betting?▾
The house edge on virtual sports is built into the odds offered, just like a margin on real sports betting. Typical overrounds on virtual sports markets are in the range of 10–20%, which is higher than competitive real sports odds but broadly comparable to fixed-odds casino games. Always calculate the implied margin before placing bets.
