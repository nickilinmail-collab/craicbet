Virtual Sports Betting in Ireland: How It Works and What to Know

Last updated: 4 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 4 November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

What Are Virtual Sports?

Virtual sports are computer-generated simulations of sporting events — football, horse racing, greyhounds, tennis, basketball — with outcomes determined by certified random number generators (RNGs). Unlike real sports, virtual events run continuously around the clock with no dependence on fixtures, weather or athlete form. Results are produced every few minutes, making virtual sports available 24/7 on most licensed Irish betting platforms.

Virtual sports are popular when no live sporting action is available, particularly during the summer close season for football or on quiet midweek periods.

How Virtual Sports Outcomes Are Determined

Virtual sports use RNG software certified by independent testing laboratories to ensure statistical fairness. The simulation assigns random values to match parameters — goals, racehorse speeds, distances — and calculates a result. The house edge is built into the odds, similar to fixed-odds sports betting or casino games. Odds on virtual sports are set by the bookmaker and reflect the RNG probabilities plus margin; they do not change based on team form, player injuries or any real-world variable.

Because outcomes are purely random, there is no such thing as form, strategy or analysis that improves your expected return over time. The house edge is fixed.

Virtual Football vs Real Football Betting

Real football betting allows bettors to use knowledge of teams, players, tactics, injuries, weather and referee tendencies to identify potentially mispriced markets. Virtual football offers none of this: every match has the same underlying probability structure regardless of the team names displayed. Engaging strategies used for real football — following team news, analysing expected goals data — have zero application to virtual football.

Virtual sports are entertainment, not a profitable betting strategy. If you bet on them, treat them as a low-stakes diversion rather than a serious wagering activity.

Are Virtual Sports Covered by GRAI Licences?

Yes. GRAI-licensed bookmakers are authorised to offer virtual sports as part of their betting product. The virtual sports software itself must use certified RNG technology, but this is an operator requirement rather than a separate licence category. Frequently Asked Questions

Can you beat virtual sports betting?

No strategy or analysis can improve your expected return on virtual sports over the long run. Outcomes are determined by certified RNG software and the house edge is fixed in the odds.

Are virtual sports available 24/7?

Yes. Most GRAI-licensed bookmakers run virtual sports events continuously, every few minutes, with no dependency on real-world fixture schedules.

Is virtual sports betting different from slots?

Both use RNG software. Virtual sports present the random outcome as a simulated sporting event rather than spinning reels. The fundamental mechanics are similar: a certified RNG determines the result and the house edge is built into the payout structure.
