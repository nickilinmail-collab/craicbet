GRAI Betting Licence: What It Means for Irish Players

The GRAI (Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland) is the statutory body established under the Gambling Regulation Act 2024 to regulate all commercial gambling in Ireland. When a bookmaker holds a GRAI remote betting licence, Irish players benefit from a specific set of mandatory protections:

  • Mandatory deposit limits: GRAI-licensed operators must offer deposit limit tools, with cooling-off periods before increases take effect.
  • NGEI self-exclusion: Ireland's National Gambling Exclusion Ireland register allows players to self-exclude across all GRAI-licensed operators simultaneously — one decision, applied everywhere.
  • Advertising standards: GRAI-licensed operators are subject to Ireland's advertising rules, restricting how and when gambling is promoted.
  • Formal complaints route: If a dispute with a GRAI-licensed operator cannot be resolved directly, you have recourse through the GRAI's alternative dispute resolution (ADR) process. First raise the complaint directly with the operator; if unresolved after eight weeks (or if you receive a final response you reject), you may escalate to the GRAI.
  • Responsible gambling obligations: Operators must provide access to gamblingcare.ie and comparable support resources, including links to the Gambling Awareness Trust helpline (1800 753 753).

To verify any GRAI licence, go to the official public licence register on grai.ie. Valid GRAI remote betting licences appear in the format GRAI-XXXX-RB-26-XXXX. For a full walkthrough, see our GRAI player protection guide.

By contrast, offshore operators without a GRAI licence offer Irish players no access to these protections. The GRAI has the power to block unlicensed offshore operators from accepting Irish players. If you bet with an unlicensed operator, you have no Irish regulatory recourse if something goes wrong.

Casino games and the poker room at Unibet operate under a Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) licence. This is a reputable regulatory framework, but it is not a GRAI licence.
