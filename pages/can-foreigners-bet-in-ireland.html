Can Foreigners Bet in Ireland? A Guide for Visitors and EU Citizens

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 Mark Craic, editor 📅 Updated 5 November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Yes — non-Irish citizens and visitors can legally use GRAI-licensed betting sites in Ireland. There is no residency requirement to place bets. However, there are practical considerations around account verification, payment methods, and tax rules in your home country. Here is the full picture.

EU Citizens in Ireland

EU citizens living in or visiting Ireland can open accounts and bet at GRAI-licensed bookmakers without restriction. As an EU citizen, you have the same legal rights under Irish consumer protection law as Irish nationals when using a GRAI-licensed operator — including access to the GRAI complaints process.

You will need to complete standard KYC verification: a valid passport or EU national ID card for identity, and proof of an Irish address (or in some cases your foreign address is accepted).

Non-EU Visitors and Tourists

Tourists from non-EU countries (UK, US, Australia, etc.) can generally access GRAI-licensed betting sites while physically present in Ireland. Some operators require a valid payment method — a debit card linked to any EU/EEA bank account, or a service like Revolut, typically works. A non-Irish proof of address is usually accepted during KYC.

The key restriction is on the operator’s side: GRAI-licensed operators are licensed to take bets from people physically in Ireland. If you return to your home country, you would then need to use a bookmaker licensed in your jurisdiction.

Geolocation and IP Considerations

Some GRAI-licensed operators use IP geolocation to restrict access when you are outside Ireland. This is a legitimate technical measure, not an Irish legal requirement. If you are in Ireland but connecting via a VPN that routes traffic through another country, you may be blocked — always use your real connection when in Ireland.

Tax in Your Home Country

Irish tax exempts winnings earned on Irish betting sites. However, your home country’s tax rules may apply to gambling winnings earned abroad. This is particularly relevant for US citizens (US tax applies to gambling winnings worldwide) and UK residents (UK winnings are generally tax-free, but check if you are domiciled elsewhere).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Non-Irish Residents Bet in Ireland?

Yes — GRAI-licensed betting sites are open to any adult resident of Ireland, regardless of nationality. As long as you are physically located in Ireland and aged 18 or over, you can register and bet. You will need to pass the standard Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification, which accepts a wide range of international identity documents.

Address Verification for Non-Irish Nationals

  • You must provide a proof of address in Ireland (not your home country) — a utility bill, bank statement, or lease agreement showing your Irish address, dated within 3 months.
  • Irish Revenue (PPS number) is not required for betting — but some sites may request additional documentation for high-volume accounts.
  • If you are a student or short-term visitor, some sites may limit withdrawals until full KYC is completed with a fixed Irish address.

Currency and Payment Options for Non-Irish Residents

  • Irish betting sites primarily operate in euros (€). If your bank card is in another currency, your bank will convert at their exchange rate — check for foreign transaction fees.
  • Revolut and Wise (TransferWise) are popular options for non-EU visitors betting in Ireland — both offer euro accounts with minimal conversion fees.
  • PayPal is widely accepted and handles currency conversion automatically.
Can a non-Irish person bet online in Ireland?

Yes — there is no residency requirement. EU citizens and visitors can use GRAI-licensed bookmakers. You need to pass standard KYC with a valid ID document.

Do tourists need an Irish address to open a betting account?

Not necessarily — many GRAI-licensed operators accept foreign proof of address for non-residents. Check the specific operator's KYC requirements.

Can UK citizens bet on Irish bookmakers?

Yes, if physically in Ireland or if the operator accepts UK-based customers (some do). UK citizens have all the usual GRAI consumer protections when using a GRAI-licensed operator.

Are my betting winnings taxed when I'm a foreign visitor betting in Ireland?

Not by Ireland — gambling winnings are tax-free in Ireland for all individuals. Your home country may tax gambling winnings earned abroad, so check your own jurisdiction's rules.

What happens if I try to bet on an Irish site from abroad?

Some operators use IP geolocation to restrict access outside Ireland. This is a technical restriction, not an Irish legal requirement. When back in Ireland, your account functions normally.
