Apple Pay is a convenient and secure way to deposit at online casinos from your iPhone or iPad. Tap to pay using Face ID or Touch ID — no card numbers exposed. Here's how it works at Irish casinos.

How Apple Pay Deposits Work at Casinos

At supported casinos, select Apple Pay in the deposit section. Your iPhone shows the payment prompt — authenticate with Face ID or Touch ID. The deposit is processed instantly via your linked card (usually Visa or Mastercard). No card details are shared with the casino.

Apple Pay Withdrawals

Fewer casinos support Apple Pay withdrawals than deposits. Most that accept Apple Pay for deposits process withdrawals back to your linked debit card, not directly via Apple Pay. This can take 1–3 business days rather than instant. Check withdrawal options before choosing a casino if fast payouts are your priority.

Security Benefits of Apple Pay

Apple Pay uses tokenisation — a unique transaction code replaces your actual card number for each payment. The casino never receives your real card details. If the casino suffers a data breach, your card isn't compromised. Additionally, Apple Pay requires biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) for every transaction.

Apple Pay Limits at Casinos

Apple Pay casino limits are typically set by the casino's standard deposit limits (€10–€50,000 per transaction depending on the casino), not by Apple Pay itself. Your linked card's own limits may also apply. Contact your bank if large Apple Pay transactions are declined.
