House Edge — Why Rules Matter

The house edge in blackjack is not fixed — it depends on the specific table rules. The single most important rule is blackjack payout:

Rule 1 for live blackjack: Always play at a table that pays 3:2 on blackjack. A 6:5 payout adds 1.4% to the house edge — this single rule change is more costly than any strategy error.

Basic Strategy — The One Tool That Reduces House Edge

Basic strategy is a mathematically derived decision chart showing the optimal play (hit, stand, double or split) for every hand combination. Playing basic strategy reduces the house edge from ~2–3% (intuitive play) to ~0.5% (optimal play). Basic strategy charts are freely available and can be referenced while playing live blackjack online — this is not against the rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the house edge in live blackjack?

With perfect basic strategy, standard 6-deck blackjack (3:2 payout) has a house edge of approximately 0.5–0.6%. Tables paying 6:5 instead of 3:2 add ~1.4% to the house edge — always play at 3:2 tables.

How does live dealer blackjack work online?

A human dealer in a studio deals physical cards from a shoe. Players join via browser or app, see a live video feed, and make decisions using on-screen buttons. Results are determined by the physical cards.

What is Infinite Blackjack?

Infinite Blackjack by Evolution allows unlimited players to join the same table simultaneously. All players receive the same starting hand but make independent decisions. It pays 3:2 on blackjack.

Can I count cards in live blackjack?

Live blackjack uses 6–8 decks with low penetration before reshuffling. Card counting at online live tables offers no meaningful edge in practice.
