Christmas Betting Ireland 2026: Racing & Football Guide

Last updated: 12 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

The Irish betting year peaks in the week after Christmas. In 2026 St Stephen's Day falls on a Saturday, so four days of Leopardstown, the King George VI Chase and a full Premier League card all land on the same weekend. This page is the calendar: every date below was checked against the racecourses, the Premier League and the PDC on 12 November 2026, and each row links to our detailed guide.

Christmas Day: Almost Nothing to Bet On

There is no racing in Ireland or Britain on 25 December, and the Premier League has not played on Christmas Day for decades. The one market that settles that day is "top of the table at Christmas", which is decided by the standings after the last round before the 25th. See Premier League top at Christmas.

St Stephen's Day, Saturday 26 December

Three Irish meetings race on St Stephen's Day: Leopardstown and Limerick both open four-day Christmas festivals, and Down Royal stages its traditional single card. Across the water the King George VI Chase heads the first day at Kempton Park. Full detail is in the St Stephen's Day racing guide and the King George VI Chase guide.

Football is back in force too. After only one Premier League game was played on 26 December 2025, seven are scheduled this year: one at 12:30, four at 15:00, one at 17:30 and one at 20:00, with the other three games of the round on Sunday 27 December.

Leopardstown Christmas Festival, 26–29 December

Four days and seven Grade 1 races make this the strongest jumps meeting in Ireland before the spring. In 2025 the running order was a Grade 1 novice chase on day one, the big handicap chase on day two, the Savills Chase and Christmas Hurdle on day three and the December Hurdle on day four. The 2026 cards are published by the course closer to the meeting. See the Leopardstown Christmas Festival guide and each-way terms at the festival.

What Changed in Britain This Year

British racing has spread its big festive races over four consecutive days for 2026. The King George stays on 26 December, Kempton's second day follows on the 27th, the Welsh Grand National moves to Monday 28 December and Newbury's Challow Hurdle card takes Tuesday 29 December. For Irish punters that means a feature race in Britain alongside Leopardstown on each of the four days.

Honest Maths for a Busy Week

  • Each-way terms vary by race. Standard terms pay 1/4 the odds for three places in handicaps of 12 to 15 runners and four places with 16 or more; non-handicaps with eight or more runners usually pay 1/5 the odds for three places. Extra-place offers change the price you get, so compare both. Use the each-way calculator.
  • Football accas multiply the margin. Seven games on one afternoon invites a seven-fold, but the bookmaker's margin compounds with every leg. Check the true cost in the acca calculator.
  • Free bets return winnings only. A festive free bet does not hand back the stake, and most expire within days. See how free bets work.
  • Set the budget before the 26th. Decide what the week can cost and set a deposit limit in your account before the first race, not after a losing day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there racing in Ireland on Christmas Day?

No. There is no racing in Ireland or Britain on Christmas Day. Irish racing resumes on St Stephen's Day, Saturday 26 December 2026, with meetings at Leopardstown, Limerick and Down Royal.

When is the Leopardstown Christmas Festival in 2026?

It runs for four days, from Saturday 26 December to Tuesday 29 December 2026. Limerick's Christmas Festival runs on the same four days.

When is the King George VI Chase in 2026?

Saturday 26 December 2026 at Kempton Park, on the first day of Kempton's two-day Christmas meeting. It is a Grade 1 chase over three miles on turf.

How many Premier League games are on St Stephen's Day 2026?

Seven are scheduled for Saturday 26 December 2026, with the other three games of the round on Sunday 27 December. Kick-off times can still move for television.

Can I bet online on Christmas Day?

Online bookmakers take bets every day of the year, but there is little sport on 25 December. Retail betting shops in Ireland are closed on Christmas Day.
