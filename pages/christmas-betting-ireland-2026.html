Christmas Betting Ireland 2026: Racing & Football Guide

Last updated: 5 November 2026

📝 CraicBet Editorial Team📅 November 2026⏱ 4 min read

Christmas week is one of the busiest betting periods of the Irish calendar. Christmas Day and St Stephen's Day feature premium National Hunt racing, festive Premier League football and several other sporting events that attract significant betting volumes.

Christmas Day Betting

Racing on Christmas Day in Ireland is traditionally limited, as most tracks do not hold meetings. The focus shifts to anticipation for St Stephen's Day, though UK fixtures at Kempton Park (King George VI Chase) and Wetherby attract Irish punters. Premier League football and other British football leagues also hold Christmas Day fixtures.

St Stephen's Day Racing

St Stephen's Day is one of the biggest racing days in Ireland. Leopardstown Racecourse opens its Christmas Festival on 26 December, with Grade 1 National Hunt racing that attracts the leading trainers and owners. This is the second most bet-on racing day in Ireland after Cheltenham. See our dedicated St Stephen's Day racing guide for full detail.

Premier League Christmas Fixtures

The Premier League traditionally holds a full round of fixtures over Christmas, often on 26 December and 28 December. These fixtures attract very high betting volumes globally. Frequently Asked Questions

Is there racing in Ireland on Christmas Day?

Most Irish tracks do not hold racing on Christmas Day itself. Leopardstown's Christmas Festival begins on St Stephen's Day (26 December). UK racing at Kempton Park is a popular Christmas Day betting option for Irish punters.

When does the Leopardstown Christmas Festival take place?

The Leopardstown Christmas Festival runs from 26 December (St Stephen's Day) to 29 December. It features Grade 1 National Hunt racing and is one of the highlights of the Irish racing calendar.

Are GRAI-licensed bookmakers open on Christmas Day?

GRAI-licensed online bookmakers are available 24/7 including Christmas Day. Physical betting shops may have reduced opening hours.

What is the King George VI Chase?

The King George VI Chase at Kempton Park on 26 December is one of the premier Grade 1 National Hunt chases in Britain. It regularly features top Irish-trained horses and is one of the most bet-on races outside the Cheltenham Festival.
