How to Choose the Right Payment Method

  • Speed: If fast withdrawals matter most, choose PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, or crypto

FAQ — Casino Payment Methods Ireland

What is the fastest withdrawal method at Irish casinos?▾
Cryptocurrency offers the fastest withdrawals, typically processed in under 1 hour. Among traditional methods, PayPal, Skrill, and Neteller are the fastest at 1–24 hours. Visa and bank transfers are the slowest at 1–5 business days. See fastest payout casinos →
Can my Irish bank block casino payments?▾
Yes. Some Irish banks (particularly AIB and Bank of Ireland) may block online gambling transactions by default. You can usually enable gambling payments through your banking app or by calling customer service. Alternatively, use Revolut, an e-wallet, or crypto to bypass bank restrictions entirely.
Are there fees for depositing at online casinos?▾
Casinos almost never charge deposit fees. However, your payment provider might. E-wallets like Skrill and Neteller charge 1–2.5% to fund your wallet. Cryptocurrency has network fees. Visa, Mastercard, Revolut, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are typically fee-free on both ends.
Do I have to withdraw using the same method I deposited with?▾
Usually yes. Most casinos require you to withdraw to the same method you deposited with, at least up to the deposit amount. This is an anti-money-laundering requirement. If your deposit method doesn’t support withdrawals (like Paysafecard), the casino will offer alternative methods such as bank transfer.
Is Revolut good for online casinos in Ireland?▾
Yes, Revolut is arguably the best option for Irish casino players. It offers instant deposits, faster withdrawals than traditional cards (1–2 days), no fees, disposable virtual cards for security, and in-app spending limits for responsible gambling. It’s our #1 recommended payment method. See Revolut casinos →
