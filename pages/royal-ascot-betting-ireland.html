Royal Ascot 2027 Betting Guide — Ireland's Biggest Flat Racing Bet

📝 Mark Craic, editor (8 years in gambling analytics) 📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

Royal Ascot is the five-day June festival at Ascot Racecourse in Berkshire that draws the world's best flat horses from Ireland, Britain, France, the US and beyond. It is the most heavily bet flat racing event of the year for Irish punters, with Irish-trained horses regularly contesting — and winning — the top races. This guide covers the festival's structure, the key races from an Irish betting perspective, and how to approach the week.

Royal Ascot: Festival Programme

Royal Ascot runs for five days in mid-June (Tuesday to Saturday). Each day features at least one Group 1 race. The principal races from an Irish betting perspective are:

  • Queen Anne Stakes (Tuesday, Group 1, 1m) — usually features top international milers.
  • St James's Palace Stakes (Tuesday, Group 1, 1m) — 3-year-old colts; classic contenders.
  • Gold Cup (Thursday, Group 1, 2m 4f) — the stayers' showpiece; Irish stayers frequently dominate.
  • Diamond Jubilee Stakes (Saturday, Group 1, 6f) — leading sprinters.
  • Golden Gate Fields Coronation Stakes (Friday, Group 1, 1m) — 3-year-old fillies.

Irish Contenders and Betting Patterns

Irish stables have dominated Royal Ascot in recent years. The Gold Cup, the Stayers races and the mile categories have all seen significant Irish representation. For Irish punters, tracking which horses from the leading yards (Ballydoyle, Closutton, Rosewell) have entered for Ascot is a standard pre-festival routine.

Market moves in the days before Royal Ascot are significant — commission agent activity from leading yards can be detected in price movements on the morning of the race. Watching for horses that drift in the market before Ascot — when they were expected to be supported — can also be informative.

Each-Way Strategy at Royal Ascot

Field sizes at Ascot vary significantly by race. Group 1 races may have 8–16 runners (3-place each-way terms at 1/4 odds for small fields; 4-place for larger). Handicaps — particularly the Royal Hunt Cup and the Wokingham — have fields of 20–30 runners and attract extended each-way terms of 5 or 6 places. The Royal Hunt Cup and Wokingham are specifically targeted by each-way punters for this reason. Use our each-way calculator to plan your Ascot week bets.

Ante-Post for Royal Ascot

Royal Ascot ante-post markets open from January for the main Group 1 races. The best prices are available in winter for horses who have already indicated an Ascot target. Prices contract rapidly after the English Classics in May and the Irish spring campaigns at Leopardstown and The Curragh. For handicap races, the draw at Ascot can be significant in soft going — high draws in some handicap fields have a historical advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Royal Ascot 2027?

Royal Ascot 2027 is a five-day festival in mid-June, typically the third week of June (Tuesday to Saturday). Exact dates are confirmed by Ascot Racecourse during the year.

What is the Gold Cup at Royal Ascot?

The Gold Cup is a Group 1 race over 2 miles 4 furlongs at Royal Ascot, run on Ladies Day (Thursday). It is the traditional stayers' championship of British and Irish flat racing and regularly attracts strong Irish contenders.

What each-way terms apply at Royal Ascot?

Terms vary by race. Group 1 races with 8–12 runners: 3 places at 1/4 odds. Handicaps with 20+ runners: 5 or 6 places at 1/4 odds. Always confirm terms before placing.

Do Irish-trained horses do well at Royal Ascot?

Yes. Irish stables have been prominent at Royal Ascot across all distances for many years. The Gold Cup, the stayers' races and the mile races have seen particularly strong Irish representation.
