What Are Instant Win Games?

Instant win games share a single defining characteristic: the outcome is determined and revealed immediately after you place a bet. There are no reels spinning for several seconds, no poker hand to build, no wheel to watch. You act, and you find out. This makes them among the simplest and most straightforward games in any online casino.

The category covers a broad range of formats. The most common types available to Irish players are:

  • Digital scratch cards — The online equivalent of a physical scratch card. You ‘scratch’ panels to reveal symbols or numbers, and matching combinations win prizes. Themes range from classic gold bar designs to licensed TV formats.
  • Keno — A lottery-style number draw game. You pick numbers from a grid (typically 1–80), and the game draws a set of winning numbers. The more of your selections that match, the larger the prize. Keno allows you to control the pace — you decide when to draw.
  • Online bingo — Cards pre-filled with numbers; a draw completes lines and full houses. Online bingo rooms often include auto-daub features and social chat rooms. The result is instant once your card is complete.
  • Virtual instant sports — Condensed simulations of horse racing, football, greyhound racing, and other sports. Each event resolves in seconds using RNG. Distinct from virtual sports betting (covered in our separate guide), as these sit in the casino section of most operators.

RTP Ranges: What to Expect

Published RTP figures for instant win games vary more than slot RTPs, which tend to cluster in the 94–97% range. For digital scratch cards, RTPs often fall in the 85–95% range — meaning a house edge of 5–15%. Higher-denomination scratch cards frequently carry a higher RTP than lower-priced ones, though this varies by game and provider. Always check the game info panel for the published figure before playing.

Keno typically carries a higher house edge than scratch cards — often 20–30% depending on how many numbers you select. This makes keno among the worst-value games in terms of house edge, though the ability to control pace and bet size can suit casual players who want a lottery-style experience without large session losses.

Online bingo RTPs vary by game type and ticket price, but licensed operators are required to publish RTP information for all games.

Instant Win Games vs Slots

The main difference is the experience rather than the mathematics. Slots use spinning reels, paylines, bonus rounds, and free spins to create a prolonged, feature-rich play session. The anticipation built during a spin cycle — especially during bonus rounds — is by design. Instant win games skip that entirely: you play at your own pace, one result at a time.

For casual players who prefer a simple, lottery-style experience without the complexity of slot features, instant win games can be a better fit. For players who enjoy the entertainment of a bonus round, slots offer more variety. Neither is inherently better value — both categories have wide RTP ranges.

Appeal for Casual and New Players

Instant win games suit players who are new to online casinos, who want a quick session without learning game mechanics, or who prefer the familiarity of a scratch card format. Mobile play is particularly smooth — scratch cards require minimal graphics and load quickly on any connection. The low minimum bets on many digital scratch cards (from €0.10 or less) also make them accessible regardless of budget.
